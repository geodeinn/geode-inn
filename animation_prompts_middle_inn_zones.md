### ZONE 4: CAMPGROUNDS (Level 4 — Exterior & Ren Faire)

1. **Subject Name:** Campgrounds Exterior & Market Field Background (Zone 4)
2. **Visual Description:** An expansive, open-air Renaissance Faire set against the rustic front facade of the Geode Inn and the treeline of an ancient forest. Canvas market stalls with red-and-purple striped canopies, rough-hewn wooden booths, and stone fire pits circle a dusty earthen courtyard. Visible structures include the rustic log facade of the Huntsman Tavern with its wide wooden porch, Solstara's canvas-roofed jeweler studio filled with workbenches, the open-air Blacksmith's forge with red coal embers, and a velvet-draped Fortune Teller booth.
3. **Linework Style:** Heavy cel-shaded (tradesman style) — bold 3-4px clean black outlines with flat fills on architectural elements, wooden beams, canvas tents, and environmental props to establish solid, grounded physical presence.
4. **Color Palette:** Canvas tan, deep grass green, warm timber brown, open sky blue; secondary accents in ale amber, forge orange, banner red, market-stripe purple, bright copper, and polished steel.
5. **Lighting/Atmosphere:** Crisp, warm open-air daylight. Sunbeams filter through canvas awnings and tree branches. Soft rising heat hazes shimmer around the forge embers and tavern chimney, contrasting with cooler shadows under tent canopies.
6. **Animation Notes:** 
   - **Idle Background Loop (60 FPS):** Banners and tent flaps sway in a gentle, rhythmic outdoor breeze. Thin plumes of forge smoke and chimney soot curl lazily into the blue sky. 
   - **Interactive Details:** Sparks occasionally float upward from the blacksmith's forge. Dust motes dance in sunbeams across the market path. Leaves drift gently down from the forest edge. Market items on stalls (beads, copper trinkets, leather goods connected to the MarketItem entity) reflect subtle light glints as the sun moves.
7. **Sound Connection:** Ambient market murmur, snapping canvas banners in wind, faint clink of copper items, distant crackle of forge fire. Underlaid by a subtle, low-frequency 42Hz earth rumble that anchors the exterior to the Inn's core.
8. **Special Notes:** Connects directly to the MarketItem entity system for player trading. Serves as the Inn's hands and public front door, transitioning the player from enclosed home warmth to open-air social energy.

### ZONE 5: STAIRCASE (Level 5 — The Spine & Ascent)

1. **Subject Name:** The Ascending Staircase Environment (Zone 5)
2. **Visual Description:** A sweeping, wide stone spiral staircase winding upward around the Inn's massive central living tree trunk. Worn stone steps feature carved alchemical symbols and musical notation along their edges. Embedded along the outer stone walls are six carved gargoyle niches at regular intervals. Midway up, a doorway opens into the brass-geared Clockmaker's Workshop; alchemical text shelves line the curved stone alcoves.
3. **Linework Style:** Sharp/angular (guardian style) — crisp, geometric 2-3px linework emphasizing architectural precision, gear teeth, stone facets, and structural strength.
4. **Color Palette:** Base stone gray, gear bronze, polished brass; secondary tones in alchemical emerald green, candle amber, and shadow purple; frequency light accents in warm white (111Hz), deep blue (64Hz), bright gold (432Hz), and radiant green (528Hz).
5. **Lighting/Atmosphere:** Evolving vertical lighting gradient:
   - *Bottom (Catacombs entrance):* Deep shadowed purple-black, dim ambient dust.
   - *Middle (Clockmaker heart):* Warm brass-gold light pulsing at the 50 BPM heartbeat tempo (dimming on off-beat, brightening on beat).
   - *Top (Cosmology entrance):* Pale silver and cold starlight white.
6. **Animation Notes:**
   - **Idle Background Loop (60 FPS):** The entire staircase visually "breathes" as ambient wall sconces and gargoyle eye-runes pulse in synchrony with the 50 BPM heartbeat. Brass gears visible inside wall cutouts turn with mechanical rhythm.
   - **Ascension Motion:** Dust motes float upward along the vertical drafts. As the camera ascends, ambient color smoothly shifts from purple-shadow to warm gold to high-key silver-white.
7. **Sound Connection:** 50 BPM mechanical heartbeat rhythm originating from the Clockmaker mechanism, rising musical pitch/key with upward ascension, origin lullaby motifs playing softly in background.
8. **Special Notes:** Represents the Inn's emotional origin—a sanctuary for a child needing space between rooms. Bridges physical matter to spiritual elevation.

### ZONE 6: CATACOMBS (Level 6 — The Archive & Deep Memory)

1. **Subject Name:** The Catacombs Labyrinthine Archive (Zone 6)
2. **Visual Description:** A vast, subterranean stone vault with towering floor-to-ceiling wooden archive shelves filled with ancient scrolls, clay tablets, bound primary sources, and geological mineral cases. Massive rough-cut amethyst formations grow directly out of stone pillars. In the center, a heavy oak desk sits littered with parchment, inkwells, and magnifiers. A rolling library ladder rests against high shelf tracks, and an illuminated glass pedestal displays the Oklahoma Foundation Stone.
3. **Linework Style:** Soft flowing (lore style) — gentle, organic 1-2px linework with fine hatching for dusty book edges, parchment rolls, mineral facets, and ancient stone carvings.
4. **Color Palette:** Deep dusty amber, dark stone slate, antique bronze, faded parchment yellow, walnut wood brown, with vivid raw amethyst purple and glowing gold accents.
5. **Lighting/Atmosphere:** Quiet, reverent, dim amber atmosphere. Low directional light emanates from brass desk lamps and glowing mineral geodes, casting long soft shadows between towering book stacks. Subtle dust motes hover in warm pools of light.
6. **Animation Notes:**
   - **Idle Background Loop (60 FPS):** Soft dust motes drift slowly through golden light shafts. Desk lamp candlelight flickers gently.
   - **Interactive Details:** Scrolls on open reading tables gently unroll or rustle. Faint amethyst light pulses from the central Foundation Stone pedestal in a deep 42Hz rhythm.
7. **Sound Connection:** Deep 42Hz tone axis resonance from the Foundation Stone, muted footsteps on stone, soft rustle of parchment, resonant echo of deep subterranean acoustics.
8. **Special Notes:** Holds the primary sources of dead civilizations and unsoftened historical memory. Functions as the Inn's grave and archive—a place where past ideas are honored and preserved rather than lost.
