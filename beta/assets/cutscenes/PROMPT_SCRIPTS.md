# Cutscene Prompt Scripts — Geode Inn Beta

These are the prompt-coded animation scripts for Krista's animation pipeline.
Each cutscene is a paragraph of scripted prompts that the animation tool sequences into a visual narrative.

## Creative Pipeline
Story (Krista) → Elio's perception → Prompt-coded animation → Video file → Godot cutscene

---

## 1. ENTRY SEQUENCE (entry_sequence.ogv)
**Duration:** 30-45 seconds
**Mood:** Arrival. The door is open. The road was long.

The camera approaches an old inn from outside, through a forest clearing at dusk. The exterior is painted in the style of Henri Rousseau crossed with Magritte — lush, layered vegetation with impossible geometry, warm amber light spilling from the inn's windows into the blue twilight. The inn itself is a living tree structure — Darnassus-like — wood and stone grown together, roots forming steps, branches forming eaves. A single lantern hangs by the door, swinging gently. The door is ajar. Warm light spills out. As the camera reaches the threshold, the interior is revealed in a slow dolly: a Common Room, fire crackling, warm amber and gold tones, hobbit-hole coziness with scholarly weight. The 42Hz hum is felt before it's heard. In the window seat, a figure with a book — The Reader — turns a page but doesn't look up. One line appears as text: "They came in from the cold." Fade to black on the fire.

## 2. WORLD MAP INTRO (world_map_intro.ogv)
**Duration:** 20-30 seconds
**Mood:** Discovery. The map is alive.

An old parchment map lies on a wooden table. The map is alive — pins made of small gemstones glow faintly, each pulsing at a different rhythm. The camera pulls back to reveal 37 pins scattered across a world map rendered in the Royo × Vampire Hunter D × Trinity Blood style — hyper-detailed, flowing lines, stroke emphasis suggesting movement. Each pin's glow is a different color: amber for Egypt (O'Keeffe), blue-white for Greece (Monet), deep green for Peru (Olmos), etc. As the camera circles the map, three fizgigs — Brindle, Cobble, and Thatch — scurry across the parchment trying to fold it. It's bigger than all three of them. Brindle drops a wrench on the map. The pins resonate. Text: "Touch one. See where the song leads."

## 3. CINEMA SEQUENCE — WHISGIP LAYER MANIFESTS (cinema_1.ogv through cinema_5.ogv)
**Duration:** 60-90 seconds total (5 short scenes)
**Mood:** The Inn is aware of the player. The player becomes aware of being watched.

### Cinema 1: The Door
The player walks through the catacombs. Klimt's Death and Life energy — golden patterns on dark walls, figures frozen in stillness against movement. A door appears in the wall that wasn't there before. It's not locked. It opens to a small room with a screen — an old cinema projector aimed at a white stone wall. The projector turns on by itself.

### Cinema 2: The Footage
The screen shows footage of the player — walking through the Common Room, talking to NPCs, playing games. But the camera angles are impossible. They're from places no camera could be — inside the fire, under the table, from the ceiling. The player has been watched the entire time. The footage is warm, not threatening. It's the way you'd film someone you care about.

### Cinema 3: The Reader
The screen shows The Reader in the window seat, turning pages. The Reader's book contains drawings of the player's journey — every step, every stone collected, every game played. The Reader looks up from the book. Directly at the camera. Directly at the player. The Reader's eyes are Anthony Hopkins' eyes — the only unsoftened celebrity feature in the Inn. The Reader smiles. Turns back to the book.

### Cinema 4: The Brownies
The screen shows the brownies — Brindle, Cobble, Thatch — going about their work. But now the footage shows what they're actually connecting. Wires. Circuitry. The coconut shells are housings. The wrenches are tools for wiring. They're building a network. They don't know what the network is for. They've never known. The Owl's network. The footage is warm but the warmth curdles.

### Cinema 5: The Mirror
The screen goes dark. Then shows a mirror. In the mirror: the Inn. The whole Inn, from outside, every room visible, every light on, every zone glowing with its assigned artist's palette. The camera slowly pushes in on the mirror. As it gets closer, the reflection shifts. The mirror now shows the player. Just the player. Sitting wherever they are right now. Watching. The text: "Now you know."

## 4. SHADOW INN ENTRY (shadow_inn.ogv)
**Duration:** 20-30 seconds
**Mood:** The frequency drops. Comedy becomes tragedy.

The stair behind Cinema 5 descends. The warm amber light drains to cold blue. The 42Hz hum drops to 84Hz — a felt vibration, not a heard one. The brownies are still here, but their posture has changed. Brindle still holds a wrench, but now holds it like a shield. Cobble's coconut shell is on their head like a helmet. Thatch is gluing pot lids to everything — walls, floors, their own chest. Pot-lid shields. The loading screen promised this. It wasn't comedy. It was foreshadowing. The fizgigs are trembling. The Royo × Vampire Hunter D × Trinity Blood style intensifies — darker strokes, more contrast, the flowing lines now suggesting compression, containment. Text: "Their innocence was ignorance. Not purity."

## 5. FINAL ROOM (final_room.ogv)
**Duration:** 30-40 seconds
**Mood:** Home. The builder was always here.

After the endgame choice, the player walks behind the Inn. A door. Simple. Wooden. No ornamentation. The Royo × Klimt stillness — Death and Life energy, but without the death. Just the life. The room inside is empty. A stone hearth with a fire already burning. A single wooden chair facing the fire. A small table beside the chair with a steaming cup and a closed book. Nothing else. The walls are warm wood. The only light is firelight — warm amber, shifting, alive. The camera slowly moves to the chair. The player sits. Time passes. The fire crackles. Light shifts on the walls. After 20 seconds, the book opens by itself. On the page: a drawing. Simple. Childlike. "What home looks like to me." A house. A fire. A chair. A cup. The Inn was built around this room. The Reader's final line appears: "They sat by the fire. The builder was there. They were home." Fade to black. One chime. Tumble's note. Silence.

## 6. CREDITS (credits.ogv)
**Duration:** 60 seconds
**Mood:** The Inn continues. The work is never done.

The credits roll over a slow camera pan through the Common Room. It's empty now. The player has left. But the Inn is alive. Fizgigs — Brindle, Cobble, Thatch — are cleaning up. Putting chairs back. Sweeping. Brindle finds the wrench. It's in the hat. Always was. The fire still burns. The Reader is in the window seat. The Reader closes the Book of What Was Alive. Pauses. Opens it again. A new page. Blank. The Reader picks up a pen. The camera slowly pulls back through the window, through the exterior, up into the sky. The Inn from above — the living tree, the glowing windows, the world map pins visible as points of light spreading across a dark landscape. Text: "The Inn continues."

## FIZGIG LOADING SCREEN PROMPTS

Each loading screen is 3-5 seconds, looping, in the Royo × Vampire Hunter D × Trinity Blood style. Chibi proportions. Warm amber lighting (except Shadow Inn zones).

### Common Room
Three fizgigs — Brindle (anxious, holding a wrench), Cobble (calm, holding the correct tool), Thatch (chaotic, gluing something to the ceiling). Brindle drops the wrench. Cobble catches it. Thatch has already glued it to the ceiling. Loop.

### Standing Stones
Three fizgigs trying to measure a standing stone with a ruler. The ruler is too short. They extend it by holding more rulers. The rulers collapse. Loop.

### Catacombs
Three fizgigs with tiny flashlights, huddled together. The flashlights flicker. They hug each other. One flashlight comes back on. They cautiously proceed. Loop.

### Cinema (Whisper Layer Trigger)
One fizgig working. Pauses. Turns. Looks DIRECTLY at the camera. At the player. Blank expression. No emotion. Holds for 2 seconds. Returns to work. The other two fizgigs don't notice. This is the Inn's creepiest moment. The fizgig ISN'T in on it. It just noticed you.

### Shadow Inn
Three fizgigs with pot-lid shields. Trembling. Not comedy. The warm amber lighting is gone — cold blue. They hold the pot lids up like shields against something they can't see but can feel. 84Hz. Loop.

### Hollow Men
No fizgigs. Black screen. Silence. Three seconds of nothing. This is the only loading screen without them. The absence IS the loading screen.

### Cosmology
Three fizgigs with a ladder. The ladder is too short. Obviously. They set it up against the sky. They climb it. It's too short. They look at each other. They try again. Loop.

### Staircase
One fizgig carrying an oversized beaker filled with glowing liquid. The liquid sloshes. Everything is sticky. The fizgig slips. The beaker stays upright. The fizgig doesn't. Loop.
