# Geode Inn — Complete Game Catalog
## Geography, Cultures & Cryptids

---

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Every game in the Geode Inn follows three principles:
1. **Real geography** — set in an actual location with real geology
2. **Cultural mythology** — draws on that region's lore from the 191-country database
3. **Cryptid/creature** — features a mythological being from that culture

Games are organized in three tiers:
- **Tier 1: Core Arcade Games** (4 games, Main Floor) — zone-unlocking, the big draws
- **Tier 2: Zone Signature Games** (9 games, one per level) — the unique mechanic of each zone
- **Tier 3: Cultural Mini-Games** (world map) — short region-specific games tied to lore pins

---

## TIER 1: CORE ARCADE GAMES (Main Floor — Zone Unlocking)

These are the four zone-unlocking games in the Main Floor Arcade. Each is inspired by a classic arcade title, set in a real geographic location, and unlocks a new zone.

### 1. STANDING STONES (Tetris)
- **Inspiration:** Tetris
- **Geography:** Salisbury Plain, England
- **Culture:** Neolithic Britain, druidic stone circles
- **Cryptid/Boss:** The Green Man (nature spirit embedded in the stones)
- **Mechanic:** Stack falling stone blocks to complete rows. Each block is a real stone type (sarsen, bluestone, granite). Completing rows builds a stone circle. When the circle is complete, a portal opens.
- **Reward:** Preseli Blue Stone (the actual bluestone from Stonehenge)
- **Unlocks:** Faerie Kingdom portal (Stonehenge entrance)
- **Sound:** 84Hz — the Stone Circle Chamber frequency (the deepest tone in the Inn)
- **Difficulty:** Easy → ramps to challenging
- **Visual:** Stonehenge under twilight sky. Blocks are rough-hewn stones, not smooth Tetris pieces. Fog rolls across the plain.

### 2. TOMB TUNNEL (Dig Dug)
- **Inspiration:** Dig Dug
- **Geography:** Valley of the Kings, Egypt
- **Culture:** Ancient Egyptian funerary texts, the Duat (underworld)
- **Cryptid/Boss:** Ammit (the crocodile-lion-hippo demon that eats impure hearts)
- **Mechanic:** Dig through layers of Egyptian tomb strata. Collect buried treasures and artifacts. Avoid/collapse tunnels on tomb guardians (sha — tomb spirits). Reach the burial chamber at the bottom. Boss fight: Ammit guards the final chamber — collapse the right tunnels to trap Ammit.
- **Reward:** Lapis Lazuli (the stone of Egyptian royalty) + Scarab Charm
- **Unlocks:** Catacombs (the deep archive)
- **Sound:** 1475Hz (lapis frequency) + ancient Egyptian percussion (sistrum, frame drum)
- **Difficulty:** Medium — maze navigation + enemy avoidance
- **Visual:** Sandstone walls with hieroglyphics that glow when you dig past them. Torches flicker. Ammit's eyes glow in the dark ahead.

### 3. ANDEAN RUN (Temple Run)
- **Inspiration:** Temple Run
- **Geography:** Machu Picchu, Peru
- **Culture:** Inca mythology, Viracocha (creator god), Pachamama (earth mother)
- **Cryptid/Boss:** El Chullachaqui (shape-shifting forest spirit that lures travelers off the path)
- **Mechanic:** Endless runner down Inca temple steps. Swipe/dodge obstacles (stone traps, vine gaps, condor dives). Collect Inca gold and stones along the path. The path forks — El Chullachaqui appears at forks offering a "shortcut" that's always a trap. The correct path shows subtle Pachamama markers (flower glyphs).
- **Reward:** Andean Opal or Rhodochrosite (Inca's stone of love and heart)
- **Unlocks:** Campgrounds / External Zone (expeditions)
- **Sound:** Andean flute (quena) + panpipe (zampoña) at 99.5Hz (apatite frequency)
- **Difficulty:** Fast-paced — reaction time
- **Visual:** Lush green mountainside, mossy stone steps, mist. El Chullachaqui appears as a silhouette at forks, smiling, beckoning.

### 4. MONSTER OF THE DEEP (Nessie Simulation)
- **Inspiration:** Feed & Grow / stealth sim
- **Geography:** Loch Ness, Scotland
- **Culture:** Celtic water mythology, the Kelpie, each-uisge
- **Cryptid/Boss:** The Loch Ness Monster (Nessie) — YOU are Nessie
- **Mechanic:** You PLAY as the monster. Navigate the loch's dark depths. Hunt fish to grow. Avoid sonar pings from surface boats. Surface briefly to be "photographed" (bonus points) but risk detection. The deeper you go, the rarer the creatures. The loch connects to the Water Zone's 7-layer deep through a hidden passage.
- **Reward:** Scottish Marble or Serpentine (the stone of the Scottish highlands)
- **Unlocks:** Water Zone (the 7-layer deep)
- **Sound:** 42Hz drone (the water's frequency) + deep underwater ambient + sonar pings as gameplay tension
- **Difficulty:** Slow start → tense as you grow and detection risk increases
- **Visual:** Dark, murky water with bioluminescent particles. Sonar pings ripple through the water as visible waves. Surface glimpses show the Scottish highlands and Urquhart Castle.

## TIER 2: ZONE SIGNATURE GAMES (One Per Level)

Each zone has one signature game that embodies its unique mechanic and theme.

### 5. THE ORRERY (Cosmology — Level 1)
- **Inspiration:** Rubik's Cube / planetary puzzle
- **Geography:** The cosmos (the 42Hz source)
- **Culture:** Norse cosmology (Yggdrasil, the nine worlds)
- **Mechanic:** 3D solar system puzzle. Align zone spheres on orbital tracks so their frequencies harmonize (the Brísingamen chord: 42+170+158+84 Hz). Rotate orbits, swap sphere positions, find the harmonic alignment. Each sphere represents a zone of the Inn.
- **Reward:** Allfather's Gaze (perception upgrade — reveals hidden frequency maps)
- **Sound:** The full Brísingamen chord plays when solved correctly
- **Visual:** Brass orrery floating in starlight. Spheres glow at their zone frequencies.

### 6. ALL HANDS (Clockwork Skies — Level 2)
- **Inspiration:** Papers Please / resource management
- **Geography:** The Arcana airship (canopy level)
- **Culture:** Age of sail / clockworkpunk
- **Mechanic:** Crew management sim. The Arcana needs 8 stations crewed. Each crew member has one skill. Assign crew to handle crises (storms, sky pirates, gear failures) before they escalate. The 50 BPM heartbeat is the timer — every beat is one action turn.
- **Reward:** Sky Chart (unlocks new airship routes)
- **Sound:** 50 BPM heartbeat as the game timer
- **Visual:** Top-down view of the ship deck. Crew members as chibi figures running between stations. Storm clouds approaching on the horizon.

### 7. BEAD BOARD BATTLE (Main Floor — Level 3)
- **Inspiration:** Bejeweled / match-3
- **Geography:** The Inn's market floor
- **Culture:** Sonic mineral signature system
- **Mechanic:** Match-3 but with stones instead of gems. Each stone type plays its frequency when matched. Match 3+ same-frequency stones to build a chord. Build the target chord to win the round. Special: chord stones (dual-frequency) act as wildcards.
- **Reward:** Bead Board materials (new wood types, tray templates)
- **Sound:** Each match plays the stone's actual frequency. Completed chords play the full harmony.
- **Visual:** The Bead Board tray as the game grid. Stones click into dips when matched.

### 8. BASTET'S HERDING (Campgrounds — Level 4)
- **Inspiration:** Pac-Man / herding puzzle
- **Geography:** Ancient Egypt (Bubastis, the cat temple)
- **Culture:** Bastet (cat goddess), Egyptian temple life
- **Mechanic:** Guide diverse cat archetypes into a basket using a sistrum pulse (sound wave that gently pushes cats in a direction). Different cats respond to different frequencies. Match the cat's frequency to guide it. Avoid Anubis jackals that scatter the cats.
- **Reward:** Cat's Eye Chrysoberyl (the stone of feline vision)
- **Sound:** Sistrum rattle as the main mechanic sound + each cat type has a different purr frequency
- **Visual:** Egyptian temple courtyard. Cats as chibi figures with distinct personalities (sphinx, bastet, mau, tabby). Golden light.

### 9. THE ALCHEMIST'S SCALE (Staircase — Level 5)
- **Inspiration:** Balance puzzle / physics
- **Geography:** The Staircase (the alchemical transition)
- **Culture:** Alchemy (transmutation, the philosopher's stone)
- **Mechanic:** Balance a two-pan scale with mineral weights. Each mineral has a specific gravity. Place minerals on the pans to achieve equilibrium. The target weight changes each round (representing different alchemical formulas). Higher rounds require combining minerals (e.g., copper + silver + gold = the Blacksmith's Chord).
- **Reward:** Alchemical charm (element charm from the ElementCharm entity)
- **Sound:** The scale plays the frequency of whatever mineral is placed on it. Balance = harmonic chord.
- **Visual:** Ancient brass scale on a stone table. Mineral specimens glow when placed. Alchemical symbols appear as the formula progresses.

### 10. THE DEEP READ (Catacombs — Level 6)
- **Inspiration:** Typing game / research sim
- **Geography:** The Crone's archive (the deep library)
- **Culture:** Medieval manuscript culture, monastic scholarship
- **Mechanic:** A reading/research game. Fragments of ancient texts scroll across the screen (in the style of their original script — hieroglyphics, runes, ogham, cuneiform). The player must identify and select the correct fragments that form a complete passage. Speed and accuracy build the "reading score." Wrong selections corrupt the text (and the Crone disapproves).
- **Reward:** Book entity record (unlocks a real archive.org text in the library)
- **Sound:** Quill scratching on parchment + the ambient 42Hz hum of the archive
- **Visual:** Towering bookshelves. An ancient desk with a quill. Text fragments float in amber light.

### 11. MARKET DAY (Campgrounds — Level 4)
- **Inspiration:** Recettear / trading sim
- **Geography:** The Renaissance Faire campgrounds
- **Culture:** Medieval trade, faire economics
- **Mechanic:** Buy stones from traveling merchants, sell finished bead designs to faire-goers. Prices fluctuate based on weather, events, and demand. Each merchant has a personality — Jay haggles, Solstara gives fair prices, the Blacksmith only trades metal components.
- **Reward:** Coins (in-game currency) + rare stones from traveling merchants
- **Sound:** Ren Faire ambient — lutes, crowd murmur, Jay's laughter
- **Visual:** Market stalls in golden daylight. Merchants behind their booths. Price tags update in real-time.

### 12. WOLF'S HUNT (Campgrounds — Level 4)
- **Inspiration:** Duck Hunt / tracking game
- **Geography:** The forest outside the Campgrounds
- **Culture:** Wolf mythology across cultures (Fenrir, Romulus & Remus, the she-wolf)
- **Mechanic:** Track a creature through the forest by following its frequency trail. Each creature leaves a sonic signature. Match the frequency on your stone detector to follow the trail. Get close enough to "tag" the creature with a charm. Different creatures appear at different times of day.
- **Reward:** Wolf charm (QuestCharm entity) + tracking stones
- **Sound:** The detector plays the creature's frequency — louder = closer
- **Visual:** Dark forest with frequency trails as visible sound waves. Jay's wolf companion helps sniff out trails.

### 13. STONE CIRCLE RESONANCE (Faerie Kingdom — Level 7)
- **Inspiration:** Simon Says / rhythm memory
- **Geography:** The Stone Circle Chamber (the deepest point of the Fae realm)
- **Culture:** Neolithic resonance, the 84Hz octave
- **Mechanic:** A sequence of stones light up and play their frequencies. Repeat the sequence. Each round adds one more stone to the sequence. The stones are the four portal stones (Stonehenge, Avebury, Callanish, Ring of Brodgar). At higher levels, the sequence includes chord stones (dual frequencies). Complete 12 rounds to activate the Stone Circle Chamber's full resonance.
- **Reward:** Fae reputation boost + rare Fae stones (Labradorite, Fluorite, Rainbow Moonstone)
- **Sound:** 84Hz (the octave — the deepest tone in the Inn) + each stone's individual frequency
- **Visual:** Four ancient stones in a circle, glowing with Fae light. The deeper the round, the more the chamber transforms.

### 14. LIGHTHOUSE VIGIL (Water Zone — Level 8)
- **Inspiration:** Rhythm Heaven / three-tempo game
- **Geography:** The Lighthouse at the Water Zone's edge
- **Culture:** Lighthouse mythology, the Lighthouse Keeper
- **Mechanic:** Three rhythm tracks at three tempos: surface (calm 60 BPM), mid (rhythmic 50 BPM), deep (still 30 BPM). The player switches between layers, maintaining the beat on each. When all three layers sync, the Lighthouse beam strengthens and reveals hidden creatures. Miss a beat and the beam dims.
- **Reward:** Water monster charm + deep-zone access
- **Sound:** The three-tempo system — the player IS the Lighthouse Keeper
- **Visual:** The lighthouse beam sweeping through progressively darker water. Creatures appear in the beam when all three tempos sync.

### 15. THE INN SONG (Undercity — Level 9)
- **Inspiration:** Music composition / final achievement
- **Geography:** The Undercity (the Klimt gold depths)
- **Culture:** The Inn's complete sonic architecture
- **Mechanic:** The ultimate crafting achievement. Arrange all 9 zone frequencies into a single composition. The composition is visual — you place zone stones on a master bench in the correct order and timing. When played, the Inn Song fills the Undercity with the Brísingamen chord + the Blacksmith's chord + the Gargoyle chord + all zone frequencies simultaneously. This is the Inn's complete arc: refuge → home → world → self → cosmos.
- **Reward:** The Inn Song (ultimate achievement) + the Soulstone (165th stone, from Mars)
- **Sound:** The Inn Song — all 9 zones' representative frequencies in one composition
- **Visual:** The Master's Bench in Klimt gold. Stones placed in a spiral pattern. The Undercity's gold ceilings pulse with the composition.

## TIER 3: CULTURAL MINI-GAMES (World Map — Region-Specific)

These are short (2-5 minute) games played from the world map by clicking a lore pin. Each draws on a specific country's mythology from the 191-country lore database. The games use shared templates, themed for each region.

### GAME TEMPLATES (10 templates → mapped to 191 countries)

#### Template A: CRYPTID CHASE (Endless Runner variant)
- **Mechanic:** Run/chase through a cultural landscape, dodging the region's mythological creature
- **Boss:** The region's signature cryptid
- **Used for:** Peru (Chullachaqui), Japan (Tengu), Greece (Minotaur), Norway (Troll)

#### Template B: TOMB DIG (Maze/Dig variant)
- **Mechanic:** Dig through cultural ruins, collect artifacts, avoid tomb guardians
- **Boss:** The culture's guardian of the dead
- **Used for:** Egypt (Ammit), Mexico (Camazotz), China (Terracotta guardians), Iraq (Lamassu)

#### Template C: WATER CREATURE (Stealth/Sim variant)
- **Mechanic:** Navigate waters, encounter the region's water monster
- **Boss:** The region's water cryptid
- **Used for:** Scotland (Nessie), Japan (Umibōzu), Scandinavia (Kraken), Australia (Bunyip)

#### Template D: RIDDLE OF THE GODS (Puzzle/Riddle variant)
- **Mechanic:** Solve cultural riddles posed by the region's deities
- **Boss:** The region's trickster god
- **Used for:** West Africa (Anansi), Norse (Loki), Greece (Sphinx), Celtic (Banshee)

#### Template E: STONE MATCH (Match-3 variant)
- **Mechanic:** Match regional stones, learn their cultural significance
- **Boss:** None — peaceful game
- **Used for:** Regions with strong mineral traditions (Brazil, Madagascar, Afghanistan, Myanmar)

#### Template F: SPIRIT HERDING (Herding variant)
- **Mechanic:** Guide cultural spirit animals to their resting places
- **Boss:** The spirit that scatters them
- **Used for:** Egypt (Bastet's cats), Japan (Kitsune), Native American (Thunderbird)

#### Template G: FREQUENCY TUNING (Rhythm variant)
- **Mechanic:** Tune stones to cultural frequencies, build regional chords
- **Boss:** The dissonance (the un-tuned frequency)
- **Used for:** All regions — the universal game, themed per culture

#### Template H: SHADOW HUNT (Hidden Object variant)
- **Mechanic:** Find hidden mythological creatures in a cultural landscape
- **Boss:** The creature that finds YOU if you take too long
- **Used for:** Transylvania (vampires), Haiti (Loa), Slavic (Rusalka)

#### Template I: BRIDGE OF SOULS (Puzzle/Bridge building variant)
- **Mechanic:** Build a bridge to the afterlife using cultural materials
- **Boss:** The ferryman who tests your worthiness
- **Used for:** Greece (Charon), Norse (Gjallarbrú), Japan (Sanzu River), Egypt (Duat passage)

#### Template J: TOTEM BUILD (Stacking/Totem variant)
- **Mechanic:** Stack cultural totems in the correct order (animal spirits)
- **Boss:** The spirit that topples totems
- **Used for:** Pacific Northwest (Raven/Orc/Bear), Africa (Anansi/Hyena/Elephant), Aboriginal (Kangaroo/Emu/Goanna)

## REWARD SYSTEM

### Per Game
- **Stones:** Each game rewards 1-3 stones from its geographic region
- **Charms:** Rare games reward ElementCharms or QuestCharms
- **Lore:** Completing a game unlocks that region's full lore entry
- **Currency:** Coins for the Market Day trading sim
- **Access:** Tier 1 games unlock zones; Tier 3 games unlock new map regions

### Progression
- **World Map:** Each country starts "fogged." Playing its mini-game reveals the full lore entry and stone data.
- **Zone Unlocks:** Tier 1 games unlock the 4 main zones (Faerie, Catacombs, Campgrounds, Water)
- **Reputation:** Tier 2 games build reputation within their zone (Fae reputation, archive standing, etc.)
- **The Inn Song:** Only achievable after all 9 zone signature games are completed

## TECHNICAL NOTES (For Base44 App Builder)

### What can be built in Base44:
- HTML5 Canvas games (Tetris, Dig Dug, endless runner, match-3)
- DOM-based games (rhythm, puzzle, hidden object, riddle)
- React component games (management sim, trading sim, composition)
- All games connect to Stone entity (rewards) and PlayerProgress entity (tracking)

### Game page structure:
1. Start screen with cultural context (region name, lore snippet, cryptid description)
2. Game canvas/component
3. Results screen with rewards (stone earned, charm earned, lore unlocked)
4. "Play Again" and "Return to Map" buttons

### Sound integration:
- Each game uses the Web Audio API to play stone frequencies
- Background music is region-matched (from the music direction document)
- Sound effects are stone frequencies (matching, collecting, rewarding)
