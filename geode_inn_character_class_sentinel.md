## THE SENTINEL

**Inspired by:** River — former Golden Gloves champion, INFJ, partner to Krista and Charles.

---

### Stat Profile

- **Dexterity: HIGH** — Boxing footwork, hand speed, precision strikes. Not brute force — timing.
- **Intelligence: HIGH** — Reads people instantly. Analyzes, inspects, categorizes, and defines before speaking.
- **Perception: HIGH** — Sees what others miss. The first to notice a threat, a lie, or a hidden door.
- **Intimidation: HIGH (passive)** — Not charisma. The opposite. Where the Bard *draws people in*, the Sentinel *holds them at distance* — not by choice, but by presence. The boxing stance is in the bones.
- **Approachability: LOW** — The intimidation is instinctive, not performed. She doesn't mean to scare people. She just does.
- **Defense: HIGH (faceted)** — Multiple layers, each one a different defense. You don't break through them. You earn passage through them.
- **Endurance: MODERATE** — Can sustain in a fight. Can't sustain the walls indefinitely. The layers cost energy to maintain.
- **Charisma: LOW (surface) / HIDDEN (core)** — Surface charisma reads as intensity, not warmth. But the core — if you reach it — is the warmest thing in the room.

### Special Traits

**Quick Read:**
- The Sentinel analyzes any NPC on first encounter, revealing their disposition, hidden stats, and secret motivations before dialogue begins.
- In tabletop terms: she rolls perception before anyone else acts.
- In the Inn: NPC dialogue trees open differently for the Sentinel — she sees options others don't because she's already read the person.
- This is the *Golden Gloves eye contact* translated to game mechanics: she knows what you're going to do before you do it.

**Faceted Defense:**
- The Sentinel has layered defenses — each layer must be engaged separately.
- In combat: each layer absorbs one hit before cracking. Multiple layers mean multiple hits before the real Sentinel is reached.
- In social encounters: NPCs must pass multiple interaction checks to gain the Sentinel's trust. Most fail at the first layer (intimidation). Those who persist reach deeper layers.
- In party dynamics: party members who haven't earned past the first layer see only the boxer. Those who break through see the soul.

**The Cosmic Riddle (Hidden Trait):**
- At her core, the Sentinel knows the answer to the cosmic riddle: *love, loyalty, knowledge.*
- This is not a stat. It's a *narrative mechanic.*
- When the Sentinel encounters a cosmic gate, a Remembrance Vision, or the Owl's deepest questions, she can bypass the puzzle by answering from her core.
- The trait activates only when the player *chooses* to reveal it — the Sentinel's layers must be voluntarily lowered.
- In the narrative spine: River is the character who already knows what the cosmology is trying to teach. She doesn't need to reach Jupiter to understand the cosmic language. She already speaks it.

**Iron Stance:**
- The boxing posture is permanent. The Sentinel cannot be knocked down by standard attacks — only by attacks that specifically target endurance or emotional vulnerability.
- In combat: immune to knockback and stagger effects.
- In social encounters: immune to the Bard's taunt *at the surface layer* — she reads the taunt as a tactic, not a threat. (The Bard can still reach her if he gets past the layers — but that requires genuine connection, not performance.)

### Playstyle

The Sentinel is the **gatekeeper class** — the one who reads the room before entering, who sees through deception, who stands between the party and danger. She is not the damage dealer (that's the Bard, enraged). She is not the puzzle solver (that's the Tinker). She is the **one who knows who to trust, when to fight, and what it's all for.**

In combat: precision strikes, not brute force. Boxing logic — dodge, read, counter. High dexterity means she avoids damage rather than tanking it. The Iron Stance means she can't be knocked around, but sustained pressure wears her down (moderate endurance).

In social encounters: the Quick Read means she starts every conversation with an advantage. She already knows what the NPC wants. But her low approachability means the NPC starts afraid, and fear is not trust. The Sentinel's social game is about *converting intimidation into respect* — a slower path than the Bard's charisma burst, but more durable when it succeeds.

In the archive: high intelligence and perception mean she sees connections between texts that others miss. She doesn't read the most entries (that's the Crone) — she reads the *right* entries. The Sentinel finds the thread that connects three seemingly unrelated texts across three different cultural zones.

### The Sentinel and the Narrative Spine

River's class has a unique connection to the Inn's cosmology. The narrative spine asks cosmic questions: *Why are we here? What happens when we merge with our creators? What is the weight of a life?*

The Owl asks these questions. The Sentinel already knows the answers.

*Love. Loyalty. Knowledge.*

This makes the Sentinel the **narratively complete class** — the one whose personal journey aligns with the Inn's cosmic journey without needing to climb the stairs to the cosmology level. She doesn't need to reach Pluto's gate to understand what's behind it. She's already understood it. The question for the Sentinel isn't *what's the answer* — it's *how do I live the answer in a world that doesn't see it?*

That's the INFJ experience. Knowing the answer and being unable to communicate it because the layers — the analysis, the intensity, the stance — get in the way.

The Sentinel's character arc in the Inn is not about learning the cosmic truth. It's about *lowering the layers so others can see it too.*

### The Sixth Class

Five classes were drawn from the family Krista was born into. The sixth was chosen.

The Artisan Thief makes things she can't sell.
The Bard Blowhard makes everyone louder.
The Tinkering Weeaboo makes solutions no one asked for.
The Librarian Crone makes knowledge into power.
The Chameleon Druid makes himself into whatever is needed.

The Sentinel makes you feel seen. Whether you wanted to be or not.

### AUTHOR'S NOTE

River's class was written from Krista's description. Every stat is an observation, not an assumption. The Golden Gloves posture is real. The eye contact is real. The layers are real. The answer — love, loyalty, knowledge — is real.

The Sentinel's design philosophy is the opposite of every other class. The other classes are defined by what they *do.* The Sentinel is defined by what she *knows* — and by the distance between knowing and being able to share it.

That's the INFJ condition. And it's why there are two INFJs in the party: the Thief who can't sell her work, and the Sentinel who can't show her soul. Same architecture. Different walls.

The Inn holds room for both.
