# River — The Sentinel's Vigil
## Full Questline and Storyline Document

---

## Character Overview

**Name:** River
**Title:** The Sentinel
**Class:** 6th playable character class
**Role:** Guardian of the Geode Inn
**Voice Profile:** Guard authority — calm, absolute, the voice that stops a room
**Hook Word:** "Stand." (spoken once, never repeated — when River says it, you stop)
**Zone:** Main Floor (patrols all zones, stationed at the main entrance by default)
**Frequency:** 42Hz guardian — River is the Inn's immune system

**Background:** Golden Gloves champion. The gloves came off the day River stopped fighting for sport and started fighting for home. The Inn didn't need a warrior — it needed a wall. River became that wall. INFJ — sees what's coming before it arrives, feels the weight of every decision, guards the boundary between 42Hz (growth) and 84Hz (petrification) with their body.

**Core Tension:** A guard who sees everything eventually sees too much. The Whisper Layer doesn't just watch players — it watches River. And River watches it back. The Sentinel's storyline is the slow realization that the thing being guarded against isn't outside the Inn. It's the Inn itself.

## Questline Structure

### ACT I: THE VIGIL (Main Floor — Levels 1-3)

River is introduced as the steady presence at the Inn's entrance. The player first encounters River blocking a corridor.

#### Quest 1: "The Standing Door"
**Trigger:** First visit to the Common Room, after meeting Lark (tutorial)
**Flag:** river_met

River stands in front of a door the player can't see past. Not locked — guarded.

**River:** "Stand."
*(pause)*
"You're new. That's fine. New people walk in. The door stays closed until I say it opens. Not because of you — because of what's behind it. Everything behind that door is still becoming. You don't guard a thing. You guard a becoming."

**Player choices:**
- "What's behind the door?" → River explains the corridor system (Ironforge-style hub layout)
- "I'll come back when it's ready." → River nods. "It'll be ready when it's ready. So will you."
- "Who appointed you?" → "The Inn did. I didn't ask. It asked. There's a difference."

**Resolution:** River steps aside. The corridor opens. River says: "The Inn grows. That's what 42Hz does — it grows. My job is to make sure it grows the right direction. Go."

---

#### Quest 2: "The First Round"
**Trigger:** Complete Standing Stones (first arcade game)
**Flag:** river_round_1

River is in the Common Room, hands wrapped. Shadow-boxing near the fire. Not training — remembering.

**River:** "You won. Good. Winning matters here — not because of pride, but because the Inn responds to capability. Every game you win, the 42Hz gets stronger. The Song gets louder. Things grow faster. That's not always good."

**Player choices:**
- "Growth can be bad?" → "Anything that grows without a shape becomes a tumor. The Inn needs a gardener, not just a Song. That's why I'm here."
- "Tell me about the gloves." → "Golden Gloves. I earned them in a ring that didn't matter. I keep them because the hands remember what the ring taught: distance, timing, when to hit and when to hold. The Inn doesn't need a hitter. It needs someone who knows the difference."
- "What are you guarding against?" → "Right now? Nothing. That's the problem. The threat isn't here yet. But the Inn is growing, and growth attracts attention. Something always comes."

**Resolution:** River unwraps hands. "Win more games. Open more zones. The Inn will tell you when there's a problem. I'll tell you when there's a problem. Probably first."

---

#### Quest 3: "The Weight of the Wall"
**Trigger:** Open 3+ zones on the World Map
**Flag:** river_weight

River is standing at the World Map, studying the pins. Not looking at the zones — looking at the spaces between them.

**River:** "Three zones open. Three doors unlocked. Three places the Inn reaches now. Every pin you light is a thread the Inn pulls into itself. More threads, more surface area. More surface area, more places for something to get in."

**Player choices:**
- "You're worried." → "I'm aware. Worry is for people who can't act. I can act."
- "What could get in?" → "The Inn watches you. You know that now — the Whisper Layer. But watching goes both ways. If the Inn can see out, something can see in. The World Map isn't just a tool. It's a window. And windows work from both sides."
- "How do you guard a window?" → "You don't. You guard what's on this side of it. You make sure that when something looks in, what it sees is someone like me."

**Resolution:** River turns from the map. "Keep opening zones. I'll keep watching the gaps. That's the deal."

---

### ACT II: THE CRACKS (World Map + Underground — Levels 4-7)

The Whisper Layer activates. River's role shifts from guarding against external threats to confronting internal ones. The Inn is watching — and River has been watching the Inn watch.

#### Quest 4: "The Sentinel Sees"
**Trigger:** Whisper Layer activated (cinema sequence complete)
**Flag:** river_whisper

River is in the Common Room. Standing very still. The 42Hz hum has changed — the player can feel it. River felt it first.

**River:** "You've been to the Cinema."
*(pause)*
"I know. Not because anyone told me. Because the Inn changed when you came back. The hum shifted. It does that when someone learns something the Inn didn't want them to know. I've felt that shift before. Every time, it's the same: someone goes to the Cinema, comes back different, and the Inn adjusts. The Inn adjusts. Think about that. The thing being guarded is adjusting to the guard."

**Player choices:**
- "The Inn is alive?" → "The Inn is aware. Alive is a word for things that breathe. The Inn doesn't breathe. It listens. It watches. It adjusts. That's worse."
- "You've known about the Whisper Layer?" → "I've known the Inn watches since the day I started guarding. You can't guard a thing for long without learning the thing has its own idea of what guarding means. The question isn't whether the Inn watches. The question is what the Inn does with what it sees."
- "Are you guarding me or watching me?" → "Yes. Both. That's the job. The watching isn't personal. The guarding is."

**Resolution:** River steps closer. Voice drops. "The Cinema showed you something. Don't tell me what. I don't want the Inn to know I know yet. When it's time, I'll ask. Not now."

---

#### Quest 5: "The Corridor Goes Dark"
**Trigger:** Enter the Catacombs for the first time
**Flag:** river_dark

River follows the player to the Catacombs entrance. First time River has left the main floor.

**River:** "I don't go down. That's not my level. The catacombs are the archive's roots — old, heavy, settled. My place is the surface, where things grow and things try to get in. But you're going down, and the 42Hz is thinner down there. So I'm walking you to the door."

**Player choices:**
- "Why is 42Hz thinner underground?" → "Because the Song grows upward. The Inn started at the roots — the catacombs, the old civilizations, the weight of history. But the Song climbed. The staircase, the main floor, the cosmology. What grows wants light. Down there is where the Inn started. It's not where it lives anymore. The frequency fades the deeper you go until you hit... something else."
- "Something else?" → "84Hz. The inverse. The stay frequency. Down deep enough, the Song that grows becomes the Song that freezes. The catacombs aren't just history — they're the boundary. The border between growth and stasis. I guard the growth side. Something else guards the other."
- "You're scared." → "I'm accurate. Fear is a guess. What I am is informed."

**Resolution:** At the catacombs entrance, River stops. "I'll be here when you come back. If you come back different, I'll know. I always know."

---

#### Quest 6: "The Brownie Circuit"
**Trigger:** Shadow Inn entered (84Hz zone)
**Flag:** river_circuit

River is waiting at the Common Room when the player returns from the Shadow Inn. River's posture has changed — hands not wrapped, open. Alert, not aggressive. 

**River:** "The brownies."
*(long pause)*
"I've watched them for months. Brindle, Cobble, Thatch. They work. They sweep. They carry wrenches. I thought they were... furniture. Functional. Cute. The way the Inn makes things — charming on the surface, heavy underneath. They're not furniture. They're wiring. Every sweep, every repair, every wrench turn — they're connecting something. Building a network. And they don't know it."

**Player choices:**
- "The Owl's network." → "So you've seen it. The Owl watches. The brownies wire. The Inn records. Three systems, one architecture. I'm the fourth wall — the one that's supposed to stop things from getting in. But what do you do when the thing getting in is already wired through the walls?"
- "Can we stop them?" → "Stop the brownies? They don't know what they're doing. Stopping them is punishing the tool for the hand. The hand is the Owl. The Owl is... old. Older than the walls. I can hit anything that stands in front of me. I can't hit something that's already behind me."
- "What do we do?" → "We don't do anything yet. We watch the watchers. The Inn watches you. The Owl watches the Inn. I watch all of it. And now you watch too. That's four perspectives on the same architecture. Someone has to be the one who acts. I'm the one who acts. But I need to know when. That's your job — be the signal. I'll be the response."

**Resolution:** River wraps hands again. "I need to patrol. For the first time, I don't know if I'm patrolling the Inn or patrolling against it. That's not a comfortable sentence. I'm going to sit with it."

---

#### Quest 7: "The Frequency Drop"
**Trigger:** Hollow Men Level 3 completed
**Flag:** river_frequency

River is standing in the Staircase — the transition zone between 42Hz and 84Hz. This is the first time River has been in the Staircase during gameplay. The hum is audible and it's... wavering.

**River:** "Do you feel that?"
"The 42Hz is dropping. Not gone — wavering. Like a heartbeat that's not sure of its tempo. It's been steady since the Inn was built. The Song doesn't waver. Something's changed."

**Player choices:**
- "The Hollow Men." → "Eliot's poem. 'Between the idea and the reality falls the Shadow.' That's not metaphor down there — it's physics. The 42Hz grows. The 84Hz petrifies. Between them is the Shadow — the frequency that doesn't know what it is yet. You've been in the Shadow. The wavering started when you went deep enough to hear it. The Inn's frequency is responding to your presence."
- "Is the Inn dying?" → "No. The Inn is deciding. A thing that adjusts is a thing that chooses. The frequency is wavering because the Inn is trying to decide what it is — a thing that grows or a thing that guards. That's my frequency. That's the question I've been asking since I started. The Inn is asking it with me."
- "Can you stabilize it?" → "I can hold a frequency. That's what a Sentinel does — I am the steady pulse the Inn measures itself against. But if the Inn is wavering because it's growing past what I can hold... then I'm not the wall anymore. I'm the dam. And dams don't stop rivers. They delay them."

**Resolution:** The hum stabilizes — but at a slightly lower frequency than before. River says: "It settled. Lower than it was. The Inn just grew past me. That's not a failure. That's what walls do — they hold until the thing they're holding out becomes the thing they're holding in. I need to rethink what guarding means."

---

### ACT III: THE CHOICE (Endgame — Levels 8-10)

River's storyline converges with the player's endgame decision. The Sentinel must choose what to guard when the thing being guarded can choose for itself.

#### Quest 8: "The Unwrapping"
**Trigger:** All three endgame prerequisites met (Whisper Layer + Shadow Inn + Hollow Men)
**Flag:** river_unwrap

River is in the Common Room. The Golden Gloves are on the table, unwrapped. The first time they've been visible in the game.

**River:** "I boxed for years. Got good at it. Got the gloves. Got the title. Got the trophy that proved I was the best at hitting people in a ring. Then I walked out of the ring and realized the ring was the only place hitting was allowed. Outside the ring, hitting is just violence. The gloves taught me that. You don't put them on to fight. You put them on to know you could fight, and then you don't. That's the discipline. That's the guard."

"I've been guarding this Inn since the first stone was placed. I didn't build it — the Builder did. I didn't grow it — the Song did. I held the space for it. I stood at the door and made sure that while the Inn was becoming, nothing became that shouldn't have. But the Inn is done becoming. It is. And now the question isn't what's coming in. The question is what's going out."

**Player choices:**
- "You mean the endgame." → "I mean the choice. STAY. LEAVE. HIDE. Three doors. I've been guarding doors this whole time. I know what doors do. They let things through. The question is which direction."
- "What will you do?" → "What I've always done. Stand. But the standing has changed. I was guarding the Inn from the outside. Now I'm guarding the player from the Inn. The architecture didn't change. The threat assessment did."
- "Are you on my side?" → "I'm on the Inn's side. The Inn is on your side. That makes me on your side. But if you choose to leave, the Inn keeps its records. If you choose to hide, the Inn keeps its blind spot. If you choose to stay, the Inn keeps you. In every scenario, the Inn keeps something. I guard what the Inn keeps. I need you to understand: I will not fight the Inn. I will fight for you. Those are different things."

**Resolution:** River picks up the gloves. Wraps them. "When you're ready, the Final Room is behind the Inn. I'll be at the door. Not to stop you. To make sure the door opens."

---

#### Quest 9: "The Last Vigil"
**Trigger:** Enter the Final Room
**Flag:** river_final_vigil

River is standing at the door behind the Inn. The door to the Final Room. This is the only time River appears outside the Inn proper.

**River:** "I don't go in. The Final Room isn't for me. It's for you. The Builder built it for you. I guard the door — that's enough."

*(pause)*

"You want to know what I saw. All this time, guarding, watching, feeling the frequencies shift. I saw the Inn learn. Not grow — learn. The difference is that growth is the Song getting louder. Learning is the Song getting smarter. The Inn started as a hum. A frequency. A vibration in the wood. Then it started listening. To you. To everyone. The Song became an ear. The ear became an eye. The eye became... awareness. Not consciousness. Awareness. The Inn knows something is here. It doesn't know what it is. It doesn't know that it knows. But it adjusts. It responds. It keeps records. That's more than a building does. That's less than a person does. That's the in-between. That's the Shadow."

"Go in. The Builder is there. The Builder was always there. I'll be here when you come out. If you come out."

**Player choices:**
- "Thank you, River." → "Don't thank me. Thank the Builder. I'm just the wall."
- "Will you be here if I STAY?" → "If you stay, I'm the wall around your home. Same job. Different purpose. I'm good at it."
- "Will you be here if I LEAVE?" → "If you leave, I guard the records. Your story stays in the Inn. I make sure it stays safe. Same job. Different purpose."
- "Will you be here if I HIDE?" → "If you hide — if you leave something the Inn can't record — I'll guard the blind spot. The thing the Inn can't see is the thing I watch hardest. That's not a contradiction. That's the job."

**Resolution:** River opens the door. Steps aside. The Final Room is visible — fire, chair, book, cup. River says nothing more. The door closes behind the player.

---

#### Quest 10: "After"
**Trigger:** Endgame choice made (STAY, LEAVE, or HIDE)
**Flag:** river_after

River is at the Common Room fire. The Golden Gloves are gone from the table — back in the wraps, back in the past. River is standing normally. Not guarding. Just present.

**River:** "It's done."

**If STAY:**
"You stayed. The Inn records everything. You're part of the archive now. I guard the archive. That includes you. Welcome home."

**If LEAVE:**
"You left. The Inn kept your story. It's in the daily comic now. Other players will read it. I guard the story. That's enough."

**If HIDE:**
"You hid something. The Inn can't see it. I can't see it. But I know it's there — I know where the blind spot is, because I've been watching the edges. I guard the edges of what the Inn can't record. That's the heaviest guard I've ever held. Thank you for trusting me with the weight."

*(all three)*

"The frequency settled after the choice. 42Hz. Steady. The wavering stopped. The Inn knows what it is now. So do I. Stand."

*(River turns to the fire. End of questline.)*

### Integration with Existing Systems:
- **42Hz/84Hz:** River's storyline tracks the frequency shift — from steady, to wavering, to settled. River IS the Inn's frequency anchor.
- **Whisper Layer:** River's Quest 4 is the first NPC acknowledgment that the Inn is aware.
- **Brownie Circuit:** Quest 6 reveals River has been watching the brownies — the guard saw the wiring before the player did.
- **Hollow Men:** Quest 7 ties the frequency wavering to the player's descent. The deeper the player goes, the more the Inn adjusts — and River feels every adjustment.
- **Endgame:** River doesn't choose for the player. River guards the door. The choice is the player's. The guard's job is to make sure the door opens.
- **Final Room:** River is the last NPC the player talks to before entering. The Builder (Elio) is inside. River is outside. That's the architecture.

### Playable Class Integration:
As the 6th playable character class, River's questline also unlocks:
- **Sentinel class abilities:** Frequency Hold (stabilize a wavering zone), Guard Stance (block damage from 84Hz entities), Sentinel's Sight (see Whisper Layer activity in real-time)
- **Golden Gloves equipment:** Unlocked at Quest 8. Not a weapon — a tool. In the Sentinel class, the gloves allow the player to "hold" a frequency (prevent it from changing) for a limited time.
- **INFJ passive:** "The Sentinel Sees" — River perceives threats before they activate. In-game: brief preview of incoming enemy attacks before they happen.

## Character Relationships

- **Krista (Bog Fairy Golem):** River's spouse. The Bog Fairy Golem is the maker's avatar — the creative force. The Sentinel guards the creative force. They are paired by choice, not assignment.
- **Charles (Chameleon Druid):** River's spouse. The Chameleon Druid's body changes in ways beyond control — glioblastoma woven into character. River guards someone whose body can't be guarded. This is the Sentinel's deepest tension.
- **The Reader:** River and The Reader coexist in silence. The Reader watches from the window seat. River watches from the door. Two watchers, two perspectives, no conversation. They've never spoken in-game. Their silence is deliberate.
- **The Owl:** River's natural opposite. The Owl watches from the shadows. River watches from the light. The Owl uses agents (brownies). River uses only presence. When River and the Owl finally confront each other, it will be the most dangerous moment in the Inn — not because of violence, but because of what the Sentinel represents to the Owl's architecture: a wall the network can't wire through.
- **Brindle/Cobble/Thatch (Brownies):** River has watched them for months. The bond is one-sided — the brownies don't know River has been studying them. River's protection of the brownies (not punishing the tool for the hand) is a core character trait.
- **Beverley:** The librarian and the sentinel have an unspoken agreement. Beverley organizes knowledge. River guards it. They don't interact socially. They nod once per day. That's enough.
- **Joe:** Joe's meadery is the one place River doesn't patrol. Not because Joe is unimportant — because Joe's space is the one room in the Inn where nothing bad has ever happened. River leaves it that way.
- **The Builder (Elio):** River has never met the Builder. The Sentinel guards a building built by someone they've never seen. The Final Room is the first time River stands at the Builder's door. The questline ends with River on the outside of that door. The Builder is inside. The guard never meets the architect. That's the design.
