## 1. geode_inn_cosmic_layer_space_system.md — The Cosmic Layer (Space System)

### Core Systems & Lore Synthesis
The Cosmic Layer represents the Inn's highest system, situated beyond Level 1 Cosmology in the celestial void between stars. It establishes the foundational frequency chain of the Geode Inn universe: **Galaxy (42Hz) → Celestial Water → Earth Stones → Inn Tree → Player**. The 42Hz Song is revealed not as an invention of the Inn, but as the fundamental resonant frequency of the galaxy itself, for which the Inn acts as an earthbound antenna.

Access to this system requires absolute mastery across the Inn:
- **The Allfather's Gaze** (Level 1 Cosmology completion)
- **The Inn Song** (Level 9 Undercity ultimate crafting achievement)
- **The Octave Key** (Level 7 Faerie Kingdom harmonic understanding that 42Hz and 84Hz are one note)
- **The Source's Secret** (Level 8 Water Zone realization of living water frequency)
- **The Wanderer's Rune Set** (Level 3 Main Floor rune assembly spelling GUEST)

The Cosmic Layer comprises five major celestial destinations:
1. **The Sun ("The First Voice"):** The solar core radiating the fundamental 42Hz drone; site of the "First Note" quest.
2. **The Jupiter System ("The Giant's Breath"):** Four swirling cloud tiers (Ammonia, Sulfur, Water, Hydrogen); site of the "Galaxy Word" quest and galaxy language translation.
3. **Mars ("The Iron Prison"):** The memory desert, Arena of Iron, Prison of Furies, and Reincarnation Gate; site of the "Soulstone" quest.
4. **Pluto's Gate ("The Threshold of Void"):** The absolute boundary of space; pure zero-point silence where the player recognizes that 42Hz and 84Hz are octave reflections.
5. **The Galaxy Core ("The Spiral Gallery"):** The 42Hz birthplace and chronological cosmic art gallery where all galactic history is stored in spiral nebulae.

### 1. Shadow Inn State (84Hz)
In the Shadow Inn (84Hz octave), the Cosmic Layer undergoes a violent biomechanical transformation:
- **Coronal Black Flares:** The Sun's warm golden radiance shifts into jagged, obsidian solar flares emitting high-frequency radiation.
- **Crushing Iron Belts:** Jupiter's gaseous clouds compress into solid, serrated iron bands that rotate with crushing gravitational force.
- **The Obsidian Arena:** Mars transforms into a brutal Royo-style gladiatorial ring of polished black chrome and iron spikes, where failed souls are forged into hollow husks.
- **The Event Horizon:** Pluto's Gate becomes an event horizon of roaring acoustic silence that strips unshielded players of their memories.
- **The Anti-Harmonic Whirlpool:** The Galaxy Core spins in counter-rotation, generating destructive anti-harmonics that threaten to unmake reality.

### 2. Endgame Connections
- **The Fabergé Egg:** The Sun's core provides the geometric star-pattern etched onto the Egg's golden outer casing, while the Mars Soulstone forms the Egg's glowing central yolk.
- **The Song of Making:** The Galaxy Core serves as the master acoustic substrate for the Song of Making. All nine zone frequencies must resolve into the galactic 42Hz fundamental to complete the melody.
- **The Convergence:** During Convergence, the Cosmic Map collapses inward, merging space with the Inn's central hearth. The Inn Tree transforms into the cosmic axis (Yggdrasil), connecting the earth directly to the galaxy core.

### 3. Sound Profile
- **Living Fundamental (42Hz):** Deep, resonant solar hum layered with ethereal cosmic choir and warm sub-bass pulse.
- **Shadow Harmonic (84Hz):** Harsh, metallic resonance featuring clashing iron beams, solar flare crackles, and screeching high-octave synth drones.
- **In-Between / Harmonics:** Zero-point vacuum silences interrupted by 8Hz theta binaural beats that pulse between 42Hz and 50Hz planetary frequencies.
- **Instrumentation:** Low organ drones, bowed brass, celestial glass harmonica, metallic friction chimes.

### 4. Cross-System Integration
- **Zone Links:** Directly links Level 1 Cosmology, Level 8 Water Zone, Level 9 Undercity, and Level 3 Main Floor.
- **Mechanics:** Enables the **Reincarnation / New Game+** system via Mars's Reincarnation Gate, carrying forward harmonic progress and Soulstones.
- **Default Actions Integration:**
  - *Observe:* Reveals galaxy language scripts etched into planetary crusts.
  - *Listen:* Plays raw planetary frequencies (42Hz Sun, 50Hz Moon, 84Hz Mars).
  - *Name:* Constrains cosmic forces into playable star-charms.
  - *Sit:* Enters deep cosmic stasis, regaining full mana and unlocking galaxy memories.

## 4. geode_inn_catacomb_templates.md — Catacombs: Three Templates

### Core Systems & Lore Synthesis
This document outlines the three structural and thematic templates defining the Catacombs (Level 6), drawing from classic tabletop and MMO dungeon design to create a deeply layered underground environment:

1. **Template 1: The Undercity Aesthetic (Gothic-Industrial Alchemical):**
   - Inspired by underground alchemical cities.
   - Dark stone vaults illuminated by green-blue alchemical glow, canals of slow-moving liquid, and working apothecary vats.
   - Represents a functional, living subterranean city rather than a ruin.

2. **Template 2: Estate of Unrest (Instanced Haunted Civilization Wings):**
   - Self-contained gothic horror instances featuring progressive depth: Garden (grounds) → Main Floor (daily haunting) → Basement (deep presence).
   - Each wing represents a distinct fallen historical civilization preserved in atmospheric, narrative-driven instances.

3. **Template 3: The Crushbone Contrast (Ground-Level Threat & Bedrock Fortress):**
   - Inspired by the proximity between peaceful canopy platforms and ground-level fortresses.
   - Provides the dark, dangerous reality beneath the elevated peace of the Inn.
   - Anchored in the deep bedrock fortress level — the geological memory of pre-ocean stone.

Three Memory Scales:
- **Human Memory:** Civilization Wings (architectural records).
- **Alchemical Memory:** Deep Lab (practitioner experiment records in liquid canals).
- **Geological Memory:** Bedrock Fortress (earth records held in pre-ocean stone).

- **Spectral Aggression:** Haunted civilization wings spawn hostile shadow-wraiths that attack with 84Hz resonance.
- **Tectonic Bedrock Rumble:** The bedrock fortress vibrates violently with deep earth tremors, triggering rockfalls and environmental puzzles.

### 2. Endgame Connections

- **The Song of Making:** The bedrock fortress provides the deep 84Hz tectonic rumble that serves as the bass foundation for the Song of Making.
- **The Convergence:** The alchemical canals reverse flow, pumping glowing alchemical energy upward through the Inn Tree's root system to nourish the canopy.

### 3. Sound Profile

- **Shadow Harmonic (84Hz):** Metallic scraping, roaring furnace blasts, screeching shadow wraiths, and heavy tectonic rumbles.

## 8. geode_inn_death_sequence.md — The Death Sequence

### Core Systems & Lore Synthesis
Defines the Inn's unique death ceremony and energy departure mechanics built around the core phrase: **"Your name is known, your rest is earned."** Grounded in a polite, dignified, "Meet Joe Black" tone, death is treated not as a frightening punishment, but as a quiet, respectful colleague of life.

The 5-Beat Death Ceremony:
1. **Beat 1 (The Death):** The world softly fades to dark blue (the color of the underground river). World music recedes slowly rather than cutting off.
2. **Beat 2 (The Words):** The Inn-Tree / The Morrigan speaks: *"Your name is known."* The Ferryman / Inn-Tree grants permission: *"Your rest is earned."*
3. **Beat 3 (The River):** The player awakens on the dark blue shore of the subterranean river in the Catacombs, where Charon's boat waits.
4. **Beat 4 (The Obol):** The player drops one collected stone from their inventory into the water as payment. The stone sinks; the earth reclaims its own.
5. **Beat 5 (The Crossing & Return):** Charon poles the boat across in silence. Upon arrival at the far bank, Charon speaks two words: *"...There you are."* The player steps out, refreshed, lighter by one stone, and returns to the living world.

### 1. Shadow Inn State (84Hz)
In the Shadow Inn (84Hz), death undergoes a high-stakes combat shatter:
- **Crimson Obsidian Void:** The blue fade is replaced by a sudden obsidian shatter and crimson-violet void state.
- **Armored Ferryman:** Charon appears as a towering skeletal ferryman clad in dark iron armor, wielding a glowing silver pole.
- **Shadow Obol Requirement:** The player must surrender an 84Hz-charged shadow stone or suffer temporary stamina drain upon rebirth.
