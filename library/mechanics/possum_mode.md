# Geode Inn — Possum Mode

## Overview

Possum Mode is a voluntary wellbeing mechanic. If a player feels overwhelmed — by difficulty, by lore weight, by the Whisper Layer's awareness, or by life — they can choose to play dead. Their avatar transforms into a possum. The world waits.

This is not a penalty. This is not a failure state. This is the Inn saying: you can stop. We'll be here.

## The Mechanic

### Activation
- **Voluntary only.** The player activates Possum Mode from a menu, a keybind, or an emote (curl up).
- **No prerequisites.** Available from the first moment of the game. No unlock, no quest, no cost.
- **No trigger requirement.** The player doesn't need to be "actually overwhelmed" — there's no detection system. The player decides. The Inn trusts the player.

### While in Possum Mode
- The avatar transforms into a small possum — curled, eyes closed, breathing slowly.
- Movement is slow. A waddle. No running, no jumping.
- The player is un-targetable by any game system. No damage, no aggro, no triggers.
- NPCs don't interact with the possum. If the player approaches an NPC, the NPC simply doesn't notice them. The possum is beneath the Inn's attention.
- **The Whisper Layer goes quiet.** The Inn cannot watch what isn't moving. The possum is invisible to the awareness system. This is the only state in the game where the Whisper Layer stops.
- No quests progress. No timers run. No leaderboards update. The world is paused for the possum.
- The possum can wander. It can explore zones visually. It can go anywhere — no locked doors, no zone requirements. The possum has no inventory, no charms, no stones. Just a small body walking through a world that isn't asking anything of it.
- Other players (if multiplayer/connected) see a possum. They can't interact with it. A small tag: "Playing possum."

### Deactivation
- The player can transform back at any time. Same keybind or menu.
- There is **no time limit.** Stay a possum for 30 seconds or 3 hours.
- On return, the game resumes exactly where it was. No penalty. No lost progress. No "welcome back" fanfare. The Inn just continues.

### The Message
When the player activates Possum Mode, one line appears:

*"You play dead. The world waits."*

When the player returns:

*Nothing. The game just continues.*

No acknowledgment. No achievement. No notification. The absence of response IS the response. The Inn respects the privacy of playing dead.

## Thematic Connection

### The 84Hz Inversion
The Shadow Inn's 84Hz is the "stay" frequency — it makes things hold still. Possum Mode is the voluntary version. The player chooses to stay. Not because the frequency forces them, but because they need to. The possum is the 84Hz made gentle — self-care, not petrification.

### The Whisper Layer Blind Spot
The Whisper Layer watches everything. The possum is the one exception. Playing dead makes you invisible to the archive — the Inn can't record what isn't moving. This connects to the HIDE endgame choice: the player who chooses HIDE leaves something in the Inn that the Inn cannot record. The possum is the small, repeatable version of HIDE.

### Possums Are Real
Possums play dead when overwhelmed. It's involuntary in nature — a stress response. In the Inn, it's voluntary. The player does consciously what the possum does unconsciously. The mechanic honors the animal's strategy without mocking it. The possum survives by being still. So does the player.

## Why This Matters

The Geode Inn is a game with deep lore, existential themes, the Whisper Layer (the game is watching you), the Shadow Inn (the frequency that stops you), and the Hollow Men mines (where things end with a whimper). That's heavy. The Inn respects that some players need to step back.

Possum Mode is the Inn's answer to: "What if the game is too much?" You don't quit. You don't log out. You play possum. The world waits. You come back when you're ready.

The possum is the Inn's most compassionate design decision. It costs nothing, it does nothing, and it might be the most important mechanic in the game.
