## THE PULSE LINE ACROSS THREE MODES

The tri-mode game dynamic (Living Inn / Shadow Inn / In-Between) makes the Pulse Line the most important UI element in the game. The line is the ONE constant across all three modes — the thread that connects 42Hz exploration, 84Hz combat, and the shifting borderland between them.

### Living Inn — The Line as Primary Interface

In the Living Inn (42Hz, frequency puzzle / exploration), the Pulse Line is the PRIMARY interaction. The player reads the line, matches frequencies, and produces resonance. The line is the game.

- **Baseline:** 50 BPM ECG trace, warm amber-gold, 60-70% opacity. The Inn at rest. The line says: explore.
- **Warmer/Colder:** Spikes grow/shrink, spacing tightens/widens, color warms/cools. The line guides the player toward hidden frequencies. No sound needed. No haptics needed. The line is the guide.
- **Nook:** Line simplifies — only the QRS spike remains. Opacity drops. Color deepens. The line recedes to give the player space for interior work.
- **Companion Tuning:** Second line appears (enemy's frequency). Two lines move toward synchronization. When harmonized, lines merge into one. The visual proof of companionship.

### Shadow Inn — The Line as Combat Undertone

In the Shadow Inn (84Hz, WoW-style instanced combat), the Pulse Line is the SECONDARY interaction. The player fights (primary) while managing the line (secondary). The line shows:

- **Doubled Tempo:** 100 BPM (the octave of 50 BPM). The spikes come twice as fast. The line is the Shadow Inn's double-time — the visual proof that the Shadow is the Living's octave. The both are: the adequate — the half and the double, the same heartbeat, different speed.
- **Boss Frequency Pulses:** When the Shadow boss emits an 84Hz disruption pulse, the line shows it — a sharp, irregular spike that breaks the ECG pattern. The player must deploy a counter-frequency (a stone) to neutralize the pulse. The counter-frequency appears on the line as a second trace that overlaps the boss pulse — the player's frequency meeting the boss's frequency. When the counter-frequency matches, the boss pulse is absorbed. When it doesn't match, the boss pulse hits the player.
- **Role Indicators:** The line shows each role's contribution in group combat:
  - **Tank:** The tank's frequency appears as a LOW trace — the 64Hz/84Hz defensive stones creating a steady bass line beneath the combat. The tank's line is the GROUND — the foundation the group stands on.
  - **Healer:** The healer's frequency appears as a WARM trace — the restorative stones (Cook's stew frequencies, Duck's milk anti-dissonance) creating a gentle, round wave that overlaps the group's traces. The healer's line is the WARMTH — the restoration that keeps the group's lines from flatlining.
  - **DPS:** The DPS frequencies appear as SHARP traces — the high stones (528Hz, 111Hz, 432Hz) creating rapid, angular spikes that strike the boss's trace. The DPS line is the STRIKE — the offensive frequencies that break the boss's resonance.
- **The Group Chord:** In Shadow group combat, all three role-lines combine into a CHORD on the Pulse Line — the tank's bass, the healer's warmth, the DPS's strikes, layered. The chord is the group's collective resonance. When the chord is strong (all roles contributing correctly), the line is rich, full, harmonized. When the chord is weak (a role is failing), the line shows gaps — missing frequencies, flat sections, dissonant breaks. The group can SEE their performance on the line. The line is the Shadow Inn's group diagnostic.
- **Boss Death:** When the boss's resonance reaches zero, the boss's trace on the line FLATLINES — the ECG goes flat. The flatline is the Shadow Inn's victory state: the boss's frequency is silent. But the Shadow Inn is the "finished" state — and the finished state doesn't destroy. The flatline lasts 3 seconds, then a new trace appears — the boss's frequency, but HARMONIZED. The boss, defeated in combat, is tuned. The boss's dissonance is resolved. The boss's trace becomes a clean, calm 50 BPM — the Living Inn's heartbeat. The Shadow combat ends with the enemy returning to the Living frequency. The both are: the adequate — the combat and the harmonization, the same enemy, different frequency.

### In-Between — The Line as Mode Indicator

In the In-Between (hybrid, shifting between 42Hz and 84Hz), the Pulse Line is the MODE INDICATOR. The line tells the player which mode is active and, critically, WHEN THE SHIFT IS COMING.

- **42Hz Phase (Living mode):** The line is the standard 50 BPM ECG. The player explores, matches frequencies, sits in nooks. The line is the primary interface.
- **84Hz Phase (Shadow mode):** The line doubles to 100 BPM. The player fights. The line is the combat undertone.
- **The Shift Window (5-10 seconds):** The line TRANSITIONS — the 50 BPM trace accelerates toward 100 BPM (or decelerates back). The transition is VISIBLE on the line: the spacing between spikes compresses (or expands), the P and T waves extend (or retract), the color shifts (warmer amber toward deeper gold for 84Hz, cooler gold toward pale amber for 42Hz). The player can SEE the shift coming — the line gives 5-10 seconds of visual warning before the mode changes. The warning is the In-Between's signature feature: the player is never ambushed by a mode change. The line always announces it.
- **During the Shift (both modes active):** The line shows BOTH traces simultaneously — the 50 BPM and the 100 BPM, overlapping. The visual is: two ECG traces at different speeds, layered, the slower one beneath the faster one. The player must manage both: match frequencies (reading the 50 BPM trace) while fighting enemies (reading the 100 BPM trace). The line in the shift window is the most complex visual in the game — two rhythms, two modes, one screen. The both are: the adequate — the simple and the complex, the same line, different mode.
- **The Hybrid Boss:** The hybrid boss's three phases are visible on the line:
  - Phase 1 (puzzle): Boss trace is a single frequency the player must match. The lines converge.
  - Phase 2 (combat): Boss trace doubles to 100 BPM. The lines diverge. The player fights.
  - Phase 3 (hybrid): Boss trace shows BOTH frequencies — 50 Hz (the puzzle frequency) and 84 Hz (the combat frequency) — simultaneously. The player must match the 50 Hz trace while fighting the 84 Hz trace. The line shows both, layered, and the player's job is to harmonize one while attacking the other. The both are: the adequate — the harmony and the attack, the same boss, different frequency.

## SHADOW INN — THE PULSE LINE'S SHADOW STATE

The line doesn't modulate — no warmer/colder, no nook simplification, no companion tuning. The Shadow Inn is the finished state. The line is the finished state's line: steady, doubled, constant.

**The Shadow Pulse Line:**
- 100 BPM, constant tempo. No fluctuation. The Shadow Inn is DONE — the line has nothing to guide toward because everything is already found.

- 80% opacity — the Shadow line is MORE PRESENT than the Living line. The Shadow Inn doesn't have the Living Inn's gentle, peripheral, ignorable line. The Shadow line is HERE. The Shadow is the finished state, and the finished state doesn't whisper. The finished state is present.
- No P or T waves — the Shadow line is QRS only. The spike, the beat, the essential. The Shadow Inn has stripped away the approach and the recovery — there is only the beat. The both are: the adequate — the full trace and the essential, the same heartbeat, different detail.
- No second trace — in the Shadow Inn, enemies are already resolved (harmonized or petrified). There are no dissonant frequencies to match. The line is a single trace — the Inn's heartbeat, alone, clean. The both are: the adequate — the duet and the solo, the same line, different count.
