# Geode Inn — Share Wood: Digital Faire Grounds

## The Product

**Share Wood** is a self-service product built on the Geode Inn platform. Renaissance faires subscribe, build their own digital zone, and publish — no approval process, no red tape, no waiting on anyone else. The subscription is the only gate. Everything else is theirs to build.

The name is both a play on Sherwood and the product's philosophy: shared woods. Multiple faires sharing the digital forest, each with their own grounds, each built by the people who know them best.

## The Model: Subscribe → Build → Publish

### 1. Subscribe
A faire owner creates an account and subscribes. That's it. No application, no review, no pitch meeting. They pick a tier, they pay, they're in.

### 2. Build
The faire gets access to the Share Wood zone builder — a drag-and-drop world-building interface built on the Geode Inn's existing building system. They:

- Upload their own media: commercial videos, promo footage, vendor photos, drone shots, performance clips
- Place structures: gatehouse, vendor stalls, stages, food court, forest paths, lore areas
- Create vendor pages: each stall gets a shop name, product images, description, and an external link to the vendor's real shop
- Set the calendar: physical season dates, special event weekends, performance schedules
- Configure the ticket portal: link to their own ticket sales page
- Write their lore entry: the history of their faire, presented in the Inn's scholarly voice
- Choose their aesthetic: the zone's visual style, music, and ambiance

Everything uses the faire's own assets. No custom art generation needed — their existing promotional media IS the zone's content. Their YouTube channel becomes the Castle Stage. Their vendor photos become the stall pages. Their drone footage becomes the greenwood.

### 3. Publish
When the faire is satisfied with their zone, they hit publish. The gatehouse opens. The zone goes live in the Inn's Faire Grounds corridor. Players worldwide can walk through it immediately.

No one reviews it. No one approves it. The faire built it. The faire owns it. The faire publishes it.

## The Zone Builder

Built on the Geode Inn's existing building system (Ironforge-style connected zones, Don't Starve gathering, 5 tiers of complexity). The Share Wood builder is a simplified version focused on faire construction:

### Structure Placement
- Drag structures from a template palette: gatehouse, vendor stalls (3 sizes), stage, food court, forest paths, lore podium, maypole, archery range
- Place them on the zone map — the layout can mirror the physical faire or be entirely custom
- Connect structures with paths — the Ironforge-style corridor system

### Media Integration
- Upload video files or paste YouTube/Vimeo links → videos play on the Castle Stage
- Upload photos → photos populate vendor stalls, gatehouse display, lore area
- Upload audio → ambient zone music (or choose from the Inn's library)
- Each media item is placed in a specific structure — videos to stages, photos to stalls, audio to the zone

### Vendor Management
- Create a vendor stall: name, description, category, product photos, external shop link
- Drag vendors onto the zone map — arrange them as they are at the physical faire
- Vendors can be hidden (tucked away in the greenwood) or featured (front of the market row)
- Each vendor manages their own page — the faire owner places the stall; the vendor fills it

### Calendar & Events
- Set physical season dates → the zone automatically switches between "Faire Season" and "Off-Season" modes
- Add special event weekends → themed banners appear in the zone on those dates
- Add performance schedules → the Castle Stage shows what's happening today
- Off-season mode activates automatically: "The physical grounds sleep. The digital grounds are open."

### Publishing
- Preview mode: see the zone as players will see it before publishing
- Publish: zone goes live instantly. No approval. No queue.
- Edit anytime: the faire can update their zone whenever they want — new vendors, new videos, new events. Changes go live immediately.
- Unpublish: the faire can close their zone anytime. The gatehouse door closes. The zone waits, not dead — dormant.

## Subscription Tiers

All tiers are self-service. No tier requires the Inn to build anything for the faire.

### Tier 1: The Clearing — $49/month
- One walkable zone with up to 20 vendor stalls
- Castle Stage video player (link up to 5 videos)
- Calendar with physical season dates
- Ticket portal (link to your ticket page)
- Lore entry in the Inn's archive
- Off-season mode (automatic)
- Basic zone templates (gatehouse, market row, greenwood, feast hall)

### Tier 2: The Village — $149/month
- Everything in Tier 1, plus:
- Up to 75 vendor stalls
- Unlimited Castle Stage videos
- Special event weekends with themed banners
- Performance schedule on the Castle Stage
- Custom zone layout (not just templates — free placement)
- Off-season content rotation (feature different vendors/videos on a schedule)
- Dual-listing: vendors who are also Geode Inn artisans get cross-linked pages
- Custom zone name and description

### Tier 3: The Kingdom — $399/month
- Everything in Tier 2, plus:
- Up to 250 vendor stalls
- Mini-game integration (archery, jousting, faire-themed games)
- Custom NPC creation (your faire characters become game NPCs)
- Quest tie-ins connecting your zone to the Inn's main storyline
- Live event hosting (stream Q&As, virtual performances, digital market days)
- Custom ambient music upload
- Multiple connected zones (main grounds + camping area + tournament field)
- Priority placement in the Faire Grounds corridor
- Analytics dashboard (visitor count, stall click-throughs, ticket link clicks)

## What Share Wood Never Does

- Never handles ticket sales — links redirect to the faire's own system
- Never handles vendor transactions — links redirect to vendor shops
- Never takes a cut of anything — the subscription fee is the only revenue
- Never approves or reviews zones — the faire publishes what they build
- Never owns the faire's content — the faire's media stays the faire's media
- Never gates access beyond the subscription — pay and build, that's it

## Connection to Existing Systems

- **Geode Inn building system:** Share Wood uses the Inn's zone builder engine, simplified for faire construction
- **MarketItem entity:** Vendor stalls in Share Wood use the same data structure as Inn market items
- **Artisan entity:** Vendors who are also Inn artisans get dual-listing automatically (Tier 2+)
- **FaireVenue entity:** Tracks subscription status, tier, and zone assignment for each faire
- **Elora Nightbloom:** The Inn's market manager NPC can introduce new Share Wood zones as they go live — but the faire builds their own zone. Elora manages the corridor, not the individual zones.
