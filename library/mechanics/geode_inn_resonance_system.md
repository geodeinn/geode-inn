# Geode Inn — Resonance System Documentation

## Overview

The Resonance System is the unifying mechanical framework of the Geode Inn. All systems connect through frequency — specifically the 42Hz Song, the foundational vibration of the Inn.

## The Three Frequencies

### 42Hz — The Song (Living)
- **Domain:** Growth, creation, the Inn itself
- **Game Mode:** Puzzle, patience, reward for waiting
- **Mechanic:** Cards/effects grow stronger each turn
- **Zone:** Main Floor, Cosmology, Garden, Campgrounds
- **Audio:** Warm, rising, the sound of things growing
- **IEM Layer:** Bone Conduction — frequencies you know

### 84Hz — Shadow (Petrify)
- **Domain:** Stillness, defense, the Shadow Inn
- **Game Mode:** Combat, disruption, making opponents stay
- **Mechanic:** Petrify opponent's cards/effects
- **Zone:** Undercity, Hollow Men mines, Poison Garden
- **Audio:** Deep, heavy, the sound of stone slowing
- **IEM Layer:** Dynamic Driver — frequencies you feel

### 111Hz — Gargoyle (Awareness)
- **Domain:** Perception, revelation, hidden knowledge
- **Game Mode:** Information, scouting, seeing what's hidden
- **Mechanic:** Reveal hidden cards/passages/secrets
- **Zone:** Staircase, Archive, Catacombs
- **Audio:** Clear, alert, the sound of attention
- **IEM Layer:** Balanced Armature — frequencies you hear

## Resonance Signature Computation

When a player creates a composition (Bead Composer) or builds a deck (Resonance card game), the system computes a resonance signature:

1. **Frequency Distribution** — Count of 42/84/111Hz items
2. **Dominant Frequency** — Most common frequency
3. **Harmony Score** — Musical interval analysis:
   - Octave (2:1) = perfect (1.0)
   - Fifth (3:2) = strong (0.8)
   - Fourth (4:3) = good (0.6)
   - Unison (1:1) = neutral (0.5)
   - Other = dissonant (0.2)
4. **Effect Tier** — Based on dominant frequency + harmony:
   - High harmony (>0.7) = strong effect
   - Medium harmony (>0.4) = moderate effect
   - Low harmony = weak effect

## Audio Implementation

The Resonance System drives the AudioManager's tri-mode architecture:
- **Living mode (42Hz):** Warm ambient, rising tones, growth soundscapes
- **Shadow mode (84Hz):** Deep drones, heavy bass, petrification tones
- **In-Between (hybrid):** Both frequencies layered, shifting dominance

Mode shifts occur dynamically based on:
- Zone entered (each zone has a dominant frequency)
- Game state (card game board composition)
- Player actions (certain abilities shift the balance)
