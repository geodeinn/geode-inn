# Geode Inn — Arcade Game Registry (Batch 2)

## New Games Added

Each game maps to an existing zone and rewards a stone consistent with that zone's geology.

---

### 1. Galaga
- **Inspiration:** Galaga (Namco, 1981)
- **Zone:** Cosmology (Dalí)
- **Genre:** Fixed shooter
- **Mechanic:** Waves of Fae moths descend from the starfield. Player controls a crystal shard that fires light beams. Captured shards can be rescued for dual-fire. The difficulty curve is the frequency — waves sync to 42Hz, 84Hz, 111Hz as they intensify.
- **Reward Stone:** Moldavite (tektite — formed by cosmic impact)
- **Unlock:** Reach the Cosmology level

### 2. Goonies
- **Inspiration:** The Goonies (Konami, 1986)
- **Zone:** Cave City (Doré)
- **Genre:** Platformer adventure
- **Mechanic:** Navigate interconnected cave systems searching for pirate treasure. Find keys, avoid traps, discover secret passages behind carved walls. The Doré aesthetic means everything is enormous — the player is small in a vast carved space.
- **Reward Stone:** Fluorite (cave mineral, multi-colored)
- **Unlock:** Complete Cave City exploration quest

### 3. Space Invaders
- **Inspiration:** Space Invaders (Taito, 1978)
- **Zone:** Cosmology (Dalí)
- **Genre:** Fixed shooter
- **Mechanic:** Rows of shadow entities descend from the cosmic ceiling. Player fires from the Observatory floor. Speed increases as rows diminish. The final invader is always the Shadow — it takes one hit but doesn't disappear. It joins the Whisper Layer.
- **Reward Stone:** Star Iron (meteoric iron — already in Material entity)
- **Unlock:** Reach the Cosmology level

### 4. Frogger
- **Inspiration:** Frogger (Konami, 1981)
- **Zone:** Bayou (Don't Starve)
- **Genre:** Crossing puzzle
- **Mechanic:** Cross the bayou — logs, gators, Spanish moss gaps, and leaping between lily pads. The bayou water has a current that shifts each level. Later levels include the caimans (First through Fourteenth) as moving obstacles — they're not hostile, they're just there.
- **Reward Stone:** Bayou Bog Agate (water-worn agate from the bayou)
- **Unlock:** Bayou zone access (default — starter game)

### 5. Kirby
- **Inspiration:** Kirby's Dream Land (Nintendo, 1992)
- **Zone:** Fae Kingdom — Seelie Court
- **Genre:** Platformer with copy ability
- **Mechanic:** A small Fae creature (chibi, round, pink-adjacent — Fae coloring) can inhale enemies and copy their abilities. The Seelie Court's riddle-quest enemies become abilities: riddle-keepers give "solve," guards give "shield," sprites give "float." The copy abilities are Fae gifts, not stolen powers — the Fae version of Kirby's mechanic.
- **Reward Stone:** Rainbow Moonstone (Fae stone, already in lore)
- **Unlock:** Activate a Fae Kingdom portal (complete Standing Stones)

### 6. Dr. Mario
- **Inspiration:** Dr. Mario (Nintendo, 1990)
- **Zone:** Staircase — Alchemy level
- **Genre:** Falling-block puzzle
- **Mechanic:** Colored alchemical reagents fall into a beaker. Match three of the same color to transmute them (they transform into the next stage in the alchemical process — nigredo → albedo → citrinitas → rubedo). The pills are the four alchemical colors. The game teaches the alchemical stages as you play.
- **Reward Stone:** Cinnabar (mercury sulfide — the prima materia of alchemy)
- **Unlock:** Reach the Staircase

### 7. PixelJunk Monsters
- **Inspiration:** PixelJunk Monsters (Q-Games, 2007)
- **Zone:** Colorado Plateau (Dixon)
- **Genre:** Tower defense
- **Mechanic:** Build defensive towers along mesa paths to protect a settlement from creatures emerging from canyon walls. Towers are made from gathered materials (links to the building system). Different materials = different tower types. The Dixon aesthetic means the towers are painted desert colors — ochre, rust, sand.
- **Reward Stone:** Turquoise (desert stone, already in lore)
- **Unlock:** Reach Colorado Plateau zone

### 8. Ultimate Attack of the Noobs
- **Inspiration:** Ultimate Attack of the Noobs (Roblox-style wave defense)
- **Zone:** Main Floor Arcade
- **Genre:** Wave defense / comedy
- **Mechanic:** Waves of "noobs" — confused new players who wandered in from outside — approach the Inn's front door. The player must guide them to the correct zone using signs, paths, and gentle mechanics. Not combat — crowd management. The noobs are harmless but disorienting. High-score = most noobs successfully directed before they wander off.
- **Reward Stone:** Common Quartz (the every-stone — the stone of beginners)
- **Unlock:** Default — available from the start

### 9. Snood
- **Inspiration:** Snood (1996)
- **Zone:** Main Floor Arcade
- **Genre:** Projectile matching puzzle
- **Mechanic:** Launch small creature faces (snoods) at a cluster of similar faces to make them fall. Geode Inn version: the snoods are chibi creature faces from the bestiary. Matching three of the same creature type frees them — they fall and scamper off screen. A gentle, cheerful puzzle with no time pressure.
- **Reward Stone:** Agate (common, varied — the friendly stone)
- **Unlock:** Default — available from the start

### 10. Candyland
- **Inspiration:** Candyland (board game, 1949)
- **Zone:** Fae Kingdom — Summerlands
- **Genre:** Path-following board game
- **Mechanic:** A path through the Fae Kingdom's sweetest region — flowers that taste of honey, rivers of sap, trees with sugar bark. Draw cards to advance. No strategy — pure journey. The first Fae game that requires no skill, only luck. The Summerlands are the Seelie Court's welcoming zone, and Candyland is how they welcome children and new players.
- **Reward Stone:** Amber (fossilized tree resin — sweet, golden, ancient)
- **Unlock:** Fae Kingdom access

### 11. Chess
- **Inspiration:** Chess (classic)
- **Zone:** Evan's Chess Hall (connected to the Hearth)
- **Genre:** Strategy board game
- **Mechanic:** Full chess game against Evan the Chess Wizard, or against other players (cross-platform pairing). Evan's AI difficulty scales to the player's skill. Pieces are Geode Inn-themed: the Inn's roster replaces standard pieces (the Crone as the Queen, the Reader as the King, gargoyles as rooks, dragon kittens as knights, brownies as pawns). Evan narrates each move in character.
- **Reward Stone:** Preseli Blue Stone (the strategic stone — Stonehenge's bluestone)
- **Unlock:** Meet Evan in the Hearth

### 12. Coup
- **Inspiration:** Coup (La Mame Games, 2012)
- **Zone:** Undercity (Gothic)
- **Genre:** Social deduction
- **Mechanic:** Each player holds two roles from the Undercity's power structure (the Owl, the Crone, the Firekeeper, the Teacher, the Shadow). Each role has a special ability. Players can lie about which roles they hold. Bluff, counter-bluff, and challenge. The Undercity's gothic atmosphere is the perfect setting for a deception game — everyone is already hiding something.
- **Reward Stone:** Onyx (the stone of secrets — black, opaque, the Undercity's stone)
- **Unlock:** Reach the Undercity

### 13. Boss Monster
- **Inspiration:** Boss Monster (Brotherwise Games, 2013)
- **Zone:** Catacombs (Klimt)
- **Genre:** Dungeon-building card game
- **Mechanic:** The player IS the monster — build a dungeon to lure and defeat heroes. Geode Inn version: the player is the Catacombs' Keeper, building rooms to test adventurers. Heroes are drawn from the Inn's NPC roster — they enter the Catacombs seeking knowledge, and the player must make the dungeon challenging enough to send them back out. The rooms use the building system's recipes and materials.
- **Reward Stone:** Obsidian (volcanic glass — the dungeon builder's stone)
- **Unlock:** Complete Catacombs exploration quest
