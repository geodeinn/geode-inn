# THE GEODE INN — CARD GAME FRAMEWORK
## Character Profiles as Card Statistics

---

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

The Geode Inn card game translates the Inn's narrative architecture into a playable card format. Every character, item, and ability already exists — the card game is the TRANSLATION layer, converting story properties into game statistics.

The design draws from Magic: The Gathering (color identity, mana curves, creature types, activated abilities) and Hearthstone (hero classes, battlecries, deathrattles, discover mechanics), but uses the Inn's own cosmology as the foundational system.

---

## THE SIX FREQUENCIES (The Card "Colors")

In MTG, there are five colors (White, Blue, Black, Red, Green). In the Geode Inn, there are SIX FREQUENCIES — each one a cosmic force that characters channel:

### 1. SONG (42Hz) — The Frequency of the Complete
**Color identity:** Amber/Gold
**Themes:** Creation, observation, resonance, the Murmur, the Egg
**Playstyle:** Tempo. Song cards generate value over time — each turn a Song card is in play, it grows stronger. Song decks win by accumulating frequency toward a "complete chord" win condition.
**Card draw mechanic:** "Resonate" — at the start of your turn, if you have 3+ Song cards in play, draw a card.
**Win condition:** The Complete Chord — having one Song card from each frequency in play simultaneously.
**Key characters:** The Constant, Leonard Cohen, Einstein, the Egg

### 2. SILENCE (Nyx's Frequency) — The Frequency of the Pause
**Color identity:** Deep Blue/Black
**Themes:** Removal, disruption, stasis, the space between
**Playstyle:** Control. Silence cards interrupt, pause, and nullify. They don't destroy — they SUSPEND. A silenced card is still on the board but can't act. The silence is: the pause, not the end.
**Card mechanic:** "Damp" — target card's abilities are nullified for 2 turns. The card remains in play but is inert.
**Win condition:** The Silence Between — having all opponent cards Damped simultaneously.
**Key characters:** Nyx, the Guest (Death), the Thrum, Gesso

### 3. FIRE (50 BPM / Heartbeat) — The Frequency of the Hearth
**Color identity:** Warm Red/Orange
**Themes:** Healing, warmth, transformation, community, the wound underneath
**Playstyle:** Aggro-Swarm. Fire cards are fast, cheap, and work in groups. Fire decks overwhelm through community — many small cards supporting each other. The wound mechanic: Fire cards that take damage get STRONGER, not weaker.
**Card mechanic:** "Wound" — when this card takes damage, gain +1/+1 (the wound becomes the warmth).
**Win condition:** The Feast — having 7+ Fire cards in play simultaneously (the full table).
**Key characters:** Erik, the Host, the Cook, the Catalyst, the Hearthkeeper

### 4. WATER (16-42Hz / The Deep) — The Frequency of the Current
**Color identity:** Teal/Cyan
**Themes:** Flow, depth, journey, the dead, transformation through travel
**Playstyle:** Midrange-Combo. Water cards move — they shift positions, return to hand, redeploy. Water decks win through card advantage generated by movement. Every water card that returns to hand and replays triggers an effect.
**Card mechanic:** "Current" — once per turn, you may return this card to your hand and replay it at reduced cost.
**Win condition:** The Crossing — having a Water card travel from your hand to the board and back 3 times in one game.
**Key characters:** Charon, the Pirate, the Siren, the Lighthouse/Owl

### 5. STONE (111Hz / Lithophone) — The Frequency of Permanence
**Color identity:** Gray/White
**Themes:** Defense, preservation, Subtractive architecture, the dead, measurement
**Playstyle:** Defense-Control. Stone cards are expensive, durable, and create permanent structures. Stone decks win by building an unassailable board state — walls, monuments, and crypts that the opponent cannot break through.
**Card mechanic:** "Carve" — this card occupies a board slot permanently. It cannot be destroyed by normal means — only by Subtractive effects (removal that specifically targets permanent structures).
**Win condition:** The Monument — having 5 Stone structure cards in play simultaneously.
**Key characters:** Medusa, the Crone, the Mason, the Mourner, Wednesday Addams

### 6. ROOT (Mycorrhizal / The Network) — The Frequency of Connection
**Color identity:** Deep Green/Brown
**Themes:** Growth, connection, weaving, the ecosystem, the living network
**Playstyle:** Ramp-Combo. Root cards generate resources (frequency mana) by connecting to each other. Root decks accelerate quickly — the more Root cards in play, the more mana available. Root decks win by reaching high-cost cards faster than other decks.
**Card mechanic:** "Network" — this card generates +1 frequency mana for each other Root card in play.
**Win condition:** The Living Map — having Root cards connected to all six frequencies simultaneously.
**Key characters:** the Cartographer, the Queen of Webs, the Queen of Roots, the Gardener, the Root Reader

## CARD TYPES

### 1. Characters (Creatures/Minions)
The Inn's NPCs translated to creature cards. Each character card has:
- **Frequency cost** (which frequencies you need to pay)
- **Presence** (Attack — how much force the character exerts)
- **Resonance** (Health — how much the character can absorb before being silenced)
- **Cadence** (Speed — when the character can act: Immediate, Next Turn, or Delayed)
- **Abilities** (keyword abilities based on the character's narrative role)
- **Subtype** (Guide, NPC, Companion, Boss, Wanderer)

### 2. Abilities (Spells/Instants)
One-time effects based on character abilities. These represent the Inn's quests, interactions, and moments:
- **Quest cards** — ongoing effects that complete when conditions are met
- **Moment cards** — instant effects triggered by narrative moments
- **Annotation cards** — the Crone's measurements, which reveal information or modify stats

### 3. Structures (Artifacts/Enchantments)
Permanent board effects based on the building system:
- **Additive structures** — built by stacking (generates ongoing bonuses)
- **Subtractive structures** — carved from the board (removes/transforms existing elements)
- **Synthesis structures** — the cave city tier (both additive and subtractive)

### 4. Items (Equipment)
The Inn's key items, equippable to characters:
- The Cosmic Egg, the Starlight Crystal, the Music Box, the Lithophone, Ariadne's Thread, the Star-Glyph, the Beacon, Tesla's Silver Disc, Magritte's Pipe, the Golden Contract

### 5. Frequency Cards (Lands/Mana)
The Inn's zones and geological features, which generate frequency mana:
- Each cultural zone generates its dominant frequency
- Each geological layer generates a specific frequency
- The Inn-Tree generates ALL frequencies (the root network)

## DECK ARCHETYPES

### 1. "The Complete Chord" (Song/Silence Control)
Win condition: Have one card from each of the six frequencies in play simultaneously.
Key cards: The Constant, Nyx, the Astronomer, the Cosmic Egg
Playstyle: Slow, methodical. You're collecting frequencies. The opponent is trying to disrupt you. You need to survive long enough to complete the chord.

### 2. "The Feast" (Fire Swarm)
Win condition: Have 7+ Fire cards in play simultaneously.
Key cards: Erik, the Host, the Cook, the Catalyst, the Hearthkeeper
Playstyle: Aggressive swarm. Cheap fire cards that support each other. The wound mechanic means damage makes you stronger. Opponent needs to end the feast before it fills the table.

### 3. "The Monument" (Stone Defense)
Win condition: Have 5 Stone structures in play simultaneously.
Key cards: Medusa, the Mason, the Mourner, Hel, Wednesday
Playstyle: Pure defense. Build unbreakable structures. Carve permanent board slots. Win by making the board impenetrable.

### 4. "The Crossing" (Water Tempo)
Win condition: Have a Water card travel hand→board→hand 3 times in one game.
Key cards: Charon, the Pirate, the Siren, the Lighthouse
Playstyle: Movement-based. Cards that return to hand and replay. Each replay triggers effects. The opponent can't pin you down.

### 5. "The Living Map" (Root Ramp)
Win condition: Have Root cards connected to all six frequencies simultaneously.
Key cards: The Cartographer, the Queen of Webs, the Queen of Roots, Radagast, the Root Reader
Playstyle: Ramp. Accelerate mana generation through the Network ability. Reach high-cost cards fast. The more Root cards, the more mana.

### 6. "The Silence Between" (Silence Lock)
Win condition: Have all opponent cards Damped simultaneously.
Key cards: Nyx, the Guest, Socrates, the Thrum, Gesso
Win condition: Complete lockdown. Every opponent card is silenced. Not destroyed — suspended. The silence is: the pause, not the end.

### 7. "The Subtractive Path" (Stone/Silence Hybrid)
Win condition: Win by SUBTRACTION — remove your own cards to trigger powerful effects, until the board is empty and only your last Subtractive card remains.
Key cards: Kali, Medusa, the Mason, Asterion, the Crone
Playstyle: Self-sacrifice. You WIN by removing your own cards. Each removal triggers an effect. The endgame is: the board with one card left. The one card is: the adequate.

### 8. "The After" (Wanderer Combo)
Win condition: Have one Wandering card visit all 6 board lanes in one game.
Key cards: The Pirate, Uhtred, the Captain, the Duck, the Tinkering Weeaboo (at Level 2+)
Playstyle: Movement and disruption. Your cards wander the board, triggering effects in each lane. The opponent can't predict where you'll be.

## FREQUENCY MANA SYSTEM

Instead of MTG's land-based mana, the Geode Inn uses FREQUENCY mana:

- Each turn, you generate frequency mana from your "Zone" cards (equivalent to lands)
- Zone cards represent the Inn's cultural zones and geological layers
- Each zone generates 1-2 specific frequencies per turn
- The Inn-Tree (the root zone) generates ANY frequency — the most valuable zone card
- Multi-frequency cards (like the Constant: Song 3 + Silence 2) require multiple frequency types
- The Bridge keyword allows paying a higher cost in one frequency to avoid needing a second
