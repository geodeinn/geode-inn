## DECK ARCHETYPES UPDATED

### 9. "The Cave" (Stone Subtractive Control)
Win condition: Win by SUBTRACTION — remove your own cards to trigger powerful effects, until the board is empty and only your last Subtractive card remains.
Key cards: The Mason, the Smelter, the Mourner Goth, the Root Reader, Kali, Medusa, Asterion
New additions: The Mourner Goth (prepares niches — destroyed cards return once), the Smelter (extracts structures for mana), the Mason (destroys structures to gain their Resonance)
Playstyle: Self-sacrifice with safety net. The Mourner's Niches prevent total loss. You're not just removing — you're PREPARING for what comes next.

### 10. "The Fae Courts" (Song/Silence Tempo)
Win condition: Have both a Seelie and Unseelie card in each lane simultaneously.
Key cards: The Seelie King/Queen, the Unseelie Court Guardian, the Seelie Court Guardian, the Threshling, the Griffin, the Stone Circle Chamber
New additions: The Threshling (riddle-gates the opponent), the Griffin (blocks water entirely), the Stone Circle Chamber (win condition: Chamber + Golden Contract = auto-win)
Playstyle: Board control through duality. Summer and Winter in the same lanes. The Griffin denies the opponent's water cards. The Stone Circle Chamber is the ultimate win condition but requires surviving long enough to build it.

### 11. "The Wandering Program" (Wandering/Fire Aggro)
Win condition: Have one Wandering card visit all 6 board lanes in one game.
Key cards: The Wayfarer, the Pirate, the Captain, the Duck, the Tinkering Weeaboo (Level 2+), the Wandering Merchant
New additions: The Wandering Merchant (trades cards as it moves), the Bearsark in Bear Form (gains Wandering)
Playstyle: Disruption through constant movement. You're everywhere and nowhere. The Merchant trades your bad cards for their good cards. The Wayfarer damps conflicts in each lane.
