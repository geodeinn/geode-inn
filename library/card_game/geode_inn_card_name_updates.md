# THE GEODE INN — CARD NAME UPDATES
## Final Names for Three Characters

---

## BELLDONNA (formerly Wednesday → Wednesday Addams)

Frequency: STONE (1) + SILENCE (2) — Total cost: 3
Type: Character — Guide — Undercity Host
Presence: 2 | Resonance: 5 | Cadence: Next Turn
Abilities: Damp, Gargoyle
Special: **"These Are My People"** — When Belladonna enters play, all STONE cards in your discard pile gain "Ferry" (return to hand for 1 mana). The dead are: comfortable. The dead are: neighbors. Belladonna is: the bridge between the living's Inn and the dead's city.
Flavor text: *"The dead sit on benches. They read books. They're perfect."*
Rarity: Epic

**Name notes:** "Belladonna" — deadly nightshade, Italian for "beautiful lady." Public domain. The name carries the gothic, cozy-but-dangerous energy of the character without referencing any copyrighted property. Everyone who encounters Belladonna's demeanor — deadpan, dark humor, genuinely comfortable with the macabre — will feel the reference without needing it spelled out.

## MADAME (formerly Madame Mimic → formerly Madame Mim)

Frequency: SONG (1) + SILENCE (1) — Total cost: 2
Type: Character — Companion — Trickster
Presence: 2 | Resonance: 2 | Cadence: Immediate
Abilities: Mimic
Special: **"But what if we did the fun wrong version?"** — Once per game, Madame may target any card in play (including your own) and replace its text with a randomly selected ability from a pool. The replaced card retains its stats. The breaking is: the adequate.
Additional mechanic: **Never the Same** — Each time Madame is played, her flavor text, voice description, and visual presentation shift. She is spoken in different tones, cadences, and styles. The name "Madame" is always the same. The Madame is never the same. The name is the fixed point. The character is the variation. The both are: the adequate.
Flavor text: *"Madame." (Said differently every time. The name is the only formula she doesn't break — and she breaks it by HOW she says it.)*
Rarity: Epic

**Name notes:** "Madame" alone — a generic title, not copyrightable. The character's identity lives in her behavior, not her name. The name is deliberately unfixed in its delivery: whispered, shouted, sung, drawled, purred, snapped — never quite the same. This IS the Mimic ability made meta: she mimics the way her own name is spoken. The spirit of the original inspiration (theatrical, dramatic, charismatic, more interesting than the heroes) lives in the performance, not the label.

## THE WAYFARER (confirmed — no change needed)

Frequency: FIRE (2) + SILENCE (1) — Total cost: 3
Type: Character — Wanderer — Warrior
Presence: 3 | Resonance: 5 | Cadence: Immediate
Abilities: Wandering, Heartbeat
Special: **"Destiny Is All"** — When the Wayfarer enters a lane with two or more conflicting characters (opposing cards in the same lane), both are Damped for 1 turn. The Wayfarer says the uncomfortable truth and the argument stops. "Destiny is all" = the argument is already decided.
Flavor text: *"He sits by a campfire between zones. The Pirate gives him rides to the next nowhere."*
Rarity: Epic

**Name notes:** "The Wayfarer" — a common English word for a wanderer/traveler. Public domain. The spirit nod to Kvothe (a wanderer who tells uncomfortable truths, who is defined by the road, who carries a name that is also a story) lives in the character's function, not in any copyrighted name or title. Safe.
