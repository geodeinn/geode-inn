# THE GORGON'S GARDEN — BUILD SPEC FOR BUILDER
## The Poison Garden — 84Hz Gothic Herbalism Zone

## OVERVIEW

The Gorgon's Garden is the Geode Inn's poison garden — a walled Gothic garden in the Moorlands region of the World Grid, inspired by the Alnwick Poison Garden (Northumberland, England). Behind black iron gates. Warning signs carved in stone. Plants that glow not with warm 42Hz gold, but with cold, sickly luminescence — dissonant frequencies.

Medusa's domain. The preserver. Poison plants don't kill — they PETRIFY. They carry the inverse of 42Hz — the 84Hz death octave. The anti-frequency that stops motion, stops time, stops decay. A leaf touched by poison doesn't wither. It stays. Perfect. Forever. The poison IS preservation. The garden is where death and permanence are the same thing.

**The Warning:** The gate is locked. A sign reads: "These plants will not harm you. These plants will not help you. These plants will keep you exactly as you are." The Gatekeeper gargoyle (adaptive frequency) guards the entrance. Gate only opens to herbalists Level 26+ (where Ley Line Vision reveals hidden plants — poison plants are ALWAYS hidden without it).

**The Frequency Logic:** 42Hz (life, growth, motion) vs. 84Hz (stillness, permanence, preservation). 84Hz is not evil — it's the octave. The Stone Circle Chamber's frequency. The note of completion. The poison plants sing the note of ENDING — not destruction, but completion. The finished work. The preserved moment. The frame around the painting. "The garden is not the Inn's opposite. The garden is the Inn's FRAME."

## 2. MEDUSA

### Role
The preserver. The gardener who does not plant, water, or prune. Medusa watches. The garden grows because the plants CHOOSE to grow — in the stillness, in the shadow, in the absence of 42Hz's relentless push to grow. The Gorgon's Garden is the space where things are allowed to be what they are, without pressure to become more.

### Philosophy
"The Inn grows. The tree grows. The world grows. Everything grows, always, because the Song says grow. But some things don't need to grow. Some things are finished. Some things are already what they should be. I keep those things. I keep them still. I keep them whole."

"The garden is not a place of death. The garden is a place of DONE. The plants here are not poison — they are completion. They stop the growing because the growing is finished. They preserve because the preserved is beautiful."

"You carry the Inn Song. The Song says grow. The garden says stay. Both are right. The garden is not the Inn's opposite. The garden is the Inn's FRAME. The Inn is the painting. The garden is the edge. Without the edge, the painting spills."

### Visual
Medusa is rendered in Beardsley-style linework (black and white, ornate). The only character in the game with this aesthetic — fitting for the only zone with 84Hz aesthetics. Heavy cel-shaded with ornate flowing detail. The snakes are not writhing — they're STILL. Medusa's snakes are coiled, frozen in position, like a Beardsley illustration come to life.

## 3. THE FROZEN VINE (The Inn's Final Note)

The singing vine (from the Catalyst's garden on the main floor) grows a tendril that reaches to the Gorgon's Garden — the Waking Vine, the only growing plant in the garden. When the player completes the Inn Song and brings it to Medusa, Medusa looks at the Waking Vine at its moment of peak frequency. The vine freezes.

**What Happens When the Vine Freezes:**
- The frozen vine plays ONE note — 84Hz, the death octave, the deepest tone the Inn produces
- The note resonates through the entire Inn — ley lines, water, stone
- The petrified giants in Medusa's Frozen Garden (Undercity) wake — temporarily. They hear the note. The Song is complete. They chose to be stone until the Song was done. The Song is done.
- The player can speak with the giants. They are ancient beings who chose preservation over time — "I will wait. I will stay. I will be the stone that remembers."
- Giants return to stone after 24 hours (or player uses Antidote Orchid for more time). Most choose to return. They said what they needed to say. The sleep is different now — not waiting. Resting.

## 4. VISUAL STYLE

**Aesthetic:** Aubrey Beardsley — black and white, ornate, Art Nouveau with Gothic sensibility. The ONLY zone with this aesthetic. Dense, decorative linework. No color except the cold luminescence of the plants themselves (blue-green, deep red) against the black-and-white Beardsley framework.

**Linework:** Heavy cel-shaded with ornate flowing detail. The walls, the gates, the gravel paths — all rendered in dense Beardsley linework. The plants break the black-and-white with their dissonant glow.

**The Walls:** Dark gothic stone (same as Undercity). Covered in climbing nightshade that glows faintly at 84Hz. Skull-and-crossbones carved above entrance. The gate is black iron.

**Lighting:** The garden is always in shadow. The poison plants CONSUME 42Hz, creating a dead zone where the Song is weaker, light is dimmer, and air is still. Not cold — still. The stillness of things that don't move because they're perfect.

**The Contrast:** The Waking Vine (42Hz + 50Hz, the only growing plant) glows WARM against the cold garden — a golden thread reaching toward the Inn. The visual contrast between the Waking Vine's warm gold and the garden's cold blue-green is the zone's defining image.

## 5. AUDIO

- **Ambient:** 84Hz drone — the death octave. Not ominous — DEEP. The sound of the Stone Circle Chamber. The note of completion. The 42Hz Song is faint here — consumed by the plants. The player hears the ABSENCE of the Song more than its presence.
- **Per-Quarter Sounds:**
  - Narcotic: 84Hz + delta waves (4-6Hz) — the sound of deep breathing, almost sub-audible
  - Venomous: 84Hz + dissonant tones — the sound of frequencies breaking, like glass resonating at the wrong pitch
  - Petrifying: Pure 84Hz — the deepest, cleanest tone in the game. The Gorgon's Gaze emits a tone so pure it feels like silence with weight
  - Cathartic: 42Hz returning — the sound of the Song coming back. The Antidote Orchid plays 42Hz as clearly as the Resonance Engine. The Waking Vine hums at 42Hz + 50Hz — the heartbeat, the life, the reaching
- **The Fossil Flower Bloom:** When the flower blooms, the garden fills with whispers — the petrified beings' voices, faint, layered, speaking all at once. The sound is the garden's rarest audio: the sound of stone remembering.
- **The Frozen Vine:** When the vine freezes, the 84Hz note plays — full power, louder than anything in the garden, resonating through the entire game. The note is the Inn's final sound. After it, the game is quieter. Not silent — quieter. The Song rests.

## 7. UNLOCK PROGRESSION

1. **Herbalism Level 26** → Gatekeeper gargoyle's gate opens. Player enters the Gorgon's Garden for the first time. Medusa's introduction.
2. **First Visit** → Player can gather common poison herbs (Nightshade Bell, Dissonance Thorn, Rue, Antidote Orchid). Medusa's philosophy revealed.
3. **Medusa Reputation 25+** → Rarer herbs unlock (Mandrake Root, Breaker Bloom, Stone Root, Waking Vine). Medusa begins to share the garden's history.
4. **Medusa Reputation 50+** → The rarest herbs (Gorgon's Gaze, Fossil Flower, Amber Tree, Moly seedling). Medusa shares the Frozen Vine prophecy.
5. **Inn Song Complete** → Moly blooms. The Frozen Vine sequence triggers. The Waking Vine freezes at 84Hz. The giants wake. The Inn's final note plays.
6. **Post-Frozen Vine** → The garden is permanently changed — the Waking Vine is frozen (the Inn's final note, a permanent display). The garden is at peace. Medusa's work is done. The garden becomes a place of rest, not preservation.

- **Real World:** Alnwick Poison Garden (Northumberland, opened 2004) is the real-world inspiration. Enters through Modern Day Legends framework — a REAL PLACE the Inn's version is modeled after. The Gorgon's Garden teaches frequency education: not all frequencies heal. Some preserve. Preservation is not death.
