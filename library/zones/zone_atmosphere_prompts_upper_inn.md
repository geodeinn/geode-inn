## ZONE ATMOSPHERE & BACKGROUND ANIMATION PROMPTS

### Zone 1: Cosmology Atmosphere & Background
- **Subject Name:** Cosmology Zone Background — The Canopy Observatory & Celestial Sphere
- **Visual Description:** A vast, open-air stone observatory dome perched at the highest point of the Great Tree above the clouds. An ancient brass telescope trained on the cosmos sits atop carved star charts embedded into deep navy floor stone, surrounded by floating star charts and hidden frequency doodles. Deep blue-black space stretches infinitely, dotted with slowly rotating constellation rings and shimmering starlight.
- **Linework Style:** **No-outline (Cosmological Style)**. All architectural elements, star charts, and atmospheric clouds are rendered strictly without line ink. Form is established through volumetric starlight, ambient edge glow, and soft depth-of-field gradients.
- **Color Palette:** Deep cosmic blue-black (`#070A14`), midnight violet (`#120D2C`), celestial gold (`#E5C158`), starlight silver (`#D8ECEF`), ambient auroral teal (`#1D4E5B`).
- **Lighting/Atmosphere:** Multi-source celestial lighting. Cold silver starlight washes down from above, balanced by warm golden light radiating gently from the garden threshold and shifting dual-tone rim light around the celestial sphere. Quiet, reverent, vast.
- **Animation Notes:** 
  - *Idle Loop (12s seamless):* Distant star fields slowly rotate in counter-clockwise motion (0.5 deg/sec). Floating star chart scrolls bob on a subtle vertical sine wave (2s cycle).
  - *Environmental Tells:* Nebular dust drifting across the observatory floor in soft light tendrils. Subtle shimmering glints along the brass telescope barrel every 4 seconds.
- **Sound Connection:** Resonates with a low, ambient **42Hz Source Tone** hum. Soft celestial resonance plays whenever star constellations align.
- **Special Notes:** Houses Bramble's hidden frequency doodles on the floor tiles. Serves as the visual peak of the vertical axis in the Inn's emotional arc (Refuge → Home → World → Self → Cosmos).

### Zone 2: Clockwork Skies Atmosphere & Background
- **Subject Name:** Clockwork Skies Background — The Arcana Airship Deck & Skyways
- **Visual Description:** The wide timber deck of *The Arcana* airship sailing high above the cloud layer under a brilliant sky. Canvas sails billow against brass rigging, copper gears turn along the ship's railing, and massive exhaust vents pulse with steam. In the far background, the towering spires of Canopy City loom through sea-foam cloudbanks.
- **Linework Style:** **Heavy Cel-Shaded (Tradesman/Mechanical Style)**. Solid, crisp black ink outlines around structural wood, iron rivets, brass cogs, and deck architecture to emphasize tactile engineering.
- **Color Palette:** Sky-blue (`#4A90E2`), cloud cream (`#F5F5F0`), polished brass (`#D4AF37`), dark copper (`#8B4513`), slate iron (`#3A3D40`).
- **Lighting/Atmosphere:** High-altitude daylight with hard metallic specularity. Direct warm sunlight casts sharp, clean shadows across the deck, reflecting brilliantly off brass gearwork and polished cloud vapor.
- **Animation Notes:**
  - *Idle Loop (8s seamless):* Deck sways gently on a rocking roll axis (1.5 degrees, 4s cycle). Cloudbanks drift past in parallax layers at varying speeds.
  - *Mechanical Motion:* Brass cogs and steam pistons stroke in perfect sync with a 50 BPM tempo. Sails billow dynamically with variable wind gusts; periodic puffs of white steam vent from the starboard engine pipe.
- **Sound Connection:** Driven by the **50 BPM Heartbeat Engine Rhythm**. Rhythmic mechanical clinks and wind gusts swell when near the helm or engine hatch.
- **Special Notes:** Background context for the "All Hands" crew management game. Represents the outward "World" phase of the Inn's progression arc.

### Zone 3: Main Floor Atmosphere & Background
- **Subject Name:** Main Floor Background — The Hearth & Central Tavern Hall
- **Visual Description:** The warm, heart-center of the Geode Inn featuring massive dark oak timber beams, polished wooden tables, a glowing stone hearth, and bustling tavern corners. The room hosts the Arcade games, the Market stalls, the Weeaboo's bar, the Innkeeper's List quest board, and a cozy geode nook resting beside the fireplace.
- **Linework Style:** **Heavy Cel-Shaded (Cozy Tradesman Style)**. Thick, warm ink outlines define the wooden beams, heavy furniture, and stone fireplace, instilling a feeling of sturdy, physical comfort.
- **Color Palette:** Hearth Amber (`#FFBF00`), Firelight Red (`#990000`), Dark Oak (`#2A1810`), Polished Brass (`#C5A059`), Warm Cream (`#FFFDD0`).
- **Lighting/Atmosphere:** Extremely cozy, warm, and inviting. Primary illumination stems from the flickering stone fireplace, casting rich amber and golden dancing shadows across the wood floor and ceiling beams.
- **Animation Notes:**
  - *Idle Loop (6s seamless):* Fireplace flames flicker naturally with organic frame-by-frame shape shifts. Dust motes dance softly in warm light shafts filtering through high stained-glass windows.
  - *Ambient Activity:* Distant tavern shadow silhouettes subtly shift; glowing embers rise slowly up the stone chimney flue.
- **Sound Connection:** Blends the **42Hz vertical tone** with the **50Hz horizontal pulse**, generating an ambient **8Hz Theta frequency** atmosphere (dreamy, comforting, relaxing tavern murmur).
- **Special Notes:** The emotional foundation ("Home") of the entire Geode Inn experience.
