# THE GEODE INN — THE CAVE CITY ZONE

## ZONE OVERVIEW

**Name:** The Cave City (the Crimean Goths call it "the Last Light")

**What it is:** The building system's synthesis tier — a zone where all four architectural tiers (shelter, castle, sacred, mausoleum) collapse into one. The player enters a cave complex carved INTO the cliff faces and plateau tops of the Inn's mountainous interior. The zone is simultaneously a home, a fortress, a church, and a tomb. The zone IS the Subtractive Family's thesis made playable: the architecture is the geology. The building is the rock. The both are the same.

**Where it sits:** The Cave City occupies the deep geological layer between the Undercity (the dead's domain beneath the roots) and the Cultural Zones (the living's platforms in the canopy). It is the MIDDLE — the zone that proves the Inn's vertical axis is not a hierarchy (better at top, worse at bottom) but a spectrum: the same stone, from root to canopy, expressing different functions at different depths. The Cave City is the depth where function and stone become indistinguishable.

**Access:** Three entry points, each requiring a different key:

1. **From the Canopy City (Top Entry):** A platform on the Neutral Platform leads to a staircase descending into the mountain. The staircase is carved — the steps ARE the rock, descending in a spiral that tightens as it goes deeper. The player who has completed the Redwood Cathedral (climbed the tallest redwood, sat in the Queen of Roots' throne) finds the staircase's entrance exposed — the root system of the ancient redwood has cracked the plateau stone, revealing the carved steps beneath. The tree's roots opened the door. The door was always there.

2. **From the Undercity (Bottom Entry):** A tunnel from Belladonna' domain leads UPWARD into the cave city. Belladonna knows the way — "the dead use it as a shortcut to the surface. Don't mind them. They're polite." The tunnel emerges in the cave city's mausoleum tier. The player who enters from below arrives in the city of the dead first — the deepest layer, the most subtractive, the most adequate.

3. **From the World Map (The Grand Canyon portal):** A world map pin at the Grand Canyon zone (National Parks Veil) leads to the Subtractive Family's deepest expression — the hidden temple in the canyon wall. The temple connects to the Cave City through a network of carved corridors that follow geological strata. The player who finds the Grand Canyon temple can travel from the canyon to the Crimean cave city through the rock itself — the journey is THROUGH the stone, not over it.

**Zone size:** Comparable to a mid-size MMO dungeon — not a single room but a network. The cave city has six distinct districts, each carved at a different depth, each serving a different function. The player can spend hours exploring, building, and uncovering the city's history. The zone is not linear — the player enters, explores, returns to the main floor, comes back with new tools, goes deeper. The zone is a hub-and-spoke design: a central chamber with corridors branching to districts, each district requiring different tools or knowledge to fully access.

## THE SUBTRACTIVE BUILDING MECHANIC

### How It Works

The Subtractive building system is the Cave City zone's unique mechanic — a fundamentally different building mode from the additive system used in every other zone.

**Additive building (every other zone):** The player gathers materials (wood, stone, thatch, metal) and PLACES them. The player selects a location, selects a material, and the material appears — stacked, arranged, constructed. The building is: added to the world. The world has more after the building than before.

**Subtractive building (Cave City):** The player selects a section of rock and REMOVES material. The player doesn't gather — the player carves. The building is: subtracted from the world. The world has LESS after the building than before. The less is: the adequate. The adequate is: the Subtractive building creates space by removing mass. The space is: the architecture. The architecture is: the absence.

**The mechanic:**

1. The player approaches a raw rock wall (uncarved limestone, designated by subtle visual cues — the raw rock is rougher, darker, without the smooth polish of carved surfaces).

2. The player selects the "Carve" tool (available only in the Cave City and the Grand Canyon zone).

3. The player selects a SHAPE — the carving patterns they've learned. Initially, the player knows only basic shapes: sleeping hollow, bench, niche. As the player learns from the Mason in the Sacred District, more complex shapes become available: columns, arches, doorways, resonance chambers.

4. The player applies the carving tool to the rock. The carving takes TIME — not instant. The player watches the material being removed, chip by chip, the shape emerging from the stone. The emerging is: the Michelangelo principle — the shape is already in the stone, the carving reveals it.

5. The carved space is: the building. The building is: the negative space. The negative space is: the adequate.

**Material properties matter:** Different rock types carve differently:

| Rock Type | Location | Carving Speed | Resonance | Notes |
|-----------|----------|--------------|-----------|-------|
| Limestone | Cave City (Crimean Gothic) | Medium | 111Hz | The default carving stone. Absorbs sound. Warm color. |
| Volcanic Tuff | Lalibela connection | Fast | Variable | Soft enough to carve with hand tools. Hardens on exposure to air. |
| Sandstone | Grand Canyon | Medium | 42Hz | The river-carved stone. Reddish. Rings when struck. |
| Basalt | Ajanta/Ellora connection | Very Slow | Deep hum | The hardest carving stone. The Kailasa temple's material. |
| Marble | Matera connection | Slow | High ring | The most resonant. The most beautiful. The most difficult. |

The player who has carved in multiple stone types understands: the Subtractive Family is not one civilization — it's a PRINCIPLE expressed through different geologies. The principle is: the architecture is the rock. The rock is: different in each place. The different is: the adequate. The adequate is: each Subtractive zone has its own resonance, its own color, its own carving feel. The feel is: the geology. The geology is: the adequate.
