# Geode Inn — THE WORLD GRID
## Open World Exploration + Passive Gathering System

---

## CONCEPT

The World Grid is the explorable open space surrounding the Inn — the terrain the player travels through between cultural zones. Where the Silk Road caravan game is the structured expedition (route management, encounters, objectives), the World Grid is the UNSTRUCTURED exploration — the player walking, riding, or wandering through a living world with no objective except gathering.

**Design Model:** Darnassus (World of Warcraft) — a beautiful, explorable world with ambient activities. Mostly open space. No combat (in the safe zones). No pressure. No timers. The player explores at their own pace, harvesting resources that feed back into the Inn's crafting systems. The World Grid is the game's most relaxing space — the 42Hz frequency plays softly, the ley lines glow in the distance, and the world is full of stones, plants, and water that the player can gather just by being present.

**The Core Loop:**
- OUT — travel the World Grid, gather resources along ley lines
- IN — return to the Inn, use gathered resources in crafting (Bead Board, alchemy, cooking)
- OUT — charge stones and crafted items by traveling ley lines again
- IN — use charged items in games, quests, and compositions

The gathering is the "out." The crafting is the "in." The World Grid makes "out" a place, not a menu.

### The Silk Road Routes on the Grid

The Silk Road routes are the PAVED PATHS through the World Grid. The player can walk the routes (structured travel — faster, with signposts and waypoints) or wander off-path (unstructured exploration — slower, but with hidden gathering nodes and secret locations).

**On the route:** Traders, waypoints, rest stops, other travelers (NPCs). The route is the SAFE path — no hazards, no getting lost. The route follows the ley line, so stones charge while walking.

**Off the route:** Wild terrain, hidden veins, rare plants, undocumented water sources. The player can explore freely but there's no path — navigation by Ley Line Vision (the ley lines are the only visible landmarks in wild terrain). Off-path exploration is where the rarest resources are found.

### The Inn's Position

The Inn (the tree) is at the grid's center. The player can see the Inn from anywhere on the grid — the tree's canopy is always visible on the horizon, glowing softly with the 42Hz frequency. The Inn is the compass. The player who is lost can always find their way back by walking toward the tree.

The Canopy City (Clockwork Skies) is the grid's HUB — the platform city in the branches. The player descends from the Canopy City to any region through the cultural platforms. The player ascends back to the Canopy City through any cultural platform (fast travel) or by walking back to the Inn's base and climbing the Staircase (the long way).

## THE THREE GATHERING PROFESSIONS

### 1. Mining (Stone Harvesting)

**The Concept:** Stones are the Geode Inn's core resource. The player already earns stones from games, but mining is the PASSIVE way to collect — finding veins, deposits, and crystal formations in the World Grid's terrain.

**How It Works:**
- Mineral veins appear in the World Grid's terrain — visible as outcroppings, color shifts in the rock, or crystal formations
- The player approaches a vein and uses a **Tuning Pick** (the mining tool — a crystal tuning fork that resonates with the stone's frequency to extract it cleanly, without fracturing)
- The mini-interaction is a brief frequency-match: the player holds the Tuning Pick near the vein, the vein's frequency plays, and the player matches it. A clean match extracts the stone. A poor match extracts a fragment (lower quality)
- Mining takes 3-5 seconds per stone. No failure — poor matches just yield fragments instead of whole stones

**Stone Quality:**
- **Raw** (from mining) — 0% charge, base frequency. The stone's natural state.
- **Vein-charged** (from mining at a ley line node) — 10-30% charge. The vein has been absorbing ley line energy for centuries.
- **Fragments** (from poor matches) — usable in the Bead Board but with weaker frequency. Fragments can be combined at the Alchemist's Scale (Staircase) to form whole stones.

**Regional Stones:**
Each region has its own mineral veins. The player who wants a specific stone must travel to the region where it forms:
- Lapis lazuli → The Desert (Egyptian region) and The Ziggurat Plains (Afghanistan border)
- Jade → The Bamboo Forests (East Asian region)
- Rhodochrosite → The Andean Slopes
- Opal → The Songline Desert (Australian region)
- Preseli Blue Stone → The Moorlands (Celtic region)

**Mining Skill Progression:**
- **Level 1-10:** Basic veins (common stones — quartz, agate, calcite). Surface deposits.
- **Level 11-25:** Uncommon veins (regional specialties). Deeper deposits, requiring exploration off-path.
- **Level 26-50:** Rare veins (stones that only form at ley line nodes — charged on extraction). Requires Ley Line Vision to find.
- **Level 51-75:** Crystal caves (small explorable caves in the World Grid's terrain — mini-dungeons with concentrated mineral deposits). Requires a charged stone to enter (the cave's entrance resonates with the stone's frequency).
- **Level 76-100:** Hollow Earth minerals (stones that only form in the Hollow Earth's unique conditions — accessible through the World Grid's deepest caves, which connect to the Dark Reaches). Requires Deep Pearl charm.

**Visual:** The Tuning Pick glows when near a vein. The vein's color hints at the stone type. When the player matches the frequency, the stone lifts out of the rock face with a soft chime — the stone's frequency, played cleanly for the first time. The moment is SATISFYING — the sound design is the reward.

### 2. Herbalism (Plant Harvesting)

**The Concept:** The mycorrhizal network is the biological ley line system. The World Grid's plants grow on this network. Herbalism is the practice of harvesting plants that carry the 42Hz frequency through the biological conductor system.

**How It Works:**
- Plants appear throughout the World Grid — visible as glowing spots (the 42Hz frequency makes the plants luminesce faintly, visible to the player with Ley Line Vision)
- The player approaches a plant and uses a **Gathering Sickle** (or simply gathers by hand for common plants)
- The gathering interaction is a brief "listen" — the plant's frequency plays, and the player harvests. No mini-game — herbalism is the CALMEST gathering profession. The player just... picks plants. The calm is the point.
- Plants at ley line nodes have BRIGHTER glow and STRONGER frequency — these are the valuable ones
- Some plants only grow at specific ley line nodes (rare herbs tied to specific sacred sites)

**Plant Uses:**

| Plant Category | Use | System Connection |
|---|---|---|
| **Frequency Herbs** | Enhance stone frequencies when combined at the Alchemist's Scale | Staircase zone — alchemy |
| **Resonance Flowers** | Decorative elements for the Bead Board — flowers woven into bead arrangements add frequency harmonics | Bead Board / Campgrounds — Solstara's shop |
| **Catalyst Ingredients** | The Catalyst's drinks — beverages that enhance perception (hearing faint frequencies, seeing hidden nodes) | The Catalyst (Staircase NPC) |
| **Cooking Ingredients** | Jay's tavern — food that provides exploration buffs (speed, frequency sensitivity, ley line detection) | Campgrounds — Jay's tavern |
| **Medicinal Herbs** | Healing items for the Hollow Earth RPG (the only zone with combat — medicinal herbs restore health) | Hollow Earth — combat preparation |
| **Ritual Plants** | Used in ritual quests (druidic ceremonies, seasonal events, solstice celebrations) | Celtic/Norse zones — seasonal events |
| **Dye Plants** | Color sources for the Bead Board's thread (the thread between beads can be dyed, adding a visual layer to compositions) | Bead Board — aesthetic customization |

**Regional Plants:**
Each region has unique flora tied to its culture and mythology:
- The Moorlands: Heather (purple, 42Hz harmonics), mistletoe (ritual plant, druidic), rowan (protective, wards off the Wild in the Hollow Earth)
- The Fjords: Cloudberries (golden, aurora-charged), juniper (cleansing, used in the Blacksmith's forge), angelica (frequency amplifier)
- The Desert: Blue lotus (Egyptian ritual, enhances dream-states — connects to the 8Hz theta frequency), papyrus (writing material — the player can write notes on papyrus in the Catacombs), frankincense (frequency resin, burned at sacred sites to amplify ley line energy)
- The Bamboo Forests: Ginseng (rare, deep-rooted — connects to the mycorrhizal network directly), lotus (water plant, 42Hz water conductor), chrysanthemum (autumn seasonal, used in the Inn's equinox events)
- The Songline Desert: Eucalyptus (the tallest — connects the surface to the canopy, the mycorrhizal network's primary conductor in Australia), wattle (golden flowers, ley line markers — wattles grow ONLY on ley lines)

**Herbalism Skill Progression:**
- **Level 1-10:** Common plants (surface growth, visible without Ley Line Vision). Simple picks.
- **Level 11-25:** Uncommon plants (regional specialties, require travel). Slightly hidden — require Ley Line Vision to spot.
- **Level 26-50:** Rare herbs (only at ley line nodes). The plants glow brighter — the player can see them from a distance with Ley Line Vision.
- **Level 51-75:** Mycorrhizal harvesting (the player can harvest from the underground fungal network directly — digging at the base of ancient trees to access the root system). Requires a crystal trowel and knowledge of the network's patterns.
- **Level 76-100:** Hollow Earth flora (the crystal gardens' exotic plants — bioluminescent, growing on crystal instead of soil. The rarest herbs in the game, used in the most powerful alchemy recipes).

**Visual:** Plants glow softly at 42Hz. When the player harvests, the plant's frequency plays as a soft chime. The plant regrows over time (real-time — 24 hours for common plants, 3-7 days for rare ones). The World Grid is a LIVING world — plants grow, seasons change, and the landscape evolves.

### 3. Fishing (Water Harvesting)

**The Concept:** Water is the 42Hz frequency's primary conductor. The World Grid's water bodies — rivers, lakes, springs, oceans — carry the frequency through the world. Fishing is the practice of harvesting the WATER's resources: fish, pearls, aquatic crystals, and deep-water minerals.

**How It Works:**
- The player approaches any water body in the World Grid and uses a **Frequency Rod** (the fishing tool — a crystal rod that resonates with the water's 42Hz, attracting fish that are drawn to the frequency)
- The fishing interaction is a WAIT — the player casts the line and waits. The 42Hz frequency plays softly. Fish are attracted to the frequency. When a fish bites, the player feels a vibration (haptic on mobile, visual ripple on desktop) and reels in
- The wait is the mechanic. Fishing is the SLOWEST gathering profession. The player who fishes is choosing to be still. The Darnassus vibe — sitting by a river, watching the water, listening to the frequency. The calm is the design
- Fish in ley line-charged water (rivers that follow ley lines) are more valuable — they carry stronger frequencies
- Rare fish from the underground ocean (accessible with Deep Pearl charm) are the most valuable

**Catch Types:**

| Catch | Source | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Common Fish** | Surface water (rivers, lakes) | Cooking (Jay's tavern — fish dishes provide exploration buffs) |
| **Ley Line Fish** | Ley line rivers (the Nile, the Ganges, fjord waters) | Cooking + alchemy (stronger buffs, frequency enhancement) |
| **Pearls** | Oysters (coastal waters, oasis pools) | Bead Board (pearls are universal frequency matches — the Deep One pearls' surface equivalent) |
| **Aquatic Crystals** | Deep water (crystal-clear pools, underground springs) | Bead Board (rare aquatic stones — form in water, not rock) |
| **Deep Water Fish** | Underground ocean (Hollow Earth access) | Trading with the Deep Ones + the most powerful cooking buffs |
| **The 42Hz Carp** | The Source (Water Zone Layer 7 — only accessible through the deep tide passages) | The rarest fish. Pure 42Hz. When placed on the Bead Board, the carp doesn't add a frequency — it amplifies every other stone's frequency by 10% |

**Fishing Skill Progression:**
- **Level 1-10:** Surface fishing (rivers, lakes — common fish). Any water body.
- **Level 11-25:** Deep fishing (clear pools, springs — uncommon fish, occasional pearls). Requires patience (longer waits for better catches).
- **Level 26-50:** Ley line fishing (rivers that follow ley lines — ley line fish, aquatic crystals). Requires the Frequency Rod (the basic rod only catches surface fish; the Frequency Rod attracts ley line-charged fish).
- **Level 51-75:** Underground fishing (caves, underground springs — rare fish, deep crystals). Requires exploration of the World Grid's cave systems.
- **Level 76-100:** Hollow Earth fishing (the underground ocean — deep water fish, the rarest catches). Requires Deep Pearl charm + the Source Creatures' trust.

**Visual:** The water shimmers at 42Hz. When the player casts, the line glows faintly. The wait is accompanied by the water's ambient sound — the 42Hz drone, filtered through water. When a fish bites, the water erupts in a small splash of bioluminescence. The catch plays its frequency as it's reeled in — each fish has a unique tone, like each stone has a frequency. The fish ARE the water's frequencies, expressed as life.

## THE WORLD GRID'S LIVING SYSTEMS

### Day/Night Cycle
The World Grid has a day/night cycle (the Inn's time, not real-world time). The cycle affects:
- **Day:** Plants are easier to spot (natural light). Mining veins are visible. Fish are less active (deeper water).
- **Night:** Plants glow brighter (the 42Hz luminescence is visible without Ley Line Vision). Mining veins are harder to spot. Fish are more active (surface feeding). The ley lines are MOST visible at night — the golden threads glow against the dark terrain.
- **Dawn/Dusk:** The transition — the best time for rare gathering. Dawn fish (surface feeders catching the first light) and dusk plants (closing flowers, concentrating their frequency).

### Seasons
The World Grid has four seasons (each lasting 2 real-world weeks — a month-long cycle). Seasons affect:
- **Spring:** Plants bloom (best time for herbalism). Rivers swell (best time for fishing). Mining is normal.
- **Summer:** Plants are mature (highest frequency yields). Rivers are warm (fish go deep — harder to catch). Mining veins are exposed (less vegetation covering rock faces — best time for mining).
- **Autumn:** Plants change color (aesthetic — the World Grid is most beautiful in autumn). Fish are feeding for winter (best variety). Mining is normal.
- **Winter:** Plants are dormant (herbs are rare — the underground mycorrhizal network is the only source). Rivers freeze (ice fishing — a special mechanic). Mining veins are exposed (best time — no vegetation, frozen ground makes extraction easier).

### Weather
Random weather events affect gathering:
- **Ley Line Storms:** Rare. A ley line surges — all nodes on that line glow brightly. All plants on the line bloom. All fish in ley line rivers surface. The storm lasts 5 minutes. The most productive gathering window in the game. Players who are online during a storm rush to the affected ley line.
- **Fog:** Reduces visibility. Plants are harder to spot. But fog concentrates the 42Hz frequency — the drone is louder, clearer. Fog is the best time for FREQUENCY-SENSITIVE gathering (the player can hear nodes before seeing them).
- **Rain:** Boosts fishing (fish are more active in rain). Washes away loose stones (mining is slightly worse). Boosts plant growth (herbalism is slightly better after rain).

## THE WORLD GRID'S CONNECTIONS

**To the Silk Road:** The World Grid IS the Silk Road's terrain. The routes are the paved paths. The gathering is the reason to explore off-path. The caravan game is the structured expedition; the World Grid is the free exploration.

**To the Inn:** The player returns to the Inn to craft. The Inn is the grid's center — always visible, always accessible. The "in to create" phase happens in the Inn's crafting systems (Bead Board, alchemy, cooking).

**To the Stone Entity:** Mining produces stones that are added to the player's Stone collection. Mined stones are RAW (0% charge) — they need ley line travel to charge. This creates a loop: mine → travel ley lines → charge → return to Inn → compose on Bead Board → go back out to mine more.

**To the Bead Board:** Gathered plants (resonance flowers, dye plants) and aquatic resources (pearls, aquatic crystals) can be used in Bead Board compositions. The Bead Board isn't just stones — it's stones + flowers + pearls + crystals, all carrying frequencies.

**To the Alchemist's Scale (Staircase):** Frequency herbs and catalyst ingredients feed the alchemy system. Potions, elixirs, and transmutations require gathered materials.

**To Jay's Tavern (Campgrounds):** Fish and cooking ingredients feed the cooking system. Food provides exploration buffs that enhance gathering effectiveness.

**To the Hollow Earth:** The World Grid's deepest caves connect to the Dark Reaches. The player who mines deep enough in the World Grid may break through to the Hollow Earth's underbelly. The Hollow Earth's crystal gardens are the source of the rarest gathering materials.

**To the Water Zone:** The World Grid's water bodies connect to the Water Zone. Deep fishing in the World Grid's underground springs can yield Source Creatures and underground ocean fish. The Water Zone and the World Grid share the same water.

**To the External Zone (National Parks):** The Outer Ring of the World Grid IS the External Zone. The 12 national parks are the grid's outermost regions — the wildest terrain, the rarest resources, the most dangerous gathering (weather, terrain hazards, limited access).

**To the World Map:** The World Map is the World Grid's TOP-DOWN VIEW. The map shows the grid's regions, ley lines, and nodes. The player can fast-travel from the map to any discovered location on the grid. The map is the navigation tool; the grid is the exploration space.

## VISUAL LANGUAGE

**Linework:** Soft flowing (lore style) for the World Grid's natural elements — terrain, plants, water, sky. The World Grid is NATURE, not architecture. No heavy cel-shading (that's for tradesmen/NPCs). No no-outline (that's for cosmological/divine). The World Grid is the LORE layer made explorable.

**Color Palette:**
- Base: natural greens, browns, blues — the Earth's surface
- Ley lines: golden amber (#E5C158) — the energy grid, visible with Ley Line Vision
- Charged nodes: bright white-gold (#FFD700)
- Plants: soft 42Hz luminescence (faint white-blue glow)
- Water: blue-teal, with gold tinge on ley line rivers
- The Inn's canopy: warm amber glow on the horizon — the tree, always visible, always home

**Sound:** The 42Hz frequency, soft and ambient, throughout the World Grid. The drone varies by region — stronger near ley lines, weaker in wild terrain. Each region adds its own ambient layer: wind in the Moorlands, sand in the Desert, water in the Monsoon Coast, birds in the Bamboo Forests. The World Grid is the game's most AMBIENT space — the sound design is relaxation, not tension.
