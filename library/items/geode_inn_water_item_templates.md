# Geode Inn — Interactive Item Templates: Water Zone
## Based on Andra's Cove (Architectural Template)

### Design Philosophy
The Water Zone is alive. Where the Hearth is fire and the Undercity is stone, the Water Zone is a living organism. The light breathes. The walls are wet. The currents move everything. Andra's cove teaches players that water is not a barrier — it's a medium. You don't build IN the water. You build WITH it.

## Lighting System (Water)
No fire. No crafted light. All light is biological or geological. The light is alive.

| Light Type | Source | Color | Range | Behavior |
|------------|--------|-------|-------|----------|
| Bioluminescent Colony | Organisms on walls | Blue, green, purple, pale gold | Medium (4-6 blocks) | Pulsing. Living. Heartsbeat-rhythm. Reproduces and spreads slowly. |
| Phosphorescent Current | Light ribbons in water | Blue-white, shifting | Variable (flows) | Moving. Currents carry glowing ribbons through the space. Never static. |
| Thermal Vent Glow | Deep floor vents | Deep red-orange | Short (2-3 blocks) | Constant. Warm. Geological. The only warm light source. |
| Surface Shaft | Crack in ceiling | Pale silver-gold | Narrow column | Distant. Dim. Moves with surface conditions above. The only "sunlight" — barely. |
| Pearl Glow | Certain rare pearls | Soft white-gold | Very short (1 block) | Constant. Held or placed. Worn by the Siren. |
| Coral Fluorescence | Certain coral types | Green, pink, blue | Short (2-3 blocks) | Only visible in near-darkness. UV-reactive. Living. Grows. |

### Light Interaction Rule
Bioluminescence + current = the light moves through the space in flowing ribbons. Bioluminescence + pearl = the pearl amplifies and focuses the glow into a steady point. Thermal glow + water = the red light diffuses upward in warm columns. Surface shaft + water = the pale light refracts into a shifting, dancing pattern on the floor. The Water Zone's light is never still. If you want steady light, you hold a pearl. Everything else breathes.

## Furniture Templates (Water)

### 1. Kelp-Woven Sleeping Hollow
**Based on:** Andra's bed alcove
**Description:** A concave depression carved into smooth tidal stone, lined with soft kelp and sea grass, draped with living seaweed curtains that sway with the current.
**Materials:** Tidal stone (structure), kelp and sea grass (lining), seaweed (curtains)
**Variants:**
- Shallow hollow (single occupant, Andra's style — a nest in the stone)
- Deep hollow (recessed, insulated by water flow)
- Shared hollow (wide, multiple depressions — family sleeping)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Player can rest here (longer rest in water — the body floats)
- Kelp and sea grass regrow over time (living bedding)
- Seaweed curtains provide privacy that moves — never fully closed, always swaying
- Temperature depends on depth (deeper = colder, closer to vents = warmer)
- Current strength affects sleep quality (gentle current = best rest)

### 2. Floating Driftwood Table
**Based on:** Andra's low table
**Description:** A low table made of waterlogged driftwood, weighted with stones so it floats at a stable height just below the water surface. Items placed on it sit at water level.
**Materials:** Driftwood, stone weights
**Variants:**
- Low (sits just below surface — the standard)
- Anchored (weighted to the floor — stable, doesn't float)
- Drifting (unweighted — actually floats and moves with current — decorative only)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Items placed on it may drift if current is strong (anchored variant prevents this)
- Surface is always wet — items must be water-resistant or in sealed containers
- Multiple players can gather around it (sitting on the floor or floating)
- Drifting variant: items placed on it slowly travel around the room — some players use this for meditation

### 3. Abalone Mirror
**Based on:** Andra's small mirror
**Description:** A mirror made of polished abalone shell, set in a coral or stone frame. The reflection is iridescent — colors shift depending on angle.
**Materials:** Abalone, coral or stone frame
**Variants:**
- Hand (small, portable, for close work — the jeweler's tool)
- Wall-mounted (medium, framed in coral)
- Full (rare, made from a massive abalone — reflects the whole room in shifting color)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Reflection shifts colors (abalone iridescence) — never a true reflection, always slightly other
- Can be used for jewelry work (the shifting light helps identify inclusions)
- Full variant: the reflection sometimes shows the room as it was, not as it is (rare, quest-linked)
- Sound: tapping the abalone produces a faint, resonant note (the sonic system — shells have frequencies)

### 4. Shell Shelf
**Based on:** Andra's collection shelf
**Description:** Natural stone shelves where shells, pearls, and found objects are displayed. Shelves are carved into the wall or built from coral growth.
**Materials:** Tidal stone or coral
**Variants:**
- Wall-carved (permanent, smooth)
- Coral-grown (living shelf that grows and branches over time)
- Driftwood (floating shelf, anchored with stone)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Items placed here are visible through the water (refraction distorts slightly)
- Coral-grown shelves expand over time (more space as the coral grows)
- Water-resistant items only (paper, leather, cloth degrade unless sealed)
- Shells placed here resonate with the sonic system (each shell has a frequency)

### 5. Pearl String
**Based on:** Andra's ceiling decorations
**Description:** Strings of pearls and crystals hanging from the ceiling, swaying gently with the current.
**Materials:** Pearl, crystal, kelp cord
**Variants:**
- Pearl only (soft white-gold glow — gentle ambient light)
- Crystal only (sonic resonance — chimes when current moves them)
- Mixed (both glow and sound — the Siren's chandelier)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Sways with current (visual indicator of water flow direction and strength)
- Crystal variant produces soft chimes (audible underwater — the sound travels far)
- Pearl variant provides steady light in an otherwise shifting environment
- Mixed variant: the chimes and the glow pulse together — light and sound synchronized
- Players can add their own found pearls and crystals (the string grows)

### 6. Water Jewel Workstation
**Based on:** Andra's small anvil and tools
**Description:** A jeweler's workstation adapted for underwater use. The anvil is coral-shaped, the tools are bone and shell, the work surface is abalone.
**Materials:** Coral (anvil), bone and shell (tools), abalone (surface), stone (base)
**Variants:**
- Siren's Station (full setup — anvil, tools, surface, storage — Andra's level)
- Travel Kit (portable — bone tools and abalone surface only)
- Community Station (shared, larger, multiple work positions)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Player can craft jewelry here (water-adapted crafting)
- Certain stones can only be worked underwater (water-sensitive minerals)
- The sonic system is enhanced underwater — stone frequencies travel further and clearer
- Crafting here produces items with "Siren-touched" quality (slight bonus to sonic properties)
- Community Station: multiple players can craft simultaneously (shared knowledge bonus)

### 7. Current Channel
**Based on:** The natural water flow through the cove
**Description:** A shaped channel in the stone that directs water current through the living space. Not furniture — infrastructure. The current is the water zone's heating, cooling, ventilation, and transport system.
**Materials:** Tidal stone (channel walls), coral (flow directors)
**Variants:**
- Gentle (slow flow — meditation, rest, passive)
- Moderate (medium flow — standard living current, keeps space fresh)
- Swift (fast flow — travel channel, can carry player between rooms quickly)
- Thermal (directs vent-heated water — radiant floor heating for the water zone)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Controls water temperature in the space (thermal = warm, deep = cold)
- Controls water quality (fresh current = clean, stagnant = murky)
- Swift variant: player can ride the current between rooms (fast travel)
- Current direction can be changed by repositioning coral flow directors
- Current strength affects all other items (kelp sway, table drift, pearl chime frequency)

### 8. Coral Garden
**Based on:** The organic growth in the cove
**Description:** A living decoration that grows over time. Planted coral that spreads across walls, floors, and furniture. The Water Zone's answer to a garden.
**Materials:** Living coral (various types)
**Variants:**
- Wall Garden (coral growing on walls — provides color and bioluminescence)
- Floor Garden (coral growing on the floor — textured terrain, provides habitat)
- Frame Garden (coral growing on a structure — shapes a doorway, arch, or partition)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Grows over time (requires patience — the garden expands daily)
- Can be shaped (player prunes and directs growth)
- Provides additional bioluminescent light (fluorescent coral types)
- Attracts small aquatic life (fish, shrimp — ambient NPCs that add life to the space)
- Can be harvested for coral material (slows growth but provides crafting supply)

## Build Rules (Water)

1. **Water is the medium, not the obstacle.** Everything exists in water. Don't fight it — shape it. Buildings are carve-outs in the water, not dry spaces surrounded by it.
2. **Light is alive.** No fire, no crystal, no crafted light. All light is biological (bioluminescence) or geological (thermal vents). If the organisms die, the room goes dark.
3. **Nothing is sharp.** Water has smoothed everything. No right angles, no hard edges. All architecture is curved, flowing, organic. Sharp = broken, wrong, dangerous.
4. **Current is infrastructure.** The water current is the heating, ventilation, transport, and communication system. Direct it, shape it, use it. A room without current is a room without air.
5. **Things grow.** Coral grows. Kelp grows. Bioluminescent colonies spread. The Water Zone is the only zone where the building materials reproduce on their own. Patience is rewarded.
6. **Depth = temperature = status.** Shallow = warm, bright, public. Deep = cold, dark, private. Thermal vents = the rare warm spots in the deep. Building near a vent is premium real estate.
7. **Sound travels far.** The sonic system is amplified underwater. Stone frequencies carry further and clearer. The Water Zone is the best place to HEAR the Inn's music. Privacy requires distance, not walls.
8. **The Siren is the source.** Andra's cove is the deepest, oldest, most established home in the water zone. All water zone building knowledge flows from the Siren. The Siren is the template. The Siren is the water.

## Player Discovery Flow (Water)
1. Player discovers the entrance to the water zone (a submerged passage in the catacombs or a pool on the main floor)
2. Enters the water — first experience of submerged movement and breathing
3. The light is overwhelming — bioluminescence everywhere, shifting, alive, beautiful
4. Discovers Andra's cove (NPC home — the template example)
5. Observes the materials, lighting, furniture, and the way current shapes everything
6. Receives basic water build tools: tidal stone, kelp, basic bioluminescent starter colony
7. Builds a basic hollow — bare, dark, still water, no current
8. Discovers that without current, the bioluminescence doesn't spread (stagnant water = dying light)
9. Learns to shape current channels (coral flow directors) — the room comes alive
10. Discovers thermal vents while exploring deeper — claims a warm spot
11. Learns to grow coral gardens (living walls that provide their own light)
12. Learns to craft pearl strings and abalone mirrors (the Siren's craft)
13. Room transforms from dark still pool to living, glowing, flowing sanctuary
14. Other players discover the room by following the current — the water brought them

The water teaches by flow. The player builds by shaping, then growing.
