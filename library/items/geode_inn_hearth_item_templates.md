# Geode Inn — Interactive Item Templates: Hearth Zone
## Based on The Firekeeper's Hearth (Architectural Template)

### Design Philosophy
The Hearth is the Inn's communal heart. Where the Undercity is private and crafted, the Hearth is public and burning. Fire is the light source, warmth is the currency, and gathering is the purpose. The Firekeeper's area teaches players how to build spaces that draw people together.

## Lighting System (Hearth)
The Hearth is defined by fire — direct, alive, moving light. No crafted sterility. The light breathes.

| Light Type | Source | Color | Range | Behavior |
|------------|--------|-------|-------|----------|
| Hearth Fire | The great fireplace | Orange-gold-amber | Large (8-10 blocks) | Living. Flickers. Casts moving shadows. Heat source. |
| Wall Crystal Refraction | Crystal veins in walls | Prismatic sparks | Short (1-2 blocks) | Passive. Dances when fire flickers. Scatters tiny rainbows. |
| Oil Lamp | Iron and glass | Warm yellow | Medium (5-6 blocks) | Steady. Can be carried. Requires fuel. |
| Candle Array | Wax and iron | Warm amber | Short (3-4 blocks) | Multiple small flames. Can be arranged. Scented variants. |
| Ember Stones | Heat-retaining stones | Deep orange glow | Short (2-3 blocks) | Placed near fire, retain heat for hours after. Slowly fade. |
| Gold-Thread Glow | Gold embroidery on surfaces | Soft gold | Very short (1 block) | Passive. Catches any nearby light. Decorative only. |

### Light Interaction Rule
Firelight + crystal = prismatic sparks dancing on the walls. Firelight + gold thread = warm gold glow. Firelight + velvet = the fabric seems to shift between crimson and black. The Hearth's light is ALIVE — it moves, it breathes, it changes what it touches. This is the zone's visual identity: light that is never static.

## Furniture Templates (Hearth)

### 1. Grand Carved Chair
**Based on:** The Firekeeper's chair
**Description:** A massive carved wooden chair with ornate detailing, positioned near the fireplace. The chair of someone who holds court by the fire.
**Materials:** Carved dark wood, iron fittings, optional velvet cushion
**Variants:**
- Thrones (high-backed, armrests, carved crest) — the Firekeeper's style
- Lodge (wide, low, cushioned) — communal seating
- Bench (long, shared, no back) — the storyteller's bench
**Interactive Properties:**
- Player can sit/rest here
- Throne variant projects authority (NPCs address you differently when seated)
- Lodge variant invites others to sit nearby (social magnet)
- Bench variant triggers story-sharing mode (players can tell/swap stories)
- Position near fire = warmer, more central
- Position away from fire = cooler, more private

### 2. Hearth Stone Table
**Based on:** The heavy tables near the fire
**Description:** A wide stone table with a fire-warmed surface. The gathering point for shared meals, maps, and negotiations.
**Materials:** Hearth stone, carved wood (legs), iron (fittings)
**Variants:**
- Round (equal seating, communal)
- Long (hierarchical, head and foot)
- Crescent (faces the fire, audience-style)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Multiple players can sit simultaneously
- Items can be placed and shared on the surface
- Stone surface stays warm (items placed on it slowly heat)
- Crescent variant faces the fire — watching the Firekeeper while talking

### 3. Crystal Goblet Set
**Based on:** The Firekeeper's goblet
**Description:** Amber glass goblets that glow when filled and held near firelight.
**Materials:** Amber glass, optional crystal inlay
**Variants:**
- Single (personal)
- Set of 4 (small gathering)
- Set of 12 (feast)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Can be filled with liquids (mead, wine, tea, water)
- Glow intensity changes with liquid type and proximity to fire
- Clinking two goblets together triggers a toast animation/sound
- Crystal-inlay variant produces a faint musical note when clinked (the sonic system — each inlay has a frequency)

### 4. Firewood Rack
**Based on:** The practical needs of the hearth
**Description:** An iron and wood rack for storing firewood near the hearth.
**Materials:** Iron, wood
**Variants:**
- Standing rack (holds 10-20 logs)
- Wall-mounted (holds 5-8 logs)
- Cart (mobile, holds 30+ logs)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Player can add logs to the fire (extends burn time, increases light/heat)
- Different wood types produce different fire colors and scents:
  - Oak: Standard orange-gold. Warm smell.
  - Pine: Brighter, crackling. Resinous scent.
  - Crystal-Infused Wood: Blue-white flames. No smoke. Rare.
  - Elder Wood: Deep red flames. Sweet smell. Ancient.
- Fire intensity affects room ambiance (small fire = intimate, large fire = festive)

### 5. Ornate Walking Cane
**Based on:** The Firekeeper's cane
**Description:** A carved walking cane — more prop than support. A status item in the Hearth zone.
**Materials:** Carved wood, iron/crystal handle
**Variants:**
- Storyteller's Cane (carved with runes, used for gesturing during tales)
- Iron-Shod (functional, heavy, can be used as weapon)
- Crystal-Headed (glows faintly, light source)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Can be carried as accessory
- Storyteller's variant: tapping the cane on the floor 3x triggers "story mode" — nearby players get a notification that a story is about to be told
- Crystal-headed variant: provides faint light when carried
- Can be leaned against any chair or wall as decoration

### 6. Velvet Cushion
**Based on:** The Firekeeper's costume texture
**Description:** Deep crimson or black velvet cushions for chairs, benches, and floor seating.
**Materials:** Velvet, iron (corner studs optional)
**Variants:**
- Chair cushion (rectangular, tufted)
- Floor cushion (large, round, for hearth-side sitting)
- Bolster (cylindrical, for back support on benches)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Comfortable seating extension (longer rest periods)
- Color is customizable (crimson, black, deep purple, gold-trimmed)
- Gold-trimmed variant catches firelight (adds ambient glow to seating area)
- Floor cushions invite informal gathering (players sit at different heights — some on cushions, some on chairs — creates varied, natural grouping)

### 7. Fire-Mantle Shelf
**Based on:** The fireplace mantle
**Description:** The carved stone mantle above the fireplace — a display surface warmed by rising heat.
**Materials:** Hearth stone, carved wood (trim), optional gold-leaf
**Variants:**
- Wide mantle (standard, displays 5-7 items)
- Deep mantle (displays 10+ items, can hold larger objects)
- Gold-trimmed (Klimt-style gold on the leading edge — catches firelight)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Items placed here are visible to everyone in the room (public display, not private storage)
- Heat from the fire keeps items warm (stones placed here become warm to touch)
- Gold-trimmed variant: items placed on it catch a faint golden glow
- Special items placed on the mantle can trigger ambient effects (e.g., a specific stone makes the fire change color)

### 8. Storyteller's Rug
**Based on:** The thick rug in the Firekeeper's area
**Description:** A large, thick rug placed before the fireplace. The gathering circle.
**Materials:** Wool, optional gold-thread patterns
**Variants:**
- Plain (solid dark color, utilitarian warmth)
- Patterned (woven geometric designs, Klimt-inspired)
- Story-Ring (circular woven pattern with a central motif — defines the listening circle)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Defines the "gathering space" — NPCs and players naturally congregate on/near it
- Story-Ring variant: when someone stands in the center, nearby seating orients toward them
- Gold-thread variant: catches firelight, creates warm golden glow on the floor
- Can be rolled up and moved (heavy — requires two players or a cart)

## Build Rules (Hearth)

1. **Fire is the center.** Every Hearth structure orients around a fire source. No fire = no Hearth.
2. **Light is alive.** Hearth light moves, flickers, breathes. No static light sources. The fire is the performance.
3. **Gathering is the purpose.** Spaces are designed to draw people together. Chairs face the fire. Tables are shared. The rug invites sitting.
4. **Warmth is currency.** The closer to the fire, the warmer, the more central, the more important. Distance from fire = distance from community.
5. **Openness is default.** The Hearth has no locked doors. Privacy exists in side rooms, not the main hall. The hearth is for everyone.
6. **Crystal catches fire.** Crystal veins in walls transform firelight into prismatic sparks. The more crystal, the more magical the room feels. But crystal without fire is just dark stone.
7. **Gold is decoration, not foundation.** Gold-trimmed surfaces are beautiful but optional. The Hearth is stone and wood and fire first. Gold is the finishing touch, not the structure.
8. **The Firekeeper is always there.** The great fireplace on the main floor has the Firekeeper. Player-built hearths in other zones can have smaller fireplaces, but the main hearth is the only one with the Firekeeper. All other fires are echoes.

## Player Discovery Flow (Hearth)
1. Player enters the Inn for the first time — arrives on the main floor
2. Sees the great fireplace, the Firekeeper, the warm amber glow
3. Observes the gathering — NPCs and players sitting, talking, sharing stories
4. The Firekeeper acknowledges the player (first interaction — sets the tone)
5. Player receives basic Hearth build tools: hearth stone, firewood, iron lamp
6. Player builds a small hearth in a side room or personal space — bare, functional
7. Discovers crystal veins in the walls — firelight creates prismatic sparks
8. Learns to craft velvet cushions and crystal goblets (comfort and community)
9. Learns to carve wood panels and beams (warmth and intimacy)
10. Discovers gold-leaf recipe (gold + fire + adhesive — the fire itself is part of the crafting)
11. Room transforms from bare stone hearth to a warm, golden, gathering space
12. Other players are drawn to the warm space — the Hearth did its job

The Hearth teaches by warmth. The player builds for comfort, then for community.

| Aspect | Undercity (Scarlett) | Hearth (Firekeeper) |

| **Light** | Crafted (crystal, fairy lights) | Alive (fire) |
