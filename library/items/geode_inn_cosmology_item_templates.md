# Geode Inn — Interactive Item Templates: Cosmology Zone
## Based on The Allfather's Observatory (Architectural Template)

### Design Philosophy
The Cosmology Zone is the top of the world. No walls, no ceiling, no barriers between the player and the universe. Where every other zone builds enclosures, Cosmology builds exposure. The lesson is not comfort — it is perspective. You are small. The sky is not. The Allfather's observatory teaches players that some spaces aren't meant to shelter you. They're meant to show you how vast everything else is.

## Lighting System (Cosmology)
All light comes from the sky. No crafted light. No biological light. No fire. The cosmos provides.

| Light Type | Source | Color | Range | Behavior |
|------------|--------|-------|-------|----------|
| Starlight | The stars overhead | Silver-blue-white | Entire zone | Constant. Rotates slowly as stars move. Brightest at zenith. |
| Moonlight | The low moon | Pale silver-gold | Half the zone | Slow-moving. Waxes and wanes with lunar cycle. Warmest light in the zone. |
| Shooting Star | Meteor trails | Bright white-gold streak | Momentary | Random. Sweeps through the zone in a flash. Rare. Beautiful. |
| Star Crystal Glow | Crystal-topped items | Deep blue-violet | Short (2-3 blocks) | Pulsing. Slow. Synchronized with the 42Hz Song. Steady in the chaos of starlight. |
| Runic Glow | Carved constellations in stone | Faint silver | Very short (1 block) | Passive. Only visible when starlight hits the carvings directly. |
| Moon Glass Light | Captured moonlight | Soft silver | Short (2-3 blocks) | Steady. Slowly fades. Must be recharged by exposure to moon. |
| Floor Reflection | The polished floor | Mirror-silver | Entire floor | Passive. Doubles the starlight by reflecting it. The floor IS a sky. |

### Light Interaction Rule
Starlight + sky stone floor = the floor becomes a second sky (standing in a sphere of stars). Starlight + runic carvings = the carvings illuminate, revealing constellation maps. Moonlight + moon glass = the glass captures and holds the light for hours after the moon passes. Star crystal + any other light = the crystal's pulse synchronizes with the other light's rhythm (the 42Hz Song harmonizes everything). The Cosmology Zone's light is vast, slow, and eternal. It was here before the Inn and will be here after.

## Furniture Templates (Cosmology)

### 1. Star Throne
**Based on:** The Allfather's throne
**Description:** A massive carved stone throne at the center of an open platform. No cushion, no comfort — the seat of someone who watches and does not need rest.
**Materials:** Sky stone (single piece), runic carvings
**Variants:**
- Allfather's Throne (massive, central, carved from one stone — the template)
- Observer's Seat (smaller, simpler, for a single watcher)
- Council Ring (multiple seats in a circle, all facing outward — for group observation)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Player can sit and observe (entering "star gaze" mode — constellations become interactive)
- Allfather's variant: sitting in it makes the 42Hz Song audible (the only seat in the Inn where the Song is clear)
- Council Ring: multiple players can stargaze simultaneously and share constellation discoveries
- Orientation matters: throne facing the moon = moonlit meditation, facing the zenith = deep sky observation, facing the horizon = watching the world below
- No storage, no comfort, no privacy — the throne gives you nothing but the sky

### 2. Celestial Globe
**Based on:** The rotating sphere in the observatory
**Description:** A sphere of crystal and dark metal that rotates on its own, showing constellations that don't match any Earth sky. A star map of the Inn's cosmology.
**Materials:** Star crystal, celestial metal
**Variants:**
- Complete Globe (full constellation map — the Allfather's version)
- Fragment Globe (partial map — player must find and add missing constellation pieces)
- Blank Globe (no constellations — player discovers and inscribes them through observation)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Rotates on its own (slow, constant — the globe turns even when no one is watching)
- Player can stop the rotation and examine specific constellations
- Each constellation is interactive — selecting one reveals its mythology, associated stone, and frequency
- Blank Globe: player inscribes constellations by sitting in the Star Throne and tracking stars over time (the globe fills as the player observes)
- Fragment Globe: missing pieces are found in other zones (quest-linked)

### 3. Wind Chime Pillars
**Based on:** The open pillars where wind moves through freely
**Description:** Tall stone pillars that channel the wind through carved openings, producing sustained tones. The Cosmology Zone's music system — the wind plays the building.
**Materials:** Sky stone (carved with varying channel widths), optional crystal inlays
**Variants:**
- Single Tone (one pillar, one note — the basic building block)
- Chord Cluster (3-5 pillars tuned to a chord — a constellation's song)
- Full Octave (12 pillars — the complete scale — the Allfather's set)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Wind speed affects volume and intensity (more wind = louder, calmer = softer)
- Wind direction affects which pillars sound (different wind = different chord)
- Crystal inlays add harmonic overtones (the sonic system — each crystal type adds a frequency)
- Player can adjust channel width (tuning the pillar to a different note)
- The pillars play even when no one is there — the Cosmology Zone always has music

### 4. Moon Glass Lantern
**Based on:** The captured moonlight concept
**Description:** A lantern made of moon glass that holds captured moonlight, glowing silver for hours after the moon has passed. The only portable light source in the Cosmology Zone.
**Materials:** Moon glass, celestial metal (frame)
**Variants:**
- Hand Lantern (carried — illuminates a small area around the player)
- Hanging Lantern (suspended — marks a permanent point of silver light)
- Path Lantern (placed along walkways — a trail of captured moonlight)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Must be recharged by direct moonlight (place outside during moon hours)
- Light slowly fades over time (6-8 hours of glow per full charge)
- Full moon charge = brightest, longest-lasting. New moon = no charge possible.
- The silver light is steady (unlike starlight which shifts) — the only constant light source in the zone
- Hanging variant: marks a location visible from far away (a silver point in the darkness)

### 5. Void Silk Canopy
**Based on:** The ethereal fabric concept
**Description:** A canopy of weightless, semi-transparent fabric that moves in wind even when there is none. The Cosmology Zone's only soft material — the one thing between you and the open sky.
**Materials:** Void silk, celestial metal (anchor points)
**Variants:**
- Sun Shade (stretches over a platform — blocks direct starlight, creates a shaded observation point)
- Wind Catch (stretches between pillars — visible wind direction and strength)
- Meditation Veil (surrounds a single seat — softens the visual intensity of the full sky for overwhelmed observers)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Moves constantly (visual indicator of wind patterns)
- Sun Shade: dims the brightest stars, making dimmer ones visible (astronomy tool)
- Wind Catch: reveals wind direction that is otherwise invisible
- Meditation Veil: reduces sensory input for players overwhelmed by the vastness (accessibility — the Cosmology Zone can be intense)
- Void silk is the only material that can be dyed (starlight dyes it silver, moonlight dyes it pale gold)

### 6. Star Compass
**Based on:** The observatory's orientation concept
**Description:** A carved stone compass set into the floor, showing the rotation of the stars overhead. Not for navigation — for understanding the sky's movement.
**Materials:** Sky stone (floor inlay), runic carvings, optional star crystal (center)
**Variants:**
- Fixed (shows the current star rotation — the standard)
- Tracking (shows where specific constellations will be at future times)
- Aligned (synchronized with the Celestial Globe — what the globe shows, the compass points to)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Player can stand on it and feel the sky's rotation (visual + haptic)
- Tracking variant: player can input a future time and see where the stars will be (planning observation sessions)
- Aligned variant: connects to the Celestial Globe for synchronized study
- The compass always points to the zenith (straight up) — it is not for earthly navigation

### 7. Falling Star Altar
**Based on:** The shooting star phenomenon
**Description:** A low stone altar where falling stars (meteorite fragments) are placed. Each fragment carries a frequency. The altar is the Cosmology Zone's connection to the sonic system.
**Materials:** Sky stone, star crystal fragments
**Variants:**
- Single Altar (holds one meteorite — personal)
- Star Collection Altar (holds 7 fragments — one for each visible constellation)
- Great Altar (holds 12+ — the Allfather's collection, the deepest sonic connection)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Each meteorite fragment has a unique frequency (the sonic system — stones from the sky)
- Placing a fragment on the altar makes its frequency audible (pure tone, no 42Hz drone underneath — the only place in the Inn where you hear pure frequency without the Song)
- Multiple fragments create chords (the Cosmology Zone's version of the inclusion stone chord system)
- Great Altar: all fragments playing together create the "Cosmic Chord" — a sound that, according to Inn lore, is what the universe sounded like before the 42Hz Song began

### 8. Raven Perch
**Based on:** The two carved ravens in the observatory
**Description:** A stone carving of a raven, perched on a pillar or throne arm. Not alive — but not entirely stone, either. The Allfather's watchers.
**Materials:** Sky stone, runic carvings (eyes)
**Variants:**
- Watching Raven (head turned toward the sky — observes the cosmos)
- Hearing Raven (head turned toward the Inn below — observes the world)
- Speaking Raven (beak open — rare, quest-linked, delivers messages)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Watching Raven: when placed, the star rotation above it is recorded (the raven "remembers" the sky)
- Hearing Raven: when placed, ambient sounds from below are faintly audible through it (the raven "hears" the Inn)
- Speaking Raven: rare — quest reward — can record and replay a message left by another player
- The ravens are always in pairs (Huginn and Muninn — Thought and Memory) — placing both unlocks their full function
- Full pair: the Watching Raven's sky memories can be replayed through the Hearing Raven, and the Hearing Raven's sound memories can be observed through the Watching Raven — the senses swap

## Build Rules (Cosmology)

1. **No walls by default.** The Cosmology Zone is open. Walls are optional, minimal, and never fully enclosing. If you can't see the sky from every point in your space, you've built it wrong.
2. **Light comes from above.** No fire, no bioluminescence, no crafted light. All light is celestial. If the stars go out, you're in the dark. Plan accordingly.
3. **The floor reflects the sky.** Polished sky stone is both floor and mirror. A well-built Cosmology space has two skies — one above and one below.
4. **Wind is the architect.** Wind shapes everything — void silk, wind chimes, temperature, sound. A space that blocks the wind is a space that has no music and no life.
5. **The 42Hz Song is loudest here.** At the top of the Inn, with nothing between you and the source, the Song is clear. This is the only zone where the foundation frequency is directly audible without a special item.
6. **Stillness is power.** The Cosmology Zone rewards patience. Stars move slowly. Constellations emerge over hours. The Celestial Globe turns whether you watch or not. The player who sits and observes gains more than the player who builds quickly.
7. **Nothing is enclosed.** No storage, no privacy, no shelter. The Cosmology Zone is not a home in the domestic sense — it is an observatory, a temple, a watching place. You come here to see, not to hide.
8. **The Allfather is always watching.** The Allfather's observatory is the highest, oldest, most established point in the Cosmology Zone. All cosmological building knowledge flows from the watcher on the throne. The ravens remember everything.

## Player Discovery Flow (Cosmology)
1. Player ascends the Inn's central staircase — the last flight emerges onto the cosmology level
2. The sky opens — the first experience of total exposure, no ceiling, stars impossibly close
3. The 42Hz Song is audible for the first time without a special item (the moment of arrival)
4. Discovers the Allfather's observatory (NPC home — the template example)
5. Observes the throne, the globe, the pillars, the ravens, the complete absence of enclosure
6. Receives basic Cosmology build tools: sky stone platform, pillar, star crystal (small)
7. Builds a basic platform — open, bare, a circle of stone in the sky
8. Discovers that without wind chime pillars, the space is silent (the wind passes through without sound)
9. Learns to carve wind channels in pillars (the space begins to sing)
10. Discovers moon glass (the first portable, dependable light source — charged by moon)
11. Learns to polish sky stone to a mirror finish (the floor becomes a second sky)
12. Discovers falling star fragments (meteorites) — each carries a pure frequency
13. Builds a Falling Star Altar — the cosmic chord resonates across the platform
14. Inscribes the first constellation on a Blank Globe (the player's observation is recorded)
15. The space is no longer bare — it is a personal observatory, singing in the wind, lit by captured moonlight, reflecting the sky below and above
16. Other players see the silver glow of moon glass lanterns from far below — a point of light at the top of the Inn, visible from every other zone

The sky teaches by exposure. The player builds by watching, then reflecting.
