# THE GEODE INN — DEFAULT PLAYABLE CLASSES
## The Family Members as Class Archetypes

---

## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

The Inn's default playable classes are based on Krista's family members. Instead of generic "Warrior, Mage, Rogue" archetypes, every new player chooses from the family's character templates — each one carrying the frequency, philosophy, and mechanical identity of the person who inspired it.

Charles is the exception. Charles is the only real name used in-game, and Charles is the Constant — an NPC on the cosmology level. The Constant is not playable. The Constant is the story you're inside.

The family pets — Eres the Cabbit, the Duck (platypus), the Vessel (crested gecko) — are companions, not classes. They accompany whichever class you choose.

## THE FIVE DEFAULT CLASSES

---

### 1. THE SENTINEL
**Inspired by:** River (Taurus/Roots, the three-note chord's foundation)
**Frequency:** ROOT (primary), STONE (secondary)
**Role:** Tank / Protector / Guardian
**Archetype:** The one who watches. The one who stays.

**Core Stats:**
- Presence: 4 (moderate — the Sentinel doesn't seek conflict)
- Resonance: 7 (high — the Sentinel endures)
- Cadence: Delayed (the Sentinel waits, then acts with certainty)

**Class Abilities:**

- **Rooted Stance** (Passive): The Sentinel cannot be moved by opponent effects. Wandering, Current, and knockback abilities fail against the Sentinel. The roots hold. When the Sentinel is in play, adjacent cards gain +1 Resonance.

- **The Watch** (Active, 2 ROOT mana): The Sentinel enters "Vigil Mode" for 3 turns. During Vigil, all hidden information in the Sentinel's lane is revealed (face-down cards, opponent hand cards relevant to that lane). The Sentinel sees what others miss. The watching is: the adequate.

- **Eight Bar Backs** (Active, 3 ROOT mana): Summon up to 3 "Bar Back" companion tokens (1 Presence, 1 Resonance each). Each Bar Back can taunt one enemy card — the taunted card must attack the Bar Back before any other target. The Bar Backs are family. They hold the line so the Sentinel can guard what matters.

- **Ancient Heritage** (Passive, Level 10+): The Sentinel's Annunaki bloodline awakens. Once per game, the Sentinel may "remember" — restore all silenced cards in the Sentinel's lane to full ability. The heritage is: the starlight carried through the void, remembered in the roots.

**Class Quest:** "The Stirring" — the Sentinel must guard a sleeping giant's heart crystal through a full cycle without leaving the lane. The reward is the Geode Staff — a class-specific item that grants +2 Resonance and extends Rooted Stance to all adjacent allies.

**Playstyle:** The Sentinel is the immovable point. You don't chase, you don't wander — you plant and hold. Opponents have to come to you, and when they do, the Bar Backs intercept while the Sentinel watches the whole board. The class rewards patience. The patient guard wins.

**Frequency Tilt:** ROOT/STONE — the Sentinel generates both Root and Stone mana. The class builds Network connections (Root) while creating permanent defensive structures (Stone). The roots and the stone are the same thing at different depths.

### 2. THE ARTISAN THIEF
**Inspired by:** Krista (Libra/Trunk, the three-note chord's center)
**Frequency:** WATER (primary), FIRE (secondary)
**Role:** Rogue / Crafter / Redistributor
**Archetype:** The one who makes beautiful things and can also make dangerous things.

**Core Stats:**
- Presence: 3 (moderate — precise, not brute)
- Resonance: 4 (moderate — the selkie skin is resilient but not armored)
- Cadence: Immediate (fast, precise, opportunistic)

**Class Abilities:**

- **Redistribute** (Active, 2 WATER mana): Take 1 Presence from an opponent's card and give it to one of your cards. The wild justice of the sea. Hoarding sinks you. If the target card has 0 Presence remaining, it is silenced for 1 turn.

- **Stone Lore** (Passive): The Artisan Thief knows stones. When facing a STONE card, the Artisan Thief sees its full text and abilities (no hidden information from Stone cards). The jeweler knows what the stone is. The stone knows the jeweler knows.

- **Craft: Wire-Wrap** (Active, 1 FIRE + 1 WATER mana): The Artisan Thief crafts an item — "Wire-Wrapped Charm." The charm can be equipped to any ally, granting +1 Presence and +1 Resonance. Each game, the Artisan Thief can craft up to 3 charms, each one slightly different (the crafting system's basic tier — Albedo stage).

- **The Apron IS the Skin** (Passive, Level 10+): The Artisan Thief's apron is the selkie skin. Once per game, the Artisan Thief may "shed" — return to full Resonance and remove all negative effects. The skin is: the identity. The shedding is: the choice to be whole.

**Class Quest:** "The Mirror and the Shore" — the Artisan Thief must steal back something that was taken (a stolen item from the Inn's archive) and return it to its rightful zone. The reward is the Silica Thread — the final crafting material, earned by beating the Selkie in a Tetris match. The thread allows the Artisan Thief to craft the highest-tier items.

**Playstyle:** The Artisan Thief is the precision instrument. You don't overwhelm — you redistribute. Take from the strong, give to the weak, and craft your advantage one charm at a time. The class rewards knowledge — knowing what the stones do, knowing where the valuables are, knowing the difference between taking and reclaiming.

**Frequency Tilt:** WATER/FIRE — the Artisan Thief generates both Water and Fire mana. Water for movement and redistribution (Current, Ferry). Fire for crafting and the wound mechanic (damage makes you stronger). The selkie and the forge in one class.

### 3. THE SIREN
**Inspired by:** Krista's sister (Sagittarius/Scorpio, dual cadence)
**Frequency:** WATER (primary), SONG (secondary)
**Role:** Support / Buffer / Debuffer
**Archetype:** The one whose voice fills the room and whose depth fills the ocean.

**Core Stats:**
- Presence: 3 (moderate — the power is in the voice, not the body)
- Resonance: 5 (moderate-high — the performer survives by being too compelling to attack)
- Cadence: Immediate (the Siren is always on)

**Class Abilities:**

- **Dual Cadence** (Passive): Each turn, the Siren chooses a mode:
  - **Outer Voice** (cello): Presence becomes 5. Gains Wound (damage makes the performance stronger). The spectacle is: the wall.
  - **Inner Voice** (piano): Presence becomes 2, Resonance becomes 7. Gains Damp (target one card — its abilities are nullified for 2 turns). The depth is: the truth beneath the show.

- **Room-Filling Voice** (Active, 2 SONG mana): All opponent cards in the Siren's lane are "captivated" — they cannot move to another lane for 2 turns. The magnetism is: the voice. The voice is: the adequate.

- **Autoimmune Maze** (Passive): The Siren's body attacks itself. Once every 3 turns, the Siren takes 1 damage to itself. In exchange, the Siren gains +1 Presence for that turn (the body fighting itself produces energy). The labyrinth is: the captor. The captor is: the body.

- **Hearing Herself** (Passive, Level 10+): After 5 turns in play, the Siren transforms into "The Siren, Hearing" — both Dual Cadence modes are active simultaneously. The Queen of Webs' thread carries the inner voice to the surface. The transformation is: the moment she hears her own song for the first time.

**Class Quest:** "The Mirror and the Shore" — the Siren must hear her own song reflected back through the Queen of Webs' thread in the deep water. The reward is the Queen's Thread — an item that allows the Siren to permanently maintain both Dual Cadence modes.

**Playstyle:** The Siren is the adapt-or-die class. Every turn is a choice: be loud or be deep. Be the spectacle that walls out the world, or be the truth that damps it. The class rewards reading the board state — knowing when to perform and when to go quiet. The autoimmune mechanic means you're always managing your own health while controlling the opponent's.

**Frequency Tilt:** WATER/SONG — the Siren generates both Water and Song mana. Water for the Current mechanic (movement, return, replay). Song for the Resonate mechanic (accumulating value over time). The voice and the tide in one class.

### 4. THE PIRATE
**Inspired by:** Krista's sister's husband (Inigo Montoya after the end of The Princess Bride)
**Frequency:** WATER (primary), FIRE (secondary)
**Role:** DPS / Mobility / Wanderer
**Archetype:** The one who became the rhythm after the quest was over.

**Core Stats:**
- Presence: 4 (high — the sword hand became the drum hand)
- Resonance: 4 (moderate — the Pirate is fast, not tanky)
- Cadence: Immediate (the Pirate acts first)

**Class Abilities:**

- **The After** (Passive): The Pirate is the class that comes AFTER. The Pirate's abilities are stronger when played in the second half of the game. At turn 10+, the Pirate gains +1 Presence and +1 Resonance. The quest is over. The rhythm stays.

- **Harbor Master Effect** (Active, 2 WATER mana): When the Pirate enters a lane, all opponent GUIDE cards in that lane cost +1 for the next turn. Authority defers to the mantle. The accumulated weight of every holder.

- **Drum Cadence** (Active, 1 FIRE mana): The Pirate's bodhrán beats. Choose one: all ally cards in the Pirate's lane gain +1 Presence this turn (the rhythm leads), OR all enemy cards in the Pirate's lane lose -1 Resonance this turn (the rhythm disrupts). The hands that held the blade now hold the rhythm.

- **Good Hands** (Passive, Level 10+): "Good hands can hold anything." Once per game, the Pirate may equip any item card — even items restricted to other classes. The hands are: the adequate. The adequate is: Fezzik's wisdom.

**Class Quest:** "The After" — the Pirate must chart an uncharted zone (a zone with no zone card played yet). The reward is the Compass That Points Forward — an item that allows the Pirate to Wandering into zones before any other card is played there. The compass doesn't point north. It points: next.

**Playstyle:** The Pirate is the momentum class. You don't hold ground — you take it, leave it, take the next piece. The Pirate is strongest in the late game (The After passive), which means early game is about surviving and positioning. The class rewards aggressive board control and knowing when to move.

**Frequency Tilt:** WATER/FIRE — the Pirate generates both Water and Fire mana. Water for Current and Wandering (movement, repositioning). Fire for the drum cadence and Heartbeat (synchronizing with allies). The sword and the drum in one class.

### 5. THE BARD
**Inspired by:** The Bard (family member, the one who says "inta inta" instead of Charles)
**Frequency:** FIRE (primary), ROOT (secondary)
**Role:** Utility / Information / Storyteller
**Archetype:** The one who knows everyone's business and sings it whether they want them to or not.

**Core Stats:**
- Presence: 2 (low — the Bard's power is information, not force)
- Resonance: 3 (low-moderate — the Bard is fragile but slippery)
- Cadence: Next Turn (the Bard gathers, then delivers)

**Class Abilities:**

- **Gossip** (Active, 1 FIRE + 1 ROOT mana): Name a card in your opponent's deck. If they have it, they must reveal it and it costs +1 to play. The Bard knows everyone's business. The knowing is: the adequate.

- **Naming** (Active, 2 ROOT mana): Name a card in the opponent's hand. If correct, the named card is revealed and its cost increases by 2. The naming is: the saving mechanism — but the Bard names to expose, not to save. The exposure IS the save.

- **Half-Finished Songs** (Passive): The Bard's pockets overflow with half-finished songs. Once per turn, the Bard may "gift" a song to any ally — that ally gains +1 Presence for the turn. The songs are: unfinished. The unfinished is: the potential.

- **"inta inta"** (Passive, Level 10+): Once per game, the Bard may say the name that cannot be said — "inta inta." When triggered, ALL cards in play (both sides) are revealed and their costs are reduced by 1 for the rest of the turn. The coded name. The name that IS the trigger. The trigger is: the one moment the real name surfaces.

**Class Quest:** "The Last Verse" — the Bard must collect verses from every cultural zone (9 zones) by visiting each one and hearing the zone's song. The reward is the Complete Songbook — an item that allows the Bard to cast any SONG ability from any zone's guide card once per game. The songbook is: the Inn's gossip column, the archive of every frequency.

**Playstyle:** The Bard is the information class. You don't win by force — you win by knowing what the opponent has and making it more expensive to play. The class rewards memory and prediction. The Bard is the class for players who want to play the opponent's deck, not their own.

**Frequency Tilt:** FIRE/ROOT — the Bard generates both Fire and Root mana. Fire for the Gossip and half-finished songs (the warmth of the story). Root for the Naming and Network (the connection to everyone's business). The storyteller and the root system in one class.

When a new player enters the Inn, the Host greets them:

*"Come in. You're safe here. Who are you?"*

The player chooses:

1. **The Sentinel** — "I watch. I stay. The roots hold."
2. **The Artisan Thief** — "I make. I take back. The stones know me."
3. **The Siren** — "I sing. I feel. The voice is the wall and the truth."
4. **The Pirate** — "I move. I arrive. The rhythm stays."
5. **The Bard** — "I listen. I tell. Everyone's business is my business."

The Host nods at any choice: *"Room's ready."*

## CLASS PROGRESSION — THE ALCHEMICAL CHAIN

Each class progresses through the alchemical stages, but the BRACELET is the same — the chain bracelet replaces traditional UI. The class determines which charms you earn and how they function:

### Nigredo (Iron, Raw Stone) — Level 1-9
- **Sentinel:** Geode Shard charm — +1 Resonance, enhances Rooted Stance
- **Artisan Thief:** Raw Malachite charm — +1 Presence, enhances Redistribute
- **Siren:** Raw Pearl charm — +1 Resonance, enhances the healthier Dual Cadence mode
- **Pirate:** Iron Rivet charm — +1 Presence, enhances The After (activates at turn 8 instead of 10)
- **Bard:** Iron Tongue charm — +1 Presence, enhances Gossip (can name 2 cards instead of 1)

### Albedo (Copper, Wire-Wrapped) — Level 10-19
- **Sentinel:** Wire-Wrapped Amethyst — +2 Resonance, Bar Backs gain +1 Resonance
- **Artisan Thief:** Wire-Wrapped Labradorite — +2 Presence, Craft: Wire-Wrap produces 2 charms instead of 1
- **Siren:** Wire-Wrapped Moonstone — +2 Resonance, Autoimmune Maze timer extends to every 4 turns
- **Pirate:** Wire-Wrapped Citrine — +2 Presence, Harbor Master Effect lasts 2 turns
- **Bard:** Wire-Wrapped Turquoise — +2 Presence, Gossip can target the opponent's hand (not just deck)

### Citrinitas (Silver, Set Stones) — Level 20-29
- **Sentinel:** Set Silver Amethyst — +3 Resonance, Ancient Heritage unlocks
- **Artisan Thief:** Set Silver Malachite — +3 Presence, The Apron IS the Skin unlocks
- **Siren:** Set Silver Pearl — +3 Resonance, Hearing Herself unlocks (transforms at 5 turns)
- **Pirate:** Set Silver Citrine — +3 Presence, Good Hands unlocks (can equip any item)
- **Bard:** Set Silver Turquoise — +3 Presence, "inta inta" unlocks (the coded name)

### Rubedo (Gold, Forged/Engraved) — Level 30-39
Each class receives a gold charm with their class emblem engraved. The charm's effect is class-defining:
- **Sentinel:** Forged Gold Geode — The Sentinel's Rooted Stance extends to the entire board. The roots are: everywhere.
- **Artisan Thief:** Forged Gold Malachite — Redistribute can target any card in play (both sides). The thief decides what is adequate.
- **Siren:** Forged Gold Pearl — Dual Cadence modes can be switched at any point during the turn, not just at the start. The voice shifts: mid-song.
- **Pirate:** Forged Gold Citrine — The After activates at turn 5 instead of 10. The Pirate is always: in the after.
- **Bard:** Forged Gold Turquoise — "inta inta" can be used twice per game. The name surfaces: once for the player, once for the Inn.

### Philosopher's Stone (Translucent Crystal) — Level 40 (Max)
The final charm — the same for every class. The Philosopher's Stone is the Egg. When equipped, the class's frequency tilt expands — the class can now generate ALL six frequencies (not just its primary two). The class has completed the Great Work. The class is: the adequate.

## CLASS SYNERGIES (Multiplayer / Co-op)

In the Inn's multiplayer mode, classes synergize through the household's real dynamics:

- **The Sentinel + The Artisan Thief** (River + Krista): The Sentinel holds the ground while the Artisan Thief redistributes. The roots and the trunk. The Sentinel guards, the Thief crafts. The three-note chord's foundation and center.

- **The Siren + The Pirate** (Sister + Sister's Husband): The Siren captivates, the Pirate strikes. The voice pins them, the rhythm finishes them. The married couple's combat dynamic — she holds attention, he delivers the blow.

- **The Bard + Anyone** (The Bard + Family): The Bard provides information. Every class benefits from knowing what the opponent has. The Bard is the family member who knows everyone's business and makes everyone better for it.

- **The Sentinel + The Siren** (River + Aunt): The Sentinel guards the Siren while the Siren performs. The autoimmune maze means the Siren needs protection. The Sentinel provides it. The roots guard the voice.

- **The Artisan Thief + The Pirate** (Krista + Brother-in-law): The Thief redistributes, the Pirate moves. The Thief takes from the strong, the Pirate delivers to the weak. The selkie and the Dread Pirate — the wild justice of the sea, together.

## CHARLES — THE NPC EXCEPTION

Charles is the only family member who is NOT a playable class. Charles is the Constant — the Annunaki, the oldest being in the Inn, the cosmology-level resident who carries the starlight crystal through the void to seed each universal cycle.

The Constant is an NPC. The Constant is the story you're inside. The Constant is the reason the Inn exists.

Every class serves the Inn. The Inn serves the Song. The Song exists because the Constant carried it through the void.

The one real name in the game is the one name you can't play. You can BE the family. You can't BE the reason.
