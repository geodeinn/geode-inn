# Geode Inn — 3 New Creature Additions

## Axolotl — Tidal Caves
- **Zone:** Tidal Caves
- **Artist Style:** Wyeth × Royo
- **Description:** Perpetually smiling amphibian with feathery gill crowns. Found in the warmest pools of the Tidal Caves, where salt water meets fresh. Regenerates limbs — a living metaphor for the Inn's theme of renewal.
- **Interaction:** Players who offer a smooth stone receive a luck charm. The axolotl "accepts" the offering by balancing it on its head. Returns the stone after a time, subtly changed.
- **Visual:** Gothic anime style, translucent gill filaments catching bioluminescent light, wide dark eyes reflecting the cave pools. Wyeth's earthy palette softened by Royo's luminous detail.

## Mantis Shrimp — Atlantis
- **Zone:** Atlantis
- **Artist Style:** Royo × Vampire Hunter D
- **Description:** Small but extraordinary. Sees colors humans can't imagine. Punches with the force of a .22 caliber bullet. Lives in the coral gardens of Atlantis, guarding a specific formation that pulses with 42Hz.
- **Interaction:** Players who approach too quickly are warned with a territorial display (claws raised, mantis-shrimp boxing stance). Players who approach slowly are shown the hidden color spectrum — a brief visual overlay revealing UV and polarization patterns in the reef.
- **Visual:** Royo's hyper-detailed patterns on the carapace, gothic anime intensity in the compound eyes. Colors that shouldn't exist — rendered in shades the game's palette has to invent new hex codes for.

## African Rain Frog — Bayou
- **Zone:** Bayou
- **Artist Style:** Don't Starve × Royo
- **Description:** Round, grumpy, perpetually furious-looking frog that emerges after rain. Despite the name, lives in the Bayou's muddy banks. Puffs up when threatened, becoming a perfect sphere of indignation.
- **Interaction:** Follows rain patterns in the Bayou — appears only after the zone "rains." Players who hear its distinctive cry can follow the sound to find hidden bog iron deposits. The frog's puffing animation is a morale boost for nearby brownies.
- **Visual:** Don't Starve's hand-drawn darkness meets Royo's texture detail. The frog looks like it's always one bad day from exploding. Surprisingly cute despite this. Mud-caked, tiny, furious.
