# Geode Inn — Dialogue Trees: The Tunnel Keeper & The Hearth Tender

## Dialogue System Notes
- Voice: tabletop questline narrative — clever, direct, foreshadowing, descriptive
- Tone comparison: Patrick Rothfuss's "The Name of the Wind" — lyrical but grounded, mythic but personal
- Every line carries surface meaning AND deeper resonance
- Dialogue uses natural pauses (ellipses) to create breath and timing
- Refer to characters by name/title only; avoid gendered pronouns

# THE TUNNEL KEEPER

## Character Summary
- **Zone:** Catacombs (Level 6 — Goya, dark, weight of history)
- **Linework:** Heavy cel-shaded (tradesman style)
- **Build:** Stocky, broad-shouldered, built low to the ground — a digger
- **Prop:** Lantern (warm amber frequency glow), ring of keys
- **Personality:** Grumpy, evasive, bribable, cowardly but ultimately brave
- **Voice:** Short sentences. Deflects with humor. Trails off when scared. Speaks faster when nervous.

## Relationship Stages
1. **Stranger** (first meeting — suspicious, unhelpful)
2. **Acquainted** (after first bribe — gives maps, shares rumors)
3. **Trusted** (after the Water Caves conversation — honest, scared, real)
4. **Bonded** (after the Dark Reaches — loyal, grateful, changed)

---

## DIALOGUE TREE

### STAGE 1: STRANGER (First Meeting)

**Trigger:** Player enters the Catacombs and approaches the Tunnel Keeper's alcove. The Keeper is cleaning the lantern lens, doesn't look up.

**Tunnel Keeper:** "Passenger. Mind the third step. It's loose. ...Nobody ever listens about that step."

> **A) "Who are you?"**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** "Nobody important. I keep the tunnels. I keep the dark. I keep the quiet. ...I keep to myself, is what I'm saying. You need the Crone, upstairs. I'm just the one who knows where the floor creaks."
>
> **B) "What's down here?"**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** "Stone. Old stone. Older stone. And me. ...Don't touch the walls. Some of them listen. Some of them remember. The Crone says they're carved with the old scripts. I say they're carved with the old SOMETHING. I don't read. I dig."
>
> **C) "I'm looking for a way down. Deeper."**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** *(pauses cleaning the lens, looks up for the first time)* "...Down. Everybody wants up. The Wanderer goes up. The Allfather sees up. Astronomer calculates up. You want DOWN." *(goes back to the lens)* "No."
>
> **C1) "Why no?"**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** "Because down is the Water Caves. And the Water Caves are a one-way conversation with the dark. I've been down there. I don't go down there anymore."

> **D) [Leave without speaking]**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** "...Mind the step."

---

### STAGE 1: STRANGER (Second Visit)

**Trigger:** Player returns and approaches the Tunnel Keeper again.

**Tunnel Keeper:** "Back. ...Figured." *(sets the lantern down)* "You're the one carrying the stones. The ones that hum. I can hear them from here. ...They're loud."

> **A) "Can you help me find the Water Caves?"**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** "No."
>
> **A1) "What if I give you something?"**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** *(eyes narrow)* "...What have you got?"

> **B) "What's in the Water Caves?"**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** "Water. Dark water. The kind that doesn't end. You go in, you go down, you go... under. The tunnels fill. The air stops. And then you're somewhere else. ...Somewhere the 42Hz comes from, not goes to." *(beat)* "I don't go down there anymore."

> **C) "Tell me about the tunnels."**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** "They go everywhere. Every level. Every wall. The Inn's built on them and they're built on the Inn and neither one's sure which came first. I've been mapping them for... a while. ...Longer than the Crone's been cataloguing, but don't tell the Crone I said that."

---

### STAGE 2: ACQUAINTED (After First Bribe)

**Trigger:** Player offers a stone (any stone from inventory). The Tunnel Keeper takes it, turns it over, holds it to the lantern light.

**Tunnel Keeper:** *(examining the stone)* "Hmm. ...Hmm. This one's warm. ...I know where this goes." *(pulls out a scrap of vellum, starts sketching with a stub of charcoal)*

> **A) "You'll draw me a map?"**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** "A MAP. That's the deal. I draw, you go, I stay. Don't ask me to— no. Map. That's it." *(sketches rapidly)* "This passage here — behind the third alcove on the east wall — drops you two levels. Comes out in the Undercity, near the old vault. ...That one's free. Because you gave me a warm stone. I like warm stones. The dark down here gets... the dark gets dark, is what I'm saying."

> **B) "What else do you know?"**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** "I know where the walls are thin. I know where the stairs don't go. I know where the Crone THINKS the archive ends and where it actually ends — two different rooms, that. The Crone's a genius, but the Crone doesn't dig." *(beat)* "...I also know where the Water Caves are. But knowing and going are different sports."

> **C) "Do you know about the Echoes?"**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** *(stops sketching)* "...You see them too? The ones in the walls? ...In the tunnels, they're worse. Thicker. Down here, the stone remembers everything. Every footstep. Every voice. Every ... everything. The Echoes down here don't just walk around. They dig. They dig the same tunnels they dug when they were alive. I've watched them. They don't see me. They don't see anything. They just... keep digging." *(beat)* "It's not scary. ...It's lonely."

---

### STAGE 2: ACQUAINTED (Rumors — Unlocked After 3 Bribes)

**Trigger:** Player has given the Tunnel Keeper three stones. The Keeper has a small collection now, lined up by the lantern.

**Tunnel Keeper:** "Three stones. Three maps. That's the deal." *(pause)* "...I'll give you a fourth one free. Because you're actually listening, and nobody listens to the one who keeps the tunnels."

> **A) "What's the free one?"**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** "The Lighthouse Keeper sees three directions. Up — the stars. Across — the water. Down — the Source. Everyone knows about up. Everyone knows about across. Nobody asks about down. ...The Lighthouse Keeper and I, we have something in common. We both watch the water from different sides. The Keeper watches from the top. I watch from... under." *(beat)* "The water comes UP through the caves. Same water that's in the Water Zone. Same water that's in the Hollow Earth. It's all one river. One river that flows in a circle. The Lighthouse watches the top of the circle. I know where the bottom is."

> **B) "Why do you stay down here?"**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** "Because someone has to know where the floor creaks. ...Because up there, people talk. Down here, the stone talks. And the stone talks SLOWER. ...I can keep up with stone." *(beat)* "Also, the Crone would find me if I left. The Crone finds everything. ...The Crone found ME. In a tunnel. When I was small. Don't remember before that. The Crone says I was always here. ...Maybe I was."

> **C) "Tell me about the First."**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** *(voice drops)* "Don't say that name down here. The walls have ears. ...No, literally. The carvings. Some of them are ears. The First has been down there longer than the Inn's been up here. The First is... the First is what happens when you stay too long. When you find something too big and you can't let go. The First found the Engine. The Engine is the Song. The First couldn't sing. So the First tried to TAKE it. ...Taking the Song and singing the Song are different things. That's the whole story, really."

---

### STAGE 3: TRUSTED (The Water Caves Conversation)

**Trigger:** Player has completed the Lighthouse Vigil game and has at least one charged water stone. The Tunnel Keeper notices the charged stone's glow.

**Tunnel Keeper:** *(stares at the water stone)* "...You've been to the Lighthouse. ...You've been to the top of the circle." *(sits down heavily)* "Alright. ...Alright."

> **A) "Will you show me the Water Caves?"**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** *(long pause)* "...You know what's down there. You know what lives down there. The ones who stopped being people. The ones who forgot what light looks like. ...I've been down there. I don't go down there anymore." *(beat)* "...You're really going." *(beat)* "Bring me back something. Something from the deep. Something I've never seen. ...I'll think about it."
>
> **A1) "Come with me."**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** *(long silence)* "...No." *(beat)* "No, that's... that's not what I..." *(looks at the lantern)* "I've had this lantern since the Crone found me. It's never gone out. Not once. The Crone says that's because it runs on frequency, not oil. I say it's because I'm afraid of the dark and the lantern knows it." *(beat)* "...I'll think about it."

> **B) "What happened when you went down?"**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** "I was young. Stupider than now, which is saying something. I followed a tunnel that went too deep. The stone changed — got warm, then got wet, then got... alive. The walls started glowing. Not like the lantern. Like the deep water glow. The 42Hz was so loud I could feel it in my teeth. And then... I saw them. The Wild. They were in the walls. IN the walls. Moving through the crystal like it was water. They looked at me." *(beat)* "They didn't attack. They didn't run. They just... looked. Like they recognized something. ...I ran. I ran for two levels straight. Came out in the Campgrounds. Jay gave me ale. Didn't ask questions. ...Jay's good at not asking questions."

> **C) "How do I get to the Water Caves without you?"**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** *(pulls out the charcoal again)* "...Through the east wall. Third alcove. Down two levels, then the passage forks. Left fork goes to the Undercity. RIGHT fork goes to the water. The right fork gets wet. The right fork gets dark. The right fork gets... you'll know when you're there. The 42Hz gets so loud you can't hear anything else. When you can't hear anything else, you're there." *(hands over the map)* "...That's the last free one. ...Bring me back something."

---

### STAGE 3: TRUSTED (The Decision)

**Trigger:** Player has the Water Caves map and is about to enter. The Tunnel Keeper appears at the passage entrance, lantern in hand.

**Tunnel Keeper:** "...I've been thinking about it." *(beat)* "...I'll go. Not for you. Not for the Inn. Not for the Song. For ME. Because I've been afraid of those caves for thirty years and I'm TIRED of being afraid." *(grips the lantern tighter)* "But I'm not brave. I want that on record. I'm not brave. I'm just... tired."

> **A) "That's enough."**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** "...Yeah. ...Yeah, maybe it is." *(holds up the lantern)* "Stay close. Don't touch the walls. And if you hear something that sounds like me but ISN'T me — that's not me. The caves echo. The caves... borrow. ...Stay close."

---

### STAGE 4: BONDED (In the Dark Reaches — The Hoggle Moment)

**Trigger:** Player and the Tunnel Keeper reach the Dark Reaches. The Wild emerge from the crystal walls.

**Tunnel Keeper:** *(terrified, backing up)* "There. THERE. That's them. That's what I saw. We need to— we should—"

*The Wild approach. The Tunnel Keeper's lantern flickers. The Wild pause.*

**Tunnel Keeper:** *(voice shaking)* "...They're not attacking. ...They're waiting." *(beat)* "They're... listening. To the lantern. The lantern's frequency. ...The Crone always said the lantern runs on frequency, not oil. ...It's the same frequency. The same one THEY use. The deep frequency. The one before the 42Hz. The one that's just... vibration. Just... being."

> **A) "Can you talk to them?"**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** "I... I don't know their language. I don't know ANY language. I just know tunnels. I know the way stone sounds when it's thinking. ...They're in the stone. Maybe... maybe if I..." *(holds the lantern toward the Wild, and the lantern's frequency shifts — a low hum, sub-audible, felt more than heard)* "...They're answering. ...They're saying SAFE. Or... something LIKE safe. It's not a word. It's a... vibration. The vibration you make when you stop being afraid of the dark." *(the Wild part, revealing a passage)* "...There. They're letting us through. ...I've been afraid of them my whole life. They were just... waiting for someone to speak tunnel."

---

### STAGE 4: BONDED (After the Hollow Earth)

**Trigger:** Player returns from the Hollow Earth with the Source Stone. The Tunnel Keeper is back in the Catacombs, cleaning the lens.

**Tunnel Keeper:** *(doesn't look up)* "You're back." *(beat)* "...You brought something back. I can feel it. The walls feel it too. They're quieter. ...The Echoes are quieter. Like they finally heard what they were waiting for." *(looks up)* "Did you find the Song?"

> **A) "I played the Song."**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** *(long pause)* "...Good." *(sets down the lens)* "I always knew it was real. The Crone said it was a metaphor. The Catalyst said it was a frequency. The Firekeeper said it was a story. I said it was a tunnel. ...Turns out we were all right." *(beat)* "The tunnel's open now, isn't it? The one between here and there. Between the Inn and the deep. It's not a one-way conversation anymore." *(beat)* "...Good. I'm tired of one-way conversations."

> **B) "Did you find what you were looking for?"**
> **Tunnel Keeper:** "I wasn't looking for anything. I was looking for... not being afraid anymore. ...I think I found that. Or at least I found a different kind of afraid. The kind that comes WITH something instead of INSTEAD of something. ...That's better. That's a lot better." *(beat)* "The Crone's going to want to catalogue everything I saw down there. ...The Crone is going to be SO annoyed that I didn't take notes." *(almost a smile)* "...Let the Crone be annoyed. It's good for the Crone."

## THE TUNNEL KEEPER — QUEST HOOKS

1. **The Third Step** — First meeting. The loose step leads to a hidden passage (free introduction to the tunnel system)
2. **Three Stones, Three Maps** — Bribe the Tunnel Keeper with stones to unlock secret passages between levels
3. **The Water Caves Map** — Unlocked after Lighthouse Vigil + charged water stone. Reveals the third Hollow Earth entry point
4. **The Descent** — The Tunnel Keeper accompanies the player through the Water Caves to the Dark Reaches
5. **The Wild's Language** — The Tunnel Keeper translates between the player and the Wild using the lantern's deep frequency
6. **The Tunnel Opens** — Post-Hollow Earth. The passages between the Inn and the Hollow Earth are now two-way

# THE HEARTH TENDER

## Character Summary
- **Zone:** Main Floor hearth (Level 3 — Matisse, joy, color, flow)
- **Linework:** Soft flowing (lore style — gentle, no hard edges)
- **Build:** Small, gentle, luminous eyes that see in the dark
- **Prop:** Small cloth bundle of collected lost things — buttons, coins, forgotten stones
- **Personality:** Devoted, selfless, observant, initially fearful. Sees what others miss.
- **Voice:** Quiet. Simple sentences. Says less than the Tunnel Keeper but means more. Every word is carefully placed.

## Relationship Stages
1. **Unnoticed** (first meeting — the Tender is part of the background)
2. **Acknowledged** (player speaks to the Tender — first time anyone has)
3. **Trusted** (player brings gifts, asks the Tender's name)
4. **Bonded** (the Tender reveals the Source Stone fragment)

---

## DIALOGUE TREE

### STAGE 1: UNNOTICED (First Meeting)

**Trigger:** Player approaches the hearth on the Main Floor. The Hearth Tender is there — small, sweeping ash, quiet. No interaction prompt appears unless the player lingers.

**Hearth Tender:** *(notices the player looking)* "...Oh. Hello. ...Sorry. I'll move." *(starts to step away from the fire)*

> **A) "Don't move. I didn't mean to interrupt."**
> **Hearth Tender:** *(pauses, surprised)* "...Nobody says that. ...People don't usually... I mean, I'm just... the fire needs tending. That's all I do. The fire needs things. Ash needs sweeping. The wood needs turning. ...I keep the fire and the fire keeps the room. That's the arrangement."
>
> **B) "What's in the bundle?"**
> **Hearth Tender:** *(clutches the cloth bundle)* "...Things. Things people drop. ...Buttons. Coins. ...A stone, once. Nobody comes back for them. So I keep them. ...Somebody should." *(beat)* "I don't steal them. I find them. After. After the people go. ...The fire sees everything. I just... clean up after what the fire sees."

> **C) [Say nothing, just sit by the fire]**
> **Hearth Tender:** *(long silence, then quietly)* "...That's nice. ...People don't sit. People pass through. The fire's just a thing they walk around. ...You're sitting." *(very long pause)* "The fire likes it when people sit. ...I can tell. The flames lean. When someone sits, they lean toward them. Like flowers. ...I'm sorry. That's a strange thing to say."

---

### STAGE 1: UNNOTICED (Second Visit)

**Trigger:** Player returns to the hearth. The Hearth Tender is there, as always.

**Hearth Tender:** "Back." *(small smile)* "The fire's better today. ...I think it knows you came back."

> **A) "You talk about the fire like it's alive."**
> **Hearth Tender:** "...Isn't it? It breathes. It eats. It grows. It dies if you don't feed it. ...It remembers. The fire burns different when the Inn is happy. When the Inn is quiet, the flames are quiet. When the Inn listens, the flames lean toward the door. ...I think the fire is the Inn's heartbeat. Not the 42Hz — that's the Inn's blood. The fire is the heartbeat. ...The Catalyst says I'm romanticizing combustion. The Catalyst is probably right. But the Catalyst hasn't sat with this fire every night for as long as I can remember."

> **B) "Have you always been here?"**
> **Hearth Tender:** "I don't remember not being here. ...The Crone says I was always here. The Firekeeper says I came with the hearth. I don't... I don't remember. I remember the fire. I remember the ash. I remember the sound it makes when it breathes. ...That's enough. ...That's more than enough."

> **C) "Do you see the things in the walls too?"**
> **Hearth Tender:** *(goes very still)* "...You see them? ...You see them." *(long pause)* "I thought it was just me. I thought... I thought the fire was doing something to my eyes. Making me see things in the stone. ...They walk through the walls. They don't see me. They don't see anything. They just... go where they used to go. Like they're still... doing what they did." *(beat)* "I've never told anyone that. ...The fire's the only one who knew. ...And you."

---

### STAGE 2: ACKNOWLEDGED (The Echoes Conversation)

**Trigger:** Player asks directly about the Echoes.

**Hearth Tender:** "I call them the ones in the walls. I don't know their real name. ...They've always been here. As long as the fire. Maybe longer. ...I think the fire KNOWS them. The fire leans toward the walls sometimes. Not toward people. Toward the walls. Like it's listening to someone on the other side." *(beat)* "When I first saw them, I was scared. Then I realized... they're not ghosts. Ghosts are... left over. These aren't left over. They're... playing. Like a song that plays the same way every time. Not stuck. Just... repeated. ...The Crone would say I'm anthropomorphizing mineral memory. The Crone is probably right. But the Crone hasn't watched them."

> **A) "What do they do?"**
> **Hearth Tender:** "They walk. They work. They... live. The same things, the same paths, the same motions. Through the walls, through the floor, through the ceiling. Like the Inn is their house and we're the ghosts. ...Some of them carry things. Tools. Stones. ...Some of them stop and look at things that aren't there anymore. ...Some of them smile. ...The smiling ones are the hardest. ...Because they're smiling at someone who left a very long time ago."

> **B) "Can I help them?"**
> **Hearth Tender:** *(looks up, eyes bright)* "...I don't know. I've never tried. ...But there's something. Sometimes, when I hold a stone near the wall — one of the ones from the bundle, one that hums — the ones in the walls... pause. They stop. Like they hear it. Like the stone is... tuning them in. ...I don't know what happens if you match the right stone to the right one. I've never had the right stone. ...You have a lot of stones." *(beat)* "Maybe you have the right one."

> **C) "Does anyone else know?"**
> **Hearth Tender:** "The Firekeeper knows. ...The Firekeeper sees everything by the fire — that's what the Firekeeper does. But the Firekeeper doesn't talk about them. The Firekeeper tells stories about other things — gods and monsters and legends. But not about the ones in the walls. I think... I think the Firekeeper is waiting for someone to ask. ...The Firekeeper is always waiting for someone to ask the right question. ...You ask good questions."

---

### STAGE 3: TRUSTED (The Name)

**Trigger:** Player has visited the hearth 5+ times and brought at least one gift (a stone, a flower from the World Grid, anything from the bundle categories).

**Hearth Tender:** *(holding the gift — a small blue flower from the World Grid — turning it in the firelight)* "You keep bringing things. ...Nobody brings me things. I'm the one who finds things. ...This is different." *(beat)* "You asked me my name once. I didn't answer. ...I don't have one. I never did. The Crone called me 'the fire-tender' and it became what I am. ...But if you're bringing me flowers, maybe I should have a name."

> **A) "What would you call yourself?"**
> **Hearth Tender:** *(long pause, looking into the fire)* "...Ember. ...No. That's too close to the fire. ...I'm not the fire. I just keep it." *(pause)* "...Wick. ...No. That's too small." *(very long pause)* "...I don't know. I've never needed one. The fire doesn't call me anything. The fire just... leans toward me. And that's enough." *(looks at the flower)* "But if YOU need to call me something... ...call me what the fire calls me." *(beat)* "I don't know what that is. ...But I think you'll know when you hear it."

> **B) "Tell me about the stone you found."**
> **Hearth Tender:** *(goes quiet — different quiet, deeper)* "...You know about that." *(beat)* "I found it in the ashes. Years ago. Before the Firekeeper came. Before the Crone started the archive. ...It was just there. In the coals. Glowing. Not like the fire glows — like IT glows. From inside. ...It hums. When the Inn is quiet, it hums. ...I kept it in the fire. I didn't know what else to do. It liked the heat. ...I think it came FROM the heat. From the deep. From under. ...I've never told anyone. ...It feels like... mine. But also... not mine. Like I'm keeping it for someone. Like it's waiting for someone to come claim it."
>
> **B1) "Can I see it?"**
> **Hearth Tender:** *(reaches into the deepest part of the hearth — the coals part, the fire opens like a curtain — and pulls out a stone that glows white-gold, pulsing at 42Hz)* "...Here. ...Be careful. ...It's the warmest thing in the Inn. Warmer than the fire. ...I think it's a piece of the origin. ...The Crone would say I'm romanticizing mineral thermodynamics. ...The Crone is probably right. But the Crone hasn't held it."

> **C) "Do you know what it is?"**
> **Hearth Tender:** "No. ...I know it hums at the same frequency as the Inn. I know it glows the same color as the ley lines. I know it's been in this fire longer than I have. ...I think it came up through the stone. Through the tunnels. The Tunnel Keeper knows about tunnels — maybe the stone traveled through them. Up and up and up until it came out in the one place that's warm enough. ...Fire is just fast warmth. This stone is slow warmth. ...I think they're the same thing at different speeds."

---

### STAGE 4: BONDED (The Song Complete)

**Trigger:** Player has completed the Inn Song and the Frozen Vine has played its 84Hz note. The Hearth Tender is at the hearth. The fire is different — quieter, deeper, like it's listening.

**Hearth Tender:** *(sitting by the fire, not tending it — just sitting)* "The fire changed. ...When the note played. The 84Hz. ...The fire leaned AWAY from the walls. Toward the middle of the room. Like it was... letting go of something. ...The ones in the walls — the Echoes — they all stopped. All at once. For the first time since I've been watching. They stopped. ...And then they started again. But different. ...I think they're... not repeating anymore. I think they're... listening." *(beat)* "You did something. ...Something big."

> **A) "I played the Song."**
> **Hearth Tender:** *(nods slowly)* "...I know. The fire told me. Not in words. In heat. The fire got warmer when you played. The whole Inn got warmer. Like the Inn was... answering." *(reaches into the hearth, pulls out the stone — it's brighter now, pulsing in time with something deep below)* "I think this is yours now. ...I think it was always yours. I was just keeping it warm." *(holds the stone out)* "Take it. ...I don't think it needs the fire anymore. I think it needs YOU. ...You're the fire now. ...You're the one who keeps the Inn warm."

> **B) "What will you do without it?"**
> **Hearth Tender:** "The same thing I always do. ...Tend the fire. Sweep the ash. Find the things people drop. ...The fire doesn't need a magic stone to burn. The fire burns because someone tends it. ...That's me. That's always been me." *(almost a smile)* "The stone was just... visiting. ...The fire is home."

> **C) "Thank you for keeping it safe."**
> **Hearth Tender:** *(looks down at the empty spot in the coals where the stone was)* "...You're welcome. ...It was never heavy. ...It was just warm. ...Like holding a small, quiet song. ...I'm glad it's going where it was always going. ...I'm glad I was the one who kept it." *(beat)* "Now sit. ...The fire wants to lean toward you. ...I think you've earned a warm seat."

---

### STAGE 4: BONDED (Final Visit)

**Trigger:** Player visits the hearth after all quests are complete. The Hearth Tender is sweeping ash, as always.

**Hearth Tender:** "The Firekeeper was here earlier. ...Said the Echoes are changing. Said they're not just repeating anymore. Said they're... responding. To the Song." *(sweeps)* "I told the Firekeeper I already knew. The Firekeeper said: 'Of course you did. You've been watching them longer than anyone.' ...That's the nicest thing the Firekeeper's ever said to me." *(sweeps)* "The Tunnel Keeper came up too. First time I've seen the Tunnel Keeper above the Catacombs. ...The Tunnel Keeper said the tunnels are quieter. I said the fire is warmer. The Tunnel Keeper said: 'Same thing, probably.' ...The Tunnel Keeper is probably right." *(sweeps)* "...Everybody's probably right. ...That's what the Song does. Makes everybody right at the same time."

*(long pause)*

"...You can sit anytime. ...The fire leans for you now. ...That's the highest compliment the fire gives."

## THE HEARTH TENDER — QUEST HOOKS

1. **The Ones in the Walls** — First mention of the Echoes (player's introduction to the Echo system)
2. **The Right Stone** — The Hearth Tender suggests matching stones to Echoes (introduces the frequency-matching mechanic)
3. **The Firekeeper's Question** — The Hearth Tender reveals the Firekeeper knows about the Echoes but is waiting for someone to ask (connects to the Firekeeper NPC)
4. **The Name** — Player earns the Hearth Tender's trust through gifts and presence (relationship quest)
5. **The Stone in the Ashes** — The Hearth Tender reveals the Source Stone fragment (key item for the main quest)
6. **The Handoff** — After the Inn Song is complete, the Hearth Tender gives the player the Source Stone fragment (merges with the Source Stone)
7. **The Fire Leans** — Post-game. The Hearth Tender confirms the Echoes are changing (world state change confirmation)

## CROSS-CHARACTER DIALOGUE (When Both Are Present)

**Trigger:** Late game, after the Tunnel Keeper has been to the Hollow Earth and the Hearth Tender has revealed the stone.

**Tunnel Keeper:** *(visiting the Main Floor for the first time in the story)* "So THIS is the fire. ...I've heard it. From three levels down. Through the stone. I always thought it was the 42Hz." *(looks at the hearth)* "...It's not. It's just a fire."

**Hearth Tender:** "Just a fire. ...And just tunnels. ...We both keep things that go down into the dark."

**Tunnel Keeper:** "...The Crone says I'm the one who maps the dark."

**Hearth Tender:** "The Crone says I'm the one who sweeps it."

**Tunnel Keeper:** *(almost laughs)* "...Same job."

**Hearth Tender:** "Same job."

*(They sit by the fire together. The fire leans toward both of them.)*
