# Geode Inn — The Raven's Angle
## A Projectile Physics Game Featuring Huginn, Muninn & The Owl

### The Concept

A projectile angle game (Angry Birds lineage) where the player launches Odin's chibi thieving ravens — Huginn (Thought) and Muninn (Memory) — at specific angles to steal stones from the Subtractive Family's architecture. The Owl watches. If the Owl sees the Raven, the stone goes back. The player must calculate the trajectory that threads the crack in the rock — the entrance to the cave, the temple, the cliff dwelling — without crossing the Owl's line of sight.

The physics is: real. The angle, the velocity, the gravity, the trajectory, the bounce. The game teaches projectile motion through the Inn's characters and architecture. The learning is: the adequate. The adequate is: the player who masters the angle has: mastered the physics. The physics is: the Inn's language — the Song is frequency, the architecture is geometry, the game is: the both.
