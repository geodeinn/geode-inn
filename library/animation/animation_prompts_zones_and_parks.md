## Part 1.1: Cosmic Locations Atmosphere Prompts

### 1. The Sun — The 42Hz Source & Galaxy Tone Generator
1. **Subject Name:** The Sun (Cosmic Layer Source)
2. **Visual Description:** A colossal, blinding star of pure radiance that serves as the galaxy's primary tone generator and largest celestial body in the Cosmic Layer. The solar surface is an undulating ocean of molten white-gold light, with vast, elegant prominence loops arching off the limb into the cosmic abyss. There are no physical surface contours or drawn boundaries; the star exists purely as brilliant, living light.
3. **Linework Style:** No-outline (cosmological pure light style — forms exist entirely through luminous gradients and radiant intensity without drawn boundaries).
4. **Color Palette:** Blazing gold (#FFD700), radiant pure white (#FFFFFF), core solar yellow (#FFF5B1), amber flare highlights (#FFBF00), deep cosmic indigo background (#0A0E2A).
5. **Lighting/Atmosphere:** Blinding central illumination emanating outward into deep space; soft coronal flares create an ethereal, high-energy aura that illuminates distant cosmic dust.
6. **Animation Notes:** The solar core breathes continuously in a rhythmic 42Hz pulse wave that propagates visible golden ripples across space. Solar prominence arches stretch, twist, and snap in slow motion, releasing micro-flares that drift away like glowing dust particles.
7. **Sound Connection:** Pure, unadulterated 42Hz fundamental sine tone — the foundational voice of the galaxy and source of the Inn's frequency chain.
8. **Special Notes:** Central node of "The First Note" quest line. Completes the cosmic loop: Galaxy → Sun (42Hz) → Water → Stones → Inn → Player → Soulstone → Galaxy.

7. **Sound Connection:** 50Hz heartbeat pulse (the Pirate's drum frequency). Intersects with the Sun's 42Hz tone to generate an 8Hz theta/dream frequency wave.
8. **Special Notes:** First stop beyond the Inn; accessible via the Pirate's Bodhrán. Links horizontal rhythm (Moon/tides) to vertical tone (Sun/galaxy).
