# Geode Inn — Master Animation Brief

## Shared Aesthetic Rules (Apply to ALL Prompts)

### Linework-as-UI System
- **Heavy cel-shaded** — tradesmen, craftspeople, working characters (Jay, Solstara, Blacksmith, Goldsmith)
- **Soft flowing** — lore characters, scholars, archive keepers (Crone, Beverley, Naut)
- **Sharp/angular** — guardians, warriors, protectors (Gargoyles, Griffin, Morrigan)
- **No-outline** — cosmological beings, divine entities, the sky gods (Allfather, Freyr, Freyja, Bramble, Cosmic Crew)
- **Fragmented** — characters in crisis, unstable identities (The Shifting Man, characters between states)

### Celebrity Softening Rule
- ALL celebrity-inspired characters have their facial features softened — evoke presence through posture, costume, gesture, and voice description, NOT through portrait likeness
- The ONLY exception: Anthony Hopkins' eyes — the clear, seeing gaze (Silence of the Lambs clarity) appears on the Crone, Charon, and the Librarian

### Color Architecture
- Overall Inn palette: warm amber, gold, cream — "hobbit-hole cozy" base
- Cosmology: deep blue-black, starlight, gold
- Clockwork Skies: brass, copper, sky-blue, metallic
- Main Floor: dark wood, firelight amber, hearth glow
- Campgrounds: green, earth tones, market color, daylight
- Staircase: stone gray transitioning to warm gold as it ascends
- Catacombs: dusty amber, deep stone, bronze light
- Faerie Kingdom: Seelie (golden-green summer) / Unseelie (shadow-ice winter)
- Water Zone: gothic blue-green, progressively darker through 7 layers
- Undercity: gothic stone with Klimt gold — vaulted gold ceilings, swirling patterns
- Cosmic Layer: deep space black, no-outline pure light, Brísingamen Nebulae (blue/gold/red/purple)

### Sound Architecture
- 42Hz = the Source frequency (tone axis, vertical — galaxy → water → stones → Inn → player)
- 50Hz = the Heartbeat (rhythm axis, horizontal — Moon → tides → Pirate → Inn)
- 42+50 = 8Hz = theta frequency (dreams)
- Each stone has a unique frequency based on Mohs hardness
- The Inn Song = all 9 zones' frequencies in one composition

### Animation Prompt Format
Each prompt should include:
1. **Subject name**
2. **Visual description** — physical appearance, clothing, pose, expression (2-3 sentences)
3. **Linework style** — which of the 5 styles applies and why
4. **Color palette** — specific colors and tones
5. **Lighting/atmosphere** — mood, light direction, ambient quality
6. **Animation notes** — idle motion, key gesture, behavioral tells, how the character moves
7. **Sound connection** — any frequency or audio element tied to this character/zone
8. **Special notes** — celebrity softening, zone context, narrative role

### The Baby Dragon Mascot
- Heraldic-style dragon, Toothless-like cat expression (curiosity, trustworthiness)
- Small, expressive, cute but not cartoonish — the Inn's warmth anchor
- Lives in a geode on the Main Floor; cave fills with gems as player progresses
- Idle: sleep-curled around a glowing stone, one eye cracking open when approached
- Color: deep amethyst purple with gold-flecked scales

### Zone Emotional Arc
refuge (Staircase) → home (Main Floor) → world (all zones) → self (Undercity) → cosmos (Cosmic Layer)
