## FORMAT REMINDER

Each stone needs:
- **Mind:** Metaphysical/psychological properties (2-4 sentences in the Inn's voice)
- **Body:** Physical healing properties (2-4 sentences in the Inn's voice)
- **Category:** Stone family (Agate, Tourmaline, Opal, etc.)
- **StoneFamily:** Mineralogical family (Chalcedony, Feldspar, Silica, etc.)

The Inn's voice for stone descriptions: scholarly, warm, direct. Not woo-woo — grounded metaphysical. Think: a knowledgeable jeweler who has worked with the stone for years, not a crystal shop poster.
