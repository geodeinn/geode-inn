## ART DIRECTION NOTES

All 16 images follow the established Geode Inn art hierarchy:
- **Klimt** = architecture (structural gold, gothic stone, weight of history)
- **Royo** = inhabitants (dark fantasy figures, dramatic chiaroscuro, powerful presences)
- **Celtic knotwork** = universal framing (gold inlay on dark stone with gothic arches)
- **Linework-as-UI** = not directly visible in concept art but informs the rendering style
- **Shadow Inn** = deep gold, doubled intensity, the finished/completed state
- **Celebrity features** = softened, evoked through posture and costume

Zone-specific layers:
- Fae Kingdom: crystal, ice, winter court
- Peru: shanshui ink-wash, Andean terraces
- Egypt: hieroglyphics, tomb architecture, desert amber
- Water Zone: gothic anime (Vampire Hunter D), bioluminescent ice
- Undercity: Klimt 'Death and Life' at maximum intensity
- Canopy City: living tree architecture, mycorrhizal network
- Clockwork: precision mechanisms, brass and bronze
- Greece: classical architecture, pantheon of gods
