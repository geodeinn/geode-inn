## NARRATIVE VOICE

The dialogue tone for the entire game is modeled on the original tabletop questline "Festival in the Middle of a Raid." The voice is clever, direct, foreshadowing, descriptive. Reading it is like reading "The Name of the Wind" — lyrical but grounded, mythic but personal, conversational but layered with subtext. Dialogue uses natural pauses (ellipses) to create breath and timing. Every line carries surface meaning AND deeper resonance.

The 42Hz Song is the answer. The universe's punchline. Douglas Adams hid the answer to life, the universe, and everything in a radio show. The Geode Inn hides it in the foundational frequency of a living building. The joke: the answer was always a frequency. You just had to know how to listen.

## ARCHITECTURAL TEMPLATE SYSTEM (Zone Templates)

**Established August 26-27, 2026.** Every zone in the Geode Inn now has a complete architectural template derived from a character home. The pipeline: Character Home → Interactive Item Templates → Player-Buildable Structures. Homes teach by example. Players build by imitation, then innovation.

| Staircase | Clockmaker's Workshop | Evolving (earth→light) | ALL methods in sequence | Build by transforming | `geode_inn_staircase_item_templates.md` |
| Fae Kingdom | The Threshold + Courts | Split (summer/winter) | Grow / Carve / Place | Build by choosing | `geode_inn_fae_kingdom_item_templates.md` |
| Lighthouse | Keeper's Chamber | Directed (the beam) | Maintain (the Vigil) | Build by holding | `geode_inn_lighthouse_item_templates.md` |

### Each Zone Template Includes:
- Material palette (unique to each zone)
- Lighting system (5-6 light types per zone, with interaction rules)
- 8 furniture templates (derived from the character home)
- Structural templates (walls, doorways, floors, ceilings)
- Build rules (7-8 zone-specific constraints)
- Player discovery flow (step-by-step learning progression)
- Zone comparison table (how all zones differ)

### Key Design Principles
- **Light is the primary differentiator** — each zone has a fundamentally different relationship with light (crafted, alive, living, celestial, scarce, evolving, split, directed)
- **Build method varies by zone** — construct, shape+grow, expose, carve, transform, choose, maintain
- **The 42Hz Song manifests differently per zone** — faint (Undercity/Hearth), amplified (Water), loudest (Cosmology), felt (Catacombs), transitioning (Staircase), deep through roots (Fae Kingdom), felt through sea stone (Lighthouse)
- **Character homes are the templates** — each zone's architectural language flows from one character's home, making the system personal before it's mechanical
