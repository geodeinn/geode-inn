## WHAT ARE MINERAL CHARM SPIRITS?

When a player holds a stone and activates the Stone Vibration Touch System, the stone's frequency becomes visible — a small elemental being appears above the stone. This is the stone's spirit: the visual embodiment of its frequency, its mineral identity, its character.

Think kodama from Princess Mononoke — small presences that reflect the essence of their stone. Some are playful. Some are solemn. Some are barely visible. They're not full characters — they're presences. Small enough to sit in your palm, powerful enough to change how you hear.

**The Spirit is the Stone's Identity Made Visible.** Color is cosmetic. Vibration is identity. The spirit IS the vibration.

## THE FOUR SPIRIT CLASSES (by IEM Driver Type)

Each stone's IEM driver type determines its spirit's visual class — the fundamental way the spirit manifests.

### Class 1: THE PRESENT (Balanced Armature — 100 spirits)
**Inn Layer:** Characters — the presences you meet
**Sensation:** "A clear, precise tone. You hear this stone the way you hear a voice in a quiet room — present, distinct, intimate."
**Visual Style:** Clear, defined cel-shaded linework. Distinct outlines. The most humanoid/personality-driven of the four classes. These spirits have FACES. They have expressions. They look back at you.
**Form:** Small humanoid or creature forms, 4-8 inches tall. Crystalline bodies with visible mineral structure. Each one has a distinct personality expression — joy, calm, curiosity, vigilance, sorrow, wonder.
**Color:** Full color matching the stone's real-world palette.
**Linework:** Defined outlines, cel-shaded. Clear shapes. The "voice in a quiet room" rendered as a small person you can see clearly.
**Frequency Visualization:** Ripples emanate from the spirit's body — concentric waves matching the stone's frequency. Higher frequencies = faster, tighter ripples. Lower frequencies = slower, wider waves.

### Class 2: THE DENSE (Dynamic Driver — 55 spirits)
**Inn Layer:** Matter — the stones you hold
**Sensation:** "A warm tone you both hear and feel. It resonates in the throat and sternum, the way a low bell carries through a stone wall."
**Visual Style:** Heavier, more textured linework. Mineral-like bodies. More creature than person. These spirits have MASS. They look like they weigh something.
**Form:** Denser, more compact forms. Stone-bodied creatures, mineral golems, crystal beasts. 3-6 inches tall but visually heavy. Less humanoid — more geological. Some are angular, some are rounded, all are SOLID.
**Color:** Deep, saturated colors matching the stone's real palette. Darker tones, richer hues.
**Linework:** Heavy, weighty strokes. Textured surfaces showing the mineral's physical properties (crystal faces, grain, inclusions). The "low bell through stone" rendered as a creature made OF stone.
**Frequency Visualization:** Pulses rather than ripples. The spirit's body visibly pulses/throbs with its frequency. The whole form vibrates. Lower frequencies = deeper, slower pulses.

### Class 3: THE DEEP (Bone Conduction — 6 spirits)
**Inn Layer:** Catacombs — the deep knowing
**Sensation:** "You feel this stone in your body before you hear it. The vibration settles in your chest and hands."
**Visual Style:** Minimal linework. Mostly shadow. Dark forms with only glowing accents — eyes, cracks, frequency lines. Ancient. These spirits are barely there.
**Form:** Shadow shapes. Silhouettes with depth. 6-12 inches tall but translucent — you can see through them. Only the glowing frequency lines and eyes are fully visible. The rest is suggestion.
**Color:** Black, dark gray, with metallic glowing accents (gold, copper, silver, iron — matching the metal's color).
**Linework:** Fragmented. Broken lines. The spirit is held together by its frequency, not by its form. The breaks in the outline ARE the frequency pattern.
**Frequency Visualization:** The spirit IS the frequency — the glowing lines trace the frequency pattern through the shadow body. The body doesn't pulse; the lines shift and realign. You FEEL this spirit before you see it.

### Class 4: THE DISTANT (Electrostatic — 3 spirits)
**Inn Layer:** Cosmology — the starlight you reach for
**Sensation:** "A high, thin presence at the edge of perception. You sense this stone more than hear it, the way you sense someone watching you from very far away."
**Visual Style:** No outline. Pure luminescence. These spirits have no body — only light. The rarest, most alien of the four classes.
**Form:** Light forms. Shapes made of glow and nothing else. 2-4 inches, but they project much larger — their light reaches farther than their form. No eyes, no face, no expression. Just light, and the light HAS character.
**Color:** Luminous — amber, gold, iridescent. The light shifts and refracts like a gem.
**Linework:** No linework. No outline at all. Pure gradient and glow. The no-outline cosmological style taken to its furthest point.
**Frequency Visualization:** The light itself IS the frequency. The spirit's glow pulses at the stone's frequency. These spirits are barely on the visible spectrum — they exist at the edge of what the eye can catch.

## SPIRIT ANATOMY

Every spirit, regardless of class, has these components:

### 1. Body Form
The spirit's basic shape — determined by mineral family (see Family Archetypes below).

### 2. Color Palette
Matches the stone's real-world color range. The spirit's body color comes directly from the mineral's natural color. This is not a design choice — it's mineralogical accuracy.

### 3. Frequency Signature
The visible manifestation of the stone's Hz — ripples (BA), pulses (DD), shifting lines (BC), or light pulses (EST). Each stone's unique frequency creates a unique visual signature.

### 4. Mineral Element
Each spirit visibly incorporates its mineral's crystal structure or physical property:
- Hexagonal crystals (quartz, beryl, tourmaline) → spiraling/prismatic body geometry
- Cubic crystals (fluorite, garnet, pyrite, diamond) → angular/faceted body geometry
- Monoclinic crystals (jade, moonstone, azurite) → flowing/asymmetric forms
- Orthorhombic crystals (topaz, olivine/peridot, andalusite) → elongated/prismatic forms
- Amorphous (opal, obsidian, glass) → flowing, non-geometric forms
- Organic (amber, pearl, jet, coral) → soft, organic, curved forms
- Metallic (gold, copper, platinum, pyrite, hematite) → liquid metal surfaces

### 5. Personality Pose
The spirit's posture/expression reflects the stone's metaphysical properties:
- Protection stones → defensive, shielded posture
- Love/heart stones → open, reaching posture
- Grounding stones → rooted, settled posture
- Transformation stones → mid-motion, shifting posture
- Intuition stones → listening, attentive posture
- Communication stones → vocal, outward-facing posture
- Calm/meditation stones → still, serene posture
- Energy/vitality stones → dynamic, explosive posture

## FAMILY ARCHETYPES

Each mineral family shares a base spirit form. Family members are VARIATIONS of the archetype — the same body type with different colors, frequencies, and personalities.

### Quartz Family (12 spirits, all BA)
**Archetype:** The Prism Singer — a small crystalline humanoid with a prismatic body. Light refracts through their transparent/ translucent form, splitting into spectral colors. Each variety has a different dominant color (amethyst=purple, citrine=yellow, rose=pink, smoky=brown, clear=white). Their body is hexagonal — visible crystal columns forming their torso and limbs. They SING — their mouths open and the frequency is visible as light waves emanating from their throat.

### Chalcedony/Agate Family (20 spirits, all BA)
**Archetype:** The Banded Dancer — a small flowing spirit with visible bands/layers running through their body. Their form is rounded, smooth, organic — not angular like quartz. The bands are their defining feature: concentric rings of color that shift and rotate as they move. Each agate variety has different band patterns (moss agate = green inclusions like vines, fire agate = iridescent rainbow bands, crazy lace = complex zigzag patterns). They DANCE — their movement is flowing, circular, like the bands themselves in motion.

### Jasper Family (7 spirits, all BA)
**Archetype:** The Earth Walker — a stocky, grounded spirit with patterned skin. Jaspers are EARTH — their bodies look like miniature landscapes. Picture jasper has actual landscape scenes on their skin. Ocean jasper has circular orbs like bubbles. Leopard skin has spots. They WALK — slow, deliberate, rooted. They don't float like other spirits; they stand on surfaces.

### Tiger Eye Family (6 spirits, BA + 1 DD)
**Archetype:** The Watcher — a feline-formed spirit with chatoyant eyes. Their bodies are fibrous — visible parallel strands running through their form, catching light like the real stone's chatoyancy. Their eyes are their defining feature: a single luminous band that catches light and follows you. Blue tiger's eye = blue-toned, red = warm orange, hawk's eye = blue-gray. Tiger Iron (DD) is denser, heavier, with visible iron bands.

### Garnet Family (5 spirits, all DD)
**Archetype:** The Faceted Guardian — an angular, dodecahedral spirit. Garnet spirits are the most geometric of the DD class — their bodies are visibly faceted, like a cut gem. Twelve faces, each reflecting a different angle of light. Deep red is the dominant color, but each variety shifts (grossular=green, hessonite=orange-brown, spessartite=orange, uvarovite=emerald green, Mozambique=deep red). They STAND — solid, unmoving, like guards.

### Topaz Family (4 spirits, all DD)
**Archetype:** The Prismatic Sentinel — an elongated, orthorhombic spirit. Topaz spirits are TALL for their class — taller and thinner than other DD spirits. Their bodies are prismatic, with a visible cleavage plane that defines their posture. They stand straight, almost rigid. Blue topaz = ice-blue, imperial=golden, clear=white. They emanate a cold clarity.

### Tourmaline Family (6 spirits, all DD)
**Archetype:** The Column Dancer — an elongated, prismatic spirit with visible color zoning. Tourmaline crystals grow in columns with different colors at top and bottom — tourmaline spirits have the same: their upper and lower halves are different colors. Watermelon tourmaline is the clearest example: pink top, green bottom. Black tourmaline is dark and protective, a shield-form. They ROTATE — their column bodies spin slowly, showing their color zones.

### Opal Family (5 spirits, 4 BA + 1 EST)
**Archetype:** The Shifting One — a form that doesn't hold still. Opal spirits are the most fluid — their bodies shift, ripple, and reform constantly. They display play-of-color: flashes of spectral light that move across their surface. Each opal variety flashes different colors (black opal=dark body with vivid flashes, fire opal=warm orange-red, water opal=transparent with blue flashes). Water Opal (EST) is the most extreme — barely a body at all, just light. They SHIMMER — their constant motion IS their personality.

### Jade Family (5 spirits, 3 BA + 2 DD)
**Archetype:** The Smooth One — a rounded, polished spirit with a soapy/waxy surface texture. Jade spirits are the most TACTILE — they look smooth, like river-worn stones. Their bodies are rounded, organic, without sharp edges. Green is the dominant color, but white jade, lavender jade exist. Guatemalan jade (blue-green), Jadeite (vivid green), Nephrite (deep green), White Jade (creamy). They REST — still, calm, unhurried. The calmest spirits in the catalog.

### Feldspar Family (6 spirits, all BA)
**Archetype:** The Light Catcher — a spirit with labradorescent/adularescent skin. Feldspar spirits catch and release light — their surfaces flash with internal light that moves when viewed from different angles. Moonstone spirits have a blue-white schiller. Labradorite has a green-gold labradorescence. Sunstone has a warm golden spangle effect. Amazonite has a turquoise-blue solid color. They GLISTEN — their skin constantly catches and releases light.

### Obsidian Family (3 spirits, all BA)
**Archetype:** The Glass One — a sharp, volcanic glass spirit. Obsidian spirits are the most DANGEROUS-looking of the BA class — their bodies are volcanic glass, with visible conchoidal fracture patterns (the curved break patterns of glass). They have edges. Black is the base, but gold sheen has gold flecks, rainbow has iridescent bands, snowflake has white cristobalite spots. They OBSERVE — still, reflective, like dark mirrors.

### Copper Mineral Family (8 spirits, BA + DD)
**Archetype:** The Blue-Green One — spirits with copper-sulfide coloring. Malachite (green bands), azurite (deep blue crystals), chrysocolla (blue-green), lapis lazuli (deep blue with gold flecks), larimar (blue-white), sodalite (blue with white veins). These spirits share a color family — the blue-green of copper minerals. Their bodies show mineral formations: malachite has banded rings, azurite has crystal clusters, lapis has gold flecks (pyrite inclusions). They PULSE with a warm copper undertone — the copper frequency visible beneath their blue-green surface.

### Metal/Element Family (7 spirits, 6 BC + 1 DD)
**Archetype (BC):** The Shadow Metal — the deepest spirits. Barely visible shadow forms with metallic glowing veins. Gold spirit = warm gold glow, copper = orange-red, platinum = white-silver, hematite = dark red-gray, pyrite = brassy gold, cuprite = deep red. These spirits are ANCIENT — they move slowly, if at all. The metal IS their frequency — their body is shadow, their metal veins are the frequency made visible.
**Covellite (DD):** Dense, dark, with an iridescent purple-blue sheen. More solid than the BC metals, but still dark.

### Fluorite Family (3 spirits, all DD)
**Archetype:** The Cube — a cubic-crystal spirit. Fluorite spirits have the most obvious crystal form: cubes. Their bodies are literally cubic — stacked, interlocking cubes of translucent mineral. Green fluorite = green cubes, purple = violet cubes, rainbow = multiple colored cubes stacked. They STACK — they can rearrange their cube body into different configurations.

### Kyanite Family (3 spirits, all DD)
**Archetype:** The Blade — a flat, bladed crystal spirit. Kyanite spirits are THIN and FLAT — like the real mineral's bladed crystal habit. Blue kyanite = blue blade, orange = orange, moss = green. They have a unique property: two different hardnesses in two directions (the kyanite chord system — 131+270+420 Hz). Their spirits reflect this: they have two postures, two "directions" they can face, each with a different energy.

### Beryl Family (3 spirits, all DD)
**Archetype:** The Hexagonal Column — a tall, hexagonal-prism spirit. Emerald = deep green with visible inclusions (the "jardin"), morganite = pink, aquamarine = blue-green. Their bodies are perfect hexagonal columns. They STAND — the tallest of the DD spirits, elegant and structured.

### Precious Stones (5 spirits, all DD)
**Ruby, Sapphire (2), Diamond, Spinel** — each unique but sharing the "precious" quality: harder, denser, more brilliant than other spirits. Ruby = red corundum with visible rutile silk. Sapphire = blue corundum with a star effect (asterism) in the Tundra variety. Diamond = the most brilliant spirit — light bends through their form in complex patterns. Spinel = octahedral form, bright red.

### Organic Family (4 spirits, mixed)
**Amber (EST):** Pure golden light. The light contains shadows — ancient insects trapped in the glow. The oldest light in the catalog.
**Jet (EST):** Pure black light — an absence that glows. The mirror of amber. Very hard to see.
**Pearl (BA):** A round, luminous spirit. Perfectly smooth, perfectly round, with a pearlescent sheen. The gentlest BA spirit.
**Abalone (BA):** A shell-formed spirit with iridescent nacre. Constantly shifting rainbow colors. The most colorful organic spirit.

### Fossil Family (6 spirits, mixed)
**Petrified Wood (BA):** A spirit that looks like a tree made of crystal. The wood grain is visible but the body is quartz. The transformation is visible — organic past, mineral present.
**Petrified Palm Wood (BA):** Like petrified wood but with the distinctive dotted cellular pattern of palm.
**Ammonite (BA):** A spiral-formed spirit. The shell is visible, coiled, mineralized. The chambers glow with different frequencies.
**Ammolite (BA):** An ammonite covered in iridescent aragonite — the spiral shell flashes with spectral color.
**Fossil Agate (Turritella) (BA):** A shell-formed spirit where the original snail shell is visible inside agate. The fossil IS the spirit's core.
**Fossil Coprolite (DD):** A dense, grounded spirit. The most humble of the fossils — an ordinary thing preserved through deep time. The spirit's personality is quiet pride in its own ordinariness.

### Tektite Family (2 spirits, both BA)
**Moldavite (BA):** A green glass spirit — the only tektite with a distinct color. Formed by meteorite impact. Their body has the distinctive "splatter" texture of moldavite. Their frequency is extraterrestrial — they don't belong to Earth.
**Tektite (BA):** A black glass spirit. Darker than obsidian, with a pitted surface (the "thumbprint" texture of meteorite glass). Their origin is cosmic — their spirit feels distant, alien, reaching.

## SPIRIT RARITY SYSTEM

Each spirit has a rarity tier that affects its visual presence:

- **Common (white glow):** The most common stones. Clear, bright, present. 70+ spirits.
- **Uncommon (blue glow):** Less common. Slightly more detailed, more distinct personality. 50+ spirits.
- **Rare (gold glow):** Hard to find. More elaborate spirit forms, more visual detail. 25+ spirits.
- **Legendary (purple glow):** The rarest stones. Spirits have unique features — extra limbs, multiple forms, rare colors. 12+ spirits.
- **Unique (rainbow glow):** One-of-a-kind stones. The spirit is unlike any other. 5 spirits (Source Stone, Moldavite, Diamond, Ammolite, Alexandrite).

Rarity glow color appears as a halo around the spirit, visible when the spirit is "at rest" (not actively vibrating).

## GENERATION ORDER

Spirits should be generated in this order for maximum visual consistency:

1. **Family archetypes first** — generate one "base" spirit per family to establish the visual language
2. **Variations within family** — generate the rest of the family using the archetype as reference
3. **BC spirits (6)** — generate as a group — their shadow style is unique
4. **EST spirits (3)** — generate last — their light-only style is the most challenging
