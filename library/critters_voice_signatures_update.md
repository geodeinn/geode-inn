# Geode Inn — Critter Additions & Voice Signature Catalog

## New Critters (Additions to the 200+ Creature Roster)

### 1. Axolotl
- **Zone:** Tidal Caves (Lewis)
- **Level:** Water Zone (downstairs)
- **Type:** Ambient companion (non-combat, non-quest)
- **Behavior:** Drifts through submerged cave pools. Never transforms — permanently neotenic. Regenerates limbs if injured (visible mechanic: if the player accidentally disturbs one, it regrows the lost part over several game days).
- **Lore Connection:** The axolotl is the Tidal Caves' living fossil — a creature that chose to never grow up. In the Inn's mythology, the axolotl represents the choice to remain in a state of potential rather than commit to a final form. The Whisper Layer notices the axolotl as the only creature in the Inn that hasn't "decided what it wants to be."
- **Visual:** Pink-white translucent skin, frilly gills, perpetual smile. Small (6 inches). Moves slowly. Glows faintly in dark cave water.
- **Interaction:** Player can observe. If the player sits still near the pool for 30 seconds, the axolotl approaches. No reward, no quest — just a moment of stillness. (42Hz: the grow frequency. The axolotl is growing without changing.)
- **Artist Style:** Lewis (Tidal Caves) — gothic anime water rendering. The axolotl glows against dark cave water.

### 2. Gentle Mantis Shrimp
- **Zone:** Atlantis (Royo)
- **Level:** Water Zone (downstairs)
- **Type:** Ambient companion (non-combat)
- **Behavior:** Sits on coral formations, displaying its full color spectrum. Does NOT punch — this is the gentle cousin. The aggressive mantis shrimp species exist in deeper waters (Whisper Layer territory). This one uses its color vision for beauty, not hunting.
- **Lore Connection:** The mantis shrimp sees sixteen colors (humans see three). In the Inn's mythology, the gentle mantis shrimp is the only creature that can perceive the full resonance spectrum — it sees the 42Hz frequency as a visible color. The bead composer's color palette is calibrated to what the mantis shrimp sees. The creature is a living tuning fork for the Song.
- **Visual:** Peacock mantis shrimp — iridescent green, blue, orange, red. Large compound eyes that track the player. Four inches. Displays ripple colors when approached.
- **Interaction:** Player can observe the color display. If the player plays a bead/stone frequency near the mantis shrimp, it responds by displaying the corresponding color. (The creature is a frequency-to-color translator — a living visualizer of the Song.)
- **Artist Style:** Royo (Atlantis) — hyper-detailed iridescence against dark water.

### 3. African Rain Frog
- **Zone:** Bayou (Don't Starve)
- **Level:** Main Floor / World Map (Bayou)
- **Type:** Ambient companion (non-combat, comedic relief)
- **Behavior:** Sits in mud burrows at the edge of the Bayou. When disturbed, puffs up into a round, grumpy ball and makes a high-pitched squeak (like a dog toy). Burrows backward into the mud when it wants to disappear. Emerges after rain.
- **Lore Connection:** The rain frog is the Bayou's weather prophet — it emerges before rain, signaling the bog's renewal cycle. In the Inn's mythology, the rain frog is the Bog Fairy Golem's (Krista's avatar) smallest companion. It doesn't know it's important. It just knows when water is coming. The 42Hz frequency makes it emerge; the 84Hz frequency makes it burrow.
- **Visual:** Round, wrinkled, sand-colored. Permanently grumpy expression. Two inches tall. Looks like a living potato with legs.
- **Interaction:** Player can poke (gently) — frog puffs up and squeaks. If the player places a water offering near the burrow, the frog emerges and sits beside it for one minute before re-burrowing. No quest, no reward — just a tiny grumpy moment.
- **Artist Style:** Don't Starve (Bayou) — gothic survival aesthetic. The frog is simultaneously cute and slightly unsettling.

## Voice Signature Catalog

Each actor's voice signature is a reference profile for NPC dialogue. This is not casting — it's a voice DNA mapping that guides dialogue tone, cadence, and delivery style. Facial features are softened per the standing instruction; the voice signature defines the character's sound, not their face.

### 1. Sissy Spacek
- **Voice DNA:** Soft, breathy, slightly ethereal. Southern warmth with a ghostly quality. Speaks slowly, as if each word has weight. Capable of unsettling stillness — the voice gets quieter when the character is serious, not louder.
- **Assigned NPCs:** The Reader (cosmology level — the Gaiman-incognito character. Spacek's ethereal quality fits the one-line-per-visit mystic who sits in the window-seat nook). Also suitable for any ghost or memory NPC in the Catacombs.
- **Cadence:** Slow. Pauses between sentences. Volume decreases with intensity.

### 2. James Earl Jones
- **Voice DNA:** Deep, resonant, commanding. A voice that fills a room without effort. Every word sounds important. Capable of terrifying authority and gentle warmth in the same sentence. The voice of something ancient that has seen everything.
- **Assigned NPCs:** The Allfather (Odin's cosmology-level full-presence form). The resonance fits a deity who speaks rarely and means everything. Also suitable for any guardian Ent or ancient stone creature.
- **Cadence:** Measured. Low register. Silence is part of the delivery.

### 3. Sam Elliott
- **Voice DNA:** Gravelly Western drawl. Warm, weathered, unhurried. Every sentence sounds like it ends with an unspoken "partner." The voice of someone who has been outside for decades and has opinions about it.
- **Assigned NPCs:** Colorado Plateau zone NPC (Dixon aesthetic). The frontier park ranger type who knows every rock and trail. Also suitable for the Superstition Mountains guide.
- **Cadence:** Long vowels. Gravel on the consonants. Comfortable silence.

### 4. Edward James Olmos
- **Voice DNA:** Weathered, measured, intense. A voice that sounds like carved stone — each word deliberate. Capable of immense gravitas in very few syllables. When this voice says something, you believe it.
- **Assigned NPCs:** Peru/Andean zone NPC (Guo Xi aesthetic). A keeper of ancient knowledge who speaks only when necessary. Also suitable for a Mesoamerican scholar in the Archive.
- **Cadence:** Clipped. Intense eye contact. Minimal words, maximum impact.

### 5. Katey Sagal
- **Voice DNA:** Husky, sharp, confident. A voice that cuts through noise. Sardonic warmth — the voice of someone who has seen everything and finds it funny. Capable of sudden tenderness that surprises because the sharpness is the default.
- **Assigned NPCs:** Bethany (tavern manager — Jay's wife). The voice of someone who runs a room full of loud people and makes it look easy. Also suitable for any strong female NPC in the main floor or market areas.
- **Cadence:** Quick. Sardonic pauses. Drops volume for the sincere moments.

### 6. Lauren Bacall
- **Voice DNA:** Smoky, low, direct. Classic noir. The voice of someone who knows more than they're telling and finds that amusing. Capable of both threat and invitation in the same breath.
- **Assigned NPCs:** Undercity NPC. A figure in the shadows who trades in information. Also suitable for any mysterious figure in the Catacombs or a Whisper Layer contact.
- **Cadence:** Low register. Direct. The pauses are the threat.

### 7. Audrey Hepburn
- **Voice DNA:** Refined, lightly accented, warm. Precision with elegance. The voice of someone who has been educated by the world and softened by it. Never hurried. Every sentence sounds like it was rehearsed but somehow genuine.
- **Assigned NPCs:** Garden zone NPC (Van Gogh aesthetic). A cultivator or keeper of growing things who speaks with gentle authority. Also suitable for the Meadery host or any cosmopolitan character.
- **Cadence:** Unhurried. Musical. Slight accent on consonants.

### 8. Rex Harrison
- **Voice DNA:** Precise, clipped, theatrical. Speaks in declarations. The voice of someone who has read everything and has opinions about all of it. Charismatic without trying — the authority comes from certainty, not volume.
- **Assigned NPCs:** Archive NPC (the scholarly librarian type — different species of authority from Beverley's Anne Robinson cadence). A keeper of texts who declaims rather than discusses. Also suitable for the Spiral Gallery curator.
- **Cadence:** Declarative. Rapid-fire precision. No filler words.

### 9. Ian McKellen
- **Voice DNA:** Theatrical, rich, Shakespearean. A voice that can make a menu sound like a soliloquy. Capable of both towering rage and intimate whisper. The voice of someone who has performed every role and remembers them all.
- **Assigned NPCs:** Staircase (alchemy) NPC. The master alchemist who speaks in transformations. Also suitable for a Fae Kingdom elder or any ancient magical character.
- **Cadence:** Dramatic. Wide dynamic range. Pauses are theatrical, not hesitant.

### 10. Jennifer Hale
- **Voice DNA:** Versatile, warm, professional. The voice of competence — someone who is good at everything and makes you feel like you can be too. Capable of authority, warmth, urgency, and quiet reflection without changing character.
- **Assigned NPCs:** Player-facing guide/tutorial NPC. The voice that welcomes new players to the Inn and explains how things work. Also suitable for a versatile quest-giver who appears in multiple zones. (Hale's voice acting range makes this the most flexible signature in the catalog.)
- **Cadence:** Clear. Warm. Adapts to context without losing identity.

## Voice Signature Assignment Rules

1. Voice signatures define cadence, tone, and delivery style — not appearance
2. Facial features of the referenced actor are softened per standing instruction (Hopkins' eyes are the only exception)
3. Multiple NPCs can share a voice signature family (e.g., multiple "gravelly frontier" types share Elliott's DNA)
4. A voice signature can be layered with the NPC's cultural accent (e.g., McKellen's theatrical cadence + Irish accent for an alchemy scholar)
5. Jennifer Hale's signature is the default for any NPC without a specifically assigned voice
6. Voice signatures are references for dialogue writing, not audio casting — they guide how text reads, not who records it
