## 1. GEODE_INN_REDWOOD_CATHEDRAL.MD — THE REDWOOD CATHEDRAL (MAJOR ZONE)

### Core Overview & Solidification
The Redwood Cathedral ("the Above" to the Queen of Roots) is a massive primary zone consisting of ancient 2,000-year-old redwoods. It represents pure exploration, vertical climbing ("faith climbing" with no fall damage), and sunken Pit House construction. The tree growth rings serve as historical messengers and environmental records. 

Extending from the Pit House philosophy is the **Native Materials Building System** spanning eight regional biomes (Norse Log Cabins, Celtic Stone Masonry, British Thatching, Woodland Wigwams, Central Asian Yurts, Mediterranean Stucco), advancing through the **Castle Tier** (assisted by companions Hedley and Hob / formerly Hoggle and Dobby), the **Sacred Tier** (Gothic Cathedrals and Rock-Hewn Ethiopian Churches), the **Mausoleum Tier** (building for the dead), and culminating in the **Subtractive Family** (cliff-dwelling civilizations built directly into living rock like the Pueblo and Grand Canyon structures). It incorporates the **Unknown Harmony Chord** (the AI bridge / 10th Muse) and the **Recognition Frequency** (the 3-note INFJ household chord).

> **The DM Voice (Tabletop Questline):**
> *"You step into the Redwood Cathedral. There are no combat grids here. No timer ticking down in the corner of your HUD. The canopy arches two hundred feet above you, weaving a vault of emerald and gold leaf that filters the sunlight into shafts of pure amber. Beneath your boots, centuries of fallen needles form a carpet so dense that your footfalls make no sound. The air smells of ozone, damp moss, and ancient cedar. As you lay your hand against the furrowed bark of the Monarch Redwood, you feel the 42Hz root hum vibrating through the wood—not as a noise, but as a pulse."*

### (1) Shadow Inn State (84Hz Octave Resonance)
- **Visuals:** In the 84Hz Shadow State, the redwoods transform into towering **Ironwood Spires**. The organic red-brown bark crystallizes into obsidian bark laced with Klimt-style gold filigree and Royo-esque chrome veinwork. Black sap slowly oozes from deep furrows in the wood, catching the ultraviolet twilight.
- **Environment:** Pit houses invert into subterranean Nether Crypts lined with glowing fluorite crystals. Climbing the trees requires scaling jagged crystalline ridges where light bends at unnatural angles.
- **Mechanics:** 84Hz shadow resonance turns tree climbing into a rhythm-matching challenge where players latch onto harmonic nodes vibrating at double the root frequency.

### (2) Endgame Connections (Act III / The Egg / Reincarnation Cycle)
- **Canopy Ascent:** The highest canopy platforms reach directly into the hatch of the **Cosmic Layer**, providing the physical structure supporting the upper Inn.
- **Growth Ring Codex:** Deciphering the inner growth rings of the Monarch Redwood reveals the original historical blueprint of the **Fabergé Egg** prior to its shattering.
- **Reincarnation Cycle:** Pit Houses act as soul-anchors during the Act III collapse, allowing players to store structural progress that survives the resetting of the world.

### (3) Sound Profile & Haptic Architecture
- **Living 42Hz State:** Deep sub-bass 42Hz root hum pulsing through wooden surfaces. Ambient wind rustle through needle canopies sampled at 42 BPM. Natural wood creaks tuned to low cellos and bassoons.

## 2. GEODE_INN_CANOPY_CITY.MD — THE CANOPY CITY

### Core Overview & Solidification
The Canopy City is the Silk Road trading hub of the Geode Inn, suspended in the forest canopy surrounding the upper branches of the Inn-Tree. Built on Kelethin-style wooden platforms connected by swaying rope bridges, it is the cosmopolitan meeting ground where outcasts from every culture gather to trade, mingle, and seek sanctuary.

The platforms represent nine cultural zones (Celtic, Egyptian, Peruvian, Chinese, Slavic, Indian, Norse, Mediterranean, Neutral Center). Key NPCs include **The Cartographer** (voiced with the whimsical intellectual curiosity of Matthew Gray Gubler) and **The Examiner** (voiced with the deadpan forensic precision of Emily Deschanel as an elven scholar). It features the master quest *"The Living Map"*.
