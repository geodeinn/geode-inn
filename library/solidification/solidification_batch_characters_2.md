## OVERVIEW & ARCHITECTURAL DIRECTIVES

This document establishes the official **Solidification Summaries** for the second batch of ten major Geode Inn characters and entities. Every entry rigorously integrates the Geode Inn core design directives:

1. **Tri-Mode Resonance Framework:** Every character operates across three distinct dimensional phases:
   - **Living Inn (42Hz):** Harmonic, grounded, organic growth, restoration, open dialogue, constructive questlines.
   - **Shadow Inn (84Hz):** Phase-shifted octave double, inverted acoustics, crystalline corruption, isolation, hostile counter-mechanics.
   - **In-Between (63Hz Threshold):** Interstitial passage, binaural twilight, unaligned potential, cross-dimensional transition.

2. **Strict Non-Gendered Identification:** Characters are referenced solely by name, title, or trade name. No gendered pronouns are permitted in narrative, dialogue, or mechanical documentation.

3. **Klimt + Royo Visual DNA:** Aesthetic designs merge Gustav Klimt’s gold-leaf patterning, Byzantine geometry, and mosaic ornamentation with Luis Royo’s metallic biomechanical linework, dark romanticism, stone-grain contours, and polished obsidian chrome. Real-world casting references are fully softened into architectural visual concepts.

4. **Tabletop Questline Voice:** Narrative tone matches an immersive Game Master manual, complete with explicit tabletop triggers, mechanical skill checks, dialogue trees, item template bindings, and sound profiles.
