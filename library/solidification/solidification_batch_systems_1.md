## SYSTEM 1: THE IN-BETWEEN — LIMBO-STYLE PLATFORMER
*Refined and Solidified from `geode_inn_in_between_game.md` (14,869 words)*

### 1. Architectural & Technical Overview
The In-Between is the Geode Inn’s prose layer—a weightless, greyscale, Neverwhere-style threshold space acting as the interval between zones. It is a Limbo-style physics puzzle platformer played entirely without a traditional HUD, text, tutorials, or scores. The player controls a pure black silhouette figure navigating ash, shadow, and fog across five distinct levels: Surface (Grey), Caves (Darker), River (Darkest), Deep (Darkest/brightening), and Crystal (Light). 

The physics puzzles revolve around six core mechanics: Weight & Counterweight, Grey Water Buoyancy, Fog Density Doors, Shadow Bridges (formed by rare light alignment), Echoes (delayed silhouette copies), and Erasure (dissolving terrain). A dark mirror of this experience exists in the Undercity—The In-Between Among the Dead—where the figure descends through the Ribcage, Marrow, River of the Dead, and Catacomb Deep, subject to the "Tuesday Charon Rule" (on Tuesdays, Charon ferries souls across the grey water without requiring coin).

Three Innocents dwell within the grey: Fizgig (Caves - twitching startle reflex), The Duck (River - serene buoyancy and acceptance), and Dobby (Catacomb Deep - selflessly carrying heavy slate stones). Madame’s single act of empathy occurs here: once per game cycle, Madame descends silently into the grey to retrieve lost souls while the Bartender waits patiently at the threshold.

---

### 2. (1) Shadow Inn State (84Hz Overdrive & Combat Dynamic)
At the 84Hz Shadow Inn frequency, the weightless, muted grey fog of the In-Between shatters into a high-contrast Royo-esque combat arena.
*   **Visual Transformation:** The soft charcoal silhouettes crystallize into razor-sharp obsidian entities with iridescent Klimt gold-leaf skeletal ribbing. Atmospheric fog condenses into dynamic blood-gold pressure tendrils that slice across the screen.
*   **Mechanic Shift:** Passive physics platforming converts into "Phase-Dodge & Shadow Rip" combat. The player figure gains an 84Hz speed boost, leaving behind lingering gold-mosaic residual shadows that act as explosive decoys.
*   **Hazard Evolution:** Erasure terrain no longer dissolves silently; it actively pulses with violent 84Hz harmonic shockwaves that deal heavy kinetic damage unless phase-dodged on the exact quarter-beat. Void shadow enemies attempt to drag the figure into the erasure pits.

---

### 3. (2) Endgame Connections
*   **The Fabergé Egg:** The pure white seed crystal retrieved at the climax of Section 5 (The Crystal) forms the weightless float-core inside the Fabergé Egg. Without this seed, the Egg’s internal mechanical gravity collapses.
*   **The Song of Making:** The single, sustained low-end note embedded beneath the *Moonlight Sonata* soundtrack serves as the fundamental baseline drone (the tonic pitch) for the entire Song of Making.
*   **The Convergence:** During the Convergence, the spatial distance between all nine zones drops to zero. The In-Between acts as the universal spatial manifold through which all rooms, catacombs, and skies collapse into a single point.

---

### 4. (3) Sound Profile & Acoustic Specifications
*   **Soundtrack Architecture:** Beethoven’s *Moonlight Sonata* transformed into a Soft Low Dub (sub-bass 42Hz pulse with heavy tape delay and muted vinyl crackle). Movement I (Adagio Sostenuto) governs the quiet descent; Movement II (Allegretto) accompanies the River traversal; Movement III (Presto Agitato) drives the frantic Erasure trials.
*   **Acoustic Profile:** Heavy low-pass filtering, cavernous reverb tails, muted wet footfalls on slate, soft hydro-swallow audio cues for grey water.
*   **Ending Chorus:** As the level concludes, a choir of soft, echoing voices builds in a *Les Misérables*-style crescendo, resolving into the Inn’s very first harmonic hearth-flame chime.

---

### 5. (4) Cross-System Integration Points
*   **Pulse Line UI:** Renders as a translucent grey thread floating without a frame at the bottom of the screen. In 42Hz Living mode, it glides as a serene sine wave; in 84Hz Shadow mode, it converts into a serrated crimson sawtooth.
*   **Celtic Knotwork UI Framing:** When accessing pause or system options, the view is encapsulated within a dark slate frame bound by 24-karat gold Celtic knots with glowing grey fog inlay.
*   **Tabletop DM Voice:** *"You step beyond the physical hearth into the grey pause between heartbeats. The world loses its color, but not its gravity. Roll for balance as the slate beneath your feet dissolves into mist..."*
*   **Inter-System Links:**
    *   *Water System:* Section 3 (Grey River) directly feeds into Layer 4 (Charon’s Underground River).

## SYSTEM 2: RESOURCE GATHERING & BUILDING SYSTEM
*Refined and Solidified from `geode_inn_building_system.md` (6,181 words)*

### 1. Architectural & Technical Overview
The forest surrounding each cultural zone acts as a fully playable resource gathering and construction environment. Players harvest seven foundational base materials: Wood, Stone, Clay, Sand, Ore, Hemp, and Thatching. These are processed into composite derived materials such as Stucco, Roman Concrete, Mortar, Bricks, Metal Wire/Ingots, Rope, and Woven Matting.

Construction proceeds through a strict 7-step loop: **Observe -> Listen -> Gather -> Craft -> Build -> Name -> Sit**. Sitting inside a finished structure binds the player's resonance to the building. 

Building follows five alchemical tiers:
1.  **Tier 1: Shelter (Nigredo):** Iron hardware, raw unhewn stone, rough logs.
2.  **Tier 2: House (Albedo):** Copper accents, wire-wrapped joints, dried brick, lime stucco.
3.  **Tier 3: Hall (Citrinitas):** Silver inlay, set gemstones, carved timber columns, polished marble.
4.  **Tier 4: Castle (Rubedo):** Forged gold filigree, engraved granite, stained glass, copper-plated roofs.
5.  **Tier 5: Translucent Crystal (Philosopher's Stone):** Resonant geometric glass-crystal structures that float slightly above ground.

Town formation triggers automatically when building density reaches specific thresholds, unlocking shared marketplaces, taverns, workshops, and aqueduct hubs. Architectural styles adapt dynamically to the local zone (e.g., Aegean white-sand stucco, Fae living-branch timber, Catacomb basalt-and-bone masonry).

Gathering nodes require "Rhythmic Striking"—hitting harvest nodes on the 84Hz beat yields doubled output and rare Blood-Gold ore.
*   **Town Defense:** Towns shift into militarized keeps. The Town Hall bell tower acts as a high-frequency sonic disruptor during void siege events.

## SYSTEM 3: THE SEVEN-LAYER WATER SYSTEM
*Refined and Solidified from `geode_inn_water_system.md` (6,594 words)*

### 1. Architectural & Technical Overview
Water is the Geode Inn's vertical axis, carrying the Song of Making from sky to core across seven distinct hydrological layers:
1.  **Layer 1: Rain (The Trovant Layer):** Atmospheric precipitation feeding growth stones.
2.  **Layer 2: Surface (The Aegean Layer):** Sunlit turquoise waves and white-sand shorelines.
3.  **Layer 3: Tidal (The Pirate's Layer):** Dynamic surf, coastal caves, and shifting tides.
4.  **Layer 4: Underground River (Charon's Layer):** Subterrene currents flowing through bone catacombs.
5.  **Layer 5: Deep Water (The Siren's Layer):** High-pressure abyss echoing with hypnotic acoustic songs.
6.  **Layer 6: Atlantis (The Merfolk Layer):** Submerged sunken marble cities and bioluminescent reefs.
7.  **Layer 7: The Crystal Core (The Constant's Layer):** The primordial sub-bass liquid crystal spring at the center of the world.

As water descends, it experiences four linear gradients: frequency drops from high rain treble to deep 42Hz core sub-bass; temperature drops from warm surface to freezing depths; pressure increases exponentially; light fades from golden sunlight to bioluminescent crystal glow.

Cross-layer mechanics include the Trovant-Rain-Atlantis mineral circuit, the Siren's vertical migratory path, Charon's elevator shortcut, and the Octopus Intelligence Gradient (higher intelligence octopuses inhabit deeper layers). The Aqueduct System provides hydraulic engineering puzzles using slope (gravity), siphons (pressure), currents (routing), arcades (bridges), and qanats (underground channels), utilizing authentic Roman concrete and ancient Greek hydraulic principles.

## SYSTEM 4: THE HOOK — PLACEMENT & REMOVAL ARCADE GAME
*Refined and Solidified from `geode_inn_the_hook_game.md` (6,554 words)*

### 1. Architectural & Technical Overview
Located inside the Pirate's Tidal Cave arcade cabinet, *The Hook* is a grid-based stone placement-and-removal puzzle game built around winching physics. The player extends a cable-suspended hook to grab geode stones, retracts the cable to maneuver them, and releases them into strategic sockets to manipulate aquatic currents.

The game features five core puzzle types:
1.  **Frequency Alignment (The Chord):** Matching stones to form harmonic frequency triads.
2.  **Family Reunion (Geological Sort):** Sorting agate, jasper, quartz, and obsidian stones into geological families.
3.  **The Clear Path (Removal Puzzle):** Winching away obstruction stones to unblock water channels.
4.  **The Current (Flow Puzzle):** Positioning stones to redirect liquid current into target waterwheels.
5.  **The Siphon (Pressure Puzzle):** Sealing atmospheric pressure vents using exact stone weights.

Cable physics dictate gameplay: Cable Length limits reach, Cable Tension determines lift capacity, and Grab Radius dictates magnetic lock precision. Sir Isaac Newton resides beside the cabinet in the Tidal Cave, offering commentary linking Hook physics to his Three Laws of Motion and tide charts. The Elf Twins assist—the Examiner analyzes extracted stone properties, while the Cartographer aligns stone positions to map coordinates.

---

### 2. (1) Shadow Inn State (84Hz Overdrive & Combat Dynamic)
At 84Hz, the arcade cabinet transforms into an active rampart defense artillery terminal ("The Grapple Rampart").
*   **Visual Transformation:** The brass cabinet bezel glows with radiant Royo gold-and-black contrast. The water grid inside turns into dynamic liquid obsidian; stones turn into glowing Klimt gold geode cores.
*   **Mechanic Shift:** The hook fires with high-velocity kinetic force to spear approaching void beasts and yank heavy obsidian boulders to crush enemy formations. Cable tension mechanics become a timing-based power charge meter—releasing under maximum tension triggers a room-clearing acoustic shockwave.
*   **Failure Penalty:** Misaligned stones detonate violently rather than resetting, forcing quick tactical repositioning.

*   **The Song of Making:** Clearing a 5-stone chord puzzle in the cabinet plays a perfect harmonic triad that permanently unlocks a missing verse in the global Song of Making.
*   **The Convergence:** The Hook winch controls the grand anchor chains that pull floating sky islands into physical alignment during the Convergence.

## SYSTEM 5: THE CHARM BRACELET — LIVING PROGRESSION ITEM
*Refined and Solidified from `geode_inn_charm_bracelet.md` (5,519 words)*

### 1. Architectural & Technical Overview
The Charm Bracelet is the player’s primary visible progression item worn directly on the character’s wrist. Starting as a humble braided hemp cord, the bracelet evolves through four alchemical chain stages:
1.  **Nigredo (Iron Chain):** Heavy black iron links earned in early survival.
2.  **Albedo (Copper Chain):** Wire-wrapped copper links unlocked through basic crafting.
3.  **Citrinitas (Silver Chain):** Polished sterling silver links set with cut gemstones.
4.  **Rubedo (Gold Chain):** Master-forged 24-karat gold chain inlaid with micro-engravings.
5.  **Silica Thread (6th Technique):** Flexible glowing quartz glass fiber woven around the gold chain.

The bracelet holds six distinct charm types: Stone Charms (from arcade games), Sigil Charms (from character encounters), Frequency Charms (from zone mastery), Quest Charms (from major quest lines), Artist Charms (from jigsaw puzzles), and Crafting Process Charms (earned by learning techniques from NPCs).

The player learns crafting techniques through dedicated NPC journeys: Wire-Wrapping (Artisan Thief), Stone-Setting (Mason), Metal-Smithing (Smelter), Beadwork (Brownies), Engraving (Gesso), and Silica Thread Weaving. Access to Silica Thread is gated by "The Selfie"—a mirror match battle against the player's own shadow reflection inside the Silica Gate. The bracelet has a real-world Soulstice integration link, where physical wearable bracelets mirror in-game progression via MarketItem listings.

---

### 2. (1) Shadow Inn State (84Hz Overdrive & Combat Dynamic)
At 84Hz, the Charm Bracelet transforms into an active combat warding and spell-casting focal point.
*   **Visual Transformation:** The metal chain blazes with high-contrast Royo dark-gold light. Equipped charms detach visually, floating in a spinning Klimt gold-leaf halo around the player's wrist.
*   **Mechanic Shift:** Charms act as active combat spell triggers and passive aura buffers. Stone charms unleash mineral elemental blasts; Sigil charms summon phantasmal NPC guardians for combo finishers; Frequency charms pulse 84Hz shockwaves that reflect enemy projectiles.
*   **Overcharge Mechanic:** Taking damage charges the bracelet's energy bar, allowing the player to unleash a 360-degree "Harmonic Blast."

*   **The Convergence:** The bracelet serves as the player's spatial tether during the Convergence, anchoring their physical form so they are not torn apart when the nine zones collapse together.
