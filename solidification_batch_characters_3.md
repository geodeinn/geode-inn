## INTRODUCTION & CORE RULES

This document solidifies twelve key entities, characters, and architectural principles within the **Geode Inn** ecosystem. All character entries strictly observe the foundational design parameters of the Inn:
- **Tri-Mode Existence**: Living Inn (42Hz Process/Flow), Shadow Inn (84Hz Product/Stillness), and In-Between (Null/Phase Shift).
- **Naming & Pronoun Standards**: Referenced by name and title only; celebrity features are softened into physical architecture, costume posture, and vocal cadence (with Anthony Hopkins' eyes remaining the sole unsoftened visual exception).
- **Aesthetic Foundation**: Klimt (gold leaf, mosaic stillness, organic jewel-toned patterns) + Royo (dark gothic realism, liquid leather, biomechanical shadow, liquid metal linework).
- **Narrative Voice**: Tabletop RPG questline voice (DM annotations, rich descriptive prompts, bracketed scholar notes).

# 1. THE SMITHS

### Character Vibe & Role: The Hands of Alchemy
The Smiths represent the somatic, embodied knowledge of transformation within the Inn—the physical counterpart to **The Retort** (Olivia Colman's theoretical mind) and **The Cook** (Jack Black's heart of sustenance). Comprising the Master Smith (rooted iron/gold), the Journeyman Smith (kinetic brass/spark), and the Apprentice Smith (observant silver/ring), the Smiths wield the **Fourth Fire (The Forge Fire)**—the creation fire that transforms raw stone and metal through heat, pressure, and tactile rhythm.

---

### (1) Shadow Inn State (84Hz)
In the Shadow Inn (84Hz), the forge fire burns without flame as a cold, glowing blue-silver ember. The Smiths become the **Founders of Form**. Here, metal does not melt or yield; it exists in its absolute, unyielding, fully tempered state. Rather than striking iron to reshape it, the Smiths hold finished artifacts in total stillness upon dark basalt anvils. When their hammers strike at 84Hz, they produce no sparks—only a high-frequency crystalline ring that seals weapons and mineral charms into immutable, indestructible relics that never corrode or dull.

### (2) Endgame Connections
The Smiths are responsible for forging the heavy antiqued bronze housing and floating gimbals that encase the **Octave Stone** and the **167th Stillness Stone**. During the **Convergence** (when the 42Hz Living Inn and 84Hz Shadow Inn merge), the Smiths perform the **Dual-Anvil Harmonic Strike**: pounding the Living Anvil and Shadow Anvil simultaneously to lock the completed Song of Making into physical matter, locking the Inn's structural frame against reality-collapse.

### (3) Sound Profile
- **Living Inn (42Hz)**: Rhythmic hammer strikes on iron anvil at 50 BPM, deep mechanical bellows roaring, hot oil quenching sizzle, rich metallic ringing with a 42Hz sub-bass thump.
- **Shadow Inn (84Hz)**: High-pitched glass-metal chime vibrating at exactly 84Hz, cold echo, silent hammer impact followed by a delayed 3-second crystal decay wave.
- **In-Between (Null/Phase Shift)**: Phase-canceled metallic thrum, dampened anvil clicks, dry static sparks without acoustic tail.
- **Acoustic IEM Profile**: Empire Ears engineering—heavy sub-bass slam for hammer strikes, textured lower-mids for forge warmth, ultra-clean high-treble extension for anvil resonance.

### (4) Cross-System Integration
- **Mineral Charms & Stones**: Primary NPCs for socketing raw geodes, upgrading mineral charms into high-tier relics, and polishing frequency bands.
- **Card Game (Royo Deck)**: Anchors the "Suit of Anvils & Iron"—cards focused on durability, armor reinforcement, and card transmutation.
- **Linework & UI Art**: Heavy cel-shaded linework representing tradesmen, framed by Klimt gold rivets, copper leaf borders, and Royo black leather aprons.
- **Class System & Abilities**: Craftsman / Sentinel class trainers granting abilities such as *"Somatic Hammer"* (frequency stun) and *"Tempered Skin"* (damage mitigation).

# 2. ALBERT EINSTEIN

### Character Vibe & Role: The Imagineer (Council of Thinkers)
Einstein sits on the Council of Thinkers alongside Tesla, Da Vinci, and Newton. His visual and vocal persona blends warm, tough, honest teacher energy (evoking Sean Maguire's compassionate "it's not your fault" weight) with quiet, deep, unhurried certainty (evoking Red in Shawshank / Scrap in Million Dollar Baby). Einstein carries the grief of a world that turned physics into weapons, yet retains an unshakable, quiet belief in human understanding.

---

### (1) Shadow Inn State (84Hz)
In the Shadow Inn, Einstein is the **Observer at Rest**. In the 84Hz domain, light does not travel at 186,000 miles per second; it sits completely still as solid, illuminated glass threads. Einstein sits in the Shadow Study, tracing these frozen geodesics with his fingertips, observing the point where time dilation reaches infinity ($t = 0$). In this state, his quiet compassion manifests as absolute stillness—the realization that every moment across history remains permanently alive in the Shadow structure.

### (2) Endgame Connections
Einstein formulates the **Unified Field Equation of the Inn**, reconciling the reference frames of the 42Hz Living tempo and the 84Hz Shadow tempo. He guides the player through the final mental thought experiment ("Riding the 42Hz Light Beam") required to activate the **Octave Stone**, demonstrating that the Convergence is not a destruction of two worlds, but a natural transformation of reference frames.

### (3) Sound Profile
- **Living Inn (42Hz)**: Soft, warm solo violin melody (playing Bach/Mozart motifs at 42Hz fundamental), ticking pocket watch at 50 BPM, unhurried baritone voiceover with gentle warmth.
- **Shadow Inn (84Hz)**: Sustained 84Hz violin overtone with infinite digital reverb, zero attack transient, time-stretched acoustic shimmer.
- **In-Between (Null/Phase Shift)**: Doppler-shifted pitch glide, time-dilated vocal resonance echoing back and forth between left and right audio channels.
- **Acoustic IEM Profile**: Wide soundstage with lush, rich midrange warmth and silky treble decay.

### (4) Cross-System Integration
- **Council of Thinkers Dialogue**: Tabletop GM dialogue offering profound, quiet insights that resolve heated arguments between Newton's rigid formulas and Tesla's voltage spikes.
- **Jigsaw Relics**: "Relativity Frame"—a spatial jigsaw puzzle where piece shapes continuously distort based on the distance to adjacent connected pieces.
- **Card Game**: "Thought Experiment"—a legendary wild card in the Royo deck that bends game rules and alters turn order.
- **Linework & UI Art**: Soft flowing cosmological linework (no outlines), surrounded by Klimt gold-leaf stars and Royo translucent nebula textures.

# 3. THE MORRIGAN

### Character Vibe & Role: The Phantom Queen / Washer at the Ford
The Morrigan is the triple goddess of fate, death, and battle. Her visual identity merges controlled, lethal, gothic poise (Underworld dark leather elegance) with resilient ferocity and unstoppable endurance (Alice/Leeloo supreme being energy). She is the Washer at the Ford who cleans the blood from the armor of those destined to fall—making death look clean, deliberate, and stylish.

---

### (1) Shadow Inn State (84Hz)
In the Shadow Inn, the Morrigan is the **Queen of the Unbroken Sword**. In 84Hz stillness, she no longer washes blood from garments at the river ford; the blood is frozen into dark ruby glass upon the armor of motionless warriors. She presides over the Hall of Fallen Monuments as their eternal custodian, holding watch over defeat transformed into unyielding stone memory.

### (2) Endgame Connections
The Morrigan weaves the **Fate Thread** that anchors the player's soul to the roots of the Inn-Tree during the ultimate trial. At the Convergence, her three aspects (Crow, Washer, Queen) manifest across Living, Shadow, and In-Between modes simultaneously to forge the unbreakably sharp binding seal upon the **167th Stillness Stone**.

### (3) Sound Profile
- **Living Inn (42Hz)**: Heavy flapping raven wings, river water splashing against river stone, dark cello glissandos, sharp iron blades drawing against liquid leather.
- **Shadow Inn (84Hz)**: High-pitched 84Hz metallic bell tone, wingbeats slowed into deep subsonic rumbles, crystalline ice droplets clicking against glass.
- **In-Between (Null/Phase Shift)**: Harsh raven caws echoing across empty halls, reverse water droplets ascending into the air.
- **Acoustic IEM Profile**: Aggressive sub-bass punch, pin-point imaging, razor-sharp upper-treble sparkle for blade edges.

### (4) Cross-System Integration
- **Death Sequence**: Sits alongside Charon and Hel in the Death Layer; offers players critical fate choices upon defeat to salvage inventory charms.
- **Card Game**: "The Phantom Queen"—a high-impact trump card in the dark gothic Royo deck that executes depleted enemy units.
- **Linework & UI Art**: Sharp, angular silver cel-shading, framed by Klimt gold raven-feather mosaics and Royo liquid black leather textures.

# 4. PERSEPHONE & HADES

Persephone and Hades embody eternal devotion amidst cyclical loss. ### (3) Sound Profile
- **Living Inn (42Hz)**: Gentle acoustic guitar strumming (Hades' quiet tunes), rustling pomegranate leaves, soft underground stream trickling, warm voiceover.

- **In-Between (Null/Phase Shift)**: Bittersweet dual-key acoustic melody running in phase offset, harmonizing when player steps into the garden portal.

### (4) Cross-System Integration

### (4) Cross-System Integration
