## ADDENDUM: SPECIAL MECHANIC PUZZLES

### 14. The In-Between — Ghost Pieces
**The transparent puzzle.** The In-Between is the liminal space between zones — the borderland, the threshold, the place that isn't quite anywhere. The puzzle pieces reflect this: they are MOSTLY TRANSPARENT.

**Visual:**
- Pieces are ~70-80% transparent — you can barely see them
- A faint iridescent edge (like soap film or thin crystal) outlines each piece so the player can find them
- The image underneath is itself a translucent, dreamlike version of the zone — shapes suggested, not defined
- When two pieces connect correctly, the joined section becomes slightly more visible (the space is "deciding to exist" there)
- As more pieces connect, the image resolves from ghostly to visible, from potential to present
- The completed image is still only ~50% opaque — the In-Between never fully solidifies

**Mechanic:**
- 30 pieces, but they feel like more because you can barely see them
- Pieces catch light at certain angles — tilting the device (mobile) or moving the cursor past them creates a brief iridescent flash that reveals the piece's position
- On desktop: mouse movement creates subtle light shifts that reveal nearby pieces
- On mobile: gyroscope/tilt creates the same effect — tilt to catch the light
- Ghost outline toggle is USELESS here — the outline is also transparent
- No difficulty levels — The In-Between is what it is. It's always hard.
- Pieces do NOT snap with a gold flash (that would break the aesthetic) — they settle silently, like two sheets of glass sliding together

**The Challenge:**
- Finding the pieces is the first challenge (they're nearly invisible)
- Identifying what the image IS comes second (it's barely formed)
- Placing them correctly requires intuition more than visual matching
- The puzzle teaches the In-Between's philosophy: some things only become clear when you stop trying to see them directly

**Audio:**
- Near-silence. A faint ambient hum that resolves into clarity as pieces connect
- Piece connection: a soft glass-on-glass sound, not stone
- Full completion: the hum resolves into a single clear tone — the In-Between, for one moment, is fully present. Then it fades back.

**Narrative Intro (tabletop questline voice):**
"The space between is not empty. It is full of things that have not decided what they are yet. You will not see them clearly. You will not see them completely. But they are there. And if you are patient — if you do not look too hard — they will show you where they belong. Rebuild the becoming."

**Reward:** "The Between" achievement + a unique iridescent stone (a stone that exists between two mineral identities — shifts depending on viewing angle, like the pieces themselves)

### Special Difficulty Notes for Existing Puzzles

**Escher Staircase (Puzzle 5):**
- Acknowledged as one of the hardest puzzles in the game
- Impossible geometry means spatial reasoning is useless — edges contradict themselves
- Players cannot rely on "this must be a corner" logic
- The Escher pieces should have connections that look valid in multiple orientations (tabs that could fit blanks from multiple directions)
- In Master+ mode with rotation, the Escher puzzle becomes nearly sadistic — every rotation looks plausible
- This is intentional. The Staircase IS impossible. The puzzle should be too.

**Beardsley Gorgon's Garden (Puzzle 12):**
- Dense black-and-white ornamental linework — no color differentiation to help
- Every piece is intricate pattern that could belong anywhere
- The challenge is distinguishing which ornamental flourish goes where
- In Master+ with rotation, the rotational symmetry of Beardsley's patterns makes it extremely difficult
- This is intentional. The Garden is ornate and confusing. The puzzle should be too.

**Van Gogh (Future Puzzle — Zone TBD):**
- Swirling brushstrokes make spatial identification nearly impossible — every piece is "more swirl"
- The image only reveals itself when nearly complete
- Like watching a painting dry in reverse — chaos resolving into vision
- Zone assignment pending (Krista to determine where Van Gogh fits in the Inn)
- Noted as a future addition to the puzzle roster
