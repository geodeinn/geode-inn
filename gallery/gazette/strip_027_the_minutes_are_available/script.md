# The Geode Inn Gazette — Strip #027: "The Minutes Are Available"

**Date:** 2026-10-08 | **Strip Number:** 27
**Zone:** The Archive (Living Room Hearth)
**Characters:** Naut (the Scholar), A Brownie
**Art Style:** Hobbit-hole warm palette (amber, gold, cream) with scholarly warmth — Gazette house style
**Player Adventures:** None — NPC feature (Naut; the Brownies' constitutional canon, the dusting-rights incident)

---

## Panel 1 — The Setup

**Art:** The Archive nook of the Geode Inn. Warm amber firelight from a low hearth. Floor-to-ceiling shelves of leather-bound books, their spines faded and gold-leafed. Naut, the four-foot squid-headed scholar, sits in a velvet wingback chair beside the fire, brass reading spectacles perched on the bridge of his beak-like face. An enormous leather tome lies open across his lap, two small facial tentacles turning a parchment page. He wears an oversized tweed professor's vest with elbow patches. The room is immaculate. Not a mote of dust. That's the arrangement.

**Caption:** "The Archive had an arrangement with the Brownies. They didn't touch the books. Naut didn't touch their minutes. The arrangement had held for three centuries, which is a long time for a building that rearranges its own hallways when no one is looking."

---

## Panel 2 — The Moment

**Art:** Naut looks up from his reading, spectacles slightly askew. A Brownie — a small figure barely a foot tall, wearing a mushroom cap like a hat, carrying a dust cloth — stands on the edge of Naut's reading table, methodically wiping the shelf above. The Brownie's posture is the calm, unhurried confidence of someone whose paperwork is in order. Naut's expression is not anger — it is the precise face of academic protocol being violated.

**Caption:** "The arrangement had held for three centuries. Today, a Brownie stood on Naut's reading table with a dust cloth and the steady certainty of someone whose union had voted, ratified, and filed the paperwork before picking up the rag."

---

## Panel 3 — The Twist

**Art:** Close confrontation. Naut, one tentacle raised, spectacles halfway off his face, beak open mid-sentence. The Brownie, not flinching, holds up a mushroom cap laminated in tree sap. Tiny script is visible on its surface. The Brownie's expression is serene due process. The scale difference is part of the joke — four feet of ancient scholarly authority meeting one foot of constitutional inevitability.

**Naut:** "What are you doing near my —"
**Brownie:** "The collective voted to expand the dusting rotation to include the reading corner. The executive officer ratified it. The minutes are available. ...We will dust. ...You will not interrupt the dusting. ...Good day, Professor."

**Caption:** "The Brownie held up the constitution — the mushroom cap, laminated in sap for durability — and cited it, article and clause, without raising its voice above the fire's crackle."

---

## Panel 4 — The Button

**Art:** The Archive nook, same warm firelight. Naut has settled back into his chair, spectacles back on, reading. The Brownie dusts the shelf above him, methodical and content. Between them on the reading table sits the laminated mushroom cap constitution — a treaty, not a concession. The firelight is warm on both of them. The building did not split. The books did not move. The arrangement was amended, not broken.

**Caption:** "Naut looked at the Brownie. The Brownie looked at Naut. Naut looked at the constitution. Naut put his spectacles back on. The Archive holds forbidden knowledge from before the written word. The Archive also holds a mushroom cap that says the dusting rotation was expanded by democratic vote. Both are load-bearing. Only one was laminated."

## Voice Notes

- **Pratchett warmth:** "The steady certainty of someone whose union had voted, ratified, and filed the paperwork before picking up the rag." The Brownie's dialogue — entire exchange is the Monty Python constitutional peasant energy, Inn-native.
- **Gaiman mythic-made-personal:** "Both are load-bearing. Only one was laminated." — the deeper truth: the building holds forbidden knowledge and a mushroom cap labor agreement, and both keep the lights on.

## Canon Notes

**First strip featuring Naut the Scholar as primary character.** Canonizes:
- Naut and the Brownies' arrangement: they don't touch his books, he doesn't touch their minutes — held for three centuries
- The dusting-rights incident: a Brownie expanded the dusting rotation to include Naut's reading corner via proper constitutional process
- The mushroom cap constitution: one page, written in Brownie script, laminated in tree sap for durability
- The Brownies' constitutional peasant energy: completely unimpressed by authority, including a four-foot squid-headed scholar with centuries of tenure
- The arrangement as amendable, not breakable — the building's theology of holding unlike things together (connects to the Tri-Canid Weave: "what was never meant to match, matched anyway")

**What the strip says about the Inn today:** The Inn's smallest residents govern themselves. The Archive's keeper is subject to a mushroom cap. The building doesn't ask anyone to agree — it asks everyone to stay. The dusting gets done. The minutes are available.
