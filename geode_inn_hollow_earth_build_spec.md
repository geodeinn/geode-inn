# THE HOLLOW EARTH RPG — BUILD SPEC FOR BUILDER
## The Deepest Explorable Zone — Ancient Crystal City Beneath the Earth

---

## OVERVIEW

The Hollow Earth is the Geode Inn's largest explorable RPG zone — a vast ancient crystal city beneath the Earth's surface. Isometric/top-down exploration (like the Fae Kingdom but urban). The player explores 9 districts, builds reputation with 3 factions, uncovers the creators' history through interactive memory echoes, and ultimately reaches the Resonance Engine — the 42Hz source — to complete the Song.

The Earth is a geode. The Hollow Earth is the crystal interior.

Scale: The city was built for beings larger than humans. Doorways are 20 feet tall, streets are 40 feet wide. The player feels small. The scale is the point.

## 1. THREE ENTRY POINTS (Each Opens a Different Starting District)

### Entry 1: The North Pole (Crystal Path)
- **Starting District:** The Crystal Approach (northern quarter)
- **Journey:** Arctic expedition → crack in ice → crystal spiral tunnel descending miles → ice transitions to stone to crystal → crystal hums at 42Hz → emerges at city's northern gate
- **Requirement:** Ley Line Vision unlocked + at least 1 charged stone (frequency key)
- **Visual:** Spiraling through crystal shifting from blue ice → violet stone → white crystal. Aurora follows player down, fades as Song grows stronger.

### Entry 2: The South Pole (Water Path)
- **Starting District:** The Deep Harbor (southern quarter, underground ocean shore)
- **Journey:** Antarctic ice shelf crossing → geode cathedral beneath ice → near-vertical descent through vibrating crystal → emerges at city's harbor
- **Requirement:** Ley Line Vision + at least 3 charged stones
- **Visual:** Steepest, most vertigo-inducing. Descends through a geode — Earth's amethyst. Ocean at bottom glows bioluminescent.

### Entry 3: The Water Caves (Deep Path)
- **Starting District:** The Dark Reaches (city's underbelly)
- **Journey:** Water Zone Layer 7 (Source) hidden passage → swim through underground ocean → salt to fresh to crystal-clear water → total darkness → navigate by frequency (charged stone pulses louder approaching city) → surface in city's lowest district
- **Requirement:** Lighthouse Vigil game completed + at least 1 charged water stone
- **Visual:** Darkest entry. Total darkness → distant bioluminescence → city seen from BELOW, rising from ocean floor. Hardest entry but drops player deepest into mysteries.

## 2. THE THREE FACTIONS

### Faction 1: The Echoes (The City's Memory)
**Philosophy:** Preserve. Memory-ghosts — frequency patterns stored in crystal walls, replaying creators' lives. Not alive, not dead — REPLAYING.

**Interaction Mechanic:** Player matches frequencies by holding a stone that resonates with an Echo's frequency → "tune in" to the memory → walk through it as invisible observer.

**Visual:** Translucent figures, no faces, made of light and frequency. Walk streets replaying routines. Don't see player. When player matches frequency, they PAUSE — memory expands, becomes interactive.

**Reputation System (4 tiers):**
- **Neutral (0):** Echoes ignore player. Observe only.
- **Resonant (1-49):** Echoes acknowledge player — glance, pause. Access more memories.
- **Harmonized (50-99):** Echoes actively respond — point to undiscovered memories, open locked doors, replay memories they WANT player to see.
- **Synchronized (100):** Echoes see player fully. Final memories unlock — creators' last days, departure, message to whoever comes next.

**Quest Line:** "The Remembered" — 7 quests

### Faction 2: The Deep Ones (The Ocean's Children)
**Philosophy:** Protect. Bioluminescent beings evolved in underground ocean. Never seen surface. Curious, not hostile. Communicate through light patterns + frequency pulses. Tend the ocean's 42Hz frequency.

**Visual:** Bioluminescent aquatic beings — not fish, not humanoid, something between. Transparent bodies, organs visible as light patterns. Fast/graceful in water, slow/awkward on land.

**Reputation System (4 tiers):**
- **Neutral (0):** Observe from water. Don't approach land.
- **Curious (1-49):** Surface when player near harbor. Mimic player's stone frequencies as light patterns. Trade: charged water stones for deep-ocean pearls + rare bioluminescent crystals.
- **Trusted (50-99):** Invite player into ocean. Swim with them — explore submerged districts only accessible underwater. Share oral history as frequency patterns.
- **Bonded (100):** Reveal ocean's secret: underground ocean connects to Water Zone AND to Hollow Earth's deepest point — sunken chamber beneath Resonance Engine where 42Hz originates. Ocean is the MEDIUM — 42Hz travels through water better than crystal. Crystal amplifies, ocean CARRIES.

**Quest Line:** "The Deepest Blue" — 5 quests

### Faction 3: The Lost (The Accidental Population)
**Philosophy:** Varies. Surface beings who fell in over millennia and never left. The city's only LIVING NPC population.

**Three Sub-groups:**
- **The Settled:** Built homes in outer districts. Adapted — eat crystal nutrients, drink ocean water, live by 42Hz rhythm. Peaceful. The player's primary social hub.
- **The Wild:** Went mad. Feral. Live in Dark Reaches. Communicate through broken frequency patterns. Not monsters — gardeners who lost their words. Can be befriended with frequency offerings.
- **The Claimers:** Want the Engine's power. Use salvaged creator technology as weapons. Most dangerous combat opponents. The First is their leader — centuries old, obsessed, not evil.

**Reputation System (4 tiers):**
- **Neutral (0):** Settled wary. Wild hostile. Claimers aggressive.
- **Tolerated (1-49):** Settled trade with player. Wild flee from bright stone light. Claimers attack on sight.
- **Allied (50-99):** Settled offer companions for exploration. Wild can be befriended through frequency matching. Claimers still hostile.
- **Family (100):** Settled share their deepest stories. Wild lead player to hidden gardens. Claimers dissolved after the First hears the Song.

**Quest Line:** "The Ones Who Stayed" — 8 quests

## 3. THE NINE DISTRICTS

### District 1: The Crystal Approach (Northern Quarter)
- Entry zone. Safe. No combat.
- Vast crystal cavern opening to city's northern gate.
- First sight: city skyline — crystal spires glowing from within, stretching into violet-dark.
- 42Hz frequency strong — player's stones visibly glow.
- **Key Feature:** The First Echo — memory-ghost at gate replaying the moment creators sealed the city. Introduction to Echo system.
- **Progression:** Pass through to Outer City.

### District 2: The Outer City (First Explorable District)
- Low-level exploration. First encounters.
- Wide crystal streets, moderate buildings, public spaces.
- Densest Echo population — daily life district. Memories of work, conversation, creation.
- Small Settled outpost — trading post for supplies/info.
- Combat: low-level. Feral creatures (crystal rats, bioluminescent insects) + minor guardians (degraded city defense systems).
- **Key Feature:** Resonance Doors — frequency-locked buildings. Each requires specific stone frequency to open. Simple single-frequency locks here — player learns the system.
- **Key Feature:** Ley Line Streets — streets follow ley line patterns. Ley Line Vision shows golden energy flowing. Player can charge stones by walking ley line streets.
- **Progression:** Unlocks Market of Echoes, Resonance Quarter, Deep Harbor.

### District 3: The Market of Echoes (Settled Community)
- Social hub. Trading. Quest hub.
- Settled's community — claimed/renovated buildings. Crystal walls with tapestries. Market stalls from salvaged creator tech. Small tavern.
- 8-10 Settled NPCs, each with backstory.
- **The Tavern:** Run by former surface sailor who fell through water caves decades ago. Serves "deep water" — ocean fresh water infused with mineral nutrients. Social hub for quests, rumors, explorer meetings.
- **The Market:** Trade charged stones for rare items (Deep One pearls, creator artifacts, ancient tools). Trade surface stones (never seen by Settled) for local knowledge (maps, district access codes, lore). Trade Bead Board designs for Settled companions (temporary party members).
- **Key Feature:** The Echo Market — once per in-game week, Echoes replay a market day from creators' era. Settled watch. Player walks through memory — city alive, populated, beings buying things, laughing, arguing. Most emotional moment in the zone.
- **Progression:** Quest hub for Settled and Echo quest lines.

### District 4: The Resonance Quarter (Frequency District)
- Puzzle district. Maintenance quests. Mid-level.
- City's frequency systems: crystal towers amplify/distribute 42Hz signal. Resonance chambers for tuning. Calibration stations for ley line output adjustment.
- Echoes here are technical — memories of creators working on frequency systems.
- Combat: mid-level. City guardian systems more active — automated defenses challenge player's frequency. Match or counter guardian's frequency to pass.
- **Key Feature:** Calibration Stations — player can TUNE stones (adjust frequency by small increments). More powerful than surface tuning. Can create CHORD stones by combining two frequencies. ONLY place in game where player creates custom chord stones.
- **Key Feature:** The Six Rings — six crystal rings around central spire (visible in distance). Each corresponds to gargoyle frequency (111Hz, 64Hz, 432Hz, 528Hz, two adaptive). Player with Gargoyle Chord can activate rings — aligning surface gargoyles with Engine's distribution system.
- **Progression:** Unlocks Inner City and Archive District.

### District 5: The Deep Harbor (Ocean's Edge)
- Social hub for Deep Ones. Underwater exploration access.
- Crystal harbor — crumbling docks, piers, crushed crystal sand beach.
- Deep Ones surface here. Trade, interact, eventually swim with them.
- Combat: low. Harbor is peaceful. Deep Ones protect it.
- **Key Feature:** Submerged Districts — flooded city sections. Only accessible by swimming (requires Deep One trust 50+). Best-preserved creator artifacts — water protected them from crystal degradation.
- **Key Feature:** Bioluminescent Reef — living formation at harbor's edge. Glows at 42Hz harmonics. Player "plays" the reef by placing stones — reef amplifies frequencies, creates light show. The Hollow Earth's Bead Board — a natural composition surface.
- **Progression:** Deep One quest hub. Unlocks underwater exploration.

### District 6: The Inner City (Creators' District)
- High-level exploration. Combat-heavy. Claimers' territory.
- Creators' residential district. Most beautiful part — crystal homes, each unique, shaped to the creator who lived there. No two buildings the same.
- Claimers have camps here. Guard access points, block bridges, challenge player.
- Combat: high-level. Claimers use salvaged creator tech — frequency blasters, resonance shields, crystal shards.
- **Key Feature:** Creator Homes — each is a personal museum. Enter with right frequency key. Personal artifacts: tools, art, frequency records. Some creators were artists, engineers, gardeners, musicians. The homes tell individual stories.
- **Key Feature:** The First's Camp — Claimers' leader in largest creator home. The First is oldest of the Lost, centuries old, obsessed with Engine. Not a villain — a person who spent centuries trying to access the Engine. Player can fight or play the Inn Song. The Song is the right answer.
- **Progression:** Defeating or convincing the First unlocks Archive District and path to Resonance Engine.

### District 7: The Archive District (Memory Core)
- Lore district. No combat. Story-heavy.
- Creator's Archive — crystal library. Each "book" is a vibration pattern in a crystal shard. Player "reads" by holding shard — frequency plays through player's stones.
- Most story-dense district. Complete history of creators, city, and Song.
- **Key Feature:** The Archive Quest — find 12 crystal shards, each a chapter of creators' history. Scattered through district buildings. Each requires different frequency to read. Player's stone collection IS the key.
- **The 12 Records:**
  1. The First Hearing — creators discover 42Hz
  2. The City's Birth — shaping crystal around Resonance Engine
  3. The Ley Line Mapping — creators trace frequency to surface
  4. The Sacred Sites — creators mark ley line nodes (first stone circles)
  5. The Ocean's Song — creators discover ocean carries frequency better than crystal
  6. The Deep Ones' Birth — first life forms evolve in underground ocean
  7. The Silmaril Moment — creators capture light in crystal (Bead Board foundation)
  8. The First Visitor — first surface being to fall in (first of the Lost)
  9. The Faction Wars — Lost's early conflicts over city resources
  10. The Departure — creators decide to leave. "The Song is complete. The city is built. The work is done."
  11. The Sailing West — creators leave through the galaxy. 42Hz follows them. City falls silent. Echoes begin.
  12. The Prediction — creators' last message: "Others will hear the Song. Others will come." The player is the predicted one.
- **Progression:** Reading all 12 records is prerequisite for main quest.

### District 8: The Resonance Engine (The Source)
- Endgame zone. The 42Hz source.
- The central spire — a crystal tower that amplifies the 42Hz frequency from the water below. The frequency flows from the Earth's core, through the underground ocean, through the crystal spire, to the ley lines, to the surface.
- **Key Feature:** The Engine's Door — final door requires ALL 12 zone frequencies (9 Inn zones + 3 Hollow Earth faction frequencies: Echo, Deep, Lost). Player composes the FULL SONG.
- **Key Feature:** The Completion — spire responds. 42Hz source harmonizes with player's composition. Song is complete. City activates. Echoes find peace. Deep Ones celebrate (bioluminescent light show). Lost see city come alive.
- **Key Feature:** The Source Stone — the 166th stone. Pure 42Hz, no mineral identity. Bead Board ultimate upgrade — amplifies all stones to 100% charged.
- **Progression:** The Return — ley lines carry completed Song to surface. Every zone receives Song. All player's stones charge to 100%. Loop completes: source → ley lines → Silk Road → Inn → player → source.

### District 9: The Dark Reaches (City's Underbelly)
- Below the harbor. The city's most dangerous district.
- The Wild live here. Dark, near-monochrome visuals — deep blue-black with bioluminescent accents.
- **Key Feature:** The Wild's Hidden Garden — the Wild have been tending a crystal garden for centuries. Beautiful. They are not monsters — they are gardeners who lost their words. Player can learn their frequency language: "safe," "danger," "home," "lost."
- **Key Feature:** The Deep Path — passages descending below the Dark Reaches to the deepest point beneath the Resonance Engine. Pure black and white visuals — absolute minimum. The deepest exploration is the most visually reduced.
- **Key Feature:** Byrd's Journal — found in the Outer City. Admiral Byrd's full account of his 1947 Hollow Earth visit. Partial city map. The apology note. (Connects to the Byrd Narrative — the 13th Record in the Archive, the only human frequency.)
- **Progression:** The Wild know a hidden passage to the Inner City (bypassing Claimers' blockade).

## 4. STONE-BASED COMBAT SYSTEM

### Combat Philosophy
Combat is a FREQUENCY-MATCHING PUZZLE, not button-mashing. The player who understands which stones affect which enemies, which ley lines to stand on, which chords to use wins without taking damage.

### Combat Abilities (4 Types)

| Ability | Input | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Stone Throw** | Select charged stone + tap enemy | Projectile attack. Damage based on stone's charge level + frequency match with enemy weakness. |
| **Frequency Barrier** | Hold stone + draw line on ground | Defensive wall. Blocks enemy frequency attacks. Barrier strength based on stone charge. Lasts until enemy frequency overwhelms it. |
| **Chord Strike** | Select chord stone + tap area | Area attack. Resonance cascade hits all enemies in radius. Requires custom chord stone from Resonance Quarter. |
| **Resonance Pulse** | Stand on ley line + release charged stone | Most powerful attack. Pulse travels along ley line, hits every enemy on the line. Requires ley line positioning. |

### Enemy Types

| Enemy | Location | Weakness | Behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crystal Guardian | Outer City, Resonance Quarter | Counter-frequency (match guardian's frequency to disable) | Automated. Patrols ley line streets. Attacks if player lacks matching frequency. |
| Feral Creature | Outer City, Dark Reaches | Any frequency burst | Animal behavior. Pack attacks. Flees when outnumbered. |
| The Wild | Dark Reaches | Light (charged stones create light) | Fear-based. Attack from darkness. Flee from bright stone light. Can be befriended with frequency offerings. |
| The Claimers | Inner City | Chord strikes (resonance cascades disrupt salvaged tech) | Tactical. Use cover, frequency blasters, resonance shields. Most challenging combat. |
| The First | Inner City (boss) | The Inn Song (not combat — the Song convinces) | Boss encounter. Can be fought, but "correct" solution is playing the Inn Song. The First is not evil — just obsessed. |

## 7. AUDIO PROFILE

### The Hollow Earth's Sound
- **Foundation:** 42Hz — CLEAR. Not ambient. Player HEARS it as a musical note. The Resonance Engine (always present).
- **Crystal:** High harmonic of 42Hz — pure, sustained, crystal glass ringing.
- **Water:** Low harmonic of 42Hz — underground ocean drone, deeper, slower.
- **Life:** Bioluminescent organisms — soft organic sounds at 42Hz harmonics.
- **Echoes:** Faint, layered — memory-ghost frequencies, audible when player tunes in.
- **The Lost:** Human sounds — voices, footsteps, tavern clink — only HUMAN sounds in the zone.
- **Combat:** Stone frequencies as weapons — each attack plays the stone's frequency. Chord strikes create resonance cascades.
- **City's Response:** When Song plays: every crystal surface vibrates. Ocean shimmers. 42Hz surges. City SINGS BACK.

### Per-District Audio Identity
- Crystal Approach: Pure 42Hz — clearest tone in game. No harmonics. Just the source.
- Outer City: 42Hz + crystal harmonics.
- Market of Echoes: 42Hz + human sounds (Settled warmth).
- Resonance Quarter: 42Hz + six ring frequencies (111, 64, 432, 528, adaptive, adaptive) — most complex.
- Deep Harbor: 42Hz + water harmonics + bioluminescent sounds.
- Inner City: 42Hz + combat sounds + Claimers' salvaged tech (discordant — only ugly sounds in zone).
- Archive District: 42Hz + 12 records' frequencies (each plays faintly near its shard).
- Resonance Engine: 42Hz at full power — loudest, most overwhelming sound in game.
- Dark Reaches: 42Hz + wild sounds (frequency-growls, crystal garden chimes, deep path echoes).

### The Silence
The most powerful sound is SILENCE. City is empty. Player is alone (before finding Lost). Silence between 42Hz pulses is the city's patience made audible.

## 8. VISUAL STYLE

**Linework:** No-outline (cosmological style) — the Hollow Earth is the Cosmology level's underground mirror. Both no-outline. Both defined by light. Cosmology looks UP at stars. Hollow Earth looks DOWN at the Source.

**The Exception:** The Lost (Settled, Wild, Claimers) are heavy cel-shaded (tradesman style). Only outlined elements. The contrast is deliberate: city is crystal and light (no-outline), people are solid and real (cel-shaded). Humans stand out against crystal the way a person stands out in a cathedral — small, solid, warm against vast, luminous, cold.

**Color Progression (strips down as player goes deeper):**
- Outer City: Rich crystal — violet, teal, gold, white
- Inner City: Simplified — gold and white, violet shadows
- Resonance Engine: Pure white and gold — 42Hz so strong color simplifies
- Dark Reaches: Near-monochrome — deep blue-black with bioluminescent accents
- The Deep Path: Pure black and white — absolute minimum

**Overall Aesthetic:** O'Keeffe — pure form, luminous. The crystal city as O'Keeffe would paint it: luminous organic geometry, light as the primary medium, color simplified to its essence.
