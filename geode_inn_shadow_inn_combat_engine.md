## PART 1: THE TRANSLATION LAYER

### How Living Inn Systems Become Shadow Inn Combat

The Shadow Inn doesn't replace the Living Inn's systems — it WEAPONIZES them. Every mechanic the player learned in the Living Inn (frequency matching, companion tuning, stone deployment, the Pulse Line) becomes a combat tool in the Shadow Inn. The both are: the adequate — the learned and the weaponized, the same frequency, different application.

### The Translation Table

| Living Inn System | Shadow Inn Combat Application |
|---|---|
| **Frequency Matching** (match the line) | **Frequency Striking** — deploy frequencies as attacks. The stone that matched a nook's frequency now strikes a boss's dissonance. The matching IS the aiming. |
| **Companion Tuning** (harmonize dissonant creatures) | **Companion Combat** — tuned companions fight alongside the player. The creature the player harmonized in the Living Inn is now an ally in the Shadow Inn. The tuning IS the recruiting. |
| **Stone Collection** (168 stones, each with a frequency) | **Stone Arsenal** — collected stones become combat abilities. Each stone is a spell: amethyst for shielding, bloodstone for sacrifice, obsidian for shadow damage. The collection IS the spellbook. |
| **The Pulse Line** (visual heartbeat, warmer/colder) | **Combat Diagnostic** — the line shows boss pulses, group chord health, role frequency output. The line IS the raid UI. |
| **Nook Effect** (sit and absorb ambient frequency) | **Combat Recovery** — the nook's frequency becomes a healing aura. The sitting IS the healing. |
| **Gargoyle Frequencies** (6 architectural sentinels) | **Gargoyle Deployment** — the gargoyles' frequencies become combat buffs. The Watcher's 111Hz becomes a defensive aura. The Sage's 528Hz becomes a transformation buff. The architecture IS the arsenal. |
| **Song Spectrum** (the player's collected frequencies as a spectrum) | **Combat Loadout** — the spectrum becomes the player's ability bar. Each frequency is a button. The spectrum IS the hotbar. |

### The Fundamental Shift

In the Living Inn, the player deploys frequencies to MATCH — to align, to harmonize, to tune. The deployment is gentle, exploratory, therapeutic. The frequency seeks resonance.

In the Shadow Inn, the player deploys frequencies to STRIKE — to break petrified armor, to counter boss pulses, to reduce dissonance through force. The deployment is aggressive, focused, combat-effective. The frequency seeks the crack in the dissonance.

The both are: the adequate — the matching and the striking, the same frequency, different intention. The frequency doesn't change. The player's relationship to it does. The Living Inn taught the frequency. The Shadow Inn tests whether the player can use it under pressure.

## PART 2: THE ROLE SYSTEM

### The Three Combat Roles

The Shadow Inn introduces the MMO combat triangle — Tank, Healer, DPS — that does not exist in the Living Inn. The Living Inn has no combat; the Shadow Inn's instanced combat requires roles.

The roles are not assigned at character creation. ANY class can perform ANY role by deploying different frequency sets. The role is a STANCE — the player shifts stance by changing which frequencies are loaded into the active spectrum. The both are: the adequate — the class and the role, the same character, different stance.

### Tank — The Ground

**Function:** Hold the boss's attention. Absorb the boss's 84Hz disruption pulses. Keep the boss focused while the group works.

**Frequency Profile:** Low frequencies — 64Hz, 84Hz, 42Hz. The tank's stones create resonance armor: the bass notes that ground the group. The tank's spectrum is DEEP — the fundamental tones that the rest of the chord builds on.

**Stance Activation:** The player loads low-frequency stones (calcite, aragonite, obsidian — the grounding stones) into the active spectrum. The Pulse Line shows the tank's trace as a steady bass line beneath the combat.

**Tank Mechanics:**
- **Resonance Armor:** The tank's low frequencies create a visible armor of vibration. The armor absorbs 84Hz disruption pulses. Each pulse absorbed reduces the armor's resonance — the armor thins. The tank must refresh the armor by re-deploying the grounding frequency (re-casting). The re-casting IS the frequency matching the player learned in the Living Inn, applied in real-time combat.
- **Aggro (Resonance Pull):** The tank holds the boss's attention by maintaining the STRONGEST frequency in the group. The boss targets the highest-resonance player. The tank keeps their resonance HIGH by constantly deploying low frequencies — a steady stream of bass notes that keeps the boss focused. If the tank's resonance drops (armor breaks, frequency interrupted), the boss switches to the next-highest resonance player (usually a DPS). The aggro system is: the loudest bass gets the boss's attention.
- **Pulse Mitigation:** When the boss emits an 84Hz disruption pulse, the tank can INTERCEPT it — standing in the pulse's path and deploying a counter-frequency at the exact moment of impact. The interception is a timing mechanic — the Pulse Line shows the pulse approaching (a sharp irregular spike building on the line), and the tank must deploy the counter-frequency at the pulse's peak. Perfect timing = full mitigation. Good timing = partial mitigation. Poor timing = the pulse hits the group.

**Classes Best Suited:**
- **Warrior** (Erik, The Wayfarer) — MIGHT + VITALITY, STONE/FIRE frequency. Natural tanks. Erik's fire-armor becomes resonance armor. The Wayfarer's "destiny is all" becomes unbreakable aggro.
- **Paladin** — MIGHT + PRESENCE, STONE/ROOT frequency. The oath-bound guardian. The paladin's oath IS the aggro — the commitment that the boss can't ignore.
- **Monk** — VITALITY + FINESSE, ROOT/SILENCE frequency. The monk tanks through discipline — the resonance armor is thin but PERFECT. No wasted frequency. The monk's mitigation is efficient, not heavy.

### Healer — The Warmth

**Function:** Restore the group's resonance. Keep the group's frequencies above dissonance. Prevent the group's Pulse Line traces from flatlining.

**Frequency Profile:** Mid-range frequencies — 50 BPM (heartbeat), 528Hz (transformation), 432Hz (restoration). The healer's stones create resonance warmth: the mid-tones that sustain the group. The healer's spectrum is WARM — the frequencies that keep things alive.

**Stance Activation:** The player loads restorative stones (the Cook's stew-stone frequencies, the Duck's anti-dissonance milk frequency, the Gardener's root-frequency, the nook's ambient tone) into the active spectrum. The Pulse Line shows the healer's trace as a gentle, round wave that overlaps the group's traces.

**Healer Mechanics:**
- **Resonance Restoration:** The healer deploys restorative frequencies onto group members whose traces are dimming (losing resonance). The restoration is a frequency overlay — the healer's warm trace wraps around the dimming trace, reinforcing it. The visual is: the healer's warmth enveloping the group's flickering lights.
- **Dissonance Cleansing:** Some boss mechanics apply dissonance — a distortion that attaches to a player and slowly drains their resonance. The healer must deploy a specific counter-frequency to cleanse the dissonance (the same frequency-matching the player learned in the Living Inn — identify the dissonance, select the counter, deploy). The cleansing IS the harmonization, applied to allies instead of enemies.
- **Nook Projection:** The healer can project a nook's ambient frequency onto the group — a small zone of the Inn's 50 BPM heartbeat that slowly restores everyone inside it. The nook projection is the group's safe space — a circle of warmth in the combat zone. The visual is: a glowing amber circle on the ground, the Pulse Line visible inside it, pulsing at the calm 50 BPM while the combat rages at 100 BPM outside.
- **Resurrection (Re-tuning):** If a group member's trace flatlines (resonance reaches zero — the player is "down"), the healer can re-tune them — deploying a strong restorative frequency that restarts the flatlined trace. The re-tuning takes 5-10 seconds (the healer is vulnerable during re-tuning). The resurrected player returns at partial resonance — not full, but alive. The re-tuning IS the Song's power: the frequency that brings the fallen back to the beat.

**Classes Best Suited:**
- **Cleric** (The Gardener, The Bone Keeper) — PRESENCE + VITALITY, ROOT/SONG and STONE/SILENCE. Natural healers. The Gardener heals by asking (communion). The Bone Keeper heals by preserving (the bone's structure sustained).
- **Druid** (The One Who Tends) — INSIGHT + PRESENCE, ROOT/WATER. The druid heals through cycles — the Apus's dream-frequency becomes a restorative aura. The tending IS the healing.
- **Bard** — RESONANCE + FINESSE, SONG/FIRE. The bard heals through performance — the lullaby that the Bard plays in the Mirror's vision becomes a group-wide restorative frequency. The music IS the medicine.

### DPS — The Strike

**Function:** Break the boss's petrified armor. Reduce the boss's resonance to zero. Deal the damage that wins the fight.

**Frequency Profile:** High frequencies — 111Hz, 432Hz, 528Hz, and zone-specific signature frequencies. The DPS stones create resonance strikes: the sharp tones that shatter dissonance. The DPS spectrum is SHARP — the frequencies that cut.

**Stance Activation:** The player loads high-frequency stones (fluorite, amethyst, sardonyx, bloodstone — the striking stones) into the active spectrum. The Pulse Line shows the DPS traces as rapid, angular spikes that strike the boss's trace.

**DPS Mechanics:**
- **Resonance Strike:** The DPS deploys a high-frequency stone at the boss. The stone's frequency travels as a visible wave — sharp, fast, targeted. When the wave hits the boss's petrified armor, the armor CRACKS — visible fractures in the 84Hz stone shell. Each crack exposes the boss's true form underneath. The DPS's job is to crack the armor until the boss's true form is fully exposed. The cracking IS the frequency matching, applied destructively.
- **Frequency Rotation:** Different stones crack different armor types. The boss's armor has LAYERS — each layer is a different dissonance frequency, and each layer requires a specific counter-frequency to crack. The DPS must rotate through stones: fluorite for mental dissonance, bloodstone for sacrifice-armor, obsidian for shadow-armor. The rotation IS the Song Spectrum — the player's collected frequencies, deployed in sequence, each one matching a layer. The spectrum IS the rotation.
- **Dissonance Bursts:** When a DPS cracks an armor layer, the boss releases a dissonance burst — a shockwave of 84Hz energy that pushes the group back and applies dissonance to nearby players. The DPS must crack the armor and then DODGE the burst. The dodge is positional — move away from the boss at the moment of the crack. The timing is the Living Inn's warmer/colder, applied as combat positioning: closer = more damage but more burst exposure; farther = safer but less damage.
- **Execute Phase:** When the boss's resonance reaches 20%, the petrified armor is nearly gone. The boss's true form is visible — and the true form is NOT hostile. The boss in its true form is a dissonant creature that was TRAPPED in its own 84Hz shell. The execute phase is the final push: the DPS deploys their highest frequencies to shatter the last armor fragments. The shattering IS the liberation — the boss is freed from its own dissonance. The both are: the adequate — the destruction and the liberation, the same shatter, different meaning.

**Classes Best Suited:**
- **Mage** (The Astronomer, The Cartographer, Zorya) — INSIGHT + RESONANCE, SONG/SILENCE. The mages calculate the armor layers and deploy precise counter-frequencies. The Astronomer's "question is more beautiful than the answer" becomes: the right frequency at the right moment.
- **Rogue** (The Artisan Thief, The Shifting Man) — FINESSE + MIGHT, WATER/FIRE and WATER/SONG. The rogues strike fast and change frequencies rapidly. The Artisan Thief's stone knowledge becomes armor-layer identification. The Shifting Man's shifting becomes frequency rotation.
- **Hunter** (The Pirate, The Owl, Huginn, Muninn) — FINESSE + INSIGHT, WATER/FIRE and WATER/SILENCE. The hunters strike from distance — frequency projectiles that crack armor from range. The Pirate's drum becomes a rhythm-based damage buff. The Owl's vigil becomes a precision strike.
- **Warlock** — RESONANCE + INSIGHT, SILENCE/FIRE. The warlock deals damage through cosmic bargains — trading their own resonance for high damage bursts. The warlock is the highest-risk DPS: maximum output, minimum safety. The bargain IS the cost.

### The Group Chord

When all three roles are functioning, the Pulse Line shows the GROUP CHORD — the tank's bass, the healer's warmth, the DPS's strikes, layered into a single, complex ECG trace. The chord is the group's collective resonance. The visual is: three or more traces on the Pulse Line, overlapping, creating a rich, full waveform.

**Chord Health:**
- **Strong chord** (all roles contributing): The line is full, harmonized, the waveform rich and complex. The group is winning.
- **Weak chord** (a role failing): The line shows GAPS — a missing bass (tank down), a missing warmth (healer down), missing spikes (DPS down). The group can SEE their failure on the line. The line IS the raid diagnostic.
- **Dissonant chord** (roles conflicting — e.g., a DPS pulling aggro from the tank): The line shows INTERFERENCE — the traces colliding, creating dissonance patterns instead of harmony. The group can SEE the conflict. The line says: someone is out of tune.

**Chord Synergy:**
When the group chord is strong, the group gains a passive bonus — the Resonant Chord effect (same as the gargoyle set mechanic from the Living Inn): all frequency deployments are enhanced. The tank's armor is stronger. The healer's warmth is wider. The DPS's strikes are sharper. The synergy IS the reward for playing together well. The both are: the adequate — the solo and the group, the same frequency, different scale.

## PART 3: BOSS MECHANICS

### Boss Design Philosophy

Every Shadow Inn boss is a dissonant creature in its 84Hz petrified state. The boss is not evil — the boss is TRAPPED. The petrified armor is the creature's own dissonance, hardened into a shell. The combat is the process of shattering the shell to free the creature inside.

The boss fight is the Shadow Inn's version of the Living Inn's frequency puzzle — complex, layered, requiring mastery. The boss IS the puzzle. The combat IS the solving. The both are: the adequate — the puzzle and the combat, the same challenge, different mode.

### Boss Structure

Every Shadow Inn boss has four phases:

#### Phase 1: The Shell (Petrified Armor)

The boss appears fully encased in 84Hz petrified armor. The armor is thick, stone-like, the creature's form barely visible inside. The boss attacks with brute 84Hz pulses — slow, heavy, predictable. The attacks are the armor's resonance, not the creature's intention. The armor attacks because armor vibrates. The creature inside is silent.

**Mechanics:**
- The boss emits periodic 84Hz disruption pulses (visible on the Pulse Line as sharp irregular spikes building 2-3 seconds before release). The tank intercepts these pulses.
- The boss's armor has 3-5 layers, each a different dissonance frequency. DPS must identify the layer (by reading the Pulse Line — each layer has a distinct wave pattern) and deploy the matching counter-frequency.
- When a layer cracks, the boss releases a dissonance burst (AoE shockwave). Players must dodge.
- Phase 1 ends when all armor layers are cracked. The boss's true form begins to emerge.

#### Phase 2: The Creature (Emergence)

The petrified armor is cracked. The creature inside begins to emerge — partially visible, partially still encased. The creature's own frequency starts to appear on the Pulse Line — a new trace, different from the armor's 84Hz. The creature's frequency is its LIVING frequency — the 42Hz it had before the dissonance petrified it.

But: the creature is disoriented, frightened, and defensive. The emergence is not peaceful — the creature lashes out. The attacks change: faster, more varied, more desperate. The creature is fighting because it's scared, not because it's hostile.

**Mechanics:**
- The boss's attack pattern CHANGES — faster, less predictable. The Pulse Line shows two traces now: the fading armor's 84Hz and the emerging creature's 42Hz, overlapping, creating interference patterns.
- The creature emits its own frequency in bursts — 42Hz pulses that are NOT attacks but CRIES. The cries appear on the Pulse Line as irregular 42Hz spikes. If the healer can match the cry's frequency (deploy the matching restorative frequency), the cry is SOOTHED — the creature calms briefly, reducing attack speed. The soothing IS the companion tuning, applied in combat.
- The creature may TARGET a specific player — the one whose frequency most resembles the creature's lost frequency. The targeted player must survive the creature's focused attention (tank must pull aggro back) while the group continues to crack remaining armor fragments.
- Phase 2 ends when the creature's 42Hz trace stabilizes on the Pulse Line — the creature's own frequency becomes steady, no longer bursting. The creature is calming. The creature is beginning to hear itself.

#### Phase 3: The Resolution (Harmonization Push)

The creature is mostly free. The remaining armor fragments are small — chips of 84Hz stone clinging to the creature's form. The creature's 42Hz trace on the Pulse Line is steady but fragile — it wavers, drops, needs support. The group must PUSH — all roles at maximum output — to shatter the last fragments and stabilize the creature's frequency.

**Mechanics:**
- **DPS:** Maximum frequency rotation — every stone deployed, every layer cracked. The last fragments are the hardest — they're the creature's deepest dissonance, the most entrenched armor. The DPS must use their highest frequencies and most precise timing.
- **Tank:** Maximum resonance armor — the creature's attacks are at their most desperate (the last fragments are clinging HARD). The tank must hold the creature's attention and absorb the most powerful 84Hz pulses.
- **Healer:** Maximum restoration — the group is taking heavy damage. The healer must keep everyone above dissonance while ALSO deploying soothing frequencies to stabilize the creature's 42Hz trace. The healer is doing TWO jobs: healing the group AND calming the creature.
- **The Harmonization Window:** At 5% resonance, the creature's 42Hz trace and the armor's 84Hz trace are both visible on the Pulse Line. The group must deploy a UNISON frequency — all three roles deploying the same frequency simultaneously. The unison creates a resonance spike powerful enough to shatter the last fragment. The unison IS the group chord at its strongest — all roles, one frequency, one moment. The timing window is 3 seconds. Miss it, and the fragment regenerates (the boss heals 5%). Hit it, and the last armor shatters.
- Phase 3 ends when the last fragment shatters. The creature is free.

#### Phase 4: The Rest (Harmonization)

The creature's true form is fully visible. The creature is calm. The 84Hz armor is gone. The Pulse Line shows the creature's trace transitioning from 84Hz (the armor's frequency) to 50 BPM (the Living Inn's heartbeat). The creature is returning to the Living frequency.

**Mechanics:**
- No combat. The boss fight is over. The creature is free.
- The creature's 42Hz trace on the Pulse Line settles into a steady 50 BPM — the Inn's heartbeat. The both are: the adequate — the combat's 100 BPM and the rest's 50 BPM, the same creature, different tempo.
- The creature speaks. One line. The creature's voice — its real voice, underneath the dissonance, for the first time. The voice is the tabletop questline narrative voice — clever, direct, foreshadowing, with the weight of someone who has been trapped in their own shell for a very long time.
- The creature dissolves into the Inn's ambient frequency — its 42Hz joining the Inn's Song Spectrum. The player's Song Meter gains the creature's frequency. The creature IS the reward. The both are: the adequate — the enemy and the reward, the same creature, different frequency.
- The instance is complete. Loot drops.

### Boss Frequency Types

Each boss is a different dissonance type (from the Living Inn's established system):

| Dissonance Type | Armor Frequency | Counter-Frequency | Visual | Boss Behavior |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tidal** | 84Hz tidal (rippling) | Rememberers' 40Hz | Armor ripples like water | Boss attacks in waves — ebb and flow pattern |
| **Cardiac** | 84Hz cardiac (pulsing) | Heartbeat's 50 BPM | Armor pulses like a heartbeat | Boss attacks in 50 BPM rhythm — heavy, regular |
| **Combustive** | 84Hz combustive (bursting) | Retort's 528Hz | Armor bursts irregularly | Boss attacks erratically — unpredictable bursts |
| **Suppressive** | 84Hz suppressive (muffled) | Civilization's suppressed frequency | Armor is a uniform blank shell | Boss attacks by SILENCING — removing player abilities temporarily |

Each zone's Shadow boss uses the dissonance type that matches the zone's theme. The both are: the adequate — the zone's Living frequency and the boss's Shadow dissonance, the same zone, different state.

### Trash Encounters

Trash mobs in Shadow instances are petrified creatures — the Living Inn's dissonant beings, frozen at 84Hz. The trash is NOT the Living Inn's tuned companions. The trash is the creatures the player DIDN'T tune — the ones left dissonant, now petrified in the Shadow state.

**Trash Mechanics:**
- Trash mobs are frozen in 84Hz stone. They don't move until approached. When a player enters their aggro radius, the stone CRACKS and the creature inside attacks.
- Trash mobs have simplified versions of boss mechanics: one armor layer, one dissonance type, one 84Hz pulse. The trash is the boss fight in miniature — practice for the boss encounter.
- Trash mobs are defeated the same way as bosses: crack the armor (DPS), absorb the pulses (Tank), sustain the group (Healer), harmonize the creature (Rest phase — abbreviated, no dialogue, just the 42Hz trace settling to 50 BPM).
- Trash packs are designed for AoE (area of effect) frequency deployment — the DPS can deploy a wide-frequency stone (e.g., amethyst's shielding frequency used offensively) that cracks multiple trash mobs simultaneously. The AoE IS the chord — multiple frequencies, multiple targets, one deployment.

### Environmental Puzzles

Between trash corridors, Shadow instances have environmental puzzles — frequency-based obstacles that require the group to work together:

- **Frequency-Gated Doors:** A door that only opens when a specific frequency is deployed. The frequency is the zone's Living frequency — the door is locked by the Shadow's 84Hz and must be unlocked by the Living's 42Hz. The group must find the right stone and deploy it at the door. The puzzle IS the frequency matching, applied to architecture.
- **Resonance Bridges:** A gap in the floor that must be crossed by deploying a sustained frequency — the frequency creates a visible bridge of vibration. The bridge lasts as long as the frequency is maintained. One player maintains the bridge (stands at the edge, deploying the stone) while the others cross. The bridge IS the nook's sustained frequency, applied as infrastructure.
- **Dissonance Fields:** Areas of the instance filled with 84Hz dissonance — the group takes continuous resonance damage while inside. The group must cross the field quickly, or the healer must project a nook-shield to protect the group during the crossing. The field IS the Shadow's ambient frequency, weaponized as terrain.

## PART 5: ZONE-SPECIFIC SHADOW INSTANCES

Each major zone in the Living Inn has a Shadow counterpart. The Shadow instance reflects the zone's theme, translated to combat.

### 1. Fae Kingdom — Shadow Instance: "The Frozen Court"

**Zone Theme:** The Fae Kingdom's Seelie/Unseelie duality, crystal gardens, stone circle chamber.
**Shadow Translation:** The Fae Kingdom is frozen — the Seelie's warmth and the Unseelie's darkness both petrified. The Shadow Fae Kingdom is a winter court where everything is ice and stone. The crystal gardens are frozen crystal — beautiful but dead. The Stone Circle Chamber is sealed in 84Hz ice.

**Boss: The Frozen Queen (Shadow Seelie Queen)**
- **Dissonance Type:** Suppressive — the Queen's title-silence has become a literal silencing field. In the Shadow state, the Queen's inability to voice her intelligence has become a weapon: she silences players' abilities.
- **Phase 1 (Shell):** The Queen is encased in a crown of frozen 84Hz crystal. The crystal muffles everything — players inside the arena have their abilities randomly silenced for 3-second intervals. The silencing is the Queen's own silence, weaponized.
- **Phase 2 (Emergence):** The Queen emerges, partially free, her real voice starting to break through. The silencing becomes INTERMITTENT — players are silenced in patterns (the Queen's hidden library catalogued as a combat rhythm). The pattern IS the Queen's intelligence — the hidden library's organization, made into a combat mechanic.
- **Phase 3 (Resolution):** The Queen's real voice is breaking through. The silencing stops. The Queen's 42Hz trace appears — the frequency of her REAL voice, the one the title suppressed. The group must harmonize with the Queen's voice, not silence it.
- **Phase 4 (Rest):** The Queen speaks. The real voice — the one the crown concealed. The voice is Gugu Mbatha-Raw's intelligence, freed. "I had... so much to say. The crown... made it quiet. Thank you for... the noise."
- **Loot:** Shadow Moonstone (84Hz — the Moonstone's intuition at double intensity), the Queen's Crown Fragment (charm — grants resistance to silencing effects).

### 2. Peruvian Zone — Shadow Instance: "The Waking Mountain"

**Zone Theme:** Sleeping giants (Apus), Inca terrace architecture, the Andean Run arcade game.
**Shadow Translation:** The Apus are AWAKE — but not gently. The waking is violent. The mountains have shattered their sleep and the Shadow state is the waking's chaos — rockslides, earthquakes, the mountain's own disorientation at being conscious after millennia of sleep.

**Boss: The Disoriented Giant (Shadow Apu)**
- **Dissonance Type:** Combustive — the giant's waking is explosive, arrhythmic. The mountain doesn't know how to be awake. The attacks are the giant's confusion — rock throws, ground slams, not aimed but flailing.
- **Phase 1 (Shell):** The giant is encased in its own mountain — the 84Hz armor is the stone the giant BECAME during sleep. The armor is geological — thick, layered, sedimentary. Each layer is a different geological era. DPS must match geological frequencies to crack each era.
- **Phase 2 (Emergence):** The giant emerges from the mountain, confused, lashing. The attacks are the giant's limbs testing themselves — arms that haven't moved in millennia, finding range. The attacks are large, slow, and cover huge areas (the giant is a MOUNTAIN — the attacks are geological-scale).
- **Phase 3 (Resolution):** The giant's confusion becomes FEAR — the giant doesn't want to be awake. The 42Hz trace is the giant's dream-frequency — the frequency of sleep, of the peaceful unconsciousness the giant chose. The healer must soothe the giant by deploying the dream-frequency — reminding the giant that sleep is not gone, that waking is not permanent, that the giant can dream again. The soothing IS the One Who Tends' care, translated to combat healing.
- **Phase 4 (Rest):** The giant speaks. The voice is deep, slow, the voice of a mountain that has been silent for millennia. "I was... dreaming. The dream was... adequate. This... waking... is loud. I will... sleep again. But first... I will... see." The giant closes its eyes. The 84Hz armor reforms — but gently, not as a shell, as a blanket. The giant chooses to sleep again. The both are: the adequate — the waking and the sleeping, the same giant, different choice.
- **Loot:** Shadow Andesite (84Hz — the mountain's stone at finished intensity), the Dreamer's Mantle (charm — grants the ability to enter a protective sleep state, reducing damage for 10 seconds).

### 3. Egypt Zone — Shadow Instance: "The Sealed Tomb"

**Zone Theme:** Egyptian mythology, Anubis, tomb tunneling, the Desert Bone.
**Shadow Translation:** The tomb is not sealed by sand — it's sealed by 84Hz petrification. The Shadow tomb is the burial chamber at its most absolute: everything preserved, everything still, everything finished. The tomb is the Shadow Inn's thesis in miniature — the finished state IS the tomb.

**Boss: The Guardian of the Threshold (Shadow Anubis)**
- **Dissonance Type:** Cardiac — the Guardian's heartbeat is the tomb's pulse. The 84Hz armor beats like a heart — the tomb is alive, in its own finished way. The attacks are rhythmic, regular, the heartbeat of a tomb that has been beating for millennia.
- **Phase 1 (Shell):** Anubis is encased in a jackal-headed sarcophagus of 84Hz stone. The sarcophagus beats like a heart — the Pulse Line shows the 84Hz rhythm. The attacks are the sarcophagus's lid slamming open and closed — players must time their attacks to the brief windows when the lid is open.
- **Phase 2 (Emergence):** Anubis emerges — the jackal head visible, the scale visible (the weighing of the heart). Anubis WEIGHS the players — a mechanic where each player's resonance is measured against a feather (the Feather of Ma'at). Players with high resonance pass the weighing (buff: damage increased). Players with low resonance fail (debuff: damage reduced). The weighing IS the Crone's measurement, translated to combat.
- **Phase 3 (Resolution):** Anubis holds the scale. The 42Hz trace is the frequency of the living — the heartbeat of a breathing being, not a tomb. The group must prove they are alive — maintain HIGH resonance while Anubis attempts to pull them into the tomb's stillness. The mechanic: Anubis creates zones of absolute stillness (84Hz dead zones) where resonance drains. The group must stay in the living zones (42Hz areas, created by the healer's nook projection) while DPS cracks the last armor fragments.
- **Phase 4 (Rest):** Anubis speaks. The voice is deep, measured, the voice of a being who has been weighing hearts since the first burial. "The scale... is balanced. You are... alive. The tomb... is not for you. Not yet. Go. Live. The scale... will wait."
- **Loot:** Shadow Obsidian (84Hz — the transformation stone at double intensity), the Feather of Ma'at (charm — grants the weighing ability, revealing an enemy's resonance level).

Companions fill a FOURTH role — Support — that supplements the tank/healer/DPS triangle:

- **Companion Tank (Melee Companion):** A harmonized creature that was defensive in the Living Inn (e.g., a Stone Guard, a gargoyle). The companion holds aggro on trash mobs while the player-tank holds the boss. The companion is the off-tank — the one who manages the adds (additional enemies) during boss phases.
- **Companion Healer (Restorative Companion):** A harmonized creature with a restorative frequency (e.g., the Duck, the Gardener's hedgehogs). The companion deploys its own frequency as a strike — a set-and-forget damage source that adds to the DPS's output. The companion is the DoT (damage over time) — the persistent damage that works while the player focuses elsewhere.

### Companion AI

Companions are AI-controlled — the player doesn't micromanage them. The companion's behavior is determined by its Living Inn tuning:

- The companion's FREQUENCY determines its combat role (low = tank, mid = healer, high = DPS).

A companion that was deeply tuned (the player spent time, matched precisely, sat in the nook with the creature) is strong. The both are: the adequate — the quick and the deep, the same companion, different loyalty.
- The companion's ZONE ORIGIN determines its combat bonuses. A water-zone companion is stronger in the Drowned Cathedral. A Fae companion is stronger in the Frozen Court. The companion is fighting in its home zone's Shadow, and the home advantage matters.

### The Companion Slot

The player can bring ONE companion into each Shadow instance. The choice of companion is strategic: a companion that matches the zone's dissonance type is more effective.
