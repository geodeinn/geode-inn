All 15 spirits in this catalog belong to **Class 2: THE DENSE (Dynamic Driver — DD)**.

- **Class Sensation:** "A warm tone you both hear and feel. It resonates in the throat and sternum, the way a low bell carries through a stone wall."
- **Class Visual Style:** Heavier, more textured linework. Mineral-like bodies. More creature than person. These spirits have MASS—compact, stone-bodied forms that look like they weigh something.

- **Frequency Visualization:** Pulses rather than ripples. The spirit's body visibly pulses and throbs with its stone frequency (Hz). Lower frequencies produce deeper, slower pulses; higher frequencies produce tight, intense rhythmic shockwaves.
- **Posture/Expression:** In the DD class, facial expressions are secondary to physical posture, weight distribution, and stance.
