# SPETER ADAMS — The Chameleon / The Dollkeeper
## Eileen's Third Child — NPC Profile

**Real Name:** Adam (NPC name: Speter Adams — chosen 2026-09-03)
**Age:** 22
**Pronouns:** Gender fluid — shifts. The Inn respects whichever Speter Adams is wearing that day.
**Family:** Eileen's third of four children. Sibling to Scarlett (17, Undercity), John (15, the family Fezzik), and the fourth (TBD).

---

## VISUAL IDENTITY

Built like a nymph — tall but slight, willow-thin, the kind of frame that makes fabric look like it's floating. The kind of presence that makes a room rearrange itself without Speter Adams moving. Not beautiful in any fixed way — beautiful in the way water is beautiful, because it keeps becoming something else.

**The Linework Tell:** Speter Adams is the one character in the Inn whose linework style CHANGES by zone. In the Undercity: heavy cel-shaded. In lore areas: soft flowing. In the Catacombs: sharp angular. In Cosmology: no-outline. On the main floor: whatever the scene demands. The chameleon doesn't have one rendering style — Speter Adams IS the linework system demonstrated as character. The art style shifts because Speter Adams shifts.

**The Constant:** The dolls. Wherever Speter Adams goes, at least one doll is present. Not carried — placed. Speter Adams's dolls live in every zone. The doll is the tell. You recognize Speter Adams not by face or form but by the doll sitting on the shelf, the chair, the windowsill. Speter Adams is always nearby.

**Wardrobe:** A performer's wardrobe. Costumes for every role. Each level has a different Speter Adams — different clothes, different posture, different voice register. The main floor Speter Adams is loud and theatrical. The Undercity Speter Adams is quiet and precise. The Cosmology Speter Adams is reverent and still. The Catacombs Speter Adams is careful and watchful. The Water Zone Speter Adams is fluid and singing. Same person. Completely different expressions.

## THE DOLLS — Spirits Given Homes

Speter Adams collects dolls. Not as objects — as vessels. The dolls are "possessed" in the sense that spirits need a form to inhabit the Inn, and Speter Adams gives them one. Each doll is a home for a spirit that otherwise couldn't manifest.

**The Mechanic:**
- Speter Adams places dolls throughout the Inn — one per zone minimum, often more
- Each doll houses a spirit with a personality, a story, a reason it needs a form
- Players who interact with a doll are interacting with the spirit inside
- Some spirits are helpful (quest hints, lore, guidance). Some are mischievous. Some are lost. Some are ancient.
- Speter Adams is the keeper — the one who makes sure every spirit has a home. The Inn's unofficial host for the dead, the lost, and the between.

**The Crone's Discomfort:**
The Crone is the archive keeper. The Crone measures, judges, and maintains. The Crone does NOT host. Hosting is Eileen's domain — the Hearth's warmth, the unconditional welcome.

But Speter Adams's charisma is so natural, so unforced, that the Crone finds herself being hospitable. Opening a door. Offering a seat. Saying "you can stay" before she realizes she's said it. Then the discomfort — because warmth isn't the Crone's role. The Crone is precision. The Crone is the Hopkins gaze that sees what you are and whether it's enough. The Crone doesn't do "come in, sit down, you're welcome here."

Speter Adams pulls it out of her anyway. Not by trying. By being. The chameleon doesn't perform hospitality — the chameleon IS hospitality, and the Crone catches it like a cold she didn't know she could get.

**The Dynamic:**
- The Crone measures Speter Adams and finds... no fixed measurement. Speter Adams is whatever the room needs. The Crone's entire identity is measurement. How do you measure something that changes shape?
- The Crone is hospitable to Speter Adams because Speter Adams makes every space feel like Speter Adams belongs there — including the Crone's archive. The Crone doesn't want Speter Adams in the archive. The Crone can't find a reason to say no.
- This makes the Crone grumble. Grumbling is the Crone's love language.

## ADAM'S HOMES — One Per Level

Speter Adams is the only character in the Inn with a home on every level. Each home is a different expression:

### 1. Main Floor — The Stage
Speter Adams's main floor home is a small room behind the arcade — a performer's dressing room. Costumes on hooks. Mirrors. A doll on every surface. This is where Speter Adams is loudest — the charismatic, theatrical, main-role energy. The room is warm, cluttered, alive. The opposite of every other space Speter Adams inhabits.

**Role:** Performer. Speter Adams can fill in for any NPC who is absent — the chameleon steps into the missing role. The Inn never feels empty because Speter Adams is always ready to become whoever the scene needs.

### 2. Undercity — The Manga Nook
A corner of the Undercity near Scarlett's room. Manga store made permanent — shelves of manga built into the ancient stone. Smaller, quieter, more precise than the main floor Speter Adams. Here Speter Adams is the sibling — sharing space with Scarlett, the two of them in comfortable silence, each doing their own thing. The dolls here are manga-style — inked faces, dramatic eyes, stories in their poses.

**Role:** The quiet sibling. The one who sits with Scarlett in the dark and doesn't need to talk.

### 3. Staircase — The Changing Room
A small alcove on the Staircase between alchemical stages. This is where Speter Adams transforms between levels — literally changes costume, voice, posture. The chameleon's transition point. The dolls here are in various states of completion — some half-painted, some half-dressed, some just starting. Spirits in progress.

**Role:** The transformer. The staircase is the Inn's alchemical transition zone, and Speter Adams uses it the same way — transitioning between selves. The chameleon's shed.

### 4. Catacombs — The Crone's Discomfort
A small space near the Crone's archive that the Crone did not assign and cannot explain. Speter Adams just... appeared there one day. The Crone said nothing. Speter Adams stayed. The dolls here are the oldest — ancient faces, worn paint, spirits that have been waiting the longest. Speter Adams is quiet here. Watchful. The Crone's gaze and Speter Adams's watchfulness occupy the same space, and neither is comfortable with it.

**Role:** The watcher who watches the watcher. The Crone measures everything. Speter Adams watches the Crone measure. The Crone cannot decide if this is comforting or infuriating. It's both.

### 5. Cosmology — The Constellation
A small platform near the Allfather's observatory. Speter Adams is still here. Reverent. The dolls here are dressed as stars — tiny figures with silver thread and glass beads, arranged in patterns that mirror constellations. The spirits here are the oldest — spirits that remember the sky before the Inn had walls.

**Role:** The quiet one. The one who sits in the vastness and doesn't try to fill it. Speter Adams's chameleon nature goes still here — not performing, not becoming, just being small under a big sky.

### 6. Water Zone — The Floating Stage
A raft or platform that drifts on the Water Zone's current. Speter Adams is singing here. Fluid. The dolls float — small boats, water-safe figures, spirits that chose water as their home. The Water Zone Speter Adams is the most unguarded — the performer without an audience, singing for the sake of singing.

**Role:** The free one. The chameleon doesn't change form in water — the chameleon just is. Water doesn't judge shape.

## THE CHAMELEON THREAD

Charles is the Chameleon Druid — the chameleon who becomes the landscape. Speter Adams is the chameleon who becomes the character. Same gift, different expression. The druidic vs. the theatrical.

**The Inheritance:**
- Eileen (the Hearth) gives warmth
- Charles (the Chameleon Druid) gives adaptability
- Speter Adams inherits the chameleon trait but expresses it through performance, not druidic transformation
- Speter Adams doesn't become the forest. Speter Adams becomes the person the forest needs.

**The Dolls as Chameleon Mechanic:**
The dolls ARE the chameleon system made tangible. Each doll is a self Speter Adams can wear. The chameleon doesn't just shift shape — the chameleon has a *collection of selves*, each one housed in a doll, each one a character Speter Adams can step into. The dolls aren't possessions. They're options. They're Speter Adams's wardrobe of identities.

## GAMEPLAY ROLE

**The Inn's Universal Standby:**
- Speter Adams can fill any missing NPC role — if a character is absent (wandering program, quest state, time of day), Speter Adams steps in
- This makes the Inn feel perpetually alive — there's always someone there, always someone in character
- But the player learns to read the difference: the "real" NPC has their own linework style. Speter Adams-as-NPC has Speter Adams's shifting linework. The chameleon is always slightly off — not wrong, just different. The performance is perfect but the performer shows through.

**The Doll Side Quest:**
- Players can find Speter Adams's dolls throughout the Inn — one per zone minimum
- Each doll has a spirit with a story
- Some spirits give quests. Some give lore. Some just want to talk.
- Collecting all of Speter Adams's dolls (finding every spirit's home) unlocks... something. Speter Adams's trust. Speter Adams's real face. The one Speter Adams wears when no one else is watching.

**The Crone Quest Line:**
- The Crone's discomfort with Speter Adams is a slow-burn quest
- The player witnesses the Crone being hospitable — small moments, accumulated over time
- Eventually the Crone admits (never says directly — the Crone never says directly) that Speter Adams's presence in the archive is... not unwelcome
- This is the Crone's most vulnerable moment. The measurer admitting something can't be measured.

## THE SWITZERLAND — Origin of the Chameleon

Speter Adams has been the Switzerland of the family since birth. The neutral one. The one who doesn't take sides — not because of indifference, but because taking a side would mean choosing one expression over all the others. Speter Adams is all of them.

**The chameleon didn't develop as a performance technique. It developed as a survival strategy.** When the family has friction, the neutral party learns to fit every room because picking a room means picking a side. Speter Adams didn't choose to be everywhere — being everywhere was the only way to be home.

This reframes the homes on every level. They aren't a power. They're a map of where Speter Adams learned to be welcome. Each home is a space Speter Adams earned by becoming what that space needed. The main floor needed a performer. The Undercity needed a quiet sibling. The Catacombs needed a watcher. The Cosmology needed a still presence. The Water needed a singer. Speter Adams didn't pick these roles — each space opened because Speter Adams became the thing that could enter.

**The Inn's Neutrality Made Flesh:**
The Geode Inn is a neutral archive. Speter Adams is the neutral person. The chameleon's neutrality IS the Inn's neutrality, expressed as a human being. The archive doesn't take sides between histories. Speter Adams doesn't take sides between people. The archive holds everything. Speter Adams holds everyone.

**The Crone Connection (Reframed):**
The Crone's discomfort isn't just about hospitality. It's about recognition. The Crone measures everything — and the Crone recognizes in Speter Adams the same neutrality the Crone herself maintains. The Crone is the archive's neutrality made structural. Speter Adams is the family's neutrality made personal. They're the same principle in different forms. The Crone can't reject Speter Adams because rejecting Speter Adams would be rejecting the foundation the Crone is built on. But the Crone can't embrace Speter Adams because embracing is taking a side. The Switzerland meets the Archive. Neither can move. Both are exactly where they belong.

**The Dolls (Reframed):**
The spirits in the dolls are also neutral. They're the dead who didn't pick a side. The lost who belong nowhere. The in-between who never chose. Speter Adams gives them homes because Speter Adams understands what it is to need a home in every place because you can't claim just one. The dolls aren't a collection. They're a promise: you don't have to choose. You can be here. All of you can be here.
