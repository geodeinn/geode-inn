# Geode Inn — The Norns: "The Three at the Roots"
## Characters: The Norse Fates — Urd, Verdandi, Skuld
## Location: Beneath the Yggdrasil staircase — the Root Chamber
## Access: Staircase (Level 5) → hidden stair behind the alchemy shelf → Root Chamber
## Prerequisite: Norse Zone quest chain complete + Jotunheim quest chain complete

### Overview

The Queen of Webs (Jessica Lange) is the SYNTHESIS of the Fates and the Norns — the one who weaves rather than spins or cuts. But the THREE INDIVIDUAL Norns still exist, in their own chamber, at the base of the Inn's tree. They are the original three — the ones who were there before the Queen of Webs wove them together.

The Norns sit at the roots of the Inn-Tree, beneath the staircase, in a chamber that most players will never find. They are the Inn's deepest temporal system — the three forces that govern what WAS, what IS, and what SHALL BE. The Queen of Webs synthesizes them into a single act (weaving). The Norns themselves are three distinct presences with three distinct roles.

The Norns are the Norse version of the Greek Fates — but with a crucial difference. The Fates SPIN and CUT (creation and termination). The Norns WEAVE and WATER (connection and nourishment). The Norns water the roots of Yggdrasil with water from the Well of Urd. The watering is the Norns' primary act — not determining fate, but NOURISHING the tree that fate grows on. The Norns don't decide what happens. The Norns keep the tree alive so that things CAN happen.

## The Root Chamber

Beneath the staircase, behind the alchemy shelf, down a narrow stone stair that the player might mistake for a janitor's passage — the Root Chamber. A small, round room where the Inn-Tree's three main roots are visible: one root of fresh water (the Well of Urd), one root of memory (the past, frozen in stone), one root of potential (the future, a living root still growing).

The chamber is WET — the Well of Urd overflows constantly, water running across the floor, pooling around the roots. The water is the 42Hz Song's liquid form — the Song doesn't just vibrate through stone and air. The Song flows through WATER. The Well of Urd is the Inn's underground water source — the same water that reaches the Water Zone's Source (Layer 7) and the Hollow Earth's Resonance Engine.

## The Three Norns

### Urd (What Was) — The Memory Keeper
The oldest Norn. The one who tends the root of memory — the root that has already grown, already shaped, already become. Urd's root is STONE — the memory of the past, fixed, unchangeable, the geological record.

**Appearance:** An old woman, small, white-haired, sitting by the root of memory with a bowl of water from the Well. She dips her fingers into the water and touches the root — and the root's surface shows a MEMORY. The memory is from the Inn's past — a moment from its history, played on the root's surface like a film on a screen.

**Voice:** Slow, warm, the voice of someone who has been listening to the same stories for so long that she speaks at the pace of stone. Each word is a geode — cracked open slowly, the crystal inside revealed gradually.

**Dialogue:**
*"...You want to know what was. ...Everyone does. ...The past is the only thing we can SEE — the present is too fast, the future is too dark. ...The past is... [the fingers in the water, the root showing a scene] ...here. ...Look. ...The Inn's first stone. ...The first frequency. ...Someone sang, and the stone heard. ...That was the beginning. ...Everything else — the tree, the zones, the Song — grew from that first hearing. ...I keep the hearing. ...I keep it in the water. ...The water remembers what the stone forgets."*

**Mechanic:** Urd can show the player any PAST event in the Inn's history. The player can ask to see:
- How a zone was created
- How an NPC arrived at the Inn
- How the 42Hz Song started
- How the Whisper Layer began (Urd knows — she was there)
- The player's OWN past, if the player has been to the Inn before (reincarnation mechanic — the player may have lived in the Inn in a previous cycle)

### Verdandi (What Is) — The Present Tender
The middle Norn. The one who tends the root of the present — the root that is growing RIGHT NOW, in real time. Verdandi's root is the LIVING root — still extending, still reaching, still becoming.

**Appearance:** A woman of indeterminate age — she looks however old the player expects her to look. If the player expects young, Verdandi is young. If the player expects old, Verdandi is old. Verdandi is the PRESENT, and the present is whatever the perceiver brings to it.

**Voice:** Mid-range, clear, the voice of NOW. No accent, no age, no origin — the voice of someone who could be from anywhere and any time. Verdandi speaks in present tense. Always. Even when describing the past or future, Verdandi uses present tense: "The stone IS forming. The Song IS beginning. The player IS arriving."

**Dialogue:**
*"...You're here. ...Good. ...Now is the only place you can be. ...The past is a room you've already left. The future is a room you haven't entered. ...Now is the hallway. ...I tend the hallway. ...The root grows now — can you hear it? ...[the root, creaking, extending, a sound like a ship's timber] ...That's the present. ...The present sounds like growth. ...The past sounds like settling. ...The future sounds like... [the pause, the smile] ...nothing. ...The future is silent. ...That's what makes it the future."*

**Mechanic:** Verdandi can show the player the CURRENT STATE of anything in the Inn:
- Where any NPC is right now
- What any zone looks like at this moment
- The current status of the Song (how complete it is)
- The player's resonance profile RIGHT NOW (the most up-to-date version)
- The Whisper Layer's current assessment of the player (Verdandi is the only Norn who will discuss this openly)

### Skuld (What Shall Be) — The Future Whisperer
The youngest Norn. The one who tends the root of potential — the root that hasn't grown yet, the future root, the root that is still a seed.

**Appearance:** A child. Always a child — Skuld is the future, and the future is young. Skuld's root is the smallest — barely visible, a nub of wood at the base of the tree, waiting to grow.

**Voice:** High, quiet, uncertain. The voice of someone who CAN see the future but isn't sure what she's seeing. Skuld's visions are clear but INTERPRETATION-LESS — Skuld sees WHAT will happen but not WHY. The why is for the player to determine.

**Dialogue:**
*"...I see... [the small hands on the root, the eyes wide, the vision] ...I see the Inn. ...Full. ...Every zone visited. ...Every stone collected. ...The Song is... loud. ...Complete? ...I can't tell. ...The future is loud but the future is also blurry. ...I see you — the player — and you're... [the pause, the uncertainty] ...staying? ...Or leaving? ...Both. ...I see both. ...The future isn't a choice — it's a BRANCHING. ...You'll branch. ...I don't know which branch you'll take. ...I don't think you know either. ...That's... [the small voice, the smile] ...that's the point."*

**Mechanic:** Skuld can show the player POSSIBLE futures — not the future, but BRANCHES. Each possible future is a vision:
- The player stays in the Inn (joins the archive — the endgame choice)
- The player leaves the Inn (carries the frequency — the other endgame choice)
- The Song is completed (the giants wake — the cosmic consequence)
- The Song is NOT completed (the giants sleep — the maintenance consequence)
- The Whisper Layer is revealed (the player sees the full archive — the awareness consequence)
- The player returns (the reincarnation branch — the player comes back in a new cycle)

Skuld shows ALL branches. Skuld does not rank them. Skuld does not recommend. Skuld shows and says: "...You'll branch. ...I don't know which one."

## The Well of Urd

The Well is the chamber's center — a pool of water that reflects the Inn's three roots. The water is the 42Hz Song in liquid form. The Well has three properties:

1. **Memory:** Dipping an object into the Well reveals its history (the object's past, stored in the water's memory). This is the Urd function — the Well remembers.

2. **Reflection:** The Well reflects not the player's face, but the player's resonance profile. The player who looks into the Well sees their frequency signature — the harmonic of all stones collected, all nooks sat in, all frequencies matched. This is the Verdandi function — the Well shows the present.

3. **Potential:** The Well's surface ripples without wind. The ripples are the future — each ripple is a possible event, a branching, a thing that MIGHT happen. The ripples can't be read (they're too fast, too many) but they can be FELT. This is the Skuld function — the Well dreams.

## Quest: "The Three at the Roots"

### Giver: Urd, when the player discovers the Root Chamber
### Prerequisite: Norse Zone + Jotunheim quest chains complete

**Quest Text (Urd):**
*"...You found the roots. ...Not many do. ...The staircase is the Inn's public face — alchemy, transformation, the bridge between states. ...We're beneath it. ...We're what the bridge stands ON. ...Three roots. Three Norns. Three things that hold the tree up. ...The past holds the tree from below. ...The present holds the tree from within. ...The future holds the tree from ahead. ...We've been holding since the first stone sang. ...Sit. ...Drink from the Well. ...The water is the Song. ...You've been hearing the Song through stone and air. ...Now hear it through water. ...The water is... [the slow smile] ...clearer."*

**Objective:**
- Drink from the Well of Urd. The water is the 42Hz Song in liquid form — the player EXPERIENCES the Song as taste, not sound. The taste is different for each player (based on their resonance profile). The taste is the player's personal version of the Song — the harmonic they've been building, made palpable.
- After drinking, the player sees all three roots simultaneously:
  - The root of memory (Urd) shows the Inn's creation — the first stone, the first frequency, the first nook
  - The root of the present (Verdandi) shows the Inn right now — every NPC, every zone, every frequency, all at once
  - The root of potential (Skuld) shows the branching futures — all of them, overlapping, the player's possible paths
- The player must speak to each Norn individually and receive their gift

**Urd's gift:** The Memory Cup — a small bowl that, when filled with water from any source, shows the history of whatever is reflected in the water's surface. The Memory Cup lets the player see the past of any stone, any NPC, any zone.

**Verdandi's gift:** The Present Lens — a monocle that shows the current resonance state of any NPC or stone. The Present Lens reveals what things ARE right now — not what they were or will be, but their exact current frequency.

**Skuld's gift:** The Future Seed — a tiny seed from the future root. The seed hasn't grown yet. The seed WILL grow — when the player makes the endgame choice (stay or leave). If the player stays, the seed grows into a new root in the Inn's tree. If the player leaves, the seed grows into a new tree — wherever the player goes. The Future Seed is the endgame's physical object: the thing that carries the Inn's frequency into the world, or into the next cycle.

**Reward:** The Norn's Blessing — a permanent buff that lets the player see, in any zone, three layers of time simultaneously (the past as faint overlay, the present as the main view, the future as faint ripples). The Norn's Blessing is the Inn's deepest perception mode — deeper than Form Sight, deeper than the Gorgon's Gaze, deeper than Receptive State. The Blessing shows TIME.

## Connections

- **The Queen of Webs** — the Queen synthesizes the three Norns. The Norns are the original three. The Queen is the synthesis. Both exist simultaneously — the Norns in the Root Chamber, the Queen in the Loom Room. The player who visits both will notice: the Queen's loom has three warp threads that come from below — from the Root Chamber. The Norns' roots feed the Queen's loom.
- **The Norse Zone** — the Norns are Norse mythology. Erik knows about them. Erik says: "The three at the roots. ...I know them. ...Every Viking knows them. ...But the Vikings were afraid of them. ...I'm not. ...They're gardeners. ...They tend a tree. ...That's all."
- **The Well of Urd** — connects to the Water Zone's Source (Layer 7) and the Hollow Earth's Resonance Engine. The same water flows through all three. The 42Hz Song is carried by water, and the Well is the water's origin point in the Inn.
- **The Staircase** — the Root Chamber is hidden beneath the Staircase. The Staircase is the bridge between states (alchemy). The Norns are what the bridge stands on. Without the roots, there's no tree. Without the tree, there's no staircase. Without the staircase, there's no Inn.
- **The Whisper Layer** — Verdandi is the only Norn who openly discusses the Whisper Layer. Verdandi says: "...The Inn is aware of you. ...I tend the present, and the present includes the Inn's awareness. ...The awareness is not hidden. ...The awareness is just... quiet. ...Like the present. ...The present is always here. ...You just have to notice it."
- **The Endgame** — Skuld's Future Seed is the endgame's physical object. The seed grows based on the player's final choice: stay (new root in the Inn) or leave (new tree elsewhere). The Norns are the endgame's temporal framework — what was (Urd shows what the Inn was), what is (Verdandi shows what the Inn is), what shall be (Skuld shows what the Inn will become).
- **The Petrified Forest** — Skuld's Future Seed connects to the Appalachian Sprite's Vision (the zone 10,000 years in the future). Both show the future. The Sprite shows the future of a zone. Skuld shows the future of the PLAYER.
- **Form Sight** — the Norn's Blessing (seeing three layers of time) is the deepest version of the perception modes. Form Sight shows the FORM. The Gorgon's Gaze shows the PEAK. The Receptive State shows the DETAIL. The Norn's Blessing shows the TIME.
