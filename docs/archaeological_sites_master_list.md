## Detailed Site Analysis & Contextual Notes

### Deep Dive: US Catacombs & Mounds (Downstairs Level)
As detailed in `geode-inn/docs/geode_inn_catacomb_sites.md`, the Geode Inn maps historic pre-1850 American subterranean and earthwork sites to the **Downstairs (Catacombs)** level:
1. **Old St. Patrick's Cathedral Catacombs (NYC):** Sealed 1800s family vaults beneath Little Italy; Catholic catacomb architecture in America.
2. **Westminster Hall Catacombs (Baltimore, MD):** Built above graves on brick piers in 1786/1852; final resting place of Edgar Allan Poe.
3. **Indianapolis Catacombs (Tomlinson Hall):** Romanesque brick-arched ruins surviving the 1958 fire beneath City Market.
4. **St. Louis Cemetery No. 1 (New Orleans, LA):** Spanish colonial above-ground tomb city (1789 mandate due to water table); tomb of Marie Laveau.
5. **Unitarian Church Cemetery (Charleston, SC):** Overgrown Gothic decay (1772) associated with Poe's "Annabel Lee".
6. **Philadelphia Quaker Burial Grounds:** Unadorned fieldstone markers reflecting Quaker equality in death.
7. **Cahokia Mounds (Collinsville, IL):** Monks Mound pre-Columbian city (c. 600–1400 CE); larger than contemporary London.
8. **Watson Brake Mounds (Monroe, LA):** 11 earthen mounds predating Egyptian pyramids (c. 3500 BCE) built by hunter-gatherers.
9. **Poverty Point Earthworks (LA):** UNESCO site with 345-acre concentric geometric ridges (c. 1700–1100 BCE).
*Note:* The **Smithsonian Suppression Narrative** (mound-builder myths and giant skeleton reports) is housed in the **Main Floor — Modern Legends Nook** as folklore on institutional history.
