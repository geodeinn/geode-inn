# Geode Inn — Norrath-Style Zone-Quest System
## Template: Champions of Norrath / Return to Arms → The Geode Inn

## Overview

This document maps the structural template of Champions of Norrath and its sequel Return to Arms onto four priority cultural regions: Egypt, Vikings (Norse Fjords), Peru (Andean Highlands), and Celtic (Sacred Grove). Each zone is a self-contained chapter with its own biome, quest arc, boss encounter, embedded arcade activity, and stone rewards. Zones connect through a world map hub and a shared reputation system.

## The Norrath Template — Adapted

### What We Keep from Norrath
- **Zone-based world traversal** — each region is a chapter with a hub, spokes, and a finale
- **Class-based co-op** — up to 4 players, each class has zone-specific utility
- **Faction/reputation branching** — choices in a zone affect which Fae Court favors you
- **Loot = stones** — boss drops and zone rewards are the Inn's stone collection
- **Boss encounters per zone** — mythological bosses from the established 60-character roster
- **Embedded activities** — arcade games exist *inside* zones, not as separate menu items

### What We Change
- **Pacing** — exploration-heavy, not combat-heavy. Combat exists but isn't the primary loop
- **Death** — you don't die. You forget. No game-over screens, no restarts
- **Difficulty** — non-assertive. The Inn stays warm. Tension comes from environment and puzzles, not punishment
- **Aesthetic** — cozy gothic alchemical, not high fantasy medieval
- **Narrative** — the Silica Thread runs underneath every zone. Each quest subtly reinforces the larger themes without hitting you over the head

## The Class System — Zone Roles

### The Six Classes and Their Norrath Equivalents

| Geode Inn Class | Norrath Equivalent | Core Role |
|---|---|---|
| Artisan Thief | Rogue | Precision tasks, crafting, lockpicking. Can't talk their way out of anything. |
| Bard Blowhard (Dwarf) | Bard | Debuff specialist. Enrage enemies to make them dangerous but stupid. Chaos agent. |
| Tinkering Weeaboo | Wizard/Caster | Puzzle solver. Hyperfocused — godlike in their affinity zone, mediocre elsewhere. |
| Librarian Crone | Enchanter/Illusionist | Archive access. Highest intelligence. Reads every text. Contextually moral. |
| Chameleon Druid | Shapeshifter | Versatility. Adapts to any biome. Low endurance — the glioblastoma mirror. |
| The Sentinel (River) | Tank/Warrior | Iron Stance. Faceted defense. The answer to the Owl's cosmic riddles. |

### Co-Op Design
- Up to 4 players per party (like Norrath)
- Each class has **zone-specific utility** — one class shines in each zone while others support
- Class combinations create emergent gameplay (Bard taunts a boss → Artisan Thief picks the lock while it's enraged → Tinker solves the puzzle behind the door → Crone reads the inscription)
- Solo play is always viable — the zone scales and provides NPC companions (like Norrath's mercenary system)

## Zone 1: Egypt — "The Sistrum and the Star"

### Hub: The Temple Courtyard
A sunlit courtyard carved into the catacombs beneath the Inn. The hub is Bastet's domain — warm, golden, cat-populated. The courtyard connects to four spokes:
1. The Cat Gardens (Bastet's Herding game)
2. The Weighing Chamber (Anubis puzzle)
3. The Hieroglyph Library (archive access)
4. The Sirius Passage (zone finale)

### Quest Arc (5 quests, Norrath chapter structure)

**Quest 1: "The Sistrum's Call" (Intro)**
- **Giver:** Bastet
- **Objective:** Find the sistrum, learn to use it, herd 3 cats back to the golden basket
- **Class Roles:**
  - Artisan Thief: Best at the herding (nimble fingers, precision)
  - Bard: Can taunt the most stubborn cat into moving (enrage = the cat gets annoyed enough to walk)
  - Tinker: Figures out the sistrum's frequency puzzle fastest (situational alpha)
  - Crone: Reads the hieroglyphs on the sistrum — access to deeper lore
  - Druid: Shapeshifts into a cat to lure other cats back (most efficient herding method)
  - Sentinel: Uses Iron Stance to create a calm zone — cats relax and follow her
- **Reward:** Cat's Eye Chrysoberyl, 50 reputation with Bastet's Temple

**Quest 2: "The Seven Cats" (Exploration)**
- **Objective:** Find and herd all 7 cats, each in a different temple area, each with a unique personality and mechanic
- **Class Roles expand:** each cat responds best to a different class approach
  - The Aloof Cat → Sentinel (calm presence)
  - The Playful Cat → Bard (chaos energy matches)
  - The Sleeping Cat → Druid (shapeshifts to match, curls up next to it)
  - The Clever Cat → Tinker (puzzle: the cat wants a specific object found first)
  - The Feral Cat → Artisan Thief (precision: must approach slowly, no sudden moves)
  - The Ancient Cat → Crone (can read the cat's true name on the wall — calling it by name calms it)
  - The Lost Cat → Any class (it's in the Sirius Passage — a preview of the finale)
- **Reward:** 7 unique cat tokens, 100 reputation

**Quest 3: "The Weighing of Understanding" (Puzzle Boss)**
- **Giver:** Anubis (shifts to Full Presence)
- **Objective:** Answer 7 questions about Egyptian culture based on what the cats taught you. Not a quiz — a comprehension test. Anubis weighs your understanding against the feather of Ma'at.
- **Boss Mechanic:** Ammit sits beneath the scales. If your understanding is insufficient, she gets closer. She doesn't attack — she waits. The tension is the scale tipping, not a fight.
- **Class Roles:**
  - Crone: Can access the archive during the test (Living Book system — but each lookup costs time, and the scales are watching)
  - Tinker: Identifies patterns in the questions (situational alpha — this is their zone to shine)
  - Bard: Knows the facts (broad intelligence) but struggles under pressure (low situational intelligence — the timer makes them fumble)
  - Artisan Thief: Can't charm Anubis (low charisma) but can read the hieroglyphs on the scales themselves — finding the answer etched into the equipment
  - Druid: Shapeshifts to match each question's context (becomes a falcon to understand sky, a cat to understand home, etc.)
  - Sentinel: Holds steady under pressure. The scales don't rattle her. She answers slowly, deliberately.
- **Reward:** Ankh Stone, 150 reputation, passage to the Sirius Passage opens

**Quest 4: "The Flood" (Zone Finale)**
- **Trigger:** Sirius rises. The Nile comes.
- **Objective:** A Remembrance Vision plays — the player experiences the annual flood through the eyes of an ancient Egyptian. They must navigate the rising water, reading the stars and the river together.
- **Class Roles:**
  - All classes must work together — the flood requires every skill set simultaneously
  - Sentinel holds the line (literally — she anchors the boat while others work)
  - Tinker calibrates the nilometer (water level measurement)
  - Crone reads the Sirius alignment
  - Bard keeps spirits up (the only time his charisma sustain matters — during a crisis)
  - Artisan Thief repairs the irrigation channels (nimble fingers)
  - Druid becomes a heron to see the flood path from above
- **Final Boss: Ammit (Full Presence)**
  - Not a fight. A choice. Ammit asks: "Will you be weighed?" 
  - The player decides whether to submit to the weighing or carry the knowledge without judgment
  - Either path is valid — this is the faction branch. Submitting gains Seelie favor (humility). Refusing gains Unseelie favor (autonomy).
- **Reward:** Nile Jasper, passage to Hel (Nine Realms gateway), 200 reputation, zone complete

## Zone 2: Norse Fjords — "The Sunstone and the Ninefold Frost"

### Hub: Winterhaven Harbor (Skálavík)
A cozy timber longhouse harbor village deep inside a dramatic fjord. Snow-blankled roofs, fish-drying racks, crackling hearths. The hub connects to:
1. Longship Sound (navigation)
2. The Runic Frost-Peaks (mining/crafting)
3. The Whispering Grove of Yggdrasil (cosmology connection)
4. Frost-Giant Pass (zone finale)

### Quest Arc (5 quests)

**Quest 1: "Navigating the Mist Fjord" (Intro)**
- **Giver:** Einar the Wayfinder
- **Objective:** Use a Norwegian Sunstone (polarizing crystal) to navigate through sea fog to 3 waypoints
- **Class Roles:**
  - Tinker: Natural affinity — the sunstone puzzle is physics-based (situational alpha)
  - Sentinel: Steadies the ship in rough water (Iron Stance = anchor)
  - Bard: Keeps crew morale up during the fog (temporary charisma burst)
  - Artisan Thief: Repairs the rigging (nimble fingers)
  - Crone: Reads the runic buoy markers
  - Druid: Shapeshifts to a seal to scout ahead
- **Reward:** Norwegian Sunstone (Iolite), 50 reputation with Skálavík

**Quest 2: "The Runic Frost-Peaks" (Exploration)**
- **Giver:** Astrid Runic-Weaver
- **Objective:** Climb to the peak, mine raw Larvikite and Thulite, learn to carve runes
- **Class Roles:**
  - Artisan Thief: Best at the carving (precision work)
  - Tinker: Figures out the rune patterns (puzzle)
  - Crone: Reads the ancient rune inscriptions (archive access)
  - Druid: Resists the cold through adaptation (shapeshift to arctic fox)
  - Bard: Can't carve runes (low situational intelligence) but can identify the right vein by ear (broad intelligence — "this sounds like the one Grandpa mentioned")
  - Sentinel: Carries the heaviest loads up the mountain (Iron Stance = endurance)
- **Reward:** Larvikite, Thulite, 100 reputation, first rune carved

**Quest 3: "The Whispering Grove" (Bridge Quest)**
- **Giver:** Sigrid (Freyja's Emissary)
- **Objective:** Enter the Yggdrasil root system — this zone connects directly to the Inn's cosmology level upstairs. The roots form a maze. Huginn and Muninn (in Wanderer form — chibi thieves) steal your items and hide them in caches.
- **Class Roles:**
  - Bard: Can taunt the ravens into returning items (enrage = they get flustered and drop things)
  - Artisan Thief: Can pick the ravens' cache boxes
  - Tinker: Maps the root maze (situational alpha)
  - Crone: Reads the root inscriptions (the roots have memories — Living Book access)
  - Druid: Shapeshifts to navigate narrow root passages
  - Sentinel: The ravens respect her — they don't steal from the Sentinel. (Her presence aura intimidates them.)
- **Reward:** Yggdrasil Root Stone, passage to Cosmology level unlocked, 150 reputation

**Quest 4: "Frost-Giant Pass" (Boss Encounter)**
- **Giver:** The Wanderer (Odin, approachable form, by the longhouse fire)
- **Objective:** Cross the Frost-Giant Pass to reach Jotunheim. The jötnar are not enemies — they are perspective.
- **Boss: Vafthrudnir the Riddle Giant (Full Presence)**
  - Not a combat boss. A riddle contest. Vafthrudnir has challenged gods. He asks questions that test wisdom, not knowledge.
  - The Sentinel can answer the cosmic riddles through intuition — love, loyalty, knowledge. If she lowers her defensive layers, she can answer the one the Owl would answer "42" to.
  - The Bard can taunt Vafthrudnir (enrage = he gets louder and less precise, making his riddles easier)
  - The Crone can use archive access to research answers — but Vafthrudnir notices and respects the effort, not the shortcut
- **Faction Branch:**
  - Answer all riddles correctly → Seelie favor (wisdom path)
  - Refuse the riddles and offer a trade instead → Unseelie favor (cunning path)
  - Answer with "42" to the cosmic question → the Wanderer laughs. No faction favor. Just respect. (The Owl blinks.)
- **Reward:** Frost Giant's Wisdom Stone, passage to Jotunheim, 200 reputation, zone complete

## Zone 3: Peru — "The Andean Highlands"

### Hub: Tambo del Apu (The Refuge of the Mountain Spirit)
A stone refuge hut high in the Andes, built into a cliff face. Warm interior, guanaco wool blankets, a small fire. The hub connects to:
1. The Lower Terraces (agriculture/foraging)
2. The Apu Ausangate Snow Peak (summit challenge)
3. The Chasqui Trail (messenger run — Andean Run arcade game)
4. The Mine of El Muki (zone finale)

### Quest Arc (5 quests)

**Quest 1: "The Altitude" (Intro)**
- **Giver:** Killa the Viscacha (companion creature — not a speaking NPC, but a guide)
- **Objective:** Ascend from the lower terraces to Tambo del Apu. The altitude is a real mechanic — movement slows, vision narrows, the player must stop and breathe (coca leaf mechanic — chewing coca leaves restores stamina, historically accurate).
- **Class Roles:**
  - Sentinel: Most affected by altitude (Iron Stance doesn't help when there's no air) — must stop most often
  - Druid: Least affected (shapeshifts to a viscacha — high-altitude adaptation)
  - Tinker: Calculates the optimal pace (situational alpha — physics/biology)
  - Bard: High agility helps on the steep paths
  - Artisan Thief: Nimble on the narrow terrace edges
  - Crone: Reads the trail markers (Quechua inscriptions — archive access)
- **Reward:** Andean Opal (Peruvian Blue Opal), 50 reputation with the Apu spirits

**Quest 2: "The Chasqui Trail" (Exploration + Arcade)**
- **Giver:** A Chasqui (Inca messenger) NPC at the trailhead
- **Objective:** Run the Chasqui Trail — this IS the Andean Run arcade game, embedded as a story activity. Temple Run mechanics on an Inca road system. The player must deliver a message to the next tambo before sunset.
- **Embedded Arcade Game:** Andean Run
  - After completing the story version, it unlocks as a repeatable score challenge
  - Submits to Leaderboard as `gameId: "andean_run"`
- **Class Roles:**
  - Bard: High agility = fastest runner (but crashes if he doesn't pace — temporary bursts)
  - Artisan Thief: Best at the obstacle precision sections (nimble fingers for rope bridges)
  - Tinker: Identifies shortcuts (situational alpha — pattern recognition on the trail)
  - Crone: Reads the quipu (knot records) at each checkpoint — access to bonus lore
  - Druid: Shapeshifts to a condor for aerial shortcuts (limited duration)
  - Sentinel: Slowest runner but never falls (Iron Stance = perfect balance on narrow paths)
- **Reward:** Peruvianite, 100 reputation, Andean Run unlocked

**Quest 3: "The Offering to Apu" (Puzzle)**
- **Giver:** An Apu (mountain spirit — Full Presence, emerges from the peak itself)
- **Objective:** Create an offering that honors the mountain. The player must gather materials (coca leaves, corn beer/chicha, a carved stone) and perform the offering ritual at the summit.
- **Class Roles:**
  - Artisan Thief: Carves the offering stone (precision)
  - Bard: Sings the offering song (charisma burst — the only class that can perform the ritual song)
  - Tinker: Calculates the solar alignment for the offering (situational alpha — archaeoastronomy)
  - Crone: Knows the ritual from the archive (but must adapt it to context — situational ethics)
  - Druid: Shapeshifts to a condor to reach the summit
  - Sentinel: Carries the offering up (Iron Stance — the weight doesn't faze her)
- **Reward:** Apu's Blessing Stone, 150 reputation, passage to El Muki's mine opens

**Quest 4: "The Mine of El Muki" (Boss Encounter)**
- **Giver:** A Quechua miner NPC at the mine entrance
- **Objective:** Enter the mine to find the rarest stones. El Muki guards the deepest vein.
- **Boss: El Muki (Chibi → Full Presence)**
  - El Muki is a creature from Andean folklore — a small, powerful being who guards mineral wealth. He lures miners deeper with lights and sounds, then causes cave-ins if they're greedy.
  - Not a combat boss. A negotiation and self-control test. El Muki offers you more stones than you need. Taking too much triggers a cave-in (not lethal — you get pushed back to the entrance and "forget" some progress).
  - The trick: take only what you need. The Inn's philosophy of non-greed.
- **Class Roles:**
  - Sentinel: Resists El Muki's lure (Iron Stance = willpower)
  - Bard: Can taunt El Muki (enrage = he gets reckless and reveals the true vein)
  - Tinker: Identifies which stones are real and which are illusions (situational alpha)
  - Artisan Thief: Can sense the cave-in before it happens (nimble fingers feel the vibrations)
  - Crone: Reads the warning inscriptions (archive access — "the miners who took too much")
  - Druid: Shapeshifts to a bat to scout the mine's layout
- **Faction Branch:**
  - Take only what you need → Seelie favor (restraint path)
  - Take extra but share them with the village → Seelie favor (generosity path)
  - Take everything and escape before the cave-in → Unseelie favor (cunning path, but you lose reputation with Apu)
- **Reward:** Peruvian Pink Opal (the mine's rarest stone), 200 reputation, zone complete

## Zone 4: Celtic Sacred Grove — "The Stone Circle"

### Hub: The Sacred Grove
A forest clearing with a ring of empty holes where stones once stood. An ancient oak tree at the center. The hub connects to:
1. The Foundation Markers (exploration)
2. The Ogham Stones (language puzzle)
3. The Star Alignment Hill (archaeoastronomy)
4. The Fae Portal Chamber (zone finale — unlocks the Fae Kingdom)

### Quest Arc (7 quests — the longest chain, this is the entry point to the Fae Kingdom)

**Quest 1: "The Empty Circle" (Intro)**
- **Giver:** Elder Cian
- **Objective:** Find 3 foundation markers in the grove. Listen to the earth at each.
- **Class Roles:**
  - Crone: Reads the grove's archive (oldest texts in the Inn — pre-Ogham)
  - Druid: Feels the earth's hum through shapeshifting (becomes a tree root)
  - Sentinel: Stands at each marker and feels the vibration (Iron Stance = grounding)
  - Artisan Thief: Finds the hidden markers (precision exploration)
  - Bard: Knows the folklore (broad intelligence — "Grandpa said the stones were taken for walls")
  - Tinker: Maps the triangular alignment (situational alpha — geometry)
- **Reward:** 50 reputation with Druidic Circle, "Grove Walker" title

**Quest 2: "The Language of Trees" (Puzzle)**
- **Giver:** Taliesin the Ogham scholar
- **Objective:** Learn 5 Ogham letters (Beith/Birch, Luis/Rowan, Fearn/Alder, Saille/Willow, Nion/Ash). Each letter is a tree. Each tree is a sound. Each sound is a power.
- **Class Roles:**
  - Crone: Master of the Ogham (highest intelligence — archive access to the oldest scripts)
  - Tinker: Identifies the tree species (situational alpha — botany)
  - Artisan Thief: Carves the Ogham letters into stones (precision)
  - Druid: Communicates with the trees themselves (shapeshift = the tree tells you its name)
  - Bard: Can sing the Ogham (charisma burst — the letters are meant to be spoken aloud)
  - Sentinel: Stands guard while others learn (the grove is not always safe)
- **Reward:** 5 Ogham Stones, 100 reputation

**Quest 3: "Raising the Stones" (Crafting)**
- **Giver:** Branwen the stone mason
- **Objective:** Quarry, transport, and raise 3 stones into the circle. Each stone must match its foundation marker.
- **Class Roles:**
  - Sentinel: Lifts the stones (Iron Stance — raw strength and balance)
  - Artisan Thief: Carves the stones to match (precision)
  - Druid: Asks the earth to release the stones (shapeshift = becomes root to loosen the soil)
  - Tinker: Engineers the lever system (situational alpha — physics)
  - Bard: Keeps the work crew energized (charisma bursts — work songs)
  - Crone: Reads the matching inscriptions (archive — which stone goes where)
- **Reward:** 3 Standing Stones placed, 150 reputation

**Quest 4: "The Star Alignment" (Archaeoastronomy)**
- **Giver:** Arianrhod the astronomer
- **Objective:** Observe the stars from the hill, align the stones to specific celestial events. The stones must catch the light of specific stars on specific dates.
- **Class Roles:**
  - Tinker: Calculates the alignments (situational alpha — astronomy)
  - Crone: Reads the ancient star charts (archive access)
  - Sentinel: Holds the alignment tool steady (Iron Stance = perfect stillness)
  - Druid: Shapeshifts to an owl to see from the stars' perspective (the Owl connection — "wherever an owl is sighted, one of us is near")
  - Bard: Knows the star stories (broad intelligence — oral tradition)
  - Artisan Thief: Adjusts the stones by millimeters (precision)
- **Reward:** Star-Aligned Stone, 200 reputation, the circle begins to hum

**Quest 5: "The Chibi Fae" (Faction Choice)**
- **Trigger:** The stones are aligned. The circle hums. Small Fae appear.
- **Objective:** The Chibi Fae offer a choice: align the circle to the Seelie Court (Summer) or the Unseelie Court (Winter). This is the zone's faction branch.
- **Faction Branch:**
  - Seelie alignment → golden light, warm breeze, flower bloom. The portal opens to the Summerlands.
  - Unseelie alignment → silver frost, cold wind, ice crystals. The portal opens to The Hollow.
  - **Both paths lead to the Fae Kingdom** — the choice determines which court favors you and which quests are available there.
- **Class Roles:**
  - Bard: Can influence the Fae (charisma burst — persuade them toward either court)
  - Sentinel: The Fae respect her (presence aura — they ask her opinion first)
  - Druid: Can sense which alignment is "natural" for this grove (shapeshift = reads the earth's preference)
  - Crone: Knows the historical consequences of each alignment (situational ethics — she adapts her advice to context)
  - Artisan Thief: Can sabotage the alignment (steal a crystal to shift the balance — but this has consequences)
  - Tinker: Can fine-tune the alignment (situational alpha — resonance engineering)
- **Reward:** Seelie or Unseelie Token, 250 reputation, portal activated

**Quest 6: "The Portal Ignites" (Finale)**
- **Objective:** The stone circle activates. A portal opens. The player steps through.
- **Boss: The Threshold Guardian**
  - Not a creature. A test. The portal requires a memory — the player must offer a memory to pass through.
  - The Crone can choose which memory (she has the most). The Artisan Thief can "steal back" a memory they've forgotten. The Sentinel offers her most precious memory without hesitation — and the portal accepts it, because the Sentinel's strength is that she holds nothing back.
  - The Owl appears on the portal's rim. Watches. Blinks.
- **Reward:** Preseli Blue Stone (the stone of Stonehenge itself), 300 reputation, Fae Kingdom unlocked, zone complete

**Quest 7 (Optional): "The Oak's Secret"**
- **Giver:** The Oak (ancient tree spirit — Ambulatory, like Cthulhu)
- **Objective:** The Oak has been watching since before the circle was built. It offers a hidden quest for players who return to the grove after the Fae Kingdom.
- **Reward:** Heartwood Stone, a unique Living Book entry (the Oak's memories — the oldest text in the Inn)

## The World Map Connection

### Zone Progression (Norrath-Style)
Unlike Norrath's linear chapter progression, the Geode Inn's zones are **open but gated**:
1. **Celtic Sacred Grove** is the recommended starting zone (unlocks Fae Kingdom)
2. After completing any zone, the world map expands to show adjacent zones
3. **Egypt** connects to Celtic through the catacombs
4. **Norse Fjords** connects to Celtic through the Whispering Grove (Yggdrasil roots)
5. **Peru** connects to all three through the world map (it's across the ocean — reached by the Viking longship after the Norse zone)
6. Each zone completion awards a **World Map Shard** — collecting all four reveals a hidden fifth zone

### Reputation System
- Each zone has its own reputation track (Bastet's Temple, Skálavík, Apu Spirits, Druidic Circle)
- The Fae Court reputation (Seelie/Unseelie) is shared across all zones — your faction choices in each zone affect which Fae Court favors you globally
- High reputation unlocks: zone-specific cosmetic items, additional quests, NPC housing (you can build a home in the zone), and rare stone variants
- Low reputation doesn't lock you out — it just means the NPCs are less warm. No hostility. Just distance. (The Inn is never punitive.)

### Co-Op Integration
- Players can join each other's zones at any time (like Norrath's drop-in co-op)
- Class combinations in co-op create emergent strategies:
  - Bard + Sentinel: The Bard taunts the boss, the Sentinel holds the line while it's enraged
  - Tinker + Crone: The Tinker solves the puzzle, the Crone reads the instructions in real-time
  - Artisan Thief + Druid: The Thief picks the lock, the Druid shapeshifts to scout what's behind it
  - Full party (4 players): Each class handles a different spoke of the zone simultaneously — the fastest completion requires coordination
- Solo players get an NPC companion (a Canopy Elf for platforming, a Chibi Fae for puzzles, etc.) — scaled to fill the missing class role
