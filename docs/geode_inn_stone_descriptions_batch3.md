# Geode Inn — Missing Stone Descriptions
## Batch 3: The Chalcedony Cousins & Color-Shifters (8 stones)
## Drafted 2026-09-04 — in the Inn's voice, Krista-approved style

---

### 1. Green Onyx
**Mind:** Green onyx is composure, layered. Onyx forms in strict parallel bands — growth laid down one disciplined season at a time — and the green version carries that structure with the heart's own color: steady, deliberate, no wasted motion. It is the stone of the person who feels everything and shows what they choose. Not suppression. Curation.
**Body:** Supports the heart and the body's quiet steadiness — the strength that doesn't announce itself before it acts.

### 2. Lemurian Blue Calcite (Blue Onyx)
**Mind:** Lemurian blue calcite is the drowned library — soft blue banded like light through deep water, holding a story about knowledge that sank before it was lost, and waited. The Inn recognizes its own in this stone: some things are not destroyed by the flood, only filed beneath it, patient, still legible to whoever dives. For memory that feels out of reach, it suggests the obvious and impossible: go deeper.
**Body:** Supports calm sleep and gentle emotional release — the body's slow rising, like things returning to the surface at their own speed.

### 3. Gem Silica
**Mind:** Gem silica is the sky poured into chalcedony and allowed to set — the most vivid blue-green the quietest stone family ever produced, so saturated it looks borrowed from somewhere else. It is the chalcedony family's masterpiece, made of the same humble silica as agate and onyx, simply taken further than anyone thought silica could go. The lesson of hidden potential in ordinary material — the Inn's favorite theme in its brightest case.
**Body:** Supports the throat and clear communication — the voice at its highest grade, saying the ordinary thing in a way no one can forget.

### 4. Hackmanite
**Mind:** Hackmanite keeps the dark's schedule. Left in shadow, it deepens to violet; exposed to sun, it fades to pale — a stone that literally remembers where it has been by changing color to match. Most stones resist their circumstances. Hackmanite records them, honestly, and reverses them just as honestly when the light changes back. It teaches the difference between fading and forgetting: this stone fades in daylight and never forgets the dark. One exposure and it will always be able to find its way back.
**Body:** Supports the skin's own light-response cycle and restorative sleep — the body's rhythm of bright days and deep dark.

### 5. Kammererite
**Mind:** Kammererite is the glimpse — lavender into violet into a purple so deep it edges toward ultraviolet, a color the eye reaches for and never quite lands on. It is the rarest of the bridge stones: not a door, not yet a destination, but the light you see through the keyhole. It grants no passage. It grants certainty that the passage exists, which is often enough to keep a traveler moving.
**Body:** Supports the crown and the deep stillness of meditation — the body's doorway state, held open just a crack.

### 6. Hypersthene
**Mind:** Hypersthene is the stone you interrogate. Hold it under light and its velvet bronze sheen swallows the glare and gives back a shimmer with no source — as if the answer was already inside, waiting to be asked properly. The old texts called it the stone of solutions, and the Inn has found this to be literally true: it does not solve the problem. It makes the problem suddenly legible, which is rarer.
**Body:** Supports the blood and the body's iron reserves — the mineral patience that keeps its own counsel until consulted.

### 7. Kyanite (orange)
**Mind:** Blue kyanite is famous for alignment — the stone that never needs cleansing, true at any frequency. Orange kyanite is its grounded sibling, and it asks the overlooked question: aligned to WHAT? Not the sky. The root. It is the stone of embodied purpose — conviction with dirt on its hands, ideals that show up on time. Alignment is not a posture. It is where you put your weight.
**Body:** Supports the lower body, hips, and the root's endurance — the foundation that carries the vision.

### 8. Kyanite (Moss)
**Mind:** Moss kyanite is the bridge grown over — the same strict blue blade of a stone, but green, softened, as if the alignment had been left standing long enough for life to climb it. It connects the head's architecture to the forest floor's patience: clear thinking that has made peace with being a creature. The blade and the moss, both honest, sharing one stone.
**Body:** Supports the heart and the body's connection to natural rhythm — the settling of a system that remembers it is part biology.
