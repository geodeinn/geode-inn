### Overview

The Einherjar in the Geode Inn are not generic pop-culture Viking warriors. They are the Inn's **ENDGAME SHADOW INN RAID TEAM** — the immortal spirits of legendary champions who, upon death, refused total dissolution and chose instead to remain within the Inn's living timber. They reside in Valhalla, a grand high-ceilinged chamber situated on the Cosmology Level (Upstairs / Asgard), anchored beside the Observatory, the Planet Chamber, the Cosmology Mural, the Ascension Canopy, and the Void.

Where casual travelers pass through the Inn across cycles, the Einherjar are the permanent combat vanguard of the 84Hz Shadow Inn. When dissonance surges through raid instances, deep dungeons, and combat arenas, these eight warriors stand ready to be recruited into the player's raid roster. They are bound by a foundational cosmology law:

> *"The both are: the adequate — the dead and the staying, the same warrior, different state."*

The Einherjar do not fear defeat because mortality is already behind them. In the 84Hz combat frequency, an Einherjar cannot be killed; when depleted in battle, an Einherjar dissolves into golden resonance, returns to Valhalla to rest beside the hearth, and can be summoned back into combat once the cooldown elapses.

The Geode Inn operates across a structured frequency progression that mirrors the player's journey from novice guest to master raid leader. This progression spans three distinct modes of reality, unlocking layers of the Inn's tree architecture over time:

### The Three Modes Defined

1. **42Hz (Living Inn Mode):** The foundation of the Inn. In 42Hz, the environment is peaceful, narrative-driven, and therapeutic. Frequencies are deployed to tune dissonant objects, match mineral charm resonances, and uncover secrets. The living timber hums at 42Hz—the cosmic background frequency of life.
2. **In-Between Mode:** The transitional state between physical reality and combat resonance. In this spectral mode, the player perceives the ghosts of former cycles, uncovers hidden doorways in the Cosmology Mural, and communicates directly with the unmanifested spirits of Valhalla.
3. **84Hz (Shadow Inn Mode):** The octave shift into active combat. In 84Hz, the peaceful architecture of the Inn weaponizes. Mineral charms become offensive spells, frequency matching becomes precision striking, and the Inn's structural harmonics convert into instanced dungeons, competitive arenas, and raid encounters.

## 5. The Einherjar Defeat Mechanic ("Defeat is Rest")

A foundational rule sets the Einherjar apart from all other NPCs in the Geode Inn:

**The Einherjar cannot die in combat because they are already dead.**

Having already passed through physical mortality in previous cycles, their spirits exist as permanent 84Hz resonance patterns embedded in the Inn-Tree's memory lattice. They cannot experience physical death a second time.

```
[ COMBAT ENCOUNTER ]
       │
       ▼  (Einherjar HP reaches 0)
[ DISSOLUTION PHASE ]
       │  • Warrior dissolves into golden 84Hz ash
       │  • System Message: "The warrior's name is known; the warrior's rest is claimed."
       ▼
[ RETURN TO VALHALLA ]
       │  • Spirit teleports instantly back to Valhalla Hearth
       │  • Warrior enters 15-minute Recalibration Cooldown
       ▼
[ RE-SUMMONING READY ]
          • Cooldown expires
          • Warrior fully restored & ready for raid deployment
```

### The Defeat Protocol in Raids

1.  **HP Depletion:** When an Einherjar's health drops to 0 during a Shadow Inn raid boss encounter, the warrior does not collapse or play a death animation.
2.  **Golden Dissolution:** The warrior's armor and form fracture into glowing golden particles of 84Hz light. The golden particles dissolve into the air while a low 42Hz bell tone rings across the arena.
3.  **System Announcement:** The raid UI displays the notice:
