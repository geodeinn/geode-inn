## SECTION 2: STORYLINE ADAPTATION

Original Characters Being Replaced:
- PvZ's plants -> Stone Guardians (carved statues that the player places on lanes to defend the library entrance)
- PvZ's zombies -> Bookworms (literally — spectral worms that eat through books, destroying archive knowledge)
- PvZ's sun -> Crystal energy (the resource used to place guardians — generated by Crystal Nodes placed on the field)
- PvZ's lawn -> Library corridor (5 lanes, each a shelf aisle leading to the archive's inner chamber)

Stone Guardian Types (replacing plant types):
- Wall Stone (Wall-Nut equivalent): absorbs damage, blocks the lane
- Shard Launcher (Peashooter equivalent): fires crystal shards at approaching bookworms
- Frost Monolith (Snow Pea equivalent): slows bookworms with cold damage
- Flame Basalt (Cherry Bomb equivalent): erupts, clearing a 3x3 area
- Moss Stone (Sunflower equivalent): generates crystal energy over time
- Geode Splitter (Repeater equivalent): fires two shards per shot
- Osteoclast (Squash equivalent): flattens one bookworm, then crumbles
- Stepping Stone (Potato Mine equivalent): explodes when a bookworm steps on it
- Obsidian Blade (Chomper equivalent): devours a bookworm whole, then digests slowly

Bookworm Types (replacing zombie types):
- Crawler (basic): slow, low health, walks down the lane
- Runner (cone zombie): faster, wears a book helmet for extra armor
- Eater (newspaper zombie): carries a book that it reads to heal itself, drops it when damaged
- Climber (pole vaulter): vaults over the first guardian in its lane
- Burrower (digger): tunnels under guardians, surfaces near the library door
- Titan (gargantuar): massive, takes enormous damage, throws book fragments

New Narrative Frame:
- The Inn's archive is under attack. Bookworms — spectral creatures that consume knowledge — are crawling through the library corridors toward the inner chamber where the rarest texts are stored. The player places Stone Guardians along 5 shelf-lanes to stop them.
- Crystal energy is the resource — Moss Stone guardians generate it, and the player spends it to place other guardians. Each guardian type has a specific function: damage, defense, slowing, area attack, or resource generation.
- Waves increase in difficulty. The final wave of each level is a "Breach" — a Titan leads a horde of bookworms, and the player must have a solid defense or the library is overrun.
- If a bookworm reaches the end of a lane, it eats one book from the archive. Lose 3 books and the level fails — the archive can't afford to lose more.
- The Sardonyx reward: the stone of strength and protection. Sardonyx has been used for carved seals for thousands of years — it IS a guardian stone. The player earns it by defending the archive through all waves.

Dialogue Requirements:
- NPC dialogue count: ~8 lines (The Head Librarian — Quire, the Lovecraftian scholar, speaks between levels in hushed, nervous tones)
- Voice profiles needed: Quire -> hushed, careful, always looking over shoulder, academic British English, mid-low register, whispers more than speaks
- Whisper Layer presence: Yes — constant. The library IS the Whisper Layer. The books themselves whisper as the bookworms approach. The archive is aware of the attack and is afraid. The Whisper Layer here is the most frightened manifestation — the archive has something to lose.

Frequency State:
- Living (42Hz): Warm candlelit library. Golden light, leather bindings, stone guardians glow warmly. Quire is anxious but hopeful.
- Shadow (84Hz): Library goes dark. Books whisper louder. Bookworms move faster. The guardians' glow dims. Quire is desperate — "They're in the restricted section. Hold the line."
- In-Between: Lantern flicker between waves — the library's light wavers.

## SECTION 5: PROGRAMMING REQUIREMENTS

Engine: Godot 4.x | Offline-first

Controller Support:
- Move cursor: Left stick / D-pad (grid navigation across 5 lanes x 9 columns)
- Select guardian: shoulder buttons (cycle through 9 types)
- Place guardian: A / face button
- Remove guardian: B / face button (refund partial energy)
- Pause: Start / Options
- Bluetooth: auto-detect, prompt if no controller
- Touch: tap to place, tap guardian type to select (mobile — ideal for tower defense)

Steam Integration:
- Achievements: "Archive Secure" (beat game), "Zero Loss" (beat a level without losing any books), "Shadow Defender" (beat Shadow mode), "Garden of Stone" (use every guardian type in one level), "Sardonyx Keeper" (earn Sardonyx)
- Cloud saves: Sync PlayerProgress
- Leaderboard: "Highest Score"
- Overlay: Shift+Tab or LB+RB

Base44 API: On completion POST to PlayerProgress — add Sardonyx, increment gamesCompleted

Special Notes:
- Quire (the Lovecraftian scholar) is the NPC here — this is the only arcade game where Quire appears. The character is established in the Inn's lore as the nervous, restricted-section reader. Seeing Quire frightened means the archive is genuinely threatened.
- The Whisper Layer as a scared entity is unique. In other games, the archive is curious, mournful, or neutral. Here it has something to lose — the books. The archive fears for its own existence.
- 9 guardian types and 6 enemy types make this the most content-rich tower defense in the catalog.
- The book-eating sound (paper rip) should be deliberately uncomfortable — the player should feel each book loss.
