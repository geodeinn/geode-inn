## SECTION 2: STORYLINE ADAPTATION

Original Characters Being Replaced:
- The Goose (Untitled Goose Game) -> The Garden itself (the player controls the garden — plants, roots, flowers, and soil — not a character walking through it)
- The Villagers (Untitled Goose Game) -> The Foragers (groups of explorers entering the garden seeking rare poisonous plants for alchemical research)
- Items the goose steals -> Rare blooms the foragers are trying to harvest

New Narrative Frame:
- REVERSAL: In Untitled Goose Game, you are the nuisance pest bothering villagers. In Poison Garden, you are the GARDEN — a sentient, beautiful, deadly ecosystem. Foragers enter to harvest your rarest blooms. Your job is to stop them — not by attacking, but by being beautiful, deceptive, and lethal.
- The player controls the garden as a living system: grow vines to block paths, bloom flowers to release intoxicating perfume (stuns foragers), open blossoms to reveal false treasures (lure foragers into dead ends), drop toxic pollen clouds (slow foragers), and grow thorns along paths they'll take.
- The garden cannot kill foragers directly — it can only misdirect, delay, and exhaust them until they give up and leave. If a forager harvests a bloom, the garden loses a piece of itself. Too many harvests and the garden dies.
- The Antidote Orchid reward: the garden's final bloom — the one flower that can cure any poison, including the garden's own. It is earned when the player successfully protects the garden through all seasons (levels). The Orchid represents the garden's mastery: it can create poison AND its cure. It chooses to create both.

Dialogue Requirements:
- NPC dialogue count: ~15 lines (The Foragers — 3 types, 5 lines each as they react to the garden's tricks)
- Voice profiles needed:
  - Scholar Forager -> Oxford English, pedantic, increasingly frustrated
  - Soldier Forager -> American Southern, gruff, tactical, gets spooked
  - Healer Forager -> Indian English, gentle, perceptive, hardest to fool
- Whisper Layer presence: Yes — the garden whispers to itself. The player hears it as internal narration — the garden's own thoughts. "They come for the monkshood. Let them find the foxglove instead." The Whisper Layer here is the player's own voice, speaking as the garden.

Frequency State:
- Living (42Hz): The garden is lush, green, vibrant. Foragers are relaxed, curious. Plants grow quickly. Perfume travels far. This is the garden at its most beautiful — and its most deceptive.
- Shadow (84Hz): The garden goes to seed. Colors deepen to bruised purples and blacks. Plants grow slower but are more toxic. Foragers are more aggressive — they carry gloves and clippers. The garden must rely on its darkest blooms.
- In-Between: Seasonal shift — the garden transitions between bloom and seed. Visual: leaves turn, petals fall, colors change in a wave across the screen.

## SECTION 5: PROGRAMMING REQUIREMENTS

Engine: Godot 4.x | Offline-first

Controller Support:
- Cursor/selection: Left stick (move selection cursor across garden grid)
- Grow plant: A / face button (on selected tile)
- Bloom flower: hold A (on selected bloom)
- Release perfume/pollen: release A (on blooming flower)
- Grow thorns: B / face button (on selected path)
- Cut root (sacrifice): X / face button (permanently block a path by collapsing it)
- Pause: Start / Options
- Bluetooth: auto-detect, prompt if no controller
- Touch: tap to select, drag to grow (mobile)

Steam Integration:
- Achievements: "Garden Master" (beat all seasons), "Zero Harvest" (beat a level without any bloom taken), "Shadow Gardener" (beat all seasons in Shadow mode), "The Cure" (earn the Antidote Orchid), "Patient Gardener" (win a level using only perfume — no thorns, no pollen)
- Cloud saves: Sync PlayerProgress
- Leaderboard: "Most Protected Blooms" (total blooms saved across all runs)
- Overlay: Shift+Tab or LB+RB

Base44 API: On completion POST to PlayerProgress — add Antidote Orchid, increment gamesCompleted

Special Notes:
- This is the ONLY game in the catalog where the player is not a character — the player IS the environment. The garden is sentient. The player thinks AS the garden.
- The reversal of Untitled Goose Game is thematically precise: Goose Game = you are the pest. Poison Garden = you are the beautiful, deadly thing being harvested. The power dynamic is inverted.
- The Whisper Layer as internal narration (the garden's own thoughts) is unique. No other game uses the Whisper Layer as the player's voice.
- 528Hz (the Antidote Orchid) is the only use of this frequency in the entire arcade. It represents cure/transformation. Paired with 111Hz (Alchemical Staircase's Stone), the Inn uses sacred frequencies only at its highest moments.
- The Healer Forager is the hardest opponent — perceptive, patient, resistant to obvious traps. The garden must use its most subtle misdirections.
