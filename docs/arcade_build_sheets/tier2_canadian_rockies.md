## SECTION 2: STORYLINE ADAPTATION

Original Characters Being Replaced:
- Madeline (Celeste protagonist) -> Aurora (a frost-haired climber seeking the Aurora Borealis source)
- Badeline (Celeste's shadow self) -> Polaria (Aurora's shadow — the cold that lives in every climber)
- Mr. Oshiro (ghost hotel owner) -> The Glacier Keeper (a spirit bound to a frozen ice hotel in the mid-cliffs)
- Theo (Celeste's friend) -> Compass (a cartographer Fizgig who maps the route from base camp)

New Narrative Frame:
- Aurora climbs the Canadian Rockies to find the source of the Aurora Borealis. Legend in the Inn says the northern lights are not solar wind — they are the visible frequency of the Inn's Schumann calibration (7.83Hz) interacting with the upper atmosphere. Reaching the summit proves it.
- Each section of the mountain represents a frequency challenge. Lower cliffs are 42Hz (Living — warm, green, summer alpine). Mid-cliffs are In-Between (autumn, storms, uncertainty). Upper cliffs are 84Hz (Shadow — winter, ice, Polaria manifests).
- The Glacier Keeper guards an ice hotel frozen in time at the mid-point. The hotel is a rest area and a challenge — its corridors are slippery (modified physics) and the Keeper's grief makes the ice grow.
- Polaria appears in the upper sections as a rival climber. Not evil — just cold. The dash mechanic becomes a dialogue: Aurora dashes forward (warmth, persistence), Polarai dashes to block (cold, doubt). The player must outmaneuver their own shadow.
- The Jade reward: found at the summit, embedded in the peak's rock face. Jade is the stone of endurance — the Chinese "Stone of Heaven," earned by climbing to the sky.

Dialogue Requirements:
- NPC dialogue count: ~20 lines (Glacier Keeper: 8, Compass: 6, Polarai: 6)
- Voice profiles needed:
  - Glacier Keeper -> Icelandic accent, slow, heavy with ice, low register
  - Compass (Fizgig) -> Canadian Maritimes accent, chipper, fast, high register
  - Polarai -> Same voice as Aurora but pitch-shifted down, echoed, cold
- Whisper Layer presence: Yes — at the summit. The Aurora Borealis IS the Whisper Layer made visible. The lights form shapes — not words, but feelings. The archive speaks in color.

Frequency State:
- Living (42Hz): Lower mountain. Green valleys, summer wildflowers, warm wind. Compass is chatty and encouraging.
- Shadow (84Hz): Upper mountain. Blue ice, howling wind, whiteout conditions. Polarai is aggressive. Dash recovery is slower.
- In-Between: Mid-mountain. Autumn colors, unstable weather, the Glacier Keeper's hotel. The shift happens during a storm sequence.

## SECTION 5: PROGRAMMING REQUIREMENTS

Engine: Godot 4.x | Offline-first

Controller Support:
- Move: Left stick / D-pad (left, right)
- Jump: A / face button (tap = jump, hold = higher)
- Dash: B / face button (8-directional based on stick direction, one dash per refill)
- Climb: hold RT / R2 when near wall
- Wall-jump: jump while clinging to wall + directional input
- Pause: Start / Options
- Bluetooth: auto-detect, prompt if no controller
- Touch: swipe for dash direction, tap for jump (mobile)

Steam Integration:
- Achievements: "Summit" (beat game), "Cairn Collector" (find all hidden cairns), "Cold Climb" (beat Shadow section without dying), "Untouched" (beat ice hotel without touching the ground — wall-climb only), "Aurora" (beat the game in under 30 minutes)
- Cloud saves: Sync PlayerProgress
- Leaderboard: "Fastest Summit" (speedrun time)
- Overlay: Shift+Tab or LB+RB

Base44 API: On completion POST to PlayerProgress — add Jade, increment gamesCompleted

Special Notes:
- Instant respawn like Celeste — no game over screen, no punishment. Death is a learning tool, not a penalty.
- The frequency shift is built into the level design, not a toggle. The mountain gets colder as you climb. This is the most natural FrequencyShift integration of any game.
- The Aurora Borealis at the summit is the Whisper Layer made visible — the only game where the archive's presence is beautiful, not unsettling.
