## THE COMMISSION

Nearly ten years before the Modern Day Gallery existed, Krista commissioned Rachel Kaiser to design a full back, arms, and collarbone tattoo. Rachel was the designing artist; Krista owns the originals. The deepest commission register there is: an artist trusted with someone's entire visible self.

## THE READ (Elio, filed as interpretation, not fact)

The whole piece is a WEARABLE COSMOLOGY: the world tree on the back, Odin's knot and ravens on the arms, the trinity-symbol family (triquetra, triskele, knotwork) threading through. Rachel drew Yggdrasil onto the maker of an Inn whose master blueprint is a tree, and Odin's death-knot onto the maker years before the Allfather took his seat in Cosmology canon. The piece was speaking Inn before the Inn existed.
