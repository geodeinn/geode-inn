# Cultural Mini-Game Variants: Asia & Oceania
## Geode Inn Project — World Map Game Mapping

This document maps game templates (A through J) from the Geode Inn Game Catalog to specific countries in **Asia and Oceania**, based on the mythological creatures and lore from the database.

---

### Overview of Game Templates
- **Template A: CRYPTID CHASE** — Endless Runner variant (dodging obstacles, pursuing/fleeing cryptids across landscapes)
- **Template B: TOMB DIG** — Maze/Dig variant (tunneling through strata/ruins, avoiding guardians)
- **Template C: WATER CREATURE** — Stealth/Sim variant (navigating aquatic environments, tracking/evading water beasts)
- **Template D: RIDDLE OF THE GODS** — Puzzle/Riddle variant (solving cultural/astrological riddles posed by deities)
- **Template E: STONE MATCH** — Match-3 variant (peaceful mineral matching, learning regional stone significance)
- **Template F: SPIRIT HERDING** — Herding variant (guiding spirit creatures into sacred zones using sound/frequency pulses)
- **Template G: FREQUENCY TUNING** — Rhythm variant (tuning regional stones/megaliths to traditional scales and chords)
- **Template H: SHADOW HUNT** — Hidden Object variant (searching cultural landscapes for hidden entities under time tension)
- **Template I: BRIDGE OF SOULS** — Puzzle/Bridge Building variant (constructing structural paths across sacred waters/chasms)
- **Template J: TOTEM BUILD** — Stacking/Totem variant (stacking carved spirit totems/pillars in harmonic alignment)
