# Geode Inn — Cultural Mini-Game Variants: Africa & Middle East

This document maps the **Tier 3 Cultural Mini-Game Templates (A through J)** to 25 prominent countries across **Africa and the Middle East**, incorporating authentic geographic settings, mineral rewards, and mythological creatures extracted from the Geode Inn database.

---

## DESIGN OVERVIEW

Each variant translates one of the 10 standard mini-game mechanics into a short (2–5 minute) regional experience unlocked on the World Map. Every variant incorporates:
1. **Real Geography**: Authentic physical landscapes, historical ruins, or natural wonders.
2. **Cultural Lore & Cryptid Boss**: Mythological beings, spirits, and deities from regional oral traditions.
3. **Reward Stone**: Regional gemstones, minerals, or organic stones connected to the local geology.
4. **2-Sentence Core Gameplay**: A focused summary of player mechanics and victory conditions.
