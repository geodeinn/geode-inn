# Geode Inn — Missing Stone Descriptions
## Batch 5: The Fossils & Oddities (15 stones)
## Drafted 2026-09-04 — in the Inn's voice, Krista-approved style

---

### 1. Ammolite
**Mind:** Ammolite is the ending that refused to be final — an ammonite shell, seventy-one million years extinct, that spent its eternity becoming a gem. The shell that once held a creature now holds every color, and the Inn finds this quietly hilarious: the fossil record's most complete loss, transformed into its most complete rainbow. For anyone whose worst chapter seems settled, ammolite offers the long view: the story is not over. The story is still becoming opal.
**Body:** Supports the skin and the body's surface renewal — the part of us that faces the weather and keeps regenerating anyway.

### 2. The Fossil Cabinet (Agatized Coprolite, Coral, Opalized Wood, Orthoceras)
**Mind:** The Inn keeps a cabinet of things that are technically gone and practically immortal: dinosaur coprolite turned to agate, coral that kept its architecture, wood that went through fire and came out glass, and orthoceras — the arrow-shaped squid-shell straightened into stone. This is the Inn's oldest wing in miniature. Everything in it lived, ended, and then continued as beauty, which the Inn notes is not a consolation prize. It is the standard outcome, given enough time and pressure.
**Body:** Supports bones, teeth, and the body's deep structure — the framework that outlasts everything built on it.

### 3. Petrified Palm Wood (Rainbow)
**Mind:** Petrified palm wood is the tree that had nowhere to run — so it became stone standing up, in place, roots still holding. Transformation without escape: the crisis came, the tree stayed, and three hundred million years later the crisis is a rainbow and the tree is still a tree, permanently. It teaches the deepest form of standing your ground: staying so long that the ground agrees to keep you.
**Body:** Supports the spine and the body's patience — the structural courage that does not require the option of leaving.

### 4. Chrysanthemum Stone
**Mind:** Chrysanthemum stone is the flower that never wilted — white crystal blossoms scattered through black stone, each one opened at the exact moment the rock froze and stayed open forever. It is the stone of the moment kept: the perfect hour, the face as it was, the bloom at its peak, preserved not by memory but by being literally made permanent. The Inn keeps a piece near the guest book. Some moments deserve to be stone.
**Body:** Supports rest and composure — the body's ability to hold one stillness fully, without fidgeting toward the next thing.

### 5. Chinese Writing Stone
**Mind:** Chinese writing stone is the mineral that appears to be drafting — white feldspar "characters" brushed across black limestone in strokes no human hand made. The Inn has studied this stone for a long time and reports the following with scholarly embarrassment: the script has not been translated, but it is clearly trying to say something, and it is clearly not for us. Some messages are in transit for geological reasons. Carry it as a reminder that the universe is writing constantly, and most of it is none of our business — but all of it is beautiful, and some of it might be, someday.
**Body:** Supports the hands and eyes of makers — the fine work of reading and writing, done carefully.

### 6. Covellite
**Mind:** Covellite is copper caught mid-becoming — indigo so deep it looks like the metal dreaming of the ocean, shot with violet and gold sheen, never finished oxidizing, always in transition. It is the alchemist's favorite color of unfinished. The stone for people in the middle of their own transformation who keep asking how much longer: covellite has been in the middle of becoming for two hundred million years, and it is the most beautiful thing in the drawer.
**Body:** Supports the body through transitions — metabolism, adaptation, the chemistry of becoming someone slightly different.

### 7. Cuprite
**Mind:** Cuprite is the ember of the copper family — deep red crystals with garnet's fire and metal's honesty, the heat source under the family's famous conductor. It grounds first and glows second. For minds that burn through their own fuel, cuprite offers the ember's method: lower, slower, longer — the difference between a flame and a fire that keeps itself alive overnight.
**Body:** Supports the blood and circulation — the body's own deep red infrastructure, kept warm.

### 8. Copper
**Mind:** Copper is the first metal humanity befriended — soft enough to shape with stone tools, generous enough to carry energy wherever asked, and patient enough to turn green in its old age without complaint. It is the messenger: the wire, the vein, the river of signal. The Inn's entire architecture of transmission — the Song carried, the frequencies felt — runs on copper's one talent, which is refusing to keep energy to itself. The oldest friendship between a metal and a maker, still conducting.
**Body:** Supports circulation, conductivity, and the body's own wiring — the signal that must arrive.

### 9. Chrysocolla Azurite
**Mind:** Chrysocolla azurite is two blues having a conversation — azurite's deep midnight, chrysocolla's gentle lagoon, blended where they grew into each other in the same copper vein. It is the wisdom stone that learned the better lesson: knowledge is common, but knowledge that speaks kindly is rare. The teacher who knows when to whisper.
**Body:** Supports the throat and gentle expression — the voice that teaches without raising itself.

### 10. Epidote
**Mind:** Epidote is the honest amplifier — the stone that gives you more of whatever you brought. Pour in generosity, receive abundance. Pour in grievance, receive grievance, doubled, with interest. It does not judge. It multiplies. The Inn respects epidote the way it respects a mirror: it is the only stone in the collection that refuses to pretend you didn't say that. Carry it for the increase, and mind what you carry in.
**Body:** Supports the nervous system's signal strength — the body's gain, turned up on whatever it is already doing.

### 11. Eudialyte
**Mind:** Eudialyte is the heart's own geode — a pink so soft it is almost private, hidden in rock that does not announce it, from lands so far north the sun takes the winter off. Traditional keepers called it a gentle heart stone, and the Inn confirms, with the addendum that gentle is not the same as small. The stone that lets the heart have a quiet hour without dismantling anything is doing advanced work.
**Body:** Supports the heart and emotional stamina — the quiet hour, made physical.

### 12. Staurolite (Fairy Cross Stone)
**Mind:** Staurolite is the meeting made mineral — two crystals that grew toward each other and met in the middle, both of them changed into the shape of the meeting: a cross, naturally, without being asked. The old roads called them fairy crosses and gave them to travelers, which the Inn considers exactly correct. This is the stone of intersections — paths crossing, lives crossing, the luck that lives at the point where two things that were traveling alone agree to become one shape.
**Body:** Supports bones, joints, and the intersections of the body — the places where structure meets structure and must agree.

### 13. Preseli Blue Stone
**Mind:** Preseli blue stone is the stone that was chosen. Quarried from the Welsh mountains and carried a hundred and fifty miles to Salisbury Plain — dragged, floated, hauled by hand — to stand at Stonehenge, where it still stands, still bluestone, still ringing faintly when struck. Most stones are found. Some stones are chosen, and the choosing is the story: someone wanted that stone, that specific stone, badly enough to move it across a country before the wheel. This is the stone that unlocks the Inn's first portal, and the Inn will not explain itself further. It was chosen. Ask it inside.
**Body:** Supports endurance and the strength of things carried far — the body's ability to be moved, rattled, and set down without breaking.

### 14. Turquoise (Tibetan)
**Mind:** Tibetan turquoise is the sky-colored stone of the world's high places — the plateau's turquoise does not pretend to be water. It pretends to be heaven, captured, portable. The old tradition set it in everything precious and traded it like weather. The Inn's entry says simply: some stones carry the ground up to you. This one carries the sky down, and the difference is the whole point.
**Body:** Supports the breath and the chest — the altitude work of living, taken in calmly.

### 15. Aquaprase
**Mind:** Aquaprase is the ocean-born cousin of the chalcedony family — blue-green so clean it looks like a lagoon that agreed to hold still, discovered so recently that the stone itself has barely been introduced. It is the youngest stone in the Inn's cases and already fluent in the family language: hidden depth, quiet water, the calm that is not emptiness but clarity. The Inn is watching this one with interest. New stones, like new guests, arrive knowing things.
**Body:** Supports hydration and flow — the body's water, kept clear and moving.
