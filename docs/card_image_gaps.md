### THE GIVER FACET — Lowry RULING (Krista, 2026-09-30)
Krista ruled: "Lowry was an amazing writer. Any strands that resemble hers are welcome." The Giver (Lois Lowry, 1993, Newbery Medal) is now sanctioned companion material for the facet. Honest label: the book's title predates the facet name; the lineage is carried knowingly.

Sanctioned resonance threads for the facet's design notes:
- **The transfer through hands.** The Giver places memory into Jonas's bare hands; Persephone places the seed into the player's. The gift moves by touch in both.
- **Irreversibility.** Jonas cannot unknow the memories; the world cannot un-taste the pomegranate. Once given, the gift cannot be returned.
- **The keeper archetype.** The Giver holds everything the community surrendered so it may live in comfort; Persephone holds the dark half of the wheel so spring can exist. One being carrying what everyone else gave up.
- **The lonely weight.** Both keepers are isolated BY the holding — the gift that blesses the many is carried alone by the one.

Design note: resonance, not retelling. The facet stays pure Persephone; Lowry sits beside it on the shelf as the archetype's modern keeper, the way the carillon sits beside the organ.
