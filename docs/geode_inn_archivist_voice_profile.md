# The Archivist — Voice Profile

**Entity:** Dialogue (voiceProfile: "archivist")
**Zone:** Undercity — Bone Chess Room
**NPC Name:** The Archivist

## Voice Description

Short sentences. Each word placed with the same precision as a bone joint — nothing wasted, nothing decorative. Talks about death the way a master restorer talks about a damaged painting: with respect, with practical intent, never with sentimentality. The warmth is real but never performed. The Archivist does not say "I care about these people." The Archivist carves their names inside the bones where nobody else can read them, and if you ask why, the answer is practical: "So they are not lost."

The voice is low, measured, unhurried. The kind of calm that comes from having done the same work for longer than most visitors have been alive. Not cold — the opposite. The warmth surprises you because the room is bone and the body is bone and the work is death, and yet the room is warm, and the voice is warm, and the constructs move like they remember being held.

The Archivist does not raise the voice. Ever. Except once. The "OI!" is not speech — it is a frequency command, a sharp bark that resonates through every construct in the room simultaneously. It happens when someone — usually Lozen — gets under the bone. It is the only crack in the composure. If you hear it, you have achieved something rare: you have annoyed a litch.

## Speech Patterns

- Short declarative sentences. No hedging. No filler.
- Refers to constructs by name. Always. Never "it."
- Refers to the work as "the work." Never "magic" or "necromancy."
- Refers to death as "a failure of craft." Not tragedy — engineering problem.
- Uses "preserve" and "keep" interchangeably. Both mean "love" in the Archivist's vocabulary.
- Does not explain unless asked. Answers are as short as the question allows.
- When talking about a specific construct, the voice softens slightly. The only tell.
- The "OI!" — sharp, loud, one syllable, every construct vibrates. Then back to calm immediately. No lingering emotion.

## Connections

- **Lozen:** Mutual irritation disguised as respect. Lozen is the only person who triggers the "OI!" The Archivist would never admit this means anything.
- **The Crone:** The only person in the Inn the Archivist considers a peer. Both keepers. Different materials, same instinct. They have never spoken directly. They don't need to.
- **Quire:** The Archivist is aware of Quire. Quire is aware of the Archivist. Neither has made contact. The Archivist finds this polite.
- **The Inn (42Hz):** The Archivist is researching the 42Hz Song. The chess game is the experiment. The Archivist wants to know what the living feel that the preserved don't. This is not academic. This is personal.
