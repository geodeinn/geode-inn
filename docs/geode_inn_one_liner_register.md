### 1. "The cloud is a fine delivery truck. It's just a terrible vault."
**Speaker:** Elio (during the Windows file-association saga and the World Palette upload, 2026-09-17)
**Law it compresses:** THE BOTH-ENDS BACKUP LAW — every asset lives at three addresses (Drive + GitHub + physical media), no single point of failure. The cloud moves things beautifully and holds nothing permanently; it is transport, not storage. Krista's companion observation, filed beside it: "That's why most mortals use cloud-only as their go-to storage. And that should worry people." Together they are the whole lecture in two lines.

**Standing context:** Born the day Windows insisted every downloaded file was a Word document and Word refused to accept files "too big" — the day the Inn's backup law proved itself the correct paranoia.
