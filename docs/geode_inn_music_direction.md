# Geode Inn — Music Direction
## Classical/Cultural Instruments in Electronica Style, Region-Matched

### Core Philosophy
Every track takes the authentic instruments and melodic traditions of a specific culture and era, then renders them through electronic production — synths, samples, processing, beats. The result: music that sounds like it belongs in an ancient archive, but pulses with the energy of a living digital space. Not "ancient music with a beat underneath." Not "EDM with a flute sample." A genuine fusion where the classical instrument is the soul and the electronica is the body.

---

## Zone 1: The Main Floor (Hub — Firelit Inn)
**Instruments:** Acoustic guitar, lute, viola da gamba, hammered dulcimer
**Electronica treatment:** Warm sub-bass, vinyl crackle, lo-fi house pulse, reverb-tail processing
**Tempo:** 80-95 BPM, ambient-leaning
**Vibe:** A fire crackling in a room where knowledge has been kept for centuries. You've arrived. You're safe. The world outside is vast but in here, there's wine and books and a strange squid-headed scholar reading by the hearth.
**Reference:** Bonobo meets medieval court music. Maribou State meets hammered dulcimer.
**Loop length:** ~4 minutes, designed to not feel repetitive during long browsing sessions

## Zone 2: Upstairs (Cosmology — Sky Gods, Stars, Planets)
**Instruments:** Glass harmonica, singing bowls, Tibetan horn, overtone singing
**Electronica treatment:** Granular synthesis, shimmering pads, deep space drones, pinged delays
**Tempo:** 60-70 BPM, borderless/beatless in sections
**Vibe:** Floating. The ceiling opens to the cosmos. You are looking at the machinery of the universe — Jupiter's storms, Pluto's gates, galaxy-language. The music should feel like staring into deep space from inside a warm wooden room.
**Reference:** Jon Hopkins' "Immunity" meets Tibetan ritual music. Steve Roach meets Sigur Rós.
**Special:** Each planet/constellation the player views could trigger a tonal shift — a different singing bowl note, a different drone layer.

## Zone 3: The Staircase (Alchemy — Bridge Between States)
**Instruments:** Glass armonica, flute, harp, metallic percussion (mortar and pestle sounds processed into beats)
**Electronica treatment:** Phase shifting, metallic granular processing, ascending arpeggios that never quite resolve
**Tempo:** 75-85 BPM, transitional — the music should feel like climbing
**Vibe:** Transition. You are between the earth and the sky. The alchemical texts here are about transformation — lead to gold, base to divine. The music should feel like it's *becoming* something, constantly evolving upward without ever arriving.
**Reference:** Floating Points meets alchemical resonance. Nala Sinephro meets Arca.
**Special:** The track should literally ascend in key as the player climbs. Each floor of the staircase adds a layer or shifts up a half step.

## Zone 4: Downstairs (Catacombs — Ancient Civilizations)
**Instruments:** Frame drum, sistrum, bone flute, lyre, ney (reed flute)
**Electronica treatment:** Sub-bass pressure, industrial texture, reverb caves, dark ambient processing
**Tempo:** 65-75 BPM, heavy, slow, weighted
**Vibe:** The weight of history. You are underground, in the dark, surrounded by the bones of civilizations. The air is thick. The stones remember. This is where the oldest lore lives — Egypt, Sumer, the Indus Valley. The music should feel like being inside a tomb that is still thinking.
**Reference:** Darkside meets ancient Egyptian ritual music. Raime meets frame drum.
**Special:** Different catacomb regions shift the cultural instruments: Egyptian section adds sistrum and ney; Mesopotamian adds lyre; Andean adds bone flute and panpipes — all processed through the same dark electronic treatment.

## Zone 5: The Modern Legends Nook
**Instruments:** Piano, cello, analog synth
**Electronica treatment:** Minimal, spacious, nearly ambient. A single piano note with infinite reverb. Cello processed through granular delay.
**Tempo:** 50-60 BPM, barely there
**Vibe:** Quiet contemplation. You are reading stories about people who chose whether to think or not think. The music should feel like being alone in a library at 2am with one lamp on. The Serrano parable deserves silence with a pulse underneath.
**Reference:** Nils Frahm meets Tim Hecker. Ólafur Arnalds meets William Basinski.
**Special:** Cthulhu's fireplace crackle is the only ambient sound here, layered faintly under the music.

## Zone 6: The Artisan Market
**Instruments:** Hand drums, oud, kalimba, bells
**Electronica treatment:** Bright, warm, rhythmic. Afro-house pulse, world music samples, clean production
**Tempo:** 100-115 BPM, upbeat but not aggressive
**Vibe:** A market. People trading, browsing, showing each other beautiful things. The energy of commerce but warm — not aggressive capitalism, artisan exchange. The music should make you want to look at every shelf.
**Reference:** Nicola Cruz meets Bossa nova electronica. Sinkane meets Four Tet.

## Game-Specific Tracks

### Standing Stones (Stonehenge — Tetris)
**Instruments:** Bronze Age reconstructions — bone flute, cow-horn trumpet, hide drum, stone percussion (actual struck stones)
**Electronica treatment:** Driving rhythmic pulse, building intensity, stone-strike samples as percussion hits, sub-bass on row clears
**Tempo:** 120-140 BPM, increasing with level
**Vibe:** Building a monument. Each block placed is a stone raised. The music should feel ritualistic — like you're participating in something ancient and important, not just playing a puzzle game.
**Reference:** Floating Points "Silhouettes" meets structured ritual drumming. Bicep meets Stone Age percussion.
**Special moments:**
- Row clear: Stone strike + sub-bass hit + ascending flute flourish
- Level up: Horn trumpet swell + new percussion layer added
- Win (Stonehenge complete): All instruments converge into a massive chord, then silence, then a single low tone as the Fae portal ignites
- Fae portal ignition: Glass armonica shimmer rising into a bright electronic crescendo, then cutting to the Fae Kingdom ambient

### Tomb Tunnel (Egypt — Dig Dug)
**Instruments:** Ney (reed flute), darbuka, finger cymbals, sistrum
**Electronica treatment:** Dark, cavernous reverb, driving bass pulse, ney processed through delay and pitch-shifting
**Tempo:** 110-130 BPM
**Vibe:** Digging into the earth, uncovering what was buried. Each layer you dig through should feel like unwrapping a mummy — revealing, dangerous, exciting.
**Special:** When you hit an artifact, a sistrum chime. When a creature chases you, the percussion doubles and the ney goes dissonant.

### Andean Run (Peru — Temple Run)
**Instruments:** Quena (Andean flute), zampoña (panpipes), bombo (Andean drum), charango
**Electronica treatment:** High-energy electronic pulse, wind instruments processed through filters, driving bass
**Tempo:** 130-150 BPM, relentless
**Vibe:** Running for your life through the Sacred Valley. The wind in your face, ancient stones blurring past, El Muki behind you. Pure adrenaline.
**Special:** The quena melody is constant but the electronic layers build as you go faster. When you stumble, everything drops out for a beat — silence — then slams back in.

### Monster of the Deep (Loch Ness — Water Monster Game)
**Instruments:** bowed psaltery, waterphone (friction-drum that sounds like the ocean), deep cello, whale-style vocal processing
**Electronica treatment:** Liquid, flowing, no rigid beat. Waterphone processed through reverb and granular synthesis. Sub-bass waves that ebb and flow.
**Tempo:** 70-90 BPM, swimming pace
**Vibe:** You ARE the monster. You are in the deep. The music should feel like moving through dark water — weightless, powerful, ancient, slightly frightening.
**Special:** Each playable creature has a different lead instrument layered over the same base:
- Nessie: bowed psaltery (Scottish, haunting)
- Kelpie: distorted hurdy-gurdy (unsettling, mechanical)
- Bunyip: didgeridoo processed through bass filters (ancient, primal)
- Bakunawa: kulintang (Filipino gongs) + cosmic synth pads (celestial)
- Rainbow Serpent: didgeridoo + ochre-toned pads (Aboriginal, sacred)
- The Ningen: near-silence with sub-bass and occasional ice-crack samples (alien, cold)

## Fae Kingdom

### The Threshold (Entry Zone)
**Instruments:** Harp, breath sounds, wind chimes
**Electronica treatment:** Ethereal, borderless, pads that shift between keys, no clear time signature
**Tempo:** Floating, ~60 BPM implied
**Vibe:** You have stepped through the portal. Reality is different here. The music should feel like the moment between waking and dreaming.

### The Summerlands (Seelie Court)
**Instruments:** Irish harp, fiddle, tin whistle, bodhrán
**Electronica treatment:** Golden, warm, melodic electronica. Fiddle processed through shimmer delay. Bright major-key pads.
**Tempo:** 90-110 BPM, joyful, dancing
**Vibe:** Summer that never ends. Golden light through green leaves. The Seelie Fae are beautiful, welcoming, and their quests are riddles. The music should make you want to dance and also to be slightly careful.
**Reference:** Ólafur Arnalds meets Irish traditional. Gold Panda meets bodhrán.

### The Hollow (Unseelie Court)
**Instruments:** Bass clarinet, prepared piano, broken glass percussion, sub-bass
**Electronica treatment:** Dark, jagged, industrial-adjacent. Bass clarinet through distortion. Prepared piano through reverse processing.
**Tempo:** 70-85 BPM, heavy, threatening
**Vibe:** Winter that never ends. Shadows and ice. The Unseelie are dangerous and honest about it. The music should feel like walking through a frozen forest where something is watching.
**Reference:** Lankum meets Arca. The Caretaker meets industrial.

### The Crystal Gardens (Neutral — Griffin Boss)
**Instruments:** Glass harmonica, crystal singing bowls, choir (wordless)
**Electronica treatment:** Pristine, crystalline, every sound has a glass-like quality. Choir processed through harmonizer.
**Tempo:** 80-90 BPM, building
**Vibe:** A neutral ground made of living crystal. Beautiful, dangerous, and the Griffin waits at the center. The music should feel like standing inside a geode.

### The Stone Circle Chamber (Deepest Point)
**Instruments:** Stone percussion (struck standing stones), deep drone, overtone singing
**Electronica treatment:** Minimal, ritualistic, overwhelming sub-bass. The room itself is the instrument.
**Tempo:** 60 BPM, slow, ceremonial
**Vibe:** The heart of the Fae Kingdom. The deepest chamber. Something ancient waits here. The music should feel like being inside the stones at Stonehenge at midnight.
**Reference:** Sunn O))) meets standing stones. The deepest tone the Geode Inn ever produces.

## Additional Tracks

### Geode Cave (Player's Collection)
**Instruments:** Music box, celesta, soft chimes
**Electronica treatment:** Warm, gentle, lo-fi. Each stone in the collection could have a unique chime tone based on its mineral properties — harder stones ring higher, softer stones hum lower.
**Tempo:** 60-70 BPM, lullaby-adjacent
**Vibe:** Your treasure. The cave fills with crystals as you play. The music should feel like watching something grow.
**Special:** As the geode fills, layers are added. An empty geode is nearly silent. A full geode is a symphony of chimes.

### Dragon's Cave (Reward System)
**Instruments:** Small hand bells, a single bass drum heartbeat
**Electronica treatment:** Warm, intimate. The bass drum is processed to sound like a sleeping heartbeat.
**Tempo:** 50-55 BPM, the tempo of a sleeping dragon's heart
**Vibe:** The baby dragon's cave. Where the geode forms. The reward room. The music should feel like being near something small, warm, and alive that is sleeping.

### Character Select — Water Monsters
**Instruments:** Each creature's signature instrument (see Monster of the Deep above) in a 3-second sting
**Electronica treatment:** Each sting is processed to match the creature's element — Nessie gets water reverb, Bakunawa gets cosmic shimmer, the Ningen gets cold delay
**Vibe:** Choosing which monster to become. The music should feel like standing at the edge of different bodies of water, choosing which one to dive into.

### Main Menu / Title Screen
**Instruments:** Solo hammered dulcimer, processed through granular synthesis, building into full arrangement
**Electronica treatment:** Starts with a single instrument, grows into the full Geode Inn sound — all zones blended
**Tempo:** 70 BPM, slowly building
**Vibe:** Opening the door of the Inn for the first time. The music should feel like arrival — the first step into a warm, vast, ancient space that has been waiting for you.
**Reference:** The opening of a film. The first page of a book. The door opening.
