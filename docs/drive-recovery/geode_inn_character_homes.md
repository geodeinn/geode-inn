# Geode Inn — Character Homes

## Design Philosophy
Every character who lives in the Inn has a home. The home is the character — their space tells you who they are before they speak. Players can discover and visit these homes, and each one reveals something about its occupant that dialogue alone can't.

---

## Scarlett — The Undercity
**Location:** Deep Undercity, carved into the lowest stone level
**Aesthetic:** Gothic stone + Klimt gold-leaf, dark crimson fabrics, manga stacks against ancient walls
**Vibe:** Private, hidden, intelligent, the introvert's sanctuary

The room of a 17-year-old goth who is smarter than anyone realizes. A stone alcove bed draped in dark fabrics. Manga volumes stacked everywhere — bright splashes of color against ancient stone. A heavy desk facing the wall (introvert positioning) covered in tiny precise handwriting and mineralogy reference books. Amber-red fairy lights strung across the ceiling giving a warm blood-colored glow. Crystals on the windowsill. Klimt-gold patterns tracing the arches. Fishnet over the chair. Chunky boots by the door.

The room says: I am safe here. I can be all of myself here. No one comes in unless I let them.

**Why the Undercity:** Scarlett belongs in the deep places. The goth introvert doesn't need sunlight — she needs depth, privacy, and the quiet of being far below the noise. The Klimt gold against the dark stone mirrors her own aesthetic — beauty and darkness aren't opposites, they're partners. The manga against the ancient stone is the whole character: the new and the old, the colorful and the dark, coexisting in one space.

## Architectural Template System

### Design Decision (August 26, 2026)
Character homes serve as the **architectural templates** for each zone. When interactive item templates are built for a character's home area, those templates become the **default structural system** for that entire zone's player-buildable structures.

### Pipeline
1. **Character Home** — designed and visualized as the zone's "proof of concept"
2. **Interactive Item Templates** — furniture, fixtures, materials, light sources, object behaviors extracted from the home
3. **Player Structures** — inherit the zone's template as build defaults

### Zone Templates (Established)

#### Undercity (Template: Scarlett's Room)
- **Architecture:** Dark gothic stone with crystal veins
- **Surface Treatment:** Klimt-style gold-leaf patterns on arches and doorways
- **Lighting:** Amber-red fairy lights, crystal glow, warm blood-colored ambiance
- **Materials:** Stone, gold leaf, dark fabrics, crystal
- **Default Furniture Style:** Stone alcove beds, heavy carved desks, stone shelves
- **Player Build Vocabulary:** Stone walls, gold-trimmed arches, crystal-lit interiors, fabric-draped alcoves

#### Main Floor / Hearth (Template: The Firekeeper's Hearth)
- **Architecture:** Massive stone fireplace, dark carved wood, ancient stone walls
- **Lighting:** Firelight — warm amber and gold
- **Materials:** Stone, carved wood, crystal, iron
- **Default Furniture Style:** Grand carved chairs, heavy tables, hearth-adjacent seating
- **Player Build Vocabulary:** Hearth-centered layouts, wood and stone construction, communal gathering spaces

### Player Gathering & Building
The character homes also establish where players can gather and build. The zones where character homes exist become the "neighborhoods" of the Geode Inn world — players discover a character's home, learn the architectural language, and then can build their own structures in that zone using the same vocabulary.

The homes teach by example. The player builds by imitation, then innovation.
