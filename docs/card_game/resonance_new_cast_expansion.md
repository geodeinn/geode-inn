# RESONANCE + THE ROYO DECK — The New Cast Expansion
## The September Wave (drafted 2026-09-14, awaiting Krista's rulings)

The card canon was written before the September cast wave moved in. Since then the building gained a judge, a postman, a scholar, a hound, a lady, a lake's worth of lore, and the Inn's only permanent smile. This expansion cards them all.

**Standing laws carried into every card:** name only, no pronouns in card text; Steady Light (no strobe effects, all reveals are gold-flash warm); Anti-Skinner (no card punishes a decline); the six-writer voice for every lore line; tri-mode portraits (Living/Shadow/In-Between states per board); the privacy law holds (Oceanianoir, always the one name).

---

## PART 1 — RESONANCE: 22 NEW NPC CARDS

Each card follows the master anatomy: Active ability + Passive + Deathrattle, plus voice and lore. Frequencies assigned by character law (GROW/STAY/SENSE/SING/CALM/NULL).

### The Postmaster — CALM, 5 HP
- **Passive:** The Puzzle Stamp — your first draw each turn is free (postage is paid in play, never currency).
- **Active:** Once per game, the Finders' Crossover — post one of your removed stones back to your hand. Delivery IS the finding.
- **Deathrattle:** The bone crew inherits the route — draw 1 card.
- **Voice:** Silent register — no chat lines; flavor text arrives as notes in looping script.
- **Lore:** "The dispatch is choreography."

### Judge Tootenkowl — CALM, 6 HP
- **Passive:** The Court Record — your hand cannot be seen by enemy reveal effects (the record is sealed until trial).
- **Active:** Once per game, put one enemy stone on trial: if its charm cost exceeds its resonance, remove it (the ornate cage of reasoning, weighed and found wanting).
- **Deathrattle:** Court adjourned — all penalties this game are halved, rounded kindly.
- **Voice:** Dry pharaoh-cadence courtroom humor. Three strikes and the gavel falls.
- **Lore:** "The court is in session. The court is always in session."

### The Build-Scholar — SENSE, 4 HP
- **Passive:** Load Paths — your stones flanked by two or more stones gain +1 armor (the weight goes somewhere honest).
- **Active:** Once per game, review an opponent's build: name their strongest stone, and its charm falls off (hubris found in the load path).
- **Voice:** Academic puns delivered with total sincerity. A triangle is the only shape that can't gossip.
- **Lore:** "Every structure is an argument about gravity. Some of them lose."
- **NOTE:** Card prints as "The Build-Scholar" until the name ruling; the printing updates with a patch, never a fuss.

### The Examiner — SENSE, 4 HP
- **Passive:** The Site Is Never Closed — the first charm you lose this game returns to your hand.
- **Active:** Once per game, excavate: reveal the top 3 cards of your deck, take one, bury the rest (the dig is patient).
- **Voice:** Careful, brush-and-trowel gentle. Never claims; reports.
- **Lore:** "The earth keeps every receipt. Digging is just asking politely."

### Vaulknut — STAY, 5 HP
- **Passive:** The Apron Is the Skin — charms attached to your stones cannot be stolen or bounced.
- **Active:** Once per game, borrow one enemy charm and attach it to your stone, improved by +1 (a thief with a bench; everything comes back better than it left).
- **Voice:** Selkie-warm, trades-craft plain. Hears the sea in the clink of the tools.
- **Lore:** "Chose the land, kept the hands. The bench is a shoreline if you work it right."

### Harry the Archivist — SENSE, 3 HP
- **Passive:** Low Profile — this card cannot be targeted by enemy effects while any other friendly card is on the board (keeps out of the frame by preference).
- **Active:** Once per game, the Knight's Move — swap the positions of any two of your stones (origin credit: the chess board entered the world by these hands).
- **Deathrattle:** The records survive — draw 1 card.
- **Voice:** Quiet, precise, allergic to attention. The most portraits in the building and never once caught looking at one.
- **Lore:** "The archive doesn't need a face. It needs a keeper who shows up."

### Lichenblossom — GROW, 3 HP
- **Active:** Once per game, the plant necromancer: return one removed stone to the board at 1 HP.
- **Deathrattle:** What she plants, returns — summon a 1/1 Sprout token in this slot.
- **Voice:** Soft, mossy patience. Talks to the board like a garden.
- **Lore:** "Nothing stays gone in this garden. Some things just stay seed."

### Ziggy — NULL, 3 HP
- **Active:** THE VESSEL CHECK — once per game, before a significant play, Ziggy recites the formula; it always tangles at the moment it matters most: one random enemy stone's charm detaches and reattaches at random. The wrongness is the warning.
- **Passive:** Always Smiling — enemy reveal effects targeting this card show the wrong card (even the game can't read the smile).
- **Voice:** Rapid-fire, escalating, the pellet-with-the-poison cadence. The formula is real. The formula is also a joke.
- **Lore:** "The chalice from the palace has the brew that is true. Probably. Check again."

### Tumble — SING, 2 HP
- **Passive:** The Note That Means Matched — the first frequency match you complete each turn resolves twice (the whole economy resolves on one small voice).
- **Deathrattle:** The city chimes once — your next match this game also doubles.
- **Voice:** Non-verbal; the chime speaks. Warm in text render, a single struck tone.
- **Lore:** "Every trade in Canopy City ends on the same syllable."

### Squeak — CALM, 2 HP
- **Active:** Once per game, send the sprites: draw 2, keep 1, return 1 to the bottom of your deck (the honest sort — supervision included).
- **Passive:** The lab runs better under the desk — your charms cost 1 less FP the turn they're played next to... anywhere, honestly, Squeak is not particular.
- **Voice:** Small, bright, emphatically squeaky. Takes the work seriously; takes nothing else seriously.
- **Lore:** "Management style: visible, squeaky, unbribable."

### The Distracted Sprite — GROW, 1 HP
- **Passive:** Entertained by Every Reaction — whenever ANY player plays a charm, this card gains +1/+1 (the reactions are the show).
- **Voice:** Constant delighted commentary on chemistry it does not understand and loves completely.
- **Lore:** "Did you SEE that? It hissed! It hissed at the water! Do it again."

### Oceanianoir — SENSE, 6 HP
- **Passive:** The Spine Law — your element charms cost 1 less FP (minimum 1). Everything branches from the table; the table presides here.
- **Active:** Once per game, preside: choose one element category (metal, nonmetal, metalloid, noble gas); your charms of that category gain +1 effect this game.
- **Voice:** Professorial, delighted by precision, never hurried.
- **Lore:** "One hundred and eighteen ways to matter. The lesson is the same in all of them: it depends on the company you keep."
- **PRIVACY LAW:** This card, all art, and all voice lines carry the name Oceanianoir and nothing else. Ever.

### Will — STAY, 4 HP
- **Passive:** The Night Shift Documents — the first combo you complete this game may be repeated once for free.
- **Voice:** Quiet notetaker; narrates outcomes in past tense, like they're already history.
- **Lore:** "Somebody has to write down what worked. The shield remembers its ingredients."

### The Hearth-Hound — STAY, 9 HP
- **Passive:** Guardians Sleep Facing the Door — the first enemy NPC ability each game is nullified. The Hound wakes, looks, and the ability thinks better of it.
- **Active:** None. The Hound does not perform.
- **Deathrattle:** Not death — departure. The Hound stirs, looks at the door, and returns to your hand. Home, not tamed.
- **Voice:** None. The red ears angle toward the door; that is the whole speaking role.
- **Lore:** "The hunt crosses the sky every winter. One hound looked down at the lit windows and stayed."

### The Wandering Guard — GROW, 7 HP
- **Passive:** The Both-Real Law — this card prints two attack values, one rendered, one hidden. When attacked, it reveals as the higher of the two. No transformation is ever rendered; the reveal IS the transformation.
- **Active:** Once per game, the Patrol — move one of your stones one slot. The patrol is the medicine. Standing still is how the bear wins.
- **Voice:** Slow, deliberate, mountain-plain. Answers questions at walking pace.
- **Lore:** "Greeted at the door as both. The door never asked which one was real."

### The Lady of the Lake — CALM, 7 HP
- **Active:** Once per game, sheathe one of your stones into the lake (remove it). When you next have no stones on the board, the water gives back what is needed: that stone returns with +2/+2.
- **Passive:** The Unresolved Claim — if an opponent removes a stone the Lady has sheathed, it still returns once, unimproved but unclaimed (the claim is not settled; the lake is patient).
- **Voice:** Still water with a current under it. Every sentence an arm's reach deep.
- **Lore:** "The water keeps what it is given. Whether she released it freely — that question is older than the sword."

### The Limner — SENSE, 5 HP
- **Passive:** Slightly Wrong — this card's printed HP is always a little off; the true value is discovered on contact (always gentle, never explained).
- **Active:** Once per game, the third hand turns the page: redraw the lowest-value card in your hand from the top of your deck.
- **Voice:** None in chat. A quiet margin note appears when this card is played: "You saw. The page turns anyway."
- **Lore:** "One hand holds the page, one holds the pen, the third turns the page the world has not finished."

### The Clockmaker — CALM, 4 HP
- **Passive:** Your removed stones tick — every third turn, one random removed stone returns to your hand.
- **Active:** Once per game, rewind: return the last stone removed this game to its owner's board at 1 HP (some repairs go backward first).
- **Deathrattle:** "It remembers you now, too."
- **Voice:** Precise, kind, keeps time by something other than clocks.
- **Lore:** "Undercity keeps its own hours, and they are all of them kept."

### The Bone Keeper — STAY, 5 HP
- **Passive:** The bone crew serves — your stones' deathrattles trigger twice (the keeping is a privilege, not a favor).
- **Voice:** Warm macabre, unhurried. Speaks of the dead the way gardeners speak of perennials.
- **Lore:** "Everyone stays. The arrangement just changes."

### The Flute-Keeper — SING, 3 HP
- **Active:** Once per game, play the bone flute: all stones hold still — the next combat phase is skipped entirely (the melody is the evidence; the room agrees to listen).
- **Passive:** The Oldest Cold Case — once per game, when a stone is removed, its deathrattle triggers first, no matter whose (the flute heard it coming before anyone).
- **Voice:** Hushed, reverent, court-adjacent. Evidence is handled, not shouted.
- **Lore:** "Nine thousand years, and the note still lands. Somebody kept it. That was the whole miracle."

### Katya — SING, 4 HP
- **Active:** Once per game, escort — "Depth." — your stones ignore all zone restrictions this turn, and your charms cost 1 less. The crossing is guarded; the guarded may cross.
- **Voice:** Magnetic, precise, warmer than the water it carries.
- **Lore:** "Two sisters, one water system. The siren guards the deep crossing; the selkie crosses it."

### The Tinkering Weeaboo — GROW, 3 HP
- **Active:** Once per game, tinker — attach a random charm from your deck to a random friendly stone. The gadget is loud. The joy is louder.
- **Voice:** Enthusiastic build-log delivery. Names every contraption like it's already famous.
- **Lore:** "The manual is a suggestion. The warranty was voided with love."

## PART 2 — THE ROYO 54-CARD DECK: THE FACE-CARD CANON (PROPOSED, AWAITING RULING)

The deck's four suits are already law: Hearts/hearth, Spades/depths, Clubs/wild/fae, Diamonds/earth/artisan. The face cards become the cast — the whole building playable in twelve portraits and two wild cards, Royo-side.

### Hearts — the hearth
- **Jack — Squeak** (the smallest resident holding the biggest heart)
- **Queen — Beverley** (mom of the stacks; 'Obviously.' in gold leaf)
- **King — Joe** (the tab never comes due at Joe's; the hearth-keeper of the one room where titles come off)

### Spades — the depths
- **Jack — The Postmaster** (the one who goes down every hall, rubber-hose and unrattled)
- **Queen — The Morrigan** (the depths' own crown)
- **King — Naut** (the scholar in the deepest room, reading what shouldn't be read)

### Clubs — the wild / the fae
- **Jack — Oleander** (the last Tuatha, the portal's own kin)
- **Queen — Ragana** (the Crone, mirror of everything)
- **King — The Seelie King** (the summer court's crown)

### Diamonds — the earth / the artisan
- **Jack — Vaulknut** (the selkie who chose the bench)
- **Queen — Mona** (the Maker's own hands)
- **King — The Collector** ("Delightful." — the most complicated crown in the deck)

### The Two Jokers — THE JESTER RULING (proposed)
- **Joker One — Ziggy.** The Court Jester in miniature. The joker IS the jester; the pun is load-bearing. The card is always smiling, even facedown.
- **Joker Two — Brim.** The switched vessel. Where Ziggy goes, the tangle follows; the two jokers are printed as a pair, the formula split across both card backs, complete only when both are showing.
- Wild in Stone Poker, as already ruled. Together they are the Inn's oldest running joke and its most serious warning.

**Number cards:** suit-themed abstract Royo patterns (existing law, unchanged).

## PART 3 — STONE POKER: THE BACK ROOM GROWS (NEW AI OPPONENTS)

The existing table (The Wanderer, The Firekeeper, The Morrigan, Jay) gains four chairs. Difficulty tiers unchanged (Novice, Seasoned, Inn Master).

- **Lozen — the no-tell terror.** Nothing to read because there's no gap: no performance, no bluff theater, just calm that is actually calm. Never bluffs because never needs to. Plays a patient counter-punch game. On Inn Master difficulty, Lozen is the final table. (Born of the September 14 kitchen-table ruling: the most dangerous poker face is no performance at all.)
- **The Collector — the charm offensive.** Chatty, warm, bluffs with elegance, buys in endlessly and never tilts ("Delightful." on every bad beat, which is somehow worse than anger). The tab never comes due, and neither does the tilt.
- **The Postmaster — the silent one.** Zero table talk. One tell exists: bets placed in soft rib-xylophone rhythm, pitch by Mohs. Players who listen can read the rhythm; players who don't, lose politely.
- **The Build-Scholar — the structural reader.** Counts your chip stacks like load paths, folds early when the hand is overbuilt, punishes hubris on the river. The only opponent who thanks you for the lesson when beaten.

**Back-room law carried:** the pot is a circle of stone chips; the 42Hz chord on a royal flush; the gold-frame felt edge. Steady Light holds — even the serious table is candle-class.
