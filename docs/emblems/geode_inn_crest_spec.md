## THE RULINGS

**THE CENTER — THE TREE.** Krista's ruling: four was the traditional crest amount, three only when a central point was part of the design — "A tree. The classic Celtic circular knot Yggdrasil." The center point is the Celtic circular knot Yggdrasil — the world tree braided into an unbroken circle. THE MELTING FURNACE IN WOODCUT: a Celtic circle and a Norse tree in one emblem, the Heathen Acknowledgment without a single word. THE BLUEPRINT, NOT A SYMBOL: the Inn's floor plan has been Yggdrasil since the founding — branches upstairs (the sky gods), trunk (the Staircase, the bridge between states), roots downstairs (the catacombs, the dead kept warm). The center of the crest is the building's own architecture. And the quiet genealogy: Yggdrasil = "Ygg's horse," and Ygg is Odin — the same god the witan's naming chain climbed; the god whose ravens named the building; whose knot (the Valknut) named Vaulknut. The center of the crest is him. The whole evening was always leading there.

**THE FOUR:**
1. **THE OWL — the Watcher.** At the crown. Wherever it is sighted, one of us is near. The witness, not the bouncer (Bohemian Grove's concrete owl could never do the job).
2. **THE RAVEN — the Memory.** In the branches, where Huginn and Muninn canonically perch — Thought and Memory fly out each morning and come home each evening to tell the tree's owner what happened, which is the Gazette's exact job description. The name of the building, finally on the building. (Alternates considered and retired: the wolf — Geri and Freki eat at the same fire; the council-instinct lives in the family layer instead. The geode — kept as the dragon's anchor, not a charge.)
3. **THE DRAGON — the Wonder.** At the roots, curled by the geode. The baby whose cave fills the more you learn: curiosity as the reward system, not fear of the gate. Anti-Skinner wearing scales.
4. **THE WEST CHARGE — THE SPIRAL STAIRCASE (ruled, 2026-09-12 night, three rulings deep).** The seat's biography: the vague smile arc first (ruled "clever — looks like an element symbol"), then the flat Fibonacci spiral (outside facing west), then the ruling: **"The spiral needs to look more like the staircase on the ad for the show Silo"** — a top-down gold helical staircase coiling down into a circular shaft, the outer mouth facing west. THE SPIRAL-STAIRCASE LAW: the west charge is the descent — the Inn's own trunk (the Staircase, the alchemical bridge between states) rendered as a helix with DOWN in it; the only charge on the shield you could actually walk into. Fibonacci stays the Geode Cave's Keeper — the flat spiral was the building's math; the helix is the building's math GIVEN STAIRS. The outside faces west, toward the setting sun — the day unwinding, the night law at 40 BPM, the spiral contracting, not stopping. The Silo visual borrow is honest key-art lineage; the philosophical rhyme taken lightly (their silo buries its truth; ours keeps the dead warm and the door open — same shaft, opposite philosophy). THE SMILE RETURNS TO THE ROOM: with the staircase taking west, Mona's smile is off the crest entirely — unfiled, humming, exactly as the saint prefers. Original pass-four reasoning kept below for the record.

4a. **(Superseded) THE SMILE — the Mystery.** Mona's smile, alone, no face needed. **THE ROCKY HORROR TOUCH (Krista's ruling, same evening): a slight theatrical lilt — the showman's knowing curve, the fourth-wall wink.** The saint in disguise, in on the show. The one thing the Archive left uncollected, rendered as the crest's honesty about itself: the filing stays humble. (Side note for the Living Line: the midnight-movie call-and-response circuit — rice thrown, lines shouted back — is itself a modern assembly of the old pattern: a place, a calendar, a rite, a roster. The smile knew.)

## THE MEANING, IN ONE LINE

Watcher, Memory, Wonder, Mystery — around a tree that is also a house: open doors, laws spoken aloud, and every seat at the fire earned by content.
