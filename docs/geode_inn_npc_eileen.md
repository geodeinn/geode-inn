# Geode Inn — NPC Design: Eileen
## The Hearth — Cave Painter, Art Teacher, The Presence

---

## The Person

Eileen. Came into Krista's world as a friend of River's who knew about rocks, looking for extra money at Soulstice. Brought four children (ranging nine to nineteen) the weekend before opening. Wavy blonde hair. A rare smile that puts an entire room at ease — the kind that drops everyone's guard, including her own.

Her shy daughter, who never approaches anyone, walked up to Krista and sat beside her on the cedar bench in the corner. Nearly on her lap. Eileen's surprise at seeing her daughter do this was the moment her own guard dropped completely. "When do you want me here to open up?" — asked with quiet certainty.

This began the most beautiful friendship Krista has ever experienced.

Eileen's magic: whenever someone enters the shop, one step within her direct presence puts anyone at complete ease. Her magic is an unconditional love available to anyone within range. If Krista were to compare her definition of what she perceives the energy of Jesus was, it would be this woman.

She is a teacher at a charter school — art and English. But her degree is in cave art. She is an incredibly talented artist in any media Krista has seen her use.
