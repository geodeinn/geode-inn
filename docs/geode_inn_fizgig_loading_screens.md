# Geode Inn — Universal Fizgig Loading Screen Specification
## The Inn's Comic Relief System Across All Zones

### Core Philosophy

The fizgigs are the Geode Inn's loading screen across ALL zones and contexts. They are small, industrious creatures in overalls — the Inn's maintenance crew, the metaphor for UI assembly itself. As the storyline deepens with the Silo integration, Shadow Inn (84Hz), and Whisper Layer, the fizgigs serve as essential comic relief to balance the narrative weight.

**The fizgigs are the one genuinely innocent element in the Inn.** Pure function, no agenda. Beverley knows. The Firekeeper and Catalyst are collecting. The Owl watches. The Reader understands. The Inn itself is aware. The fizgigs just wire. In a building where everything has a second meaning, the fizgigs are the one thing that means exactly what it says.

---

## Universal Fizgig Design

### Appearance
- Small (knee-height to a human), round-bodied, big-eyed
- Wearing overalls that are slightly too big — one strap always falling down
- Carrying wrenches that are comically oversized for their hands
- Tool belts with tiny hammers, screwdrivers, and a single spark of electricity
- Color palette: warm copper, brass, leather brown, with the occasional blue spark
- No two fizgigs are identical — varying heights, hat styles, mustache shapes

### Animation Principles
- Exaggerated effort for simple tasks (straining to lift a small bolt)
- Determined waddle-walk
- Tools spark occasionally — a small blue flash and a startled jump
- When working together, they form accidental Rube Goldberg chains
- Their expressions shift between concentrated focus, startled surprise, and proud satisfaction
- They never speak — all communication through gestures, grunts, and tool sounds

### Sound Design
- Tiny metallic clinking (wrenches, bolts, copper pipe)
- Occasional fizgig grunt of effort
- A single "eep" of surprise when a tool sparks
- No music — the fizgigs work in their own rhythm
- The ambient Celtic background music dips to 30% volume during loading screens

## Zone-Specific Loading Screen Scenarios

### 1. Main Floor — "The Welcome Wiring"
**Context:** Loading into the main floor / market area
**Visual:** Three fizgigs polishing the Inn's welcome sign. One is on a ladder (too tall, wobbling). One is on the ground holding the ladder with one hand and a wrench in the other (not paying attention to the ladder). The third is inside the sign itself, only legs visible, sparks flying out periodically.

**Animation loop (5-7 seconds):**
1. Ladder fizgig polishes, wobbles, steadies
2. Ground fizgig taps the ladder with wrench — "fixed"
3. A spark shoots out of the sign, inside-fizgig's legs kick wildly
4. Ground fizgig looks up, unconcerned
5. Repeat

**Copy (tabletop questline voice):**
> "The Inn's wires hum with welcome. The fizgigs see to it."

---

### 2. Catacombs — "The Scared Electricians"
**Context:** Loading into the downstairs / ancient civilizations level
**Visual:** Two fizgigs in hard hats with tiny flashlights. The flashlights flicker. One fizgig is visibly scared — wide eyes, clutching a wrench like a security blanket. The other is pretending not to be scared, walking ahead with forced confidence, but keeps glancing back. In the background, faint Celtic knotwork glows on the ancient stone walls — just enough to see by.

**Animation loop (5-7 seconds):**
1. Brave fizgig walks forward, flashlight sweeping
2. Scared fizgig shuffles behind, flashlight pointing everywhere at once
3. A low rumble (distant, ancient) — both freeze
4. Brave fizgig's hat slips, catches it without looking back
5. Scared fizgig's flashlight goes out — "eep" — Brave fizgig's light reveals scared fizgig has grabbed Brave fizgig's leg
6. Repeat

**Copy:**
> "Down where the old ones rest, even the fizgigs keep their voices low."

---

### 3. Staircase (Alchemy) — "The Oversized Beaker"
**Context:** Loading into the staircase / alchemical texts level
**Visual:** Four fizgigs struggling to carry a beaker of glowing liquid up the spiral staircase. The beaker is nearly as tall as they are. Liquid sloshes and a single drop falls out with each step. One fizgig is at the front pulling, two in the middle carrying, one at the back pushing. The back fizgig keeps getting splashed.

**Animation loop (5-7 seconds):**
1. Coordinated waddle upward, liquid sloshing
2. One step — the beaker tilts, a drop falls on back fizgig's head
3. Back fizgig shakes it off, keeps pushing
4. A bright spark from the liquid — all four freeze mid-step
5. Resume climbing
6. Repeat

**Copy:**
> "The staircase turns knowledge into gold. The fizgigs carry the ingredients."

---

### 4. Shadow Inn (84Hz) — "The Pot-Lid Shields"
**Context:** Loading into Shadow Inn / combat frequency mode
**Visual:** Five fizgigs in dark overalls (same design, darker palette — charcoal, dark copper, cold steel). They're in formation, hiding behind a shield wall — but the shields are clearly pot lids from the Inn's kitchen. One fizgig peeks over the top. Another is trying to sharpen a wrench on a whetstone (it's not working). The atmosphere is darker, the lighting is cold blue.

**Animation loop (5-7 seconds):**
1. Pot-lid wall holds steady
2. One fizgig peeks over — ducks back down fast
3. Sharpening fizgig tries wrench — it sparks — startled
4. Formation wobbles, one pot lid falls — the fizgig behind it is tiny, shaking
5. A low 84Hz rumble — all fizgigs grip their pot lids tighter
6. Repeat

**Copy:**
> "Even in the shadow frequency, someone has to keep the lights on."

---

### 5. Cosmology Level — "The Too-Short Ladder"
**Context:** Loading into the upstairs / cosmology level
**Visual:** Two fizgigs on top of a rickety wooden ladder trying to wire a constellation. The ladder is comically too short — they're stretching on tiptoes, one arm fully extended upward, the other gripping the ladder. The constellation they're wiring (a few connected dots) glows faintly above them. One fizgig is reaching with a wrench toward a star that's just barely too far.

**Animation loop (5-7 seconds):**
1. Both on tiptoes, reaching
2. Fizgig almost touches the star — the ladder wobbles
3. They freeze, balance restored
4. Second fizgig pushes first fizgig up by the boots
5. Star connects — brief golden flash — both fizgigs shield their eyes
6. Repeat with next star

**Copy:**
> "The cosmos takes its time. So do the fizgigs."

### 6. Fae Kingdom — "The Portal Mix-Up"
**Context:** Loading into the Fae Kingdom zone
**Visual:** Three fizgigs standing in front of a stone circle portal. They're holding a map upside down. One points at the map, one points at the portal, one shrugs. They look at the map, look at the portal, look at the map again. The portal shimmers green. One fizgig steps toward it, hesitates, looks back at the others.

**Animation loop (5-7 seconds):**
1. Map fizgig turns map right-side up — startled recognition
2. Portal fizgig points again, more confidently
3. Shrugging fizgig shrugs again, then notices something in the portal
4. All three lean toward the portal — it shimmers brighter
5. One fizgig's hat blows off in the portal wind
6. Repeat

**Copy:**
> "The old ways open for those who know the stones. The fizgigs know the stones. They just can't read maps."

---

### 7. Connection Screen — "The Solar Storm Response Team"
**Context:** Network disconnection / reconnection attempt
**Visual:** Fizgigs on a rooftop in a stormy sky. Tinfoil hats, wrenches raised, green aurora swirling above. Celtic knotwork gold frame around the screen. This is the most detailed fizgig scene — the emergency crew.

**Animation loop (continuous):**
1. Lightning flash — all fizgigs flinch
2. One fizgig adjusts tinfoil hat, determined
3. Another raises wrench toward the sky — a spark connects
4. Aurora green glow pulses around the frame
5. Repeat

**Copy:**
> "The Inn's connections waver. Somewhere above, solar wind scours the sky, and the fizgigs — those tireless little mechanics who keep the building's signals flowing — have taken to the rooftops with tinfoil hats and wrenches too small for the job. They are, as always, undaunted. Please wait while they work."

**Secondary line:**
> "Atmospheric interference detected. Reconnection attempt in progress..."

**Retry button:**
> "Send the fizgigs back up"

**Error state (full failure):**
> "The fizgigs have done all they can. The storm is too loud tonight. The Inn will be here when the signal returns — it always is."

**Reconnecting state:**
> "The fizgigs are re-soldering the sky..."

**Easter egg:** Clicking the fizgig in the loading screen 3 times opens the Connection Repair Screen as a hidden page.

### 8. Whisper Layer Trigger — "The Pause"
**Context:** Triggered ONCE per playthrough, at Whisper Layer Tier 3 (Whisper — NPC glitch awareness)
**Visual:** A single fizgig, mid-task, wiring something on a wall. The work is normal — the same determined effort as always. Then the fizgig pauses. Slowly, it turns its head and looks directly at the player. Completely blank expression. Not hostile. Not knowing. Not aware in the way the Inn is aware. Just... noticing. For exactly one second. Then the fizgig turns back to the wall and resumes wiring.

**This is the Inn's creepiest moment — because the fizgig ISN'T in on it.** It didn't receive a signal. It didn't glitch. It didn't break character. It just noticed you. The one creature in the building with no agenda, no awareness system, no second meaning — and it looked at you. What does that mean? The Inn never explains. The fizgig never does it again. No other fizgig ever does it.

**Animation (single play, never repeats):**
1. Normal fizgig wiring — 2 seconds of typical work
2. Pause — hands stop, body goes still (0.3 seconds)
3. Slow head turn toward player (0.5 seconds)
4. Blank stare — directly at the camera/player (1.0 second)
5. Head turns back (0.3 seconds)
6. Resume wiring as if nothing happened
7. Loading completes normally

**Sound:** All ambient sound cuts out during the stare. Complete silence. Then it returns.

**Copy:** None. No text. The loading screen continues as normal after.

**Technical note:** This should be a one-time trigger stored in PlayerProgress (whisperTier >= 3 AND fizgigPauseSeen = false). After it plays, set fizgigPauseSeen = true. Never play it again for that player.

## Technical Implementation

### CSS/Animation
- Each loading screen is a CSS keyframe animation loop (5-7 seconds, infinite)
- Fizgig characters rendered as SVG or CSS-drawn shapes (simple, scalable)
- Use `transform` and `opacity` for all animation (GPU-accelerated)
- The Whisper Layer pause is a JS-triggered one-shot animation, not a loop

### Color Palettes by Zone
| Zone | Background | Fizgig Palette | Spark Color |
|------|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| Main Floor | Warm amber | Copper, brass, leather brown | Blue |
| Catacombs | Dark stone | Copper, brass, leather brown (dimmed) | Amber |
| Staircase | Warm amber to gold gradient | Copper, brass, leather brown | Green (liquid glow) |
| Shadow Inn | Cold blue-black | Dark charcoal, cold steel, dark copper | Blue-white |
| Cosmology | Deep indigo | Copper, brass, leather brown | Gold |
| Fae Kingdom | Green shimmer | Copper, brass, leather brown | Green |
| Connection | Storm grey-green | Copper, brass, tinfoil silver | Green (aurora) |
| Whisper Pause | Normal zone palette | Same as current zone | None (silence) |

### Trigger Points
- **Page load:** Main floor loading screen
- **Zone transition:** Zone-specific loading screen (smooth fade, 1.5s)
- **Game start:** Game-specific loading screen (use the zone the game belongs to)
- **Connection loss:** Connection screen (solar storm)
- **Whisper Layer Tier 3:** One-time fizgig pause (overlays current loading screen)

### Frequency Behavior
- During normal loading: ambient music dips to 30% volume
- In Shadow Inn loading: 84Hz drone replaces ambient music (low, felt more than heard)
- Whisper Layer pause: ALL audio cuts to silence for the 1-second stare
- Connection screen: distant wind + tiny metallic clinking only

## Design Rules

1. **The fizgigs never break character.** They are always working. Even in the Whisper Layer pause, they were working — they just stopped for a moment.
2. **The fizgigs never speak.** All communication through gesture, effort, and tool sounds.
3. **The fizgigs are never scary.** The Whisper Layer pause is unsettling because of context, not because the fizgig does anything frightening. The fizgig's expression is blank, not menacing.
4. **The fizgigs are never in danger.** Even in the Shadow Inn, they're behind pot-lid shields — comically safe, not actually threatened.
5. **The fizgigs are never part of the awareness system.** They are the Inn's immune system — pure function. This is what makes them sacred.
6. **Each zone's loading screen reflects that zone's tone** — the catacombs fizgigs are scared, the cosmology fizgigs are ambitious, the Shadow Inn fizgigs are grimly determined — but the fizgigs themselves remain the same earnest creatures.
7. **The Celtic knotwork frame is present on ALL loading screens** — gold-on-dark-stone, subtle pulse animation, the Inn's visual signature.

---

*The fizgigs are the Inn's constant: the one thing that never changes, never deceives, and never stops working. In a building that is aware of you, they are the one presence that is simply... present.*
