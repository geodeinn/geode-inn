# THE GARNISH LAYERS — The Add-In Layer Ledger
## Status: PLANNED — deliberately deferred until core game design is complete
## Ruled: Krista, 2026-09-18

---

## THE LAW

The Inn's core game design comes first, all the way to finished. The living-contributor layers get added AFTER, as garnish — the fresh herbs at the end, never part of the base. This is deliberate sequencing, not neglect: a building that hosts living artists needs its rooms done before the housewarming.

## THE LAYERS (the ledger)

### 1. THE MODERN DAY GALLERY — undiscovered living artists
A gallery of real, undiscovered artists who CHOOSE to contribute their work. The living counterpart to the Inn's galleries of the anonymous dead — the anonymous canon's forward mirror. Not juried by fame; curated by the Inn (honest-label law: real artists, real attribution, their choice to hang).

**Natural first exhibitor (thread parked, not forgotten):** Aurallia. If her decades-silent work ever shows, this is the room it shows in — same rules as everyone else's: her choice, her timing, the character earns it first (the Living Story law, filed in the main floor weave draft).

### 2. THE AUTHORS' SHELF — living writers who contribute
Same law as the gallery, different medium. The Archive's dead masters gain a living annex: writers who choose to hang their words in the Inn.

### 3. THE WOODWORKERS' ROW — living makers of the object
Same law again, the craft register: woodworkers, artisans, pretty much anyone who makes a thing worth displaying. Overlaps the Artisan market's real-goods bridge (MarketItem/Artisan entities) but distinct: the market SELLS, the garnish layers DISPLAY.

### 4. OPEN SEAT — the category law
"Pretty much anyone who wants to display what they create." The ledger does not close. A new medium that wants in gets a row when it arrives, not before.

## THE HONEST LABEL, BOTH DIRECTIONS
Every garnish contributor is real, credited, and willing. Every display stays theirs — removable, never owned by the Inn. The Inn is the host, not the collector.

## SEQUENCING NOTE
No build work on these layers before core design completion. This doc exists so the layers survive distraction — the ledger is the memory, the garnish comes at the end, in its own season.
