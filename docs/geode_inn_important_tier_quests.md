# PART 2: THE CARTOGRAPHER'S FORM SIGHT
## Quest Chain: "The Invisible Architecture"

### Context
The Cartographer is a stoic, ink-stained archivist who maps the Inn's shifting Escher-style architecture. The Cartographer is Dutch, with a clipped, precise accent. The Cartographer's maps are not static — they update in real-time as the Inn shifts. The Cartographer's quest chain reveals Form Sight, the perception mode that lets the traveler see the Inn's structure as a tree.

### Form Sight (The Perception Mode)
Form Sight is one of three perception modes in the Geode Inn:
1. **Normal Sight** — the default. The traveler sees the Inn as rooms, corridors, zones.
2. **Form Sight** — the tree structure revealed. The traveler sees the Inn-Tree's architecture — the roots below, the trunk (staircase), the canopy (cosmology), the branches (cultural zones). The rooms are nodes on the tree. The corridors are branches. The zones are canopies.
3. **Mother Sight** — the mother creation principle. The traveler sees the 42Hz flowing through everything — the creative impulse made visible. (Unlocked through the Mona / Mother questline.)

### Quest 1: "The Shifting Map"
**Location:** Main Floor, near the Archive
**Giver:** The Cartographer
**Prerequisite:** 20+ stones collected + 5+ games completed

The Cartographer is at a drafting table, ink-stained fingers working on a map that keeps changing. The corridors redraw themselves. The rooms shift position. The map is alive.

**Dialogue:**
> "The Inn does not stay still. I have been mapping it for — [the Cartographer pauses, checks the map] — three cycles. The corridors move. The rooms rearrange. The stairs go to different places on different days."
> 
> "This is not a problem. This is ARCHITECTURE. The Inn is a tree. Trees grow. Trees shift. Trees add rings. The Inn does the same. My maps are not wrong. My maps are INTERIM. Each map is the Inn at a moment."
> 
> "You want to navigate. I can help. But you need to understand: the Inn is not a building. The Inn is a STRUCTURE. The structure is: a tree. The rooms are leaves. The corridors are branches. The staircase is the trunk. The catacombs are the roots. The cosmology is the canopy."
> 
> "I need you to carry a map. The map will update as you move. But the map only works if you understand what you're looking at. The map is not a floor plan. The map is a TREE DIAGRAM."

**Objective:** Take the Cartographer's Map (inventory item — key item). The map displays the Inn as a TREE DIAGRAM, not a floor plan. The player must navigate to 3 different zones using the tree map (instead of the normal minimap, which shows the floor plan).

**Mechanic:** The tree map replaces the minimap when equipped. The tree map shows:
- The trunk (staircase) as a vertical line
- The roots (catacombs/undercity) branching downward
- The branches (cultural zones) branching outward
- The canopy (cosmology) at the top
- The player's position as a glowing node on the tree

The tree map is DISORIENTING at first — the player is used to floor plans. The tree map shows RELATIONSHIPS, not directions. The player must learn to read the tree: "the catacombs are BELOW the main floor" becomes "the roots are BELOW the trunk" — a structural relationship, not a spatial one.

**Reward:** Tree Map Navigation (permanent). The player can toggle between floor plan and tree map. The tree map reveals shortcuts — paths between zones that the floor plan doesn't show. These shortcuts are ROOT PATHS — the player can travel between two zones by going THROUGH the root system instead of around the main floor.

**Location:** Various zones
**Giver:** The Cartographer

The visual transition is gradual — the surface fades and the structure emerges. When all three locations are activated, Form Sight is PERMANENT — the player can toggle it at any time.

**Form Sight View:**
When active, Form Sight overlays the tree structure on the normal view:
- The walls are translucent — the tree's vascular system is visible beneath
- The corridors are branches — the tree's growth patterns are visible
- The stones in the player's collection glow as nodes on the tree — each stone is a harmonic point

**Reward:** Form Sight (permanent, toggleable). This is one of the three perception modes. Form Sight reveals:

# PART 4: THE NORNS AND THE WEAVER
## Fate-Weavers of the Inn

### Context
The Norns — Urd (Past), Verdandi (Present), Skuld (Future) — are the Inn's fate-weavers. They are decoupled from the Queen of Webs. Each Norn occupies a different level of the Inn:
- **Urd (Past):** The Bone Room (catacombs) — weaves the thread of what has been
- **Verdandi (Present):** The Main Floor — weaves the thread of what is
- **Skuld (Future):** The Cosmology Level — weaves the thread of what will be

The Weaver is a separate entity — the master loom operator. The Weaver sits at the Inn's central loom (in the Undercity, at the root junction where the three primary roots meet). The Weaver takes the three threads from the three Norns and weaves them into the Inn's tapestry — the living fabric of the Inn's story.

### The Norns' Mechanic: Resonance Weaving

The Norns don't give quests in the traditional sense. The Norns WEAVE. The player's Resonance Profile is the THREAD the Norns work with. Each Norn reads a different aspect of the player's profile:

**Urd (Past) — The Bone Room:**
