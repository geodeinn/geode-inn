## Design Notes (Evan's Direction)
- Head is a COMPONENT socketed into the frame, not a person wearing a body
- No flesh visible anywhere except the eyes (amber glow)
- Lean frame, not bulky — narrow vertebral spine-column
- Six overlapping thin bone arms (mechadendrite-inspired, MTG stitcher)
- Dark hooded robe draped over the frame, open enough to show mechanical bone structure
- Amber marrow-glow is INTENSE — furnace-level, lighting from within
- Mood is dramatic and moody
- Reference: Adeptus Mechanicus tech-priest silhouette, translated to bone/amber
