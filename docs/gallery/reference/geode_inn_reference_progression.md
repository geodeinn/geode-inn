# The Progress of a Society — Spiral Gallery Reference Progression

## Spiral Gallery Architecture & Exhibition Concept

The **SPIRAL GALLERY** is the flagship art museum wing of the Geode Inn project. Designed as a continuous, gently ascending Guggenheim-style spiral ramp around a multi-story central glass atrium, the gallery organizes human visual culture chronologically across ten distinct **Ramp Rungs** (Levels 0 through 9). 

### Suggested Walking Order
1. **Bottom-Up Chronological Ascent**: Visitors enter at **Level 0 (Prehistoric Era)** at the base of the spiral and walk counter-clockwise upward along the main ramp.
2. **Dual Display Layout**:
   - **Outer Wall (Curved Gallery Wall)**: Houses two-dimensional murals, tomb frescoes, mosaic panels, and framed paintings.
   - **Atrium Balustrade Alcoves**: Houses freestanding architectural casts, relief metopes, caryatid supports, and carved stone columns overlooking the central courtyard.
3. **Pre-Industrial Scope**: The progression begins with Upper Paleolithic cave art (~36,000 BCE) and concludes with the High Renaissance summit (~1512 CE), terminating prior to modern industrialization.
