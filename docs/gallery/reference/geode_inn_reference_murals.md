## CRIMEAN GOTHIA — THE GOTHIC CAVE CITIES (added 2026-09-12, Krista's request)

The Crimean Goths — Ostrogoths who refused Theodoric the Great's march on Italy (490s) — became the longest-lasting Gothic people (3rd–18th c.). They carved entire towns into the limestone plateaus of southwest Crimea: Mangup-Kale (Doros, later the Principality of Theodoro, the last Gothic state, fell to the Ottomans 1475), Eski-Kermen, Chufut-Kale. Their rock-cut churches and cave monasteries carry medieval frescoes (notably Mangup's southern monastery). Two zone uses: the UNDERCITY's dwelling register made historical — people who lived IN the stone — and the guestbook law's descendant: donor figures painted into a cave church's walls.
