# Strip #011 — "Three Skins"
## The Geode Inn Gazette — September 20, 2026

**Zone:** The Norse Coast
**Characters:** The Viking King (three ages), The Traveler
**Art Style:** Rackham ink-and-watercolor with Klimt gold-leaf touches
**Canon Source:** The Viking King — Three Generations Painted + Shift Arc Woven (2026-09-19, commits 51a6369 + 1f35d79)

---

## PANEL 1 — The Setup

**Art:** Norse coastal cliffs at dawn. A boy, arms spread, leaning into the wind. Merlins dart past him like arrows.

**Caption:** *The boy wanted to be the wind. The merlins, who were the wind, found this adorable.*

---

## PANEL 2 — The Moment

**Art:** The warrior-king in his prime, mid-transformation. The berserk bear erupting through skin and armor. Foes scatter.

**Caption:** *The man became the bear. The bear did not ask permission. That's the thing about bears.*

---

## PANEL 3 — The Twist

**Art:** The elder-king, white-haired, by a fire. Still. The Arctur form — a great bear of stars — shimmering above him in the night sky.

**Caption:** *The old one does not shift anymore. The sky-bear shifts around him.*

---

## PANEL 4 — The Button

**Art:** A traveler at the fire's edge, staring up at the constellation. The elder sips from a horn. Calm.

**Caption:** *"What does the Arctur form do?" the traveler asked.*

*"It stays," said the king.*

*The young fly. The strong fight. The old learn the hardest shape of all — the one that holds.*

---

## Voice Check

- **Rothfuss precision:** "the merlins, who were the wind" — the craftsmanship of the compressed phrase
- **Pratchett warmth:** "found this adorable" / "That's the thing about bears" — humor without cruelty
- **Gaiman mythic-made-personal:** the elder sipping tea while his constellation-bear shimmers overhead — the sacred made domestic

## Design Law Woven

The Progressive Lean (the Shift Arc's three-step: given/earned/chosen). The Viking King is Proof Three after Mona and Egypt: youth = the given (flight, effortless), prime = the earned (the bear, fury paid for in scars), elder = the chosen (the staying, the hardest form, the one nobody has to take).
