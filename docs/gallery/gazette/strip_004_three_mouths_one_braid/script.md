# Gazette Strip #004 — "Three Mouths, One Braid"
**Published:** 2026-10-07
**Strip Number:** 4
**Zone:** The Bench — Vaulknut's Workshop
**Art Style:** Hobbit-warm (amber, gold, cream)
**Characters:** Vaulknut (the Selkie), The Traveler
**Player Adventures:** None — NPC feature (Vaulknut the Selkie). Commission Ledger seed #1: The Tri-Canid Weave (ruled 2026-09-14).

---

## Panel 1 — The Setup
**Caption:** "The traveler laid three teeth on the bench. A horse who ran, a wolf who hunted, a dog who stayed. 'Braid them,' he said. The selkie looked at the teeth. The teeth did not look back."
**Art:** Warm workshop, amber lamplight. Three teeth laid out on a wooden bench — a broad flat horse tooth, a curved wolf fang, a sturdy dog canine. A traveler's hand placing the last one down.

## Panel 2 — The Moment
**Caption:** "She held the horse tooth against the wolf's fang. The horse tooth was a wall. The wolf's fang was a curve. The dog's sat between them like a diplomat at a table that served two different dinners."
**Art:** Vaulknut leaning over the bench, studying the teeth up close. Wolf fang held beside the horse tooth, measuring with her eyes. Seal-dark hair, leather apron, work-worn hands.

## Panel 3 — The Twist
**Caption:** "The morning taught her what three predators already knew: they do not agree on a shape. The wolf's fang slipped twice. The horse tooth was too broad to braid. The dog's sat in the tray, unbothered, as if the whole project were someone else's concern."
**Art:** Vaulknut mid-work, tools scattered, half-woven braid with the wolf fang slipping out. The dog tooth sitting alone in a small wooden tray, content and patient. Comedic frustration.

## Panel 4 — The Button
**Caption:** "The braid held. Three mouths, one chain — what was never meant to match, matched anyway. The traveler paid in stones. The blessing was the ask. Some people carry three predators just to keep their own company."
**Art:** The finished braid — three teeth woven into one silver chain, held up to golden lamplight. The selkie's quiet satisfaction. The chain catches the light.
