# Gazette Strip #004 — "The Hound Who Stayed"
**Published:** 2026-09-15
**Strip Number:** 4
**Zone:** Main Floor — The Hearth
**Art Style:** Hobbit-warm (amber, gold, cream)
**Characters:** The Hearth-Hound, The Wild Hunt
**Player Adventures:** None — NPC feature. Inspired by the Hearth-Hound Legend canon (ruled 2026-09-13).

---

## Panel 1 — The Setup
**Caption:** "Every Inn needs a keeper. This one keeps by sleeping."
A great white hound with red ears asleep at the hearth, head toward the front door.

## Panel 2 — The Moment
**Caption:** "The hunt crossed the sky above the Inn, the way it does every winter. The cry sounds farther as it comes nearer. One ear moved."
The Wild Hunt races across the night sky. The Cwn Annwn's false-near cry. One ear of the sleeping hound twitches.

## Panel 3 — The Twist
**Caption:** "And one hound — just one — looked down at the lit windows. The door that never locks. It hesitated."
One hound from the pack breaks formation, hovers on the wind, looks down at the Inn's lit windows and the unlocked door.

## Panel 4 — The Button
**Caption:** "Home, not tamed. The Inn doesn't ask which kind you are. It asks whether you're staying."
Two white hounds with red ears curled up together asleep at the hearth.

## Notes
**Voice:** Pratchett warmth (panel 1), Gaiman mythic-made-personal (panels 2-3), Rothfuss precision in the button line. Played straight — the Child-Actor Law's spirit.

**Canon ties:** The Hearth-Hound Legend (2026-09-13) — guardians sleep facing the door; the Wild Hunt crosses the sky each winter; the Cwn Annwn's false-near cry (sounds farther as it comes nearer); the Inn's inversion — one hound looked down at the lit windows and the door that never locks, and stayed. Home, not tamed.

**What the strip says about the Inn today:** The Inn's oldest watch-law is belonging, not capture. The door that never locks is the whole thesis in one architectural choice.
