# The Spine and the Jellyfish

Strip #3 — 2026-09-13

Characters: Oceanianoir, The Distracted Sprite, The Beaker Sprite, The Chart Sprite
Zone: The Water Zone — Oceanianoir's Lab
Art style: Gazette house style — gothic anime (Vampire Hunter D palette), deep blues with bioluminescent accents

Player adventure: None — NPC feature (Oceanianoir)

1. The ruling came down on a current. Oceanianoir's chemistry was the spine now — every material thread in the Inn would pass through her lab.
2. Her crew took to the new mandate with varying dedication. The beaker sprite fetched. The chart sprite noted. The distracted sprite found something better than canon.
3. "You're documenting the sodium reaction." / "The sodium doesn't glow, Oceanianoir. This glows."
4. The spine holds the body upright, but the body also wiggles. She gave the sprite one more minute. The charts would keep. The jellyfish was going somewhere.

Voice notes: Rothfuss precision ("The ruling came down on a current" — the legal arrives as the physical); Pratchett warmth ("the body also wiggles" — the joke that the spine's job includes the wiggle it supports); Gaiman mythic-made-personal (the jellyfish as the thing more important than the mandate — the small wonder that justifies the lab). Played straight: Oceanianoir is a good boss, not a frustrated one. The sprite is genuinely captivated, not lazy.

Lore notes: THE SPINE LAW (2026-09-12 ruling — Oceanianoir's chemistry section is the curricular spine of all material threads, Project Bible Design Principle #8). THE SPRITES ARE HER LAB CREW (six slightly clumsy assistants — beaker duty, chart monitoring, one easily distracted by anything moving, an underwater dilemma). The strip canonizes the distracted sprite's defining trait and Oceanianoir's management style: the chart can wait, the wonder can't. Ties to the Shield Mixer (the sprites are the same crew) and the Hidden Materials series (the sodium reference is the Shield Mixer's flagship compound, NaCl).
