# The Postmaster’s Route

Strip #1 — 2026-09-12

Characters: The Postmaster
Zone: Main Floor / Bone Room Dock
Art style: Main Floor warm whimsical-macabre, 1929 rubber-hose register

1. The Postmaster had one route: from the Bone Room to the mail slot.
2. Today, the Inn gave him a camera blueprint. Front. Three-quarter. Side.
3. “And the back?” asked the Postmaster. “Reserved for walkers.”
4. He delivered the letter backward anyway. The Inn accepted it. The Postmaster accepted that a route is just a walk with paperwork.
