# THE DEFAULT TRAY REGISTRY — The Spiral Defaults
## Ruled in by Krista, 2026-09-13: bead trays ship with defaults — all the well-known spirals, from the triskelion to the labyrinth to the Fibonacci. Especially the logo's stepped Fibonacci: the dividing layers that sort tons of different patterns at once.

---

## THE LAW

The default trays are the world's spirals. Not arbitrary grids — the patterns nature already proved, each one a different way to bring order to a scatter of small precious things. A tray is a spiral you can hold; sorting is the hand walking the pattern. Every default carries: the pattern's real-world pedigree, its sorting thesis (what it's best at), its dip count, and where the Inn first shows it to you.

The master default is THE FIBONACCI STEPPED SPIRAL — the logo's own. Because its cells are the growth sequence itself (1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21), every dividing layer is a different size class: one tray, many sorts. Small beads find the small squares, statement beads find the big ones, and a mixed harvest sorts ITSELF into size bands as you pour. The pattern that grows by adding the last two sizes is also the pattern that sorts by them. (Fibonacci is the Geode Cave's Keeper — canon, the Spiral Heartbeat spec. The Keeper's tray is the default.)

## THE DEFAULT SET (six spirals + the maker's bench)

1. **THE FIBONACCI STEPPED SPIRAL** (the logo's) — 8 dips (1,1,2,3,5,8,13,21 squares, each a size class). The every-pattern tray: sorts by size, by count, by progression simultaneously. THE DEFAULT — unlocked at first pour, the one the tutorial hands you. Its quiet teaching: the sequence is already in the scatter; the tray just lets it show.
2. **THE TRISKELION** — the triple spiral, three arms from one hub; 4 dips (3 arms + center hub). Carved at Newgrange before anyone was keeping records — the Inn's original portal's own mark (the Tuatha canon ties here). Sorting thesis: three families and the shared center — mind/body/spirit stones, or the three waters, or any triad; the hub holds the one bead that belongs to all three. Unlocked with the Celtic Highlands / Stone Circles country.
3. **THE CLASSICAL LABYRINTH** — the unicursal seven-circuit spiral (Chartres/old Europe); 8 dips (7 circuits + the rosette center). THE ONE-PATH LAW: no wrong turns exist — one path in, the same path out, and every ring is passed on the way. Sorting thesis: the order-of-arrival sort; you sort by walking, circuit by circuit, and the center holds the finished pick. The meditative tray — the goblins' favorite (they never get lost in it, which they resent a little). Unlocked with the labyrinth country.
4. **THE GOLDEN SPIRAL** — the continuous one: no dips at all, a single deepening groove. The pour tray: loose mixed beads roll the spiral to the center, and the groove's narrowing does the separating (density and size arrive at the middle in order). The tray for the big unsorted harvest. Unlocked with the color works / the gold thread country.
5. **THE AMMONITE** — the logarithmic spiral in chamber plan; 9 dips, each chamber a step larger than the last, the way the real shell grew. Sorting thesis: gradient sorts — one stone family laid out across its whole size/color range, chamber to chamber, so the eye sees the drift. The fossil tray (the stone book's own shape); unlocked with the deep water / fossil country.
6. **THE SUNFLOWER** (phyllotaxis) — the many-spiral tray: 21 dips in two interlocking spiral families (each floret belongs to a clockwise AND a counterclockwise count). Sorting thesis: the community tray — same-size beads by the dozen, the layout that holds the most in the least; the pattern flower heads use to fit a thousand seeds with no crowding. Unlocked with the campgrounds gardens.

Plus, standing beside the defaults: **THE MAKER'S BENCH** — the blank Bristol-flat tray, no pattern, dips where the maker puts them. The tray that becomes YOUR tray (the Vaulknut precedent — Krista's own real-world tape-and-Bristol trays are the master reference; the tray-crafting system that lets players build their own layouts will spec its own session). The default set teaches the famous patterns; the bench honors the original method: tape, board, and the maker's own eye.

## LAWS THAT HOLD

No-Fail (no wrong dip — a stone in the "wrong" square is just an opinion the tray is holding); Steady Light (the trays' recessed lines catch the candlelight, no gloss-glare); Anti-Skinner (no sort timers, no bonus for speed — the sort is itself the fidget, the rest state, the hands' meditation); the REC law (each tray is one master; skins and tints ride free via the BoardSkin system).
