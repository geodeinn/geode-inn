# THE GEMSTONE EGG ECONOMY
## The spawn-based pet system. Its own economy, ruled by Krista 2026-09-10: separate from the story-grown seed companions and the Hatchery.
### "People get really focused on this type of spawn-based dynamic."  The design answer: honor that focus without weaponizing it.

---

## WHAT THIS IS (and what it is NOT)
The Inn's THIRD egg-adjacent system, and the only one that is an ECONOMY:
- The SEED COMPANIONS (the Catalyst's trade): bespoke, story-grown, one per story  the soul's pet. NOT this.
- The EGG HATCHERY: the community engine, retired characters, the vote  the Inn's pet system is NOT this either.
- **THE GEMSTONE EGG ECONOMY: the player's personal collection line. Eggs SPAWN as level-win rewards, hatch into Pokmon-class pets, and build a whole economy around the chase.** Its own system, its own rules, its own pull.

The thesis: the collection drive is real and good  the Inn's version keeps the FOCUS and refuses the exploitation. Spawn-based dynamics get people obsessed; the Inn's answer is to reward presence, patience, and mastery, never fear.

---

## THE SPAWN LAW (how eggs drop)
- **EARNED, NEVER BOUGHT.** Eggs drop from WINNING levels. No cash-for-eggs, ever (the no-pay-to-win law; the only real-world bridge is the physical one, below).
- **ZONE-THEMED POOLS.** Every game draws from its zone's egg pool (Tomb Tunnel hatches Egypt-zone pets; Andean Run hatches Andes; the parks' games hatch their own). The pool IS the geography lesson  the pet line doubles as the world map's living index.
- **RARITY TIERS** tied to the Stone entity's canon (the six frequency colors already rule the bracelet's frequency charms: amber/obsidian/red/teal/grey/green). Common hatch from any win; rare from high scores; legendary from the zone's deepest completions (100% chains, the permanences, the standstill-tier events).
- **SEASONAL SPAWN WINDOWS on the Beat Line.** Persephone's position changes spawn pools (the tide's own pet cycle); the aurora season hatches the north's rarest; the standstill is a once-a-generation spawn window. THE 18.6-YEAR EGG: a spawn window most players never see  the no-FOMO law at its deepest.

## THE HATCH LAW (how eggs open)
- **HATCHING RUNS ON PRESENCE.** No punishing timers. The egg knows you came back (the Ziggy return-count precedent): warmth accrues through presence and play  days present, not days elapsed. An abandoned egg never rots; it waits (the Inn's staying thesis, in shell form).
- **THE HEARTBEAT NEST.** The geode cave (the collection bar itself) gains a nest: the eggs sit in the player's cave, warming at 50 BPM. The pet hatches INTO the cave's collection  the geode fills with living things as well as gems (the original canon: the cave fills with gems upon level completion; now the cave also fills with what the gems become when they're loved).
- **SPECIES = STONE + ZONE.** Each pet's form grows from its stone's canon (mind + body  temperament + traits) in its zone's linework style. The Stone entity already holds the data; the pet is the stone PLAYED.

## THE ECONOMY (the whole point  handled honestly)
- **COLLECTION + COMPLETION.** The census drive (the index  the visual orthographic instinct made game): the egg-dex, a living index of every species, zone pools with visible completion states. Getting really focused is the FEATURE.
- **TRADING  GATE (the load-bearing ruling owed).** The Treetop Marketplace runs on NO CURRENCY (frequency-based, 50 BPM heartbeat trade). Does the egg economy trade? PROPOSED: eggs and pets trade at the Treetop on the frequency law (a trade must RESONATE  both parties' wants must hum at the same frequency; the market refuses pure grind-for-profit)  or the Inn keeps the pet line personal (no trading, the collection is the point). Krista's ruling.
- **THE VIVARIUM.** Pets live with the player (the cave, the homestead, the campsite)  the Don't-Starve building system gains the vivarium build tier. Pets have FUNCTION: the gargoyle pup guards, the tidal octopus watches water games, the rune beetle fetches archive hints  pets are helpers, not trophies.
