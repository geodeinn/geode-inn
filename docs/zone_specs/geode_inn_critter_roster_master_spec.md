# THE CRITTER ROSTER — Master Specification
## Every Creature, Every Level — The Ambient Life of the Inn

---

## WHAT THE CRITTER ROSTER IS

Every living creature in the Geode Inn that is NOT a named NPC. These are the ambient life — the critters that make each level feel inhabited. Each is tied to existing games, lore, or zone mechanics. Each operates at its level's frequency. Each has a job, even if the job is just "being there."

The critters are the Inn's immune system, its pollinators, its housekeepers, and its dreams. They are the evidence that the Inn is alive — not because NPCs walk through it, but because things LIVE in it.

## REMAINING LEVELS (3-10)

The full roster extends through every level:

- **Level 3 (Campgrounds):** Firefly wisps, wood sprites, raccoon-like scavengers, owls (the real ones — not the bloodline Owl), fox-like creatures
- **Level 4 (Canopy City):** Fizgigs (the boardwalk residents), cloud squirrels, leaf-surfing beetles, the Duck (Pinniped Pilferer)
- **Level 5 (Staircase):** Alchemical sprites — salamanders (fire), undines (water), sylphs (air), gnomes (earth). Each stage's element made small.
- **Level 6 (Catacombs):** Dust motes that are actually micro-fossils. Bone beetles. Shadow mice that are NOT Shadow Inn — they're just mice that live in shadows. The catacomb choir (tiny stone-vibration creatures that hum at 42Hz).
- **Level 7 (Undercity):** Crystal-shrimp in Mason's water troughs. Iron filing insects that follow Mason's magnetic chisel. The Undercity's mechanical ecosystem.
- **Level 8 (Water Zone):** Plankton-lanterns (bioluminescent micro-organisms at every layer's depth). The pressure fish (deep-zone only). Abyssal jellyfish (the Hadal's only light).
- **Level 9 (Fae Kingdom):** Fae-fireflies (not fire — frequency light). Mycorrhizal sprites (root-level trade network). Sprite-couriers. Bill the Platypus (the Crystal Gardens' impossible resident).
- **Level 10 (Cosmology deep):** Void Wisps (above). The Norns' threads (living thread-creatures in the Weaver's Loom Chamber).

## DESIGN PRINCIPLES

| Principle | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Each critter has a job | No critter is purely decorative. Even the Void Wisps — whose job is "being the negative space" — serve a narrative purpose. |
| Each critter operates at its level's frequency | The Cosmology critters pulse at 432Hz (the Caller). The Main Floor critters hum at 42Hz. The Water Zone critters carry the water's frequency. |
| Each critter is zone-appropriate in art style | Dalí critters are surreal. Van Gogh critters are textured. Gothic anime for water. Rackham for canopy. |
| The critters are the Inn's immune system | If the Inn is alive, the critters are its flora — the organisms that maintain the ecosystem. |
| Some critters connect to mechanics | The Mead Bees produce mead-honey (a consumable). The Ink Sparrows create map-fragments (a navigation aid). The Gravity Wells are collectible. |

Every critter resonates at its home level's frequency. The critters ARE the frequency made small — the same way the gargoyles are the frequency made large. The gargoyles are the macro; the critters are the micro. Together, they make the Inn hum at every scale.
