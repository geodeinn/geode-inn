## WHAT THIS ZONE IS

The Inn's Japan holds the river game everyone knows (the Kappa's toll, the Tengu's gusts) — and now its anchor: THE MOUNTAIN, the summer home of Belladonna. The Undercity below, the mountain above: the two homes of the Addams who lives between worlds — the deep stillness in the cold months, the summer height when the light comes. The Inn's second seasonal resident, and the symmetry is deliberate: PERSEPHONE rides down in autumn and rises in spring; BELLADONNA goes up in summer. The two cycles braid: when the below-world holds its winter guest, the mountain holds its summer one, and the Inn is never without a resident on holiday.

And the tell is the zone's own name: DEADLY NIGHTSHADE BLOOMS IN SUMMER (real botany — Atropa belladonna flowers June through August, berries blackening at the season's end). The mountain's namesake garden blooms exactly when the resident is home. The rest of the year, the bare stems stand quiet, and the locals know: the shrine is empty until the belladonna says otherwise.

## THE SHRINE (really lovely — as ruled)

The shrine at the summit grove, and it is beautiful the way mountain shrines are beautiful: the long path up through the bamboo and cedar, the torii gates spacing the climb like measures in a score, the purification fountain at the threshold, the honden's aged wood and quiet gold, the view from the top where the world goes blue with distance. The mountain keeps itself (the below-court's law, echoed above): no resident priest, no guardian NPC — the shrine has stood for eons the way the mountain has, and its upkeep is the mountain's own work. The fox statues at the gates wear their centuries well. There are offering boxes, and something is always left in them. No one sees who leaves it.

The resident's touch, in season: belladonna planted along the shrine's south face — the lovely danger, tended with an Addams's precision (the berries counted, the paths kept clear of them; the garden's beauty and its warning are the same gesture). And the single tell of the homecoming: the first bloom opens the day Belladonna arrives. The mountain's summer begins when the nightshade says so.
