## ENTERING THE FAE KINGDOM (two doors, both live)

**DOOR ONE — THE STONE CIRCLES (from the world map).** Four surface portals: Stonehenge, Avebury, Callanish, Ring of Brodgar. The first portal wakes when the player holds a Preseli Blue Stone — Standing Stones' reward, earned in hour one at the Arcade. The player who plays the Tetris-class game first arrives here months before the player who saves it, and both are correct. (The Inn never orders the journey. It doors it.)
- **Stone Circle Resonance** (the Threshold's portal-key game): tuning the circle's stones to the player's note. Each portal has its own chord; the player learns the circles are older than the Inn that grew around them.

**DOOR TWO — THE FAR DOOR (from below).** The Stone Circle Chamber's summer-and-winter door, opened from the Fae side first. The player who descended to the Source in Dissection 06 arrives here WET — the only players in the Kingdom who entered from underneath, and the fae notice the water. "You came the long way," the Threshold's gatekeeper says. Not approval. Recognition. (The same word the Host used at the staircase. The Inn's vocabulary is small on purpose. Every repetition is a root.)

**AESTHETIC LAW:** Arthur Rackham holds the Kingdom (ink + watercolor). Alan Lee holds the Seelie court, John Bauer the Unseelie, Escher the Between. The player crosses between three linework grammars in one zone — the visual DNA's deepest showcase, and the player feels the shifts as weather, not as style change.

Six sub-zones. Every option live.

### OPTION 1 — THE THRESHOLD
The entry meadow where the circles root. Fae here are shy — chibi sprites, half-glimpsed, the zone's tutorial-by-ambush: nothing attacks, things REVEAL.
- **Play Stone Circle Resonance** (all four chords; the game that keeps the doors open)
- **Meet Bill the Platypus** (the Threshold's honest resident — the only fae who says exactly what it means, every time. The Kingdom's north star for players learning which court can be trusted how far. The fae's rule of speech is: true, never honest. Bill is both, and the other fae find it disconcerting.)
- **Watch the portals.** Other travelers arrive while the player stands there. The Inn's community, visible mid-journey — the only surface-level place where players watch players enter a place.

### OPTION 2 — THE SUMMERLANDS (the Seelie Court)
Alan Lee's line: gold-green, high summer, the court where the light is generous. Riddle-quests, not combat. Reputation builds here in warmth.
- **Take a Seelie riddle-quest.** The court's contract system: the Seelie never lie and rarely help. Every riddle is true, and the reward is always more interesting than the riddle suggested — the Seelie pay in AGENT: favors that unfurl later, in other zones, weeks of play later. (The fae economy: kindness with a delay.)
- **Play The Faerie Quest** (the SMW-class platformer — the Summerlands' own cartridge)
- **Play The Dream Gardener** (the SMB2-class — Dream Quartz is grown here, not won; the class-quest's sleep-chapter)
- **Earn the rare stones.** Labradorite, Fluorite, Rainbow Moonstone — the Kingdom's rewards, unobtainable from surface games. The collector's reason to be here, made honest: the deep stones live in the deep country.

### OPTION 3 — THE HOLLOW (the Unseelie Court)
John Bauer's line: black-ice, winter, the court where the dark is honest. Combat-reputation builds here. The Unseelie Queen's presence presides — gown of woven frost, the raven-feather cape (permanent canon, every rendering), the black-ice crown.
- **Petition the court.** The Unseelie's speech rule inverts the Seelie's: they may lie, and they tell you so, and their honesty about their dishonesty is the most reliable thing in the Kingdom. Petitions cost a truth the player hasn't told anyone. (The Hollow's entry fee is one secret, freely given — the only cost the Inn ever names before the door, because the dark is honest about its prices.)
- **Meet Malachi, the Unseelie King.** Malachi's court-lore is its own archive (Labyrinth of Crete spec — Malachi the architect, the love letter written in walls, Asterion's sanctuary). Malachi's dialogue runs on the Unseelie rule: Malachi never says anything untrue about the player — only unwelcome truths, delivered with perfect courtesy. (Bowie is craft reference only; the character is Malachi's own.)
- **The Hollow's trial.** The Unseelie quest-line: the winter trial, combat-or-endurance, the court testing whether the player MEANT to come dark or only wandered in. The Morrigan's shapes from Dissection 04 are recognized here — the roost and the Hollow share a fence.

### OPTION 4 — THE BETWEEN (the borderlands)
Escher's margin: the shifting borderland where the two courts blur — stairs that climb to the court they came from, doors that open on whichever side needs them less.
- **Play The Between (#63).** The margin-walk game: the player is a lost resident of one court trying to get home through the other court's country, and THE DOOR HOME ONLY OPENS BY HELPING — each act of aid widens the door, each hoarded shortcut narrows it. No failure state; the unhelpful player simply keeps walking, and the walking is the lesson. (The game's thesis IS the borderland's law: the Between is crossed by those who carry something for someone else.)
- **Meet the Norns.** Escher's geometry bends around three weavers at the margin's center — the ones who braid the Between's paths. The Norns do not riddle; they OVERHEAR, and answer questions the player hasn't asked yet. (The Whisper Layer's oldest relative. The player is being woven about, and the weaving is kind.)
- **Find Asterion's thread.** The Labyrinth's resident reaches the Between through cracks — Asterion's thread trails here, and following it backward leads (eventually, via #62 The Thread's own dissection) to the Labyrinth of Crete. The Kingdom's back door to the Mediterranean. The bull-shaped resident's sanctuary is the Inn's gentlest lore, and the Between is where its thread surfaces.

### OPTION 5 — THE CRYSTAL GARDENS (the neutral ground)
No court claims the Gardens. Crystal grows in court-lit silence: half the formations summer-warm, half winter-cold, none of them throne.
- **Play The Crystal Paddle** (the Arkanoid-class — Beryl's game; the gardens' crystal is the playfield, and the Griffin watches from the centerpiece formation)
- **Face the Griffin.** The Gardens' boss — the only creature in the Kingdom that belongs to no court and answers to no queen. The fight is optional, repeatable, and honest: the Griffin's health bar is the only one in the Inn that shows BOTH the player's stones AND the player's unanswered question count. The Griffin fights harder against players who hoard answers. (The Inn's combat philosophy in one boss: what you carry into the fight IS the fight.)
- **Harvest crystal.** The Artisan's fae-tier materials, grown not mined — the Garden of Gold's cousin, and the fae price is always the same: something given, freely.

### OPTION 6 — THE STONE CIRCLE CHAMBER (the deepest point)
Twelve Preseli Blue Stones in a circle — the same circle the player touched from below at the Source, now entered from above. The water system and the fae system meet at the roots, and this room is the meeting.
- **Hear the deepest tone.** The Stone Circle Chamber produces the deepest tone the Inn ever produces — and the player who stood here wet from Dissection 06 hears it now dry, and the tone is the same, and the player understands the two deepest doors were always one room.
- **THE GOLDEN CONTRACT.** Hidden here: a golden scroll on a Preseli Blue Stone pedestal. The master storyline, verbatim: **"The Song shall not be silenced. All who learn it shall be bound to sing it forward. The Song is not owned — it is carried."** The gargoyle frequencies are the signatures. The player who has learned the Song — and every player has, by now, without being taught — reads the Contract and understands the Inn's whole copyright philosophy as a story object: the Song is carried, not owned. Sovereignty, made myth. (This is the Share Wood shield, the MP3-of-images principle, the patent-first paperwork law — the business canon — grown here as lore, so the business and the world say the same thing in the same room.)
- **SIGN IT.** The player's option, freely chosen: add a signature. Not ink — the player's hatching-note, hummed once at the pedestal. The Contract records the frequency, and the player's note joins the gargoyles' on the scroll. Bound to sing it forward. The only contract in the Inn with no penalty clause, because carrying the Song is its own enforcement: you cannot unlearn your own note. (Full circle to the Source: the player signed with the same frequency the Egg hummed back.)
- **THE CHAMBER BENEATH.** Below the Contract's pedestal, sealed: the vault beneath the vault, the room beneath the room, the silence beneath the Song. The player can hear it — 30ms of the Guest's silence, once per heartbeat. It does not open from here. It opens from the Cosmology dissection, which is where the three choices live, and the Chamber is the silence they will be chosen in. The bomb under the table, final placement: the player now knows the Inn has one door left, and that it is beneath the deepest thing they have read.

## THE CONVERGENCE — CARRIED, NOT OWNED

Every court, walked every way, teaches one law in six dialects: nothing here is owned — the Seelie's delayed favors, the Unseelie's priced honesty, the Between's kindness-keyed doors, the Gardens' given crystal, the Chamber's carried Song. The Fae Kingdom is the Inn's sovereignty philosophy made a country, and the player leaves having signed the only contract that ever mattered: carry it forward.
