# THE SCIENCE SIDEKICK LAW — Master Specification
## Critter companions for the science-bearing quest-givers: information sticks when it's carried by something you love.
### Drafted 2026-09-14 from Krista's ruling: "a set of science-y sidekicks to make the science side stick better. School of Rock and Sesame Street, though meant for kids, had this down to a science."

---

## WHAT THIS IS

Every science-bearing quest-giver in the Inn gains a CRITTER SIDEKICK — a small companion whose behaviors ARE the lesson. The references (TONE REGISTER ONLY — the principle is borrowed, never the characters): **Sesame Street's law** — the character embodies the concept (the one who counts is the one you count along with), and learning arrives by OVERHEARING, explained to the small curious one while the player listens in; **School of Rock's law** — fun is not the reward for learning, it is the VEHICLE; the curriculum is smuggled inside the thing they already love doing. Both shows proved the same thesis: retention runs on affection. The Inn already believes this (the honest-core law, the Whimsical-Macabre warmth); this spec aims it at the curriculum.

**The proof of concept already exists:** the six sprites at Oceanianoir's Shield Mixer bench — the beaker sprite's fetch, the chart sprite's field notes, the distracted sprite's permanent delight. The lab crew IS the sidekick law, running since before it had a name. This spec generalizes the bench to the whole building.

---

## THE FIVE LAWS OF THE SIDEKICK

1. **THE OVERHEAR LAW.** The NPC explains to the SIDEKICK, not to the player. The sidekick asks the questions the player is too proud to ask ("but WHY does it hiss at water?") — and the player learns by eavesdropping. Curiosity is licensed by the small curious one; nobody is lectured, everybody is included. (Sesame's deepest device: the student surrogate.)

2. **THE EMBODIMENT LAW.** The sidekick physically IS its science. Not a mascot wearing a theme — the behavior demonstrates the concept. Orbiting IS the orbit lesson; the dusting IS the preservation lesson. The Count law: the character and the concept are the same body.

3. **THE HONEST-FAILURE LAW.** The sidekick fails visibly, warmly, and often — wrong answers performed as normal by something beloved. (The distracted sprite precedent: every reaction is motion, and the distracted one cannot look away.) No-Fail holds: the sidekick never shames, never scores, never keeps the player's mistakes on file.

4. **THE CENSUS LAW (Species Law compliance).** Every sidekick is a NAMED INDIVIDUAL of an existing census species — never a new paint line. The class master already exists; the sidekick is one member of it with a name and a job. Ambient = pet = sidekick: one census, three jobs now. (Session canon rule 6 rides along: the zone isn't done until its sidekicks are cast.)

5. **THE PAIRING LAW.** One sidekick per science-bearing quest-giver — the Quest Mark's companion. The glow marks the giver; the sidekick carries the curriculum. Where the mark says "talk to the face, not the menu," the sidekick is the reason the face is worth talking to.

**REPEATED WITH VARIATION (Sesame's repetition law):** the same demonstration runs every visit — never the same twice. Tiny variations keep the tenth viewing as warm as the first. Anti-Skinner absolute: no streak for watching, no counter, the repeat is comfort, not compulsion.

## FIRST ROSTER PROPOSALS (from the existing census — every one a named individual of a living species)

- **OCEANIANOIR'S BENCH — the six sprites (standing, already canon):** the proof case. The lab crew's duties stand as specced; this law names what they were already doing.
- **THE EXAMINER (the dig sites, archaeology) — a bone beetle who dusts.** The specimen-brush work embodied: the little one dusts every find with ceremonial care, and the Examiner narrates WHY — preservation, context, the find's neighbors matter as much as the find (the stratigraphy lesson smuggled inside a beetle's vanity about its work).
- **THE CLOCKMAKER (horology, the 32,768Hz wing) — a chrono-tick.** Time made visible, wearing a tiny monocle of its own insistence. The wing's teaching example already walks in loops; the named one is the metronome the Clockmaker never needed and always wanted.
- **THE ORRERY'S KEEPER (Cosmology) — a star sprite who orbits the player.** The embodiment law's purest case: the lesson IS orbits, the sidekick orbits YOU, lands on your shoulder at periapsis, and the Overhear Law asks why it only lands when you stand still.
- **THE ZOOLOGIST (the water census, the hunt) — a plankton-lantern.** Bioluminescence embodied: the little light that follows the census-taker, demonstrating that the deep's light is communication, not decoration (Steady Light's own deep-water child).

**Naming:** every sidekick's name is a gate for the maker (the Cian precedent — the names travel better said out loud). The roster scope is a gate: these five first, or a different first five.

## GATES (the Witan's)

1. The roster scope — the five proposed first, or Krista's own order.
2. The names — each sidekick's name is the maker's ruling.
3. The Overhear Law's register — full eavesdrop mechanic (dialogue written to the sidekick) vs. ambient flavor (the sidekick demonstrates, the NPC narrates without direct address)?
4. ✅ THE SPRITES' ANONYMITY — RULED (Krista, 2026-09-14): "The crew keeps its anonymity. They will probably get named by the players." The lab sprites stay unnamed BY THE MAKERS — the naming right passes to the community. THE FOLKLORE RULE: whatever the players come to call them IS their name (the emergent-naming precedent, per the folk-history canon: resident-led records are the primary representation). If a player-given name sticks hard enough, the Witan may canonize it — but the crew is born free of paperwork, and the building's only residents named by nobody official. The anonymity IS the charm.
