## WHAT THE APPALACHIAN ZONE IS

The Appalachian Range is 300 million years old — older than the Himalayas, older than the Rockies, older than most living things. The range has been eroding for longer than most mountains have existed. The Appalachians are the earth's PATIENCE — the mountain becoming a valley, slowly, over geological time, without complaint.

The zone is structured as FIVE LAYERS — five strata of Fae, each representing a different era of the range's life, from the deepest bedrock (the Gnomes, who remember when the mountains were taller than the Himalayas) to the surface canopy (the Sylphs, who live in the wind that has worn the peaks down). Each layer is deeper, older, more patient.

**Access:** Main Floor → World Map → Appalachian Range pin
**Prerequisite:** Mediterranean Zone Quest 5 (The Bearsark's border crossing)
**Art Style:** Thomas Hart Benton (the muscular, flowing landscape style — earth as movement frozen in form)

## THE ZONE'S THESIS

The Appalachian Zone is the Inn's meditation on PATIENCE as a geological, biological, and spiritual force. The mountain is not fighting. The mountain is not surrendering. The mountain is BECOMING — slowly, over 300 million years, wearing from a Himalayan-scale peak to a gentle ridge. The Fae in each layer represent the mountain's own consciousness of its transformation.

The zone's design principle: erosion is not loss. Erosion is the mountain's gift to the valley. Every grain the wind carries east becomes soil somewhere else. The mountain is not being destroyed — it is being DISTRIBUTED. The mountain is becoming everywhere.

|-------|-------|-------------|

The stone's frequency is: the slowest vibration in the Inn. The stone vibrates once per year. |
