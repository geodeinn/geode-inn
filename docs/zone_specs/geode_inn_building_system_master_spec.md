# THE BUILDING SYSTEM — Master Specification
## The Forest as Playable Layer — Resource Gathering, Crafting, and Town Formation

---

## WHAT THE BUILDING SYSTEM IS

The forest surrounding each cultural zone is a playable resource layer where players gather raw materials from the local geology and ecology, then build progressively — from a simple yurt to a castle to whatever they can imagine. Player-built structures naturally aggregate into towns that become trade hubs on the Silk Road.

The building system mirrors the tree's vertical architecture: the forest is the canopy's raw material, the buildings are the trunk's expression, and the stone foundations are the roots. The gathering is the reaching. The building is the becoming. The town is the belonging.

---

## THE SEVEN BASE MATERIALS

Every building is made from combinations of seven base materials, each gathered from the zone's natural environment:

| Material | Source | Stage | Inn Connection |
|----------|--------|-------|----------------|
| **Wood** | Trees (fell, split, carve) | Nigredo (raw shelters) | The present tense — the material that is alive. The Gardener's communion applies (ask the tree; the tree may offer a branch instead of falling). |
| **Stone** | Geological features (quarry, mine, collect) | Citrinitas (refined stonework), Rubedo (monumental) | The future memory — the material that remembers. Medusa freezes things in stone. Subtractive architecture (cave cities) IS stone — the negative space remaining once stone is removed. |
| **Clay** | Riverbanks, lakebeds (dig, knead, fire) | Albedo (first transformation) | The first transformation — the material that teaches alchemy. Clay grows when you work it. Wet becomes hard. The transformation is: the alchemical principle in material form. |
| **Sand** | Rivers, beaches, deserts (scoop, sieve, wash) | Citrinitas (glass), Rubedo (concrete) | The gap between — the material that fills the space between stones, between what you have and what you need. The In-Between is: the sand of the Inn. |
| **Ore** | Veins, riverbeds, caves (mine, pan, smelt) | Citrinitas (metal reinforcement), Rubedo (monumental metalwork) | The hidden strength — the material that sleeps in stone and wakes in fire. Erik's fire wakes the ore. The Artisan Thief casts the ore. Klimt gold leaf from ore. |
| **Hemp** | Cultivated (plant, grow, harvest, ret, spin, weave) | All stages — the universal connector | The quiet revolution — the material that holds everything together. Rope, fabric, paper (archive books), oil (lamp fuel, waterproofing). The Inn doesn't argue; the Inn uses it. |
| **Thatch** | Wetland plants (cut, bundle, weave) | All stages — the roof | The universal skill that makes everything possible. The roof is the difference between a structure and a home. The thatch is the Inn's first welcome. |

The seven base materials combine through fire, water, and skill:

| Mortar | Sand + Clay + Water | All | Binding agent for stone walls |
| Wattle-and-daub | Wood + Clay | Nigredo/Albedo | Walls |
| Fired brick | Clay + Fire | Albedo | Walls, floors, roofs |
| Glass | Sand + Fire | Citrinitas | Windows, decorative elements, Silica Thread |
| Smelted metal | Ore + Fire | Citrinitas | Hardware, tools, reinforcement |
| Bronze | Copper ore + Tin ore + Fire | Citrinitas | Hardware, weapons, decorative |
| Roman concrete | Sand + Clay (lime) + Crushed stone + Water | Rubedo | Foundations, domes, monumental |
| Gold leaf | Gold ore + Fire + Hammering | Rubedo | Surface finish (Klimt aesthetic — gold on stone) |

The derived materials are the crafting tree. The player learns one material, then learns what happens when materials meet. The meeting is the alchemy.

## THE GATHERING LOOP

1. **OBSERVE** — Walk into the forest, observe what's available (zone's geology and ecology determine materials)
2. **LISTEN** — Listen to the material source. A tree's frequency tells wood quality. A stone deposit's resonance tells hardness. Clay's tone tells plasticity. The listening is the asking.
3. **GATHER** — Gather at 50 BPM (the heartbeat tempo). Living materials require the Gardener's communion (the player asks the plant; the plant may offer a branch instead of falling). The Crone measures quality: "Adequate" or "Inadequate."
4. **CRAFT** — Process raw material at a workbench. Wood → planks. Stone → blocks. Clay → bricks. Ore → ingots. Hemp → rope. Thatch → bundles.
5. **BUILD** — Build using the five-tier alchemical progression
6. **NAME** — Name the structure. The naming is the saving mechanism. The named structure appears on the Cartographer's map.
7. **SIT** — Sit in the finished building. The 42Hz hum becomes audible. The building is home.

## THE FIVE BUILDING TIERS

| Tier | Alchemical Stage | Materials | Time | What You Build |
|------|-----------------|-----------|------|----------------|
| 1. Shelter | Nigredo (Iron/Raw Stone) | Wood, hemp, thatch (+ optional clay) | 1 session (2-3 hrs) | Yurt, lean-to, wigwam, tent — the first roof |
| 2. House | Albedo (Copper/Wire-wrapped) | + Clay, sand, early ore, fired bricks | 3-5 sessions | Cottage, longhouse, stone house, adobe, workshop — the first home |
| 3. Hall | Citrinitas (Silver/Set Stone) | + More stone, sand, ore (iron/silver), hemp, thatch | 7-10 sessions | Mead hall, market stall, workshop, chapel, library, smithy — the first community space |
| 4. Castle | Rubedo (Gold/Forged) | 200+ stone blocks, 150+ planks, 200+ clay, 100+ ore, 100+ hemp, 100+ thatch | 20-30 sessions (a month+) | Castle, tower, temple, fortress, palace, great hall — the landmark |
| 5. Whatever the Player Dreams | Philosopher's Stone (Translucent Crystal) | All materials, no constraints | Indefinite (never finished) | ANYTHING. Glass bridges, crystal towers, living structures, submerged halls, floating platforms, buildings that are instruments, buildings that are songs |

## TOWN FORMATION

When enough player-built structures aggregate, a town forms:

| Structures | Level | What Happens |
|------------|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Homestead | Single structure on the map. Cartographer notes it. |
| 3-5 | Hamlet | Cluster visible. NPCs begin visiting. Paths form between structures. |
| 6-10 | Village | Appears on Silk Road map as a trade stop. NPC vendors. Erik brings communal fire. The mead begins. |
| 11-20 | Town | Gets a name (chosen by builders). Cartographer maps in silver. Market square forms. Artisan Thief workshop. Bard collects gossip. |
| 21-50 | City | Gets a charter (from the Host). Cartographer maps in gold. Major Silk Road hub. Zone guides visit. The Crone comes to measure. |
| 50+ | Wonder | Cartographer maps in translucent crystal. Becomes a landmark on the scale of the five hubs. The Inn has grown. |

|---------|------------|-------------|

| Market Square | Village | Trade stalls. Players trade materials, items, stones. Silk Road connects. |
| Workshop | Village | Shared crafting space. Better tools than personal workbenches. |
| Inn | Town | A player-built Inn within the town. The Host visits: "Room's ready." |
| Archive | Town | Player-built library. The Examiner visits. Books shared. The town's memory. |
| Garden | Town | The Gardener plants. Singing vines grow. 42Hz hum becomes audible. |
| Bone Room | City | The Bone Keeper visits. A quiet room. The town's stillness. |
| Gargoyle | City | One of six gargoyles takes residence. Frequency protects the town. |
| Music Room | Wonder | A composer may visit. The Song is heard in the town. |

## ARTIST STYLE

The building system uses the zone's assigned artist (the zone where the player is building determines the visual style). The buildings transition between styles at zone borders — the same blending used in the World Grid. Gold leaf (Rubedo tier) uses Klimt's gold technique. Crystal construction (Philosopher's Stone tier) uses no-outline cosmological style.
