The map is never finished. That is not the map's failure. That is the map's subject.

### Quest 2 — "The Cartographer"

Everything costs frequency — stones, songs, favors, seeds, secrets, time — and the quest is the player's first trade, conducted honestly: something given that cannot be bought (a stone from the player's own collection, a song the player has actually heard, a secret the player has actually kept), received by the canopy at its true price: the relationship. **Reward:** The trade-right (the player's standing in the OTHER economy: the frequency prices legible forever; a stall in the marketplace's player rows).
