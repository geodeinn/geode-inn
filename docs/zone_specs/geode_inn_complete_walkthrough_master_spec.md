## THE 50 BPM HEARTBEAT

The Inn beats at 50 BPM. This is not a design choice — it is the Inn's fundamental law. Originates from the underground altar where crystal and root merge.

Propagates through: the altar (source) → the Inn's stone structure → the lighthouse beam → the Pirate's bodhrán → the Arcana's gearwork heart → the starlight crystal → the cabbit's purr → the Pulse Line (the visible ECG line at the bottom of every screen).

50 BPM is the carrier wave; 42Hz is the content. The previous cycle ran at 41.7Hz. The current cycle runs at 42Hz.

---

## THE PULSE LINE (ACCESSIBILITY)

A thin ECG line at the bottom of the screen, pulsing at 50 BPM. Warm amber-gold, 1-2 pixels, ~65% opacity. The Inn's vital sign, not a UI element.

Modes: baseline, warmer (approaching a node), colder (moving away), nook (deep work state), combat (second line syncs), arcana (mechanical tick-tock), cosmology (crystalline + cabbit warmth).

Three toggleable modalities: Audio, Haptic, Visual. Minimal Mode strips all to simplest forms. Silent Mode: audio off, haptic off, visual only. The entire frequency system navigable through vision alone.
