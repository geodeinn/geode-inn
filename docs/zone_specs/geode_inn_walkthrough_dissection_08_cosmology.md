## THE COSMOLOGY'S LAYER LAW

Downstairs is history. The main floor is the present. The Cosmology is the only level that is a QUESTION — the upstairs does not add rooms; it adds unknowns. Its linework is the cosmological mode: NO OUTLINE (the visual DNA's law — things that predate edges). Its light is Turner's: skies that are weather happening to color. Its architecture is Dalí's: time bent until it cooperates.

The level's thesis: every zone below answers something. The Cosmology asks. And its six questions (the Astronomer's) are the walkthrough's whole spine — every dissection so far has been a measurement in a ledger the player hadn't seen yet.

**THE ASCENT LAW:** the player arrives from the Alchemical Staircase (D03) with the Complete Tuning — the full-octave, 12-semitone chord built on the climb. The Cosmology is the only level that HEARS the player coming. The chord precedes the player like a name announced at a door.

## THE SUB-ZONES (every option, before continuing)

### 1. THE VOID — 0Hz, the silence between the Song's notes
No floor, no walls, no artist — the Void is the one zone with no rendering because it is the zone where rendering rests. Nyx holds it: primordial goddess of night, Doc Martens, Alanis Morissette energy — the silence between the notes, not their absence. THE FRAME, not the canvas.

- **The Guest (Death) is here.** Thanatos on holiday — guest upstairs, host downstairs (the Underworld's pairing, the Charon loop's other end). Carries the music box: the mechanism that plays Death's original note. The silver harmonica is the Guest's; the music box is the Inn's. The Borrowed Body quest (the music box mechanism) and Nyx's Silence quest (the ability to hear silence between notes) both live here.
- **THE REST (the Void's game, spec complete):** the holiday zone — the game that rewards stillness. The Guest assists exhausted "stuck" notes in the Song to reach graceful conclusion, using 0Hz stillness as a tool. The anti-game: no failure state, only unfinished rests. (The Void is the only zone where doing nothing is the mechanic.)
- **THE META-NARRATIVE (canon, from the walkthrough audit):** the Constant compresses dying universes; Nyx is the Shifting Mound — the container. The Starlight Crystal holds the compressed Songs: previous cycle 41.7Hz, current 42Hz. The Void is where the last cycle's question is still faintly asking.
- **DOWNWARD TIE:** the Abyss (water system Layer 7's dark extension) is NYX'S downward extension — the Void and the water system's deepest point are the same darkness from both ends. (D06's source point and D08's silence point are one room with two doors.)

### 2. THE OBSERVATORY — 42Hz + starlight, the level's mind
Dalí's mind-palace architecture; Turner's light in the lenses. Armillary spheres, brass pocket watches, the Astronomer (Sherlockian polymath, plays violin to think) — and the Allfather, cosmic form, silent ravens perched, watching the void from the hull throne. The Wanderer is the main floor's warm Odin; the Allfather is the same god, upstairs, at cosmic scale — the dual-identity law (canon) rendered in one room's distance.

**THE SIX QUESTIONS (the level's spine, quest chain complete in the important-tier docs):**
1. What is the Song? — measured from six harmonic stones. The Song IS, not merely sounds.
2. When did the Song begin? — a Resonance Crystal from the Hollow Earth's Engine base: ~4.5 billion years. The Song is Earth-old.
3. Who is singing? — a stone carried 20+ hours: the Song is ALL voices, passed voice to voice, folk-song style. (The mother poplar is the first singer — Da Vinci's Mona, one floor down, still humming.)
4. What is the Song for? — a Whisper Layer stone: the stones are the ARCHIVE. The 42Hz encodes the player's experience onto crystal. The collection was always the library.
5. Can the Song end? — the Complete Tuning (D03's octave) measured: the chord is STABLE. STAY and LEAVE are both viable. The Inn can survive the singer's departure.
6. WHEN? — the question the Astronomer refused to complete, because the question was more beautiful than the answer. The answer: now. The answer is always now. The answer is always adequate. **The Chronometer of the Final Note** is the reward — the device that marks the moment the player chooses. The third of four Ragnarok pillars.

The Astronomer's arc ends the level's inquiry: pen down, ledger closed, violin picked up. "I don't want to know what you'll do. I want to hear it." The Astronomer carries the violin to the Ascension Canopy and hands it to the traveler. The calculation is someone else's now.

- **The Orrery (Rubik's-class):** the planet-gear puzzle — the system's orbits solved by hand.
- **Resonance Break (Arkanoid-class):** starlight bricks, harmonic rebounds.

### 2B. THE SIGNAL DOCK — the DDLC-adapted meta-narrative game (game #7)
The small craft at the top of the Cosmology — the highest dockable point of the Inn. The console flickers on when the player approaches; someone left it warm and lived-in. **THE SIGNAL** is the Inn's most psychologically daring game: a spacecraft exploration adventure (5-6 hours, one playthrough, no loop — it escalates once) that breaks its own fourth wall. The game knows the player is playing it. The characters know the player is there — as a separate entity from the avatar.

- **The design template:** Doki Doki Literature Club — not jumpscare horror; the horror of being SEEN. In every other zone the Inn's awareness is ambient (the Whisper Layer, the pulse anomalies, the feeling of being watched). In The Signal, the awareness becomes DIRECT. The Archive stops watching and starts talking.
- **THE WATCHER THREAD'S PAYOFF:** this is where "the Inn always watching" (the Owl, the Masquerade, the Whisper Layer, Memory's Illusion's trusting river) stops being foreshadowing and becomes address. The player is no longer watched. The player is KNOWN.
- **The loadout law:** the player's Bead Board composition IS the ship's frequency loadout — each equipped stone becomes a ship system (navigation, shielding, communication, scanning). The collection was always the equipment.
- **The five acts:** the Launch (comfortable space game, faint anomalies — a star blinking in morse, a log that says "Welcome back") → the Crew (beings of frequency and light, older than the Fae) → the escalation: the game's awareness climbs act by act, ending in direct address.
- **Entry requirements:** Cosmology unlocked + 30+ stones + 3 zone frequencies attuned on the Bead Board.
- **Full spec:** docs/library/specs/the_signal_ddlc_spacecrafts_spec.md (complete). Roster: standalone product #7, meta-narrative category.

### 8. THE ASCENSION CANOPY — the endgame assembly, 42Hz → silence
Rackham's crown of the tree — the canopy above the Cosmology where the endgame gathers. The Egg, the Violin (handed up by the Astronomer), the Chronometer of the Final Note, and the three choices:

- **STAY** — play the complete Song, take the crystal, become the new Constant. The Inn continues with the player as its heart.
- **LEAVE** — play silence. The stones complete one by one; the player descends the staircase in reverse (Rubedo → Albedo → Nigredo) and walks out the front door into morning. The Inn continues without the player.
- **HIDE** — play the Song, then a silence the Inn can't record. The Nest is built inside the Song — a life in the space between frequencies. The Inn continues, but doesn't know it carries something unrecorded.

The thread through all three: **"The Inn continues. The ravens fly. The adequate is enough."** The choice the Astronomer wouldn't make, the Guest's music box finally played, the Chronometer marking the moment the player decides — because the Song ends when the player chooses. Not before. Not after. When.
