## WHAT THE NINE MUSES ARE

The Nine Muses form the metaphysical patron system for all artistic pursuits within the Inn. Situated on the Cosmology Level (the highest structural layer), they manifest as non-corporeal entities of pure celestial light. Artistic pursuits are not aesthetic decoration — they are essential vibrational channels that stabilize the Inn's physical reality and shield inhabitants from chaotic frequency erosion.
