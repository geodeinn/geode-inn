## THE QUEST CHAIN STRUCTURE

Each Muse has a 3-quest chain:

| Quest | What Happens |
|-------|-------------|
| Quest 1: The Hearing | The player hears the Muse's frequency for the first time. A nook on the Cosmology level begins to glow. |
| Quest 2: The Instrument | The player retrieves or restores the Muse's signature instrument from the Spiral Gallery. The corresponding Spiral Chamber is restored. |
| Quest 3: The Performance | The player performs with the Muse's instrument in the Music Room. The frequency is permanently unlocked. The Muse's nook fully brightens. |

Completing all 9 quest chains = the Full Spectrum — the complete harmonic architecture of the Inn, all frequencies unlocked, the Music Room fully operational.
