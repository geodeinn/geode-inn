## PART ONE: THE SPIRAL ROCK FAMILY (the Newgrange entrance style, scattered game-wide)

The kerbstone spirals  the triple spiral of Newgrange's great entrance stone, the oldest signature the Inn has  scattered through the Campgrounds first, then everywhere, in four sizes:

1. **BENCHES**  spiral-carved sitting stones (the spirals carved where the shoulders meet the stone; a bench that holds you the way a carved thing holds warmth).
2. **NOOKS**  the spiral as the Inn's mark for "a place to be": any alcove, corner, or hollow marked with the carved spiral is a sanctioned quiet spot (the Decomp Room's sign, the architecture's own Margin Note).
3. **PEBBLES**  pocket-sized spiral stones, found anywhere (the collectible register  found, not bought).
4. **TOKENS**  THE SPIRAL TOKEN, the carved triskele: the passage mounds' own coin. Leave one in a mound's heart chamber and it stays (the guestbook signature, the Irish cousin of the Norse Coast's runes-as-guestbook law  the player's mark, carved and left, for no reason except having-been-there). Tokens are never currency; they are presence (the Adequacy Law).

## PART TWO: THE LITTLE NEWGRANGES (passage mounds, explorable, scattered)

Small grassy mounds  a carved kerbstone at the entrance, a short stone passage, a heart chamber holding ONE thing (a stone, a token bowl, the zone's own resonance). One per area, the Campgrounds first: the mini-henge's megalithic sibling (the sisters above ground, the passage below).

**THE LIGHTBOX (the mechanic  the real site's own magic):** Newgrange's roof box admits the winter solstice sunrise down the full passage into the chamber  once a year, one dawn. THE INN'S VERSION: every little Newgrange has its own alignment day and hour. On that day, standing in the heart chamber, the light enters and crosses the heart stone  the zone's own solstice, kept. The player who learns the alignments (the Cartographer can teach them) collects the Inn's light moments  the quietest collectible in the building: nothing is taken, the light is WITNESSED (the Colorado Plateau's law: seen, not collected; photographed, not kept).

## PART THREE: THE SITE SHORTLIST (other underground places to explore  proposed, sources worn openly)

1. **DERINKUYU'S ROLLING DOORS (Cappadocia, Turkey  on the master list, #10):** the compact maze  a few small rooms, ventilation shafts, and THE ROLLING STONE DOOR, full physics: the player pushes it shut and the maze goes dark and quiet (the No-Demand Law's underground cousin: the door the player closes is the room the player owns). The real site: eighteen levels, doors that still roll after three thousand years. The Inn's: three levels, one door, and the satisfaction of stone meeting stone.
2. **MAESHOWE (Orkney, Scotland  proposed):** the winter-solstice passage tomb where Vikings broke in and CARVED RUNES ON THE INSIDE  the real-world living proof of the Norse Coast's runes-as-guestbook law. A Neolithic tomb with Norse graffiti is the Inn's whole thesis in one chamber: every generation signs the stone. The spiral token's bowl sits in Maeshowe's heart.
3. **SKARA BRAE (Orkney, Scotland  proposed):** the stone village with FURNITURE STILL IN IT  stone dressers, stone beds, cupboards, after five thousand years. The Campgrounds' HOMESTEAD gets its stone-furnished nook: the little cottage where everything is part of the building (the Don't-Starve build system's oldest ancestor, visitable).
4. **THE HYPOGEUM OF HAL-SAFLENI (Malta  on the master list, #11):** the Oracle Room  the carved chamber where a voice at the oracle hole fills the room (the real site's 111Hz resonance  the gargoyles' own frequency, already canon). The Inn's version: THE WHISPER ROOM  the underground chamber where a whisper spoken into the hole is carried to the whole mound (the Whisper Layer's acoustic heart, and the Clarity Mix Law's one sanctioned exception: here the ROOM is the instrument).
5. **THE EAR OF DIONYSIUS (Syracuse, Sicily  proposed):** the cave the shape of an ear, with acoustics so sharp a whisper at the bottom reaches the top  the acoustic amphitheater (if this is the amphitheater Krista means; the ruling hers). One chamber, one voice, the whole cave listening.
6. **THE SUN LODGE KIVA (Chaco Canyon  on the master list, #26):** the kiva with the sipapu, the small hole in the floor marking the place of emergence  the Inn's upstairs/downstairs cosmology in one small room: the hole that is a door.
