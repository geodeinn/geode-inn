# THE DRINK MECHANIC — Master Specification
## Story-Specific Beverage Architecture & Social Systems

---

## WHAT THE DRINK MECHANIC IS

Within the Inn's acoustic-crystalline architecture, drinking is not a survival mechanic or a stat-buff placeholder. Beverages are LIQUID ACOUSTIC CATALYSTS — substances brewed, steeped, or distilled to resonate with specific vibrational layers of the Inn's 42Hz prime frequency.

The Inn's central gathering space is Thorin's Meadery — a massive mead hall constructed inside the overturned hull of an ancient Norse longship. The keel forms the vaulted spine of the ceiling, the curved timber ribs frame warmth-radiating stone archways, and massive oak tables host travelers, scholars, Fae royalty, and wandering gods.

---

## THORIN'S MEADERY

Managed jointly by Thorin (the master brewer and stone cataloger) and Marijke (the sharp-eyed logistician and keeper of the Inn's registry). The Meadery is the narrative and social anchor for the entire beverage system.

Every zone has a beverage stall. Every culture has a drink. The Meadery is where they all come together — one longship hull, one long table, every drink the Inn can pour.

## CORE MECHANICS

| Mechanic | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Primary Resonance Buff | Each drink applies a tangible mechanical benefit — movement speed, combat stability, puzzle aura expansion. |
| Perception Layer Shift | Drinks alter visual shaders, ambient lighting, and acoustic filtering — exposing hidden world elements: Dream Layer, Water Connections, Hidden Cosmology, Whisper Layer Audio, Rune Traces, Shadow Deceleration. |
| Pulse Line Interaction | Drinks modify the bottom UI ECG Pulse Line — smoothing to 42Hz, dampening 84Hz surges, or holding steady during In-Between shifts. |
| The First Drink Ritual | The first time a player consumes a new beverage, a 4-6 second stylized first-person animation plays — preparation and presentation in the zone's cultural aesthetic. |
| The Drink Journal | Every drink is catalogued with tasting notes, NPC quotes, and sommelier completion tracking. |

## SOCIAL SYSTEM

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Multi-Stage Camaraderie | The more a player drinks WITH an NPC, the more that character strips away defensive facades — granting access to high-tier story secrets and emotional vulnerabilities. |
| Free Tier | Choices-matter branching dialogue tree for structured conversations over drinks. |
| Premium Tier | Real-time conversational AI for unstructured, deep discussions with NPCs over drinks. |
| The Nook Effect | When drinking in a Nook (the Inn's ambient contemplative spaces), the acoustic beverage resonance combines with the Nook's warmth to produce the quietest, most powerful contemplative state in the Inn. |

## ZONE BEVERAGE STALLS

Every cultural zone has its own drinks:
- Norse (Erik's Mead Stall): traditional meads, skaldic ales
- Greek: wine mixed with olive and herb
- Egypt: date palm wine, barley beer
- Celtic: heather ale, elderflower cordial
- Slavic: birch sap wine
- India: spiced milk, saffron tea
- China: rice wine, jasmine tea
- Peru: chicha de jora, coca leaf tea
