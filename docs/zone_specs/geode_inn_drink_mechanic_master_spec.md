# THE DRINK MECHANIC — Master Specification
## Story-Specific Beverage Architecture & Social Systems

---

## WHAT THE DRINK MECHANIC IS

Beverages served throughout the Inn function as liquid acoustic catalysts — substances brewed, steeped, or distilled to resonate with specific vibrational layers of the Inn's 42Hz prime frequency. Drinking is not survival, not stat-buffing, not roleplay flourish. Drinking is perception-shifting and relationship-building.

The Inn's central gathering space is Thorin's Meadery — a massive mead hall constructed inside the overturned hull of an ancient Norse longship. The keel forms the vaulted ceiling spine, curved timber ribs frame stone archways, massive oak tables host travelers, scholars, Fae royalty, and wandering gods. Managed by Thorin (master brewer and stone cataloger) and Marijke (logistician and keeper of the Inn's registry).

## THE CAMARADERIE SYSTEM

Multi-stage social intimacy through shared drinking:

| Stage | What Happens | What Unlocks |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| 1: The First Drink | The NPC shares a surface-level story. Formal, guarded. | Basic dialogue tree — public persona |
| 2: The Second Drink | The NPC's guard loosens. Anecdotes become personal. The NPC mentions a private detail — a fear, a memory. | Middle dialogue tree — personal anecdotes, mild vulnerabilities |
| 3: The Third Drink | The NPC strips the facade. The real story emerges — the secret, the wound, the thing the NPC doesn't tell anyone. | Deep dialogue tree — high-tier story secrets, emotional vulnerabilities, quest hooks |
| 4: The Shared Flask | The NPC passes their own flask. This is the final stage — the NPC treats the player as a confidant. | Full dialogue tree — everything the NPC knows, including things that change the game's political landscape |

The Crone's third drink reveals the notebook entries. The Pirate's third drink reveals the compass's origin. The Bone Keeper's third drink reveals why the mead hall was built (remembering the flood). The Bard never reaches stage 4 — the Bard shares everything at stage 1 because the Bard doesn't have a filter.

## THE DRINK JOURNAL

Each drink is cataloged in the Drink Journal — a tasting-notes system:

| Field | Content |
|-------|---------|
| Beverage Name | The drink's name |
| Zone of Origin | Where it's brewed/served |
| Flavor Profile | Taste description in the Inn's narrative voice |
| Frequency | The drink's hertz value |
| Perception Unlocked | What the drink reveals |
| First Served By | Which NPC served the player this drink first |
| Quote | The NPC's line when serving the drink (each drink has a unique server quote) |
| Pairing | Which nook the drink resonates with (Nook Effect synergy) |

The completed Drink Journal = the Sommelier Achievement — the player who has tasted every zone's beverage and experienced every perception layer.
