# THE MONSTER OF THE DEEP — Master Specification
## Nessie & 32 Water Monster Game — "You Are Not the Observer. You Are the Observed."

---

## WHAT THE MONSTER OF THE DEEP IS

The arcade game where the player IS the water monster. Not a hunter, not a survivor — the creature itself. The game teaches empathy through embodiment: you swim as Nessie through Loch Ness, experiencing the loch from below. The humans on the surface are small, distant, and occasionally terrifying (they have boats and cameras).

Entry point: The Loch Pool — an arcade cabinet on the Main Floor near the Lighthouse, styled as a Victorian diving bell with a brass-rimmed screen. "Monster of the Deep — Loch Ness Division. You are not the observer. You are the observed."

## CORE MECHANICS

| Mechanic | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| Swim | 8-way directional movement |
| Dive/Surface | Hold to change depth (depth meter on left) |
| Sonar Pulse | 42Hz pulse — reveals hidden objects, stuns small fish (2s cooldown) |
| Breach | Swipe up from deep to surface — Nessie breaches (bonus points, scares tourists, camera flashes) |
| Camera Dodge | When boat spotlight sweeps, hold still to avoid detection (or breach intentionally for "Sighting" bonus) |

## LEVEL STRUCTURE

| Layer | Depth | What's There |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| Surface | 0-10m | Boats, tourists, camera flashes, noise. Bright, risky. Breaching earns Sighting bonuses. |
| Mid | 10-50m | Kelp forests, fish schools, moderate visibility. Most stones here. |
| Deep | 50-200m | Dark, cold, bioluminescent. Rare stones. Other water monsters appear. |
| Abyss | 200m+ | Pitch dark. Sonar only. Deepest stones. Most frequent monster encounters. |

---

## DIFFICULTY

Non-lethal (the Inn's design rule — you don't die, you forget). If Nessie is hit by a boat propeller or caught in a strong current, the player "forgets" some progress and respawns at the surface. No game-over screen. The screen dims, the Whisper plays, and Nessie is back.

---

## PROGRESSION

- Each dive awards stones (added to the Stone entity / geode cave)
- Cumulative depth milestones unlock new water monsters as playable characters
- The "Sighting Count" (total breach points) unlocks Lighthouse entry and the Water Zone

---

## THE 32 WATER MONSTERS

All 32 water monsters from the bestiary are playable characters, each unlockable through depth milestones. Each monster has its own loch/biome and unique swimming mechanics.
