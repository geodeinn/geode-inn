# MONSTER OF THE DEEP — Master Specification
## The Game Where You ARE the Monster

---

## WHAT MONSTER OF THE DEEP IS

The player IS the water monster, not a hunter, not a survivor — the creature itself. The game teaches empathy through embodiment: you swim as Nessie through Loch Ness, experiencing the loch from below. The humans on the surface are small, distant, and occasionally terrifying (they have boats and cameras).

32 water monsters, each playable, each with unique mechanics tied to the region's mythology. The game is the underwater layer's arcade expression — the entry point to the 7-layer water system.

**Entry Point:** The Loch Pool — an arcade cabinet on the Main Floor near the Lighthouse area. Styled as a Victorian diving bell with a brass-rimmed screen. Plaque: "Monster of the Deep — Loch Ness Division. You are not the observer. You are the observed."

---

## CORE MECHANICS

| Control | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Swim | 8-way directional movement |
| Dive/Surface | Hold to change depth (depth meter on left side) |
| Sonar Pulse | Tap to emit 42Hz pulse — reveals hidden objects, stuns small fish (2-second cooldown) |
| Breach | Swipe up from deep to surface — Nessie breaches (bonus points, scares tourists, camera flashes) |
| Camera Dodge | When a boat spotlight sweeps, hold still to avoid detection — or breach intentionally for a "Sighting" bonus |

**Non-lethal design (the Inn's rule — you don't die, you forget):** If Nessie is hit by a propeller or caught in a current, the player "forgets" some progress and respawns at the surface. No game-over screen. Screen dims, the Whisper plays, respawn at surface.

## LEVEL STRUCTURE

Each play session is a single dive. The loch is procedurally generated within parameters.

| Layer | Depth | What's There |
|------|-------|-------------|
| Surface | 0-10m | Boats, tourists, camera flashes, noise. Bright, noisy, risky. Breaching earns Sighting bonuses. |
| Mid | 10-50m | Kelp forests, fish schools, moderate visibility. Most stones are here. |
| Deep | 50-200m | Dark, cold, bioluminescent. Rare stones. Other water monsters appear. |
| Abyss | 200m+ | Pitch dark. Only sonar reveals anything. Deepest stones. Most frequent monster encounters. |
