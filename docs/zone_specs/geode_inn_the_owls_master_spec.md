## WHAT THE OWLS ARE

The Owl is the sovereign watcher of the Geode Inn — a full-presence supernatural character modeled as a barn owl, woven into the Inn's ecology, sonic architecture, and underlying narrative spine. The Owl operates as a silent sentinel across three operational frequencies: 42Hz (Living Inn), 84Hz (Shadow Inn), and 0.3Hz (Cosmic Void).

The core axiom of the bloodline magic: "Wherever an owl is sighted, one of us is near." The Owl is neither pet nor spirit animal. It is the animal counterpart to the Allfather's watchers (Huginn/Muninn), the living ocular sensor of the Whisper Layer, and the physical link between the mortal traveler, the ancient bloodline, and the cosmos above.
