## WHAT THE STORYLINE ATLAS IS

The Storyline Atlas is the narrative MAP of the Geode Inn — every major storyline traced from its highest point (the cosmology/skies) to its deepest point (the sleeping giants/the Egg), showing every character, quest, and connection point along the way.

Each storyline is a VERTICAL THREAD — it runs through multiple layers of the Inn, connecting characters and zones that seem distant but are structurally linked. The player encounters each thread at different points in their progression, and the threads interlock: a character met on the main floor connects to a quest in the Undercity, which connects to a mechanic in the Cave City, which connects to the endgame in the geological depths.

## HOW TO READ THIS ATLAS

For each thread, the atlas traces:
- **Skies** — where the thread starts (cosmology level)
- **Descending layers** — how the thread moves DOWN through the Inn
- **Connection Points** — where this thread touches other threads
- **Giants** — where the thread ends (the deepest point)
- **Player Impact** — how the player experiences this thread

The Atlas is NOT a walkthrough. It is a MAP. The player experiences the threads in NON-LINEAR order — encountering a thread's middle before its beginning, a thread's end before its middle, and connecting threads through play rather than sequence.

## THE CAMPAIGN NARRATIVE

The Campaign Narrative documents the Inn's story from PROLOGUE to EPILOGUE:

**Prologue: The Murmur** — Before the Song, there was the Murmur. Not sound — the potential that exists before frequency. The Cosmic Egg is the Murmur made solid. The Constant carries the starlight crystal (41.7Hz, the ghost of the previous universe) through Nyx's void, seeding the next cycle. The Astronomer calculates the Song's progression (41.7 → 42Hz, +0.3Hz per cycle) but doesn't want to complete the calculation. "The question is more beautiful than the answer." Nyx IS the silence between the notes — without her, the Song would be undifferentiated drone. The Guest (Death on holiday) holds the music box — the note Death MADE, not caught, creating Nyx's silence in portable form.

The Campaign Narrative moves through all 10 levels of the Inn, weaving the 13 threads together. Each level introduces new threads and advances existing ones. The key structural principle: the deepest threads (the Murmur, the Egg, the sleeping giants) are the FOUNDATION. The highest threads (the Song, the Silence, the cosmology) are the EXPRESSION. The building goes UP from the Murmur. The player goes DOWN to find it.
