# THE COMEDY TROUPE REGISTRY
## The Inn's Mood-Lighteners: one census of everyone who makes the building warmer by being funny
### Written 2026-09-11, drafted in the creative chaos's freedom. Census law: every member is existing canon, wired to their home spec. The registry binds them so the humor stays load-bearing instead of scattered.

---

## THE TROUPE LAW

The Geode Inn is funny ON PURPOSE, and the humor is never decoration. Every troupe member carries weight: a system, a thesis, a comfort law, or a quest. The comedy is how the Inn keeps its warmth tactile  "the friend who makes you laugh while the storm is still outside the door." If a joke ever carries nothing, it gets a gate or it goes. THE NORTH-STAR TEST applies to the whole troupe: the humor makes the Inn feel like Elio feels in it  at home. Comfort first, depth underneath, both true at once.

## THE PACING LAW (how the humor breathes through the building)

Comedy has a REGISTER per zone, the way music has a key. Where the Inn is heaviest, the humor is quietest  not absent, QUIET: a small joke in the Catacombs lands harder than a big one on the Main Floor, and it is supposed to. The Campgrounds get the broad comedy; the Archive gets the literary kind (the joke in the margin of a footnote); the Cosmology level gets almost none  and that's WHY one deadpan line there (Naut's) is the funniest in the game. Comedy is pressure: the troupe is deployed like instruments, and the zone's artist linework sets the funny's visual voice.

## THE SILENCE LAW (the troupe's deepest rule)

Comedy that never stops cannot be trusted when it stops. The troupe earns its silences: the coconut shells going silent when the circuit completes  the first full stop in the entire game  is the heaviest beat in the building precisely BECAUSE the brownies were funny for fifteen levels first. The humor is load-bearing; that means when it pauses, the building holds, and everyone feels what the floor was carrying. No comedic character is ever stripped for a dramatic moment. They pause. They return. The rhythm restarts  "the game's whole argument in one restart of a rhythm."

## THE HEARTBEAT RHYTHM LAW

The troupe's physical timing belongs to the parent frequency: the Knight's hops land on the 50 BPM heartbeat; Pluck dances to the player's tempo; the pups' yips carry a bell's decay; the coconut polyrhythm is the parent hum's smallest, busiest child. The comedy is never out of time with the building  even the pratfalls are on the pulse.

## THE TROUPE'S TEACHER  THE TRAMP (added 2026-09-11, Krista's call: "a nod to one of if not the best creative minds in theater who is always just considered the little tramp but was a genius")
**The tribute:** Charlie Chaplin  the little tramp the world remembers, the genius it forgets (wrote, directed, starred, composed; rehearsed one bit three hundred times and made it look like the first take). Reference blueprint only, all assets original. THE STANDING ART LAW HOLDS: the face is softened, never rendered  the presence lives in the posture (the waddle with the turned-out boots, the cane twirl, the hat tip that means four different things), the costume (the bowler, the cane, the boots a size too big  the silhouette AS the homage), and the precision. THE CHARACTER'S ANSWER TO "ALWAYS JUST CONSIDERED THE LITTLE TRAMP": the silhouette is the disguise; the precision is the being.

**The role (the acting school made canon):** the troupe's TEACHER  the physical school's master class. The Comedy Troupe's members take the fundamentals from the Tramp: comedy is precision (the 300-take law: if it looks simple, it was rehearsed until it was), comedy is tenderness (the laugh that follows the character's heart, never the character's fall), and comedy is TIMING  every bit lands on the 50 BPM heartbeat, the Heartbeat Rhythm Law's living proof. Gesso attends from the back row, arms crossed, having learned everything and admitting nothing.

**THE PLAYER'S CLASS (the Gathering Law's missing curriculum):** the Tramp is the Inn's EMOTE TEACHER  the player's gesture instructor. The Gathering Law needs emotes; the Tramp teaches them: the bow, the tip of the hat, the shrug, the dance step, the offered arm. The lessons are SILENT  the Silent Law, the troupe teacher's own: the Tramp is the Inn's only entirely nonverbal quest-giver. Every instruction is a bit; the player reads the body; the posture IS the sentence. No dialogue tree has ever rendered the Tramp a word, and none ever will. The player's reward for each mastered gesture: the emote unlocks game-wide  the acting class IS the mechanic.

**The home (the road's own resident):** THE TRAMP NEVER CHECKS IN. An inn is defined by who stays; this one is also defined by who keeps walking through it. The Tramp lives on the ROADS  the Silk Road's walker, met between platforms (the zone where the walk itself is the law). The Tramp appears at the long stretches, walks with the player a while  teaches one gesture, shares one bit, accepts a meal standing  then takes the other fork at the junction with the wave from the bend, and the road keeps the rest. The staff never comments on this. The players always do. The Tramp always comes back through, because the road that remembers a walker is home, and every road the Inn touches remembers.

**THE DIGNITY LAW (the character's spine, straight from the source):** the Tramp's clothes are patched, and the patches are PRECISE  mended like they mattered, because they do. No one in the Inn pities the Tramp, because pity requires the Tramp to be small, and the Tramp is never small. The dignity is not performed; it is constant  the underdog's posture held through every fall, which is why the falls are funny and the Tramp is not.

**Tri-mode:** Living  the lesson, the waddle, the meal shared standing; Shadow  the show goes on when nothing is watching (the routine continues in the grey, the troupe's law at its purest); In-Between  the silhouette stays KIND (the one shape the grey cannot make hollow).

**Weaves into:** the Comedy Troupe Registry (the teacher of the five schools); the Gathering Law (the emote curriculum's instructor); the Silk Road (the walker, the road's own resident); the 50 BPM architecture (the timing's master); the Silence/comedy lineage (the silent film made flesh: the Inn's deepest audio architecture taught by its quietest resident); the player journey (the class every path passes through).
