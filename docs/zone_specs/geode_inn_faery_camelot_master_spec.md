# THE FAERY KINGDOM AND CAMELOT — Master Specification
## The Deep Roots: Seelie/Unseelie Courts, Arthurian Layers, and the Nine Realms

## WHAT THIS IS

The detailed design for the Faerie Kingdom (Level 7), the Camelot zone system, and the Nine Realms Norse architecture. These are the deepest cultural zones — the ones that connect the Inn's roots to specific mythological traditions.

---

## THE FAERIE KINGDOM (LEVEL 7)

The deep roots of the Inn-Tree. Accessed through stone circle portals. Warcraft/Everquest-style layout.

| Court | Frequency | Style | Key NPCs |
|-------|-----------|-------|---------|
| Seelie (Summer) | 42Hz | Alan Lee — warm, golden, illustrative | Seelie King, Summer Queen |
| Unseelie (Winter) | 84Hz | Grimshaw — dark, gothic, atmospheric | Unseelie King, Winter Court |
| The Between | In-Between | Shifting borderland | The Norns (Urd, Verdandi, Skuld) |

Stone Circle Chambers: Stonehenge, Avebury, Callanish, Ring of Brodgar — each a portal to a different Fae zone. The deepest point: The Stone Circle Chamber — the deepest tone the Inn ever produces.
