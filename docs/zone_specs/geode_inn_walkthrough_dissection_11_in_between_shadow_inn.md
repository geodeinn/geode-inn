## THE LAWS (the constants that govern all three states)

1. **THE HARD-CUT LAW (tectonic, not dissolve).** The Inn never fades between states. It snaps — like tectonic plates. The shift is a 450ms hard cut; the background-image swaps to the shadow or grey sheet with no crossfade. The moment between brushstrokes is a MOMENT, not a movie.
2. **THE HEARTBEAT CONSTANT.** 50 BPM in all three states. The heartbeat doesn't change between frequencies — it's the biological constant beneath the architectural one. The grey has a pulse. The Shadow has a pulse. The player who listens through the wall can't tell which room they're in by heart — only by ear.
3. **THE SKETCH LAW.** The In-Between is always GREY — desaturated, the color stripped, every artist's style recognizable but drained. The Living Inn is the painting in progress. The Shadow Inn is the finished painting. The In-Between is the SKETCH. Three states of the same work.
4. **THE OCTAVE LAW.** 42Hz and 84Hz are the same note, different octaves. The Geode Cave is the one place they coexist without conflict (the Hollow Earth pass, canon). Everywhere else, the states are separated by the flip.
5. **THE MARGIN LAW.** The In-Between is not a third Inn. It's the GAP. What lives there: lost items, forgotten NPCs, rooms that exist in the margin between versions — the things that don't belong in either frequency.
6. **THE INNOCENTS' CONCENTRATION LAW (today, canon).** The In-Between game is anchored by the named cast: the Duck, the fizgigs, Dobbie, and Brim's one empathy — with Eres the Vessel as the grey's back door (the recognition scene) and Ziggy as the grey's counter-thesis (the creature the grey GAVE BACK). The elaborate storylines exist precisely so the grey can cost something. The In-Between doesn't create its loss. It collects it.

## THE EIGHT TECTONIC STATES (the visual set, now with layer definitions)

### STATE 1 — THE MAIN FLOOR (split hall, tectonic seam)
**The grey version:** a seam runs the hall's length — half the hall leaning Living, half leaning Shadow, the split visible in the grain. The hearth burns grey: present, unfelt. The market's trades hang mid-handoff; the goods hover; the Settled's lost bargains live here (the trades that never finished — the grey market, where the Duck's stolen objects surface before they're found).
**The 84Hz mirror, room-for-room:** the hearth banked and preserved, the fire's shape held without heat; the markets frozen mid-trade — every trade COMPLETED in the Shadow (the deal struck, the handshake held); the gargoyles mid-resonance; the brownies' repair UNDONE (canon, the trio's cruelest image); the innkeeper's ledger open to the last entry, forever.
**Gameplay:** the seam walk — the player crosses the hall's center and the hall decides which side of them they're on; the grey market's barter puzzles (goods mid-flight can be re-aimed).

### STATE 2 — THE STAIRCASE (spiral split, mirrored alembics)
**The grey version:** the spiral splits — one flight ascending through Nigredo-to-Rubedo, one descending in reverse, the mirrored alembics distilling both directions at once. The ascending-key music (canon: the staircase rises in key) is here a chord that can't resolve — held, weightless, the note between notes.
**The 84Hz mirror, room-for-room:** the staircase as the finished climb — every alchemical stage complete and sealed; the alchemical texts (canon: readable only at 84Hz — motion blurs what petrification preserves); Gesso's mural half-restored (the Shadow preserves the RESTORATION mid-vocation — the grumpy gnome, upside down, brushes frozen mid-stroke, the might-never-be-finished finally paused, not finished).
**Gameplay:** the counterweight puzzles use the mirrored alembics — what the player pours up must balance what pours down; the unresolving chord is the puzzle's timer.

### STATE 3 — THE CATACOMBS (doubled passages, cold candles)
**The grey version:** every passage has its unforgotten twin — doubled corridors, the dead-lamps cold but LIT (grey flame, heatless). The history here is UNDECIDED: the texts half-legible, the walls half-carved. The margin where the archive hasn't chosen yet.
**The 84Hz mirror, room-for-room:** the complete archive — every text readable, every carving sharp (canon: the Catacombs at 84Hz reveal what motion obscures); the Ragana's Shadow archive is the COMPLETE archive, every text ever catalogued, frozen in place; the Anubis scale at rest — the weighing done, the verdict held.
**Gameplay:** the echo puzzles (the figure's echo walks the twin passage); the cold-candle maze (grey flame lights what it doesn't warm).

### STATE 4 — THE COSMOLOGY (two skies through one oculus)
**The grey version:** day-sky and night-sky at once through one opening — the orrery running both directions, the stars half-pinned, half-drifting. This is the VESSEL'S HARBOR: Eres idles here (canon, today — the void-ship's idle state IS the grey; between crossings, the ship that purrs parks in the margin). Ziggy's count is VISIBLE in this state — the returns, the whole absence, the number the gecko keeps, legible in the grey the way texts are legible in the Shadow.
**The 84Hz mirror, room-for-room:** the petrified observatory — the stars pinned (the chart never redrawn), the telescopes fixed on their final targets, one pointed at a galaxy no one has named yet (the Annunaki's passage, preserved); the Astronomer's last calculation complete; the Owl's eyes the only moving thing.
**Gameplay:** the two-skies navigation (some constellations exist only in the day-version, some only at night — the player must read both through one oculus); the Vessel's stardust trail (follow it to the ship's harbor — never the ship, only the harbor).

### STATE 5 — THE SEELIE COURT (summer vs. ice-flower frost)
**The grey version:** eternal summer meeting first frost — mid-shift. The Summerlands' gold is draining to grey; frost-flowers bloom at the meadow's edge, each one a promise the court hasn't made yet. Oleander's court mid-gesture: the welcome, the riddle, the warmth — all held.
**The 84Hz mirror, room-for-room:** the Summerlands in full ice — the Seelie gold preserved as gilt frost, every bloom held open forever, the court frozen mid-dance (the Shadow's gentlest freezing: nothing dead, everything KEPT).
**Gameplay:** the frost-flower riddles (bloom one to hear the court's answer, held mid-breath); the season-swap crossing (state 5 and 6 are each other's mirrors — see the ruling below).

### STATE 6 — THE UNSEELIE COURT (deep winter meeting the thaw)
**The grey version:** the mirror of state 5 — deep winter meeting the THAW. The Hollow's ice is loosening; meltwater runs grey through the frost; the court's stillness acquires the sound of dripping. Malachi's labyrinth mid-turn — the walls half-built, half-dissolved, the love letter in walls half-written.
**The 84Hz mirror, room-for-room:** the Hollow in preserved deep winter — the ravens' feathers mid-fall, the black-ice crown at its coldest, the Unseelie Queen's raven-feather cape (permanent canon) held forever mid-settle; the labyrinth complete, every wall of the love letter built.
**Gameplay:** the melt puzzles (the thaw unlocks what the frost sealed — the reverse of state 5's frost-blooms); the brothers' bridge appears in the grey — the broken Escher bridge half-mended, walkable only while undecided.

### STATE 7 — THE WATER ZONE (two seas, frozen mid-motion)
**The grey version:** the tide hung mid-turn (canon, the Stillwater Shore law): two seas in one frame — the swell rising and the swell fallen, neither happening. The water's full voice is here split (canon: 42Hz tone from the Source + 50Hz rhythm from the Moon — in the grey, the water SINGS without BEATING, a song with no pulse). Charon's river runs grey: Memory's Illusion at its most honest — the layer between the water and what the water reflects.
**The 84Hz mirror, room-for-room:** the stillwater perfect mirrors (canon: the Shadow's surfaces show rooms and passages reflected from below — the Hollow Earth visible from the Water Zone's Shadow state); Charon's ferry moored, the obol-law frozen mid-payment; the lighthouse beam mid-sweep, the light held.
**Gameplay:** the two-seas crossing (the player must cross on the swell that is neither rising nor fallen — the weightless puzzle from the In-Between game, at zone scale); the grey river's reflection-reading.

### STATE 8 — THE WHOLE INN (the flip itself, geode heart split)
**The grey version:** the Inn's own state — the geode's crystal heart split mid-flip, the tectonic seam visible in the crystal. This is the only state where all eight zones' seams are visible AT ONCE (the whole Inn's grey is the union of every zone's grey). The figure from the In-Between game walks here — and this is where the final crystal lives (the game's Section 5: grey-white, familiar — the Vessel's words, the recognition, the 42Hz hum becoming audible, the game ends).
**The 84Hz mirror, room-for-room:** the Shadow Inn as ONE ROOM — the whole building petrified as a single held chord; the Keeper in the rocking chair, holding the eye, waiting (canon, the Hollow Earth pass: even the Shadow can't silence the Song at its source — 42 and 84 coexist in the Geode Cave, the same note, different octaves).
**Gameplay:** the flip itself is the puzzle — the player learns to stand INSIDE the 450ms (the one place the hard-cut can be experienced as a room); the endgame's threshold (the three choices are visible from here: the doors appear in the canopy's air, and one of them opens onto the grey).
