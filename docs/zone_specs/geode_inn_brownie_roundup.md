## THE FOUR THREADS (the convoluted storylines, compiled)

### Thread 1: THE ENTOURAGE (the Keeper's followers  source: characters/geode_inn_brownies_and_grit.md)
Three six-inch brownies follow the Keeper everywhere except the Fae Kingdom ("too big, too shiny, too many teeth"). She did not invite them. She cannot make them leave. She has stopped trying.
- **BRINDLE**  the self-appointed leader; loudest, brave, secretly terrified of the dark; tiny crystal-shard dagger; treats every Grit rescue as a military operation; usually gets stuck himself. ("Where d'it go?")
- **COBBLE**  the thinker; has read three archive books (the Ragana's reading list); the only brownie who understands Newton's equations; sits on Newton's desk (Newton thinks he's a bug); always right, always ignored. ("Right proper, that.")
- **THATCH**  the heart; smallest, quietest, actually gets things done; the only brownie the Keeper has acknowledged by name (Thatch didn't speak for three days from the honor); wiggles Grit free in four seconds, says nothing. ("Needs more glue.")
- **GRIT**  the very small, very round, very stuck fuzzy creature (Dark Crystal Fizzgig); the reason the entourage exists; the rescue economy: Brindle plans it, Cobble calculates it, Thatch does it.
- Formation: Brindle scouts, Cobble thinks, Thatch watches. The Keeper walks slowly enough that they can keep up. She has never said she does this.
- Sources worn openly: Willow (1988) + Fizzgig  tone-teacher borrowing, not character copying.

### Thread 2: THE CONTRACT (the folklore overlay  source: geode_inn_brownies_folklore_overlay.md)
**The comedy is load-bearing.** Folkloric brownies: helpful household spirits, work at night for cream/bread/honey  but the contract is unspoken and the terms are THEIRS, not yours. Break the routine, show unkindness, or (worst) GIVE THEM CLOTHES  and the helper becomes the BOGGART. Same creature, same hands, redirected intent: the precision that cleaned becomes the precision that ruins.
- **The Inn's version: THE CONTRACT IS THE CONSTITUTION.** The one-page constitution on the mushroom cap IS the folklore contract, made explicit. The Catalyst's nightly saucer (cream, bread roll, honeycake) is the terms. The Fae Courts don't get to modify the contract because they aren't PARTIES to it. The Host never asks the brownies to do anything  he tends the fire, they tend the floors; the beneficiary respects the contract, so the contract holds.
- **THE BOGGART TRIGGER:** break the contract and the collective votes  always unanimous  the executive officer ratifies  the decision is recorded in the minutes, in the section titled "The List" (available to no one but brownies). The transition is PROCEDURAL, not dramatic. (The moderation canon's deep cousin: the frog transformation has a contract-law shadow.)

### Thread 3: THE REVELATION (the Owl's unwitting infrastructure  source: geode_inn_brownie_revelation.md)
**The fizgigs ARE brownies**  and every wire they run feeds the Owl's network. THE BROWNIES DO NOT KNOW THIS.
- **The awakening chain:** (1) Surface: coconut shells, dropped wrenches, pure comedy. (2) WHISPER TIER 3, "THE PAUSE": one brownie pauses mid-wire and looks directly at the player  the circuit it was completing carried enough of the Owl's frequency to briefly wake its OWN awareness. It doesn't understand why it looked. It goes back to work. **The moment the player should file away.** (3) THE CIRCUIT COMPLETES: when Eres reaches the cosmology level  the top of the tree, the Owl's home  every wire the brownies ever ran forms a closed loop; the network goes live; they ALL feel it. **The coconut shells go silent**  the first full stop in the entire game. They look at their hands. Then one picks up a wrench and goes back to work. Because what else do they know how to do?
- **The Shadow Inn Brownie Class (84Hz, optional playable):** the tragedy you can play  aware, skilled, conflicted. The wrench is deliberate now; the pot-lid shield is real now; the coconut rhythm is a war drum at 84Hz. The lore question the class carries: was going back to work loyalty, or the inability to imagine being anything else?
