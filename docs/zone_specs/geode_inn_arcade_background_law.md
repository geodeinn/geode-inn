# THE BACKGROUND LAW — ARCADE & ZONE BACKGROUND CANON
## How Every Game and Space Shows Its World
### Written 2026-09-11 (ruled into canon after the design thread with Krista)

---

## THE FIRST PRINCIPLE

Every game's background is tied to its HOME ZONE'S ENVIRONMENT ART  not a bespoke background per game. The zone's ENV master (the ambience panel from the zone kit) is the source image: a zone's games play in front of its room's actual walls. ONE PAINT, MANY GAMES. Standing Stones plays in front of Stonehenge; Tomb Tunnel plays in front of Egypt; Andean Run plays in front of Peru. No game commissions its own world.

This is the Room Kit Law extended to the screen: the ENV master serves the room, the arcade cabinet, and the engine at once.

---

## THE THREE REGISTERS

### REGISTER 1: THE ZONE WIDE (static games)
Puzzle and board games (Standing Stones, the board games) hold ONE zone establishing wide, fixed, never scrolling. The ENV master itself is the background. No parallax, no tiling, no loop  the room is still, and stillness is the point.

### REGISTER 2: THE PARALLAX PANORAMA (scrolling games)
Side-scrolling and runner games (Andean Run, the World Walker, the tumble runs) use the zone's WIDE PANORAMA: 1920x800 or taller, painted parallax-ready, plus TILEABLE TEXTURE STRIPS for long corridor stretches. The strips tile seamlessly by design  at panorama width, a player travels a long time before the seam shows. The wide scrolls, the strips repeat, and both are the zone's art.

### REGISTER 3: THE TILE SET + DEPTH PANELS (tunnel/maze systems)
The tunnel systems run a TWO-LAYER system:
- THE TILE SET (the maze layer): corridor pieces  horizontal, vertical, four corners, the T-junctions, the cross, the solid wall  that repeat infinitely and assemble a new maze every play. THE REPEAT IS THE MECHANIC: same pieces, new labyrinth. (Canon proven: the Catacomb Labyrinth's 11-piece set.)
- THE DEPTH PANELS (the set-piece layer): full-panel backgrounds for the fixed moments  the level thresholds, the interludes, the boss rooms. DEPTH EQUALS REGISTER: the deeper, the richer (shallow catacombs in warm Dore stone -> mid-depth Klimt skull-pattern walls -> deepest black marble with gold-leaf mosaics; the subterranean river between levels, boat silhouette). Each depth panel carries its shadow variant. (Canon proven: Tomb Tunnel's 6, the Labyrinth's 8.)

A tunnel background NEVER scrolls  it assembles. The maze is drawn from the tile set; the depth panel sits behind the moment.

---

## THE GATHERING ZONES: THE INHABITED WIDE (the third way)

Gathering spaces (the great hearth, the Meadery, the Seven Sisters fire pit, every room the Gathering Law mandates) work opposite to all three: THE BACKGROUND IS INHABITED, NOT PLAYED IN FRONT OF.

One establishing wide  the room's ENV master, one-point perspective  and it never scrolls, tiles, or loops. What moves is everything IN FRONT of it: the NPC cycles (Ryan's idle tap, the murlocs, Zeke's shanties), the karaoke stage, the drum circles, the campfire's flicker. The flicker is candlelight-class  a sine wave, per the Steady Light law  ambient, never a background swap.

THE ROOM DOESN'T PERFORM; THE PEOPLE DO. The gathering zone's background is the room the player stands IN; the life animates over it.

---

## THE LAWS THAT HOLD IN ALL REGISTERS

1. **TRI-MODE HOLDS EVERYWHERE:** every background  wide, panorama, tile, depth panel  carries its living (42Hz warm), shadow (84Hz, via the spectral-shift script, never hand-tinted), and in-between states. The background shifts with FREQUENCY STATE, never on a timer. Same art, two moods.
2. **THE STEADY LIGHT LAW:** no strobe transitions between states  the shift is a sine, candlelight-class. The campfire's flicker IS the aesthetic.
3. **ONE PAINT, MANY GAMES:** the ENV master serves the room, the cabinet, and the engine. No game owns private world art.
4. **DEPTH EQUALS REGISTER:** in any system with depth (tunnels, levels, tiers), the background's richness grows with descent  the art itself rewards going deeper.
5. **THE REC LAW:** masters first, recolors after  tile variants are recolors of the class tile; depth panels are masters.
6. **ANTI-SKINNER:** backgrounds never animate to demand attention  the room is the sanctuary, not the stimulation loop.

## QUICK REFERENCE (the builder's table)

| Space type | Background system | Source art | Repeats? |
|------------|-------------------|-----------|----------|
| Static/board game | One fixed zone wide | ENV master | Never |
| Scroller/runner | Parallax panorama + tileable strips | Zone wide (1920x800+) | Strips loop seamlessly |
| Tunnel/maze game | Tile set (assembles) + depth panels | Corridor tiles + depth masters | Tiles repeat by design; panels never |
