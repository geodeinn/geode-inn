## LAYER 4: THE TIMBRE (SPECIFIC GRAVITY)

Two stones with the same Mohs (same Solfeggio) sound DIFFERENT because they have different Specific Gravities. SG determines the timbre — the voice quality.

- High SG (dense, heavy — like galena at 7.5): the tone is darker, richer, more compressed. The voice is deep.
- Low SG (light, airy — like pumice at 0.5): the tone is brighter, more open, more resonant. The voice is high.
- Medium SG (2.5-3.5 — most gemstones): balanced. The voice is warm.
