# THE ARCADE GAME SPECS — Master Specification
## Standalone Game Designs Beyond Monster of the Deep

---

## WHAT THE ARCADE GAME SPECS ARE

Full design specifications for the standalone arcade games beyond Monster of the Deep (already consolidated). Each game is an independent, downloadable product with original Geode Inn storylines, characters, music, Bluetooth controller support, and commercial readiness.

---

## THE GAMES

### Tumble Run (476 lines)
Standalone arcade game ($4.99). Side-scrolling runner set in Canopy City with Canopy City Evil Version DLC. The fizgigs in overalls are the loading screen characters (canonical — NOT brownies+Tumble, which was rejected for looking like Daleks).

### Vampire Survivors Variant (460 lines)
Geode Inn-themed survival game. The player defends the Inn against waves of Shadow Inn creatures at 84Hz. Uses the tri-mode frequency system — the 42Hz state is calm (day), 84Hz is survival (night), and the In-Between is dusk transitions.

### The Hook Game (349 lines)
A fishing-themed puzzle game. The player casts lines into the Water Zone from the Campgrounds dock. Each catch reveals lore about the Water Zone's creatures. Teaches the frequency system — different fish respond to different Hz.

### Sandfall Game (324 lines)
Desert Bone zone game. An hourglass mechanic where sand falls through layers (Nigredo to Rubedo). The player redirects sand flows to solve alchemical puzzles. Georgia O'Keeffe visual style.

### Pinball Design (225 lines)
Geode Inn pinball. The table IS the Staircase — the ball climbs from Nigredo (bottom) to Rubedo (top). Each bumper is an alchemical stage. The win condition: reach the Philosopher's Stone at the top.

### Cabochon Knotwork Game (304 lines)
A puzzle game combining cabochon stone cutting with Celtic knotwork patterns. The player cuts stones into cabochon shapes that fit into knotwork puzzles. Teaches the stone family system.

### Doki Doki Gameplay Mapping (240 lines)
The mapping of Doki Doki Literature Club-style meta-narrative mechanics onto the Geode Inn. The Archive becomes self-aware (Tier 5 Whisper Layer). The game breaks its own fourth wall. Not horror — existential. The Inn asks: are you a player or a guest?

### Bead Composer Standalone Product (729 lines)
The SaaS version of the Bead Composer — a standalone jewelry design tool outside the game. Offline-first architecture. 198 stones with faceting and optical data. 16 bead shape templates. Separate product from the in-game version.

### Breadcrumb / Loading Screens (288 lines + 257 lines)
Fizgigs in overalls = canonical loading screen characters. Loading screens show the fizgigs working behind the scenes of the Inn — mining stone, polishing gems, arranging the Archive. Pot-lid shields that crack after the Shadow revelation.
