# THE EXTERIOR (ROUSSEAU + MAGRITTE) — Master Specification
## The Approach and the Open Secret

---

## WHAT THE EXTERIOR IS

The player's first experience of the Geode Inn is not the Inn. The player's first experience is the FOREST — the ancient wood that surrounds the Inn-Tree, painted in the manner of Théodore Rousseau's "Forest of Fontainebleau, Morning." Then the Inn-Tree itself — Magritte's "The Double Secret" made architecture: a form that contains sky.

The Exterior is the Inn's HONEST space before the metaphor begins. The trees are trees. The ground is ground. The morning is morning. Then the Magritte reveal: the building is a face, and behind the face is the cosmos. The Double Secret: the Inn has been telling you what it is the whole time.

---

## PART ONE: THE FOREST (Théodore Rousseau)

### The Aesthetic — Honest Ground

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| The Light | Morning. Dawn. Sun low, shadows long, forest half-awake. Light in shafts through canopy gaps — ACTUAL morning light. Says: the day is beginning. You are beginning. |
| The Trees | Old. Ancient. Each tree is an individual, not a symbol. Bark rough and specific. Roots break the ground. Branches reach without reaching FOR anything. The being is enough. |
| The Ground | Leaf litter, moss, stones. The forest floor. The same earth as the sleeping giants, but here covered in life. The life is the cover. The cover is the kindness — the forest dresses the stone in green so the player walks on something soft before descending to something hard. |
| The Atmosphere | Cool. Damp. Morning mist that hasn't burned off. Not the Starry Night void — WEATHER. Actual water vapor. The mist is the forest breathing. The breathing is the alive. |

### The Path

Linear. The forest surrounds it — dense, deep, Rousseau density that blocks every sightline except the one ahead. The player can't see the Inn yet. Only the next few steps. The not-seeing is the anticipation. The anticipation is the approach.

One moment: a clearing. Morning light falls fully — no canopy, just sky and ground and light. The Rousseau moment: the honest, simple, beautiful moment of standing in light. The threshold between forest and Inn. The player stands in the clearing and breathes.

Then the player sees the Inn-Tree.

### The Sound — Silence and Then the Hum

The forest is genuinely quiet. No frame drums. No taglharpa. No singing vines. Natural sounds: birds, wind, creak, rustle.

But under the natural sounds — faintly, barely, like a memory of sound — the 42Hz hum. The player doesn't hear it consciously. The player FEELS it. The mycorrhizal network carries the Song to the forest floor. The forest has been humming since before the player arrived. The hum is the first contact: I'm here. Keep walking.

## PART TWO: THE INN-TREE (Magritte)

### The Double Secret

Magritte's "The Double Secret" (1965): a face, painted classically, but a section is cut away — and behind the face is blue sky with clouds. Not darkness. Not a hidden truth. SKY. The face is a mask, and behind the mask is the OPEN. The "double secret" is that there IS no secret — the face was always sky, wearing a face.

The Inn-Tree IS "The Double Secret."

The player sees: a massive tree, ancient, the biggest living thing they've ever seen. Solid. Real. A BUILDING — rooms, shelves, stairs visible in the trunk, windows in the knots, the door at the hollow base. A face. A game hub. An archive.

And BEHIND the face — not hidden, OPEN — the sky. Not the forest's sky. The SKY of the cosmology level. The void the Constant walks through. The Nyx-dark that holds the stars. The Inn-Tree is a form that contains sky. The building is a face that is actually vastness.

### The Magritte Aesthetic

Magritte doesn't make things surreal. He makes things HONEST — so honest that the honesty looks surreal. The pipe that is not a pipe. The face that is not a face. The window that is not a window but the thing the window opens onto. Familiarity is the lie. Openness is the truth.

The Inn-Tree: you walk up to a tree, and the tree is a building, and the building is a mask, and behind the mask is the cosmos. You don't enter a building. You enter a face that is sky.
