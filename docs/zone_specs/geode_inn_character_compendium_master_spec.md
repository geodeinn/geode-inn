## THE INN-TREE: THE LIVING ENT

The Geode Inn is built inside a living tree. Not metaphor — the tree IS an Ent. One of the oldest. So old it has forgotten its own name.

The tree became aware slowly, over millennia, as its roots reached deep enough to touch the Song of Making in the earth's bones. The Song woke it. The tree learned to shape the Song. The gargoyles grew from its bark where it needed to listen. The crystals formed where it needed to think. The hollows opened where it needed to shelter.

The Ent's body IS the Inn:
- Main Floor = the heartwood hollow (the Ent's heart is here — the 42Hz hum is strongest)
- The Staircase = the trunk (resonance increases with height — the Ent is doing it on purpose)
- The Catacombs = the roots (reaching into the earth's memory)
- The Canopy = the branches (the Fae Kingdom, the cultural platforms)
- The Clockwork Skies = the space above the branches

The warmth is not just the fire. It's the Ent's body heat. Living things are warm. The Inn feels warm because the tree is alive.
