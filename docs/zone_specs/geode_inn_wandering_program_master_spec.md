## WHAT THE WANDERING PROGRAM IS

The Wandering Program is the core simulation framework governing the real-time movement, daily schedules, dynamic behaviors, and social interactions of 60+ NPCs navigating the Inn's shifting topology. The Inn operates not as a static building but as an organic, living architectural tree structure whose spatial geometry shifts along harmonic vibrational axes.

The Inn is partitioned into six structural tiers aligned with arboreal anatomy:
1. **The Trunk** (Main Floor & Hearth) — central hub of social density
2. **The Roots** (Catacombs & Undercity) — subterranean archival depths
3. **The Branches** (Cosmology & Clockwork Skies) — high-altitude celestial levels
4. **The Aquifers** (Water Zone & Abyssal Pools) — aquatic passages and tidal grottos
5. **The Canopy** (Fae Kingdom & Glades) — twilight realm of Seelie and Unseelie
6. **The World Grid** (Cultural Zones) — the 9 exterior regions surrounding the Inn

## DYNAMIC SCHEDULE MODIFIERS

Routines are never rigid. Four drivers cause deviations:

### 1. Inn Weather Engine
The Inn experiences localized micro-climates generated by its mineral core's shifting resonance:

| Weather | Zone | NPC Response |
|---------|------|-------------|
| Ash Rain | Main Floor | Firekeeper and Catalyst locked to hearth. External wanderers seek shelter. |
| Fog of Memory | Catacombs | Beverley restricts deep stack travel. Crone lights bone-tallow candles. |
| Starlight Frost | Cosmology | Astronomer stays indoors. Clockwork Avians roost in heated arches. |
| Obsidian Breeze | Water Zone/Undercity | Water creatures move to surface grottos. Naut stirs in the deep pool. |
| Solar Flare | Fae Kingdom | Seelie hold processionals. Unseelie retreat into shadows. |
| Amber Twilight | Cultural Zones | Merchants set up open-air trading carpets. |

### 2. Player Interaction Interrupts
When the player engages an NPC, the movement timer freezes. Conversations crossing quadrant boundaries complete before transition.

### 3. Whisper Layer Events
When player activity triggers a Whisper Event (unlocking hidden vaults, spending time in forbidden zones), relevant NPCs adjust schedules to investigate or monitor.

### 4. Frequency Phase Shifts
The Inn's global frequency state (42Hz Living, 84Hz Shadow, In-Between) overrides standard routines.

### Deviation Formula
P(dev) = W(base) + (α · S(whisper)) + (β · M(weather)) + (γ · F(shift))

- W(base): inherent deviation weight (0.05-0.25)
- S(whisper): suspicion score (0.0-1.0)
- M(weather): weather intensity (0.0-0.3)
- F(shift): frequency variance (0.0 for 42Hz, 0.4 for 84Hz, 0.6 for In-Between)
- α=0.3, β=0.2, γ=0.5

## REAL-TIME TRAVERSAL

NPCs do not teleport. They physically traverse the Inn:

| Method | Speed | When Used |
|--------|-------|-----------|
| Walking | 1.2-1.8 m/s | Adjacent nodes within same level |
| Staircase climbing | Variable | Vertical traversal between levels |
| Portal/Ley-Line transit | Instant (with resonance pause) | Distant cultural zones or Escher wing shifts |

### Mid-Travel Encounters
The player can intercept NPCs mid-journey. NPCs turn their head, offer an ambient greeting ("Heading up to check the astrolabe..."), and can be engaged in full dialogue. If unmolested, the NPC continues along their pathing vector.

### Escher Spatial Topology Rerouting
The Inn's architecture periodically shifts — hallways stretch, staircases rotate, doorways reconnect. If a corridor shifts while an NPC is en route, the pathfinding engine dynamically recalculates via A* graph traversal. If a path is severed, the NPC pauses at the nearest architectural anchor, performs an "orientation idle," and reroutes through the central stairwell or ley-line portal.

## FREQUENCY PHASE BEHAVIOR

### 42Hz Living Mode
- Postures: uncurled arms, expansive gestures, open body language, warm nods
- Gathering: high social density around hearth, bar, dining hall
- Quest availability: fully accessible, amber linework indicators

### 84Hz Shadow Mode
- The Storyteller ceases telling stories — sits in silence, staring at cold embers
- The Firekeeper's hearth dims to violet ember, voice drops to cryptic whispers
- Beverley locks the archive doors, patrols with brass lantern
- Gargoyle Puppies harden into motionless stone statues with crimson eyes
- Fae Court members put on porcelain masks, retreat to spider-silk alcoves, speak in reversed poetic couplets
- Water creatures retreat to deep abyssal trenches

### In-Between Mode Oscillation
- Visual: NPC sprites phase-flicker, cast dual shadows
- Voice: dialogue shifts between warm resonant and hollow whispered mid-sentence
- Pathing: NPCs interrupt walking paths, pause to inspect wall corners, turn around
- Mathematical model: V(t) = sin(2π·42·t) + sin(2π·84·t) = 2·sin(2π·63·t)·cos(2π·21·t)
- Creates a 21Hz modulation beat — behavior flickers every 0.047 seconds between relaxed openness and guarded paranoia

## WHISPER LAYER INTEGRATION

### 5 Progressive Whisper Tiers

| Tier | Name | What Changes |
|------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Surface Awareness | NPCs acknowledge player with polite nods. Baseline routines unchanged. |
| 2 | Pattern Recognition | NPCs notice repeated routes, begin waiting in preferred rooms 5 minutes early. |
| 3 | Harmonic Synchronization | NPCs share secret dialogue, invite player to private routines (morning tea, late-night stargazing). |
| 4 | Archival Memory | NPCs reference past player choices from earlier sessions, adjust schedules to assist active quests. |
| 5 | Deep Resonance | Global schedule overrides unlock (e.g., the Reader leaving the Solarium for the deep Catacomb vault). |

### The Positive Reinforcement Loop (Expectation Mechanics)
If the player repeatedly visits an NPC during a particular quadrant over consecutive days, the NPC modifies their routine to ANTICIPATE the player:

| NPC | What They Do |
|-----|-------------|
| The Crone | Arrives 15 minutes early. Tea already steeping. Second cup placed on the side table before the player enters. |
| The Reader | Leaves an open manuscript marked with a silk ribbon on the adjacent bench. |
| The Catalyst | Sets aside a specific amber glass on the bar counter when the player enters the tavern wing. |
| The Astronomer | Pre-calculates star charts, leaves celestial coordinates ready on the drafting table. |

### The Avoidance Loop (Isolation Messengers)
When the player neglects a zone for several consecutive days, the zone's NPCs send messages through cross-zone travelers:

| Neglected Zone | How They Reach Out |
|---------------|-------------------|
| Catacombs | Beverley or Charles writes a folded note, hands it to a wandering Brownie or the Catalyst for delivery: "Beverley notes your absence in the stacks. A ledger regarding your ancestry remains unread." |
| Cosmology | Clockwork Avians fly down to the Main Floor courtyard, dropping star-chart scrolls at the player's feet. |
| Fae Kingdom | Shimmering silver petals appear under the player's chamber door, bearing scented glyphs of invitation. |
| Water Zone | Salt-encrusted seashell scrolls appear on the tavern counter, echoing distant whale song. |
