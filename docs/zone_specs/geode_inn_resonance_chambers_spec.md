## THE THESIS (why this is a system, not just a place family)

The Inn's frequency canon has always been lore: 7.83Hz (the earth's own), 42Hz (the Song), 50 BPM (the heartbeat), 84Hz (the shadow), 111Hz (the gargoyle's oracle). Five load-bearing frequencies. THE RESONANCE CHAMBERS MAKE THEM PHYSICAL: each frequency has a chamber, somewhere in the world, carved by ancestors who tuned stone to sing at exactly that pitch. The player's job: find them, tune them, keep their tones  and reassemble the Song's scattered body, one chamber at a time. **This is the stone collection system applied to the building itself: the chambers are the stones of the architecture, and the Song is the geode cave they fill.**

## THE MECHANIC (the loop: discover, tune, keep, chord)

### 1. DISCOVERY (by ear)
The chambers are hidden  some inside passage mounds, some in world-map zones, some deep underground. THE WALK-BY-EAR LAW gets its deepest use: some zone hums are CHAMBERS CALLING. The player who follows a hum finds a chamber entrance (the Cartographer can teach the skill: "listen for the room that's already singing before you arrive"). No marker, no quest arrow  the ear is the compass.

### 2. TUNING (the listening puzzle)
The player enters the chamber. It's quiet  waiting. The interaction: find the resonance. Hum into the chamber at the right pitch, strike the right stone, or play the right tone from the songbook. THE NO-FAIL LOOP (the Adequacy Law holds): the chamber is patient. The player tries; the chamber answers when the match is CLOSE (approximate is enough  the chamber meets you where you are). The tuning is a LISTENING puzzle, not a skill gate: the player leans, hums, adjusts, and the room blooms into resonance when the pitch is right. The feedback is the room itself  the walls brighten (the Mauryan gleam), the tone sustains, the chamber SINGS. (The Steady Light Law holds: the bloom is a slow sine, never a flash.)

### 3. THE KEEP (the songbook wire)
Once tuned, the chamber's tone logs as an EchoMelody (new melody type: CHAMBER_RESONANCE  source: the chamber, the frequency, the zone). The songbook gains a new voice. The player can replay the chamber's tone anywhere (the melody carries  the building's voice, portable). Each chamber also teaches its real acoustic principle (the parks law: the science is the lesson): standing waves, Helmholtz resonance, material hardness and sustain, water-driven resonance, the Mauryan polish's acoustic effect, the double-resonance phenomenon. The chamber's "field note" (the Archivist's margin note) carries the lesson in one line.

### 4. THE CHORD (the Clockmaker's Turning)
The Clockmaker evaluates the songbook's harmony (the EchoMelody canon: the Turning turns when the melodies form a chord, not a total). The resonance chambers are the CHORD'S FOUNDATION: the five canonical frequencies are the root, third, fifth, and color tones of the Inn's harmony. Each chamber found enriches the chord; all five found completes it. THE TURNING: the full chord turns the Clockmaker's machine  the Song's completion, the deepest gameplay reward, the cosmological reveal (what the Song actually IS, heard for the first time in full). (GATE: the reveal's content  Krista's deepest ruling.)
