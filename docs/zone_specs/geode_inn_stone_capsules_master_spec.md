# THE STONE CAPSULES & COMPANIONS SYSTEM — Master Specification
## The Pokémon System, Reimagined as Geology

---

## WHAT THE STONE CAPSULES ARE

Two mechanics merged into one system:

1. **Stones are capsules.** Each stone the player collects is stored in inventory as a Pokémon-esque capsule — a small, hand-held stone that contains a frequency. The stone IS the capsule. The Inn's medium is stone. The capsule is not technology — it's geology.

2. **Harmonized enemies become companions.** When you win a resonance combat encounter (harmonize an enemy), the harmonized creature joins your adventures as a fighting companion — stored in a stone capsule, deployed in future combats, carrying its own frequency and its own story.

Together: the player carries a pouch of stone capsules. Each capsule contains a frequency (the stone's natural vibration) and possibly a companion (a harmonized being stored inside). The stones are the Pokéballs. The harmonized enemies are the Pokémon. The geode cave is the PC box.

---

## THE STONE CAPSULE

### What It Is
A stone capsule is a GEODE — a plain stone exterior, a beautiful interior. The Inn's core image made into a game mechanic. The player collects stones throughout the Inn. Each stone has a frequency (from the 198-stone database, each with Mind/Body properties and a vibration). The stone is stored in inventory as a capsule.

### The Pouch
The inventory is not a menu screen. The inventory is a POUCH — a physical, leather, heavy-thing-in-your-hand pouch of stones. The player feels the stones' vibrations through the pouch (haptic feedback — a subtle, constant hum, different for each stone). The pouch is a portable geode cave, carried on the belt.

### In Combat
The player selects a stone from the pouch and deploys its frequency. The stone IS the projectile — thrown or placed, vibrating, sending its frequency as a wave toward the enemy. Different stones counter different dissonance types. The stone returns to the pouch after deploying (the frequency is infinite — the stone vibrates as long as it exists). Combat is about CHOOSING the right stone, not running out.

| Stone | Frequency | Counter |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Ammolite | 40Hz, tidal timing | Tidal dissonance |
| Preseli Blue Stone | Steady frequency | Cardiac dissonance |
| Bluestone (Fae Kingdom) | Shimmer | Suppressive dissonance |

### Out of Combat (Passive Effects)
Each stone's Mind/Body properties influence the player's state:
- Calming stone (amethyst) → enhanced Nook Effect recovery
- Protective stone (black tourmaline) → reduced dissonance damage
- Attuning stone (clear quartz) → enhanced Resonance Pulse (stronger warmer/colder signal)

Effects are subtle — not game-breaking, not min-max-able. The player who knows their stones knows which to carry. The player who doesn't carries what feels right and discovers through experience.

### Charged Stones (Silk Road Integration)
Stones charged along ley lines are stronger: +20% combat effectiveness, can resonate with zone features, and unlock Ley Line Gates. The pouch shows charge level as a subtle glow.

## THE COMPANION SYSTEM

### What They Are
When the player harmonizes an enemy in resonance combat, the enemy's true form is revealed (dissonance drops to zero, distortion clears, the real creature emerges). The harmonized creature joins the PLAYER as a COMPANION.

The companion is stored in a stone capsule — a geode forms around the harmonized creature at the moment of harmonization. The creature's frequency, now consonant, crystallizes into a stone. The creature rests inside, present but compact, the way a crystal rests inside a geode.

### How Companions Work in Combat
Open the stone capsule → the geode splits along its natural fracture line → the interior glows → the companion emerges. Every companion deployment is a geode opening — the Inn's thesis: what's inside is more beautiful than what's outside.

| Combat Role | Function | Pokémon Equivalent |
|-------------|----------|-------------------|
| Frequency support | Vibrates at consonant frequency, amplifying the player's deployed waves. If companion's frequency matches the stone's, AMPLIFY (same-type bonus) | Attack |
| Dissonance absorption | Absorbs enemy's counter-attack, protecting the player from resonance loss. Partially neutralizes dissonance. | Tank |
| Timing contribution | Has its own rhythm from origin culture. Syncs with player's timing (more effective deployments) or counters enemy's timing (disrupts dissonance pattern) | Support |

After combat, the companion returns to its capsule — the geode closes, the companion rests. Not "fainted" — RESTING. Recovery happens in the nook (the Nook Effect restores companions as well as the player). The nook is the Pokémon Center. The nook is the Inn within the Inn.

### The Companion's Story
Each companion has a story — why it was dissonant, what it was before, what it becomes after harmonization. The story unfolds over time as the player adventures with the companion. The player who LISTENS to the companion in nooks hears the story. The player who only deploys in combat gets the function but not the story.

The listening IS the friendship. The Thrum's teaching: hear the timing, and the timing tells you who the companion is.

### Story Companions vs. Wild Companions

| Type | How Earned | Examples |
|------|-----------|----------|
| Story Companions | Earned by completing questlines. Named characters with full backstories. The most powerful and narratively rich. | The Pirate's drum rhythm (timing support — the beat that was a mantra became a companion). Kali's severed head (dissonance support — a false self that serves the real you). |
| Wild Companions | Earned by harmonizing enemies in random encounters. Unnamed, procedurally generated from zone frequency palette. Simpler stories, less powerful, more numerous. | The Pokémon you catch, train, and deploy. |

Story companions = LEGENDARY Pokémon. Wild companions = regular Pokémon. Both are stored in geode capsules. Both are deployed by opening the geode.

### The Geode Cave — The PC Box
The player's pouch has limited space (six active capsules). Companions not in the active pouch are stored in the GEODE CAVE — the baby dragon's reward home. The cave is the PC box. The cave's walls are lined with geodes — each geode a stored companion, each glowing with consonant frequency.

The baby dragon tends the cave. The dragon is the PC box operator — watching over stored companions, keeping geodes warm, letting the player know when a stored companion's frequency has strengthened (a resting companion recovers). The dragon doesn't explain. The dragon lives there, among the geodes. The player learns by watching.

The geode cave is also the VISUAL representation of macro-progression. A full cave = many harmonized enemies, many stones, many quests. The cave's fullness IS the XP bar — visual proof that the player has been here a long time.

## THE 165TH STONE — THE SOULSTONE

The Soulstone (from the Cosmic Layer) is the 165th stone — the one that completes the geode. 164 earth stones + 1 cosmic stone = the Inn's full voice. The Soulstone is the PLAYER's life encoded as a mineral. The dragon curls around it. The geode becomes a complete resonator.

The Soulstone is earned on Mars (Cosmic Layer — "The Life You Lived" quest). The player finds their own stone among millions of memory-stones — the one that resonates at the player's exact Inn Song frequency.
