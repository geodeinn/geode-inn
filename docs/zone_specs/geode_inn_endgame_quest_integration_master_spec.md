## WHAT THE ENDGAME QUEST INTEGRATION IS

The bridge between prose and mechanics for the Inn's endgame. This document takes the narrative scenes (the Einstein Syndrome, the Descent, the Grand Collision, the Final Room) and translates them into quest triggers, dialogue trees, and interactive mechanics.

The Inn's endgame has extensive prose (the three choices, the gap scenes, the final room) but many of those scenes are currently cutscenes without player interaction. This spec defines the MECHANICAL layer — what the player DOES, not just what the player SEES.
