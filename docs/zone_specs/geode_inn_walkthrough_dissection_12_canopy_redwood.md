## PART 3 — THE REDWOOD CATHEDRAL (Mona, the queens' tree)

**What it is:** the PURE zone — no combat, no dissonance, no timer, no objectives. Bierstadt subdued (the grandeur without the spectacle). The oldest was a seedling when Rome was young; a mature tree when the first stone circle was built. The catacombs hold the MEMORY of ancient civilizations; the redwoods hold the LIVING PRESENCE of time. Living memory, not stone memory — and mortal, which is the haunting.

**The five climbing layers (canon, the master spec):**
1. **THE FOREST FLOOR** — permanent twilight; ferns, moss, the root systems like wooden rivers. The zone's home base.
2. **THE TRUNK (0-100 ft)** — bark ridges as handholds; the redwood's skin, alive with smaller things. The rings readable BY TOUCH (the timing law: wide years, narrow years, fire years — the bark mirrors the rings; the reading is the touch).
3. **THE UNDERSTORY (100-200 ft)** — first branches, canopy soil, air gardens: the middle world between earth and light.
4. **THE CANOPY (200-300+ ft)** — full light, windy, interlocking branches as walkable paths. The reward: looking DOWN on the forest that looked so enormous from below.
5. **THE CROWN (300+ ft)** — the highest reachable point in the Inn. The Queen of Roots' worn branch (canon: the Queen of Roots is GONE — moved to the Inn-Tree — the zone remembers the sitting).

**Mona's three spaces (canon, Krista 2026-09-10, D07B):** the mother redwood holds the queens' spaces in her three layers — the Seelie Queen's BOWER in the canopy, the Unseelie Queen's WINTER HALL in the hollow trunk, the Queen of Roots' HEARTWOOD COURT at the roots. THE QUEENS' LAW: the brothers (Oleander/Malachi) split one root in two directions; the queens never split — one trunk holds all three.

**Game #45 — the full climb (the dissection's core ruling):** the Redwood Cathedral's game is THE FULL CLIMB, and the climb passes all three queens' spaces. This pass rules the climb ASCENDS THE LADDER: the Heartwood Court (the roots) → the Winter Hall (the hollow trunk) → the Bower (the canopy). The three queens' dialogue laws encountered in spatial order: growth/expansion (roots) → transaction (trunk) → asking better (canopy). The player learns the ladder by climbing it: the queens never split, and the climb never forks — one trunk, top to bottom, all three thrones.

**The sitting law (canon):** the most important thing the player can do here is not build, not climb. SIT. The sitting is the building. The presence is the contribution. "The forest will remember you too."
