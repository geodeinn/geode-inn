# THE TRIFOCAL GAZE — Master Specification
## The Lighthouse's Third Lens

---

## WHAT THE TRIFOCAL GAZE IS

The Lighthouse (Water Zone, Level 8) has a telescope. The telescope was designed with two settings — the Bifocal Gaze: UP (Cosmology) and ACROSS (7 water layers). But the telescope has always had a third direction: DOWN. Through the Source (the deepest water layer), through the ceiling of the underground ocean, into the Hollow Earth.

The Trifocal Gaze is the third lens. Three directions. Three watchers. Three tempos. The Lighthouse is the only point in the Inn where you can see the stars, the water, and the source beneath the water — all through the same lens.

| Direction | What It Sees | Tempo | Watcher |
|-----------|-------------|-------|---------|
| UP | The Cosmology — stars, the 42Hz source above | The Owl (Eternity) | The Allfather's mirror |
| ACROSS | The 7 water layers — the Water Zone's full depth | The Tidal Cycle | Persephone's Vigil |
| DOWN | The underground ocean — the Hollow Earth's interior sea | The Heartbeat (50 BPM) | The Pirate's drum, reaching the deep |

---

## PERSEPHONE'S SECRET

Persephone has been keeping the Vigil for years — monitoring the 7 layers, reading the Bifocal Gaze, maintaining the Water Zone's harmony. But Persephone has always known there's something below Layer 7.

The Source isn't just the water's origin — it's a CEILING. The Source is the top of the underground ocean. Below it: the Hollow Earth's interior sea. Below the water: the crystal city. Persephone has seen it — when the tide is lowest and the water stillest, Persephone has looked DOWN and seen a steady, ancient golden light. Not bioluminescence. An older light. A light that was here before the Lighthouse, before the Water Zone, before the Inn.

Persephone has never told anyone until the player earns the Lighthouse Keeper's full trust (Vigil Mastery + Deep Pearl).

Persephone's Hidden Dialogue:
"I have kept the Vigil for longer than I can count. The seven layers. The tides. The heartbeat. But there is an eighth layer. Below the Source. Below the water's origin. I have seen it only three times — when the tide was lowest and the water was still as glass. Below the Source, there is more water. And below that water, there is light. Not our light. Not the bioluminescence. Not the Lighthouse's beam. An older light. A light that was here before the Lighthouse. Before the Water Zone. Before the Inn."

## THE THIRD LENS MECHANIC

### The Lighthouse Telescope
The telescope has three lenses. The first two (UP and ACROSS) are available from the first visit to the Lighthouse. The third (DEEP) is hidden — the lens is physically present but covered by a brass cap engraved with a spiral. The cap doesn't remove. The cap unlocks.

### Unlocking the Deep Lens
The Deep Lens unlocks when the player:
1. Has earned Persephone's full trust (Vigil Mastery rank + Deep Pearl acquired)
2. Has spoken to Persephone at the Source (the Source Descent quest, already designed)
3. Persephone gives the player a key — a small crystal key carved from the Source's crystal floor

The player returns to the Lighthouse, uses the crystal key on the brass cap. The cap unscrews. The third lens is revealed.

### What the Deep Lens Shows
When the player looks through the Deep Lens:
- The view descends through the Source's clear water
- Below the Source: the underground ocean — vast, dark, lit by the crystal city's golden glow
- The crystal city is visible — spires of quartz and gold, the Resonance Engine pulsing at the city's center
- The Pirate's 50 BPM drumbeat is audible through the water — the Deep Ones call it "the Above-Beat"
- The Owl's gaze is reflected — looking DOWN through the Deep Lens, the player sees the Owl looking UP from the Cosmology. The two gazes meet. The Owl blinks. The player understands: the UP lens and the DOWN lens are looking at the same thing from opposite ends. The Owl knows. Persephone knows. They've been keeping each other's secret.

### The Pirate's Hidden Dialogue
Unlocked when the player has the Deep Lens AND the Pirate's Bodhrán (the Pirate's drum):

"The drum. You have it. Good. I've been beating that drum for longer than the Lighthouse has been standing. Not for the Water Zone. For what's below it. Every beat — every single beat — goes through the water, through the Source, into the deep. I've felt something down there listening. I've felt it since the first beat. I don't know what it is. But it keeps time. It keeps MY time. 50 beats per minute. Whatever it is, it knows the heartbeat."

If the player has the Pirate's Bodhrán AND the Deep Lens AND stands at the Source, the player can play the drum. The 50 BPM beat descends through the water. In the Deep Lens view, the crystal city's lights pulse in rhythm. The Deep Ones hear the source of the Above-Beat for the first time.

## THE THREE WATCHERS

The Trifocal Gaze reveals a hidden system: three NPCs watching in three directions, each keeping a secret about the same lens.

| Watcher | Direction | What They Know | What They've Seen |
|---------|-----------|----------------|-------------------|
| Persephone | ACROSS (maintaining Vigil) | Knows about the Deep Lens. Has seen the light below the Source. Has never used the Deep Lens (it's not hers to use). | The crystal city's glow through still water. |
| The Owl | UP (Eternity gaze) | Knows the Lighthouse has a third lens. Has been mirroring the Allfather's gaze — looking down through the stars while Persephone looks through the water. The Owl and Persephone have been keeping separate secrets about the same lens. | The same light — from above. The Owl sees the 42Hz source from the Cosmology side. |
| The Pirate | DOWN (the heartbeat) | Doesn't know about the lens. Knows something is listening to the drum. Has felt the listening since the first beat. Doesn't know what it is. Knows it keeps time. | The feeling of being heard from below. The rhythm answered. |

The three watchers form a triangle: Persephone (the keeper), the Owl (the mirror), the Pirate (the signal). The Trifocal Gaze is the moment the triangle closes — when the player unites all three perspectives through a single lens.
