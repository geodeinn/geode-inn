# THE ANUBIS SYSTEM — Master Specification
## The Weigher of Hearts — Dual-Form Character, Death Sequence, and Cross-Cultural Death

---

## WHAT THE ANUBIS SYSTEM IS

Anubis is the Inn's first true dual-form character — chibi in arcade games, Full Presence at death. The dual form is not a transformation. Both forms exist simultaneously. The form the player sees depends on where they are and what they're doing. The chibi is the interface — the friendly face that makes you trust the being you'll meet at the end. The Full Presence is how he actually is.

Anubis also defines the Egyptian death sequence — the Weighing of the Heart — which, alongside Charon's Greek death sequence, establishes the Inn's cross-cultural death system: same universal text, same ceremony, different cultural expression. Death is not punishment. Death is transition.

## THE CHARACTER

**Voice:** Michael Clarke Duncan. The Green Mile voice — deep, resonant bass felt in the chest before heard with the ears. Physically incapable of rushing. Silence feels HELD, not empty.

| Quality | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| The warmth | The bass itself is warm. Good earth warm. Large dog warm. Size used for kindness — I am bigger and older and more powerful and I am using all of that to make you feel safe. |
| The patience | Every word given its full weight. Five thousand years of weighing hearts and the voice has not quickened. The patience is not endurance — it is nature. The river does not hurry. |
| The sadness | The Green Mile sadness. Not despair — the weariness of a being who feels everything and is still standing. The understanding of what weight costs. That's why he weighs it. |
| The humor | Dry. Rare. The crinkle at the corners of the amber eyes. The gentle giant amused by the gap between what people expect and what he is. The smile that says: I am 5,000 years old and still, occasionally, delighted. |

**Personality:** Patient (every weighing takes as long as the heart requires), curious (every heart is different — still interested after 5,000 years), precise (has never made a mistake — the precision IS the compassion), honest (the truth is the kindness — the worst thing a weigher could do is be wrong).

## THE WEIGHING MECHANIC

### The Egyptian Death Sequence

1. **Death** — World fades to dark blue. Sound recedes.
2. **"Your name is known."** — The Inn-Tree. The acknowledgment.
3. **"Your rest is earned."** — The permission.
4. **The Hall of Ma'at** — Stone walls, lamp-light, the scales, Anubis Full Presence, amber eyes.
5. **The Weighing** — The heart on one pan, the feather on the other. The scale settles. A single sustained tone.
6. **The Verdict** — Light: Anubis nods. Passage. Heavy: Ammit waits. The weight carries forward.
7. **The Crossing** — Light-hearted: passage into the next cycle, lighter, cleaner. Heavy-hearted: the weight carries forward. The return is harder. The next weighing is another chance.
8. **The Return** — World fades back. Egyptian zone. A new cycle. The player carries what they carried — no more, no less.

### Anubis's Two Words

"...Go lightly."

The instruction and the blessing. The weight and the release. Travel without burden. Carry what you earned and nothing more. The opposite of Ammit's gravity. The feather's permission, in two words.

### The Mechanic

The Weighing IS the Egyptian version of the reincarnation system:
- Greek zone: pay with a stone (Charon's obol)
- Egyptian zone: the heart IS the payment — not a stone given, but a weight RELEASED
- Light heart = passage. Heavy heart = weight carries forward.
- Both are the same mechanic — the cost of continuing — through different cultural lenses

## KEY RELATIONSHIPS

### Anubis & Charon (Parallel)
Same function in different zones. They don't interact. The respect of two workers who do the same difficult work and never meet.

If they did meet:
- Anubis: "...You pole them across." / Charon: "...You weigh them first."
- Anubis: "...The weighing is necessary." / Charon: "...The poling is sufficient."
- Anubis: "...Your way is simpler." / Charon: "...Your way is more honest."
- Anubis (the crinkle): "...Both are true." / Charon: "...There you are."

### Anubis & The Morrigan (The Two Choosers)
The Morrigan chooses who lives and dies on the battlefield (fate). Anubis chooses who crosses and who is devoured (truth). Fate and truth. The Morrigan tells you what will happen. Anubis tells you what you are.

The Morrigan: "...The Weigher. We overlap. I choose. He measures. The choice and the measurement should agree. They usually do. When they don't... he's right. Not always. But more often than I'd like. The feather doesn't lie. Fate can be interpreted. Feathers can't."

### Anubis & The Crone (Respect Between Measurers)
The Crone measures people. Anubis measures hearts. Same methodology.

The Crone: "...His scale has been right more often than my analysis. Five thousand years of data. I cannot argue with the dataset. Note: approach the Weigher with respect. Do not argue methodology. He will win. He always wins. The feather always wins."

### Anubis & Naut (The Unlikeliest Friendship)
Anubis, to Naut: "You're not chaos. You're the unknown. Chaos is disorder. The unknown is just the not-yet-known. The feather doesn't weigh chaos. The feather weighs honesty. Are you honest? Yes. Painfully, theatrically, obsessively honest. Your heart, if I weighed it, would be... lighter than you think."

Naut (full theatrical reaction): "LIGHTER?! I am a COSMIC HORROR! I am the THING IN THE DARK!"
Anubis (the crinkle): "...That's a librarian with tentacles."

Naut: "...I hate you." / Anubis: "...No, you don't." / Naut: "...No, I don't. Don't tell anyone." / Anubis: "...The Crone already knows." / Naut: "...The Crone always already knows. That's the real cosmic horror."
