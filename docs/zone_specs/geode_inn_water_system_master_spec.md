# THE WATER SYSTEM (7-LAYER DEEP) — Master Specification
## The Subterranean Ocean — Persephone's Seasonal Cycle

---

## WHAT THE WATER SYSTEM IS

The Water Zone is Level 8 of the Inn's subterranean hierarchy — a massive, vertically stratified subterranean ocean extending below the basement docks and misty exterior loch. The architecture is a physical, navigable manifestation of Persephone's Seasonal Cycle, dividing the ocean into seven distinct vertical strata across three narrative movements:

The Descent (Layers 1-3): The downward journey into shadow and underworld depths. Light fades, pressure increases, the ocean transforms from recognizable surface waters into claustrophobic stone vaults and oceanic jet streams.

Rock Bottom (Layer 4): The Solstice of the Mind. Pitch-black oceanic desert. Total descent. Winter isolation. Home to the most dangerous apex predators.

The Ascent (Layers 5-7): Rebirth, thermal ignition, crystal freezing, and ascension back toward cosmic genesis.

**Art Style:** Gothic anime for water zones (the Inn's standing design decision).

## THE LIGHTHOUSE KEEPER

The narrative anchor NPC. Located at the Lighthouse — the entry point to the Water Zone from the Main Floor.

| Phase | What the Keeper Does | Visual Language |
|-------|---------------------|----------------|
| Phase 1: The Descent (Layers 1-3) | Direct searchlight guidance. The Keeper's light reaches down through the first three layers — the player follows the beam. Acoustic guidance: the Keeper's bell tolls at intervals matching the layer's frequency. | Hodler (Ferdinand Hodler — the Swiss Symbolist. Angular, repetitive, formal figures. The light IS the guidance. |
| Phase 2: Rock Bottom (Layer 4) | The light CANNOT reach Layer 4. The Hadal Trench is beyond the Lighthouse's beam. The player is truly alone. The Keeper's bell tolls, but the sound is DISTORTED by depth — the player hears it as a deep groan, barely recognizable. | Hodler in darkness — the angular figures barely visible, the repetition almost menacing. The Keeper's absence IS the presence. |
| Phase 3: The Ascent (Layers 5-7) | The Keeper's light returns — not from above (the Lighthouse) but from BELOW. The Source (Layer 7) emits a Cosmic Light Pillar — the primordial genesis light. The Keeper's guidance inverts: the light comes from the deep, not the surface. | Hodler inverted — the formal figures pointing UP, toward a light from below. The ascent is guided by the source, not the surface. |
