## WHAT THE WATER SYSTEM IS

Level 8 of the Geode Inn's subterranean hierarchy — a massive, vertically stratified subterranean ocean extending below the basement docks and misty exterior loch. The spatial architecture is a physical, navigable manifestation of Persephone's Seasonal Cycle: descent, rock bottom, ascent.

The ocean beneath the floorboards is not a passive abyss, but a living cathedral tuned to the fundamental harmonics of existence. Every drop of water is a note, every creature a frequency, and every stone an anchor to the grand chord.

The Lighthouse Keeper's telescope has two lenses (bifocal gaze): UP (Cosmology) and ACROSS (7 water layers). The Trifocal Gaze adds a third: DOWN (through the Source into Hollow Earth). Persephone has known about the third lens the whole time.

| Phase | Movement | What the Light Does |
|-------|----------|-------------------|
| Phase 1: The Descent (Layers 1-3) | Direct searchlight | Guides the player down. Acoustic guidance. |
| Phase 2: Rock Bottom (Layer 4) | Hadal blackout | The light goes out. Solitary vigil. The player is alone. |
| Phase 3: The Ascent (Layers 5-7) | Thermal signals + cosmic light pillar | The light returns differently. Not guiding up — revealing what was always there. |
