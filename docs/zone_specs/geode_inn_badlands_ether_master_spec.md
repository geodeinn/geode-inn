# THE BADLANDS ETHER — Master Specification
## The Dark Spirits and the Exposed Rememberers

---

## WHAT THE BADLANDS ETHER IS

The badlands are where the stone has been stripped away too fast. The Alberta Badlands (Drumheller, Dinosaur Provincial Park, the Red Deer River valley) are in the same formation as the ammolite — the Bearpaw Formation. The mountains are the ocean's memory in a frame. The badlands are the ocean's memory without a frame.

The ether is raw, unstructured frequency — released when the stone that held it eroded away. The fossils on the surface are Rememberers pulled from the dark stone into the open light. The exposure is traumatic. The ether is their distress: 75 million years of uncontained memory, loose in the air.

The ether is not a gas, not a liquid, not a solid. It is an atmospheric frequency — a vibration that permeates the badlands the way mist permeates the Smokies. Where the mist is the sprites (light, new, immigrant), the ether is the Rememberers' distress (dark, ancient, exposed).

## THE ETHER'S PROPERTIES

| Property | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| It absorbs | Eats light, sound, energy. The badlands feel dim even in full daylight — the ether is consuming the photons. Colors muted, distance compressed, one stop darker than the camera expects. |
| It weighs | Physical weight felt in the body — not gravity (which pulls) but depth (which surrounds). You're standing inside 75 million years of uncontained memory. The heaviness Krista felt in southern Alberta. |
| It silences | Dampens sound. Absorbs high frequencies first, leaving only the bass, the rumble, the subsonic. The badlands sound underwater because the ether IS the water — the ocean's memory, atmospheric, pressing sound down. The dominant frequency is 40Hz. |

The dark spirits of folklore ARE the exposed Rememberers. Not evil — exposed. Beings of the deep ocean floor, of compressed sediment, now in the open air under the sun. Dark the way a deep-sea creature is dark when pulled to the surface: in the wrong medium, in a light they were never meant to see.

## THE QUEST: "THE ETHER'S FRAME"

The player enters the badlands and immediately feels the ether — the heaviness, the dimming, the silence. The crystal (the player's accumulated 42Hz frequency) is dampened. The Song is weakened. The 40Hz ether is dominant.

**The Mechanic:** The player must structure the ether by finding the right container — not to contain it (it's too vast) or suppress it (the memory is real), but to give it a FRAME. The frame is not a building. The frame is a SONG.

**The 41Hz:** The player plays 42Hz (the crystal/Song) and 40Hz (the ether/memory) simultaneously. The two create a 2Hz beat pattern — the gap made audible. The player must find the resonance point where 42Hz and 40Hz stop beating and produce a third tone: 41Hz. The difference tone. The frequency that is both the ocean's memory and the Inn's Song. The bridge frequency. The midpoint.

The 41Hz says to the exposed Rememberers: you are not the ocean anymore, and you are not the Song yet, and that is okay. You are the transition. You are the becoming.

**The Reward:** The Frame — a sustained 41Hz vibration in the badlands' ether that persists after the player leaves. The ether is not gone. The ether is structured. The dark is not defeated. The dark is given a song. The shimmer changes from random heat haze to a slow, rhythmic 41Hz pulse, visible in the air. The dimness pulses. The weight has a rhythm. The silence has a tone.

## THE TOWN AND THE ETHER

The ether is geological. The town was human. The two overlapped, amplified each other. The ether made the town heavier. The town made the ether darker. The bridge where Krista could see the spirits falling was where the two darknesses met.

The Geode Inn does NOT make a game zone out of the town. The town is human. The town goes on the Modern Legends shelf, in Krista's words. The ether is the game zone — the geological dark. The Inn respects the difference. The geological dark can be structured (41Hz frame). The human dark has to be spoken.

## THE BADLANDS IN THE FAE GEOGRAPHY

The badlands are the negative space of the parks system. Every other park has Fae that DO something — gnomes erode, redcaps maintain, Unseelie resist, thunderbirds carve. The badlands have Fae (the exposed Rememberers) who are not doing anything. They are suffering.

The badlands are the parks system's conscience — about the relationship between Fae and themselves, about the cost of exposure. The badlands are also the warning: the erosion that exposed the Rememberers is the same erosion the gnomes cause, just faster. The badlands are what happens when erosion outpaces patience. The exposed Rememberers are the casualties of speed.

The Granny Woman's word: "...My friend says the badlands are where the stone cried. Not wept — cried. The way a child cries when the blanket is pulled away. The stone was the blanket. The erosion took the blanket. The Rememberers are in the cold. The ether is the crying. Not loud. Not dramatic. The ether is the thing that fills a room when a child has been crying for a long time."
