## THE HONEST LABEL FIRST (the origin is the lesson)

**TETRIS BATTLE** (Tetris Online, Inc., Facebook, c. 2010–2012): the real PvP falling-block duel of the pre-Meta Facebook era. Two players, live, sending garbage rows at each other until one board tops out. The ladder topped out at a literal rank: **GOD OF TETRIS**. Honest label carried forward from the era's own records: the gods ended up alone at the top — surviving posts from ranked players lament that gods could not even match each other ("rank difference is too big"). The real game's summit was a quiet room. The Inn keeps that fact and answers it.

(The KGB-psychological-weapon Tetris story is an urban legend and stays filed as a Modern Day Legends specimen, not here. The spy-cipher ruling from tonight is the Inn's own invention, honestly labeled as such — inspired by real steganographic tradecraft, not a documented Tetris case.)

## THE GAME

**THE DUEL:** the Inn's own falling-piece puzzle duel (legally its own system — the Inn's pieces, the Inn's board; the honest label credits the lineage, the trademark does not transfer). Two boards side by side, live matches, cleared lines sending rows across to the opponent. The 10x20 grid is already canon: the spy-cipher protocol (ruled tonight) lives on this same board. ONE CABINET, TWO LANGUAGES: a casual half-finished game left on the board is a dead drop (the spy's message); a ranked match is the ladder. Nobody questions a puzzle cabinet with a game in progress. That is the point.

**THE LADDER:** the Inn's ranked path, honest-label-named after the original: the summit rank is **GOD OF THE BOARD** (name pending the naming law; "God of Tetris" is the specimen's name, not ours to wear). The climb is the Toll Booth law made visible: every rank up extracts a price — the opponent you beat to take it is named to you, remembered, and met again on the way back down if you fall. No anonymous ladder. The Inn does not do faceless numbers.

**THE PRIZE (the anti-Skinner exit):** reaching godhood does NOT open a higher tier. There is no higher tier. There never was — the honest label proves it: the real game's gods stood in a silent room. The Inn's answer: **the god is released from the ladder.** The rank stops matching you (the real game's flaw, kept as law: "rank difference is too big"). The reward is company: the new god earns the right to walk the grounds with **ODIN** and pester the All-Father. The god follows Odin around the Norse wing and the grounds — asking about the missing eye, the ravens, the horse with eight legs, whether Huginn or Muninn gossip more — and Odin, who has waited a very long time for someone whose company is deliberately annoying, endures it with the specific patience of a one-eyed king who knows the pestering is the tribute. Pester long enough and Odin starts talking back. The real prize: the god came for the rank and stayed for the conversation.
