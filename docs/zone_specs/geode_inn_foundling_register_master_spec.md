## THE PATTERN (the register's law)

**One foundling per game area.** Each area's foundling is young, small, and temporarily stranded — the critter appears lost, far from where it belongs, and the player's role is COMPANIONSHIP: feed it, guide it home, learn its zone's lesson on the way.

**The three design laws, binding:**
- **Anti-Skinner:** foundlings are companions, never collectibles. There is no foundling counter, no completion percentage, no reward loop that farms care. The care IS the content. A foundling that has been guided home may greet the player by name thereafter — the relationship is the only currency.
- **No-Fail:** a fed foundling stays fed; a guided foundling arrives. Failure is information: the wrong food is refused with honest signals (the nibble test — the foundling tells you what it eats by what it tries), never a gate, never a punishment.
- **Honest Label:** every foundling carries its real-world source worn openly. The Rock Biter lineage is The NeverEnding Story (1984) — design language and emotional register, not likeness (the cabbit-precedent law); the gentle giant's melancholy is the deep-caves register. The falling stars wear the Mario-type platformer lineage the same way.

**The mechanic spine (shared by all foundlings):**
1. **THE STRANDING** — the foundling is somewhere it shouldn't be (the wander up, the fall, the wrong bed).
2. **THE FEEDING** — the player learns what the foundling eats from the zone itself (the honest-label classroom: stone identification for the biters, light and heat sources for the stars, soil and insect law for the flytrap).
3. **THE GUIDING HOME** — an escort through the zone's real geography, teaching the level map as care, not as tutorial. No-Fail holds at every step of the walk.
4. **THE ARRIVAL** — the foundling's home knows it's back (the greeting is ambient; the zone visibly relaxes).

## THE THREE SEEDED MEMBERS

### 1. THE LITTLE ROCK BITERS — the deep caves' children
**Home:** the Deep Roots, beneath the Catacombs (Level 6 depth) — the same strata as the Five Sleepers.
**The stranding:** they wander UP — from the petrified-marrow deep, through the Upper Roots, into the Catacombs and occasionally the Inn proper. Tiny, round, pebble-mouthed; the NeverEnding Story's Rock Biter as a toddler — the gentle giant's melancholy not yet learned, only the appetite.
**The feeding (the mineral classroom):** rock biters eat rocks, and the Catacombs is a geology course wearing a basement. Each biter has a specific appetite — limestone, marble, granite, geode shell — and the nibble test is the lesson: the player learns stone identification by feeding. The Stone entity's canon (families, zones, finishes) is the menu. A biter that bites a geode shell finds the crystal inside and is so delighted it hums — the first hint of the 40Hz dream-frequency, felt before it's ever explained.
**The guiding home:** down through the Upper Roots, past the Bone Room, to the 40Hz threshold — the walk teaches the descent the player will one day take to the Heartbeat Altar, at companion pace.
**Design note:** the biters' wanderings are the Deep Strata's gentle leakage into the Inn — the building's deepest layer, saying hello through its smallest children.

### 2. THE BABY STARS — the sky's children
**Home:** Cosmology (Level 1) — the Observatory's star nurseries.
**The stranding:** Mario-type falling stars — they tumble from the Cosmology layer and land, wick-cool and dimming, somewhere in the Inn's middle levels. Small, bright-bellied, frightened by ceilings.
**The feeding:** stars eat light and warmth — hearthlight, candle flame, lantern glow, the glowing wooden staffs of friendly witches. The honest-label classroom is light sources and heat.
**The guiding home (the co-op law):** this foundling CANNOT be guided home alone — the star must be carried, and the carry requires one of the Inn's flying NPCs, who must first be COAXED. The lesson is asking for help: the player learns which flying resident likes which approach (the Quest Mark law's young cousin — approach marks read on a winged friend). The star rides home in the coaxer's grip or pouch, glowing brighter the whole climb.
**Open gate:** the flying NPC roster for coaxing (candidates from existing canon to be listed at ruling time).

### 3. THE BABY VENUS FLYTRAP — the garden's voice
**Home:** the Impossible Garden (Level 3, the Catalyst and Oriel's ground).
**The stranding:** not lost — PLACED. A seedling sprouted in the wrong bed (the greenhouse's propagation shelf, a pot in the music room), and needs repotting, not rescuing.
**The feeding:** the honest-label classroom is carnivorous botany — the real Dionaea muscipula's law taught warm (the snap is a hunt, the plant is a predator, the wonder is that a plant can move). It eats what the garden sends it; the player learns the garden's pest ecology by delivery.
**The special role:** this foundling SPEAKS FOR THE GARDEN — the seedling is the garden's youngest and loudest voice, translating the garden's needs to the player in small declarative sentences ("WET. MORE. THE THIRSTY ONE IS THE FERN."). The garden's opinion, finally quotable.
**The guiding home:** a short, gentle carry to the right bed — the garden chooses the spot by scent, and the player digs.

2. **The individual names** — the biters, the stars, and the flytrap are unnamed. The flytrap's naming session belongs with the garden NPCs; the stars may name themselves (stars do); the biters' names should sound like pebbles.
3. **The flying NPC coax roster** — which winged residents can be recruited for star-carrying.
4. **The admin question** — does the register have a keeper NPC (a registrar of found things), or do the zones themselves keep the count? (The Lost and Found is the Greeblins' office — a natural cousin, but no ruling.)
5. **The Catacombs frequency** — whether the biters' geode-hum foreshadows the 40Hz threshold as a scripted beat or stays ambient (scripting is Anti-Skinner-adjacent; ambient is the current lean).
