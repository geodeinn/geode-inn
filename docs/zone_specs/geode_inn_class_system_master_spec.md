# THE CHARACTER CLASS SYSTEM — Master Specification
## "Festival in the Middle of a Raid" — Five Classes, Five Playstyles

---

## WHAT THE CLASS SYSTEM IS

Five character classes from Krista's original tabletop questline. Each has deliberate stat tradeoffs that create distinct playstyles AND social friction between party members. These are not stat blocks — they are personality engines that generate their own drama.

The system's core thesis: personality is a mechanic. Your stats don't just determine what you can do — they determine HOW you RELATE TO OTHER PLAYERS.

---

## THE FIVE CLASSES

### 1. The Artisan Thief
| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Dexterity | HIGH |
| Charisma | LOW |
| Special | Nimble Fingers |

The hands character. Excels at physical mini-games, crafting, lockpicking, and any task requiring precision. Cannot talk their way out of situations — every social encounter must be solved with skill, not words.

- Best prices at the workbench, worst prices at the market
- Can create jewelry items other classes can't
- Krista's self-insert class: a jeweler who makes beautiful things and is terrible at selling them
- For players who want to DO things rather than TALK to people

### 2. The Bard Blowhard (Dwarf)
| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Charisma | HIGH (temporary — bursts, doesn't sustain) |
| Broad Intelligence | HIGH (knows a little about everything) |
| Situational Intelligence | LOW (can't apply knowledge under pressure) |
| Agility | HIGH (temporary) |
| Special | Taunt (Enrage) |

The chaos agent. Buffs charisma in bursts to bluff, charm, or intimidate, then crashes.

**Taunt — Enrage mechanic:**
- Target gains +2 Strength
- Target loses -4 Intelligence
- Target loses -2 Loyalty
- Makes enemies stronger but STUPIDER and less coordinated

- Reads archive entries (knows the lore) but fails puzzle checks (can't apply it)
- Temporary charisma is a rhythm-based mini-game — hit the right buttons to maintain the bluff, miss and charisma crashes
- For players who want to be the life of the party — chaotic, effective, accidentally heroic

### 3. The Tinkering Weeaboo
| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Dexterity | HIGH |
| Intelligence | HIGH |
| Charisma | LOW |
| Tolerance | LOW (impatience, low frustration threshold) |
| Special | Situational Alpha |

The specialist. Extremely capable when the situation matches their expertise, useless otherwise.

- **Situational Alpha:** when a puzzle aligns with their expertise, stats temporarily MAX OUT. When it doesn't, they're average at best
- Zone affinity system: godlike in 1-2 zones, mediocre everywhere else
- Low tolerance = shorter patience bar in mini-games (fewer attempts before refusing to try again)
- Honors the ADHD-pattern hyperfocus that built the Inn
- For players who want to SOLVE THE HARD PUZZLES and ignore everything else

### 4. The Librarian Crone
| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Intelligence | HIGH (highest in the game) |
| Empathy | LOW |
| Maintenance | HIGH (requires more resources/upkeep) |
| Resistance to Enrage | LOW (vulnerable to Bard's taunt) |
| Situational Ethics | HIGH (moral flexibility based on context) |
| Capability Potential | HIGH (steep growth curve) |

The glass cannon intellect. Highest raw intelligence, but fragile.

- Maximum depth access to the Living Book system — other classes get surface-level, the Crone gets full dialogue
- Branching dialogue based on zone context — in a Celtic zone might side with villagers, in a Norse zone might side with raiders. Not immoral — CONTEXTUALLY moral
- Resource management mechanic — needs to eat, rest, and maintain equipment more often
- Late-game powerhouse — starts weak, grows exponentially. The investment class
- The Bard's natural counter — maximum party friction
- The Inn itself in playable form: high intelligence, high ethics, low empathy, high maintenance
- For players who want to READ EVERYTHING, KNOW EVERYTHING, and MAKE THE HARD CALLS

### 5. The Chameleon Druid
| Stat | Value |
|------|-------|
| Versatility | HIGH |
| Dexterity | HIGH |
| Intelligence | HIGH |
| Tolerance | LOW |
| Endurance | LOW |
| Resistance to Taunt | LOW |

The adaptability class. Can become anything — shapeshifter, mimic, chameleon.

- **Stance switching:** temporarily adopt other classes' strengths. Be a Thief for 30 seconds, a Bard for 20, a Scholar for 60, then revert
- Only class that can access ALL zone types without zone-specific equipment — shapeshifts to fit the environment
- The fast-burn class: incredible in short bursts, useless in prolonged encounters
- Makes indecision a superpower — you don't have to choose, you just have to keep moving
- For players who want to TRY EVERYTHING but commit to nothing

## CLASS INTERACTION MATRIX

### Combat Dynamics
| Source → Target | Effect |
|----------------|--------|
| Bard Taunt → Enemy | +2 STR, -4 INT, -2 Loyalty (enrage) |
| Bard Taunt → Librarian Crone | Devastating — Crone loses primary stat |
| Bard Taunt → Chameleon Druid | Disrupts stance — loses focus, reverts |
| Bard Taunt → Artisan Thief | Minimal — Thief doesn't rely on intelligence |
| Bard Taunt → Tinkering Weeaboo | Dangerous — INT drops but DEX remains |

### Social Dynamics
| Pairing | Dynamic |
|---------|---------|
| Artisan Thief + Bard | The Thief does the work, the Bard takes the credit |
| Librarian Crone + Tinkering Weeaboo | Intellectual rivalry. Both think they're the smartest. Different kinds of smart. |
| Chameleon Druid + anyone | Adapts to whoever they're with. Chameleon personality. |
| Bard + Librarian Crone | Maximum friction. Chaos vs. control. |
| Artisan Thief + Chameleon Druid | The Thief makes things. The Druid copies them. Tension. |
| Tinkering Weeaboo + Bard | The Tinker hates the Bard's broad-but-shallow knowledge. The Bard doesn't care. |

### Zone Affinity
| Class | Best Zone | Worst Zone |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Artisan Thief | Artisan Market, Workshops | Cosmology (no fine motor work) |
| Bard Blowhard | Festival zones, Celtic/Norse | Archive (too quiet, too much reading) |
| Tinkering Weeaboo | Workshop, Egyptian puzzles | Festival zones (too social, no puzzles) |
| Librarian Crone | Archive, Living Books | Festival zones (too chaotic, too loud) |
| Chameleon Druid | Everywhere (briefly) | Anywhere requiring sustained effort |

## THE STAT SYSTEM

### Core Stats
| Stat | What It Measures |
|------|-----------------|
| Dexterity | Fine motor skills, crafting, mini-games |
| Intelligence | Archive access, puzzle solving, dialogue options |
| Charisma | NPC interactions, bartering, diplomacy |
| Endurance | Sustained activity, long quests, combat stamina |
| Tolerance | Patience, frustration threshold, willingness to retry |

### Derived Stats
| Stat | What It Measures |
|------|-----------------|
| Broad Intelligence | General knowledge (archive access breadth) |
| Situational Intelligence | Applied knowledge (puzzle solving under pressure) |
| Situational Ethics | Moral flexibility based on context |
| Capability Potential | Growth rate / XP curve |
| Maintenance | Resource consumption rate |

### Special Traits
| Trait | Who Has It | Effect |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| Nimble Fingers | Artisan Thief | Bonus to crafting and precision tasks |
| Situational Alpha | Tinkering Weeaboo | Temporary stat spike in zone of expertise |
| Temporary Charisma/Agility | Bard Blowhard | Burst stats that decay over time |
| Versatility | Chameleon Druid | Temporarily adopt other class traits |
| Contextual Morality | Librarian Crone | Ethics shift based on zone context |

## CONNECTIONS

| Connection | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| The Wandering Program | Each class has different NPC interaction patterns. The Artisan Thief gets crafting dialogue; the Crone gets deep archive dialogue; the Bard gets social dialogue. |
| The Arcade Games | Each game has class-specific advantages. The Thief excels at precision games; the Bard at rhythm games; the Tinker at puzzle games. |
| The Nook Effect | Each class experiences the nook differently. The Tinker's nook is the workshop. The Crone's nook is the archive. The Druid's nook is wherever they are. |
| Stone Capsules & Companions | Different classes deploy companions differently. The Thief uses frequency support. The Bard uses timing (the taunt companion). The Crone uses dissonance absorption (knowledge of what the enemy IS). |
| The Building System | Each class builds differently. The Thief builds workshops. The Bard builds halls. The Tinker builds puzzles. The Crone builds archives. The Druid builds... whatever, briefly. |
| The Whisper Layer | Each class triggers different Whisper Tier progressions. The Crone triggers through reading. The Bard through socializing. The Thief through crafting. |
| Charles (The Chameleon Druid) | Charles's glioblastoma is woven into the Chameleon Druid's character — the shapeshifter whose body changes in ways they can't control. The class honors the real. |
