## DUAL MODE (RULED, Krista 2026-09-12 night)
The player CHOOSES at the platform, before flight:
- **DEW MODE — silly in the sky, no record.** Pure play. Nothing logged, nothing counted, no leaderboard eyes. The sky sandbox. The wings exist for this first; the scoring is a costume.

The mode is consent, not skill: switching is free, unlogged, and never shamed. The recording never watches a dew-mode flight. Anti-Skinner holds in both.
