## WHAT THE FAE GEOLOGY IS

The Fae are not randomly distributed. They follow the rock. Different geological processes — volcanic, erosional, tectonic — attract different Fae types. The National Parks are not just containment zones for supernatural activity. They are ECOLOGICAL NICHES for Fae who have been living in American geology since before the concept of "America" existed.

The indigenous peoples knew this. The Cherokee knew the Little People lived in the eroding mountains. The Shoshone knew something lived in the thermal features. The Ute knew the high peaks had something in them. The government didn't discover the Fae — the government ZONED around them.

The surface parks are where the Fae live above ground. The Fae Kingdom (the underground MMO zone, accessed through stone circles) is where ALL Fae types converge — the deep meeting point. The parks are surface outposts. The Fae Kingdom is the capital.

## KEY PARK FAE (DEEP DIVES)

### Yellowstone: The Redcaps
Industrial Fae. Working class. Three to four feet, broad-shouldered, basalt-dark skin, amber eyes (same as thermal features), red hands (iron oxide — not blood, TRADE). Old Faithful is a shift-change whistle. The redcaps maintain the magma chamber the way janitors maintain a building. They know about the Sleeping Giant — the giant is their workplace. The giant's digestion (geothermal features) is their machinery. The thermal frequency is 111Hz — the Watcher gargoyle's frequency. The redcaps PUT the 111Hz in the stone by heating it.

Ranger Marge has been watching them for 31 years. "They're not dangerous. They're busy. There's a difference."

### The Appalachians: The Gnomes
Erosion workers. 300 million years of wearing down mountains that were once Himalayan. Five layers of Fae: deep gnomes (subsonic), undines (soil moisture), surface sprites (mist), sylphs (canopy), and the Song BORN here — the Appalachian zone's 42Hz emerging from the deepest layer and rising through all five. The gnomes are the beings who made the Appalachians gentle. The violence of the original mountains was gnomed away. The gnomes are patient AF.

### The Badlands: The Exposed Rememberers
Fae displaced from stone by rapid erosion. The Badlands erode at an inch per year — too fast for the Rememberers to adjust. The Fae are EXPOSED. They are the suffering Fae — the ones whose stone home is gone. The ether is the Rememberers' distress signal at 40Hz. The Badlands are the parks system's conscience — the warning that erosion can outpace patience.

### The Peruvian Andes: The Draconians
Volcanic sculptors. The Andes are draconian carvings — the landscape is THEIR art. Each mountain range is a sculpture, each valley a chisel mark. The draconians work at 272Hz — the sound of carving. The Andean opal and rhodochrosite are the draconians' pigment — the minerals they left in the stone as they carved.

### The Bayou: The Bog Fairy Golem
The bayou's gatekeeper. Holds the door between layers — the surface water and the underground water system. The Bog Fairy Golem is Krista's avatar in the game. The bayou filters the 42Hz Song through swamp water — the Song's quietest expression. The murmur.
