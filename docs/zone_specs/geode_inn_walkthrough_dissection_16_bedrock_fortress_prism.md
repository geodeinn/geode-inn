## ENTERING THE BEDROCK FORTRESS
**FREQUENCY:** This is the first room where the heartbeat is NOT the deepest sound. The 50 BPM pulse carried down from the Main Floor arrives here and is met by something older: the Earth's own hum, 7.83Hz, the Schumann resonance  the standing wave that rings between the planet's surface and its ionosphere, the frequency that has been going since the sky was young. THE LAW THIS ROOM TEACHES: the Inn's heartbeat is the Earth's hum's CHILD. 50 BPM is what 7.83Hz sounds like when it loves something. Every rhythm the player has felt  the tide, the forge's triplets, the second-line's 50 BPM  has been the same parent frequency wearing different clothes. The Bedrock is where the player meets the parent.
**AESTHETIC:** The guardians' own linework: sharp, angular, unapologetic  the only zone drawn in the guardian hand throughout (the Stone Guard's walls are joints and keystones; Del Toro's worn asymmetry is ABSENT here on purpose  this is the one room the Inn did not furnish, because the Inn did not build it. The Bedrock was here first). Kubrick's symmetry at its most geological: the corridors are crystallography, the angles are cleavage planes, the light is the Stone Circle Chamber's mirror glow carried down through a shaft of quartz. At the Prism: the linework dissolves  the deepest room is the no-outline room, the cosmological mode, because the mirror of reality cannot have edges.
**AUDIO:** Almost silence  the deepest quiet in the Inn, and under it the hum. 7.83Hz is below hearing; the player feels it in the chest before they identify it, and the game never names it until the Gatekeeper does. (The frequency that was always there: the player's own chest has been the key since the first visit. The lesson lands retroactively  every room above was humming on borrowed ground.)

The Bedrock Fortress presents seven options. All live, all forever, all beneath everything.
**If you present yourself at the Gate of the Stone Guard, go to OPTION 1.**
