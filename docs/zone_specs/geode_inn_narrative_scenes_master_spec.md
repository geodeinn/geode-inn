# THE NARRATIVE SCENES — Master Specification
## Grand Collision, Einstein Syndrome, Sync Paradox, Death Sequence, and Story Seeds

---

## WHAT THE NARRATIVE SCENES ARE

The specific narrative sequences that bridge major story moments — the scenes between the systems. These are the prose-level designs: what happens, what the player sees, what it means.

---

## THE SCENES

| Scene | Lines | What Happens |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| The Grand Collision | 170 | All 37 artists converge in a single scene. The moment the Inn reveals itself as a unified visual system. The most ambitious visual sequence in the game. |
| The Einstein Syndrome | 145 | The scene where the Inn tests the player's need to understand everything. The Einstein Syndrome: the compulsion to solve. The cure: sitting with not-knowing. |
| The Sync Paradox | 138+130 | The temporal paradox scene. The Inn exists in multiple times simultaneously. The player encounters themselves from a previous (or future) playthrough. |
| The Death Sequence | 98 | How the Inn handles the death of a character. Charles. The Chameleon Druid. The shapeshifting stops. The body settles. The Inn goes quiet — truly quiet, for the first time. The 42Hz drops to 41Hz. Then returns. The Inn continues. |
| The Shifting Man | 162+99 | The NPC who IS the narrative — shifting identity, shifting role. The Inn's meta-character. |
| The Thrum | 141 | The moment the player first hears the 42Hz Song as a distinct sound, not just a feeling. The moment of awareness. |
| Story Seeds | 85 | Unplanted narrative seeds — hooks for future content, DLC, expansions. |
| Six Unfinished Sentences | 122 | Six sentences the Inn never completes. Each is a philosophical question disguised as a sentence. The player can spend the entire game trying to finish them. |
| The Messenger Principle | 125 | How information moves through the Inn. The ravens, the gargoyles, the Whisper Layer, the sticky notes in The Royal. All messengers. All carrying the same message in different forms. |
| Loose Ends Audit | 159 | The audit of every narrative thread that hasn't been resolved. The 15 original loose ends tracked to completion status. |
