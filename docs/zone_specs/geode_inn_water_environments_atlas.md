## THE LAW UNDER IT

The water system is not one water. It is the world's waters — each environment type with its own physics, its own light, its own census, its own law. The Atlas is the water axis's Room Kit: every water-bearing zone's ENV row names its environment type, and the type carries the kit (color/clarity, light behavior, sound signature, species census, gameplay verbs). The difference between waters IS the teaching — real aquatic ecology as the curriculum, the chemical-spine law's water-side sibling.

THE ESTUARY LAW (canon, same conversation): the meeting place is where the life is. Two waters, one third environment that neither could make alone. The Atlas generalizes it: every boundary layer — halocline, thermocline, chemocline, ice roof, water table — is where the story lives. The Inn's water architecture is made of boundaries.

## THE ENVIRONMENTS (grouped by the squiggly-line axes)

### THE SHORE MEETINGS (boundary waters)
1. **THE ESTUARY** (brackish meets clear; stratified layers; the halocline; oligohaline reach) → **the bayou, the Bog Fairy Golem's territory.** Kit: layered shimmer, cypress knees as upthrust geometry, the halocline as a ceiling mirror (from below, the surface reflects — the Lady's reflection-first appearances are physics). The meeting place where the life is.
2. **THE BLACKWATER** (tannin-dark, tea-colored, acid, slow; the surface a dark mirror) → **the bayou's backwaters.** Kit: near-black glass surface, gold light on black water (Klimt's home turf — the Inn's visual soul IS blackwater), everything seen by reflection first.
3. **THE HYPERSALINE** (brine pools, the Dead Sea, pink halobacteria lakes) → **the salt workstream's water.** Kit: water so heavy it holds the swimmer like a hand; the pink lake (the whole ecology one color, and it's pink); halite crystal growth at the shore. Ties: the salt dossier, the Shield Mixer's brine cousins.

### THE STILL SURFACES (lake country)
4. **THE DEEP LAKE** (oligotrophic — cold, clear, nutrient-poor, dark bottom) → **Loch Ness and the lake monsters' home waters; the lake-monster games.** Kit: clarity that deceives (the water is clear but the eye still can't find the bottom — the monster's habitat is the physics of depth), the thermocline as an invisible floor you swim through.
5. **THE ALTITUDE LAKE** (thin, sun-harsh, high; water like hammered metal) → **Titicaca — Viracocha's forge.** Kit: the maker seen as reflection (canon), light like flatter at altitude, totora reed islands as the floating architecture.
6. **THE GLACIAL TARN** (meltwater clarity, rock-flour blue, cold silence) → **the Rockies' tarn — the Fallen Things Ledger.** Kit: the lake that receives and gives back (canon), blue so pure it reads as color, the one-minute stillness.
7. **THE FROZEN LAKE** (ice roof, water below; sound carries through ice) → **the Norse Coast's frozen center (canon: 42Hz carries through frozen water).** Kit: the ice as a drumhead ceiling, light filtered to ice-blue, the heartbeat audible from above the roof.

### THE COVERED WATERS (roofs over water)
8. **THE KARST** (underground rivers, cenotes, the aquifer; stone honeycombed with water) → **Charon's river beneath the streets (84Hz), the Undercity's water table, the Maya cenotes (the underworld's front doors — the Sacred Cenote at Chichen Itza, where offerings crossed down).** Kit: water in the dark, clarity that comes from no light at all, the water table as the underground's own sky.
9. **THE SUNKEN CITY** (drowned architecture as habitat; streets with fish traffic) → **the underwater chambers roster — Heracleion, Yonaguni, Baiae, Dwarka, Bimini Road.** Kit: doorways that open onto water, columns as reef pylons, the city that keeps its grid under the green. Ties: the resonance chambers (sound 4.3x faster — the chord clearest here, canon).
10. **THE ANOXIC LAYER** (no oxygen, no decay; the Black Sea's deep) → **the water's own catacombs — THE KEEPING WATER.** Kit: perfectly preserved wood, rope, hulls — centuries down and nothing rots; the Lady's law in chemistry: the water keeps what it is given. Ties: the catacombs weave (the keeping is a privilege), the Whimsical-Macabre law (the dead met, not cleared — and here the water does the meeting). THE INVERSION: the world calls the anoxic depth a dead zone; the Inn reads it as the water's archive wing.

### THE FLOWING (moving water)
11. **THE LOTIC** (rivers, rapids, the braided channel) → **the Inn's river systems, the mountain melt streams, the deltas.** Kit: current as character (each river's pace is its personality — the braided river is the capricious one), sound of continuous gravel and rush, the streambed's sorting (lapidary as physics: the river is the world's oldest tumbler — every river stone is a cut the water made).

### THE OPEN WATER (no roof, no shore)
12. **THE HADAL** (the open deep, pressure, the trench) → **the deep crossing — Katya the Siren's escort, the pressure fish's census.** Kit: pressure as presence (the water's weight leaning in — kindness at that depth is a structural fact), light only where life makes it (bioluminescence — living light, candle-class, Steady Light's deepest register), the euphotic-to-dark gradient as the Inn's own descent made horizontal.

## KIT FIELDS (per environment, per the Room Kit Law pattern)

- **WATER COLOR/CLARITY** — the environment's honest hue (blackwater tea, glacial blue, brackish layer-shimmer, ice-blue, no-light karst)
- **LIGHT BEHAVIOR** — how Steady Light behaves here (reflection-first, filter-blue, bioluminescent-only, the halocline mirror)
- **SOUND SIGNATURE** — the 42Hz Song's local dialect + surface noise (or its absence)
- **SPECIES CENSUS** — the critter roster per environment (Species Law: ambient life = the pet line = the storefront)
- **GAMEPLAY VERBS** — dive, listen, surface, sort, keep, receive (each environment's own verbs — the estuary's is "meet," the anoxic layer's is "keep," the hadal's is "withstand")
- **ZONE TIES** — the surface/underground/water addresses it serves

## LAWS THAT HOLD

Steady Light in every register (water light is already sine-soft — the sea's own flicker taxonomy is candle-class or bioluminescent; the Inn adds nothing harsher than the water does); No-Fail (drowning mechanics stay gentle — the water is a carrier, not a threat); Anti-Skinner (no oxygen timers, no air bars — the pressure is atmospheric, never mechanical); the Species Law (one census per environment, ambient + pets from the same roster); the REC law (one class master per species).
