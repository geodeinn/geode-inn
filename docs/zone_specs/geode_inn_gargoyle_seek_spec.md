# THE GARGOYLE SEEK — The World-Wide Hide-and-Seek (Arcade roster #87 candidate)

**Ruled in (Krista, 2026-09-19):** "a gargoyle hide and seek of all the locations they are found, those critters are everywhere." Born the same day as the Cities of the Dead level and the funerary arts chapter — the seek game that spans them all.

## THE HONEST LABEL FIRST (the science is the lesson)

The scariest faces on the cathedrals are PLUMBING. The gargoyle is a waterspout — the word is gargouille, "throat" (the same root as gargle): a decorated gutter that spits rainwater clear of the masonry so the stone walls stay dry. The grotesques (the cousins with no spout) don't drain anything — they WATCH. Two registers, one skyline: the ones that work and the ones that witness. The gargoyle is the building's throat; the grotesque is the building's eye. The Inn teaches both: the load-bearing faces carry the water; the decorative faces carry the stories.

## THE GAME

**THE SEEK:** every zone holds gargoyles and grotesques — roosting, watching, and (per the building's own law) occasionally hiding. The player's ambient discovery game across the WHOLE world grid: the Inn's eaves, the Cathedral row, the Cities of the Dead (the tomb city's watchers — the water-table zone's natural residents: the place that refuses burial spits its rain through stone faces too), Camelot's turrets, the Norse Coast, the Spiral Gallery's ledges, the sky-village, everywhere. "Those critters are everywhere" IS the mechanic: the census spans the map, no zone without its watchers.

**THE LAW OF THE HIDE:** the gargoyles do not hide to defeat you — they hide to GREET you (the cub layer's cousin: found by patience, never chased). A found gargoyle holds still, lets itself be seen, and delivers one line — the perched witness's observation, the 500-year view in one sentence (the Mona register worn in stone). Each found gargoyle then tips the next one's hiding place: "try the north cornice, past the second sinner." THE SEEK CHAINS — the game's traversal is gargoyles passing you to each other across zone lines, the fluid walk again: the found watcher names the next watcher.

**THE REGISTER (honest-label, dated and sourced):** the real sightings honored straight — Notre-Dame's Stryge (the famous brooder, 19th-c. restoration), the National Cathedral's Darth Vader grotesque (real: a 1980s children's competition, a 13-year-old's winning design carved among the traditional watchmen — Modern Day Legends entry candidate: the ancient art form accepting a modern god, no explanation needed), Yale and Princeton and Oxford's campus watchers (the real spotting-tour culture — the seek game's honest ancestor), the Cologne dripstone kings. Each zone's gargoyle census cites its real kin and adds the Inn's own.

## THE TIES (the Inn's gargoyle canon, already standing)

- **THE FLIGHT TEACHERS (Given Wing):** the gargoyles who teach flight — the seek's found reward escalates: the gargoyle that trusts you enough to be seen by name eventually teaches the perch's launch (the flight mechanic's tutorial layer, hidden inside the seek).
- **THE LISTENER AT 64HZ (the Unvoiced treaty gate):** the seek's deepest gargoyle — the one who hears everything, including the Unvoiced's overfeeding. Finding the Listener is the seek's one load-bearing discovery (the treaty's first contact lives inside the game).
- **THE PEDESTAL RING SIGNATURES (the Stone Circle Chamber):** the gargoyle signatures honored in the deepest point's pedestal — the seek's final chain link points there.
- **WATER ARCHITECTURE (the Cities of the Dead tie):** the water table refuses burial; the gargoyle refuses the rain. The same law from two directions: the building and the ground both answer water. The tomb city's gargoyles perch on the society tombs — the watchers of the addresses that never expire.

## THE LAWS IT KEEPS

Anti-Skinner (no collection bar, no completion meter — the census counts itself quietly and the total is never displayed as progress), No-Fail (a gargoyle you walk past is not lost; it is still there tomorrow, patient as stone), Steady Light (a found gargoyle's glow is candle-class, the amber catch-light in stone eyes — the Quest Mark's quiet cousin: no glow leads you to them, the light only confirms the finding), the REC law (one class master per region's gargoyle register, recolors after), Honest Label (the real sightings dated and sourced).
