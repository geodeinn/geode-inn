## WHAT THE CLASS MAPPING IS

The full design for all 6 player classes, including mechanics, frequency profiles, zone affinities, and connections to Krista's real family. Each class is designed so its gameplay captures something real about the person it represents.

---

## THE SIX CLASSES

### 1. The Artisan Thief (Krista)
- **Archetype:** Selkie — a seal person who chose the land for the stones
- **Key Mechanic:** Dive ability (unique — can access underwater traversal layers other classes can't reach)
- **The jeweler's apron IS the selkie skin** — never takes it off
- **"Theft" is redistribution** — the selkie's wild sense of justice from the sea, where hoarding sinks you
- **Personality:** Literal, precise, caring through craft not social performance, blunt because bluntness is accuracy
- **Shares the same personality as the Examiner** — one examines the past, one corrects the present. Same frequency, different pressure.
- **Visual:** Feminine Sandman (Netflix's Morpheus but feminine) — pale, angular, dark-haired, gothic elegance, androgynous beauty, ancient eyes
- **Zone Affinity:** Tidal Caves, the forge, the composer

### 2. The Sentinel (River)
- **Archetype:** Guardian — former boxer, Golden Gloves champion, INFJ
- **Key Mechanic:** Iron Stance (tank defense), faceted defense (absorbs and redirects), answers the Owl's riddles
- **In-game name:** Lozen (NOT a class title — a character name)
- **Questline:** The 10-part "Sentinel's Vigil"
- **Guards the Inn**
- **Visual:** Tall, angular, watchful. Presence through stillness, not movement.

### 3. The Chameleon Druid (Charles)
- **Archetype:** Shapeshifter — body changes in ways beyond control
- **Key Mechanic:** Adapts to any biome. Low endurance.
- **Glioblastoma woven into character** — the shapeshifting IS the condition
- **Low endurance is the mechanical truth of the illness**
- **Zone Affinity:** The Garden, the Forest zones, by the fire
- **Found in:** Garden, forest zones, fireside

### 4. The Bard Blowhard
- **Archetype:** Dwarf — loud, confident, the life of the party
- **Key Mechanic:** Debuff — enrage enemies to make them dangerous but stupid
- **Chaos as a strategy**
- **In-game bard songs reference real Inn lore**

### 5. Tinkering Weeaboo
- **Archetype:** Puzzle/caster — hyperfocused
- **Key Mechanic:** Godlike in affinity zone, mediocre elsewhere
- **The hyperfocus IS the power** — in the right context, unstoppable. Out of context, barely functional
- **Zone Affinity:** Clockwork Skies, the Arcana, the Undercity workshop

### 6. The Librarian Crone
- **Archetype:** Archive/intelligence — reads every text
- **Key Mechanic:** Highest intelligence. Contextually moral.
- **Reads every text in the Inn** — has access to all archive content
- **Zone Affinity:** The Archive, the Catacombs, the library
