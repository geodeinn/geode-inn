## PART 1 — THE DEEPEST CAVE (what's already canon, pulled up)

**The Miskatonic Annex** — hidden beneath the already-underground archive: a building that looks like it ran out of funding in 1937, with passages behind its walls that predate the building by millennia. The Annex was built AROUND the passages. The passages were carved by the Inn-Tree's root system through the rock, widened by vibration, shaped by frequency into staircases — as old as the sleeping giant beneath everything.

**The Heartbeat Altar** — the Inn's deepest secret: the contact point where the Inn-Tree's deepest root meets the sleeping giant's crystal heart. The chamber was not built — it was CARVED BY VIBRATION (42Hz, pulsing for millennia, wearing stone the way water carves cave). The chamber IS the giant's chest cavity: the player stands inside the heartbeat. The altar is not a structure; it is a RELATIONSHIP — the root grown into the crystal, wood and mineral one thing. THE LAW: the 42Hz Song is the tree singing. The 50 BPM pulse is the giant beating. The tree is the melody. The giant is the rhythm. The complete Song needs BOTH.

**What the altar gives (canon):** the player who places a hand on the contact point CARRIES THE HEARTBEAT — finds all hidden passages (the pulse is the universal key), feels the giant's dream-state (calm = calm, storms = faster, aurora = skipping), and hears the RHYTHM under the Song for the first time.

**The third passage (canon, deliberately open):** below the Altar, deeper than the giant's heart — undocumented. What lies beneath the giant's heart is not yet known.

**The Five Sleepers** — beneath the Upper Roots (the Catacombs, the Bone Room, the 40Hz threshold), the Deep Roots hold five dream chambers. The stone is petrified marrow; the veins are compressed starlight:

| Sleeper | Force | The Dream Contains | The Posture |
|---------|-------|--------------------|-------------|
| The First | Gravity (The Pull) | All things that have ever fallen — rain, stones, civilizations, stars | Coiled, knees to chest: the weight that made the Inn possible |
| The Second | Time (The Count) | All moments, counted. The counting never stops, even in sleep | Arms outstretched, fingers spread: each finger a counting rod |

| The Fifth | Void (The Gap) | All nothing — the absence that makes the something possible | Arms open, reaching into nothing: the void as gift |

**The Cosmic Egg** — at the center of the five sleepers: a river stone, smooth, dark, unassuming. 0.3Hz — one beat every 3.3 seconds. THE INCREMENT: the previous universe was 41.7Hz, the current is 42Hz, the progression is +0.3Hz per cycle — the Egg beats at the NEXT 0.3Hz, waiting. The Murmur made solid; raw potential; the source from which the Song was organized. The Egg must not be broken. The final act is WITNESSING — seeing the potential without claiming it.

**The Dreaming Depth chain (canon, 5 parts):** The Pull (let yourself fall) → The Count (match the rhythm) → The Weight (set everything down) → The Thought (accept without acting) → The Gap (exist in the void). Five keys, all surrender-keys.

## PART 3 — THE HEARTBEAT LAW (the unification)

One sleep. Many giants. One pulse-grammar.

1. **ONE BEING-CLASS.** The Five Sleepers beneath the Inn and the mountain giants of the parks are the SAME KIND of being — geological sleepers, crystal hearts, dream-content that predates the buildings on top. The Inn's giant is the one the tree found; the parks' giants are the ones the fae staff. The deep strata and the parks are one body at two scales: the Sleepers are the deep-Root chambers; the parks are the surface expressions.
2. **THE SLEEP-DEPTH = THE HEARTBEAT.** The deeper the sleep, the slower the beat. Restless (Rockies) → standard 50 BPM (Yellowstone, the Inn's altar) → the slow whisper (the Smokies) → stillness (the Plateau). THE ALTAR IS THE TUNING FORK: the player who carries the heartbeat (post-altar) can READ any park's giant by pulse alone — the sleep state, the dream's emotional weather, the approach of the stir. Every park quest begins by LISTENING.
3. **THE MELODY AND THE RHYTHM (the Inn's law, extended to the map).** The 42Hz Song is the tree; the 50 BPM is the giant. The parks carry only the RHYTHM (no tree above them) — the pulse in the ground, the thermal beat, the erosion pace. The Inn is the only place melody and rhythm meet. THAT is why the Inn matters to the giants: it is the one place their heartbeat is part of a song.
4. **THE STIR IS THE ENDGAME'S SURFACE WITNESS.** The Song completes → the skip-beat → the giants stir → the mountains answer. The parks are the endgame's visible proof at world-map scale: the geyser intervals shift, the Smokies' hills groan, the Rockies sharpen, the Plateau... the reclining giant OPEN RULING: does the Plateau stir at all? (This spec's lean: NO — the Plateau giant is already awake; the waking changes nothing there. The still one was never the problem. The tension of the endgame lives in the Smokies, where the gnomes grieve a schedule they cannot outlast.)
