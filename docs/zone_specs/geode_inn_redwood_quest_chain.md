## WHAT THIS CHAIN IS

Game #45's full climb was designed as the zone's structure; the Queens' Law (ruled 2026-09-10) made Mona's three layers the three queens' spaces — the Bower in the canopy, the Winter Hall in the hollow trunk, the Heartwood Court at the roots. This chain is the story spine that binds them: the climb that passes all three spaces, in the zone where the Inn's systems go silent and the trees are the whole mechanic. Five climbing layers, three queens' spaces, one empty throne, and the discovery that the most important thing to do here is sit.

**Giver:** None. The Redwood Cathedral has no quest-giver — the zone IS the giver. The chain unrolls through what the forest does. (The zone's own law: "You are a guest here.")
**Access:** Canopy City → the bridge from the Neutral Platform descends into the forest
**Prerequisite:** The Queens' Law spaces are passable but their queens' larger chains (the Fae Kingdom's) are NOT required — the Cathedral is a guest of the queens, not a subject. No gate.
**Reward spine:** the pit house → the ring-reading → the three spaces → the crown → the sitting.

**CANON RECONCILIATION (flagged):** the zone spec (written earlier) has the Queen of Roots as the ABSENT guide — she moved to the Inn-Tree, and her worn throne sits empty at the crown. The Queens' Law (ruled later) seats her Heartwood Court at Mona's roots. Both stand: her COURT remains hers at the roots — a court does not close when the queen steps out — and the empty throne at the crown is her LEGACY, the message she left in growth, not in knife-work. The chain treats the Court as hers-in-absence: maintained, waiting, and the only one of the three spaces with no toll.

## THE CHAIN

### Quest 1 — "Empty Hands"
The threshold. The bridge from Canopy City ends at the forest edge, and the zone takes what the player carries: the pouch, the frequencies, the Song, the resonance — left at the edge like coats at a door. The Resonance Pulse goes SILENT for the first time in the game. The player walks into permanent twilight with nothing, and the first "quest" is a moment, not a task: a fallen log near the threshold, worn smooth by others who stopped first. The chain begins when the player sits on it. The forest does nothing. That is the lesson.
**Reward:** Nothing. (The zone's first gift is the absence of one — the first step of the chain is the Nook Effect made playable.)

### Quest 2 — "The House the Forest Gave"
The pit house. Built by hand on the forest floor from what the forest has already released: fallen bark, shed branches, dropped fronds. No tools, no machinery — the thud of a redwood shedding a branch is the delivery system (the forest provides; the timing is its own). The house persists across reincarnation, and on the player's first return it is already being absorbed — moss on the bark, ferns through the frame. The player's choices (renovate / rebuild / leave it as a ruin) begin the personal archaeology: the pit house ruins ARE the player's own growth rings.
**Reward:** The pit house (the building system's foundation lesson: gather, don't take) + the first ring of the player's own.

### Quest 3 — "The Heartwood Court"
The roots. The climb begins at the bottom — Mona's root flare, taller than the player, spread like a cathedral's flying buttresses. The Queen of Roots' Court is here: her space, kept, waiting, empty of her — because she moved to the Inn-Tree, and the roots remember where she sat. No toll is asked. The Court's gift is the root-reading: the player touches the flare and feels the tension the Queen's message names — the roots push up, the canopy pushes down, the tree grows in the tension. The oldest living thing the player has touched.
**Reward:** The tension-read (the zone's thesis, felt before it's said) + passage up.

### Quest 4 — "The Winter Hall"
The hollow trunk. The Unseelie Queen's space: frost fern on living wood, the cold INSIDE the tree that the tree survives anyway — the raven-feather dark woven through the bark. She is transactional (her law): the toll for passage up is honest — "What will you leave here that the forest can use?" The player leaves one building material from their pouch. (The only zone where giving something away IS the mechanic.) The Hall is not hostile. It is a winter room in a living house — and the tree holds both.
**Reward:** Passage to the understory + the Unseelie's one line, dry as frost: "The cold is not cruelty. It is the tree's own weather." (Played straight — the Child-Actor Law's spirit.)

### Quest 5 — "The Air Gardens"
The understory into the canopy: canopy soil ON the branches — soil made of fallen needles and decomposing epiphytes, whole gardens hundreds of feet in the air that have never touched the ground. Wind for the first time. The Seelie Queen's Bower is here, in the full light — and her toll is her law: she does not ask for an item. She asks better: "What did the rings say, at the base?" — and the player who touched the bark and felt the furrows (Quest 1's log, Quest 3's roots) can answer. The answer has no wrong version. The asking is the point.
**Reward:** The Bower's view-passage (the interlocking branches become walkable paths between trees) + the second half of the Queen of Roots' message, delivered by growth-lines in the Bower's bark: "...the forest will remember you too."

### Quest 6 — "The Slowest Frequency"
The Crown — 300+ feet, the highest reachable point in the Inn. The worn branch: a throne shaped by centuries of sitting, empty, and beside it the full carved message, grown into the bark, older than the player's civilization: "I sat. The forest remembered. That is enough. That is all you ever did. That is all you need to do here. Sit."
The final quest has one instruction and no timer: SIT. The player sits on the throne, and the zone does what it has been doing the whole time — nothing, at the speed of wood. The wind moves. The creak sounds — below 7.83Hz, the slowest frequency in the Inn, the rhythm of growth measured in rings, not beats. And after a while (the zone's own while, not the game's), one detail arrives: the sound of the player's own heartbeat, audible for the first time since the threshold — because this is the only zone where the Inn's 50 BPM is absent, and in its silence the player can finally hear their OWN. The Inn's heartbeat stopped so the player's could be audible. That is the zone's final gift.
**Reward:** Peridot (the Queen of Roots' stone — the gem of the sitting, of growth held in tension, the only reward the zone gives and the only one it needs) + the crown's total silence, permanently returnable.

## TONE LAW
Bierstadt subdued — the grandeur without the spectacle. The zone plays its silence straight: no trick endings, no hidden boss, no final reveal. The most important thing the player can do in the Redwood Cathedral is sit, and the chain never winks at that. Gaiman's hush for the spaces, Bradbury's green-gold light for the canopy, Rothfuss's precision for the rings. The creak is not distress. The creak is the oldest living thing the player knows, breathing.
