## WHAT THE WATER MONSTER BESTIARY IS

The definitive design manual for all 78 water monsters in the Geode Inn's Water Zone — the 7-Layer Deep. While 32 aquatic entities were already designed in the Inn's primary character catalog (Andra the Siren, Bill the Platypus, the Abyssal Tide Keepers), a lore database audit identified 78 ADDITIONAL water monster entries from mythological traditions spanning 191 countries. These 78 were categorized and mapped but never formally designed — until now.

Every water monster operates on a dual-frequency state engine:

| Mode | Frequency | What Happens |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| Living | 42Hz | Living puzzles. Cannot be harmed. Understand, decipher, harmonize, or pacify. Solve spatial/rhythmic puzzles, place resonance stones on altars, mimic signatures using the hydro-organ, decipher mythic riddles. Reward: Pristine Archive entry, permanent acoustic buffs, rare stone nodes, lore pages. |
| Shadow | 84Hz | Lethal combat bosses. Bullet-hell projectile patterns, sweeping AoE wave attacks, dark anime dash strikes, frequency-shifting shockwaves. Reward: Corrupted Shard Drops, weapon crafting materials, combat trophies, Shadow Archive logs. |
| In-Between | Hybrid | Creatures cycle between 42Hz and 84Hz during combat. Attack aggressively in 84Hz for 42 seconds, then enter vulnerable 42Hz puzzle phase where the player aligns acoustic chimes while dodging remnant waves. |
