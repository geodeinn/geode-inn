# THE CHARACTER ART CATALOG — Master Specification
## The Visual Roster — Aesthetic Hierarchy and Design Rules

---

## WHAT THE CHARACTER ART CATALOG IS

The canonical reference for every character's visual design in the Geode Inn — the aesthetic hierarchy, the design rules per category, and the full roster of designed characters. This is the document the artists and AI generators consult before rendering any character.

---

## THE THREE AESTHETIC TIERS

| Tier | Who Gets It | Why |
|------|------------|-----|
| Full Presence (Non-Chibi) | Deity-level figures and ancient forces | Chibi treatment would be disrespectful to the source mythology. Rendered with full gravitas, human-scale or larger. |
| Chibi | Tricksters, arcade NPCs, guides, minor creatures | Small proportions (big head, small body), playful but semi-realistic. Studio Ghibli meets illuminated manuscript. NOT cartoonish. |
| In Between | Powerful figures who serve as guides or puzzle enemies (Anubis, Medusa) | Chibi treatment but with dignity — power expressed at small scale. |

**Full Presence Rule:** A character gets Full Presence when their core power is raw force, divine authority, or cosmic significance — when you *can't* underestimate them even if you try.

**Chibi Rule:** A character gets Chibi when their core power is charm, cunning, or being underestimated. A tiny creature that can still destroy you is funnier and more unsettling than a big one.

## DESIGN PRINCIPLES (ESTABLISHED)

| Principle | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Soften celebrity features | Evoke presence/energy through posture and costume, not likeness |
| Hopkins' eyes | Anthony Hopkins' eyes are the ONLY unsoftened celebrity feature in the Inn |
| Name/title only | Refer to characters by name/title. Avoid gendered pronouns |
| Tri-mode | Every character has Living (42Hz), Shadow (84Hz), and In-Between versions |
| Zone-matched artist | Each character rendered in their zone's assigned artist style |
| Linework-as-UI | Heavy cel-shaded (tradesmen), soft flowing (lore), sharp/angular (guardians), no-outline (cosmological), fragmented (crisis) |
| Creative pipeline | Story → Elio's perception → visual output |

## ORIGIN

The Character Art Catalog was first designed in docs/geode_inn_character_art_catalog.md (1,501 lines). This consolidated specification was compiled September 2, 2026. The catalog's thesis: the character's power determines the aesthetic. Raw force = Full Presence. Charm and cunning = Chibi. Power expressed at small scale = In Between. The adequate is: the design that respects the source. The source is: the mythology, the bloodline, the stone. The adequate is: the character rendered in their zone's artist style, speaking at their zone's frequency, wearing their zone's linework. The both are: the face and the voice. The voice and the face. The adequate is: the character who IS the zone, and the zone that IS the character.
