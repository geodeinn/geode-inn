## WHAT THE QUARTER IS

The Bayou's urban crown: the swamp's carved jewel, the city at the water's edge where all the swamp's threads come ashore. The Bayou is the murmur at rest; THE QUARTER IS THE MURMUR MADE A CITY — the murmur with iron lace on it, the murmur at a dinner party with good candles.

THE BEAUTIFUL DECAY is the Quarter's law: the city is not ruined, it is AGED IN PLACE, and the aging is the beauty. Del Toro's worn asymmetry at full polish. (Tone teacher law: Rice is craft/tone reference only. The Inn's residents are originals — the gothic is the architecture, not the cast.)

## THE VOOODO CRAFT SYSTEM (the hoodoo/voodoo craft layer)

**GRIS-GRIS — the charm-bag crafting:** swamp materials + one stone + one intent = a charm that does ONE SMALL TRUE THING. (Wired to the charm systems: a gris-gris IS an ElementCharm or QuestCharm in a bag.) The one-thing law: the charm that does one thing works; the charm that promises everything is a fraud, and the Quarter has frauds, and Marie's consultation queue exists to sort which is which.
**THE VEVE — frequency diagrams:** the drawn patterns, each one a SIGNATURE — the Inn's law: a veve is drawn to tune, not to summon. Each pattern is literally a frequency diagram (the player who traces a veve correctly hears the tone; the ones who trace it carelessly hear nothing, and that is the whole lesson in one mechanic). THE DRAWING IS THE LISTENING.
**THE CONSULTATION (Marie's mini-game):** the player brings one problem. Marie names one material, one stone, one intent. The player makes it themselves — Marie's craft is taught, never sold. "You'll make it yourself. It works better when you do."

**The girls:** The dead who stayed. Played with the Inn's honest-core law and the Shield of Lore's restraint (no morbid mechanics, no spooky costume, the heavy history honored in silence, prose quiet): the coffee girls are the Quarter's answer to the Bayou's keeper thesis — the Bog Fairy Golem stays as the keeper placed in the kept; the coffee girls stayed because SOMEONE HAS TO POUR FOR THE TRAVELERS WHO ARRIVE AT 3 A.M. ### THE COOK'S NAME — CIAN, PRONOUNCED LIKE CAYENNE (ruled, Krista, 2026-09-14)

**Name law:** CIAN on the card, "cayenne" out loud. The spelling keeps the mother's ambition; the saying keeps the Cook's.
