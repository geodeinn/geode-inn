## WHAT THE SEVEN UNWOVEN SYSTEMS ARE

Seven major game systems were fully designed — mechanics, characters, dialogue, zones, quests — but never written into the level docs. They exist as standalone design documents. This spec specifies exactly where and how each system weaves into the existing level architecture.

## SYSTEM 1: THE TRIFOCAL GAZE

The Lighthouse telescope has three lenses, not two. The existing Bifocal Gaze sees UP (Cosmology) and ACROSS (7 water layers). The third lens — the Deep Lens — sees DOWN through the Source into the Hollow Earth's underground ocean and crystal city.

Persephone has known about the third lens the whole time and never told anyone. The Source isn't the water's origin — it's the CEILING of the underground ocean. The Pirate's 50 BPM drumbeat reaches the Hollow Earth through the water. The Deep Ones call it "the Above-Beat."

---

## SYSTEM 2: THE SILK ROAD LEY LINE NETWORK

The Inn's ley lines are its nervous system. Energy flows along geological fault lines, crystal veins, and ancient trade routes. The Silk Road caravan system (already designed) IS the visible expression of this energy network. The Cartographer can read the ley lines and chart safe paths.

---

## SYSTEM 3: THE MYCORRHIZAL NETWORK

The Fae Kingdom's underground trade network. Forest fungi that trade nutrients between trees, repurposed as a trade network between Fae zones and the Inn's root system. The Mycorrhizal Network is the ROOT system — the Silica Thread is the energy spine, the Mycorrhizal Network is the trade spine. One carries frequency, the other carries goods.

---

## SYSTEM 4: THE WORLD GRID

The open world beyond the Inn's walls. The National Parks, the World Map, the physical geology that the Fae follow. The World Grid extends the Inn's architecture outward — every geological formation IS an Inn zone, just without a roof.

---

## SYSTEM 5: THE PULSE LINE

Already consolidated in the Complete Walkthrough. The neurodivergent accessibility system. Three toggleable modalities (audio, haptic, visual). Minimal Mode, Silent Mode. Weaves into every level and every game.

---

## SYSTEM 6: STONE CAPSULES & COMPANIONS

The Pokémon system as geology. Stones aren't just collected — they're companions. Each stone has a personality derived from its mineral properties. Quartz is reliable. Obsidian is sharp. Calcite is gentle. The geode cave isn't just storage — it's a habitat where stones interact.

---

## SYSTEM 7: THE DARK SIDE OF THE INN

Where 42Hz becomes visible. In normal gameplay, 42Hz is felt, not heard. In the Dark Side, 42Hz becomes a visible amber thread that the player can see and follow. The Dark Side isn't evil — it's the counter-frequency. The Shadow Inn (84Hz) is the shadow. The Dark Side is the 42Hz made visible. The difference: the Shadow Inn is the INVERSE. The Dark Side is the REVEAL.
