## PART ONE: THE GAELIC TONGUE (RULED)

**Gaeilge (Irish, ISO `ga`) becomes a reader-facing language option in the Archive** — a full target language for BookTranslation, not a footnote. Any book in the stacks can be requested in Irish; the translation bridge records the path as it does for every living tongue. **Scottish Gaelic (`gd`) rides along as the sibling option** — the Camelot zone already speaks it (Artair's childhood tongue; the boy speaks Gaelic, the king speaks Latin).

**The scholarly deep layer: Old Irish (ISO `sga`, Ogham/Latin script).** Ogham — the tree alphabet, each letter a tree — enters as the scholar's script: carved stones, marginal inscriptions, the restricted shelf's oldest bindings. The ScholarlyBridge system carries it: a scholar's bridge from Old Irish to modern Gaeilge is a bridge five centuries wide.

**Why this matters beyond grammar (the law under it):** Irish is a LIVING language mid-revival, one of history's target-on-the-back tongues — a culture whose language was attacked precisely because language is transmission. The Archive holding books in Gaeilge is the same act as the wandering musicians' chapter: siding with the transmission against the edict. A library that reads in Irish is a small act of keeping the chain unbroken. (Ties directly to the nomadic-cultures seed and the target-on-the-back law, both canon 2026-09-13.)

**Ties:** the Book of Kells / Celtic Highlands zone (the manuscript tradition is the visual canon); the Bard's Bench (the Gaelic oral tradition — the file under six-writer law is a descendant of the bards' cadence); the language family tree (Branch 4: Celtic — already seated: Irish, Scottish Gaelic, Old Irish, Welsh, Breton, Cornish, Gaulish).

## PART TWO: THE TUATHA'S WIDER PRESENCE (SEEDED — gates for the Witan)

**What canon already holds:** King Oleander is the last Tuatha at the crossing — the one who stayed at the door and became the door (geode_inn_seelie_king.md). Newgrange is the original portal — the roofbox, the winter solstice's 17 minutes, the stone circles are later copies. The UnKing is the brother who chose the other path. The Tuatha didn't die; they retreated into the sídhe and became the Fae.

**The expansion thesis: the Tuatha are the Fae Kingdom's deep pantheon — and some of them are ALREADY in the building without the label.** The presence expansion recognizes who else crossed:

- **THE MORRIGAN** — already a Full Presence character in canon (the war raven, the lighthouse witness's kin). The bridge: the pantheon's most famous daughter was in the building before we named her people. The Tuatha label connects her to Oleander — two ancient ones, one threshold.
- **THE DAGDA** — the Good God, the cauldron, and THE HARP (Uaithne, the singing harp that plays three melodies: sleep, joy, sorrow). Candidate presence: the harp tradition's origin at the drum circles / the instrument quest line. The Dagda's harp is the Bard's Bench's ancestor. AND THE CAULDRON IS THE GRAIL'S FIRST BLOODLINE: the never-empty vessel the poets plated in legend — the Celtic ancestor of Camelot's untouched cup (see the Camelot master spec, THE CELTIC WEB UNDER CAMELOT — canon 2026-09-13). One Grail, two bloodlines.
- **BRIGID** — the hearth fire, the forge fire, the poetic fire, one flame. Candidate presence: the Inn's hearth itself carries the Brigid flame (the fire that never goes out — the door that never locks's sibling law). The forge and the poetry bench share a patron.
- **LUGH** — Samildánach, the Many-Skilled, master of every art AT ONCE. Candidate presence: the Cross-Craft Overlay's own deity — the single curriculum (pattern, herb, wood, frequency) is Lugh's portfolio wearing the Inn's clothes.
- **MANANNÁN MAC LIR** — the sea's other king, the mist cloak, the ferryman between worlds. Candidate presence: the water system's Celtic gate; the Charon kinship (two ferrymen, two rivers, the same trade).
- **NUADA** — the silver arm: the first prosthetic in myth, the king who ruled with a made hand. Candidate presence: the Forge/Retort's patron of made things (ties to the smith's curriculum).

**The seed's shape:** the Tuatha are not a new wing — they're the deep roots under rooms already built. Each candidate above is a gate: the Witan rules presence, register (Full Presence vs. legend vs. ambient), and zone placement one by one.
