## THE ROOM

The Restricted Archive  one level below the Library Nook, carved DOWN when the Nook ran out of honest light. THE SHELVING IS WELSH SLATE: the black stone that remembers being mountain, cut into shelves that hold their books the way a cliff holds its strata  each shelf a layer of the kept. The slate swallows sound; the room's quiet is not empty. IT IS THE SECOND-FULLNESS: quieter than silence, it is Welsh.

THE HUSH IS THE DOOR'S LESSON: the room teaches listening by being the thing that listens. A dropped pin does not echo  it is RECEIVED. (The acoustic cousin of the Barabar boum inverted: there, every voice becomes one tone; here, every voice becomes none. The Adequacy family's quietest member.)

## WHAT IS RESTRICTED

Nothing forbidden  everything PROTECTED. The restricted shelves hold: the texts that mention living persons too directly (the legend-layer shielding law, the Shield of Lore's library cousin); the one-copy survivals (the books whose siblings time took  the Ajivika shelf's TEXT-KEEPING cousins); the untranslatables (the books that only open in their own language  the Welsh shelf holds the deepest of these, the survivor's shelf). THE WELSH SHELF: the language that outlasted every empire that swallowed it  the library's proof that keeping IS winning.

THE MARGINS: Quire reads in the margins of the Restricted Archive texts (canon, standing)  the scholar in the neighbor nook, hushed, always looking over a shoulder. THE AJIVIKA SHELF stands in the neighbor nook: the empty spaces held ON PURPOSE, the memorial, not the waiting. The player who heard the boum in the Barabar chambers can play the held hum at the shelf (canon, ruled).

## LAWS

- THE RECEIVED WORD: no echo. The room's design brief is acoustic mercy.
- THE HEARD-TEXT LAW HOLDS HERE HARDEST: everything restricted is still available aloud  the audio is the protected thing too. Nothing silent is also unspoken.
- THE SURVIVOR'S RULE: no book is restricted for its ideas. Only for its fragility, or someone living's privacy.
- STEADY LIGHT: candle-class only, the warmest dark in the Inn.
