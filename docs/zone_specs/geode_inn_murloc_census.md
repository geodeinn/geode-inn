# THE MURLOC CENSUS  The Roundup
## One doc for every murloc in the building (Krista's call, 2026-09-11: "round up all the murlocs hiding everywhere")
### They weren't hiding. They were scattered across ten documents, gargling. Now they're one herd.

## THE MURLOC LAW (the roundup's first finding: they have always had a law)
**The murlocs don't have quests. Don't have dialogue. Don't have names. They HAVE PRESENCE.** The Coast Hub's ambient population  the thing that makes the town occupied rather than merely inhabited. The ocean is not curated. The ocean sends everything, and some of what it sent is... these guys.

**THE WoW HOMAGE LAW:** the gargle is a direct, deliberate homage (the recognition: "oh, I KNOW this")  but the Inn's murlocs are NOT WoW murlocs. They don't aggro, don't drop worthless loot, don't swarm. They just ARE. **THE ADEQUACY LAW:** the murloc rewards NOTHING. Everything else in the Inn rewards deep engagement; the murloc is the Inn saying: you don't have to understand anything. Stand on a dock, listen to a fish-person gargle. The enough is the murloc.

**THE NAME LAW (the critter-groups gate, answered):** murlocs stay NAMELESS BY DESIGN. The exception that proves the rule: SQUEAK (below). The murlocs need no names  the gargling is the community.

## THE TWO THREADS (the census's cross-ties)
- **THE SOUND LAW:** the murloc gargle is the town's CROWD NOISE  mixed under the foghorn, waves, wind: the distant gargle is the town being ALIVE. It BREAKS the contemplative coast  **and the breaking is the kindness.** The coast is heavy; the murlocs are the smile that's real.
- **THE SEALKIE DIASPORA:** the selkie chose the land for the stones; the murlocs chose the land for unclear reasons. The same choice at different levels of consciousness. The murlocs follow the selkie (she smells like the sea). The ocean's diaspora gargles.

## ART + WIRING OWED
- **Art:** murloc ambient cycle sheets (the dock sit-loop, the road shuffle, the lighthouse walk)  TIER 2 movement priority; the congregation needs ONE shared design + variants (the herd is one body, twelve postures  the Room Kit Law applied to critters).
- **Audio:** the gargle sample + the crowd mix (under the foghorn; distance-lawed  quieter up the lighthouse path, absent in the caves).
- **Voice:** deliberately NONE (the law). The murloc speaks "Mrggl" and the Owl answers "Adequate."  the pair works because only one of them uses words.
- **Zoologist note:** the murlocs are the hidden-item layer's comic rung in the Coast Hub  spottable, never collectible. They cannot be hatched, traded, or studied into bestiary entries. Some things the songbook doesn't need. The murloc is the murloc.
