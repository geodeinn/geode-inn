## WHAT THE BAYOU ZONE IS

The Bayou is a World Grid zone — the American South (Louisiana/Gulf Coast). Three layers of civilization stacked vertically: a colonial church (300 years old), buried pre-colonial cities (1,500 years old), and the jungle/earth beneath (60 million years old). The zone's thesis: the Song isn't anyone's. Different voices, same frequency. 42Hz. The Adequate.

**Art Style:** Don't Starve / Tim Burton hand-drawn gothic
**Vibration:** 42Hz Murmur — the Song's quietest expression
**Access:** Main Floor → World Map → Bayou pin

## THE FOUR QUESTS

### Quest 1: "The Door in the Basement"
The traveler finds the colonial structure being consumed by swamp, finds the Bog Fairy Golem, learns of the basement door. The door hums at 42Hz when touched.

### Quest 2: "Going Down Slow"
The Bog Fairy Golem's instruction: "Go down. But go slow. The cities are patient. They can wait for you to take your time."

### Quest 3: "The Buried Cities"
Three intact cities preserved beneath the jungle canopy:

| City | What It Is | Central Element | NPC |
|------|-----------|----------------|-----|
| The Mound City | Oldest. Built around a Mississippian earthwork mound. The oldest architecture in the Inn — a pile of earth, surviving because it is the most adequate structure ever built. | The Mound (42Hz resonance chamber — the Hollow Earth's surface expression) | The Mound-keeper (dry earth and dust golem, older than the Bog Fairy Golem, hums 42Hz, doesn't speak) |
| The River City | Middle. Built along a river that no longer exists. Buildings are PETRIFIED wood — biological becoming geological through time and water. | The petrified boat (transformation made permanent) | The Ferryman (older than Charon, charges a story instead of an obol) |
| The Circle City | Deepest. Built around a North American stone circle. The Inn's architecture (tree, circle, center, radiating zones) was being built here 1,500 years before the Inn existed. | The stone circle (connects to Fae Kingdom via ley lines) | The Stone-singer (sings 42Hz into the stone — the bayou's version of Mona, the mother creation principle in stone instead of wood) |

Touching each city's central element awakens the 42Hz resonance. The cities wake up.

**Reward:** The Bayou Stone — a piece of the Circle City's largest stone. Humms at 42Hz, but filtered through earth and water — the murmur frequency. The quietest 42Hz in the Inn. "The Song doesn't have to be loud to be present."

### Quest 4: "What Was There Before"
Return to the Bog Fairy Golem. The golem reveals: the colonial structure is not the truth — it's the DOOR. The cities are the truth. The circle is the truth. The song in the stone is the truth.

"The Song isn't European. The Song isn't African. The Song isn't Indigenous. The Song isn't anyone's. The Song is: the Song. The mother hummed it in the poplar. The stone-singer hummed it in the circle. The mound-keeper hummed it in the earth. Different voices. Same frequency. 42Hz. The Adequate."

**Rewards:** Bayou Stone (key item) + The Bog Fairy Golem's Keeping (passive buff — sanity drains 20% slower in all zones) + Circle City stone circle as fast-travel node (connects to Fae Kingdom ley lines).
