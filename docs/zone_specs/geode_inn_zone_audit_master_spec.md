## WHAT THE ZONE AUDIT IS

The comprehensive audit of all cultural zones in the Geode Inn, covering: Atlantis, the Cave City, the Desert Bone, the Peruvian Zone, the Norse Realms, the Slavic Zone, the Mediterranean, the Canopy City, the Bayou, India, the Petra Zone, and the Norrath quest system. Each zone has its own design doc — this spec catalogs them all and identifies connections.
