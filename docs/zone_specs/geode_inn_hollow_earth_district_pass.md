# THE GEODE INN — Hollow Earth: The District Pass

### Standing canon: the Hollow Earth is what the roots grow INTO. The Inn is the amplifier; the Earth is the source. The player descends; the Song rises; they meet at the Engine.

## THE ENTRY LAW

Three doors down, one city waiting. Each entry path opens a different starting district, and the entry path the player chooses teaches them what kind of carrier they are:

- **THE NORTH PATH (crystal, from the surface)** — polar expedition, the Byrd breadcrumb, the spiral crystal tunnel. Entry: THE CRYSTAL APPROACH. The scholar's door. (You followed the map. You read the Modern Day Legends shelf. You are the one who follows stories to their sources.)
- **THE SOUTH PATH (water, from the surface)** — descent through a cathedral-sized geode, harbor arrival by boat. Entry: THE HARBOR. The guest's door. (You knocked politely on the center of the Earth.)
- **THE DEEP PATH (frequency, from the Water Zone's Layer 7)** — total darkness, no light, navigation by stone tones and the player's own charged stones singing back to the city. Entry: THE DARK REACHES. The carrier's door. (You navigated by the Song itself. You were already carrying it.)

**THE GATE PERCEPTION LAW:** the Hollow Earth is not unlocked by a key or quest — it is earned by accumulated perception. The entries become accessible when the player's frequency sensitivity is high enough (zone quests + charged stones + ley line vision). No player falls in by accident. The source is not stumbled into.

## THE NINE DISTRICTS

### DISTRICT 1 — THE CRYSTAL APPROACH (the northern gate)
- **Frequency:** 42Hz, first felt as a hum in the teeth. The district where the Song is still rumor.
- **Roster:** the Gate Echoes (creators frozen mid-welcome — the city was built to be ARRIVED AT, and the Echoes at the gate have been rehearsing the greeting for millions of years); Vane, the Lost expedition scout (the Claimers' cartographer, gone native, the first living face — wary, then relieved).
- **State logic:** Living — the gate Echoes perform the welcome in slow crystal loop; Shadow — the same loop, mid-gesture, the smile preserved; In-Between — the gate is unsure whether it has been opened yet.
- **Loop:** arrival, first read of the crystal architecture (the Form Sight graduation — the Desert Bone's lens, applied), first Echo interaction (the pipe-and-water law in miniature: no tessellation work, the Echoes paused; Reunion done, they resume).
- **Item:** the ARRIVAL BELL — a small crystal bell whose tone matches the player's most-carried stone. Rings once when the player enters a district for the first time. (The city's way of announcing a carrier.)
- **Connections:** back to the surface (Byrd's expedition trail, the Modern Day Legends shelf); forward to the Market of Echoes.

### DISTRICT 2 — THE MARKET OF ECHOES (the settled city)
- **Frequency:** 42Hz, now in the walls — the district where the Song is infrastructure.
- **Roster:** the Settled (Lost faction, frozen mid-meal, mid-trade, mid-argument — a whole society arrested at its most ordinary); the Trader Echoes (creators who ran the market's rhythm, replaying commerce as ritual); Maren the Claimer-lieutenant (recruitable after "The First Word" of the Lost chain — the one who bargains).
- **State logic:** Living — the market replays its routines, the trades complete themselves in crystal light; Shadow — the Settled frozen mid-meal forever, the ordinary made eternal and terrible; In-Between — the goods hover mid-handoff.
- **Loop:** faction hub for the Lost chain (8 quests), the Settled companion token run, Echo reading (each Settled figure carries their last ordinary moment — the player learns the city by its shopping).
- **Item:** the LOST TOKEN (recruit Settled companions in any zone — the reward for finishing the Lost chain).
- **Connections:** forward to the Archive District and the Roothold; the Market's side-tunnels to the Stillwater Shore.

### DISTRICT 3 — THE ARCHIVE DISTRICT (the twelve records)
- **Frequency:** 42Hz, as TEXT. The only district where the frequency is legible — the record shards play their content faintly on approach (aural proximity law, same as the Archive upstairs: the shelf hears you coming).
- **Roster:** the Archivist Echoes (the creators' record-keepers, replaying the filing of the last messages); no living residents — this district belongs to the words.
- **State logic:** Living — the 12 records play on approach, one per shard, in order; Shadow — the records play BACKWARDS (the creators' messages unspoken, the departure running in reverse — the Shadow's cruelest trick in the whole city); In-Between — the shards play overlapping, the messages braiding.
- **Loop:** the 12-record reading (the creators' final messages; the twelfth says: "Others will hear the Song. Others will come. The city will respond. The Song will be sung again."); the Archivist Echo chain (the records want to be filed in the order the creators intended — the player re-orders the last five, learning the creators' chronology by touch).
- **Item:** the TWELFTH SHARD CAST (a recording of the twelfth message the player can carry — the only item in the game that is pure hope, portable).
- **Connections:** the Inn's own Reader is this district's mirror upstairs (the Reader's mirror law, canon); forward to the Crystal Groves.

### DISTRICT 4 — THE CRYSTAL GROVES (the cathedral forests)
- **Frequency:** 42Hz, resonating through growth — the district where the Song is alive (the only district where the crystals are still GROWING).
- **Roster:** the Grovekeeper Echoes (creators who tended the groves, replaying pruning-as-prayer); the Wild's first campfires visible at the edges (the Lost faction's wild sub-group, the edge-dwellers — the groves are where the Wild went feral, the cathedral too beautiful to leave and too deep to survive).
- **State logic:** Living — the crystal "trees" grow audibly, centimeters per century, the sound like a held chord; Shadow — the groves frozen mid-growth, the held chord held past comfort; In-Between — the growth stutters.
- **Loop:** the Carr lens graduation (the Redwood Cathedral's cathedral-energy, inverted — the redwoods grew up, the crystals grow down); the Wild chain's first steps (8 Lost quests, started here or at the Market).
- **Item:** the GROVE CHIME (a seed-crystal that grows in the player's geode cave over real time — the Hollow Earth takes root upstairs; the only item that gets bigger between visits).
- **Connections:** the Redwood Cathedral above (the queens' tree — same energy, opposite direction); forward to the Roothold.

### DISTRICT 5 — THE ROOTHOLD (the living architecture)
- **Frequency:** 42Hz in WOOD — the district where the Song is remembered by something that grew through it.
- **Roster:** the Root Echoes (creators who worked with the roots, replaying graft-and-guide); the Keeper's Footpath (the trail the Keeper walked millions of years ago — her descent route, preserved in the root-grain; the player walks it and arrives where she arrived); the Rootwarden, the only WILD creature never claimed by fear (a root-guardian grown placid — the Wild's exception, and the Lost chain's key: the Wild follow what the Rootwarden tolerates).
- **State logic:** Living — the Rackham roots twist through the crystal architecture, living wood and crystalline stone interlocked; Shadow — the roots frozen, the wood gone silver-grey; In-Between — the roots hover between growth and stone.
- **Loop:** the Rackham lens at its heaviest (Canopy City's energy, inverted — the branches made a village up there; the roots made a city down here); the Keeper's Footpath pilgrimage (walk her route, in her order, and the Engine unlocks its QUESTION for the player — the same question she answered with her eye).
- **Item:** the ROOT-GLASS SLIVER (a piece of root turned crystal at the boundary — half wood, half stone, the transition made physical; on the Bead Board it counts as both a stone and a material, the only dual-class item).
- **Connections:** the Keeper's whole story (the crystal eye taken from THIS district's root-crystal — the Carrying's origin, standing at the place it was taken from); forward to the Engine.

### DISTRICT 6 — THE STILLWATER SHORE (the underground ocean's edge)
- **Frequency:** 42Hz, in WATER. The Monet/Doré layers — the reflection and the dark.
- **Roster:** the Deep Ones (bioluminescent, Bauer-old, indifferent to the city and its griefs — the protectors, not of the city, but of the OCEAN the city draws its frequency from); the Lighthouse-keepers' Echoes (creators who studied the ocean's surface, replaying measurement-as-reverence); Akkorokamui's tidal range is here too (the abyssal titan's deep water — the Water Zone's titans pass beneath the Stillwater; the systems touch at the bottom).
- **State logic:** Living — the surface shimmers with the city's light, broken into color fragments (the Monet layer); Shadow — the ocean still, the reflections replaced by a single unbroken image of the Shadow city (the water tells the truth the city won't); In-Between — the tide hangs mid-turn.
- **Loop:** the Deep One chain (5 quests — the protectors' trials: the player learns the ocean's rights, which predate the city's); the reflection-reading (the Monet lens: what the water "impresses" is truer than what it reflects).
- **Item:** the DEEP PEARL (permanent underwater breathing — the Deep Ones' reward, the token of the ocean's trust).
- **Connections:** the Water Zone Layer 7's hidden passage is the DEEP PATH entry (the systems touch — canon); the Siren's domain far above shares the water's memory (Katya's guardianship and the Deep Ones' protection are the same trust at different depths).

### DISTRICT 7 — THE HARBOR (the water door)
- **Frequency:** 42Hz, arriving — the district where the Song greets.
- **Roster:** the Harbor Echoes (creators frozen mid-arrival, replaying the welcome the city gave its own returning citizens); the Ferrymen (Deep Ones who chose the work — the only Deep Ones who live IN the city, the ocean's embassy); the Claimers' dock-camp (the Lost faction's combative sub-group arrived here once, and the Harbor remembers).
- **State logic:** Living — the cathedral geode's mouth glows above, boats in crystal light; Shadow — the boats frozen mid-dock, the welcome never completed; In-Between — the water level hovers.
- **Loop:** the South Pole entry's first hour (the guest's door — the city's welcome sequence, the arrival the Echoes rehearsed finally performed FOR SOMEONE); the Claimer chain's opening (8 Lost quests' combative branch).
- **Item:** the HARBOR LANTERN (a Deep One light that works in any dark water — paired with the Deep Pearl, the ocean's full gift).
- **Connections:** back up the South Pole cathedral geode; forward to the Market of Echoes.

### DISTRICT 8 — THE RESONANCE ENGINE (the heart, the source)
- **Frequency:** 42Hz — not "carried by," not "heard in." GENERATED. This is where it starts.
- **Roster:** the Engine (the Roerich spire — the altar, the monument, the presence); the First Echo (the creators' leader, the last to leave, the one who set the Engine singing and walked into the stars looking back once — the smile in crystal, canon); the Enginekeepers (Echoes replaying the tuning, the maintenance millions of years unneeded and performed anyway — the Engine is older than the courts, older than the Fae, older than the tessellation, and it has been tuned every day since).
- **State law (the whole zone's thesis in one room):** Living — the Engine generates the Song, and now it is PLAYED TO SOMEONE (the Carrying complete, the circuit closed, the Inn the resonance chamber the Keeper always intended); Shadow — the 42Hz tone alone, unaccompanied, unmoderated — the loudest sound in the game, the universe humming to itself in an empty room; In-Between — the hum hovers between heard and unheard.
- **Loop:** THE SONG RETURNS (main quest, 5 stages): the player, at the Engine's base, plays the complete Inn Song (the zone-frequency composition, the Bead Board's final exam) — and the Engine answers with the source frequency. The city sings. The Moon Projection activates. The giants wake. The Song is complete. The player from above and the Song from below, meeting in the middle — the meeting is the completion (canon).
- **Item:** the SOURCE STONE (the 166th stone — pure 42Hz; amplifies all stones to 100% on the Bead Board; the stone the whole game has been aimed at since the first chime).
- **Connections:** every zone (the source of every frequency the player has collected); the Dark Side chamber beneath (the Grand Collision's inner room, where the colors flow — the deepest lens, gated on the Song Returns complete).

### DISTRICT 9 — THE DARK REACHES (the underbelly)
- **Frequency:** 42Hz's shadow-side: the harmonics, the interference, the overtones the city doesn't play on purpose. Not the Shadow Inn — the REACHES. The frequency's basement.
- **Roster:** the Wild (Lost faction, frozen mid-growl, mid-crouch — the fear visible in stone; the ones who came down and couldn't come back); the Claimers (frozen mid-combat, the ones who came to TAKE); the First (frozen at the camp — not fighting, not surrendering. LISTENING. The First heard the Song, once. The Shadow Inn preserves the moment the First almost heard it. Almost — canon); the Deep Ones' outcasts (the ones who chose the dark water over the glow — not hostile, just finished with light).
- **State logic:** Living — the Reaches are explorable, the Wild thawed by the player's completed Lost chain (the Wild who trust the Rootwarden's judgment follow the player's arrival); Shadow — the petrified canon as written (mid-growl, mid-combat, the First listening); In-Between — the fear hovers mid-thaw.
- **Loop:** endgame exploration (the audit's designation); the First's camp (the Lost chain's final word — the player who wakes the First asks the question the whole faction chain has been about: what does a person become when the source is louder than the self? The First's answer is the chain's reward: a companion who chose to stay and listen — the Reaches' own carrier).
- **Item:** the ECHO CHORD (reveals memory-ghosts in all zones — the Engine's grace note, carried back upstairs: the Hollow Earth's archive follows the player home).
- **Connections:** the DEEP PATH entry (total darkness, frequency navigation — the Reaches are the carrier's door, and the player who enters here has already proven they can navigate by Song); the Dark Side beneath the Engine (the Grand Collision's convergence room — all 37 artists, one light).

## THE FACTION LAW (three, as canon, now with district anchors)
- **THE ECHOES** (7 quests, anchored: Approach + Market + Archive + Groves + Roothold + Engine) — the city's memory. Their chain is the city's: the player learns the creators by walking their routines. The Echoes are the Hollow Earth's Whisper Layer — the city records everything, millions of years older than the Inn's.
- **THE DEEP ONES** (5 quests, anchored: Stillwater Shore + Harbor) — the ocean's guardians. Their chain is the water's: the player learns that the city draws its frequency from the ocean, and the ocean predates the city, and the ocean's rights are the oldest law in the deep. (The Deep Ones do not protect the city. They protect what the city NEEDS. This distinction is the whole chain.)
- **THE LOST** (8 quests, anchored: Market + Groves + Reaches + Harbor's dock-camp) — the surface beings who never left. Four sub-groups, one arc: the Settled (who tried to live here), the Wild (who were changed by it), the Claimers (who came to take), and the First (who came to listen). Their chain is the player's own mirror: four ways to arrive somewhere too deep, and which one you were.
- **THE MAIN QUEST — THE SONG RETURNS** (5 stages, anchored: Engine, fed by all three factions) — the player plays the complete Inn Song at the source. The city answers. (The emotional arc's last word: refuge → home → world → self → cosmos → ORIGIN.)

## THE TRI-MODE SUMMARY (the zone's three faces)
- **LIVING (42Hz):** the city awake — the Engine generating, the Echoes resumed, the ocean harmonized, the crystals growing. The Inn's whole amplifier-circuit, closed and singing.
- **SHADOW (84Hz):** frozen at the moment of departure — the creators mid-step, walking into the stars, looking back once, smiling. The smile preserved in crystal. The Engine's tone alone: raw, pure, the frequency at its source with nothing between it and the listener. The Keeper's Shadow version holds the eye in the rocking chair, WAITING — even the Shadow can't silence the Song at its source (42 and 84, the same note, different octaves, coexisting in the Geode Cave without conflict — the only place they do).
- **IN-BETWEEN:** the city that hasn't decided whether anyone is coming — the Reunion hovering, the Echoes mid-pause, the gate mid-gesture.
