## WHAT THE WEAVER IS

The Weaver resides in a stone vault carved directly beneath the Observatory glass on the Cosmology Level. Where the Astronomer tracks celestial trajectories and Nyx watches the abyssal dark, the Weaver converts raw cosmic data and player experience into physical, tactile reality — thread, knot, and pigment.

The foundational truth: **The Storyteller tells the story; The Weaver shows it.** One works in acoustic memory and spoken legend; the other works in thread, knot, and pigment. Word and image: both are adequate, but only together do they form the complete record of existence.

The Weaver experiences 42Hz not as audio hum or spectral light, but as continuous mechanical tension across an infinite warp frame.
