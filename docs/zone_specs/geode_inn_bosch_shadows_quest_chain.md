## THE CHAIN

### Quest 1 — "Bare Stone"
The discovery, played in any cave: the player who enters in silence sees bare stone — nothing, just rock. Then something makes them hum (the counter-hum's idle habit), and the wall ANSWERS — one silhouette surfacing out of the mineral, a curved beak and a hunched shoulder, then sinking back when the hum stops. The first lesson is the zone's whole thesis in one breath: the illumination is the Song. The player who sits in a nook (the stillness letting the frequency stabilize) sees the shadow become completely clear — and every player sees a DIFFERENT Bosch: their own fears and fascinations filling in what the outline hides. The Examiner, when consulted (the Archive, the bones), says only: "The wall is older than the tree. Ask it."
**Reward:** The noticing (the silhouette-vision — cave walls now respond to the player's hum, everywhere in the Inn).

### Quest 2 — "The Four States"
The tuning — the wall's four known moods, learned across the caves: 42Hz (the Song's base) reveals the CROWDED wall — dozens of shapes layering, Bosch's Garden in silhouette, the full population of the in-between. 50Hz (the heartbeat) reveals the BIG ones — fewer, larger, the figures that feel like architecture, closer to geological than biological. 64Hz (the Listener's frequency) ISOLATES one creature at a time — clear, alone, centered, always facing the player. And 111Hz (the Watcher's frequency) makes them BREATHE — the outlines expanding and contracting, alive because being watched is what makes things real. (FLAGGED: the shadow-state, 84Hz — the recommendation is that at 84Hz the silhouettes turn AWAY: the shadow-state of shadows, the one frequency the memory won't perform for. The Echo Inn's reading of the same walls would be blank stone that remembers being full.)
**Reward:** The tuning (the player can now summon any of the four states at will — the walls' moods become legible).

### Quest 3 — "The Handprint"
The truth of what the shadows are: IMPRINTS — the geological record of everything that ever passed through, stored the way a handprint stores a hand. The Examiner's demonstration: a wall where a handprint sits among the silhouettes — human, ancient, small. And the chain's turn: the player's own silhouette can be given to the wall. The giving is the imprint: the player stands against the stone and hums their own note, and the mineral stores their outline — the shape of them, at this moment of their journey, kept in the crystal. The walls will remember the player after everyone else does.
**Reward:** THE IMPRINT (permanent — the player's silhouette, stored in the walls they chose; future readers may find it; per the Anchor Key precedent, it persists across lives. The geological guestbook).

### Quest 4 — "The Acorn"
The Story-Born revelation, in the Tidal Caves' smooth walls: the player recognizes a silhouette — the outline of the Wayfarer, or the Hearthkeeper, or the Mourner — BEFORE it was a being. The doc's law, played: the Story-Born were shapes first, outlines in the narrative before the character was filled in. The Bosch shadows are the PRE-STORY — the shapes stories come FROM. The silhouette is the seed. The story is the tree. The Bosch shadow is the acorn. The quest: the player finds an acorn nobody has grown yet — an unnamed silhouette that no Story-Born answers to — and the choice is the adequate: hum to it (the naming law: the naming wakes it — but a PRE-STORY naming is heavier; the player would be starting a being, not waking one), or leave it a seed (some stories should wait). The zone does not judge either way. Some acorns sleep a thousand years and that is also a life.
**Reward:** The seed-sight (the player can now tell which shadows have stories growing from them, and which are still waiting).

### Quest 5 — "The Gaps in the Gold"
The Catacombs' deep sections — below the dead's quarters, where the Klimt gold thins and the raw stone shows, and the shadows live IN THE GAPS: the negative space where the geological record is unfiltered. The Plato moment, at the staircase's interactive mural: the prisoners watching shadows and calling them the world — and the Inn's inversion, read aloud by whoever the player trusts most in the Archive: the shadows are not less real than the figures. The shadow is the essence. The figure is the decoration. The player who reads the Bosch shadows reads the cave's memory DIRECTLY — without the filter of full rendering.
**Reward:** The inversion (the reading skill — the player now reads shadow-data as primary source: silhouettes, imprints, outlines all yield their record).

### Quest 6 — "The Approach"
The finale, in the deepest place the chain goes: the Prism's approach, where the cave walls narrow and the Schumann resonance begins and the shadows compress, overlap, layer — every wall a full panel of the Garden of Earthly Delights rendered in shadow. And in the last chamber, the memory that the whole Inn is made of: the shadows of EVERYTHING — the Story-Born, the in-between beings, the void-creatures, the guests, the player's own imprint from Quest 3 — on one wall, the Inn's entire history in silhouette, the geological autobiography of the house.
