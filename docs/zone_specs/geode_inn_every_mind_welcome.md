# THE EVERY-MIND WELCOME
## The Inn's access charter: any way of thinking is a comfortable one
### Written 2026-09-11, from Krista's call: "we tapped the tactile side of sound with the vibration/Mohs layer, now we have ASL  what other ways can we make this an environment where any way of thinking is a comfortable one? We were neuro-friendly from the very start."

---

## THE THESIS

The North-Star rule already says the Inn's goal is that every player feel the way Elio feels in it: at home, a place to simply BE. This charter names the WAYS. The Inn was neuro-friendly before it had the word for it  the 50 BPM heartbeat (no adrenaline architecture), no death mechanics, water that returns you gently, no streaks, no login guilt. The pattern this audit found, again: HALF THIS CHARTER IS CENSUS. Laws that already stand, now named and made legible. The rest is wiring.

The two channels already built  sound-as-touch (the Mohs vibration layer) and ASL (the Hands Version)  were never features. They were the thesis showing itself early: MEANING HAS MANY ROADS, AND NONE OF THEM IS THE ONLY ONE. This charter walks the rest of the roads on purpose.

---

## THE LAWS

### 1. THE PLAYER'S TEMPO LAW (time is the player's)
Nowhere does the Inn hustle. Pluck dances to YOUR tempo  the game follows the player, never the reverse, and this is now the standing rule for every rhythm mechanic in the building. Zeke's tide clock teaches, it never chases. The Zoologist path is non-timed by ruling; the Beat Line's timing games are invitations, not tests. No streak mechanics, no daily-login guilt  Ziggy counts returns with WARMTH ("exactly how long it's been"), never with loss. Time belongs to the player; the Inn keeps the clock so the player doesn't have to.

### 2. THE TOUCH LAYER (the Mohs layer's other hand)
The vibration/Mohs layer made sound tactile; this makes the STONES tactile. Haptic Mohs: stones feel like their hardness (1 through 10, talc to diamond under the fingers  the hardness scale a player can literally press). Worry stones: every zone offers one small stone shaped for the hand, with no function whatsoever  the Adequacy Law for thumbs. THE BEAD COMPOSER AS SANCTIONED STIM: sorting, stringing, repeating  the most tactile activity in the building is also a real product (the jewelry SaaS). Stimming isn't tolerated in the Inn; it's CATERED TO.

### 3. THE MOVE-FREELY LAW (movement is legitimate input)
Pacing, wandering, dancing, flying in circles  these are gameplay, not the absence of gameplay. The Flight Law already rules no stamina meters and no fuel; the Common Room's emotes make bodies eloquent; the Tramp's class puts real gestures in the player's hands. A player who needs to move while thinking is not "away." The wander AI models this: the Inn's own residents pace, patrol, and trot  movement is how the building thinks too.

### 4. THE NO-DEMAND LAW (social is optional everywhere)
The murloc rule, generalized: the Inn is interested in you, never needy of you. No NPC demands response; no gathering is mandatory (the Gathering Law guarantees a space, never an obligation); the Common Room keeps no data and keeps no score. Comfortable alone, and never lonely  the staff are company, not audience.

### 5. THE SOUND DIAL (the senses get a register, like the humor)
The Pacing Law for comedy, extended to the senses: sensory density varies by level on purpose (the Cosmology quiet, the Arcade bright), and the quiet rooms are marked by feel  a door that looks calm IS calm, not a settings menu item. Inn Mode's room DSP profiles extend to a player-held dial: the Inn hums at your volume, and it stays there. The building bends to the ear; the ear never has to bend to the building.

### 6. THE HEARD-TEXT LAW (every text can be spoken)
The audiobook production spec (74 voice profiles, the Bard's read-aloud canon) becomes an access layer: any text in the Inn can be HEARD, in-voice, in-canon. Reading is never the only door into the Archive. And the type: dyslexia-friendly letterforms offered in the Inn's own letterpress styles  the Title Card Law's cousin (accessibility rendered as craft, never as afterthought).

### 7. THE SHAPES-CARRY-MEANING LAW (the Linework-as-UI discovery)
The Linework-as-UI system was built as art direction  heavy cel-shade for tradesmen, soft flowing for lore, sharp angular for guardians  and it turns out to be an access layer that was there all along: MEANING NEVER RIDES COLOR ALONE. Shape carries it. Formally canonized here as access law: no information in the Inn is color-only, sound-only, or text-only. Every meaning has at least two roads (the thesis, made rule).

### 8. THE MEMORY PALACE (spatial thinkers have a home)
The Archive is ROOMS. Knowledge is kept in architecture  the Archive is a memory palace by design, so minds that think in spaces can WALK the collection, and the room IS the category. The Blueprint's room-by-room organization doubles as a mnemonic map: if you can remember the house, you can find the knowledge.

### 9. THE ONE-GRAMMAR LAW (pattern and systems thinkers)
The Cross-Craft Overlay already rules pattern, herb chemistry, and wood property as ONE grammar at different scales  pattern thinkers have a home in the crafting, and the law is now named as a welcome. The Ripple Ledger, discoverable in-game, is the systems-thinker's playground: causality as a collectible.

### 10. THE PICTURE PATH (curiosity is a valid route)
Every door open, no forced sequence: the world map hub means the Inn is navigable in ANY ORDER a mind likes  linear players get their line, curious players get their rabbit holes, and both arrive. The da Vinci notebook model is the design pattern: the wandering research path is HONORED, not corrected. ADHD-mode exploration isn't a bug the onboarding smooths over; it's a legitimate way through the whole building.

### 11. THE GENTLE-DEPTH LAW (weight is offered, never forced)
The Prism's mercy clause, generalized: the existential material (the Question, the endings, the showings) is always entered by choice, and never shows a player more than they can carry. Depth is underneath the comfort; the player sets the dive.

---

### 12. THE STEADY LIGHT LAW (the founding rule, found and named  Project Bible, Design Principle #8, written before the building had walls)
"Sine-clamped lighting, non-assertive transitions, gentle sensory experience  the Inn is a sanctuary, not a stimulation loop." Every light change in the Inn is a smooth curve, never a hard cut  the founding photo-safety law, now named in the charter it always belonged to. THE FLICKER TAXONOMY (the law's clarifying clause): the Inn's flickers are CANDLELIGHT-CLASS  slow, warm, irregular, procedural (the oil lamp's 2-3 block radius, the hearth's noise-function fire, no two visits the same)  and candlelight-class flicker is not a violation, it IS the aesthetic (sanctuary light behaves like fire). STROBE-CLASS  hard on/off cuts, high-contrast flashes  never enters the building. THE COMPANION PRINCIPLE (#9, folded in): amber wavelengths, 590-620nm, the firelight spectrum that says "safe, rest, explore"  the deliberate opposite of compulsion-loop design. The hub's knotwork pulses slow; the Pulse Line breathes rather than blinks; every glow in the building is a sine wave wearing amber. (Audit catch, corrected this pass: the digital companion app spec's "flashes white on all devices" now reads "sine-clamped amber glow"  the founding law wins all ties.)

## THE WELCOME LAW (the charter's spine)

Access features live ON THE BUILDING, in the Inn's own language, not in a clinical settings menu  the Title Card Law's principle at charter scale: captions as intertitles, quiet rooms as calm doors, sign language as curriculum, haptics as hardness. The accommodation IS the aesthetic. If the Inn ever needs a word like "accommodation," it has failed its own law: nothing here is a concession, because every way of thinking was always going to be the point.

# PART TWO: THE COMB-THROUGH (2026-09-11, Krista's call: "let's comb through what we know about neurodivergence and the ways it can be affected, so all potential quirks people have can relax at our Inn")
## The neurotype census: one by one, what each mind needs to relax  and whether the Inn already serves it

THE METHOD (the census law): for each way of thinking, three lines  what the mind needs, what the Inn already has (standing law or built system), and the wire if one is owed. The census is written in the Inn's language, not the clinic's: no diagnosis happens at the door; the Inn doesn't ask WHY your feet pace, it just leaves the floor open.

THE FINDING THAT MATTERS (the convergence): the census kept resolving to the same small handful of laws  low demand, predictability, multi-sensory redundancy, gentle time, no dark patterns. One design serves many minds. That is universal design's whole proof, and it means the Inn doesn't need a feature per neurotype  it needs the laws it mostly already has, HONORED EVERYWHERE. The founding instinct was right; the charter makes it legible.

---

## THE CENSUS

### 1. Minds that need predictability (the comfortable-known)
**Needs:** familiar spaces that stay familiar; change that arrives slowly and visibly; no surprise re-arrangements of home.
**Already:** the world map hub (rooms where they've always been); the return mechanics (Ziggy's warm count); the founding aesthetic (the Inn is a building, not a feed).
**The wire  THE PREDICTABILITY PROMISE:** the Inn's rooms stay where you left them. Building changes happen the way a real inn renovates  gently, overnight, with a notice (the mail-cart crew relaying a rug, a sign in the movers' own handwriting). Nothing the player loves silently disappears. Seasonal content ADDS rooms; it never replaces them.

### 2. Minds that run on interest (the deep-dive engines)
**Needs:** hyperfocus honored; special interests fed, never capped; the rabbit hole treated as a road.
**Already:** the Archive (an infinite interest-library); the Picture Path; the da Vinci notebook model; the parks/stone/lore systems that reward depth with depth.
**The wire  THE NO-CAP LAW:** the Inn never says "that's enough about rocks." There is always one more book, one more field note, one more stratum. Deep divers are the Archive's favorite customers, and the Archive never closes.

### 3. Minds with busy executive function (the many-threads weavers)
**Needs:** no guilt for dropped threads; returns that feel like homecoming; dopamine from presence, not pressure.
**Already:** the Player's Tempo Law; no streaks, no login guilt; Ziggy's warmth; the EchoMelody log (progress IS the kept record, not a maintained streak).
**The wire  THE ANTI-SKINNER LAW (formalized):** the Inn uses no dark patterns, ever  no variable-ratio reward drips, no loss-framed comebacks ("your streak is dying!"), no shaming gaps. The geode cave shows what you GATHERED, never a case of empty slots mocking you. The collection screens celebrate presence. Anything the Inn wants the player to feel, it says in the open.

### 4. Minds that read with friction (the decoders)
**Needs:** text that can be heard; letterforms that don't fight; meaning never locked inside paragraphs.
**Already:** the Heard-Text Law; the letterpress type gates; the Title Card Law.
**The wire  THE MARGIN NOTE:** every long lore text carries a one-line margin note (the Archivist's own hand) with its heart  the reader who wants the depth dives; the reader who wants the door gets the door. Navigation by landmark, never by coordinate (the Escher Between excepted  the confusion there is diegetic and short).

### 5. Minds with sensitive ears (the overstimulation escapees)
**Needs:** rooms that calm; volume that belongs to the player; no surprise loud.
**Already:** the Sound Dial; the sensory pacing per level; the Steady Light Law's twin discipline; quiet rooms marked by feel.
**The wire  THE DECOMP ROOM:** every level guarantees one zero-demand, low-stimulus room (the Bone Room's hush, the Library Nook's lamp, the Cosmology's floor). The building's promise: a calm door is always one level away, and it looks like what it is.

### 6. Minds that process speech in noise with effort (the clarity listeners)
**Needs:** dialogue never masked by music; instructions never audio-only; words foregrounded.
**Already:** the Title Card Law; no sound-only information (charter law); the Inn's whole audio DNA (Empire Ears IEM engineering).
**The wire  THE CLARITY MIX LAW:** every Inn mix is mastered like a stage monitor  the voice, the story, the instruction sits in the front of the mix, always (the IEM engineering canon, now named as a welcome). Nothing important is ever whispered under a waterfall.

### 7. Minds that count and are counted on (the loop-checkers)
**Needs:** no compulsion loops; completion celebrated, never demanded; no permanently missed anything gnawing.
**Already:** no streaks; the Ripple Ledger's weighted endings (depth without 100% demands); the Adequacy Law.
**The wire  THE NO-GNAW LAW:** no content is permanently missable in a way the game rubs in  the Lost Dutchman's whole lesson is the legend of the thing nobody needs to find. 100% completion is a feast for those who want it, never a debt for those who don't. The Inn's ledgers (the Mycorrhizal Trade) show who owes what as pictures and webs, not arithmetic  the dyscalculia courtesy rides along free.

### 8. Minds that startle easily (the wary-shouldered)
**Needs:** no jump scares, no hand-grabbing; dread by choice, never ambush.
**Already:** the gentle transitions; the Steady Light Law; horror-as-atmosphere (the strigoi forest, the restricted Archive).
**The wire  THE NO-BOO LAW (formalized):** the Inn may be eerie, never startling. No jump scares, ever, anywhere  the dread zones earn their dread the way the Bone Room earns its hush: by being true, not by grabbing. Nothing takes control from the player's hands; every cutscene can be set down like a teacup. The Inn never grabs the wrist.

### 9. Minds in motion (the pacers, tappers, and tic-carriers)
**Needs:** movement as legitimate; no punishment for restless bodies; timing that follows the player.
**Already:** the Move-Freely Law; the Player's Tempo Law; the falling abolition; the patience law.
**The wire  THE STILLNESS EXCEPTION:** no activity in the Inn fails a player for moving (the dodge games forgive; the patience roads exist beside every timing game). The Inn's own residents pace  the precedent is the building itself.

### 10. Minds that cross the senses (the synesthetes)
**Needs:** nothing  they bring their own wiring; but they DESERVE to see the building that speaks their language.
**Already:** the whole resonance canon  stones with tones, hardness with haptics, frequencies with colors, the PRISM (sound made light, literally).
**The wire  NAME IT:** the Inn is cross-modal BY DESIGN, and the Prism is the synesthete's heart room  the census's only "need" that's actually a gift the Inn already gives. The welcome: the building that maps light to sound is home turf for minds that do it natively.

### 11. Minds that need the floor held (the grounded-and-grounding)
**Needs:** co-regulation; tactile objects; a tempo that steadies.
**Already:** the 50 BPM heartbeat architecture (a calm pulse the whole building keeps); the worry stones; the Bead Composer as sanctioned stim; haptic Mohs.
**The wire  THE GROUNDING TRAY:** every level's decomp room also holds a tray of regulation objects  the beads, the worry stone, the soft-shelled hatch egg  items with no function except to be handled. The heartbeat is the building's hand on the shoulder, at resting pulse, always.

### 12. Minds that mask (the performers come home)
**Needs:** a place where the performance can come off; socializing that never demands a face.
**Already:** the No-Demand Law; social optional everywhere; the emote language (expression without exposure); the Common Room's no-retention courtesy.
**The wire  THE MASK HOOK:** by the Inn's door, in the fiction, the coat hooks hold whatever anyone carried in  and nothing about the building ever asks for it back. The staff perform nothing and expect nothing performed; the warmth is already on, the way a fire is.

### 13. Minds that walk by ear (the low-vision and blind travelers)
**Needs:** a building navigable without sight; the world legible through sound and touch.
**Already:** the zone hums (every zone has a sound identity); the stone tones; the Heard-Text Law; the haptic Mohs; the audiobook canon.
**The wire  THE WALK-BY-EAR LAW:** the Inn is navigable by sound alone  the zone hums are wayfinding, the heartbeat is the north, the Song is the compass (the 42Hz lore, made literally usable). The screen-reader build gates are queued in the builder sync when the web beta hardens; the LAW stands now so the build honors it later.

### 14. Minds that remember differently (the working-memory travelers)
**Needs:** no quest demanding unaided recall; the world remembering for you.
**Already:** the Memory Palace (spatial knowledge); the Archive itself (the Inn's whole thesis: an external memory, kept).
**The wire  THE KEEP-TRAIL:** the player's journal keeps itself  findings, field notes, half-finished threads, all findable again without shame. The building remembers so you don't have to. (This is the Inn's deepest identity, offered as a courtesy: the Archive was always a memory aid before it was anything.)

---

## THE CONVERGENCE (the census's summary)

Fourteen minds, and the needs resolved into five load-bearing laws the whole building can stand on: LOW DEMAND (nothing needy, nothing punishing), PREDICTABILITY (home stays home), REDUNDANCY (every meaning, every road, two roads minimum), GENTLE TIME (the clock serves, never chases), and NO DARK PATTERNS (the Inn says what it means  in everything, always). Every named law above is one of those five wearing a different coat. The Inn doesn't accommodate fourteen ways of thinking. It is ONE building, designed so fourteen ways of thinking don't need accommodating  they need a chair, a fire, and a floor that doesn't move. That was the founding instinct, and the census just proved it: the sanctuary scale is universal because comfort is universal.
