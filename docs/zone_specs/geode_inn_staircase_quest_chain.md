## THE SIX QUESTS

### Quest 1 — "The Fourth Fire" (the Smiths)
The player learns the Inn's three fires — Erik's feast fire, Kali's cremation fire, the Wayfarer's campfire — and then is handed the hammer. The Smiths don't lecture. The Smiths hand you hot metal and watch what your hands know that your mind doesn't. The lesson is somatic: the metal tells you when it's ready. "Listen with your hands." The quest's success state is not a quiz — it's the moment the player stops flinching and starts hearing. **The gate: the fourth way of knowing (embodied) joins the Inn's epistemology — timing, measurement, sensing, and now the body.**

### Quest 2 — "The Gear That Remembers" (the Clockmaker)
As designed: the master gear that drives the ascending music is failing. The player diagnoses by resonance, then chooses — Reinforce (the Blacksmith's chord as new foundation), Bridge (the resonance bridge to the Fae frequency), or Accept (the gear transforms; the staircase breathes rather than ticks). **The choice retunes the entire zone's music permanently — the player's first state change that the BUILD hears.** Reward: the Clockmaker's Gear charm (tune any mechanism in the Inn).

### Quest 3 — "The Conversation" (the three steps)
Socrates at the bottom step (the question), Plato at the middle (the Form), Aristotle at the top (the category). The player climbs — and each philosopher asks ONE question and won't take a borrowed answer. The Cave, the Forms, the Ladder of Being — but as conversation, not curriculum. **The gate: the player must answer each in their own words; the philosophers' approval is the staircase's key change made audible.**

### Quest 4 — "The Conversation Continues" (the Council of Thinkers)
The philosophers' descendants — Tesla, Da Vinci, Newton, Einstein — on the landing's benches, mid-argument that has lasted centuries: the framer became the visualizer (Plato → Da Vinci), the classifier became the calculator (Aristotle → Newton). The player carries a question from each philosopher up to their scientific descendant. **The through-line of the whole Inn in one room: the conversation never stopped; it just changed instruments.** (The Council's own records live in the engine — the chain wires them into the climb.)

### Quest 5 — "The Retort and the Cook" (mind and heart)
The Staircase's two residents in residence: the Retort (the MIND of alchemy — theory, distillation, the one who thinks) and the Cook (the HEART — care as a load-bearing ingredient, the one who feeds). Together they teach the player the same transmutation twice: once as theory, once as sustenance. **The gate: the player performs the same transformation with the Retort's glassware and the Cook's pan — and must notice the difference is not the process. The difference is the attention.**

### Quest 6 — "The Stone" (the Philosopher's Stone stage — the finale)
The top of the ascent: the stage. The five emblems' journey completes — Nigredo's blackening (the bottom flight) through the Stone's gold at the top. The player stands on the stage and the ascending music resolves. **The Turn-key turns: the Stone is not given — the player IS the last transmutation. The stage's light is the albedo after the whole Inn's nigredo.** The reward is the STAGE itself: a venue the player can return to and play — the Inn's own open mic at the top of the world. (Connects: the Stone Circle Chamber's chord, the Cosmology level above, the Novel's Part 4 — the Ascent.)
