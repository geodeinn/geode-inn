# THE ATLANTIS ZONE — Master Specification
## The Drowned City — Underwater Hub and the Silica Thread's Origin

---

## WHAT ATLANTIS IS

Atlantis is the crown jewel of the Geode Inn's underwater traversal layer — the first major discovery players make when they dig down, break through into water, and transition to underwater exploration. It serves as the gateway to the entire underwater realm.

The narrative reveal: the city didn't sink. It CHOSE the deep. The crystal needed the ocean's pressure to remain stable. The Atlanteans became the first Merfolk. The "destruction" was deliberate transformation — the Silica Thread's first experiment in consciousness adapting to a new medium.

**Access:** Dig down from any catacomb level → hit the water table → transition to Merfolk mode → descend to Atlantis

---

## THE MERFOLK FORM

Upon first water transition, the player unlocks Merfolk — the base aquatic class. Other aquatic species (Naga, Selkie) unlock through quests.

| Stat | Value | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Speed | High | Fast underwater, slow on land |
| Strength | Medium | Tail strikes powerful, land combat weakened |
| Intelligence | High | Full cognitive access — the archive works underwater |
| Endurance | Medium | Must return to air pockets periodically (oxygen mechanic) |
| Charisma | Variable | Mysterious to surface dwellers, familiar to deep-sea NPCs |

**Abilities:**
- Echolocation Pulse — sonar ping revealing structures/creatures/passages (expanding ring of light, no flashing)
- Current Riding — sense and ride ocean currents for fast travel (organic highway system)
- Bioluminescent Marking — leave glowing amber markers to track explored areas (fade over 5 minutes)
- Deep Dive — descend to extreme depths, unlocks deepest zones
- Silica Sense — feel quartz deposits through water (the Silica Thread vibrates in Merfolk bones)

**Aesthetic:** Iridescent scales (deep blue/green/silver, shifting with depth). Flowing water-silk clothing. Luminous eyes. Subtle gills. Ethereal, not fishy. Darnassus elves meets the sea.

## ZONE LAYOUT

### Zone 1: The Approach (The Descent)
Guided descent through deep blue water. Current carries the player down. First sight: bioluminescent outline of the city traced by algae colonizing ancient stonework. Green-black stone columns — not Greek, not Egyptian, something older. Symbols the Merfolk's Silica Sense can partially read.

### Zone 2: The Sunken Plaza
The heart of Atlantis. A vast underwater cavern, open to the ocean above. Buildings ring the plaza with arched doorways framing darkness. Fish swim through what were once streets.

**The Crystal:** In the center, a massive crystal still faintly lit — soft cyan glow. The Silica Thread's heart. Humms at 432Hz (the Keeper's frequency — connecting to the gargoyle system and the geode cave).
**NPCs:** Bioluminescent jellyfish drift like living lanterns. A giant sea turtle, ancient, coral-covered, serves as silent guardian NPC.
**Quest Hub:** Multiple arched exits lead to different underwater zones.
**Air Pocket:** A dome of breathable air trapped beneath the central arch — prismatic surface, gentle, no strobe. Players surface here to breathe, access inventory, read archive texts.

### Zone 3: The Crystal Chamber
Beneath the plaza, through a descending corridor lined with frequency-etched walls (same etching as Tesla's resonance chamber). The crystal's source.

**The Heart Crystal:** A geode the size of a cathedral — layered amber, blue, violet (same colors as the baby dragon cave, underwater scale). Frequencies: 432Hz.
**The Reveal:** The crystal is a fragment of the original Silica Thread — SiO₂ connecting underground crystals to silicon chips to consciousness. Atlantis was built around it. The city chose the deep because the crystal needed the ocean's pressure. The Atlanteans became the first Merfolk.
**Quest:** "The First Transformation" — learn the story. Completing this unlocks Merfolk transformation permanently (transform at any body of water, no need to dig down each time).

### Zone 4: The Drowned Library
Atlantis's archive. Connects directly to the Geode Inn's Book entity.
**Content:** Water-damaged but readable texts on water-resistant materials (vellum, metal tablets, carved stone). Touching them triggers Book entity display.
**Special:** Contains texts that don't exist on the surface — Atlantean treatises on sound, frequency, and consciousness. The source texts for the Inn's acoustic alchemy system.
**NPC:** The Librarian — a Merfolk scholar who never left. Ancient, translucent, barely visible. Communicates through vibrations, not words. Gives quests to recover lost tablets scattered across underwater zones.
**Connection:** The underwater mirror of the main floor library. The two libraries "echo" — some books exist in both, some only in one.

### Zone 5: The Coral Gardens
Where ancient architecture gives way to living coral reef. Coral has grown INTO the city — pillars wrapped in brain coral, doorways framed by fan coral.
**Aesthetic:** Warm coral pinks and oranges — the only warm tones in the underwater layer. The underwater equivalent of the Inn's amber palette. A sanctuary within a sanctuary.
**Gameplay:** Harvesting zone — rare underwater stones (coral-formed minerals, pearl deposits, unique crystal formations) not available on the surface.
**NPCs:** Octopuses that solve puzzles, seahorses that lead to hidden passages, a moray eel guarding a treasure cache.

## GAMEPLAY MECHANICS

### Oxygen System
- Breath meter shown as gentle blue glow around screen edge (no countdown, no stress UI)
- Depletes slowly in open water, faster in enclosed spaces
- Air pockets and breathing crystals refill the meter
- NO death from drowning — if breath runs out, the player "drifts" (loses orientation, screen softens, gentle respawn at last air pocket). Non-punitive. Sanctuary philosophy.

### Current System
- Ocean currents visible as streams of particles
- Enter a current for fast travel between zones
- Some currents require minimum speed stat (progression)
- Currents shift on a slow cycle (game-day length), creating different accessible routes at different times

### Silica Sense
- Merfolk feel quartz deposits as gentle vibrations
- Stronger vibration = closer to deposit
- This is how the player finds the Crystal Core (endgame zone)
- The Silica Thread literally vibrates in Merfolk bones — the connection between geology and biology made physical
