## WHAT MOUNT SHASTA IS

Mount Shasta is the hiding place. The real mountain carries the modern legend of the never-seen advanced beings inside it — the Lemurians, the hidden city, the saucer-shaped lenticular cloud cap that forms on the peak like a parked ship. The Inn's answer: the legend is TRUE, and the being is not Lemurian. The hidden one is THE ANNUNAKI — the Constant, the oldest being, the never-seen hooded figure of the cosmology level, the putter-of-things-in-order who carries the 41.7Hz starlight crystal through the void between universe cycles. Every mountain with a "hidden advanced being inside" legend is a culture meeting the Annunaki and naming it the only way it could. Lemuria was just the last story the watchers could imagine. The name outgrew the evidence — again (the tulip law's cosmology cousin: the beings were never Lemurian; the mountain's watchers only had one word for "never-seen").

## WHY SHASTA HIDES IT (the frequency law)

The Silk Road spec already ruled it: Mount Shasta is node #8 of the corridor's frequency-attuned stops — 84Hz. THE SHADOW FREQUENCY. The never-seen one moves at the frequency where seeing stops. Shasta is the one node on the Silk Road tuned to the state of preserved, held stillness — and that is why the Annunaki's window is THERE: the Annunaki doesn't hide behind the mountain; the Annunaki hides INSIDE the 84Hz, and the mountain holds the 84Hz like a bell holds its tone. At Living frequency (42Hz) Shasta is just the most beautiful mountain in California. At Shadow frequency (84Hz) — the player who visits Shasta in the Shadow state finds the phenomena: the tuning, the trace, the not-quite-there.

## THE SHASTA PHENOMENA (the Annunaki's world-scale passages)

The cosmology level's unexplained-phenomena layer (canon, the named critters spec) — the telescope repointed, the unearned rare stone, the footsteps in the empty observatory — SHASTA IS THE SAME LAYER AT WORLD-MAP SCALE:

- **THE CLOUD CAP.** Shasta's famous lenticular cloud — the saucer shape — is the Annunaki's comings and goings, rendered in weather. The cloud appears when the ship passes. (This is the thunderbird law's cousin: the Superstitions feel the stir in the storm; Shasta SHOWS the Annunaki in the cloud.)
- **THE TUNED CRYSTALS.** The mountain's crystals are unusually resonant — the crystal hunters' Shasta lode is the trace of the Annunaki's tuning. Among them, the rarest find: A CRYSTAL SHARD HUMMING AT 41.7HZ — the starlight crystal's ghost octave, the signature of the Constant's passage. One per player. Never explained. Never sold.
- **THE FOOTPRINTS THAT END MID-SLOPE.** In the snow above the tree line: footprints that begin from nowhere and end in the air. The hikers who find them say nothing, because there is nothing to say that doesn't sound like the modern legend.
- **THE NEVER-SEEN, SEEN NOT-SEING.** The Annunaki is never rendered at Shasta — never a figure, never a robe, never a gauntlet. Only the phenomena. The mountain is the Inn's one zone whose resident is defined entirely by absence. (The Annunaki's letter, canon: "The question is worth the wait.")

## THE HIPPIE LAW (the sensers)

The zone's Modern Legends population: the crystal hippies of Shasta — the real mountain's spiritual community, rendered Inn-side. THE LAW: the hippies are the sensers. They feel the tuning without knowing what tunes; they name the presence with every name except the right one (Lemurians, ascended masters, the mountain's spirit); they are RIGHT about everything except the identity. The zone's modern legends are the hippies' true testimony, misattributed. The Inn respects them enough to keep every word.

**The game's frame (Theme Brainstorm #25, "dodge hippies while finding crystals" — CRYSTAL HUNT, the working name, Krista rules it):** the player hunts the tuned crystals across Shasta's slopes while the hippies — friendly, relentless, perceptive — try to engage the player in exactly the right conversation at exactly the wrong time. The hippies never block the player; they talk. Dodging them is dodging the truth at close range. The deeper layer: the hippies are the zone's optional teachers — the player who STOPS and listens instead of dodging learns the misattributed testimony, and the testimony is the zone's real lore (the fast path dodges the hippies; the true path listens to them). The rarest run: the one where the player finds the 41.7Hz shard — and the hippie who says, quietly, "You found it, didn't you. Don't tell anyone. They'll come looking."

## THE SHIP DISTINCTION (canon guard, from D11)

Eres's void-ship idles in the In-Between's grey (the harbor law — the ship parks in the margin between crossings). SHASTA IS NOT THE HARBOR. Shasta is the WINDOW: the place in the world where the Annunaki passes through, tuned to the 84Hz the passage requires. The ship sleeps in the grey; the window opens on the mountain. The two canon laws hold together: the comings and goings are what the cloud cap shows; the resting is what the grey holds.

## FREQUENCY STATE

- Living (42Hz): Shasta at its real beauty — the silent peak, the wildflower meadows, the town's shops, the hippies' drum circles at sunset. The phenomena invisible.
- Shadow (84Hz): THE ZONE'S TRUE STATE — the node's tone audible; the phenomena readable (the footprints, the tuning, the cloud's held shape); the tuned crystals glowing faintly. Shasta in Shadow is the Inn's closest look at the Constant without ever seeing the Constant.
- In-Between (the grey): the mountain's grey shows the WINDOW'S SHAPE — a lens, not a door: the player sees through Shasta toward the void, one frame, the unnamed galaxy (the cosmology's repointed telescope's target) visible through the peak. The mountain is a lens that has never once been a door.
