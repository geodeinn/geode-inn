## THE CORE LAW
**NO OXYGEN METER. EVER.** The water zones are ROOMS, not hazards (the no-timer law holds underwater: a breath gauge would make the ocean a clock, and the ocean is the one thing in the building that has never once been in a hurry). **THE DROWNING ABOLITION:** the Inn's water NEVER kills. The failure state is being carried home  the water goes dark and cold and the murmur gently turns you upward, and the tide delivers you back to the surface like a parcel. The sea does not punish. The tide returns what it loves too soon.

## THE THREE FORMS (the chain every player walks)
**1. THE HELD BREATH (arrival  everyone).** Dips are free: shallows, the Tidal Caves' lit layer, the Coast Hub's dock-water. The deep is soft-gated by feel, not force: go too deep unwelcomed and the water gets cold, then dark, then UP (never damage, never a screen, just the water's firm opinion).

**2. THE GIVEN BREATH (the Diver's Stone  THE FORM).** AQUAMARINE  the water's own stone (the etymology IS the law: the stone literally named seawater; the water zone's own nook-stone, and already Nessie's award  the existing canon converges on this one). Earned, never bought (the Spawn Law). Worn AT THE CHEST  where the Inn keeps every key (the 7.83Hz key lives there; the songbook lives there; now the water's promise lives there). The water recognizes the stone as a promise: worn, the player BREATHES WATER in every water zone. **THE SIGN OF THE GIVEN BREATH: NO BUBBLES.** Bubbles are the body arguing with the water. The murlocs never bubble. The welcomed never bubble. And underwater SPEECH WORKS  the murmur carries words whole (the Deep's voices are heard clear, never muffled  the zone's own proof that the water is a room).
- **THE AWARD QUEST:** Monster of the Deep  Nessie's sealed-exhaust encounter already awards Aquamarine (canon, existing). The Diver's Stone IS Nessie's award: the water's own creature vouches for you to the water. First depth after the award: the Lozen Breath moment  one held shot, the first inhale that isn't air, and the murmur saying (felt, not heard): finally.

**3. THE WATER'S OWN (the deep trust  presence, not stones).** The deepest layers  the anchialine below-court, Charon's Lozen, the abyss  do not open by charm. The hatching law's cousin: THE WATER MUST KNOW YOU. Earned by time-in, by the Beat Line kept, by water quests done without rushing. High tide knows you before low tide does (Persephone's presence: her seasons open her rooms). At water-system completion, the tide carries you anywhere  and the river tells you its name.
