# THE CULTURAL QUEST CHAINS — Master Specification
## Greek, Celtic, Jotunheim, Desert Bone, Selkie Crafting, Festival Quests, + Mother's Many Faces

---

## WHAT THE CULTURAL QUEST CHAINS ARE

The detailed quest chains for each cultural zone in the Geode Inn. Each chain has a giver, a series of quests, unique rewards (stones, charms, perception unlocks), and ties to the larger narrative spine.

---

## THE QUEST CHAINS

### Greek Quest Chain (288 lines)
The Mirror of the Sun. The Gardener tends the Mediterranean garden. The player learns that Greek mythology isn't about gods — it's about light and what grows in it. Monet's visual style.

### Celtic Quest Chain (239 lines)
The knotwork quests. Stone circle activation. The player learns the Portal Arrow mechanic here — the Celtic zone is where navigation by arrow first. Builds to the Fae Kingdom entry.

### Jotunheim Quest Chain (233 lines)
The frost giant quests. The deep Norse. The player enters the ice caves beneath the Norse longhouse. The Einherjar companions are first encountered here for Ragnarok Phase 3.

### Desert Bone Quest Chain (226 lines)
The source of Form Sight. 7 quests in O'Keeffe's visual style. The player learns to see the essential geometry of every zone. The Examiner is the quest giver — literal, precise, caring through craft.

### Selkie Crafting Questline (242 lines)
Krista's personal questline. The seal who chose the land for the stones. The jeweler's apron IS the selkie skin. Teaches the Bead Composer crafting system. The "theft" is redistribution — justice from the sea, where hoarding sinks you.

### Festival Quests (375 lines)
"Festival in the Middle of a Raid" — Krista's original tabletop campaign adapted into the Inn. Quests that happen during celebrations interrupted by events. The player must balance festival participation with crisis response.

### Mother's Many Faces (206 lines)
8 quests tracing the 42Hz amber thread connecting every depiction of the mother across 30,000 years. Venus of Willendorf → Inanna → Aphrodite → Mary → Eve → Mona Lisa → Olympia → Artemisia. All the same mother. All the same frequency. Unlocks Mother Mode (the third perception mode).

### Observatory Quest Chain (266 lines)
The six-quest chain that leads to "The Question." Requires all three perception modes (Form Sight + Silica Overlay + Mother Mode). The gateway to the endgame.

### Aughra Quest: The Carrying (266 lines)
Aughra — the weight of observation. The keeper who carries too much. The player learns that seeing IS a burden, and the Inn respects the seer enough to let them rest.
