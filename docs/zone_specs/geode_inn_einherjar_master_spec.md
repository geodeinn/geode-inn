# THE EINHERJAR — Master Specification
## Valhalla's Champions — Endgame Shadow Inn Raid Team

---

## WHAT THE EINHERJAR ARE

The Einherjar are not generic Viking warriors. They are the Inn's ENDGAME SHADOW INN RAID TEAM — the immortal spirits of legendary champions who, upon death, refused total dissolution and chose to remain within the Inn's living timber. They reside in Valhalla, a grand chamber on the Cosmology Level (Upstairs / Asgard), anchored beside the Observatory, the Planet Chamber, the Cosmology Mural, the Ascension Canopy, and the Void.

When dissonance surges through raid instances, deep dungeons, and combat arenas, these eight warriors stand ready to be recruited into the player's raid roster.

"The both are: the adequate — the dead and the staying, the same warrior, different state."

The Einherjar do not fear defeat because mortality is already behind them. In the 84Hz combat frequency, an Einherjar cannot be killed. When depleted, an Einherjar dissolves into golden resonance, returns to Valhalla to rest beside the hearth, and can be summoned back once the cooldown elapses.

## RAGNAROK — THE ENDGAME RAID

Ragnarok is the Shadow Inn's ultimate raid — the event the Einherjar were recruited for. When the 44Hz frequency approaches (the frequency that would wake the Sleeping Giants), dissonance surges through the Inn. The Einherjar are the vanguard.

| Phase | What Happens | Boss |
|-------|-------------|------|
| Phase 1 | The Shadow Inn's zones weaponize. 6 instanced zones with local Ragnarok events. | Zone-specific bosses |
| Phase 2 | The Cosmology Level becomes a battlefield. The Observatory, the Planet Chamber, the Void. | 3 Ragnarok bosses with mechanics |
| Phase 3 | The Ragnarok resolution. The player must prevent the 44Hz from completing. The question is more beautiful than the answer. | The final dissonance — the 44Hz chord, unfinished |

The Ragnarok resolution: the player does NOT defeat Ragnarok. The player PREVENTS it — by keeping the 42Hz Song incomplete. The 44Hz must not arrive. The question must remain open. The Astronomer's thesis: "The question is more beautiful than the answer." The answer is Ragnarok. Keep the question.
