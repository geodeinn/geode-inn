## THE GEAR REGISTER (cross-zone component canon)

Ruled by Krista, 2026-10-05: "Gears are quite possibly the most versatile component we've had to work with." Gears are hereby a REGISTER, not a zone prop — every zone that builds with gears gets its own linework and finish register:

1. **The air pirate ship** — exposed working flight gears, salt-and-brass finish, rigging-adjacent. Adventure-register gears: loud, weathered, load-bearing.
2. **The da Vinci layer (Clockwork Skies, z02)** — Renaissance register: wooden-toothed gears, mirrored geometry, sketchbook linework. Leonardo's Host canon (Crystal Canticle) is the gear-as-invention face.
3. **The Tesla layer** — high-frequency register: gears married to coils and arcs, brushed steel and copper, the gear as tuned circuit.
4. **The clock building areas** (the grandfather clock build plan, the horology rooms) — precision register: escapements and balance wheels, the gear as timekeeper. The smallest gears in the building and the largest share one component law.
5. **The engine room** — the master register: the industrial source of them all. Every zone's gears are dialects; the engine room is the mother tongue.

**The register rule:** same component, per-zone linework (Linework-as-UI law holds). A player should identify the zone by the gear's finish before reading the room.
