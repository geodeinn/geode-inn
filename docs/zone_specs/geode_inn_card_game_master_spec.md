## "Resonance" — Hearthstone-Style CCG with Frequency Mechanics

---

## WHAT THE CARD GAME IS

A Hearthstone-style digital Collectible Card Game called "Resonance." The core mechanic is the Frequency system — cards resonate at specific Hz values, and the board's ambient frequency determines which cards are empowered. The Inn's 42Hz is the baseline; shifting the board's frequency is the key strategic move.

## CORE MECHANICS

| Mechanic | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| The Frequency Board | The board resonates at a Hz value (starting 42Hz). Cards played at matching frequency get bonuses. |
| SONG Cards | 42Hz cards — the Inn's heart. Power resonance and heartbeat cards. |
| Shadow Cards | 84Hz cards — empowered when the board shifts to Shadow frequency. |
| Frequency Shift | The key strategic move — shift the board from 42Hz to 84Hz (or back). Changes which cards are empowered. |
| Board Art | 37 zone-based boards, each in its assigned artist style. |
| AI Opponent | NPC decks with established character voices — the AI plays AS the NPC. |
