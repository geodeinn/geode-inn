# THE TAP — THE INN'S COMMUNAL SINGALONG TRIGGER (Krista, 2026-09-20)

Freddie Mercury tribute (Zoroastrian Parsi family, honest label per the Sep 20 session: the sacred-chant thread is Zoroastrianism, one of the oldest continuous chant traditions, not Rosicrucian; the title's "Bohemian" is the artist-counterculture word, itself a French misnomer for the Romani). ## THE MECHANIC
Any NPC, at any bar in the building, leans into THE POSTURE (the Wayne's World car headbang register, or the double stomp and clap) and taps the bar twice — THE TAP. Every patron within earshot answers the rhythm. The Convention Engine does the rest: the gesture IS the song (the flag IS the horse). The rhythm is public domain by nature; the Inn's arrangement of it is its own (REC law, region-matched instruments).

## THE LAWS IT CARRIES
- **THE QUEST MARK LAW**: the tap is an approach mark — the lovelier the light, the bigger the singalong it summons.
- **ENTRAINMENT, HONESTLY LABELED**: antiphony (call-and-response) synchronizes breathing and heart rhythms in groups — the same mechanism as sacred chant everywhere (Gregorian, Sufi dhikr, the drum circle, Zoroastrian Gathas recitation). Belonging without a membership card: Freddie's whole trick, no label required.
- **NO-FAIL**: if nobody answers, the tapper is not embarrassed. The room just wasn't ready. The tap can be repeated.
- **ANTI-SKINNER**: the tap summons nothing the player "earns." The singalong is its own reward. It never becomes a task.
