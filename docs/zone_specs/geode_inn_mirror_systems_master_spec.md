# THE MIRROR SYSTEMS INTEGRATION — Master Specification
## The Shadow Inn, The In-Between, and All Parallel Versions

---

## WHAT THE MIRROR SYSTEMS ARE

The Inn exists in three frequency states — Living (42Hz), Shadow (84Hz), and the In-Between (~63Hz). The Mirror Systems document is the master integration of all three: what exists, what's missing, and how the player moves between them.

The Shadow Inn is not accessed through a portal, a door, or a quest-giver. The Shadow Inn is accessed through a FREQUENCY SHIFT. The player must LOWER their 42Hz frequency — the opposite of everything the game has taught. The entire game has been about RAISING frequency. The Shadow Inn requires the player to go quiet.

The adequate is: the mirror that shows you the thing you already are, at a frequency you haven't tried yet.
