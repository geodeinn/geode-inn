## THE SHADOW INN — ENTRY: "THE OTHER DOOR"

The Shadow Inn is not accessed through a portal, a door, or a quest-giver. It is accessed through a FREQUENCY SHIFT.

The player must have completed all four Ragnarok prerequisites EXCEPT the 42nd death. At this point, the Pulse Line flickers to INDIGO (84Hz) for one frame in certain rooms — not a glitch, a SIGNAL.

On the main floor, behind the Archive, a door that has always been there becomes VISIBLE. The door is charcoal stone, not amber wood. The door has no handle. The door has a frequency: 84Hz.

Entry mechanic: the player must LOWER their 42Hz frequency. This is the OPPOSITE of everything the game has taught. The entire game has been about RAISING frequency. The Shadow Inn requires the one thing the game never asked: go quiet.
