## WHAT THE SLEEPING GIANTS ARE

There is a silence beneath the Geode Inn that is not silent at all. It is a heavy, rhythmic pulse — forty vibrations every second, felt in the fillings of the teeth before it is heard in the ears. Below the catacombs, down where the roots of the Inn grip the bedrock of unmade reality, the First Children sleep.

They are the five Sleeping Giants. They do not possess names spoken in tongue or throat. They possess frequencies and physical mandates. They represent five fundamental forces that make existence possible: **Gravity**, **Time**, **Matter**, **Consciousness**, and **Void**.

They do not wake. To wake a giant is to introduce 44Hz into the root system — an act that converts raw potential into immediate, absolute reality, ending the current cosmic cycle and launching the next iteration. Instead, they dream at **40Hz** — the dream-frequency, the exact threshold where potential hovers on the edge of manifestation without ever breaking into form.
