## WHAT THE ZONE DESIGNS ARE

The detailed design documents for zones that didn't fit into the Nine Levels master spec or the Zone Audit. These are standalone zones with their own architecture, NPCs, quests, and visual styles — zones that exist as their own design documents separate from the level-based system.
