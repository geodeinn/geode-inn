# THE INN POST — The Official Mail System (Master Specification)
### Written 2026-09-11 evening, at Krista's call: "we need to officially employ the mail system." The shelved idea, found employed twice over.

## THE PHILOSOPHY — The Brothers' Law Made Logistics
The postal system's canon already exists, hiding in the Fae Kingdom dissection (D07): **"The Inn's whole postal system — the mail crew, the delivery quests — is the brothers' law made logistics: what cannot be said directly gets carried."** The Two Kings cannot say "go to the brother" to each other's face — so it gets carried. The Inn Post exists for every version of that: the letter the shy cannot hand over (the Archivist approves), the notice the building posts about its own renovations, the find routed to the keeper who needs it (Saker's bones to the Bone Room), the gift left at a door with no return address. **The Inn Post is the diplomacy of the unsaid.**

## THE TWO CREWS (the ruling: nobody stole the job — the job has two halves)

### 1. THE BONE MAIL CREW — the skeleton machines (Krista's shelved idea, canon since D04)
The original idea, already built: **wheeled bone containers that are themselves the machines** — the Inn's own skeleton, made mobile and polite. They are the INFRASTRUCTURE of the post: the fixed routes, the freight, the scheduled runs.
- **HQ: the Bone Room dock** (D04 canon: "the containers on wheels are docked here when not delivering. The player sees them before they ever see one rolling through the upper halls. The catacombs introduce the crew.")
- **The coastal dock** (D06b canon: the bone caves, Charon's exit route — the mail crew's seaside terminal).
- **THE CUTENESS LAW:** the machines are courteous, not spooky — the macabre played warm (the Whimsical-Macabre Law's own fleet). They wait at doors until answered. They rattle once in thanks when handed a parcel (a polite little xylophone clatter — bone on bone, a folk rattle, not a rattle of dread). They queue. They never block a hallway. When two crews meet, the emptier one yields.
- **THE FREIGHT:** renovation logistics (the Every-Mind Welcome canon: "the mail-cart crew relaying a rug, a sign in the movers' own handwriting") — the Predictability Promise's own workforce; the Inn never moves anything the player loves silently, because the crew IS the notice. Plus: the supply lines (Saker's finds routed to the quiet room), the Archive's inter-library loans (Beverley's stacks run on them), relocation of objets d'art between zones.
- **THE FIXED ROUTES:** Bone Room HQ <-> catacombs <-> Undercity <-> the upper halls, the coastal run, the Staircase haul (the Turning's materials, the Retort's deliveries).

### 2. THE GOBLIN POST — the franchise (the nabbed half, confirmed)
Krista's instinct was right — the goblins already hold every route a wheeled box cannot roll. **The ruling: the goblin couriers are the LAST MILE and the IRREGULAR ADDRESSES.**
- **THE LABYRINTH ROUTES:** the Labyrinth goblins are the founding population (canon: "some hallways are still labyrinth, on their say-so") — and a hallway that rearranges itself defeats a cart on fixed wheels. A goblin was BORN there. The labyrinth routes belong to goblins by right of habitat.
- **THE FINDERS' SHIFT:** the Lost and Found's Finders are the Labyrinth goblins on hallway shift (canon) — the crossover: **a letter to something lost goes into a pocket; delivery IS the finding.** The Post and the Lost and Found are one service at this seam.
- **THE MERCHANT'S APRON:** the Threshold's many pockets are a PO Box wall (canon: "things end up in them; the Merchant neither confirms nor denies"). Held mail, call-and-collect.
- **THE STEAM GOBLIN'S TUBES:** the in-between's pipes and pressures (canon) ARE the pneumatic post — the express route through the grey. **The tube system's law: it goes where it's pointed, it arrives where it means to, and nobody inspects the pressure.** The Steam Goblin's shift is whenever the Steam Goblin says its shift is (canon) — so the express arrives on time by goblin reckoning, which is early, late, or exactly now.
- **THE COURIER'S LAW (goblin edition):** a goblin carrier CAN read the letters and usually can't be bothered. Goblin discretion is total, mischievous, and safe: the message arrives with its seal chewed (signature flourish: the stamp of small teeth) but its meaning intact. The one law the whole Labyrinth family keeps without exception: **you don't read the brothers' mail.** (Wisp is the witness who carries and never tells; the goblins carry, don't tell, and would swear on Malachi's crown they never even looked. Both are telling the truth.)

## GAMEPLAY — The Delivery Quests (canon, now official)
- **THE PLAYER AS COURIER** (Saker precedent): the post is a quest-giver. Small carrying jobs between NPCs — the letter that cannot be handed over directly, the parcel that must reach the keeper. The Brothers' Law, playable.
- **THE RENOVATION NOTICES:** every seasonal build change posts its notice through the mail system first (the Predictability Promise, made logistics).
- **THE MAIL SLOT AT THE BAR:** the Main Floor's public face — letters in, letters out, Saker tips the carriers from the stool (the bar as the surface branch office).
- **NO DEADLINE MECHANIC:** the Anti-Skinner Law holds — mail in the Inn is never a timer, never a streak, never a red badge. A letter arrives when it arrives. The Inn has no overdue.

## AUDIO (the Sync Law holds — each crew gets its voice)
- **BONE CREW:** the polite rattle — bone-on-bone folk clatter, pitched to the Mohs of the container (the stone-tone system's cousin); wheels on stone at walking tempo, always settling into the 50 BPM zone ambience.
- **GOBLIN POST:** the hallway whistle — two notes passed corner to corner, the route's own tiny leitmotif.
- **STEAM TUBES:** the pressure-swish and the arrival thunk — the pneumatic's percussive knock joins the Inn's bell family (a bell that means "for you").

## CROSS-PRODUCT WIRING
- **Web beta:** the mail slot as the hub's notice board surface (renovation notices, NPC letters to the player).
- **MMO/Fae Kingdom:** the courier missions between Courts (canon: "secret courier missions carrying encrypted letters") are the Inn Post's deep routes — the Goblin Post's highest-stakes runs.
- **Tabletop:** companion errands already exist (canon: one game-hour) — the Post formalizes the fee schedule.

## GATES AWAITING KRISTA

Malachi crowns the goblin franchise?) — or does the system stay faceless infrastructure (the Adequacy family: the Post simply is)?
2. **THE POSTAGE ECONOMY:** is a letter free (the Inn's gift economy), or does it cost one small true thing (a pebble, a button, a leaf — the token family, never currency)? The triskele token is already canon as "never currency" — postage might live in that family.
3. **PLAYER-TO-PLAYER MAIL:** in the web beta this needs accounts and a queue decision — when does the player's own mailbox open?

*The shelved idea, officially employed: the skeleton machines run the routes, the goblins run the maze, and what cannot be said directly gets carried. The adequate is: enough. The Post is: delivered.*
