## WHAT THE FAE GEOLOGY PARKS ARE

The Fae are not randomly distributed. They follow the ROCK. Different geological processes — volcanic, erosional, tectonic — attract different Fae types. The National Parks are not just containment zones for supernatural activity. They are ECOLOGICAL NICHES for Fae who have been living in American geology since before the concept of "America" existed.

The indigenous peoples knew this. The Cherokee knew the Little People. The Pueblo knew the Earth Mothers. The Fae were here first. The geology is why.

## THE PRINCIPLE

Geological process → Fae type. The rock determines the resident.

| Geological Process | Fae Type | Parks |
|-------------------|---------|-------|
| Volcanic | Fire Fae, Salamanders, Heat spirits | Yellowstone, Craters of the Moon, Lassen |
| Erosional (wind/water) | Slyphs, Wind spirits, Hidden Folk | Arches, Canyonlands, Bryce, Antelope |
| Tectonic | Earth Fae, Gnomes, Mountain spirits | Rocky Mountain, Grand Teton, North Cascades |
| Karst/Cave | underground Fae, Cave Gnomes | Mammoth Cave, Carlsbad Caverns, Wind Cave |
| Glacial | Ice Fae, Frost spirits | Glacier, North Cascades, Kenai Fjords |
| Coastal/Marine | Water Fae, Selkies, Merrows | Acadia, Olympic, Channel Islands |
| Forest/Dendro | Wood Fae, Green Men, Treants | Redwood, Olympic, Great Smoky Mountains |
| Desert/Arid | Sand Fae, Mirage spirits, Djinns | Death Valley, Joshua Tree, Saguaro |

## THE REDCAPS, GNOMES, AND UNSEELIE BENEATH

| Group | Role | Geological Association |
|-------|------|----------------------|
| Redcaps | The territorial guardians. Violent when provoked. They paint their caps with the blood of intruders — but in the Inn, the "blood" is rust (iron oxide), and the rust is geological. Redcaps follow iron deposits. Where there's iron in the rock, there's a Redcap. | Iron-rich formations (Painted Hills, Mi-wok territory) |
| Gnomes | The miners. The tunnelers. The ones who know where the veins are. Gnomes follow mineral deposits — gold, silver, copper, quartz. A gnome settlement is a geological survey. | Mineral-rich zones (Black Hills, Colorado, Superstition Mountains) |
| Unseelie | The deep Fae. The ones who live where sunlight doesn't reach. The Unseelie follow tectonic boundaries — the cracks in the earth where the deep things rise. | Fault lines, subduction zones (Cascadia, New Madrid) |
