## THE ROOM

Every staircase has flights; every climb has a landing. THE LANDING IS THE PAUSE BETWEEN STATES: the flat where the ascent breathes. The staircase's music ascends in key as you climb (canon, founding)  the Landing is where the old key RESOLVES and the new key is first heard. THE MUSIC'S SEAM, MADE A ROOM.

The five alchemical emblems hang the Landing's walls (Nigredo, and the road to the Stone  assets live): read left to right, they are the climb itself in five pictures. THE PLAYER WALKS THE EMBLEMS between flights  the transformation happens on the stairs; the Landing is where you notice it did.

## THE LAW OF THE LANDING

**NO TRANSFORMATION ON THE LANDING.** You arrive as one state; you leave as another; ON the Landing, you simply are. The one room in the Inn where nothing changes  the pause is sacred. (The in-between family's KEPT member: the Threshold is the crossing, the Between shifts  the Landing RESTS.)
