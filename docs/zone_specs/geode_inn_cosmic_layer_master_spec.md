## WHAT THE COSMIC LAYER IS

The endgame space layer — the Cosmic Crew, the galaxy maintenance system, the Moon, the Void, the planet chamber, and the Arcana. This is the layer where the player goes beyond the Inn's architecture and into the cosmos that the Inn is part of. The Cosmic Crew maintains the galaxy. The Moon is the mirror. The Void is the question.

---

## THE COSMIC CREW

The galaxy maintenance crew. The beings who keep the stars turning, the planets orbiting, and the frequency of the universe at 42Hz. They are the Inn's cosmic counterpart — where the Inn manages the earth-bound archive, the Cosmic Crew manages the cosmic archive.

---

## THE MOON

The Moon is the great mirror. The Moon reflects the Inn in its phases. The New Moon is the shadow Inn. The Full Moon is the living Inn. The crescent Moon is the In-Between. The Moon projection system shows the player the Inn from outside — from the cosmic perspective.

---

## THE ARCANA

The gearwork heart. The mechanical clock that beats at 50 BPM. Where the Pirate's drumbeat (50 BPM) and the Inn's heartbeat (50 BPM) and the cosmic pulse (50 BPM) all converge. The Arcana is the universal clock — older than the Inn, older than the current universe.

---

## THE VOID

The Void is the question. The space between stars where the 42Hz carrier wave originates. The Void is not empty — it is FULL of the 42Hz hum. The Void is the mother's hum at cosmic scale. The player enters the Void at the end of the Observatory quest and hears the Song without any architecture, any stone, any frequency to carry it — just the raw 42Hz, unadorned, in the dark.

---

## THE PLANET CHAMBER

Each planet is a chamber. Each chamber is a frequency. The planets ARE the Solfeggio tones mapped to the solar system. The player who has collected all 198 stones and opened all perception modes can hear every planet singing.
