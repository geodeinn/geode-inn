# THE UNVOICED — Frequency-Feeder Class — Master Specification
**CLASS NAME RULED (Krista, 2026-09-17): The Unvoiced. The Shadow Knights remain the order's name.**
## The Rogue Path's Advanced Class — "Shadow Knights" (name gate open)
**Ruled by Krista, 2026-09-17. Advanced class unlocked from the ROGUE path (the Artisan Thief) at level-up.**

---

## WHAT THIS CLASS IS

The Inn's first class where the lore and the law are the same sentence: **feed like a bat, not like a count.**

A playable class of frequency-feeders — vampires of the bat side of the mythology, never the Vlad side. They feed on the vibrational hums of the Geode Inn itself: the 42Hz root song, the stone tones, the gargoyle frequencies, the drum circle. Because they live within the tree, the feeding dynamic is parasitic by default — and the class's entire moral architecture is what each member does about that.

**Core sensory truth:** every member of this class is born UNVOICED. They cannot produce their own tone. They must borrow or consume sound to speak, to travel, to defend. What a player does about being born without a voice is the class's whole story question.

---

## THE TWO REGISTERS (the predator/pollinator duality)

Bats don't drain — they pollinate. Half the desert blooms because of them. The class inherits both sides of the bat:

### THE ROGUES — true parasites
- Skim tones from zones until the hum goes quiet
- **Their crime is silence.** Not blood, not broken bones — dead spots. A wing of the tree where the song is gone. In a building held together by frequency, this is the one unforgivable thing.
- Rogues dig MOATS OF SILENCE: because the class can only pouf where the song reaches (see Travel below), the quiet zones are walls. The rogues' tactical geometry is manufactured deafness.

### THE SHADOW KNIGHTS — the trained register (the playable path)
- Feed the way real bats pollinate: they skim the OVERTONES — the excess resonance — and the zones they feed on ring CLEANER after. Pruning, not wounding.
- Keep the balance against the rogues. The No-Fail law holds even here: rogues are not killed — they are RETUNED. The tuner's weapons correct, not stake.
- Players reach this register through the rogue path (Artisan Thief) at level-up: the class plays as the redemption of the parasite — the character who could take everything and keeps the balance instead.

## THE DUO — Leaders of the Shadow Knights (Blade/Whistler mapping, names are a gate)

- **THE TUNER (the Whistler):** gruff, earned, walks the tree with a kit of tuning-fork armament. Keeps the TONE-LARDER — the reserves of borrowed sound the knights spend like ammunition. Can travel anywhere in song.
- **THE DAYWALKER (the Blade):** half-feeder themselves. The only one who can hear a rogue's hunger — and the only one who can enter the silent zones on foot, because they carry their own sustenance. This is why the duo is a duo: the tuner can travel anywhere in song; the daywalker is the one who can go where there is none.

Register: earned gruffness, never performed. The Whistler casting law (the man who'd already done the harder version of the job) is the template — the duo's authority is competence, not title.

## TRAVEL — THE POUF (Nightcrawler homage, acoustic)

Borrow is homage without the lift (the Frank Wright law). Nightcrawler was always half-bat to begin with — pointed ears, bat-referenced acrobatics, the swashbuckler soul wearing the most feared face. The class borrows the silhouette, the rafter-perching, the gentle-giant register, and the teleport:

- **They travel through the building's sound.** The pouf rides the hum the way Nightcrawler's teleport rides the shadow dimension.
- **They can only pouf where the song reaches.** Silence is a wall. Rogue dead-zones cannot be poufed into — the deep quiet must be walked, and only a daywalker can walk it.
- **No arrival is silent:** each pouf lands with a borrowed chime — a puff of deep indigo resonance shaped like the tone it rode. Cymatics made visible: the pouf cloud is literally the note's shape.
- **Travel spends carried tones.** The tone-larder is ammunition; a knight rations sound.
- **THE POUF WORD (gate):** every teleport needs its bamf — a chime-word, to be ruled.

## STANDING LAWS THAT CARRY

- **No-Fail:** rogues are retuned, never killed; failure teaches, the retune is the lesson
- **Anti-Skinner:** no streak bonuses for feeding; the pollinator path pays in zone-health, not points
- **Steady Light:** pouf chimes are candle-class shimmer, never flash
- **Honest-label law:** the bat mythology is taught real (pollinators), the Vlad mythology is filed as folklore — the Inn does not teach vampires wrong
- **REC law:** one class master per state; individuals are recolors of the indigo
