# THE GIVEN WING  The Flight Law
## The player learns to fly as their own form (Krista's call, 2026-09-11, after Aion: flight as the character's own, never a mount)
### The Water-Breath Law's sibling: the given breath at the chest, the given wing at the back. The pair of forms.

---

## THE CORE LAW
**NO MOUNT. EVER.** Flight is learned, not ridden  the wing is the player's own form, grown from the player's own rhythm. **NO STAMINA METER. EVER.** A fuel gauge would make the sky a clock, and a timer on flight is the one flaw the classics never fixed (Aion's wings ran on a countdown; the Inn's never will). The wing is not equipment and does not run out  it runs on PRESENCE, like everything else the building gives. **THE FALLING ABOLITION:** the Inn's ground NEVER hurts. Before the wing, the ground catches  every fall in the game lands soft (the ground is family; it has been catching the player the whole game). After the wing, the player stops needing to be caught. The wing is graduation FROM being caught.

## THE STANDING WAVE THESIS (the physics the Inn already owns)
The Schumann resonance is a STANDING WAVE  it has two ends: the floor of the sky and the ceiling of the ground. The Bedrock taught the bottom: 7.83Hz felt in the CHEST. The sky has the other half of the same note, and the wing grows where the two are felt whole. **THE PLAYER MUST HAVE STOOD AT THE BOTTOM BEFORE THE SKY WILL LIFT THEM** (the Bedrock's hum is a prerequisite  the Inn's oldest law: down before up). The wing is not lightness  it is the standing wave's crest, held between ground and sky, and the player learns to BE the crest. (The gargoyles' secret, confirmed: stone flies. Flight was never about weight. It is about knowing where the wave holds.)

## THE THREE FORMS (the chain, water-law shaped)
**1. THE HELD GROUND (arrival  everyone).** Climbing is free: the canopy's boughs, the staircases, the towers, the peaks-by-path. Falls land soft. The unreachable is VISIBLE (Kubrick's promise: every high perch in the game is drawn so the player wants it  the yearning is the tutorial).

**2. THE GIVEN WING (earned form  THE FORM).** Six lessons, six teachers: THE RESONANT GARGOYLES  the Inn's most impossible fliers, the proof of the thesis (stone that flies teaches flight the way water that remembers teaches breath). One lesson per gargoyle at the gargoyle's own frequency (the Watcher's hover at 111Hz, the Listener's glide at 64Hz, the Sage's turn at 528Hz, the Caller's climb at 432Hz, the Gatekeeper's LANDING  the hardest lesson, taught at the Bedrock's door: the safe return, the catch that the wing now performs). The sixth lesson is the KEEPER's (the adaptive frequency): the wing opens  the Keeper teaches at the player's own note, and the wing grows in the player's own cadence, because the wing IS the player's own note made visible at the shoulder blades.
- **THE SIGN OF THE GIVEN WING: THE SHADOW FIRST.** The player's shadow gains its wings before the player does (the Klimt stillness holding the moment  the body believes it last; the Inn renders the truth early). In flight after: no flapping at cruise  THE GLIDE IS THE SIGN (flapping is the body arguing with the air; the murlocs never bubble, and the winged never scramble).
- **THE AWARD QUEST:** the six-perch circuit  one lesson at each gargoyle's roost, the last at the highest reachable-without-wings point in the Inn (the climb the whole game has been teaching). The dragon's ride: before the final lesson, the mascot carries the player once around the building  the whole Inn seen from above in one held shot (Spielberg's awe, the player's future seen as gift). First wingbeat after: the Lozen-Wing moment  the wave's crest, felt in the back, and the gargoyles (heard, not seen) all resonating at once: finally.

**3. THE SKY'S OWN (the high trust  presence, not wings).** The low sky is free with the wing: the Clockwork Skies' unreachable gears, the canopy's crown, the Lighthouse lantern winds, the mountain caps. The HIGH sky  the cosmology's edge, the In-Between's upper canopy, the aurora chamber's ceiling  opens by trust (the hatching law's cousin, the water law's cousin: THE SKY MUST KNOW YOU). Known by time-in-air, by the gargoyles' roosts all visited, by flights taken without destination. The high sky's proof: the player who arrives is not flying anymore. They are being held.

- **THE SELKIE EXEMPTION'S SKY VERSION:** the fae-born fly without lessons; the dragon kittens fly by accident; the pixies are mostly flight. The gargoyle circuit is for the land-born  and for them it works, which is the whole proof.

- **TRI-MODE:** Living = the glide (no scramble, full range). Shadow (84Hz) = THE STONE WING: the gargoyle's mode  frozen wings, glide without motion, the held fall that never lands (flight as the Silt Breath's sky-cousin: the shadow wing is still). - **THE WORLD-MAP NOTE:** the wing does not replace travel  the caravan, the ferry, and the walking paths stay canonical (the wing is for the INN'S OWN verticals, not for skipping the road. The Silk Road cannot be flown over; it must be walked. Some journeys are the lesson).
