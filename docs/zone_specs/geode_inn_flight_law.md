# THE GIVEN WING  The Flight Law
## The player learns to fly as their own form (Krista's call, 2026-09-11, after Aion: flight as the character's own, never a mount)
### The Water-Breath Law's sibling: the given breath at the chest, the given wing at the back. The pair of forms.

---

## THE CORE LAW
**NO MOUNT. EVER.** Flight is learned, not ridden  the wing is the player's own form, grown from the player's own rhythm. **NO STAMINA METER. EVER.** A fuel gauge would make the sky a clock, and a timer on flight is the one flaw the classics never fixed (Aion's wings ran on a countdown; the Inn's never will). The wing is not equipment and does not run out  it runs on PRESENCE, like everything else the building gives. **THE FALLING ABOLITION:** the Inn's ground NEVER hurts. Before the wing, the ground catches  every fall in the game lands soft (the ground is family; it has been catching the player the whole game). After the wing, the player stops needing to be caught. The wing is graduation FROM being caught.

## THE STANDING WAVE THESIS (the physics the Inn already owns)
The Schumann resonance is a STANDING WAVE  it has two ends: the floor of the sky and the ceiling of the ground. The Bedrock taught the bottom: 7.83Hz felt in the CHEST. The sky has the other half of the same note, and the wing grows where the two are felt whole. **THE PLAYER MUST HAVE STOOD AT THE BOTTOM BEFORE THE SKY WILL LIFT THEM** (the Bedrock's hum is a prerequisite  the Inn's oldest law: down before up). The wing is not lightness  it is the standing wave's crest, held between ground and sky, and the player learns to BE the crest. (The gargoyles' secret, confirmed: stone flies. Flight was never about weight. It is about knowing where the wave holds.)

THE TRIALS (comical, and the comedy is ACCURATE  each machine fails exactly as physics demands; the science is the lesson, the pratfall is the delivery):
- **THE ORNITHOPTER** (Trial 1): flapping is not flying  lift is not effort. The player pedals; the wings beat; the machine HOPS. Sketch #1: the player mid-hop, labeled (mirror-writing) "effort, sincere. lift, absent."
- **THE AERIAL SCREW** (Trial 2): spin without the airfoil. The screw turns beautifully  a foot of honest altitude, a sound the whole Inn hears  and gently sets back down while the brownies take notes for the minutes.
- **THE PARACHUTE** (Trial 3): the pyramid chute WORKS  history's one that did (the Falling Abolition's comic proof: falling slowed is a kind of flight). The first real float. The ground catches. da Vinci sketches the landing and titles it the first SUCCESS  the first success is the catch.
- **THE GLIDER** (Trial 4): the closest machine. A true glide across the great hall  the room goes silent  and it lands (the foreshadow: THE GLIDE IS THE SIGN; the machine that works is the one that stops trying to climb).
- **THE CEILING GNOME (the Workshop's uninvited examiner, canon per Krista, 2026-09-11: "judging you the whole time, no doubt"):** the little ceiling gnome follows the player to the Workshop rafters and watches EVERY trial  upside down, arms crossed, expression unreadable at any angle. The gnome never comments. The gnome never helps. The gnome never blinks. When the ornithopter hops, the gnome's arms cross tighter. When the parachute works, one eyebrow (still upside down). The Sketchbook of Falls includes one sketch of the gnome; da Vinci's mirror-caption translates: "the true professor."** The trials' comedy now has a live studio audience  and the player KNOWS it is grading them.
- **THE BIRD WATCH** (the Codex phase, Trial 5): da Vinci takes the player to the canopy and they WATCH  the actual old school (Codex on the Flight of Birds: he learned flight from the birds, not the machines). The lesson that ends the machine era: the machines were the right questions wearing the wrong answers.

**3. THE SKY'S OWN (the high trust  presence, not wings).** The low sky is free with the wing: the Clockwork Skies' unreachable gears, the canopy's crown, the Lighthouse lantern winds, the mountain caps. ## THE FAMILY NOTES
- **THE SELKIE EXEMPTION'S SKY VERSION:** the fae-born fly without lessons; the dragon kittens fly by accident; the pixies are mostly flight. The gargoyle circuit is for the land-born  and for them it works, which is the whole proof.
- **THE WING'S RENDER:** the player's own path made visible  the wing renders in the player's dominant collected style (Klimt-gold for scholars, Rackham-ink for fae-touched, O'Keeffe-bone for deep-walkers  the wardrobe of the path, worn at the back).
- **TRI-MODE:** Living = the glide (no scramble, full range). Shadow (84Hz) = THE STONE WING: the gargoyle's mode  frozen wings, glide without motion, the held fall that never lands (flight as the Silt Breath's sky-cousin: the shadow wing is still). In-Between = hovering: half-up, half-down, both true.
- **AUDIO (the Empire Ears law's highest room):** air is thin and honest  flight zones carry the Inn's high end the way water carries the low. The sky's version of the murmur: the wind's chord (the standing wave's ceiling heard as breeze). The first wingbeat is scored as the high end arriving in the shoulders.
- **THE WORLD-MAP NOTE:** the wing does not replace travel  the caravan, the ferry, and the walking paths stay canonical (the wing is for the INN'S OWN verticals, not for skipping the road. The Silk Road cannot be flown over; it must be walked. Some journeys are the lesson).
