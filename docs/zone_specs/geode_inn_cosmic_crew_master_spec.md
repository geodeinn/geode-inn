# THE COSMIC CREW — Master Specification
## The Galaxy's Maintenance Staff — The Beings Who Keep the 42Hz Song Playing

---

## WHAT THE COSMIC CREW IS

The Cosmic Crew is the galaxy's maintenance staff — the beings who keep the 42Hz Song running. Not gods (the Vanir are the gods). Not NPCs. The crew. The working stiffs of the cosmos. The people (beings? entities?) who show up, do their jobs, keep the frequencies aligned, and go home.

The 42Hz Song plays because THESE PEOPLE keep it playing. If they stopped, the Song would drift. The galaxy would lose harmony. The 42Hz would become 41.9, then 41.8, then silence. The Cosmic Crew is the reason it doesn't.

## THE SECRET

The Static is not a malfunction. The Static is ESSENTIAL. Without interference between frequencies, the galaxy would be a single, undifferentiated tone — 42Hz everywhere, at the same volume, forever. The interference creates CONTRAST — the difference between frequencies, the beating, the texture. The Static is the galaxy's TEXTURE. Without The Static, the 42Hz Song would be a monotone. The Static makes the song INTERESTING. The Static knows this. The Static has never told anyone. The Static prefers to be thought of as a problem. Problems are more fun than features.
