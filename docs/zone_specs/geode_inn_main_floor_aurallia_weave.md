## THE WEAVE — space by space (main floor, Level 3's eight rooms)

### 1. The Great Hall — THE CEDAR BENCH (proposed load-bearing anchor)

In the corner of the Great Hall, near the hearth's light but out of its traffic: a cedar bench. This is Aurallia's true seat — not a throne, not a room. A bench.

**THE SEED:** it is the exact bench from the real story. The weekend before opening, Aurallia's shy daughter — who never approached anyone — walked up and sat beside Krista on the cedar bench in the corner. Nearly on her lap. And Aurallia's guard dropped completely when she saw it. The bench is where the shyest person in the room chose to sit. It carries that forever.

**THE MECHANIC:** the Aura of Ease is centered here. The bench is the one furniture in the Inn where any NPC, no matter how guarded, may spontaneously sit down next to the player without ceremony. The radius: one step. The bench radiates the permission the real bench granted. If the player sits on the cedar bench, the SIT mechanic deepens — the camera leans, the details emerge, and sometimes a shy NPC (a kid, a dragon, a hermit regular) just sits down nearby, saying nothing. The both are: the adequate.

**THE RULE:** nobody owns the bench. Not even Aurallia. She tends it — keeps the light right, keeps the corner warm — but the bench belongs to whoever needs it. That's what "The One Who Tends" means.

### 2. The Market — THE TENDING CIRCUIT (proposed)

Aurallia's walking route through the Market: straightening trays no one noticed were crooked, refilling water for the artisans' sprigs, moving one candle three inches so a shy vendor's work catches better light. Never a stall of her own — she came to Soulstice for extra money and found a calling; in the Inn she is the reason everyone else's booth looks like home. Ambient, background, optional flavor. Players who watch learn the SIT principle by example: presence over performance.

### 3. The Library Nook — READING HOUR (proposed)

Aurallia teaches art AND English. At certain hours, the Library Nook hosts Reading Hour: Aurallia reading aloud to whoever gathers — kids, dragons, the Wanderer pretending not to listen. No quest, no reward, no attendance taken. The honest seed: a teacher at a charter school, teaching because teaching is the love. Optional ambient event, Anti-Skinner compliant by design (missable, and stays missable — the point of Reading Hour is that it happens whether you attend or not).

### 4. The Dragon's Geode Corner — THE DRAGON'S CHOICE (already canon, needs the thread pulled)

The kids' doc already rules it: the dragon respects John but doesn't nest with John; the dragon sleeps near the shy daughter. The Baby Dragon's Main Floor questline (Level 3 roster) gains one quiet canonical thread: the dragon chose the bench corner too. Dragons and shy daughters pick the same warm spots. No new mechanic — one line of continuity, filed in both docs.

### 5. The Firekeeper's Hearth / Naut's Nook — NEIGHBORS, NOT OVERLAPS

The Hearth belongs to the Firekeeper (Ember). Naut's Nook belongs to Naut. Aurallia is their neighbor, not their replacement — her territory is the BETWEEN: the bench corner, the walking routes, the rooms' edges. Where the Firekeeper tends the fire, Aurallia tends the people warmed by it. Explicit boundary so the main floor never has two hearth-keepers competing for one job.

### 6. The Arcade — NO WEAVE (deliberate)

The Arcade is noise and glee. The Aura of Ease does not reach here, and should not. The main floor needs one room where the heartbeat stays quick. Aurallia doesn't game; her kids do. The kids' Arcade rights live in their own doc.

## THE WORKING STUDIO (Krista's addition, ruled same day)

The cave studio is not just where Aurallia paints with the kids. It is HER studio — her own easel, her own wall, her own work.

**THE SEED (River's testimony, relayed by Krista):** Aurallia is ridiculously talented at sketching and painting — GALLERY SHOWS were held on her work, decades ago, before the silence set in. Krista only knows this because River told her. What Krista has seen firsthand: Aurallia 'doodling' Robert Bateman-level critters on some mornings before the shop opens — casual, unremarkable, the way other people check their phone.

**THE REGISTER (honest-label law):** Robert Bateman — the Canadian wildlife master — is the reference register for her skill tier: meticulous, alive, the animal present in its own weather. Reference credited, never copied; Aurallia's subjects are her own (Inn-critter kin, the beasts the kids invent, whatever the cave light suggests).

**THE ROOM:** one corner of the cave studio is untouchable — HER easel, angled so the work faces the wall. Finished work doesn't hang in the Inn's galleries; the habit of decades is privacy. What the player sees is the evidence: sketchbooks in stacks, pencils worn to stubs, a work-in-progress glimpsed at an angle and never offered. The kids' paintings pinned at their eye heights; her sketches at nobody's — she doesn't display.

**THE AMBIENT CANON (the mornings):** players who reach the Spiral Gallery early find Aurallia at the easel mid-'doodle' — a gallery-grade wolf or heron taking shape like it's nothing, coffee cooling, kids not yet awake. No quest marks, no purchase, no press. The most talented painter in the building treats it like a morning stretch.

**THE OPEN THREAD (no gate yet — noted, not asked):** whether her work ever shows again is a story thread the Inn holds quietly. Decades of silence don't break for a mechanic. If it ever happens, it happens because the character earns it — the Living Story law. Parked. **UPDATE (2026-09-18):** Krista's ruling — if Aurallia chooses to show in the Inn, that would be awesome. The natural home now exists on the plan ledger: THE MODERN DAY GALLERY (geode_inn_garnish_layers.md), the garnish layer for undiscovered living artists who choose to contribute. Her thread stays parked — the choice stays hers — but the room it would hang in is now on the books.
