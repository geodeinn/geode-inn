## THE SYSTEM AS RULED (2026-10-05)

### The Entry Wall
Each level's entry walls are done in that zone's own art register (Linework-as-UI holds). The portraits of the zone's characters hang on the walls. The world/level itself is shown in a Stargate-style wall (1994) — the one you face while entering, and you walk through it to enter. Ceremony, not a loading screen: the chevron-lock anticipation read as ritual.

### The Three-Click Portrait Ladder
One click = a sassy comment. Two = more depth. Three = the full backstory. Maps onto the Spoken Road's Four Layers complexity ladder: depth is always there, nobody is required to go deeper than they want. Sassy-first is the Inn's register: nobody opens with their trauma.

### Dungeon Master State (residue, not health)
The portraits carry state the way Dungeon Master (FTL Games, 1987) party portraits did — but in the Inn's register. Not "hungry, wounded": residue. Charles's portrait wearing last season's feather. Lozen's getting a little more relaxed the more times you've crossed the threshold. Two visits vs twenty read differently. The wall keeps receipts (Gazette #024).

### The Exit Wall (the goodbye)
The wall is bidirectional: leaving, the portraits acknowledge you on the way out. The gate remembers where you've been — crossed zones wear a different finish on the world-wall. The gate as a growing atlas.

### The Voices
The three-click ladder speaks in the character's actual voice profile (Dialogue canon voiceProfile per NPC). You meet the zone with your ears before your feet.

## THE KNOCK LAW (approved 2026-10-05)

**Honest label:** Morse code — Samuel Morse & Alfred Vail, operational 1844 ("What hath God wrought," the first message). Dots and dashes: a real alphabet you can tap on a door with your knuckles. SOS (... --- ...) the famous specimen, 1906/1908.

**The law:** each zone has its own knock — a short dot-dash pattern that is the zone's signature in miniature. A rhythm signature, tying directly into the Inn's rhythm canon (drum-circle cadence, the engine room's gears turning to the rhythm above, the REC law): the knock is the zone's tempo tapped out on wood. Learn the Great Hall's knock and the Great Hall knows you're coming.

**The mechanics (wired to standing laws):**
- **Courtesy, not a gate (No-Fail law):** the wrong knock never locks anything. A wrong knock gets an answer, not a wall — a sassy reply from the other side of the door. The knock is a handshake, not a password.
- **Once-only family (Postmaster precedent):** a correct knock answers fully once per visit; repeat knocking gets the reduced reply. The Inn rewards attention, not repetition (Anti-Skinner law).
- **Teaching ladder:** the knock is learned, never bought. An NPC teaches it — most naturally at portrait depth level two or three (the same ladder, one more gift). The knock belongs to the people who stayed to listen.
- **The Screamer's door:** at the Catacombs hard seam, the knock is the courtesy before the seal — you knock, the door answers with a beat of silence, THEN it opens and seals behind you (Screamer seed dossier, 0949ae7). The polite warning before the Dungeon Master beat.
- **Judge Doom lineage:** the "Shave and a Haircut" knock (Who Framed Roger Rabbit, 1988) is the tonal ancestor already canonized in the Lloyd file — the knock that cannot go unanswered. The Inn keeps the resonance, drops the menace: answering is warmth here, not compulsion.
- **The Inn as telegraph (open, Krista's ruling):** knocks as messages between zones. Zones that share a wall can trade tapped words. The building has a nervous system that runs on knuckles. Whether the knock alphabet is true Morse (translated), or the Inn's own dot-dash dialect per zone, is the load-bearing ruling owed.
