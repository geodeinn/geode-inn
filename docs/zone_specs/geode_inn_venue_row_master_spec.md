## THE RAZOR ROW (the naming law, honestly labeled)

The Razor Cabinet is the official naming source for the seated venues. Every name wears its citation, dates attached:
- Occam's razor — William of Ockham, c. 1287-1347 (the canonical Latin phrasing is John Punch's 1639 tidy-up — the label says so)
- Hanlon's razor — coined 1980 (Robert J. Hanlon, a Murphy's Law book submission)
- Sagan's razor — Carl Sagan, "extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence" (Cosmos, 1980)
- Grice's razor / cooperative principle — Paul Grice, 1975 (the maxims as courtesies)
- Chesterton's fence — G.K. Chesterton, The Thing (1929)

The row as a whole: a philosophy shelf you can eat at. The honest-label law in commercial form.

## THE STAFFING LAW (ruled by Krista, 2026-10-14)

**Restaurant staff are ROTATING STORYLINE CHARACTERS of that level.** No dedicated new NPC rosters per venue.

- Keeps staffing simple (no roster bloat: the venues are cast from people we already love)
- Gives casual face-time to the imposing cast — the dramatic roster seen at rest, apron on, behind a counter; the kitchen is where titles come off
- The rotation runs on the trading-card system: staffing shifts are card draws from the level's deck. Shuffle, pull a server — same restaurant, different face, a new little story every visit
