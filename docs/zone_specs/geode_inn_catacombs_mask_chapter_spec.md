# THE MASK CHAPTER — The Catacombs' New Room

**Ruled in (Krista, 2026-09-19):** "things like death masks are an art in and of themselves and i just made us a whole new chapter for the catacombs." Born from the Terracotta Army construction thread (the how, the maker's mark, the clay) — the chapter the dark seam opened.

## THE LAW

The death mask is memory's first technology. Before photography, the mask was the only way to keep a face — the family's last commission, taken in the quiet after. The Catacombs already hold clay as the first WRITING surface (The Stacks' tablets, the first words humans pressed into anything that kept them); this chapter holds clay and plaster as the first PORTRAIT. One material, two miracles: the tablet kept what was said, the mask kept who said it. Both are impressions. Both are the same law: press the real thing into the permanent thing, and the real thing survives.

**THE ART, HONEST-LABELED (dated and sourced, gravity register where dark, taught warm where it was made with love):**
- Egyptian funerary masks (Tutankhamun's gold, c. 1323 BCE) — the face made divine for the journey; ties the Egypt zone and the Weighing.
- Roman wax IMAGINES — the ancestor masks carried in funeral processions, the family's faces walking through Rome one last time (the procession as procession; ties the Faire's ceremony register).
- Renaissance plaster masks (Dante, 14th c.) — the poet's face kept by the city that exiled him.
- LIFE masks vs death masks (Newton, Beethoven) — the face in use vs the face at rest; the same craft pointed at the living.
- L'INCONNUE DE LA SEINE (late 1880s) — the unknown drowned woman of Paris, the serene face cast by the morgue, the mask that hung in drawing rooms and was kissed by a generation of poets, then became the face of Resusci Anne, the CPR training mannequin — LITERALLY THE MOST KISSED FACE IN HISTORY: the face of an unknown dead woman is the face that taught the living to bring each other back. Modern Day Legends shelf candidate and the chapter's crown: the Progressive Lean in one specimen (the unknown living face, given — the morgue's mask, taken — the world's choice to make her the teaching face, chosen). The darkest origin arriving at the warmest use.

## WHAT THE ROOM IS

A chamber off The Stacks — the shelves of clay tablets open onto the shelves of faces. THE MASK SHELF: the Inn's mask collection, honest-labeled, each mask carrying its provenance plaque (who, when, taken by whom, kept why). The room's register: the Gallery's dignity worn by the Archive's patience. Stone shelves, amber light, each face lit like a portrait, never like a specimen.

**THE CRAFT LESSON (the interactive):** the player learns the TAKING — the clay, the plaster, the negative and the positive, the pour, the reveal. And the masks are NOT spooky (the Whisper Layer's warm law): the faces are at rest, not trapped. The room's line: the mask is how love kept a face before cameras existed.
