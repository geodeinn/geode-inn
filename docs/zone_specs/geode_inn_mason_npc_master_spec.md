# MASON, THE STONE MASON — Master Specification
## Master Stonemason NPC — Acoustic Architecture

---

## WHO MASON IS

In a tavern where ancient entities hold cosmic titles, Mason's name IS the job. When asked whether the name came from the trade or the trade from the name, Mason offers no philosophical commentary — only a blank look and a heavy hammer blow against a chisel block.

Mason is the Master Stone Mason of the Geode Inn. Permanent NPC. Resident of the Undercity Workshop, adjacent to the Bone Room. The being who carves the Inn's acoustic architecture — the gargoyle sentinels, the catacomb resonance chambers, the tuning fork arrays. Mason works stone the way the Weaver works thread — by hand, by feel, by the frequency the stone already contains.

## WHAT MASON DOES

| Job | Detail |
|-----|--------|
| Gargoyle Carving | Mason carves the six resonant gargoyles. Each gargoyle is tuned to a specific frequency by the stone Mason chooses. The Watcher (111Hz) was carved from thermal-area stone the redcaps heated. |
| Acoustic Architecture | Mason shapes the catacomb walls, the resonance chambers, the echo corridors. The catacombs' acoustic properties are Mason's design — the places where Gregorian chant activates, where 42Hz amplifies, where silence damps. |
| Stone Testing | Mason tests every stone for pitch. The tuning fork array is the tool. Each stone vibrates at its Mohs-to-frequency value. Mason can hear the frequency by touching the stone — no instrument needed. Mason IS the instrument. |
| Catacomb Maintenance | The catacombs shift, crack, and settle. Mason maintains them. The dust floor records every step, and Mason's footprints are the only ones in the lower catacombs — no one else goes that deep. |

## THE TAVERN RUNNING JOKE

Regulars and fellow craftspeople debate whether Mason was christened with a functional noun or if the Inn simply bestows functional names upon anyone who works a chisel long enough. Mason regards such discussions as a waste of daylight and breath.
