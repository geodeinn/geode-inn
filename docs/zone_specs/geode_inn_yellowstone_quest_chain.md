# YELLOWSTONE — Quest Chain Master Spec
## The Matter seat's Weight-key chain: the digestion, the busy ones, and the setting-down
### Build order #2 of the parks sweep (thin flag 22), written 2026-09-10. The park-quest grammar: opens with the heartbeat reading, plays the Third Sleeper's dream key (THE WEIGHT-KEY: set things down — the letting-go), ends at the park-scale altar. Sources: the fae geology parks doc (the redcaps, Marge, the digestion, the 111Hz), the geological sleep master spec (the Matter seat), the national parks veil doc (Marge's testimony), the artist audit (Benton, ruled), the Stone entity (obsidian).

## THE ZONE IN ONE BREATH

Yellowstone is the sleeping giant's digestion — the magma chamber is the stomach, the thermal features are the metabolism, and the redcaps are the gut flora: the working class of the Fae Kingdom, the only fae who work with fire. THE HEARTBEAT IS THE STRONGEST HERE: the 50 BPM pulse is felt in the boardwalks more strongly than anywhere else on the surface (canon: "felt strongly at Yellowstone and the Inn's altar"). The Weight-key's home: the digestion takes things in and changes them — the zone's whole grammar is transformation, and the player's work is to feed the transformation by SETTING THINGS DOWN. What the player lets go of here, the giant keeps; what the giant keeps, it returns CHANGED.

**THE STIR (canon, unchanged):** FIRE = ADJUSTMENT. The redcaps feel the stir first, in the heat. The eruption intervals shift. The data shows it. Nobody publishes it.

## THE QUESTS (the Weight-key chain, five)

### QUEST 1 — THE READING (the heartbeat reading, the opening)
The player arrives carrying the heartbeat and reads the strongest surface pulse in the parks system: warm, steady, DIGESTIVE — not the sleeping whisper of the Smokies or the steady recline of the Plateau, but a working rhythm, the metabolism of a geological being in deep sleep. The first NPC: **MARGE** — 31 years at the park, first female backcountry ranger, the thermos, the stack of geological surveys that are actually activity logs. Marge's testimony is EARNED IN STAGES (the veil canon): first the surface ("People come here for the geysers. The geysers are nice. The geysers are also not the point."), then, as the player proves they can sit still at a thermal feature without asking what it's for, the deeper layers. Quest completes when the player feels the pulse in the boardwalk and Marge says, without looking up: "You feel that? Then you're one of maybe a dozen this year."

### QUEST 2 — THE SHIFT WHISTLE (the schedule as rhythm)
Old Faithful. THE LAW: the geyser is not a random eruption — it's a redcap schedule; the eruption is the end-of-shift pressure release, the SHIFT WHISTLE. The intervals have been slowly speeding up since 1872 because the redcaps have been working harder — the load is building. THE QUEST: the time-reading mini-game — the player sits through three eruption cycles and learns to read the rhythm: the difference between the schedule's REST (the pool filling, the quiet breathing) and the BUILDING (the pre-ermission surge the instruments measure and nobody publishes). The rock-lover beat: the sinter formations around the pool are the geyser's own stonework — thousands of years of dissolved mineral, deposited one droplet at a time; the player who looks closely enough reads the eruption history in the rings (the tree of the thermal world). Quest completes when the player calls the next eruption from the pool's breathing alone — and Marge, watching, pours a second coffee without being asked.

### QUEST 3 — THE STEAM FACES (first contact; the Weight-key applied)
The faces in the steam are redcap faces — the workers going about their work in the mineral vapors. THE LAW (Marge's, canon): "They're not dangerous. They're busy. There's a difference. Most people can't tell the difference between something that's dangerous and something that's busy." THE QUEST: the player earns first contact by NOT REACTING — standing at the hot spring's edge while the steam faces come and go, doing the redcaps the courtesy of treating them as workers, not apparitions. Presence without pursuit; the camera never raises. The contact: **THE REDCAP FOREMAN** — broad-shouldered, three-foot-eight, skin of cooled basalt, amber eyes (the same amber as the thermal features and the Inn's hearth light), hands always red (iron oxide — the red is TRADE, not blood; the cap is iron-stained fabric, the hands are the primary visual). The Foreman never stops working during the conversation. Hook word: "Busy."

### QUEST 4 — THE ADJUSTMENT (the post-stir recalibration; the letting-go as gameplay)
The stir's local expression: the intervals shifting, a new mud pot where no mud pot was, a hot spring's color changing overnight. The redcaps respond — ADJUSTMENT, not fear — and for the first time in the parks system, the player is asked to HELP SERVICE THE DIGESTION. THE WEIGHT-KEY, MADE MECHANIC: the work is impossible with full pockets. Each station — the pressure valves (geysers), the heat exchangers (hot springs), the pressure relief (mud pots) — requires both hands, and both hands require setting things down. The inventory-lending loop: the player's stones and charms are PLACED (not dropped — placed, deliberately, like tools on a bench) at the stations; the giant's body keeps them while the player works. THE ROCK-LOVER'S REWARD LOOP: the digestion gives back CHANGED. The items retrieved at quest's end come back mineral-stained — the heat's colors on them, iron-rich oranges and blues, geyserite films, the thermal signature of the work done. The giant keeps nothing; the giant returns everything transformed. (The Inn's whole law in one mechanic: the letting-go is how the changing happens.)

### QUEST 5 — THE POOL (the park-scale altar)
The deepest thermal basin at the moment of eruption — THE ALTAR: THE EXHALE, FROM INSIDE THE SCHEDULE. The player stands at the pool's edge as the shift changes — the redcaps' end-of-shift, the pressure release, the whistle — and feels the full digestion from within: the water's climb, the breathing surge, the eruption as the giant's exhale made visible. The carried heartbeat and the eruption lock: 50 BPM felt in the chest, the column standing on it. THE FINAL LETTING-GO: the player chooses ONE item from their pack to set down at the pool's edge, permanently — the Weight-key's graduation (the one thing you learned you can live without; each player's is different, and the zone remembers). **THE REWARD: OBSIDIAN** (the Stone entity's three — Gold Sheen, Rainbow, Snowflake: "the volcano's sudden grief, three ways") — the digestion's fire, cooled to a blade. The rock lover's stone, earned at the stomach.
