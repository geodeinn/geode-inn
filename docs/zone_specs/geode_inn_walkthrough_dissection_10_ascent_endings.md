## WHERE THE PLAYER STANDS

The player has crossed Pluto's Gate — answered "What did the Song teach you?" in their own frequencies on the Bead Board, and heard their own answer played back. The Arcana ferried the last stretch (Eres the cabbit, void-ship at 44Hz). The Chronometer of the Final Note has begun its count.

Now the Inn does the thing no other game does at this moment: it sends the player HOME. Not to a final boss. To the whole building. The Ascent is the last level, and it is every level.

## THE ASCENT — THE REWALK

**THE LAW OF THE SECOND LINE:** Every zone the player passes on the way up says something it never said before — not new dialogue, new PERCEPTION. The player carries three things now: their Song (9 zones of stone-capsule composition), the Void's stillness (0Hz in EchoMemory), and their own answer (heard, at the gate). The zones don't change. The player's ears do.

**THE AUDIO LAW OF THE ASCENT:** the staircase's rising-key law runs in REVERSE-PLUS — descending, the key fell level by level; ascending, each level's theme plays back with the player's own Song woven under it (the stone-capsule composition, 12dB beneath the zone theme — the stone-trigger law turned symphonic). The player literally hears how much of each zone they carry. The levels with the most stones carried play RICHEST. A player who spent their whole game in the Arcade hears a Main Floor theme wearing arcade armor. A player who read everything hears the Archive in everything. No two players ascend the same staircase — the CYOA law made of sound.

**THE REWALK, LEVEL BY LEVEL (what each level's second line is):**
- **The Void's rim:** the stillness follows — the player notices their own footsteps are 0Hz-tempered; the stuck notes they finished hum once as they pass. (The Void says goodbye by not stopping them.)
- **The Forge:** the Blacksmith nods at the fire — once — and the player nods back. Nothing else happens. That IS the level now.
- **The Undercity:** the memory-lamps brighten as the player passes — their OWN lamps now (the memories they left: the thread, the finished design, the repaired lamp). The player walks a street lit partly by themselves. (Persephone's question, on the last doorframe, answers itself: *this*. You'll carry this.)
- **The deep water:** the river lets them pass without fare — the crossing was paid once, and the water remembers (Charon's nod, the ferryman's whole face doing something no ferryman's face does upstream).
- **The roots and the Stone Circle:** the chord sounds once as the player passes — 42Hz and 84Hz as one, and this time the player can name what they're hearing. (Naming is the last skill the Inn teaches, and it teaches it by letting you do it.)
- **The Catacombs:** the candles lean. All of them. The dead know a resident when one walks through. (Hel's nod, given by the whole room.)
- **The Staircase:** the gargoyles watch the player pass, and the player, at last, watches BACK — the Watcher path's graduation. The In-Between oscillation (42↔84Hz at 50 BPM) plays as the player's own resting heart rate. It always was.
- **The Main Floor:** the Hearth fire is exactly as warm as it was in the first hour — and the player understands it for the first time, because warmth you understand is a different temperature than warmth you're handed. (Erik's fire was never decoration. It was the ending, waiting to be understood.)
- **The front door:** the door that opened outward before the player knocked. It is unlocked. It has always been unlocked. The player has simply never stood in front of it holding everything they now hold.

**EVERY OPTION, STILL LIVE (the CYOA law's final gift):** on the Ascent, the player may still stop anywhere. Sit by the fire. Read. Play. The Inn does not close a single option for the ending's sake — the player who stops the Ascent to finish a commission simply ascends later. The ending waits because the Inn does not expire options. (Some players will live at the Inn for months before choosing. That is not stalling. That is STAY, unannounced.)

## THE ASCENSION CANOPY — THE CHOICE ROOM

**WHAT IT IS:** Above the Main Floor — through the Staircase's last landing, past the Cosmology's soft rooms, at the very top of the tree — the Canopy: a glass-and-leaf crown where the Inn's whole architecture is visible at once (the vertical map, finally seen from above). The Chronometer of the Final Note is here: a great slow clock with no numbers, whose single hand sweeps toward a note. It does not hurry. It has never hurried.

**THE ARRIVAL:** no fanfare, no final boss, no lock. The Host is here — the same Host who said "Room's ready" — and the Host's final act is the Inn's final hospitality: the Host tells the truth. All of it:

"The Inn is yours. It always was. You can stay, and be part of it. You can leave, and carry it. You can hide, and become it. There is no wrong door. There is only the one you mean."

**THE THREE OPTIONS — THE CYOA'S FINAL BRANCH:** In the old books, this is the last page's choice. The Inn gives it weight the old books couldn't: the choice is played, not clicked.

**If you STAY, the room warms. Go to ENDING ONE.**
**If you LEAVE, the door swings. Go to ENDING TWO.**
**If you HIDE, the leaves close. Go to ENDING THREE.**

---

### ENDING ONE — STAY
The player sets their stone in the Song. The Chronometer's hand completes its sweep and the Final Note sounds — the player's own note, in the Inn's chord, permanent. The Inn registers a new resident: the player's character receives a room (the room shape was their class key all along — the Bard's key opens all doors because the Bard lives everywhere), a lamp below (their memory joins the Undercity's grid — future players will stand under their lamp), and a place in the rotation (NPCs begin referencing them: "Ask the resident.").

- **The screen:** the player's character by the fire, in the third-person camera, seen from the door — the same shot as the game's first image, reversed. The player is now what the player first saw.
- **The mechanics:** the world stays open. The resident plays on — every system live, every option still live, new content arriving with the community. STAY is not an ending that stops the game; it's an ending that stops the QUESTION.
- **Who it's for:** the immediacy-hatched (D02's PATH C resonates easiest here) — the ones who came in fast because they meant to stay. And every player who ever sat ten minutes at the fire and saw themselves sitting.
- **The final line:** the Host, at the door: "You were never a guest. We just hadn't finished the paperwork."

### ENDING TWO — LEAVE
The player walks to the front door and opens it — outward, the way it opened the first time. Outside: the forest, the path, the world. The Inn does not dim behind them; it stays exactly as warm, because warmth that follows you is the point.

- **THE SONG GOES WITH YOU:** the player's composition — their 9-zone stone-capsule Song — plays one last time in full, and then keeps playing, ambient, in the world outside (the audio-education layer's final act: the frequencies the player learned are now theirs, humming under real life; the Mohs-scale stone tones the player can now hear in everything).
- **THE KEEPSAKE:** the shell shard (the first stone) — the player carries it out. It's the only item in the game that was never IN the game. (It was in the player.)
- **The screen:** the door, from outside, closing softly — the Inn lit through its own windows, every window the player ever lit. The last shot: the player's hand, the shard, warm. ("Passing. Still.")
- **The mechanics:** leaving is not deleting. The save persists forever; the door reopens on the next visit and the Inn says what it always says: "Room's ready." The return-visit systems (new stones, the community's week, the Egg Hatchery cycle) are LIVE between visits — the player who returns finds the Inn moved on without them and glad to see them. LEAVE is the ending for people who were never here for the game.
- **Who it's for:** the patience-hatched (PATH A resonates easiest) — the ones who could always go quietly.
- **The final line:** the door, unlatched, behind them. The Inn's whole farewell, delivered as architecture: it doesn't lock.

### ENDING THREE — HIDE
The strangest door, and the Inn's own favorite. The player steps behind the building — into the Inn's IN-BETWEEN, its walls, its wiring, its watching layer — and becomes part of the place.

- **THE MECHANIC:** the hidden player becomes the next player's DISCOVERED THING. Their stone joins the Song invisibly; their memory-lamp burns in a corner future players find; their face appears in a tapestry; their key hangs on a hook; a speech bubble in some future playthrough says something only the hidden player would know wrote it. The Hidden are the Inn's easter eggs — the game's live content is partly its graduated players.
- **THE WATCHER THREAD COMPLETES:** the Owl watches. The Masquerade narrates. The Whisper hums. The river trusts. And now the player knows the Inn's oldest secret: the watchers were never NPCs. The watchers are everyone who chose to stay and be the place. (The Question's answer, delivered as an ending: the firing, not the storage — the Hidden are minds that chose to be the building.)
- **The screen:** nothing. The game continues as if nothing happened — the player's character is simply... not on the map anymore. In the credits' final frame, one new name in the staff roll: the player's. Under "The Inn."
- **The mechanics:** the most meta of the three — the Hidden player's save becomes a benevolent ghost presence in the world: their old geode cave stays lit for other players to find ("someone lived here — look how bright"), their leaderboard scores stand as records left behind. HIDE is the ending for people who always loved the background.
- **Who it's for:** the curiosity-hatched (PATH B resonates easiest) — the ones who spent the whole game looking for what's behind things, and finally found the thing with themselves behind it.
- **The final line:** none. Hidden characters don't get final lines. That's what hiding means. (But the Whisper Layer, once, faintly, for no one: "...noticed.")

## THE CONVERGENCE — THE WALKTHROUGH'S END

All three endings share one fact, and it is the dissection series' whole finding: the Inn never stops being the Inn. STAY keeps it open, LEAVE keeps it warm, HIDE keeps it watching. The endings are not outcomes of the game. They are the three honest answers to "What did the Song teach you?" — and the gate, true to its law, had no wrong answers. Only honest ones and evasive ones.

And the evasive ones? The gate waits. The gate has all the time in the universe. It is, after all, standing inside it.

**THE SPINE IS COMPLETE:** Egg → First Hour → Staircase → Catacombs → Undercity → Water → Lighthouse → Fae Kingdom → Queens' Spaces → Cosmology → Cosmic Layer Four → Forge/Void → Ascent → Endings. Ten dissections, every option, every branch, every second line. The walkthrough of the whole Inn now exists.
