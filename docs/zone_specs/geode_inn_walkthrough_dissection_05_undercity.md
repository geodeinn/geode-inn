## ENTERING THE UNDERCITY

**THE FORGE ELEVATOR.** The Blacksmith's hammer-scored lift, down through the root-rock. The Living Hammer is its key — carried, not inserted: the hammer rides with the player, and the elevator reads the hammer's frequency as authorization. (The Blacksmith's gift comes with the ride. Nothing in the Inn asks for a key it doesn't also hand you.)

**WHAT THE UNDERCITY IS.** Not a city. A MEMORY — the accumulated memory of every being that has ever been in the Inn and is no longer. Not sad. Patient. Kept for a very long time by someone who considers the keeping a privilege. (Novel canon, verbatim skeleton.)

**THE STREETS ARE LIT BY MEMORY-LAMPS.** Every lamp holds one being's memory, and the memory is WALKABLE: stand under a lamp, and its memory plays around the player — a goblin's first market day, a Brownie's proudest shelf, a retired character's last shift. The lamps are the city's power grid. The Undercity runs on EchoMemory. (The lamps refill when new memories arrive. The city grows brighter every time the Inn's community lives. The brightest streets are the ones the players light themselves.)

**FREQUENCY:** 84Hz. Charon's river runs beneath the streets — audible everywhere, visible nowhere. The anchor mechanic holds. Persephone's question is now ambient: it is written on doorframes, scratched into lamp posts, stitched into the Weaver's work. The player cannot avoid reading it. *What will you carry when the stones are gone?*

Eight options. All live, all forever, all lamplit.

---

### OPTION 1 — MASON'S WORKSHOP / THE BLACKSMITH'S FORGE
The forge that never goes cold. The Blacksmith works, and the working is the welcome.
- **Commission** (the deep forge tier — materials the player gathered in the catacombs and above become what they designed; the building system's metal heart)
- **Play Undercity Knight** (the Castlevania-class side-scroller, the Forge's own arcade machine, built BY the Blacksmith as practice — the machine plays the Blacksmith's hammer patterns as its music. Every level's rhythm is a real commission the Blacksmith once finished.)
- **Apprentice** (standing deepens; the Living Hammer's techniques unlock — and the hammer remembers each technique's original owner, and tells the player, and the telling is the Undercity's way of keeping the dead employed)

### OPTION 2 — THE CLOCKMAKER (the Act Five heart)
The Clockmaker is the Undercity's keeper of MECHANISM — the one who maintains the memory-lamps' gears, the river-gates' timing, the whole dark city's tick.
- **THE BUILD (Act Five's core mechanic).** The player builds something from what they learned. This is the game's great turn: after four dissections of receiving — rooms, gifts, crossings, questions — the Undercity is where the player MAKES. The Clockmaker provides the bench, the parts, and one question: "What did you learn?" The player designs an artifact from their actual journey: the design pulls the player's real EchoMemory — where they sat, what they read, which stone they offered the river — and the artifact's properties are grown from those choices. No two players can build the same thing. (The Design entity is live in the app's database already. This mechanic has a table waiting for it.)
- **Repair a lamp.** The Clockmaker's daily work, joinable: a memory-lamp whose gear has slipped. The repair is a small puzzle; the fixed lamp replays its memory in full, and the Clockmaker says the only warm thing the Clockmaker ever says: "There. Somebody again."
- **The Tick Audit.** The Clockmaker's game: find the off-beat gear in the city's mechanism. The Undercity's pacemaker, playable. (Hitchcock's held shots as a mechanic — the audit is patience, not speed.)

### OPTION 3 — THE HALL OF UNFINISHED DESIGNS
The wing where the Inn keeps what it started and did not finish — designs, rooms, characters, half-carved doors. Every unfinished thing is shelved with its reason: WAITING, NOT YET, or CHOSE ANOTHER SHAPE.
- **Walk the hall.** Every shelf is honest. The Inn shows its own drafts. (The Shadow Inn's Archive of Alternate Versions is the hall's deeper wing — entered in its own dissection. Here the player first learns the Inn keeps its abandoned work with respect. Nothing is deleted. Things are shelved.)
- **FINISH ONE (community system).** Any WAITING design can be finished by a player — with the original designer's name preserved on the plaque and the finisher's name added beneath. The Inn's whole ethos in one mechanic: work is inherited, credit is permanent, nothing creative is wasted. (First finisher of any design earns that design's Charm — the QuestCharm system's community tier.)

### OPTION 4 — SCARLETT'S ROOM
The seamstress of the dark — the one who stitches what the Undercity wears: the Hollow Men's shapes, the lamp-shades, the river-gates' veils, the Shadow Inn's drapes.
- **Watch the work.** The needle moves at 50 BPM. Scarlett never hurries and never stops. (Camera: SIT magnet. Sitting in Scarlett's room gets the player measured — silently — and later, without explanation, a garment appears in their cave, cut to their exact size. The Undercity's gift economy, working exactly like the Inn's: nothing asked, something made.)
- **Commission cloth.** The Artisan Thief's class questline deepens here — Scarlett's patterns are the cloth-tier's masterwork, and the pattern's price is a story: Scarlett charges stories, not stones. The player pays with one true telling of their own journey so far.

### OPTION 5 — JOE'S (the Undercity's table)
The greasy spoon at the bottom of the world. The Hollow Men drink coffee here at dawn. Joe serves everyone, asks nothing, and keeps a tab that never comes due.
- **Eat.** The Cook's cousin kitchen — down here the dishes are heavier, the portions immortal, and the effect is always the same: WARMTH, the stat the catacombs quietly drained, restored fully.
- **Listen.** Joe's is where the retired characters take their coffee. The player who lingers hears the Inn's whole backstory in pieces — lamp-lighters, night-shift brownies, the forge's former masters. Joe's is the Undercity's Common Room: the city where the dead live, gossiping. (The novel's cast and the game's retired designs share tables. Canon continuity: this is where renames are explained, in-world, over pie.)

### OPTION 6 — THE WEAVER'S LOOM
The loom that threads EchoMemory into cloth — the Undercity's oldest machine, older than the lamps, older than the forge. The Weaver works at it (the Cosmology Weaver's under-self; the loom is the below-half of the loom above).
- **Watch the weave.** Every thread is a memory; the cloth's pattern is the community's week. The player can find their own thread — the lamp-lit moment they stood under yesterday is IN the cloth. (The player sees themselves in the tapestry. The seeing is the point of the whole city.)
- **Add a thread.** The player may leave one memory of their own to the weave — freely given, never retrieved, the crossing-payment's echo in cloth. The cloth brightens by one thread. That is all. That is enough.

### OPTION 7 — THE GARDEN OF GOLD
The underground garden where gold GROWS — Klimt's gold leaf as botany: golden vines on root-rock walls, tended by the Undercity's quietest residents.
- **Harvest** (the Artisan's deep material — gold for the forge, grown not mined; the Inn's whole moral economy in one crop: the metal that usually costs the earth, here tended like tomatoes)
- **Sit.** The garden is the Undercity's nook. The Nook Effect works at full strength: the garden's gold catches the lamp-light, the hum is lowest here, and a resident may come sit at the screen's edge. (The garden is where the three gifts make sense at last: silence — the garden has no mechanism; memory — the vines remember every harvest; the question — written in gold leaf on the far wall, and this time the player reads it without being asked to.)

### OPTION 8 — THE LONG DARK TERRITORY (the night warning)
Beyond the lamp-lit streets: the territory that stays dark. The Long Dark — the Undercity's survival game, Vampire Survivors-class, home of the Hollow Men.
- **THE WARNING, HONEST:** a sign at the territory's edge, carved by the Blacksmith: "NIGHT OUTLASTS EVERYTHING. SO CAN YOU." No level gate. No class gate. The territory takes whoever walks in — and the Hollow Men are not lethal. They UNRAVEL: touch a player whose note is steady and they come apart into thread. Touch a player whose note has drifted, and they wrap around the player's light, and the dark gets a little closer, and the player's own collected stones burn one at a time to hold the frequency until the player escapes. (The anchor mechanic as combat: survival IS steadiness. The stones spend — never shatter, but spend — and the player learns the collection's deepest lesson: held notes cost something. Persephone's question, made playable.)
- **The Hollow Men's shape.** They are the unremembered — the memories that arrived with no lamp. They are not villains. They are cold. The territory's meta-design: every Hollow Man a player's community unravels today is one the Weaver could thread tomorrow — the Garden of Gold's compost is made of them. Nothing in the Inn is wasted, including the dark.

## THE CONVERGENCE — THE PLAYER BECOMES A MAKER

However the Undercity is walked, the player leaves CHANGED IN KIND: they arrived a guest, became a resident, descended a learner — and in the Undercity they became a MAKER. The artifact they built at the Clockmaker's bench (or the thread they wove, or the design they finished, or the pattern they paid for in story) is the first thing in their cave they did not receive. The class key's second movement: from how you arrived to what you make.

And the three gifts hold: the streets are lit by memory, the Long Dark is survived by silence, and the question — *what will you carry when the stones are gone* — has begun answering itself. The player is carrying something now. They made it.
