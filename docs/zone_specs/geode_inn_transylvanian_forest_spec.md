## WHAT THE ZONE IS
The Inn's Transylvania is NOT Dracula's. THE ANTI-SUPERSTITION SHOWCASE: the vampire is Hollywood (1897, a novel by an Irishman who never visited); the FOREST is real, and the real thing is better. The Carpathians hold EUROPE'S LAST GREAT WILDERNESS  the largest brown bear population on the continent, the virgin beech forests that were never logged, the karst caves under the ridge. The zone's thesis: the deepest dark wood in Europe is not a horror set. It is the most alive place left.

**FREQUENCY:** The owl's screech  the striga. Moonlight through canopy; the ruin on the ridge (a real one, historical, not Stoker's); Rackham's twisted-forest energy at the edges (the Fae's east cousin).
**THE NIGHT LAYER:** the forest reveals its full self only at night. The day is beautiful. The night is TRUE. (The night map: the moonlit paths, the eye-shine in the dark  the bears', the wolves', the lynx's. Play straight: the dark here is not danger. The dark is PRIVACY  the wilderness's own.)

## THE SCIENCE (the lesson)
- THE VIRGIN FORESTS: primeval beech stands, unlogged since the last ice age's retreat  Europe's original forest survives here (the living archive: the trees are the Catacombs' cousins  history that stayed standing).
- SCARIȘOARA: the ice cave that has held winter for 3,500 years  the glacier underground (the ice grows one layer a year, a ring like a tree's, a climate record in frozen water  the ice REMEMBERS. The zone's geologic memory stone is made of water).
- THE MYTH OR THE MAN: Vlad the Impaler vs Count Dracula  the prince who defended a border (the history) and the novel that borrowed his name 400 years later (the fiction). The zone teaches the difference as a sorting game  the anti-superstition law as gameplay.
