## WHAT BILL IS

In the hierarchy of the Geode Inn, creatures are understood through mythological lineage or mineral frequency. The Fae Kingdom boasts noble Seelie lords, frost-forged Unseelie knights, ancient Ents, and soaring Griffins. The Archive classifies artifacts through Mohs hardness, chemical composition, and acoustic resonance.

Then there is Bill. Bill is a platypus.

Bill sits in the Crystal Gardens — the neutral sanctuary zone linking the Seelie and Unseelie territories — dipping a broad leather bill into singing-bowl crystal pools and looking at passersby with mild, unbothered curiosity.

The core thesis: **Reality does not need mythology to be strange. Reality IS mythology.** Science and myth are not opposing truths — they are the same language spoken at different frequencies. Bill is the proof.
