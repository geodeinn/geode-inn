# THE MYCORRHIZAL NETWORK — Master Specification
## The Inn's Root-Level Trade and Communication System

---

## WHAT THE MYCORRHIZAL NETWORK IS

The Mycorrhizal Network is the Inn's deepest trade system — a living network of fungal threads (mycelium) connecting every zone at the root level. Where the Artisan Market is surface trade (walking, talking, exchanging goods face-to-face) and the Silk Road is cultural trade (caravan routes along ley lines), the Mycorrhizal Network is ROOT trade. The system that runs beneath everything, connecting zones through the earth itself.

In the real world, mycorrhizal networks (the "Wood Wide Web") allow trees to share nutrients, send chemical warnings, and support each other through underground fungal connections. The Inn's version is the magical equivalent: Fae creatures can send messages, trade stones, and share frequencies through the network without ever leaving their zones.

The network's thesis: the growing is slower. The growing is more honest. You can't rush a tree. You send. You wait. It arrives. The waiting is the trust.

---

## HOW IT WORKS

### Form Sight
The mycelium is invisible unless the player has Form Sight — a perception mode unlocked through the Fae Kingdom questline. In Form Sight, the mycelium appears as a glowing white network beneath the ground, pulsing at 42Hz, connecting every zone's root system.

### Sending
The player can SEND through the network by placing an item on any mycelium node (visible in Form Sight as a glowing white point on the ground). The item is absorbed into the network and appears at a DIFFERENT node — one the player has visited before.

What can be sent:
- **Stones** — arrive with a slight 42Hz harmonic added to their natural frequency, creating richer sonic properties in the Bead Composer
- **Messages** — short text in the tabletop voice, appears as text on a leaf at the destination node. NPCs can read and respond
- **Frequencies** — played on an instrument, travels the threads and plays at the destination — a root-level serenade

### Receiving
NPCs send items through the network too. The player who has a mycelium node near their favorite nook will occasionally find items left there. Receiving is not instant — items travel at the speed of GROWTH (a few real hours). The waiting is part of the system. The mycorrhizal network doesn't rush. The network is a tree, and trees don't rush.

### Thread Thickness
The mycelium is ALIVE — it grows and responds to the player's activity. When the player visits a new zone, the mycelium extends a thin thread to that zone. The more the player visits and SITS (the Nook Effect), the THICKER the thread. Sitting in nooks IS connection — the nook system and the network are the SAME system. The player who sits is the player who connects. The player who rushes gets thin threads and slow delivery. The player who sits gets thick threads and fast delivery.

A Fae creature made of white threads, barely visible — the network's caretaker. Voice: whispered, from below, the sound of roots growing. Speaks from the ground, not the air. The voice comes UP through the soil.

Core dialogue: "...The network connects. ...That's all it does. ...Stone to stone. Root to root. Zone to zone. ...The surface trades by walking. ## QUESTS

### Quest 1: "The Root Connection"
- **Giver:** The Mycelium Tender (Fae Kingdom)
- **Prerequisite:** Fae Kingdom access + Form Sight + 5+ zones explored
- **Objective:** Visit 5 zones, sit in a nook for 5 real minutes in each. Activate Form Sight and watch the thread thicken from thin white line to thick, pulsing 42Hz cable
- **Reward:** The Root Key — permanent access to the FAST LANE. Items arrive in minutes instead of hours. The network rewards the player who SITS.

### Quest 2: "The Dead Root"
- **Giver:** The Mycelium Tender
- **Prerequisite:** "The Root Connection" complete + Undercity access
- **Objective:** Go to the Undercity's Bone Room. Find the broken mycelium thread. Place a stone with frequency above 500Hz on the break point. The stone's frequency bridges the dead root.
- **Reward:** The Bone Keeper's gift — a fossil stone vibrating at 40Hz (the Rememberers' frequency, from the Western Interior Seaway era). Creates a cross-zone connection between the Undercity's dead and the Appalachian Range's deepest layer.
