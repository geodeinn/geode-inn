# THE IMPORTANT TIER QUESTS — Master Specification
## Da Vinci's Staircase, The Cartographer's Form Sight, The Observatory, The Norns & The Weaver

---

## WHAT THE IMPORTANT TIER QUESTS ARE

Four high-priority quest chains that form the narrative spine of the mid-to-late game. These are the quests that teach the player the three perception modes (Form Sight, Silica Thread Overlay, Mother Mode) and unlock the Observatory — the gateway to the endgame.

---

## QUEST CHAIN 1: DA VINCI'S STAIRCASE — "The Mirror Script"

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Location | Staircase, mid-level (between Albedo and Rubedo) |
| Giver | Da Vinci |
| Prerequisite | 30+ stones + Whisper Layer Tier 2 |
| Quests | 5 (The Mirror Script, The Root Blueprint, The Wall Blueprint, The Window Blueprint, The Door Blueprint) |

Da Vinci is the Inn's architect of perception. The mirror script is not backwards writing — it's FORWARDS writing seen from the other side. Da Vinci writes the Inn's blueprints from the FREQUENCY'S perspective. From the Song's perspective. Not from the builder's, not from the traveler's.

Mona is the 42Hz mother creation principle, humming in the poplar. Mona doesn't speak. Mona HUMS. The hum is the 42Hz carrier wave. The staircase's ascending semitone (each step a half-step higher) is built ON TOP of Mona's hum. Mona is the root note. The staircase climbs from Mona.

**Mechanic: Reflection Reading.** The player approaches mirror-script panels and must find a reflective surface (the poplar panel, a stone facet, the Crone's measuring tape) and ANGLE it so the reflection is legible. Each panel reveals one architectural truth.

---

## QUEST CHAIN 2: THE CARTOGRAPHER'S FORM SIGHT — "The Essential Geometry"

The Cartographer teaches Form Sight — the ability to see the essential geometry of every zone. Strip away the detail. See the shape. The canyon is a spiral. The colonnade is six cylinders. The Inn is a tree.

---

## QUEST CHAIN 3: THE OBSERVATORY QUEST — "The Question"

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Location | Cosmology Level, the Observatory |
| Prerequisite | All three perception modes (Form Sight + Silica Overlay + Mother Mode) |
| Quests | 6 (The six-quest chain) |

The Observatory is where the player asks "The Question" — the question that the Inn exists to answer. The six quests build to the moment of full perception: Triple Vision. The player sees the Inn as it actually is — form, frequency, and meaning simultaneously.

---

## QUEST CHAIN 4: THE NORNS & THE WEAVER — "The Threads"

The Norns (Urd, Verdandi, Skuld — past, present, future) sit at the base of the Inn-Tree, weaving the threads of every life. The Weaver is the loom. The threads are the lives. The player's quest: to understand that the thread is not a path — it is a VIBRATION. The Norns don't weave destiny. They weave FREQUENCY.
