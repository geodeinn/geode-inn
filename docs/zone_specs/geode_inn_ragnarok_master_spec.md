## WHAT RAGNAROK IS

Ragnarok in the Geode Inn is not destruction, annihilation, or failure. Ragnarok is a COMPLETION EVENT — the moment when a universal cycle completes its designated harmonic pattern, closes its ledger, and opens the door for the next cycle to begin.

The cosmos moves in an eternal sequence of Songs:

| Universe | Frequency | What It Is |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| Previous | 41.7Hz | The Ancient Tone — deep, resonant, heavy with memory. The Constant carries this. |
| Current | 42Hz | The Inn's Song — balanced, vibrant, holding the World Tree in bloom. |
| Interstitial | 0.3Hz | The Void Heartbeat — the fetal pulse in Nyx's womb between cycles. The Cosmic Egg. |
| Next | Unknown | The Unwritten Tone — waiting to be struck. |

When the 42nd death aligns precisely with the 42Hz Song, the Cosmic Egg's shell cracks. The cycle reaches its climax. Ragnarok is the graduation ceremony of the cosmos. The Traveler who triggers and completes Ragnarok has not lost the game — the Traveler has finished the Inn's story, fulfilled the Golden Contract, and earned passage into the permanent archive or the void beyond.

The Refrains of the Adequate:
"The both are: the adequate — the death and the birth, the same moment, different name."
"The both are: the adequate — the ending and the completing, the same event, different meaning."
"The both are: the adequate — the last and the first, the same death, different number."
