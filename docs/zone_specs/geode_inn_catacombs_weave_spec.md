# THE CATACOMBS WEAVE  the underground's dwelling geography
## Krista's call (2026-09-11): "Let's weave our catacombs into the underground system, they are scattered around along with the mausoleums"

---

## THE LAW (the catacombs are a geography, not a level)

The Catacombs have always been the Inn's downstairs  the home entrance. **THE WEAVE: the downstairs is the HOME, and the underground axis holds the world's catacombs and mausoleums wherever the real geography puts them  SCATTERED, like the passage mounds and the resonance chambers.** The dead get the squiggly line's full treatment: their dwelling geography spans the three axes (surface mausoleums that open down, underground corridors, the water tombs). The player descends from the Inn's downstairs into the ONE SYSTEM, and finds the family scattered across the world beneath the world map's feet.

## THE THREE REGISTERS (the dwelling layer's family tree)

1. **THE PASSAGE MOUNDS  the small houses.** The oldest register: Newgrange and the little Newgranges. Communal, ancient, light-boxed. The passage mound is the dwelling layer's SEED  what the oldest ancestors built when "a house for the dead" was a hill and a passage and a stone that catches one dawn a year. (Already specced.)
2. **THE CATACOMBS  the shared roads.** The communal corridors: the dead's streets, walkable, the shared address. Rome's catacombs (the roads beneath the roads), the Paris ossuary (the ARRANGED  the femur walls, the bones as architecture, the dead stacked into pattern by the living who stayed to tend them), the world's ossuaries wherever the soft ground asked for them. The catacomb is the passage mound's URBAN cousin: the same communal instinct, scaled to a city's worth of neighbors.
3. **THE MAUSOLEUMS  the grand houses.** The singular dwellings: the surface doors that open down. Halicarnassus (the Wonder that NAMED the whole register  Mausolus's tomb), the Taj Mahal (the India region's white marble heart), the pyramids (Egypt's surface doors), the Serapeum (the bulls' granite vaults  the Man-Made-Is-the-Miracle law's exhibit), Qin's mound (the Terracotta Army's house  see THE UNOPENED DOOR). The mausoleum is the loudest SOMEONE WAS HERE the archive keeps  the Ajivika shelf's law family, built in stone instead of held in gaps: one person, honored past death, the address never expiring.

**THE MEETING LAW (the Bone Room's law, extended to the whole weave):** at every dwelling, the dead are MET, not cleared. "You're not cleaning it. You're meeting it." applies from the smallest passage mound to the grandest mausoleum. The dead are neighbors, not props.

## THE ACOUSTIC SPLIT (the resonance wire's other half)

The resonance chambers SING; the dwelling layer LISTENS. **The Silence Law (from the Cave City's mausoleum district, canon): the dead damp ALL sound  the second place in the Inn without the 50 BPM heartbeat (the Redwood Cathedral was the first).** So the underground axis now holds its own acoustic spectrum: the resonance roads (the catacombs' corridors carry the hum  the dead's streets are the underground's resonance roads, sound traveling the way it travels in real catacomb tunnels) and the silent rooms (the mausoleums, where the hum stops at the threshold and the heartbeat is gone). THE DESIGN MEANING: the player who has learned the chambers' tones walks the catacombs and hears the building's voice carried on the dead's roads  and steps into a mausoleum and the building goes quiet, out of respect. The both are: the adequate. (GATE: the silence's edge  does the heartbeat fade at the threshold or stop? The gentle fade is the Steady Light Law's cousin.)

## THE UNOPENED DOOR (the weave's Valhalla cousin  Qin's mound)

The Terracotta Army is already canon (the garrison; Persephone's winter station in the pit). The garrison guards a door that has never been opened: the emperor's actual mound, unexcavated in the real world (the mercury rivers in the old texts are real  the soil reads toxic; the archaeologists who test it back off). **THE INN RENDERS THE MOUND UNOPENED  the Valhalla door law family: the thing honored by staying closed.** The player sees the door, reads the mercury, and the choice is made FOR everyone: the dead keep their address, the garrison keeps its watch, the mound stays whole. The Inn's answer to the whole "what's inside" genre: the keeping is the seeing. (The Man-Made Is the Miracle law's respectful silence.)
