# THE WORLD GRID — Master Specification
## Open-World Exploration + Passive Gathering System

---

## WHAT THE WORLD GRID IS

The World Grid is the explorable open space SURROUNDING the Inn — the terrain between cultural zones. Darnassus-style (WoW) beautiful explorable world with ambient activities. No combat (in safe zones). No pressure. No timers. The player explores at their own pace, gathering resources that feed back into the Inn's crafting systems.

The most relaxing space in the game. 42Hz plays softly. Ley lines glow in the distance. The world is full of stones, plants, and water features. The World Grid is the Inn's OUT — the space the player enters when they leave the building and walk the Earth.

**The Core Loop:** OUT (travel grid, gather along ley lines) → IN (return to Inn, craft) → OUT (charge stones on ley lines) → IN (use charged items in games/quests/compositions). The gathering is the "out." The crafting is the "in." The World Grid makes "out" a place, not a menu.

## GATHERING SYSTEM

### Stone Gathering
Stones appear in the World Grid as ROCKS — visible on the ground, in cliff faces, in riverbeds. The player mines them (pickaxe, same tool used in the Catacombs' The Deep). Different regions yield different stones (the region's "Primary Stones" column above). Stones from the World Grid are UNCHARGED (0% charge) and must be carried along ley lines to charge.

Stone spawning:
- Common stones respawn every 24 hours (real time)
- Rare stones respawn every 7 days
- Legendary stones are unique (one per region, one per character) — they don't respawn
- Mining at a ley line node yields pre-charged stones (50-75% charge)

### Plant Gathering
Plants are gathered for crafting supplies (food, charms, trade goods, alchemical ingredients). The player uses a sickle (crafted in the Undercity) or bare hands (slower, loses some material). Plants are region-specific and respawn on timers matching real-world growing seasons (bamboo respawns fast, lotus respawns in cycles, cloudberries respawn slowly).

### Water Features
Water in the World Grid is functional:
- Drinking water restores endurance (same stat as the Silk Road caravan)
- River water carries 42Hz from ley line nodes — swimming in a ley-line river charges stones in the pouch at 1% per 10 seconds
- Still water at a ley line node charges stones at 3% per 10 seconds (the stone is immersed in the frequency)
- The Outer Ring ocean is the Hollow Earth's ceiling — doesn't charge stones (the frequency is too deep)

## SAFE ZONES AND WILD ZONES

Not all of the World Grid is safe.

| Zone Type | Behavior | Where |
|-----------|----------|-------|
| **Safe Zone** | No combat. No enemies. Pure exploration and gathering. 42Hz ambient. | Within 2 "zones" of any cultural platform (a radius around each entry point) |
| **Wild Zone** | Encounters possible. Animal encounters (not monsters — wolves, snakes, scorpions, big cats, region-appropriate). Player can flee or harmonize (resonance combat — the animal becomes a companion if harmonized). | Beyond the safe radius of any platform |
| **The Threshold** | The border between regions. Where one culture's aesthetic meets another's. Gargoyles guard the thresholds (they switch between zones' styles). The passable points between regions are gargoyle-marked. | The borders between the 9 regions |
