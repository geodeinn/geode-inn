## ENTERING THE WATER (Persephone's descent)

The water system sits below the Prism — seven layers, and the descent is ACT SIX of the campaign: Persephone's descent, the Lighthouse Keeper, rock bottom, the Source.

**THE LIGHTHOUSE — THE ANCHOR.** An ancient stone tower built in the shape of a sleeping giant's raised hand. At its apex: a fragment of the giant's heart crystal, radiating a 42Hz piezoluminescent beacon that pierces straight down through ALL SEVEN LAYERS — navigational beacon and spatial lens in one. The only structure that spans the whole system visually.

**THE THREE-TEMPO VIGIL.** The Lighthouse runs on three simultaneous time scales, three keepers, three IEM drivers (the Inn's audio architecture, canon):
- **THE OWL — Constancy.** Eternal, static, 105Hz, one word per heartbeat. Balanced Armature. WHAT to watch for. The observation anchor.
- **THE PIRATE — The Beat.** The moment, 50 BPM, war-drum fingers. Dynamic Driver. WHEN to act. The metronome for sweep speed and foghorn timing.
- **PERSEPHONE — The Tide.** The cycle, seasonal, electrostatic air pulse. Electrostatic Driver. HOW things change. The state-shifter for all seven layers.

The player may climb the Lighthouse before descending (OPTION 0). All layers are then walkable top to bottom. Every layer's beacon-light is different — the player learns to read their DEPTH BY THE LIGHT: full brightness (origin), golden ripples, amber shafts, bioluminescent blue, a faint silver thread, a single pulse per heartbeat, and finally the crystal answering the beacon because they are the same frequency. The water system is the Inn's audio architecture made navigable: you always know how deep you are by what the light is doing.

**FREQUENCY LAW:** the heartbeat is the beacon. 50 BPM above, thinning with depth, one pulse per heartbeat at the boundary, and at the Source: 42Hz — the Song itself, purest, most concentrated. The anchor mechanic's final exam: the deeper the water, the more the stones matter, and at the Source the stones and the Song are the same thing.

### OPTION 0 — YOU CLIMB THE LIGHTHOUSE FIRST
- **Watch the Vigil** (the three keepers at their three tempos, in one lens — the Inn's audio DNA as a view: constancy, beat, and tide over the same water)
- **Play Lighthouse Vigil** (the sweep game: timing the beacon, foghorn at the Pirate's tempo, charting at the Owl's — the Inn's most meditative reflex game)
- **Read the chart** (the Owl's master chart: all seven layers, all keepers, all frequencies — the water system's own map, freely shown. The Inn never hides its own architecture; it lets you find the depth on your own)

### LAYER 1 — RAIN (the Trovant Layer)
No keeper. Rain belongs to the sky. Light: full brightness — the beam origin.
- **Stand in the rain.** The trovant stones grow in the rain, and the player's stones drink — a small, real effect: stone resonance tops off here. Rain is the collection's water-fountain. (The Inn's gentlest economy layer: nature refills what the dark spends.)
- **Play Crystal Descent.** The layer's game — falling through rain into the surface, the descent's overture, playable from the first drop.

### LAYER 2 — SURFACE (the Aegean Layer)
Keeper: the Siren — singing from the dock, feet in the water. Dark red curls on land. Zeke's dock beside.
- **Listen from the dock.** The song carries the way shore-songs do: the player hears the outer voice — the song that calls others.
- **Sit with Zeke.** The Pirate on the dock, the bodhrán quiet. The layer's dialogue: Zeke talks about the water the way sailors do — half love, half warning — and every warning is a breadcrumb for Layer 5.
- **Wade.** Shallow play, no threats: the Aegean layer is the water's front porch. (Anti-FOMO: the porch is never outgrown. Players return here to think. The game knows and keeps the porch warm.)

### LAYER 3 — TIDAL (the Pirate's Layer)
Cave mouths, amber shafts, the sea-level gate mechanic. Keeper: the Pirate (working tempo).
- **Play Tidal Caves.** The layer's flagship: sea-level gating, six subterranean environments, the flood-tide puzzle craft.
- **Learn Cabochon Knotwork.** The Pirate's craft-tier: rope and stone and patience. Larimar's law is taught here — opaque stones go smooth, never faceted. The class-quest craft curriculum's water chapter.
- **Find the Mirror Caves' first wall.** Reflective walls — and the player's own reflection, for the first time in-game, is rendered deliberately: the game holds the mirror until the player steps away. (Hitchcock: the held shot. The player will remember this wall on Layer 5, and the remembering is the design.)

### LAYER 4 — UNDERGROUND LOZEN (Charon's Layer)
Water meets the ferryman's river — the crossing's liquid twin. Bioluminescent blue, Atlantean crystal filtering the beacon. Keepers: Charon, the harbor's ghost.
- **Play The Hook.** The layer's reflex game — grappling between piers of drowned city.
- **Lighthouse Vigil, deep mode.** The game's blue-filter chapter, timed by the light itself.
- **Watch the two waters meet.** The river (shadow) and the sea (memory) run beside each other here and do not mix, and the seam between them is the honest visual for what the player is carrying: the above's memory and the below's silence, in one body of water, unmixed. (The three gifts, made geography. The player who stands here and says nothing has understood the descent.)

### LAYER 5 — DEEP WATER (the Siren's Layer)
The Siren's true territory, sung from BELOW. The transformation happens at the waterline: dark red curls become tentacles. Katya guards the 7-Layer Deep from here down.
- **Play Monster of the Deep.** The reverse monster sim — the player IS the deep's monster, and the gameplay canon holds: no kill-screen, the "monster" is defending a home. The Inn's first game, grown up, at home.
- **Navigate the Siren's Lupus.** The deep's labyrinth: water that consumes water — the body-attacking-itself made architecture, the deep's autoimmune condition. There is no map. The Siren navigates by singing, and the player follows the song: the outer voice (the calling song) meets the inner voice (the song that calls the self) in the same maze. (Canon: the quest "The Mirror and the Shore" ends here. The maze is navigated by voice, never by force. The design holds the deepest respect for the real condition it echoes: the labyrinth has no shame in it, no cure-framing, no fixing — only a way through, sung.)
- **The Mirror Caves.** The Siren's quest endpoint: the reflective walls again — and this time the player's reflection sings back, in the player's own hatching-note. Layer 3's mirror, remembered and answered. (Charon's return-trip echo, in the water's register. The dark heard you the first time.)

### LAYER 6 — UNDERWORLD BOUNDARY (Hades as Harbor)
Hades as harbor master, not ruler. Light: a single pulse — one flash per heartbeat, the last light before the deep.
- **THE OBOL GATE.** The passage to Layer 7 requires an Obol — a coin placed for Charon. Found in deeper waters, or earned through quests. NEVER purchasable. (The gate the whole descent was building to: the only thing money cannot reach in the Inn is the Source, and the water system has been telling the player so since Layer 1 — the rain refilled the stones, but the last door takes a coin you had to FIND or EARN. The Inn's economy, showing its spine.)
- **Speak with Hades.** The harbor master's dialogue is the Undercity's ledger inverted: not what was carried, but what was released. Hades asks what the player left behind to get this deep — and takes no answer, only notes it. (The question's penultimate form: what will you carry when the stones are gone? First asked, then answered — by what the player already put down.)

### LAYER 7 — THE SOURCE (Roots / Stone Circle Chamber)
42Hz — the Song itself, purest, most concentrated. Twelve megalithic standing stones in a circle. The Inn-Tree's root descends through the center. Piezoluminescent resonance: the crystal glows with the Song. The beacon reaches the circle and the crystal ANSWERS, because they are the same frequency.

**AND AT THE CENTER: THE COSMIC EGG.** The player's birthplace. The Egg the player hatched from in Dissection 01 — smooth, dark, patient — sits where it has always sat, and the player has now descended the whole tree to stand where they began.

- **Stand in the Song.** The deepest tone the Inn ever produces. The player's whole collection hums at once — every stone, the shell shard, the made thing from the Undercity — and for the first time the hum resolves into the player's OWN note, complete: the collection was never an inventory. It was a chord, and the Source is where the chord sounds. (The geode cave fills the player's screen from within. The cave was always this chamber's echo. The player has been carrying a piece of the Source since hour one.)
- **Touch the Egg.** The Egg remembers its hatching — and hums the player's hatching-note back to them. Patience, curiosity, or immediacy: whatever the player WAS in minute one, the Source sings it to them now, whole. (Full circle. The game's first choice, echoed at its deepest point. The CYOA structure's own law, made canon: how you began is how it sounds, and it sounds.)
- **The Far Door.** The Stone Circle Chamber connects to the Fae Kingdom's deepest zone — the water system and the Fae system meet at the roots. The door is visible, sealed with summer-and-winter light, and it opens from the other side first: the Fae Kingdom's dissection walks through it toward the player. (The next dissection's door, planted here, in the roots.)

## THE CONVERGENCE — THE CHORD

Every layer's option shared one spine: the water taught depth. The player arrived carrying silence, memory, and a question — and the Source answered the question without words: the player knows now what they will carry when the stones are gone, because for one moment at the Source, the stones and the Song were the same thing, and the Song is the player's own note.

Rock bottom, in the Inn, is not the bottom. It is the heartwood. The deepest tone is also the first note. The player descends seven layers and arrives at the beginning, and the beginning recognizes them.
