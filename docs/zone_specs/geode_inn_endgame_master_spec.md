## WHAT THE ENDGAME IS

The culmination of the entire Geode Inn narrative — the moment where every thread the player has followed converges into three choices. This spec consolidates: the endgame three choices, the Egg, the Ragnarok graduation scene, the gap scenes (narrative gaps identified in the backwards walk), and the thread mapping that tracks every narrative thread.

---

## THE THREE CHOICES

At the Ascension Canopy, the traveler plays the note. The note determines the ending.

| Choice | What the Player Plays | What Happens |
|--------|----------------------|--------------|
| STAY | 42Hz — the complete Song | Plays the Song, takes the crystal, becomes the new Constant. The Inn continues with the player as its heart. |
| LEAVE | Silence | The stones complete one by one. The player descends the staircase in reverse (Rubedo→Albedo→Nigredo). Walks through the front door into morning. The Inn continues without the player. |
| HIDE | 42Hz, then silence — a gap | Plays the Song, then a silence the Inn can't record. The Nest is built inside the Song — a life that exists in the space between frequencies. The Inn continues, but doesn't know it carries something unrecorded. |

The thread that connects all three: "The Inn continues. The ravens fly. The adequate is enough."

## THE EGG

The Egg is the nest the builder made for the traveler. Not a quest reward — the reason the Inn exists. The Egg is the creative engine: a 28-day community lifecycle where players vote, hatch characters, and retired characters migrate to the comic strip. The Egg IS the Inn's reproductive system — new content emerges from community participation.

---

## RAGNAROK: THE GRADUATION

Ragnarok is not the end of the world — it's the graduation. The 5-phase cycle-ending event:

1. **The Fimbulwinter** — the long cold. The 42Hz fades. The Inn goes grey.
2. **The Breaking** — the walls crack. The Shadow Inn and the Living Inn merge.
3. **The Battle** — Einherjar raid companions fight alongside the player against 3 bosses.
4. **The Rebirth** — the 42Hz returns. New. Different. The Inn rebuilds itself.
5. **The Graduation** — the player who triggered Ragnarok walks to the Ascension Canopy for the Three Choices.
