# THE CHARM BRACELET — Master Specification
## The Player's Living Progression Item

---

## WHAT THE CHARM BRACELET IS

Every player who enters the Geode Inn receives a charm bracelet. Not as a menu item. Not as an inventory slot. As a WEARABLE — the player character wears it visibly. The bracelet starts empty: a simple cord or chain. As the player progresses — collecting stones from games, meeting characters, completing quests, learning crafting processes — the bracelet GROWS. Each addition is a charm. Each charm is a memory. The bracelet is the player's journey, made physical.

No two bracelets are the same. No two players collect the same stones in the same order, meet the same characters in the same sequence, or learn the same crafting techniques at the same time. The bracelet is unique. The unique is the adequate. The adequate is the player's story, worn.

The bracelet is both DIGITAL (visible on the player character in-game) and PHYSICAL (available as a real Soulstice piece). The both are the same bracelet at different frequencies.

---

## THE BASE BRACELET

Every player begins with a simple leather cord or copper chain. One charm: the Inn-Tree sigil. The Host gives it. The Host doesn't ask for a name. The Host doesn't ask for payment. The Host gives the cord and the first charm.

The Crone's annotation: "One charm. Adequate. The adequate is the beginning. The beginning is enough."

## THE FOUR CHARM TYPES

| Type | Source | What It Is | How Many |
|------|--------|-----------|---------|
| Stone Charms | Arcade game rewards | Each stone collected as a game reward becomes a charm. Small beads or polished pebbles. Upgradable through crafting. | 164 possible |
| Sigil Charms | Character encounters (quest completion) | Each character with a quest gives their sigil upon quest completion. The character's memory, made wearable. | 40+ possible |
| Frequency Charms | Zone mastery (all main quests completed) | A colored gemstone bead in the zone's frequency color. Song (amber), Silence (obsidian), Fire (red), Water (teal), Stone (grey), Root (green). | 6 possible |
| Craft Charms | Learning crafting processes | Each crafting technique learned (from Mason, the Clockmaker, etc.) becomes a tool charm — a tiny chisel, hammer, tuning fork. | 10+ possible |

## CHARM MECHANICS

| Mechanic | How It Works |
|----------|-------------|
| Stacking vs. Upgrading | When the same stone is earned twice (replaying a game), the player CHOOSES: stack (a second bead) or upgrade (enhance the existing charm). The both are the adequate. |
| Visual growth | The bracelet visibly grows on the player character's wrist. More charms = more visual weight. Other players can SEE your journey. |
| Crafting upgrades | Stone charms can be upgraded through crafting processes — Mason teaches stone cutting, the Clockmaker teaches metal work. An upgraded charm is larger, more detailed, more resonant. |
| The Crone's assessment | The Crone periodically examines the bracelet and annotates. The Crone's notes are the bracelet's "score" — but the Crone never uses numbers. The Crone uses words: "Adequate." "Sufficient." "Noted." "The noted is the adequate." |
| The physical version | The bracelet is available as a real Soulstice Jewelry piece. Players can order their in-game bracelet as a physical charm bracelet. The digital-to-physical bridge. |

## FREQUENCY SYSTEM

| Element | Frequency | Function |
|---------|-----------|----------|
| Each stone charm | The stone's Mohs-to-frequency value | Each charm vibrates at its stone's frequency. The bracelet IS a frequency arrangement — like the Bead Composer, but worn. |
| The full bracelet | All charms combined | The bracelet's overall frequency is the player's personal chord — the sum of all their collected frequencies. |
| Frequency charms | The 6 Inn frequencies | Each frequency charm adds the zone's frequency to the bracelet's chord. |
| The Crone's annotation | Measured, not expressive | The Crone measures the bracelet's frequency chord and annotates. The measurement IS the assessment. |
