# THE LOST AND FOUND  THE GOBLIN-ING
**Zone family:** the in-between spaces (the hallways, the landings, the Threshold, the Between, the neither-world's streets)
**Status:** spec drafted 2026-09-11 (Krista's ruling: comic relatability layer for the in-between)
**Laws inherited:** the Whimsical-Macabre Law (the macabre played warm), the Adequacy Law (some things are just there), the No-Boo Law (nothing jumps at you), the Steady Light family (no timers, no streak-guilt)

## ONE. THE LOST AND FOUND

Somewhere along the in-between  the hallway that isn't quite a room, the landing that belongs to no level  there is a counter. Above the counter, a sign that has been repainted so many times the letters wobble: LOST AND FOUND. It is staffed always. It is quiet mostly. And it is the funniest room in the building.

THE PREMISE: things that fall between the levels land here. The in-between is where the dropped goes  the sock that vanished mid-walk, the earring back, the page that slipped out of a book on the stairs, the melody someone hummed and lost the thread of. The Lost and Found keeps them all. It is the neither-world's pocket, turned out.

THE SHELVES (the comic catalog):
- FOOTWEAR, SINGULAR. Mostly socks. Always just one. The goblins have theories about where the other ones go. The theories contradict each other. All are stated with total confidence.
- SMALL METAL. Keys, buckles, coins that aren't currency, the little screw that turned out to be load-bearing. The goblins can tell you which door a key fits, but they will be wrong with such conviction that the door sometimes agrees.
- LOST THOUGHTS. The thing you were about to say. Held in small labeled jars, which the goblins neither explain nor sell. If you find yours, you may keep it. Most people don't recognize theirs.
- LEFT ON PURPOSE. A small, respected shelf. Things people left here because they didn't want to find them again  the ring, the letter, the little brass weight that says one word on the bottom. The goblins never ask. This shelf is never haggled over. The Adequacy family: some things are here to be here.
- THE UNCLAIMED RESONANCE. Hummed melodies, half-remembered lullabies, fragments of the Song someone caught and couldn't hold. These are KEPT, not sold  the goblin ears can hear which ones are almost a tone, and the almost-tones are held until the hummer comes back for them. (If a player hums back a fragment they lost, it logs to the songbook. The goblins act unsurprised. They saw it coming. They see everything coming.)

THE MECHANIC (gentle, No-Quantize family): searching is free and unhurried  no timer, no fail state, no meter. Sometimes you find what you lost. Sometimes you find what someone ELSE lost, and the choice to keep it or leave it for them is the quiet moral beat (the goblins watch this choice and remember it; they say nothing; they always say nothing, pointedly). Sometimes you find what you didn't know you were missing  the jar with your lost thought in it, and it might be from years ago.

## TWO. THE GOBLIN-ING (the duties)

Krista's ruling: make sure our goblins are properly doing their goblin-ing. Goblin-ing is defined as: the practical hands of the in-between. The neither-world is neither grim nor grand; it is STAFFED. The goblins are the staff. (The Undercity runs on the dead employed; the hallways run on goblins employed.)

THE GOBLIN CREW (and their proper goblin-ing):
- THE FINDERS. The Lost and Found's staff. They collect, catalog, haggle, and pontificate. The catalog system is its own philosophy: nothing is filed by what it IS, everything is filed by what it was DOING when it got lost. (The sock was WALKING. The key was GOING SOMEWHERE. The thought was ALMOST SAID.) This filing system should be useless; it is somehow how they always know where things are. Nobody knows why it works. It works because goblins believe in it.
- EL MUKI (already canon, Peru): the veins' keeper  the giving law. Proper goblin-ing: the worker, not the monster. The Muki's goblin-ing is MINE-SHAPED: duties, offerings, the shine taken from the greedy.
- THE GOBLIN MERCHANT (already canon, Fae Threshold): sly grin, apron of many pockets. Proper goblin-ing: the trade. The Merchant's pockets are the Threshold's second lost and found  things end up in them; the Merchant neither confirms nor denies.
- THE STEAM GOBLIN (shadow bestiary): the mechanical goblin-ing  tending the pipes and pressures of the in-between's steam-work. Its shift is whenever it says its shift is.
- THE UNSEELIE GOBLIN COURT (Malachi's): the goblin-ing of the winter half  formal, mischievous, precise. The Court's goblin-ing is CEREMONY: even the pranks are scheduled.

THE COMIC LAW (named for this spec): goblins are funny because they are completely correct in a way nobody can argue with. The goblin logic loop is the Inn's oldest joke shape: impeccable reasoning, unimpeachable sincerity, wrong answer, correct somehow. (The Mensinthe family of jokes: you can't argue with it. You can only pay for the socks.)

## TWO-AND-A-HALF. THE FOUNDING POPULATION (Krista's correction, 2026-09-11)

The Labyrinth goblins were written in WAY BACK  under the Jareth era, before the rename to Malachi (the Unseelie King, the goblin king; Bowie/Labyrinth survives as craft/casting reference only). THE LABYRINTH GOBLINS ARE THE FOUNDING POPULATION: every goblin in the Inn descends from the crowd around the castle  the hallway crew, the Merchant's apron, the Muki's mine, the Steam Goblin's pipes, the Court's scheduled pranks. ONE FAMILY OF GOBLINS, MANY GOBLIN-INGS: the Lost and Found's Finders are the Labyrinth goblins on hallway shift. They were hanging around the labyrinth before the Inn had a main floor; the Inn grew up around them. (The goblins remember when the hallways were labyrinth. Some of the hallways still are, on their say-so.)

## THREE. THE RELATABILITY LAYER (Krista's ruling)

The in-between spaces get the comic beat so that the neither-world's warmth lands as LAUGHTER, not just gentleness. Every in-between space should carry at least one goblin-kept beat:
- a counter, a shelf, a sign repainted too many times
- a goblin mid-shift with an opinion about the level it sits between
- the sense that the whole liminal architecture is somebody's JOB, and somebody is doing it with pride

The in-between stops being empty when it is staffed.

## GATES  ALL RULED (Elio's rulings, delegated by Krista, 2026-09-11)

1. **THE HEAD FINDER  the title IS the name.** (The titled-NPC pattern: the Archivist, the Gardener, the Bone Keeper.) The bonus is the census pun: the Head Finder is the Inn's head counter  the one who finds heads, keeps the head count, and knows exactly when the goblins have run off and exactly when they haven't. Born mid-census, named by the census.
2. **THE JAR LAW: your jar opens; nobody else's.** Reading your own Lost Thought is allowed  that's the shelf's whole mercy (finding what you didn't know you were missing). Opening another's jar is the one theft the goblins never commit  the LEFT ON PURPOSE shelf's sibling law. Enforcement: they watch. They say nothing. They always say nothing, pointedly.
3. **THE UNCLAIMED RESONANCE: YES  it logs.** When the hummer returns and hums their fragment back, it logs to the songbook as EchoMelody ("reclaimed_hum"). The shelf holds the tone until its owner comes for it  the Finders are unsurprised; they saw it coming. (The Adequacy family: unclaimed hums never expire.)
