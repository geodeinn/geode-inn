## WHAT THE EXTERNAL ZONE SYSTEM IS

The External Zone System is the Inn's connection to the real world. The Inn is the HUB (interior, permanent, home). The World Map is the INTERFACE between the Inn and the earth. The National Parks are the EXPEDITION ZONES — real-world locations where the player goes to collect stones, discover lore, and experience the geology and mythology the Inn is built from.

The dynamic is fundamental: **The player goes OUT to collect. The player returns IN to create.**

The Inn processes what the Parks provide:
- Stones collected in parks → the dragon's geode (Main Floor)
- Lore discovered in parks → the archive (Catacombs) + Modern Legends shelf (Main Floor)
- Minerals found in parks → the Bead Board (Undercity)
- Geological knowledge → Charles's Natural Archive (Catacombs)
- Cultural knowledge → the Library Nook (Main Floor)

The National Parks are the Inn's REAL-WORLD ANCHORS. The player who learns about Yellowstone's obsidian in the game can visit Yellowstone and see real obsidian. Real geology, real mythology, real geography, through play.

## THE EXPEDITION DYNAMIC

| Element | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Visitor Center | Entry point. Park ranger NPC. Information, quest board, gift shop. |
| Trails | Paths through the park. Each trail leads to collection points and lore sites. Difficulty ratings (easy walk → strenuous hike). |
| Collection Points | Geological sites where stones can be found: outcrops, creek beds, mines, eroded surfaces. Each yields specific stones. |
| Lore Sites | Cultural sites: petroglyphs, ruins, sacred sites, historical markers. Each reveals regional mythology. |
| Seasonal Cycle | Park changes with seasons. Some stones only accessible in certain seasons (winter ice reveals outcrops, spring melt exposes creek beds). |
| Weather System | Rain = slippery (stamina drains faster). Heat = timer shortens. Snow = trails close. |
| Expedition Timer | Natural end: sunset, supplies running out, weather changing. Creates urgency — can't collect everything in one visit. |
