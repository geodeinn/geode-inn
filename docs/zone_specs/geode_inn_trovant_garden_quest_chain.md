## WHAT THIS CHAIN IS

The Trovant Garden is the Inn's patience made visible — the zone where the fastest thing that happens takes a thousand years, and the player is invited to match it. The real geology is the curriculum (the teaching law): trovants are REAL — the growing stones of Costesti, Romania, sandstone concretions that accrete mineral rings from rainwater, ~4-5 centimeters per thousand years, with tree-like rings inside that record every earthquake and every rain. The science is the lesson: stone is not still. Stone is slow. The story is the delivery: some stones are not even slow — they are creatures at two speeds, dry and rain.

The chain's thesis, carried by the recovered doc: the trovant is not a transformation — it's a REVELATION. The stone was always the creature. The naming wakes it. The music doesn't build wings; the water listens, and the mineral deposits are the score made permanent. And underneath it all, the zone holds the Inn's smallest homecoming: Grit — the living note who has been getting stuck in everything for years, always looking for something that can ANSWER — meeting the stone that answers back.

**Giver:** STANCA (the Ring-Reader — see recommendations; an original face, the Costesti keeper's echo: the stone-keeper whose family watched the growing fields for generations and knows every trovant by its rings).
**Access:** The Romania/Transylvania forest; the census pin z32.
**Prerequisites:** None — the garden accepts anyone patient enough to arrive.
**Reward spine:** the waiting → the naming → the playing → the seeing → the choosing → the answering.

## THE CHAIN

### Quest 1 — "The Stones That Wait"
Arrival in the garden: the trovant field after rain — spheres, mushrooms, fused aggregates, all of them subtly wrong for stone (too smooth, too placed, too ALIVE), all of them completely still. Stanca's first lesson is the zone's whole law: "Do not watch for the movement. Watch what moved." The player walks the field and learns to read the ground — the trails behind stones, the dried ring-residue, the place where a trovant was yesterday and is not today. Then the naming: Stanca gives the player the choice of an unnamed trovant, and the player names it. Nothing visibly happens. Then, on the walk back, the named stone is FOLLOWING — twenty feet back, then fifteen, patient as geology. The naming didn't transform it. The naming woke it. (The naming law, the Inn's oldest magic: Lozen's river, Artair's bear, and now the garden.)
**Reward:** The name (the player's first named trovant — a companion slot in the garden; the stone follows when it rains).

### Quest 2 — "Grit Comes Home"
The chain's centerpiece scene, played exactly as the recovered doc wrote it: Grit — the four-inch ball of fur, the living note — becomes stuck to the trovant's surface, and the brownies mobilize (Brindle's siege operation, Cobble's calculations, Thatch's stillness). And then the turn: Grit isn't struggling. Grit is HUMMING. The place where Grit sticks is the place the Song is strongest on the stone — and that's where the wings should form. The Keeper, passing: "The note found the instrument. Let it play." The player learns the template: play the water toward where Grit sits.
**Reward:** Grit's resonance map (the living tuning fork — with Grit placed on the trovant, the game's true mode unlocks: the shapes more organic, the Flower more willing, the attention the difficulty).

### Quest 3 — "Composing the Wings"
The music game proper: the player guides rain across the trovant's surface with played notes. LOW notes — wide slow ripples, heavy deposit, mass and earthbound density. HIGH notes — tight delicate filigree, glass-thin and unable to carry stone. RHYTHM — steady hands make clean sacred geometry; noise makes noise. The player is composing wings, and the mineral deposits are the score made permanent. And between the last rain and the first wing: THE CHRYSALIS — the trovant seals in its own mineral shell, the player cannot play, the water works where no one can see. The silence is the transformation happening where no one can look. (The Void's rest, the Bone Room's patience, and the Grail's stillness — the same discipline, taught by a stone.)
**Reward:** The first wing bud (the Citrinitas stage — the patterns extending outward, catching light).

### Quest 4 — "The Flower in the Periphery"
The hidden game surfaces: every ripple the player ever played has been radiating outward, and circle has been intersecting circle in the water NOBODY was watching. The Examiner arrives, and the Examiner looks at the water, not the stone: "The pattern is complete." The FLOWER OF LIFE is drying into the earth around the trovant — the residue visible only from above, where the Astronomer, at the Observatory, sees fields of Flowers of Life in the Transylvanian forest and knows: someone is playing well down there. The lesson the zone never says out loud: the important thing was happening where you weren't looking. You can't make the Flower directly. You can only make it by doing something else beautifully.
**Reward:** The Flower's residue (a permanent Flower of Life at the player's garden site — and the Astronomer's recognition: the Observatory notes the player's field).

### Quest 5 — "The Rain's Children"
A long rain multiplies the field (the Gizmo rule — but propagation, not corruption): the parent splits the way a cell divides, and the rain also wakes the smaller trovants — the children, rounder, smoother, fewer rings. The player can only guide one transformation at a time; the others grow on their own, at their own patient thousand-year pace. The choice: which child to nurture — the choice is the adequate. And the chain reveals the deepest gift: the companion's RINGS now record the PLAYER's journey — each zone chain the player completes grows one new ring in the named trovant, the geological biography written in the player's own adventures. The stone keeps the player's history the way the garden keeps the earth's.
**Reward:** The second companion (a child trovant, unnamed — the player may name it, or leave it to grow wild; both are the adequate) + the RING LAW (companions' rings now record the player's completions, visible when the Examiner reads them).

### Quest 6 — "The Stone That Answered"
The finale, and the homecoming. The wings complete — Rubedo: crystalline, symmetrical, catching light like Tiffany glass, tuning forks of mineral. Grit moves to the wingtips and hums. And the trovant HUMS BACK. The brownies freeze — they have never heard the Song answer Grit before. Thatch's eyes fill. Brindle lowers the crystal-shard dagger. Cobble stops calculating. Somewhere else in the Inn, the Keeper smiles and doesn't know why. The 42Hz passes through the wing structures and the stone lifts — not flying, RESONATING — and the player who completes the Flower while the wings sing achieves the Philosopher's Stone stage: the geometry overriding the sculpting, the Song correcting the artist, the stone butterfly moving between zones.
**Reward:** THE ANSWERING (the player's named trovant may now travel — the companion that follows across zones at its own patient pace, arriving a few minutes late, always exactly where it said it would be) + the Chrysalis Shard (the shed mineral shell of the transformation, ringed with the trovant's whole history — the zone's craftable charm, reading like a biography) + Grit's note (Grit may ride the wingtips of any transformed trovant — the living note and its instrument, together).
