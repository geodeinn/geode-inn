# THE LONG NIGHT — Master Specification
## A Tri-Mode Gothic-Anime Survival Game (Vampire Survivors Adaptation)

---

## WHAT THE LONG NIGHT IS

The Geode Inn's adaptation of Vampire Survivors — a 30-minute survival game where the player faces relentless waves of Shadow Inn creatures. Cabinet IX in the East Wing Arcade Den.

In the world beyond the Inn, games tell tales of surviving against endless vampire hordes until the clock strikes thirty. In Nyx's domain, night is not a curse — it is a FREQUENCY. When the sun sinks below the mountain and the Inn's roots shift into the 84Hz Shadow State, the creatures of the deep catacombs, the dark waters, and the Fae wilds emerge to test the player's resonance.

The player does not wield ordinary steel. The player wields stones, the pulse of the house, and the voices of its dwellers.

---

## THE TRI-MODE GAMEPLAY

| Mode | Frequency | What Happens |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| Living | 42Hz | Harmonic equilibrium. Enemies slow 60%. Puzzle nodes manifest. Visual blooms into Klimt gold ornamentation. |
| Shadow | 84Hz | The surge. Enemy waves accelerate. Aggressiveness doubles. Projectile-dense combat. Art sharpens to Gothic Anime (Vampire Hunter D aesthetic). |
| In-Between | Hybrid | The player shifts between 42Hz and 84Hz via the Pulse Line. Tactical shifts turn overwhelming combat into puzzle-based energy harvests. |

---

## THE PULSE LINE

Running across the bottom of the screen — a thin, glowing amber ECG waveform:
- 84Hz: violent, sharp, jagged red-amber peaks (intense combat)
- 42Hz: smooth, rhythmic sinusoidal wave (enemy deceleration, puzzle nodes)
- 7.83Hz (dawn): deep golden, tranquil earth-heartbeat hum

The Pulse Line provides haptic, audio, and visual feedback — the Inn's triple-modality neuro-accessibility standard.
