# THE GEODE INN — Portal Registry (Master Spec)
## The official placement of every portal, door, and threshold in the Inn
### Created 2026-09-10. Status: ACTIVE — no portal exists in canon without a row here.

---

## PORTAL LAW (the standing principles)

1. **No zone is an island.** Every zone has a door in and a door out. (Core structure law)
2. **Portals are never menu toggles.** Travel is a narrative event — you walk through a real thing, in a real place. The interface is the place itself; you click a door to go through it. No fast travel except through actual doors.
3. **Every door hums its destination.** A portal's threshold carries the frequency signature of where it leads: the Charcoal Door hums at 84Hz before you know why. Players learn to hear where a door goes before they open it.
4. **Every portal has a key.** A stone, a game won, an act of kindness, a duration of stillness. Nothing opens because the plot needs it to.
5. **The Portal Registry is law.** A portal without a row here does not exist. A row without a PLACEMENT does not ship.

## REGISTRY NOTES

- The Stonehenge portal is the PILOT PORTAL — the Standing Stones game was the first game built and the first door keyed. Every portal design decision should reference it.
- The Void Door is the only door keyed by NOT DOING. It is the Inn's exception, on purpose.
- The Grey Door is the only door that hides from purpose. Every other door can be sought; the In-Between can only be found.
- The Door Home (Between) is the only door that requires someone else's need. The Inn's kindness engine, in one threshold.
