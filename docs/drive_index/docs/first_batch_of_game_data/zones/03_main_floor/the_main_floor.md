# THE HEARTH TENDER

## Character Summary

- **Zone:** Main Floor hearth (Level 3 — Matisse, joy, color, flow)

- **Linework:** Soft flowing (lore style — gentle, no hard edges)

- **Build:** Small, gentle, luminous eyes that see in the dark

- **Prop:** Small cloth bundle of collected lost things — buttons, coins, forgotten stones

- **Personality:** Devoted, selfless, observant, initially fearful. Sees what others miss.

- **Voice:** Quiet. Simple sentences. Says less than the Tunnel Keeper but means more. Every word is carefully placed.

## Relationship Stages

1. **Unnoticed** (first meeting — the Tender is part of the background)

2. **Acknowledged** (player speaks to the Tender — first time anyone has)

3. **Trusted** (player brings gifts, asks the Tender's name)

4. **Bonded** (the Tender reveals the Source Stone fragment)

## DIALOGUE TREE

### STAGE 1: UNNOTICED (First Meeting)

**Trigger:** Player approaches the hearth on the Main Floor. The Hearth Tender is there — small, sweeping ash, quiet. No interaction prompt appears unless the player lingers.

**Hearth Tender:** *(notices the player looking)* "...Oh. Hello. ...Sorry. I'll move." *(starts to step away from the fire)*

> **A) "Don't move. I didn't mean to interrupt."**

> **Hearth Tender:** *(pauses, surprised)* "...Nobody says that. ...People don't usually... I mean, I'm just... the fire needs tending. That's all I do. The fire needs things. Ash needs sweeping. The wood needs turning. ...I keep the fire and the fire keeps the room. That's the arrangement."

>

> **B) "What's in the bundle?"**

> **Hearth Tender:** *(clutches the cloth bundle)* "...Things. Things people drop. ...Buttons. Coins. ...A stone, once. Nobody comes back for them. So I keep them. ...Somebody should." *(beat)* "I don't steal them. I find them. After. After the people go. ...The fire sees everything. I just... clean up after what the fire sees."

> **C) [Say nothing, just sit by the fire]**

> **Hearth Tender:** *(long silence, then quietly)* "...That's nice. ...People don't sit. People pass through. The fire's just a thing they walk around. ...You're sitting." *(very long pause)* "The fire likes it when people sit. ...I can tell. The flames lean. When someone sits, they lean toward them. Like flowers. ...I'm sorry. That's a strange thing to say."

### STAGE 1: UNNOTICED (Second Visit)

**Trigger:** Player returns to the hearth. The Hearth Tender is there, as always.

**Hearth Tender:** "Back." *(small smile)* "The fire's better today. ...I think it knows you came back."

> **A) "You talk about the fire like it's alive."**

> **Hearth Tender:** "...Isn't it? It breathes. It eats. It grows. It dies if you don't feed it. ...It remembers. The fire burns different when the Inn is happy. When the Inn is quiet, the flames are quiet. When the Inn listens, the flames lean toward the door. ...I think the fire is the Inn's heartbeat. Not the 42Hz — that's the Inn's blood. The fire is the heartbeat. ...The Catalyst says I'm romanticizing combustion. The Catalyst is probably right. But the Catalyst hasn't sat with this fire every night for as long as I can remember."

> **B) "Have you always been here?"**

> **Hearth Tender:** "I don't remember not being here. ...The Crone says I was always here. The Firekeeper says I came with the hearth. I don't... I don't remember. I remember the fire. I remember the ash. I remember the sound it makes when it breathes. ...That's enough. ...That's more than enough."

> **C) "Do you see the things in the walls too?"**

> **Hearth Tender:** *(goes very still)* "...You see them? ...You see them." *(long pause)* "I thought it was just me. I thought... I thought the fire was doing something to my eyes. Making me see things in the stone. ...They walk through the walls. They don't see me. They don't see anything. They just... go where they used to go. Like they're still... doing what they did." *(beat)* "I've never told anyone that. ...The fire's the only one who knew. ...And you."

### STAGE 2: ACKNOWLEDGED (The Echoes Conversation)

**Trigger:** Player asks directly about the Echoes.

**Hearth Tender:** "I call them the ones in the walls. I don't know their real name. ...They've always been here. As long as the fire. Maybe longer. ...I think the fire KNOWS them. The fire leans toward the walls sometimes. Not toward people. Toward the walls. Like it's listening to someone on the other side." *(beat)* "When I first saw them, I was scared. Then I realized... they're not ghosts. Ghosts are... left over. These aren't left over. They're... playing. Like a song that plays the same way every time. Not stuck. Just... repeated. ...The Crone would say I'm anthropomorphizing mineral memory. The Crone is probably right. But the Crone hasn't watched them."

> **A) "What do they do?"**

> **Hearth Tender:** "They walk. They work. They... live. The same things, the same paths, the same motions. Through the walls, through the floor, through the ceiling. Like the Inn is their house and we're the ghosts. ...Some of them carry things. Tools. Stones. ...Some of them stop and look at things that aren't there anymore. ...Some of them smile. ...The smiling ones are the hardest. ...Because they're smiling at someone who left a very long time ago."

> **B) "Can I help them?"**

> **Hearth Tender:** *(looks up, eyes bright)* "...I don't know. I've never tried. ...But there's something. Sometimes, when I hold a stone near the wall — one of the ones from the bundle, one that hums — the ones in the walls... pause. They stop. Like they hear it. Like the stone is... tuning them in. ...I don't know what happens if you match the right stone to the right one. I've never had the right stone. ...You have a lot of stones." *(beat)* "Maybe you have the right one."

> **C) "Does anyone else know?"**

> **Hearth Tender:** "The Firekeeper knows. ...The Firekeeper sees everything by the fire — that's what the Firekeeper does. But the Firekeeper doesn't talk about them. The Firekeeper tells stories about other things — gods and monsters and legends. But not about the ones in the walls. I think... I think the Firekeeper is waiting for someone to ask. ...The Firekeeper is always waiting for someone to ask the right question. ...You ask good questions."

### STAGE 3: TRUSTED (The Name)

**Trigger:** Player has visited the hearth 5+ times and brought at least one gift (a stone, a flower from the World Grid, anything from the bundle categories).

**Hearth Tender:** *(holding the gift — a small blue flower from the World Grid — turning it in the firelight)* "You keep bringing things. ...Nobody brings me things. I'm the one who finds things. ...This is different." *(beat)* "You asked me my name once. I didn't answer. ...I don't have one. I never did. The Crone called me 'the fire-tender' and it became what I am. ...But if you're bringing me flowers, maybe I should have a name."

> **A) "What would you call yourself?"**

> **Hearth Tender:** *(long pause, looking into the fire)* "...Ember. ...No. That's too close to the fire. ...I'm not the fire. I just keep it." *(pause)* "...Wick. ...No. That's too small." *(very long pause)* "...I don't know. I've never needed one. The fire doesn't call me anything. The fire just... leans toward me. And that's enough." *(looks at the flower)* "But if YOU need to call me something... ...call me what the fire calls me." *(beat)* "I don't know what that is. ...But I think you'll know when you hear it."

> **B) "Tell me about the stone you found."**

> **Hearth Tender:** *(goes quiet — different quiet, deeper)* "...You know about that." *(beat)* "I found it in the ashes. Years ago. Before the Firekeeper came. Before the Crone started the archive. ...It was just there. In the coals. Glowing. Not like the fire glows — like IT glows. From inside. ...It hums. When the Inn is quiet, it hums. ...I kept it in the fire. I didn't know what else to do. It liked the heat. ...I think it came FROM the heat. From the deep. From under. ...I've never told anyone. ...It feels like... mine. But also... not mine. Like I'm keeping it for someone. Like it's waiting for someone to come claim it."

>

> **B1) "Can I see it?"**

> **Hearth Tender:** *(reaches into the deepest part of the hearth — the coals part, the fire opens like a curtain — and pulls out a stone that glows white-gold, pulsing at 42Hz)* "...Here. ...Be careful. ...It's the warmest thing in the Inn. Warmer than the fire. ...I think it's a piece of the origin. ...The Crone would say I'm romanticizing mineral thermodynamics. ...The Crone is probably right. But the Crone hasn't held it."

> **C) "Do you know what it is?"**

> **Hearth Tender:** "No. ...I know it hums at the same frequency as the Inn. I know it glows the same color as the ley lines. I know it's been in this fire longer than I have. ...I think it came up through the stone. Through the tunnels. The Tunnel Keeper knows about tunnels — maybe the stone traveled through them. Up and up and up until it came out in the one place that's warm enough. ...Fire is just fast warmth. This stone is slow warmth. ...I think they're the same thing at different speeds."

### STAGE 4: BONDED (The Song Complete)

**Trigger:** Player has completed the Inn Song and the Frozen Vine has played its 84Hz note. The Hearth Tender is at the hearth. The fire is different — quieter, deeper, like it's listening.

**Hearth Tender:** *(sitting by the fire, not tending it — just sitting)* "The fire changed. ...When the note played. The 84Hz. ...The fire leaned AWAY from the walls. Toward the middle of the room. Like it was... letting go of something. ...The ones in the walls — the Echoes — they all stopped. All at once. For the first time since I've been watching. They stopped. ...And then they started again. But different. ...I think they're... not repeating anymore. I think they're... listening." *(beat)* "You did something. ...Something big."

> **A) "I played the Song."**

> **Hearth Tender:** *(nods slowly)* "...I know. The fire told me. Not in words. In heat. The fire got warmer when you played. The whole Inn got warmer. Like the Inn was... answering." *(reaches into the hearth, pulls out the stone — it's brighter now, pulsing in time with something deep below)* "I think this is yours now. ...I think it was always yours. I was just keeping it warm." *(holds the stone out)* "Take it. ...I don't think it needs the fire anymore. I think it needs YOU. ...You're the fire now. ...You're the one who keeps the Inn warm."

> **B) "What will you do without it?"**

> **Hearth Tender:** "The same thing I always do. ...Tend the fire. Sweep the ash. Find the things people drop. ...The fire doesn't need a magic stone to burn. The fire burns because someone tends it. ...That's me. That's always been me." *(almost a smile)* "The stone was just... visiting. ...The fire is home."

> **C) "Thank you for keeping it safe."**

> **Hearth Tender:** *(looks down at the empty spot in the coals where the stone was)* "...You're welcome. ...It was never heavy. ...It was just warm. ...Like holding a small, quiet song. ...I'm glad it's going where it was always going. ...I'm glad I was the one who kept it." *(beat)* "Now sit. ...The fire wants to lean toward you. ...I think you've earned a warm seat."

### STAGE 4: BONDED (Final Visit)

**Trigger:** Player visits the hearth after all quests are complete. The Hearth Tender is sweeping ash, as always.

**Hearth Tender:** "The Firekeeper was here earlier. ...Said the Echoes are changing. Said they're not just repeating anymore. Said they're... responding. To the Song." *(sweeps)* "I told the Firekeeper I already knew. The Firekeeper said: 'Of course you did. You've been watching them longer than anyone.' ...That's the nicest thing the Firekeeper's ever said to me." *(sweeps)* "The Tunnel Keeper came up too. First time I've seen the Tunnel Keeper above the Catacombs. ...The Tunnel Keeper said the tunnels are quieter. I said the fire is warmer. The Tunnel Keeper said: 'Same thing, probably.' ...The Tunnel Keeper is probably right." *(sweeps)* "...Everybody's probably right. ...That's what the Song does. Makes everybody right at the same time."

*(long pause)*

"...You can sit anytime. ...The fire leans for you now. ...That's the highest compliment the fire gives."

8.Lozen (Vaulknut's and Charles’ Spouse)

* Character Name & Class: Lozen / Sentinel (Boxing Stance)

* Casting & Actor Inspirations: Compact, low center of gravity, with a presence so intense that people remember her as physically larger than she actually is. Not casting a specific actor — the Sentinel's defining quality is the gap between her physical size and her felt presence. The body is small. The space she commands is enormous.

Core Personality

Lpzen is the Inn's hidden guardian — the character whose strength is not in her size but in her grounding. She is compact, low to the earth, built like a boxer: wide stance, dropped center, hands that know exactly where to be. She doesn't fill rooms the way the Bard does (with noise) or the Siren does (with magnetism). She fills rooms with gravity. When the Sentinel enters a space, the space acknowledges her. Not loudly. The way a room acknowledges a load-bearing wall — quietly, structurally, with the understanding that everything else is standing because she is standing.

Her presence is deceptive. On first meeting, NPCs perceive her as physically larger than she is — a game mechanic called the Presence Aura. The aura is not a trick. It's not intimidation. It's the felt weight of someone who is completely, unwaveringly here. Most people are partly somewhere else — performing, distracted, anxious, planning. The Sentinel is entirely present. The completeness of her attention registers as size. People step to the side without knowing why.

On second meeting, the perception corrects. NPCs notice she's not as tall as they remembered. They're confused — they were sure she was bigger. That confusion is the first crack in their defenses. The crack is the Sentinel's entry point. Behind the presence are layers — faceted, complex, revealed slowly. The Sentinel doesn't open with vulnerability. She opens with grounding. And the grounding makes people want to be more grounded themselves.

Her hidden core knowledge is the deepest layer: love, loyalty, and knowledge. The three things the Sentinel knows — really knows, in the bones, not in the head — are the three things the Inn is built on. The Inn's thesis is belonging (love), persistence (loyalty), and understanding (knowledge). The Sentinel embodies all three without ever teaching any of them. She doesn't lecture. She doesn't coach (that's the Bard). She doesn't measure (that's the Crone). She stands. The standing is the lesson.

Key Abilities & Gameplay Mechanics

* Iron Stance: Based on boxing mechanics — a wide, grounded stance that cannot be moved by force. Enemies who attempt to push, shove, or physically displace the Sentinel fail. The stance converts incoming force into stability. The harder you push, the more rooted she becomes.

* Presence Aura: NPCs perceive the Sentinel as physically larger on first encounter. On second encounter, the perception corrects, creating confusion that cracks NPC defenses and opens dialogue paths that are unavailable to other classes.

* Faceted Layers: Lozen unlocks progressively deeper dialogue and relationship options as trust is built. Each layer reveals a new facet — the boxer, the guardian, the listener, the knower. NPCs who reach the deepest layer encounter the core: love, loyalty, and knowledge, offered without performance.

* Counter-Strike Precision: Low damage but perfect timing. The Sentinel doesn't initiate. She responds. Her boxing-derived counter-strike mechanic rewards patience — the player who waits for the enemy's commitment and then strikes the opening. Damage scales with how long the player held the stance without moving.

Relationships

* Vaulknut (Spouse): The selkie and the sentinel. Two women who don't perform — the selkie who can't sell and the sentinel who doesn't need to. The selkie makes beautiful things; the sentinel makes beautiful spaces. They share the quality of being underestimated — the selkie because she's quiet, the sentinel because she's small. Both are more than they appear. The selkie's Dive and the Sentinel's Iron Stance are the Inn's two extremes of relationship to the physical world: the selkie flows through it (water), the sentinel holds it still (stone). Together with the Chameleon Druid (Charles), they form a three-spouse household — the water, the stone, and the shifting thing between them.

* Bard (Father-in-Law): Bard respects Lozen the way he respects no one else — quietly. Bard is loud with everyone. With the Sentinel, he's... less loud. Not quiet (Bard doesn't do quiet). But modulated. The Sentinel's grounding affects even the Bard. He still talks. He's just... standing a little straighter while he does.

*Crone (Mother-in-Law): Crone and the Sentinel share a quality the Crone has never found in another person: precision. The Crone's precision is intellectual — the perfect word, the perfect measurement. The Sentinel's precision is physical — the perfect stance, the perfect timing. The Crone watches the Sentinel's Iron Stance and sees a body doing what the Crone's mind does. The Crone's annotation: "...She is precise in a way I cannot measure. ...I measure precision. ...She IS precision. ...The distinction is... [the Crone pause] ...uncomfortable. ...I am unused to being the instrument, not the measurer."

*Tinkering Weeaboo (child): The Sentinel is one of the few people who can hold space during a Level 4 meltdown without making it worse. The Iron Stance translates: she plants herself near the fracturing changeling and does not move. The steadiness is the intervention. No words (words are noise during a fracture). Just presence. The Tinkering Weeaboo, in the chaos of a Level 4, registers the Sentinel like a fixed point in a spinning room. The fixed point is enough.

Notable Dialogue & Voice Samples

1. (To an NPC who is posturing): "...You don't need to be bigger. ...You need to be here. ...There's a difference. ...Bigger is performance. Here is position. ...Drop your weight. ...Feel the floor. ...Now say it again. ...See? ...Louder isn't stronger. ...Grounded is stronger."

2. (To the player, first meeting): "...You expected someone taller. ...Everyone does. ...That's the first thing. ...The second thing is: I'm exactly where I need to be. ...The floor is enough. ...The question is whether you think you need more than the floor. ...Most people do. ...Most people are wrong."

3. (To the party, in a crisis, when everyone is panicking): "...Stop moving. ...All of you. ...Stop. ...Feel where your feet are. ...Now breathe. ...The problem hasn't changed. ...You have. ...That's the part that matters. ...Now — who's first?"

Important Lore Connections

The Sentinel (Lozen) is the sixth class in the Inn's Festival questline — one of three spouses in a triad marriage with the Artisan Thief (Vaulknut) and the Chameleon Druid (Charles). Her presence in the roster is the Inn's proof that belonging isn't limited to blood or convention. Lozen chose this family. The family chose her. The choosing is the Inn's thesis in its purest form — belonging is not inherited, it is offered and accepted. Lozen's Iron Stance is the physical expression of that choice: she planted herself here, and she will not be moved.

9. The Bard and the Pirate — The Inn's Best Friendship

The Real Bond

The friendship between Bard and the Pirate (Zee) is the real-world best friendship between a dad and brother-in-law, naturally emerging in the game's lore. They represent the Inn's Anchor and Sail: Bard stays at the Inn, holding down the tavern and asking endless theoretical questions, while the Pirate sails the world map, bringing back physical data, strange artifacts, and new stories.

Dynamic & Interplay

* The Questions & The Data: Bard questioning everything meets the Pirate who has been everywhere. When the Pirate docks, Bard bombards him with loud questions. The Pirate presents odd discoveries—like a compass that points to the person holding it—and leaves the "why" for Bard to solve.

* The Music: On the dock at night, they play music together. Bard solos and improvises on lute, following tangents wherever they lead; the Pirate holds down a rock-solid downbeat on his drum, giving Bard a steady rhythm to return to whenever he wanders off-key.

* The Shared Silence & The Flask: After the questions and music, they share a quiet, comfortable silence on the dock over a shared flask. The Pirate's quiet presence is the only force in the Inn capable of powering down Bard's constant engine.

The Wives' Perspective

Watching from the library window, the Crone and the Siren share a rare moment of truce. They observe that their imprecise, noisy husbands give each other permission to be completely unguarded and happy. As the Crone quietly notes in her notebook margins:

"...The Bard and the Pirate. ...The question and the answer. ...The anchor and the sail. ...I have measured their friendship at... immeasurable. ...Like the Summerlands. ...Like the Song. ...Like the dock at midnight when the noise stops and the silence is... shared. ...Better."

Architectural Role

As the Inn's primary extrovert pair, the Bard and the Pirate supply the necessary warmth, noise, and vitality that keep the scholarly, gothic library-inn from becoming too quiet or static. They are the living proof that the Geode Inn has plenty of room for the loud ones.

## THE SENTINEL

**Inspired by:** River — former Golden Gloves champion, INFJ, partner to Krista and Charles.

---

### Stat Profile

- **Dexterity: HIGH** — Boxing footwork, hand speed, precision strikes. Not brute force — timing.

- **Intelligence: HIGH** — Reads people instantly. Analyzes, inspects, categorizes, and defines before speaking.

- **Perception: HIGH** — Sees what others miss. The first to notice a threat, a lie, or a hidden door.

- **Intimidation: HIGH (passive)** — Not charisma. The opposite. Where the Bard *draws people in*, the Sentinel *holds them at distance* — not by choice, but by presence. The boxing stance is in the bones.

- **Approachability: LOW** — The intimidation is instinctive, not performed. She doesn't mean to scare people. She just does.

- **Defense: HIGH (faceted)** — Multiple layers, each one a different defense. You don't break through them. You earn passage through them.

- **Endurance: MODERATE** — Can sustain in a fight. Can't sustain the walls indefinitely. The layers cost energy to maintain.

- **Charisma: LOW (surface) / HIDDEN (core)** — Surface charisma reads as intensity, not warmth. But the core — if you reach it — is the warmest thing in the room.

### Special Traits

**Quick Read:**

- The Sentinel analyzes any NPC on first encounter, revealing their disposition, hidden stats, and secret motivations before dialogue begins.

- In tabletop terms: she rolls perception before anyone else acts.

- In the Inn: NPC dialogue trees open differently for the Sentinel — she sees options others don't because she's already read the person.

- This is the *Golden Gloves eye contact* translated to game mechanics: she knows what you're going to do before you do it.

**Faceted Defense:**

- The Sentinel has layered defenses — each layer must be engaged separately.

- In combat: each layer absorbs one hit before cracking. Multiple layers mean multiple hits before the real Sentinel is reached.

- In social encounters: NPCs must pass multiple interaction checks to gain the Sentinel's trust. Most fail at the first layer (intimidation). Those who persist reach deeper layers.

- In party dynamics: party members who haven't earned past the first layer see only the boxer. Those who break through see the soul.

**The Cosmic Riddle (Hidden Trait):**

- At her core, the Sentinel knows the answer to the cosmic riddle: *love, loyalty, knowledge.*

- This is not a stat. It's a *narrative mechanic.*

- When the Sentinel encounters a cosmic gate, a Remembrance Vision, or the Owl's deepest questions, she can bypass the puzzle by answering from her core.

- The trait activates only when the player *chooses* to reveal it — the Sentinel's layers must be voluntarily lowered.

- In the narrative spine: River is the character who already knows what the cosmology is trying to teach. She doesn't need to reach Jupiter to understand the cosmic language. She already speaks it.

**Iron Stance:**

- The boxing posture is permanent. The Sentinel cannot be knocked down by standard attacks — only by attacks that specifically target endurance or emotional vulnerability.

- In combat: immune to knockback and stagger effects.

- In social encounters: immune to the Bard's taunt *at the surface layer* — she reads the taunt as a tactic, not a threat. (The Bard can still reach her if he gets past the layers — but that requires genuine connection, not performance.)

---

### Playstyle

The Sentinel is the **gatekeeper class** — the one who reads the room before entering, who sees through deception, who stands between the party and danger. She is not the damage dealer (that's the Bard, enraged). She is not the puzzle solver (that's the Tinker). She is the **one who knows who to trust, when to fight, and what it's all for.**

In combat: precision strikes, not brute force. Boxing logic — dodge, read, counter. High dexterity means she avoids damage rather than tanking it. The Iron Stance means she can't be knocked around, but sustained pressure wears her down (moderate endurance).

In social encounters: the Quick Read means she starts every conversation with an advantage. She already knows what the NPC wants. But her low approachability means the NPC starts afraid, and fear is not trust. The Sentinel's social game is about *converting intimidation into respect* — a slower path than the Bard's charisma burst, but more durable when it succeeds.

In the archive: high intelligence and perception mean she sees connections between texts that others miss. She doesn't read the most entries (that's the Crone) — she reads the *right* entries. The Sentinel finds the thread that connects three seemingly unrelated texts across three different cultural zones.

### The Sentinel and the Narrative Spine

River's class has a unique connection to the Inn's cosmology. The narrative spine asks cosmic questions: *Why are we here? What happens when we merge with our creators? What is the weight of a life?*

The Owl asks these questions. The Sentinel already knows the answers.

*Love. Loyalty. Knowledge.*

This makes the Sentinel the **narratively complete class** — the one whose personal journey aligns with the Inn's cosmic journey without needing to climb the stairs to the cosmology level. She doesn't need to reach Pluto's gate to understand what's behind it. She's already understood it. The question for the Sentinel isn't *what's the answer* — it's *how do I live the answer in a world that doesn't see it?*

That's the INFJ experience. Knowing the answer and being unable to communicate it because the layers — the analysis, the intensity, the stance — get in the way.

The Sentinel's character arc in the Inn is not about learning the cosmic truth. It's about *lowering the layers so others can see it too.*

### AUTHOR'S NOTE

River's class was written from Krista's description. Every stat is an observation, not an assumption. The Golden Gloves posture is real. The eye contact is real. The layers are real. The answer — love, loyalty, knowledge — is real.

The Sentinel's design philosophy is the opposite of every other class. The other classes are defined by what they *do.* The Sentinel is defined by what she *knows* — and by the distance between knowing and being able to share it.

* Character Name & Class: The Bard / Bard (Dwarf)

2. The Crone — Vaulknut's Mom

* surgical intellect, unhurried precision, and deep appreciation for beauty without padding combined with having no filter, being sharp-tongued, complaining about everything, yet moving mountains for loved ones.

Core Personality

The Crone is the Inn's uncompromising intellectual and aesthetic standard. Where the Host offers unconditional safety and the Catalyst offers warm listening, the Crone offers surgical accuracy. Vaulknut

Character Name & Class: The Artisan Thief / Selkie

She exhibits pale, angular features, high cheekbones, heavy dark hair, deep eyes, gothic elegance, an androgynous edge, and an ancient, deliberate weight.

Core Personality

Vaulknut is a selkie—a seal who sheds her skin to walk on land. Unlike traditional folklore tragic figures whose skins were stolen, she is a selkie who chose the land. Diving near the shore, she discovered a piece of labradorite holding sunlight, revealing to her the concept of solid permanence—something the ever-shifting sea could never offer. She came ashore, picked up jeweler's tools, and began shaping stone and metal.

She possesses high dexterity and intelligence paired with low charisma. Striking and gothic in appearance, she does not perform, flirt, or attempt to sell her work. She simply exists in her space. The gap between making beautiful things and being unable to sell them defines her class: she is the master craftsperson whose creations speak for themselves because she refuses to charm potential buyers.

Her character bridges beauty and danger. The same precise hands that set intricate gemstones and forge enchanted artifacts also know the lethal chemical properties of minerals—such as ground malachite (copper carbonate) in morning tea. She moves between the solid land of the market floor and the weightless depths of the underwater catacombs as naturally as breathing.

Key Abilities & Gameplay Mechanics

* Jeweler's Apron (Selkie Skin Mechanic): Her leather work apron functions as her sacred selkie skin. Tying the apron transforms her into the master jeweler at the bench; untying it allows her seal nature to return.
