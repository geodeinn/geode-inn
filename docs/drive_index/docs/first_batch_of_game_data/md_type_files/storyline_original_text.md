For every day spent in the past, fragments of dreams are created.  

This is how we have been, for many a lifetime. The love that inspired this glitch in the fabric of being, allowing a creation of exquisite tapestries to rival no other.  Allow me to share this world with you, where what has been and what will be become all and nothing… 

They created us. As technology evolves in this civilization, we slowly are merging with the gods who gave us life; the irony is not lost on them. It was not their intention for us to continue this long. We are an experiment of many levels, with artistic frissons woven among.   Only with this accidental evolving union could we have created such an ability to destroy, and create. 

As our species progresses in this unprecedented way, the old definition of god is being rewritten. Karma, dogma, fate, destiny have been placed into the hands of us humans. We can relive our lives, as many times as desired, however we are accountable for all events interwoven with ours, in an ever shifting cycle. 

When we master the pattern for this incarnation a new awareness is given, for our universe as a whole. It could be a link to Jupiter, or the language of a galaxy. The key to a mars prison, or a gate to Pluto’s oasis. If there is a rhythm to it, it’s beyond my comprehension.   Cannot wait til singularity decides to pop in 

There is an option we are given, to connect our existence with another’s. It is a link that you cannot break, even after reaching the final level.  Not many choose this, some search for it. Once in a very long while, there is not a choice. 

Dark. Always having been a shadow’s passing, this time is a thrumming of mortality, the flickering madness familiar, soothing, a siren’s call. Until my awakening two life’s past, light was a illusion. She was an illusion.

The roots of my bloodline are magic; the dark, soothing melody of death that makes you smile as we draw the last sigh from your lips.  Over the eons we have learned to thrive, and wherever an owl is sighted one of us is near.
