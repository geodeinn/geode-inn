Lowa: The supreme primordial deity who emerged from an endless sea to lift the heavens and form the coral atoll rings of the Ratak and Ralik chains.

Letao: A clever, unpredictable trickster god born from Wullep who introduced both wonder and conflict into the world, often testing human wits.

Jebro: Associated with celestial navigation and canoe building, locking cultural knowledge into stellar paths and wave patterns.

Loktanur and Litarmelu: Mythic women and mentors who passed down the secret arts of wave-reading, star-pathing, and stick-chart navigation to younger generations.

Landmarks and Giants: Legends often explain local atoll formations, reefs, and deep channels through the movements of ancient giants wading across the Pacific.

Customs: Storytelling (bwebwenato) was strictly regulated by societal hierarchy and reserved for specific times to preserve community values.
