Aisha Kandisha: A famous legendary, siren-like spirit of the countryside and waterways.

Djinn and Kings: Belief in unseen entities, including spirit kings like Sidi Shamharoush residing in the Atlas Mountains.

Guedra: A hypnotic, spiritual healing dance from the southern Sahrawi people.

Aissawa: Sufi-inspired music and spinning dances meant to invoke blessings and divine protection.

Gnawa: Trance-fusion music and ritual healing performances that incorporate deep African diaspora roots.
