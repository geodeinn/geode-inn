The Primordials & Titans: Creation began with Chaos, giving rise to Gaia (Earth) and Uranus (Sky). Their Titan children, led by Cronus, ruled the cosmos until overthrown by the Olympian gods in the Titanomachy.

The Olympians: The twelve Olympian gods, led by Zeus, shaped the moral and physical world, influencing everything from war and harvest to the arts.

Heroes & Monsters: Enduring tales feature heroes like Odysseus and Theseus battling mythological threats such as the Minotaur, Medusa, and the deadly whirlpool Charybdis.

Demotic Music: Modern Greek folk music evolved from Byzantine and ancient roots. Regional instruments define the sound, such as the Cretan lyra, mainland clarinets, and bagpipes in Macedonia.

Mourning Songs (Mirolóyia): A cappella laments traditionally sung by older women in the Epirus and Mani regions to honor the dead.

Folk Customs: Practices are often tied to the land and religion, such as placing copper coins over the eyes of the deceased or passing babies around the hearth for protection.
