Kakamora: Small, elf-like people under four feet tall with hairy bodies and sharp nails, known in Kakamora lore to live in caves and fear the color white.

Adaro: Malevolent sea ghosts from Makira depicted with shark-like dorsal fins, fish tails or finned feet, and Adaro spirits that hurt swimmers using flying fish.

Solomon Island Giants: Towering humanoid cryptids said to live deep inside the dark jungles and mountains of Guadalcanal.

Kahausibware / Agunua: Major cosmic serpent and creator deities linked to the ocean, fertility, and sacred rituals.

The Great Flood (Ruarua): An ancient deluge myth where a leader named Umaroa saved his people and animals in a big canoe, while those who drowned turned into stone pillars.

Chief Tiola: A foundational legend from Nduke detailing how Tiola and his five brothers emerged fully grown from a sacred cave to become early island chiefs.

The Dugong Legend: A traditional Lau tabu story explaining how a human woman turned into a dugong, making the marine mammal sacred and forbidden to eat.
