The Solomonic Dynasty: The cornerstone of Ethiopian tradition is the tale of the Queen of Sheba traveling to Jerusalem to meet King Solomon. Their union birthed Menelik I, who legend dictates brought the Ark of the Covenant to Ethiopia.

Arwe the Serpent: Before the Solomonic line, the kingdom of Axum was said to be ruled by Arwe, a giant serpent-king that demanded a monthly tribute of young women until he was slain by the hero Angabo.

Lalibela: Myth and history blend in the story of King Lalibela, who was instructed by God to carve 11 churches out of solid, red volcanic rock in the mountains.

Buda: A prominent figure in Ethiopian folklore, the Buda is a malicious, shape-shifting spirit capable of possessing humans and casting the "evil eye" to cause misfortune.

Mythical Hyenas: Hyenas hold a prominent place in stories, often portrayed as possessing beautiful voices or engaging in trickery with the donkey, which traces back to regional animal fables.
