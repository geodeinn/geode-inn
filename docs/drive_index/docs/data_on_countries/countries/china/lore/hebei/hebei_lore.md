The Sansei: A mischievous, one-legged mountain monster with backward-facing feet. It steals salt from miners but is easily defeated if someone yells out "Batsu" (a drought spirit). [1]

Zoomorphic Deities: Traditional folk religion in the region heavily reveres the "Four Great Immortals" (Sìdàxiān): the Fox, Weasel, Hedgehog, and Snake Gods. They are often consulted by local shamans. 7

Xihe Dagu: Traditional drum ballads that have carried local, centuries-old stories across villages.

Shadow Puppetry: Tangshan shadow plays are world-famous, bringing regional folk stories to life through intricate leather silhouettes.

Lunhua: A 100-year-old intangible cultural heritage tradition where performers in Luanping County toss charcoal and iron shards into a flower, creating a spectacular cascade of sparks.

Qilin Dances: In many Hebei villages, performers swap the traditional lion for the mythical Qilin (a benevolent creature) to bring prosperity and good fortune.

Yuxian Paper-Cutting: A unique, 200-year-old style of paper-cutting where artisans use carving knives rather than scissors.

Cangzhou's Iron Lion: A massive, over-1,000-year-old iron statue steeped in legend and local cultural pride.
