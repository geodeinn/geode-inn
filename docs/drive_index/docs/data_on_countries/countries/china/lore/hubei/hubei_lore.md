Deep in the mist-covered mountains of the Shennongjia forest reserve lives the legend of the Yeren (Chinese "Wildman"). For generations, locals have reported sightings of a large, elusive, ape-like creature covered in reddish or dark hair. The region is also named after Shennong, the mythical Divine Farmer who is said to have tasted hundreds of herbs here to discover traditional medicine.

Hubei is the historical heart of the ancient State of Chu. Chu mythology is distinct and romantic, featuring a pantheon of gods, spirits, and fantastical beasts. One prominent figure is Qu Yuan, a patriotic Chu poet who threw himself into the Miluo River. His legacy birthed the Dragon Boat Festival, celebrated widely across the province with zongzi (sticky rice dumplings) and dragon boat racing.

The Legend of Dong Yong: Particularly popular in Xiaogan, this tale tells the story of an impoverished but filial man who sells himself into servitude to bury his father. Touched by his devotion, the Seventh Fairy Maiden (daughter of the Jade Emperor) descends from heaven to marry him, though they are ultimately torn apart by the heavens.
