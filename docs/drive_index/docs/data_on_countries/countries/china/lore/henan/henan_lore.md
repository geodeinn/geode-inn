The Yellow Emperor (Huangdi): Legend places the birthplace and capital of Huangdi, the mythical ancestor of the Chinese people, in Xinzheng, Henan. The Yellow River valley is where he is said to have united tribes and established the core tenets of Chinese civilization.

The Kitchen God: The widely known Chinese legend of the Kitchen God (Zao Jun) and his wives originates from Henan folklore. The tale, which spans from heaven to hell, weaves traditional rural customs, marital loyalty, and divine judgment.

Creation Myths: In nearby Huaiyang county, the mythological figures Nüwa (who created humanity) and Fuxi (who created the Eight Trigrams and fishing nets) are heavily revered, with ancient temples built in their honor.

Shaolin Kung Fu: Centered in the Songshan Mountains of Dengfeng, Henan, the Shaolin Temple is the legendary birthplace of Zen Buddhism and Shaolin Kung Fu. Its folklore emphasizes both physical discipline and harmony with the universe.

Tai Chi: The Chen Village (Chenjiagou) in Wen County, Henan, is the official birthplace of Tai Chi. The martial art’s folklore roots itself in observing the natural harmony of the Yin and Yang.

Datiehua (Beating Iron Flowers): Originating in the Song Dynasty, this thrilling firework folk art was created by ancient metal craftsmen. Performers in heavy sheepskin coats throw ladles of molten iron into the night air against wooden planks, creating a spectacular shower of golden sparks.

Dragon Boat Festival Roots: Deeply embedded in the Central Plains culture, Henan folklore champions the dragon as a central totem. The reverence for the dragon as a divine, weather-controlling creature is highly associated with the life-giving (and sometimes devastating) Yellow River.

Zhuihu Ballads: Traditional Henan folk storytelling features the zhuizi—a narrative singing style accompanied by a bowed lute. The lyrical themes are heavily based on Taoist stories, ancient chants, and everyday life.

Only Henan (Zhongyuan Drama): Modern-day Henan heavily preserves its own local stories and theatrical legacy. The massive immersive theatrical park "Only Henan" brings centuries of Central Plains history, folk beliefs, and ancient capital culture (such as Kaifeng and Luoyang) to life for millions of visitors.
