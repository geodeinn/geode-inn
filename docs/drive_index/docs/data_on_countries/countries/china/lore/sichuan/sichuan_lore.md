Mount Emei's White Snake: One of China’s most famous love stories is rooted in Sichuan's Mt. Emei. It follows a thousand-year-old white snake spirit (Bai Suzhen) and her green serpent companion (Xiaoqing) who take human form to experience the mortal world and find love in the face of a rigid Buddhist priest.

Sun Wukong (The Monkey King): While popularized in Journey to the West, regional Chuan Miao (River Miao) folklore features different variations of a monkey spirit that escorts emperors and tends to the Dragon King's injuries.

The Jiuzhaigou Goddess: Tibetan folklore from northern Sichuan tells of a beautiful girl named Wonuo who was gifted golden bells by a goddess; her ringing bells brought crystal-clear creeks to the drought-ridden, barren mountains.

Shan Xiao (山魈): A one-legged mountain spirit (originally described in classical texts) that ancient Sichuan travelers believed caused fever and madness, scaring them into shouting loudly in mountain passes to drive spirits away.

Jiangshi (殭屍): The concept of the hopping vampire is deeply tied to regional Sichuan folklore, where early Daoist-influenced stories describe rigid corpses animated by terror or improper burial.

Qiang Folk Religion: Deep in the mountains of Ngawa, traditional Qiang shamans known as duāngōng act as the custodians of local mythology. They perform complex rituals to mountain and sky gods, culminating in intense rites like the "sitting on top of the mountain".

The Sichuan Massacre Myth: Historical folklore from the Ming-Qing transition centers around the rebel leader Zhang Xianzhong, with legends (partially spun as Qing dynasty propaganda) attributing the mass depopulation of the province to his supernatural wrath and bloody conquests.

Sichuan Opera (川剧): Traditional lore comes alive through this regional art form, famous for bian lian (face-changing) and folk dramas like Pijin's Tumbling Lantern and The Story of Funu.

Shadow Play: A disappearing art form historically prominent along promenades in Chengdu, depicting ancient folklore through intricate shadow puppets.
