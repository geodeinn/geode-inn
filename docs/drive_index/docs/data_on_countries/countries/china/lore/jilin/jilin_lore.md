The Qilin: Known as the Chinese unicorn, this auspicious beast symbolizes good fortune, prosperity, and serenity. Legend describes it as a dragon-bodied, deer-hoofed protector that punishes the wicked and walks on clouds so it never crushes a blade of grass.

Changbai Mountain Monster (Tianchi Monster): Folklore surrounding Heaven Lake at the peak of Changbai Mountain tells of a massive, lake-dwelling creature similar to the Loch Ness Monster, occasionally spotted by locals and tourists.

Manchu Shamanism: Jilin is a historic heartland of Shamanic culture. Traditional shamans still act as spiritual healers and community pillars, performing rites using animal-hide drums, bird-feather headdresses, and waists adorned with dozens of bells.

Northeast Folk Religion: A syncretic blend of Han Chinese settlers and Manchu traditions, focusing heavily on worship of the Fox Spirit, the Weasel (Huang Daxian), and other animal totems.

Ginseng Spirits: Changbai Mountain is legendary for its wild ginseng. Local lore dictates that the "Ginseng Child" (a childlike spirit) can run away or vanish into the ground to hide from greedy harvesters.

Intangible Crafts: Traditional regional arts—such as Manchu paper-cutting and northeastern "Clay Figure Zhang"—preserve historical village life, farming customs, and mythical tales.

Korean Folk Heritage: In areas like Yanbian, Korean folklore heavily influences the region with vibrant agricultural dances, traditional folk games, and rhythmic pansori storytelling.
