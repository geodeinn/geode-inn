May and Ghilân: A foundational Al Khor legend about a boat race between a wealthy fleet owner (Ghilân) and a skilled female captain (May) that inspired the invention of the dhow boat sail.

Bu Daryah (Lord of the Sea): A malevolent water spirit or jinni said to stalk pearl divers and sailors at night, causing psychological distress or pulling men into the dark depths.

The Donkey Lady (Homarat Al-Guyla): A half-woman, half-donkey creature with hoofed feet used by parents during the midday heat to scare children from wandering outside during siesta time.

Al Fisaikra: A magical porgy fish from a popular folktale that helps a mistreated young girl escape and find a better life.

Al-Ardha: The traditional sword dance performed during national holidays, weddings, and the Qaffal ceremony welcoming back pearl divers.

Oral Transmission: Traditionally shared in the majlis or around desert campfires, these dynamic stories have been modernly cataloged by projects like the Qatar Museums Hazawy Series.
