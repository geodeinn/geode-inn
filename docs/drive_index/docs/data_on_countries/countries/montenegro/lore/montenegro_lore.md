Psoglav: A terrifying mythical creature described as a man with a horse or dog’s head, human teeth, and horse legs, featured in regional mountain lore.

Zduhač: A supernatural human protector believed to possess the power to guide weather patterns and protect their clan's fields from devastating storms.

Our Lady of the Rocks (Gospa od Škrpjela): A famous maritime legend from the Bay of Kotor where sailors dropped rocks onto a reef starting in July 1452 after seeing an icon of the Virgin Mary, eventually creating an artificial island.

The Three Sisters (Tre Sorelle): A tragic romance folklore from Prčanj about three sisters who loved the same sailor and walled up their windows one by one as they aged and waited in vain. [1]

Crnogorsko Oro: The national "Eagle Dance," where dancers form a large circle, couples step into the middle, and men climb onto each other's shoulders to mimic soaring eagles. ]

The Gusle: A primitive, single-stringed arched instrument played with a horsehair bow while a bard (guslar) dramatically chants long historical epic poems of heroism and past battles.

Royal Inspiration: Traditional Montenegrin attire is heavily embroidered with glittering gold and silver threads, originally styled to mimic old royal court garments.

Family Wealth: These elaborate outfits were historically considered the most valuable possession a household could own, often valued as much as a small parcel of land.
