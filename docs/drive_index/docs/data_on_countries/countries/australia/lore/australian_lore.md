The Dreamtime: The foundational spiritual worldview explaining the creation of life, law, and the physical features of the continent by ancestral spirits over 60,000 years.

The Bunyip: A fearsome monster said to lurk in swamps, billabongs, and waterholes, known for its terrifying nighttime bellows.

The Rainbow Serpent: A powerful creator deity that slithered across the dry earth, carving out rivers, valleys, and deep channels.

Tiddalik the Frog: A famous observational legend about a massive frog who drank all the fresh water in the land and had to be made to laugh to return it.

Ned Kelly: The iconic armored bushranger and outlaw whose final stand at Glenrowan made him a symbol of anti-authoritarian defiance.

The Swagman: The restless wandering traveler immortalized in Australia's unofficial national anthem, "Waltzing Matilda".

The Yowie: A tall, hairy, ape-like cryptid of the remote Australian wilderness, similar to Bigfoot.

Drop Bears: A modern satirical legend of predatory, sharp-toothed koalas that drop from trees onto unsuspecting campers.
