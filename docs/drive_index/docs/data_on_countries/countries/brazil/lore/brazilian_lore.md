Saci-Pererê: A famous, mischievous one-legged Black boy who smokes a pipe and wears a red cap. He travels inside mini-whirlwinds, hiding in the woods to play tricks on farmers and travelers.

Curupira: A jungle spirit recognized by his bright red hair and backwards-facing feet. He uses his backward tracks to confuse hunters and protect the forests.

Iara: The Amazonian siren. A beautiful, half-human, half-fish water spirit who sings enchanting melodies to lure men into the rivers.

Boto Cor-de-Rosa (The Pink Dolphin): Originating from the Amazon, this legend claims that pink dolphins transform into handsome, well-dressed men during the nighttime to seduce local women.

Boitatá: A giant, fiery serpent or dragon with glowing eyes that acts as a fierce protector of the Amazon, targeting those who start forest fires.

Mula sem Cabeça (Headless Mule): A terrifying creature in the form of a mule with flames bursting from its neck, which thunders through the fields to punish those who break moral codes.

Cuca: A fearsome, alligator-like witch with long blond hair who steals and curses disobedient children.
