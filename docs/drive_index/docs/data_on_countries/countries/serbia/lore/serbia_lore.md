Zmaj: A benevolent, wise winged dragon or serpent that protects tribes, brings rain, and fights evil.

Ala: A malevolent, storm-bringing demon or multi-headed dragon enemy that ruins crops.

Vila: Wild, beautiful female forest fairies associated with natural springs and prophetic powers.

Baš Čelik: A famous steel-coated villain/ogre king from fairy tales representing trapped immortal strength.

Kosovo Myth: A central national narrative centered on the 1389 Battle of Kosovo, where Prince Lazar chose a heavenly kingdom over earthly victory.

Epic Songs: Oral poems sung to the accompaniment of the gusle, recounting heroic feats by figures like Marko Kraljević and Miloš Obilić.

Kolo: A traditional, fast-paced circular group dance protected as intangible cultural heritage.

Opanak: Traditional, laced-free leather shoes featuring a distinct upward-curved toe.
