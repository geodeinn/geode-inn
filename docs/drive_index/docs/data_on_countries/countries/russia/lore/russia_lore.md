Baba Yaga: A fierce, ambiguous witch who flies in a mortar, sweeps away her tracks with a broom, and lives in a forest hut that stands on giant chicken legs.

Koschei the Deathless: A skeletal, wicked sorcerer whose soul is safely hidden outside his body in a nested sequence of a duck, hare, chest, and egg.

The Firebird: A dazzling, luminous bird of glowing copper and gold feathers that acts as both a blessing and a difficult quest item for heroes.

Ivan the Fool: The youngest, underestimated son who wins the day through unexpected kindness, patience, and pure luck rather than brute force.
