Long before European colonization, the Mi'kmaq people inhabited the island, which they called Epekwitk (or Abegweit), meaning "cradled upon the waves". [1, 2]

The Tweedlers: One of PEI's most famous indigenous legends features the Tweedlers—a race of small, preternatural beings with extraordinary abilities. After a bitter conflict over kidnapped Mi'kmaq children, the two groups fought a bloody three-day war at Pretty Stream in Kellos Hollow. The surviving Tweedlers were cast out to sea, but islanders still claim to see them flitting through dense forests and hiding in caves.

The Flaming Ghost Ship: The Northumberland Strait is frequently haunted by a phantom schooner. Sightings describe a burning ship enveloped in flames with white sails and visible crew members climbing the rigging. It is widely considered an omen of an approaching severe northeastern storm.

The Phantom Bell Ringers: In one of Charlottetown's most enduring legends, a ship captain and church sexton at the Kirk of St. James saw phantom women in white entering the church as a bell tolled. The tolling coincided exactly with the tragic sinking of the Fairy Queen off the coast, which claimed eight lives.

The White Lady: Woven into island lore (and famously referenced in L.M. Montgomery's Anne of Green Gables), the ghost of a "White Lady" is said to wander through graveyards and woods, often appearing as a harbinger of death within a local family.

Mischievous Fairies: Settler tales are full of stories about fairies tampering with daily life. Farmers tell of waking up to find their horses' manes neatly braided in their barns. Conversely, if a house was built on a "fairy path," furniture was said to move on its own, and pans would fly off the shelves until the annoyed homeowners physically relocated.

Captain Kidd's Treasure: Like many maritime provinces, PEI has a rich history of pirate lore. Stories claim Captain Kidd buried untold amounts of treasure in the red-sand shores of the island, resulting in generations of islanders digging up the coast, only to be scared off by eerie, supernatural phenomena.
