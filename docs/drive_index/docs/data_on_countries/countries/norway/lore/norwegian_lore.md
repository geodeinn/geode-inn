  Trolls: Large, slow-witted creatures living in mountains or caves that turn to stone if hit by sunlight.

  Huldra: A seductive forest spirit with a cow’s tail who lures men deep into the woods.

Nøkken: A dangerous shape-shifting water spirit that plays magical fiddle tunes to lure people into deep lakes and rivers.

Nisse: A tiny, bearded household spirit who guards the farm if treated with kindness or plays mean tricks if angered.

Draug: A ghostly, animated corpse of a drowned sailor that foretells storms or death near the coast.
