Gorogly (Görogly): The grand national epic detailing the heroic deeds of Gorogly and his 40 horsemen, filled with hundreds of traditional songs and values of courage and loyalty.

Destans: Oral historical compositions that blend poetry, romance, fairy tales, and moral codes, traditionally chanted to listeners.

Oghuz Khan Legend: The foundational origin myth tracing the lineage of Turkmen tribes back to Oghuz Khan.

Bakhshi: Revered itinerant bard-minstrels who travel from village to village preserving history and reciting epics from memory.

Dutar: A two-stringed long-necked lute considered the soulful heart of Turkmen musical folklore

Tuyduk & Rituals: Traditional flutes linked in folklore to creation myths involving Adam, accompanied by symbolic circular shamanistic movements.

Ependi (Khoja Nasreddin): Beloved humorous and wise folkloric character whose witty anecdotes teach moral lessons.

Magtymguly & Kemine Cycles: Folk tales and satirical jokes built around historical classical poets and local wits.

Superstitions and Creatures: Beliefs in protective charms against the evil eye, alongside legendary beings like the Tulpar (winged horse) and forest spirits
