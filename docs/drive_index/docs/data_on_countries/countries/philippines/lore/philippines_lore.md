Aswang: A general term for shape-shifting evil entities that act as vampires, ghouls, or witches.

Kapre: A tall, dark, hairy giant that sits on tree branches smoking a large pipe.

Tikbalang: A half-man, half-horse creature that misleads travelers in the woods.

Tiyanak: A demon-infant that mimics the cries of a real baby to trick wayfarers.

Duwende & Nuno sa Punso: Small earth dwarfs or goblins living in mounds and yards.

Bathala: The supreme deity in Tagalog mythology.

Bakunawa: A giant serpent-like dragon from Visayan mythology believed to swallow the moon, causing eclipses.

Hinilawod: An ancient epic poem from Panay following the adventures of demigod brothers like Labaw Donggon.
