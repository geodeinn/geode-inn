Dievs: The supreme god of the sky and the ultimate creator.

Laima: The revered goddess of fate, luck, and destiny who determines the length of human life.

Māra: The earth mother and protector of women, nature, and the home.

Pērkons: The powerful god of thunder, war, and fertility.

Saule & Mēness: The personified Sun (female) and Moon (male) deities, who feature heavily in cosmic wedding stories.

"Mātes" (Mothers): Various female nature spirits governing the elements, such as Jūras māte (Mother of the Sea), Vēja māte (Mother of the Wind), and Lauku māte (Mother of the Fields).

Lāčplēsis (The Bear Slayer): The iconic national epic created by Andrejs Pumpurs. Born to a human mother and a bear father, Lāčplēsis possesses superhuman strength and fights dark crusaders to defend Latvia.

Lieldienas (Spring Equinox): Celebrates the rebirth of nature. Traditions include the dyeing and rolling of eggs to ensure fertility.

Jāņi (Summer Solstice): The most significant cultural holiday. People weave wreaths of oak leaves and wildflowers, light massive bonfires, and search for the mythical "fern flower" on the shortest night of the year.
