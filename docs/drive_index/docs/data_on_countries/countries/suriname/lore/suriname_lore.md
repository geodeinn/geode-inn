Bakru: A trickster spirit from Winti religion that is the size of a child with a body half-flesh and half-wood. People say they can bring wealth in exchange for a family member's well-being.

Asema: A ghost that appears as an old person during the day and turns into a blood-sucking creature at night.

Yorka: Spirits of the dead who must be fed special offerings without salt because the deceased cannot eat it.

Kunu: Vengeful ancestral or demonic spirits that bring misfortune or illness to families as payback for past wrongs.

Anansi Stories: Cunning spider tales brought from West Africa where Anansi outsmarts bigger animals and humans.

Oral Traditions: Deeply preserved through storytelling by elders, warning youth about unseen jungle and river forces.

Obia and Winti: Traditional healing practices and magic where ritual drumming and herbal remedies treat spiritual illnesses
