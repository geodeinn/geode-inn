  La Tulivieja (Tulevieja): A tragic river spirit—blending elements of La Llorona and La Cegua—depicted as a weeping, monster-like woman with backward feet searching for her lost child near streams.

  El Cadejo: A mystical dog appearing as a white protective spirit or a black harbinger of misfortune on dark paths.

  El Chivato: A half-man, half-goat creature representing mischief or temptation in rural farmlands.
