Kintu and Nambi: The foundational Baganda creation story where the first man, Kintu, marries Nambi, the sky god’s daughter, and accidentally brings death (Walumbe) to earth. [1, 2]

Balubaale: The pantheon of traditional gods in the Buganda Kingdom, including Katonda the creator, Mukasa of Lake Victoria, and Kawumpuli the god of plague.

The Bachwezi: A semi-divine, mystical dynasty said to have ruled the Great Lakes region, credited with introducing iron smelting, long-horned cattle, and centralized rule before mysteriously disappearing into lakes and forests.

The King of the Snakes (Sasota): Popular rural legends detailing brave peasants or tricksters overcoming giant mythical serpents terrorizing villages.

The Clever Hare: A recurring trickster figure who regularly outsmarts larger, stronger animals like the lion or leopard, teaching listeners that intelligence and wit triumph over brute strength.
