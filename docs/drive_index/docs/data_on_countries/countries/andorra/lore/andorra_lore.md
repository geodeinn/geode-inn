Tamarros: Mischievous, mythical little creatures said to hide in the deep forests and stone paths of Andorra's valleys.

The White Lady of Aubinyà: A protective and ghostly spirit of local mountain lore said to wander near historical stone pathways.

Lake Engolasters Legend: A popular folktale stating that a selfish village refused food to a weary beggar (who was actually an angel or holy figure); only one kind girl shared bread, and as a punishment, the wicked town was flooded overnight to form Lake Engolasters, which became a gathering place for witches.

Contrapas and Marratxa: Ancient, formal ceremonial folk dances passed down through generations, danced in public squares during parish festes (festivals).

Buners: Traditional bagpipe players who perform with drums and pipes during high-mountain gatherings like the Trobada de Buners in Ordino.

Our Lady of Meritxell: Dedicated to Andorra's patron virgin, representing the spiritual and national heart of local religious folklore.

Parish Festas: Annual summer celebrations hosted across parishes like Canillo, Encamp, and Andorra la Vella featuring traditional Sardana dancing and communal feasts.
