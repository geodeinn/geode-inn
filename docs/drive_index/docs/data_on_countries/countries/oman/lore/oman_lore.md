The Jinn of Bahla: The ancient oasis town of Bahla is famous across Oman as a stronghold of jinn (supernatural spirits), with urban legends claiming its 13-kilometer wall was built in a single night by magic.

Shapeshifting and Monsters: Traditional local stories from the interior warn of fire-mouthed hyenas and humans cursed or tricked into turning into donkeys.

Sindbad the Sailor: The coastal city of Suhar claims the legendary mariner Sindbad as its native son, tying Oman's historical Indian Ocean trading empire directly into classic maritime folklore.
