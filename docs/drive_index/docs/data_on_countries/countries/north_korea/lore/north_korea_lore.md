  Dangun Wanggeom: The legendary founder of Gojoseon, said to be the grandson of heaven born from a union between Hwanung (a divine prince) and a bear turned into a woman. North Korea promotes this ancient myth and claims to house Dangun's actual tomb near Pyongyang to tie the region to deep historical legitimacy. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]

  The Chollima: A mythical winged horse capable of traveling 1,000 li (about 400 kilometers) a day. Too swift for mortal riders, it became the ultimate national symbol of speed, progress, and the post-Korean War industrial mobilization (the Chollima Movement).

  Kirin and Unicorn Lairs: State media announced the "reconfirmation" of a historic unicorn lair (Kiringul) in Pyongyang, linking ancient Korean kings who rode mythical chimeras directly to the capital's heritage.
