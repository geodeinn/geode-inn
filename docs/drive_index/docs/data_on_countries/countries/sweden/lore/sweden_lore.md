Trolls: Large, dim-witted mountain or forest beings who feared church bells and lightning.

Skogsrå: A seductive, beautiful forest guardian spirit with a hollow back or a cow's tail who lured lonely loggers deep into the woods.

Näcken: A shape-shifting water spirit who played an enchanting violin in streams and waterfalls to lure people into drowning.

Älvor (Elves): Tiny fairy-like spirits or ethereal women who danced in early morning mist (älvdans), leaving mushroom rings behind.

Tomte (Gårdstomte): A small, bearded invisible gnome who lived on the farm, protected the livestock and home, and demanded a bowl of porridge with butter at Christmas.

Vittra: Invisible, subterranean wights or beings who lived in parallel societies underground and required respect when humans crossed their land.

Myling: The vengeful or tragic ghost of an unbaptized, abandoned infant crying out in the night.

Nattmara (Mare): A nightmare spirit that sat on a sleeper's chest, causing heavy sleep paralysis and terrifying dreams.

Draugen: A fearsome, corpse-like sea ghost tied to shipwrecks and storms off the rocky coastlines.
