Basajaun (Basque Country): A giant, hairy "Lord of the Woods" who protects nature and flocks.

Culebre (Asturias and Cantabria): A giant winged dragon that guards underground caves and hidden treasures.

Santa Compaña (Galicia and Asturias): A nocturnal procession of lost souls carrying candles that foretells misfortune for anyone who sees them.

Trasgu (Asturias and Cantabria): A tiny, mischievous house goblin who plays harmless practical jokes.
