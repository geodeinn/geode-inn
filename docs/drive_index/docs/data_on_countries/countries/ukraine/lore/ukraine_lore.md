Mavka: A vengeful, long-haired female forest spirit or mermaid who lures young men into the woods.

Domovyk: A small, hairy household guardian spirit living near the hearth or threshold who protects the home.

Chuhayster: A giant, hairy, blue-eyed forest god who protects humans and dances away evil spirits.

Rusalka: Water spirits associated with willow trees and deep rivers, tied to spring and harvest fertility rituals.

Kotyhoroshko: A hero born from a pea who gains massive strength and defeats a wicked serpent to save his family.

Kyrylo Kozhumyaka: A legendary tanner who strangles a fierce dragon with his bare hands to rescue a princess.

Ivasyk-Telesyk: A boy born from a wooden log who outsmarts an evil witch trying to cook him.

Ivan Kupala Day: Midsummer celebration involving bonfires, flower wreaths, and water rituals for fertility and cleansing.

Kolyadky: Traditional Christmas carols with ancient pagan roots used to wish prosperity.

Vesnyanky: Circle and ritual songs performed in spring to welcome the new agricultural season.
