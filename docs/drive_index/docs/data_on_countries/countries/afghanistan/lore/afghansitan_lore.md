Div (Dev): Ogre-like giant monsters from ancient mythology representing pure evil.

Al (Almasti): A cruel, feminine demon with sharp claws associated with harming newborns and childbirth.

Simurgh: A wise, benevolent, and giant mythical bird from Persian-influenced traditions that aids heroes.

Karkadann: A fierce, unicorn-like violent beast of massive strength.

Pai Girdak: A type of Jinn with round feet said to frequent public bathhouses at night.

Buzak Chini (The Porcelain Goat): A famous traditional story about a clever goat outwitting a big bad wolf.

Khastakhumar and Bibinagar: An epic quest tale of a maiden searching for her supernatural husband in snakeskin, sharing thematic parallels with Cupid and Psyche.

40 Dukhtaran (Forty Girls): A regional legend celebrating the bravery and legacy of women.

Imra: Historically viewed in Nuristani traditions as a creator god who breathed life into minor deities.

Gish: The ancient regional god of war, pictured using a rainbow as a sling.

Jamshed (Yama): A primordial mythical king who saved the earth from a catastrophic winter and whose legend is tied to Noruz (New Year).
