The Adze: A viric entity in Ewe folklore of Togo and Ghana that transforms into a firefly, beetle, or ball of light at night, slips through keyholes to drink sleeping people's blood, and can possess humans as witches. [1, 2]

Mawu: The supreme creator sky god who represents order, benevolence, and peace.

Legba: The divine trickster and linguist who translates messages between humans and the supreme deity Mawu, presiding over change and divination.

Vodun (Voodoo): A dominant spiritual practice deeply embedded in daily life, rural customs, and sacred sites like Togoville.

Animism and Nature Spirits: Belief that rivers, forests, and animals possess powerful spirits that must be respected through rituals and offerings
