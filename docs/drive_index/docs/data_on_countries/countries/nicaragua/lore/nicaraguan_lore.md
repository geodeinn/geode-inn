  La Mocuana: The ghost of an Indigenous chief's daughter who went mad after being tricked and abandoned by a Spanish lover; she now lures men into the woods. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]

  La Carreta Nagua: A ghostly, invisible or skeletal ox-drawn cart draped in chains that roams the midnight streets, warning of impending death. [1, 2, 3]

  La Cegua: A shape-shifting witch or beautiful phantom who entices late-night wandering men on dark roads before revealing a terrifying horse-like or skull face.

  El Cadejo: A mythical dog spirit appearing in two forms—a helpful white dog and a malicious black dog—that guards or stalks nocturnal travelers.
