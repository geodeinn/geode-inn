Dokkaebi (도깨비): Mischievous goblins endowed with magic tools like invisibility hats and clubs that summon items.

Gumiho (구미호): A fox with nine tails that can transform into a beautiful woman to deceive humans.

Imugi & Yong (이무기 / 용): Lesser ocean serpents (Imugi) aspiring to become full-fledged celestial dragons (Yong).

Gwisin (귀신): Restless human ghosts—often depicted as pale, floating figures with long black hair—tied to tragic, unresolved deaths.

Samsin Halmeoni: The grandmother goddess responsible for childbirth, pregnancy, and the health of infants.

Jowangshin: The hearth goddess who watches over the kitchen, fire, and family well-being

Munshin: The protective guardian deity of the household doors and entryways.

Heungbu and Nolbu: A moral fable about a kind brother rewarded by an injured swallow versus his greedy brother.

The Rabbit's Liver: A witty fable where an outwitted dragon king attempts to secure a rabbit's liver for medicine.

Kongjwi and Patjwi: A traditional Korean variant of the classic Cinderella theme emphasizing patience and inner kindness.
