Areop-Enap: A legendary spider deity who existed in the beginning when there was only endless water. In traditional belief, this spider created the moon and the sun out of two snails and separated a giant mussel shell to form the earth and sky with the help of a worm. [1]

The Douwogira Tree: An alternate creation motif where a god climbs a sacred tree of life. Its falling leaves and branches drift across the ocean and solidify into the islands of the Pacific, with Nauru settling where a leaf landed.

The Coconut Palm: Said to have grown from the grave of an unlucky buried ancestor. This origin explains why the markings on a coconut shell resemble human facial features (eyes, nose, and mouth).

Gob and the Hammerhead Shark: The story of a child named Gob who slipped away while being washed, fled into the sea, and flattened his head against the coral to transform into a hammerhead shark.
