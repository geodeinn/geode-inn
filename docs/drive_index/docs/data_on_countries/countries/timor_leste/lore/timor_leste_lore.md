The Good Crocodile (Lafaek Diak): An origin myth where a kind boy rescues a stranded, weak crocodile. Growing old, the grateful creature transforms its body into the island of Timor, its ridged back forming the rugged mountain ranges. The Timorese consider themselves descendants of this crocodile. The Half-Snake Son (Wewatais): A traditional narrative from the border regions detailing a woman who gives birth to a boy with a snake's tail who can control weather and the sea.

Timor-Les

The highest peak in the country is revered as the spiritual dwelling place of ancestral spirits, acting as a bridge between earth and the heavens.

Timor-Leste

Associated in lore with a powerful guardian serpent that watches over the land's environmental harmony.

Uma Lulik

Sacred spiritual houses built using strict rituals, believed to shelter ancestral guardians and maintain lineage memory.

Tara Bandu: A customary system of community-led laws and ecological taboos used to protect sacred forests, water sources, and specific trees through ritual enforcement
