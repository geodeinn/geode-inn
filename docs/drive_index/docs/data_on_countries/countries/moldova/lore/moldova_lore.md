Miorița ("The Ewe Lamb"): The most famous ancient pastoral ballad about a shepherd who accepts his destined fate with cosmic serenity.

Meșterul Manole: A tragic architectural myth about sacrifice to build a great monastery.

Doina: A lyrical, nostalgic style of folk song expressing deep emotion, longing, or sorrow.

Făt-Frumos & Ileana Cosânzeana: The classic heroic prince and the beautiful princess he rescues from evil dragons.

Păcală and Tândală: Two humorous, witty peasant tricksters featured in hundreds of comical local folk jokes.

Hora: A slow, circular community dance full of inspiration and unity.

Sârba: A very fast-paced, high-energy circle/line dance.

Joc: A legendary national folk dance showcasing high physical dexterity and the temperament of the people.
