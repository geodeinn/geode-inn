Kalulu the Hare: The most famous clever trickster in Zambian and Bantu folklore (known in Tumbuka and Bemba traditions) who outsmarts powerful beasts like the lion and elephant using wit.

Fulu the Tortoise: Regarded as the wisest and most grounded animal, sometimes the only one who can match or check Kalulu's schemes.

Chimbwi the Hyena: Frequently cast as the greedy, foolish villain who falls for Kalulu's traps.

Leza / Lesa / Nyambe: The Supreme Creator and sky god, recognized under varying names across different linguistic groups like the Tonga, Bemba, and Lozi.

The Sky Is So High: An early belief stating the sky used to hang low like a mosquito net, allowing people to talk directly to God before human behavior pushed it upward.

Nyami Nyami: The sacred river god of the Tonga people, depicted as a dragon-like serpent with a fish head residing in the Zambezi River near the Kariba Dam.

Ilomba and Ikisonga: Mythical familiars and monster-slayers tied to traditional bush and river superstitions.
