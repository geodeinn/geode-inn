Tengri: The supreme god of the eternal blue sky, creator of all things and father of early rulers.

Etugen Eke: The Mother Earth goddess, representing fertility, land, and the life of nature.

Erlik Khan: The ruler of the underworld and king of death, associated with sickness and subterranean forces.

Epic Literature and Legends

The Secret History of the Mongols: The 13th-century foundational chronicle beginning with the ancestry of a blue-gray wolf and a fallow deer.

Epic of King Gesar (Geser): A massive Central Asian and Mongol religious epic about a prophetic warrior-king of Tengriism and Buddhism.

Jangar: A heroic epic detailing the exploits of brave warriors and their utopian land of Bumba.

Manggus: Multi-headed malevolent monsters often defeated by culture heroes or clever tricksters.

Erkhii Mergen: The tale of an arrogant archer who missed a shot at the sun and vowed to cut off his thumb if he failed, becoming the marmot.

The Camel Versus the Mouse: An animal fabled explanation of why the camel lacks front teeth and why the camel rolls in ashes regarding the lunar calendar.
