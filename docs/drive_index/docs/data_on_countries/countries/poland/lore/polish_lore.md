The Wawel Dragon (Smok Wawelski): A fierce monster in Kraków defeated when a clever shoemaker fed it a sulfur-stuffed sheep, causing it to drink from the Vistula River until it burst.

Lech, Czech, and Rus: Three brother leaders whose people split up; Lech saw a white eagle's nest at sunset, founded Gniezno, and made the white eagle Poland's symbol.

King Popiel and the Mice: A cruel 9th-century ruler who poisoned his family and was subsequently eaten alive by a swarm of mice in his tower at Lake Gopło.

The Warsaw Mermaid (Syrenka): A half-fish, half-human protector of Warsaw who swore to defend the city.

Leshy (Bór / Leśnik): A tall forest spirit with bark-like skin who protects woodland animals and misleads disrespectful travelers.

Rusałki: Dangerous and beautiful water nymphs of young women who lure people into deep water.

Strzyga: A flying, vampire-like creature with two souls from Slavic folk belief.

Chochlik: A mischievous, small house demon prone to playing tricks.
