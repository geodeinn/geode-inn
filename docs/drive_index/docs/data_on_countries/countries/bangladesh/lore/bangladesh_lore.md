Manasamangal: An epic centered on the snake goddess Manasa and the unmatched devotion and grit of human heroine Behula.

Mymensingh Gitika: A famous collection of romantic folk ballads featuring tragic local love stories like Malua and Mahua.

Nath Geetika: Oral poems detailing the spiritual transformations and trials of historic figures like Prince Gopi Chandra.

Baul Tradition: A mystical, lyric-driven genre of wandering minstrels focused on divine love within.

Bhatiali & Bhawaiya: Boatmen's and pastoral melodies reflecting longing, separation, and the rhythms of the rivers.

Gambhira: A dramatic narrative folk performance traditionally utilizing a grand-uncle and grandson dialogue to blend satire and social commentary.

Nishir Daak: A phantom nighttime voice calling out a person’s name; answering it is believed to lead to disappearance.

Aleya: Mysterious, flickering ghost lights seen over marshes, believed to be the wandering souls of the drowned.

Khona’s Sayings: Traditional agricultural and meteorological rhyming couplets by the legendary female astronomer Khona, still used to guide crop planting.

Thakumar Jhuli: The classic compilation of grandmother's fairy tales filled with magic, talking animals, and mythical creatures collected by Dakshinaranjan Mitra Majumdar.
