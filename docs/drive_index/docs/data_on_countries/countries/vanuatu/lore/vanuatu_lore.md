Lisepsep: Small, elf-like or wild humanoid creatures in the folklore of islands like Malekula, known for living deep in the forests and interacting mysteriously with humans.

Tamate: Ancestral and forest spirits in northern Vanuatu who command deep respect and govern sacred or taboo places.

Local Land & Sea Spirits: Entities inhabiting volcanoes, the ocean, and deep caves, requiring rituals (such as laying green branches) to prevent misfortune or appease natural forces.

The Origin of Kava: A northern legend detailing how the sacred pepper plant sprouted from the grave of a slain sister, turning grief into a soothing, unifying ritual drink.

The First Sandalwood Tree: A tale of a persecuted maiden whose spirit transformed into a fragrant sandalwood tree, letting her gentle voice and resilience carry on the tropical breeze.

The Great Deluge: Flood myths tied to local geography, such as Qat escaping a rising flood in a canoe filled with all living creatures.

Modern "Kastom" and Cargo Cults: Twentieth-century traditions like the John Frum movement on Tanna island, which blend ancient mountain-spirit beliefs with historical encounters with Western military presence.
