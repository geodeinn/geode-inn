Sassuma Arnaa (Mother of the Sea): The most famous figure in Greenlandic mythology. She controls all sea creatures. When humans are greedy or break social taboos, human indifference dirties the water and tangles her hair, trapping the animals. The shaman must then travel to the ocean floor to comb her hair and restore the food supply.

Arsarnerit (The Northern Lights): Rather than just a scientific phenomenon, the Aurora Borealis is believed to be the souls of the dead playing a heavenly game of soccer.

Nanook: The revered polar bear god who tests the skill and respect of hunters in the Arctic.

Qivittoq: A tragic figure of a human who, out of shame, anger, or social banishment, exiles themselves into the wilderness. They are said to survive the harsh cold and roam the mountains as vengeful, magical hermits.

Tupilak: A terrifying, malevolent monster that can be physically crafted by a shaman using animal bones, hair, and sinew. It was used as a tool of dark magic to exact revenge upon enemies, though it required immense risk to create.
