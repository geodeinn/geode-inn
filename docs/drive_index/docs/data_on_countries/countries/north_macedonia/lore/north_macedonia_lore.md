Samovila (Fairies): Wild, beautiful spirits living in mountains and forests who guard nature.

Vampir / Talasam: Restless spirits or shadow creatures believed to haunt houses or rural areas.

Fates (Sudenici): Three mystical women who appear at a newborn's birth to decide their destiny.

Zmej (Dragons): Powerful protectors of villages, often fighting evil storm demons.

Oro (Circle Dance): Traditional dance performed at weddings and festivals in a joined circle to show community unity.

Life Cycle Rites: Deeply rooted folk practices surrounding birth, protective marriage rituals, and structured funeral traditions.

Seasonal Festivals: Winter and spring masquerades, bonfires, and weather-divination songs tied to the agrarian calendar.
