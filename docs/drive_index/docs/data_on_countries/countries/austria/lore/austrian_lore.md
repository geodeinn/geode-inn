Krampus: A horned, half-demon winter beast who accompanies Saint Nicholas in early December to punish misbehaving children.

Frau Perchta: An ancient winter goddess or-jugment spirit who rewards the diligent and famously slits the bellies of the lazy or untidy.

Perchten: Wild, masked animalistic spirits that parade through Alpine towns during midwinter to chase away dark and cold energies.

Tatzelwurm: A mythical serpent-like creature with a cat-like face rumored to live in alpine crevices.

The Lindwurm of Klagenfurt: A founding legend where brave locals used a bull as bait to defeat a swamp-dwelling dragon.

The Basilisk of Vienna: A deadly beast born from a rooster's egg that turned people to stone until a clever apprentice neutralized it with a mirror.

Most users on Reddit agree that the legends of the sleeping emperors inside the Untersberg mountain—waiting to wake for a final battle—are among the most captivating pieces of regional lore.
