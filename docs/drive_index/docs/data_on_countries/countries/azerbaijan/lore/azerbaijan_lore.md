Book of Dede Korkut: An ancient Oghuz Turkic epic centered around wise storytelling, tribal honor, and heroic battles.

Epic of Koroghlu: A legendary dastan about a blind hero turned rebel fighting against unjust rulers and tyrants.

Oguzname: Historical-heroic narratives outlining the roots and adventures of ancient Turkic tribes.

Jirtdan: A tiny yet clever boy who uses his sharp mind to outwit monsters.

Tepegöz: A terrifying, one-eyed giant reminiscent of classical cyclops legends.

Simurgh: A giant, wise, and benevolent mythical bird that assists heroes in critical times.

Meşə Adam / Ağac Kişi: The "Tree Man" or forest spirit said to inhabit mountainous woods.

Qulyabani: A desert or graveyard-dwelling monster with eerie attributes.

Bayati: Four-line lyrical and philosophical poems following a strict syllable and rhyme scheme.

Ashughs: Traditional bard-poets who play stringed instruments like the saz and sing dastans and folk songs.

Ceremonial Songs: Seasonal dramatic performances and rituals like Kosa-Kosa, Godu-Godu, and Novruz greetings.
