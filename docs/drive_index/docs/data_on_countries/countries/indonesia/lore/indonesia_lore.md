Malin Kundang (West Sumatra): The ultimate cautionary tale about an ungrateful son who becomes wealthy overseas but returns home and denies his impoverished mother. Heartbroken, she curses him, turning him and his ship into stone.

Legenda Danau Toba (North Sumatra): Tells the story of a man who breaks his promise never to reveal that his wife was once a magical fish. His broken vow causes a massive flood that creates Lake Toba, with Samosir Island in the center.

Sangkuriang & Tangkuban Perahu (West Java): A tragic Oedipus-like tale of a young man who falls in love with a beautiful woman, unaware she is his ageless mother. When he tries to build a lake and a giant boat overnight to win her hand, she tricks him into failing, which results in the upturned boat shape of Mount Tangkuban Perahu.

Roro Jonggrang (Central Java): A princess stalls a marriage proposal from a powerful, giant prince by challenging him to build 1,000 temples in a single night. She tricks the spirits into ceasing their work at 999 temples, and in his rage, he curses her into stone (the statue in the Prambanan temple complex).

Timun Mas (Central Java): A brave young girl uses magical items (cucumber seeds, needles, and salt) to escape a ravenous, green giant (Buto Ijo) who wants to eat her.

Keong Mas (Golden Snail) (East Java): A cursed princess is transformed into a golden snail, but eventually uses her divine cooking skills to reveal her identity and reunite with her true love.

Bawang Merah dan Bawang Putih (Riau): The Indonesian equivalent of Cinderella, contrasting a greedy, lazy step-sister (Bawang Merah) with a kind, diligent step-sister (Bawang Putih).

Manik Angkeran & Naga Besukih (Bali): A young man goes to a holy mountain dragon to steal gold to pay off his gambling debts. When he cuts off the dragon's tail, he burns to death. The dragon later saves him but creates the strait that separates Bali and Java.

Si Kancil (National): The beloved, cunning mouse-deer (Kancil) who outwits larger, more dangerous jungle predators like crocodiles and tigers using his brain rather than brawn.

Nyi Roro Kidul (Southern Sea Queen): A highly revered, mystical spirit who rules the turbulent Indian Ocean and protects the Javanese royal kingdoms.
