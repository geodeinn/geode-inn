The Clever Hare: Often portrayed as a trickster who uses his wits to outsmart larger, more dangerous animals (similar to the character known as Kalulu in Chichewa).

Spirits and Deities: Stories frequently center around Chiuta, the supreme deity associated with rain and the sky, as well as ancestral spirits believed to reside in mountains, trees, and sacred stones.

Mythical Creatures: Traditional oral history includes stories of unique creatures, such as the Limba (a crested snake that crows) and the Nguluka (a beast with a snake's head on a guineafowl's body).

Folktales in Malawi are not just spoken; they are deeply tied to performance arts. Traditional dances are deeply intertwined with local folklore, acting as a visual storytelling medium and a connection to the spiritual realm.
