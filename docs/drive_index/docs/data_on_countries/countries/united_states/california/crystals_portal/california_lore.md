The Dark Watchers (Big Sur)

Dating back to Spanish colonial times and noted by Indigenous communities, these mysterious, towering (7 to 10 feet tall) shadowy figures are seen in the late afternoon standing on mountain ridges silently observing travelers.

Lake Monsters and Cryptids

Tahoe Tessie: California’s version of the Loch Ness monster, Tessie is said to be a 30- to 60-foot serpentine creature living in the deep, underwater caves of Lake Tahoe.

Elsie of Lake Elsinore: A scaled, long-necked beast that locals and Indigenous observers claim roams the waters of this Southern California lake.

Fresno Nightcrawler: Described as a bizarre, cartoonish pair of white, wide-leg pants with no torso, this stumbling cryptid was famously captured on security cameras in 2007 in Fresno and later in Yosemite.
