Appalachian tall tales. Notable legends include Altie (the Altamaha-ha), a 20-foot river monster haunting the Altamaha River, and Fairy Crosses (staurolite crystals) in Fannin County that supposedly bring good luck.

Distinct Regional Folklore

Coastal & Gullah Culture: Rooted deeply in West African heritage, the Georgia coast features the distinct Gullah language, antiphonal gospel singing, and Hoodoo (conjure) folk magic. Ghost tours in Savannah frequently pull from this dark, colonial history.

The Appalachian Foothills: Historically shared around firesides and "liars' benches," these tales feature ghost stories, folk medicine, and humorous yarns.

Urban Legends: Other localized phenomena include the "Witch of Bonaire," where a local Gravity Hill supposedly pulls cars uphill in neutral due to a buried witch.
