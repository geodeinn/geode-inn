Ohio folklore blends deep Native American history, frontier myths, and spooky modern cryptids. Discover legends from the Adena culture's Great Serpent Mound in Adams County to Doylestown's Rogue's Hollow, a former mining hub known for Ohio's headless horseman.

Ohio Cryptids & Monsters

The Loveland Frogman: A four-foot-tall, bipedal frog-like creature spotted near the Little Miami River.

Lake Erie Monster (Bessie): Tales of a colossal, horned serpent residing in the waters of Lake Erie.

The Charles Mill Monster: A shadowy aquatic cryptid said to inhabit the reservoir waters near Mansfield.

Haunted History & Ghost Towns

Utopia: A short-lived 19th-century spiritualist and anarchist commune along the Ohio River in Clermont County. Its chapel ruins are now a popular spot for ghost hunters.

101 E. Main Street (Mason): This historic building is the site of a gruesome 1901 unsolved murder. Ongoing sightings of apparitions and disembodied sounds are still witnessed on the property.

Historic & Supernatural Legends

Johnny Appleseed: The historical figure John Chapman traversed Ohio planting apple seeds, becoming a folk hero celebrated for his woodland expertise and barefoot travels.

The Lincoln Funeral Train: Folklore suggests the ghost train of assassinated President Abraham Lincoln haunts Urbana. According to myth, when the train passes, clocks and watches stop ticking.
