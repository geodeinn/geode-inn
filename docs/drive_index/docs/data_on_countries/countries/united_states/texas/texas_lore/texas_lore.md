The Marfa Lights: Strange, dancing, glowing orbs occasionally appear on the horizon outside of Marfa, Texas

The Donkey Lady of San Antonio: A cautionary tale of a half-woman, half-donkey creature said to haunt a bridge just south of San Antonio, often linked to the tragic loss of her family.

La Lechuza: Originating from Hispanic and Tejano folklore, this terrifying creature is a vengeful witch capable of transforming into a massive, 7-foot-tall white owl that mimics the sound of a crying baby to lure unsuspecting victims at night.

The Chupacabra (Cuero)

Old Rip the Horned Toad: Perhaps Texas's most famous "living" legend, Old Rip was a horned lizard sealed inside a time capsule in the Eastland County courthouse cornerstone in 1897, and emerged alive 31 years later.

The Screaming Bridge / Goatman (Old Alton Bridge): Located between Denton and Lewisville, this historic iron bridge is famous for the legend of Oscar Washburn, a Black goat farmer who was lynched in the 1930s. His spirit is said to haunt the bridge as a menacing goat-man hybrid.

The Beer King (Marble Falls): A localized folklore tale from 1901 detailing a "bear king" (a hairy, foul-smelling, half-man/half-bear) that supposedly kidnapped a woman and held her captive in a cave, a legend popularized by the Kikapoo people.
