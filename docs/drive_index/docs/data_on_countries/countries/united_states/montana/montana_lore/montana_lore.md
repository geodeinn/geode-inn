Monsters and Cryptids

Flathead Lake Monster ("Flessie"): Said to inhabit the depths of Flathead Lake in northwestern Montana, this 40-foot, eel-like creature has been reported by locals for over a century, tracing its origins to Kutenai tribal legends.

Shunka Warak'in: A massive, high-shouldered, and downward-sloping canine beast that Native American folklore says used to sneak into camps to hunt dogs.

The Little People of the Pryor Mountains: According to Crow Tribe legend, these ferocious yet spiritually wise dwarfs live in the jagged peaks of the Pryor Mountains. They famously imparted visions and shaped the destiny of the iconic Crow Chief Plenty Coups.

Haunted Locations & Urban Legends

Sacrifice Cliff

A tragic 19th-century Crow legend tells the story of tribal members leaping to their death over a cliff in grief or to sacrifice themselves after smallpox decimated their families.

Garnet Ghost Town

This real, abandoned gold rush town east of Missoula is the subject of numerous phantom sightings. Visitors and historians have claimed to see the spirit of the old general store owner, Frank Davy, glaring from the ice house doorway at dusk.

Airport Road Ghost Jogger

In Billings, drivers have long reported seeing a spectral runner wearing coyote pelts dashing along the roadside at night, thought to be the ghost of a man hit by a truck.
