The Mercy Brown Vampire Incident (Exeter)

In 1892, Exeter resident Mercy Brown died of tuberculosis. As other family members grew ill, townsfolk believed the dead were draining the living. Her body was exhumed, and her heart was burned to stop the alleged "vampirism". Her grave at the Chestnut Hill Cemetery remains a popular stop for ghost tourists.

Cryptids and Monsters

The Glower Ghoul (Glocester): Reports of an ape-like cryptid roaming northwestern Rhode Island's forests.

Big Rhodey: The state's local Bigfoot, with sightings allegedly dating as far back as 1765.

The Block Island Ghost Ship: A phantom vessel said to periodically appear as a burning ship off the coast, tracing back to colonial lore.

Local Superstitions and Ghosts

Providence Athenaeum Well: Legend says that if you drink from the 1873 fountain outside the library, you may leave the state but will always return. Edgar Allan Poe’s ghost is also rumored to haunt the building.

The Witch Tree (Burrillville): Located at Log and Mann School Roads, this site claims to trap a local witch's spirit. Driving around it three times is said to summon a phantom following you home.

Campus Hauntings: Multiple historic universities, including Brown University, RISD, and Providence College, feature lore about haunted dormitories and spectral students.
