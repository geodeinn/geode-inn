Cryptids & Monsters

Bigfoot: Oregon is a global epicenter for Bigfoot (Sasquatch) lore. The Pacific Northwest forests—particularly the remote areas around the Cascade Range and the Coast Range—are the most heavily reported regions for sightings.

Wallowa Lake Monster: Eastern Oregon's Wallowa Lake is home to "Wally," a creature described since 1885 as a 100-foot-long beast with a hippo-like head.

Colossal Claude: A 40-foot serpent-like monster historically spotted by sailors and locals at the mouth of the Columbia River.

The Agropelter: A mischievous, long-limbed creature from lumberjack folklore that hurls heavy tree branches at woodsmen.
