  Peris and Fairies: Benevolent or capricious winged fairy entities from northern regions like Chitral, where mountains like Terichmir are believed to hide fairy kingdoms. [1, 2]

  Azhdaar: Huge serpentine dragons with raptor wings, fish mouths, and golden manes said to fiercely guard hidden mountain treasures. [1]

  Jach and Varoti: Indigenous earth and soil guardian spirits, with Suchi mountain fairies aiding locals and Varoti representing their fierce male counterparts.
