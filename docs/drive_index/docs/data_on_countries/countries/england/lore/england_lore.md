East Anglia: Home to Black Shuck—a massive, red-eyed spectral dog that foretells death—and the Green Children, who mysteriously appeared in the 12th century speaking an unknown language.

The Midlands: The stomping ground of outlaw Robin Hood, who took from the rich and gave to the poor in Sherwood Forest.

The South West: Cornwall and Devon are steeped in magic, featuring tales of mischievous Pixies and ancient legends of King Arthur at Tintagel Castle.

The North: Features terrifying water hags like Jenny Greenteeth and the destructive Lambton Worm dragon (County Durham).

London & The South East: Protected by the legendary giants Gog and Magog, and haunted by forest spirits like Herne the Hunter in Windsor.
