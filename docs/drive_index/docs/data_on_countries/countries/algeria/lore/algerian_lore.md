The Ghoul (or Ogress): A classic shape-shifting monster of North African folklore, often portrayed as a blind or trickster creature inhabiting remote areas.

Errouhbani: Forest-dwelling shape-shifter spirits in Berber folklore known for playing pranks on humans.

Bouberrak: A traditional folkloric entity associated with sleep paralysis and heavy pressure on the chest.

The Blacksmith’s Daughter: A famous Berber-infused traditional tale where a humble smith's daughter marries a prince, gives birth to celestial-marked children, and overcomes palace jealousy.

The Seven Brothers and Their Sister (Yasmina): A tale of immense sacrifice and disciplined silence where a sister must weave shirts out of stinging nettles without speaking a word to break a bird-curse on her brothers.

The Blue Jackal: A popular animal fable about a jackal who dyes himself blue, tricks the other animals into obeying him, but ultimately falls to arrogance when exposure to rain washes away his disguise.
