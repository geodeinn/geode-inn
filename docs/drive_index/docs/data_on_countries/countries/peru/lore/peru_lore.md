Viracocha: The great creator deity who shaped the universe, sun, and moon from Lake Titicaca.

Manco Capac and Mama Ocllo: The first Inca emperor and queen sent by the sun god Inti to civilize humanity and found Cusco.

Pachamama: Mother Earth, revered through traditional offerings (pagos) for agricultural fertility and protection.

The Apus: Sacred mountain spirits believed to watch over Andean communities.

Chullachaqui: A mischievous jungle dwarf with one animal foot who mimics loved ones to lead travelers astray.

El Tunche: A terrifying forest phantom in the Amazon that hypnotizes victims with a chilling whistle.

Jarjacha: A cursed Andean hybrid monster born from severe sins like incest.

El Muki: A tiny cave goblin known to miners in the central Andes.
