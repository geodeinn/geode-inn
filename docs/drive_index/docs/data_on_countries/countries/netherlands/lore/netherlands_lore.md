Witte Wieven: White-veiled female spirits of the mist who roam ancient burial mounds, tied to prophecy and eerie warning.

Bullebak: A malevolent water monster lurking in canals and rivers, historically used to warn children away from dangerous deep edges.

Blauwe Gerrit: A blue nocturnal phantom from the Veluwe region that leaps onto night travelers' backs, growing heavy until they collapse.

Kabouters: Small, gnome-like earth creatures from Dutch lore who assist with farm tasks or guard hidden bells.

The Lady of Stavoren (Het Vrouwtje van Stavoren): A rich merchant's proud daughter who demanded the world's most precious treasure, lost everything to arrogance, and left her city ruined by a grain bar blockage.

The Hero of Haarlem: The fictionalized tale (popularized for international audiences) of Hans Brinker putting his finger in a leaking dike to save his town.

The Giants Ellert and Brammert: Two fierce highwaymen giants from the Drenthe peat moors who robbed and terrorized travelers.
