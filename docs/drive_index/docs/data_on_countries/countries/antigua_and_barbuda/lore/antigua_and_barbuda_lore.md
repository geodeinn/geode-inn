Nancy and Toukouma: Popular West African-influenced trickster characters central to traditional Antiguan oral storytelling.

Jumbies: Mischievous or malevolent spirits rooted in animistic beliefs that overlap with traditional Obeah practices.

Obeah: An outlawed yet historically influential system of herbal medicine, spiritual protection, and curse-casting.

Devil's Bridge

A natural limestone arch where local lore says enslaved people leaped to their deaths to escape bondage, inspiring tales of dark forces.

Boggy Peak

The highest point in the Shekerley Mountains, historically fabled as the lair of a mountain boogeyman targeting runaways.

Coal-pots: Traditional handmade clay stoves around which families historically gathered at sunrise and dusk to share folktales and ghost stories.

Folk Wisdom: Enduring maxims such as "mout open tory jump out" (speaking carelessly leads to trouble) passed down across generations.
