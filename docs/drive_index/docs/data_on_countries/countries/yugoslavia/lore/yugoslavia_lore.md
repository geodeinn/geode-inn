Vile: Nymphs or fairies living in forests and near water, often depicted dancing in white dresses.

Baba Roga: A mythical hag or witch figure traditionally used to frighten misbehaving children.

Vuk (Wolf): A symbolic protective name historically given to firstborn sons to ward off evil spirits.

Gusle: A traditional single-stringed bowed instrument used by bards to recount historical heroic epics.

Kolo: A communal circle dance performed at weddings and festivals, accompanied by accordions, flutes (frula), or bagpipes (gajde).
