Orature: Stories are passed down by voice through generations, often shared at night by the fire with drums, music, and dance.

Krio Folklore: Includes collections of localized fables, proverbs, and witty neighborhood legends (such as the historic church bell rivalry between Wilberforce and Murray Town).

Animal Tricksters: Characters like the greedy or clever tortoise (Àjàpá) and spider (Anansi) teach moral lessons through outsmarting bigger foes.

Creation and Nature: Mende and other ethnic groups pass down foundational myths about human emergence from the earth or water, with staple symbols like the palm tree and rice holding high status in daily folklore.
