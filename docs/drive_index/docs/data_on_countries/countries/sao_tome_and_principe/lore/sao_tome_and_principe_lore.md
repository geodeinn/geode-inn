Tchiloli: A dramatic dance-theater unique to São Tomé that ts out a 16th-century European tragicomedy by Baltazar Dias about the Marquis of Mantua and Emperor Charlemagne, accompanied by local music, flutes, and percussion. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]

Auto de Floripes: Celebrated annually every August on the island of Príncipe, this traditional street theater re-enacts medieval conflicts between Christians and Moors, centered on Emperor Charlemagne and Floripes.

Danço Congo: A traditional performance deeply rooted in African heritage featuring rhythm, dance, and movement styles reflecting historical resistance and cultural memory.

Why Dogs Don't Talk: A moral legend about a talking dog that helped its owner, only to grow permanently silent out of offense after the owner leaked its secret.

The Clever Tortoise: A trickster tale where a tortoise outwits others and wins the king's daughter by proving universal truths about hunger in animals.

The Singing Roosters: Folklore describing an island once filled with joyously crowing roosters whose continuous noise led to legendary community conflicts.

Healing Cults and Fetishes: Traditional spiritual practices utilize specific trees, plots of land, iron shards, red cloth, and carved wooden dolls placed at roots to ward off spirits or invoke protection.
