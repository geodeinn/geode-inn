The Nibelungenlied: A monumental 13th-century epic poem. It follows the hero Siegfried, his slaying of a dragon, and the tragic vengeance of his wife Kriemhild. It blends heroic action with themes of betrayal and greed.

The Song of Hildebrand: One of the oldest surviving pieces of Germanic literature. It tells the tragic tale of an aging father and son who are forced to fight each other in single combat because neither can refuse a challenge without losing his honor.

Wayland the Smith: An ancient myth of a magically skilled, enslaved blacksmith who takes brutal vengeance on a greedy king, forging wings from feathers and jewels to escape captivity.

Hansel and Gretel: A tale of two siblings abandoned in the woods who must outsmart a wicked witch in a gingerbread house.

Rapunzel: The story of a captive girl with impossibly long hair, locked in a high tower without stairs.

The Seven Ravens: A classic story where a father's hasty curse transforms his sons into ravens, and their sister journeys to the edge of the world to break it.

The Lorelei: A beautiful siren who sits on a steep, treacherous rock along the Rhine River. Her haunting singing distracts passing sailors, causing their ships to crash into the jagged stones.

The Pied Piper of Hamelin: A legend from 1284. A colorful piper is hired to rid the town of Hamelin of a rat infestation using his magical flute. When the greedy town refuses to pay him, he lures away the town's children as revenge.

Krampus: A horned, demonic-looking figure from Alpine folklore who accompanies St. Nicholas during the Christmas season. While St. Nicholas rewards good children, Krampus punishes the misbehaving ones.

Barbarossa: A sleeping emperor (Frederick I) legend, who rests in a cavern inside the Kyffhäuser Mountains. His red beard has supposedly grown through the stone table he sits at, and he will awake to save Germany in its darkest hour.

The Wolpertinger: A famous German cryptid from the Bavarian Alps, described as a mythical hybrid animal with the body of a rabbit, the antlers of a deer, and the wings of a bird.
