Four Branches of the Mabinogi: Medieval prose tales detailing heroes like Pryderi, Rhiannon, and Bran the Blessed.

Math fab Mathonwy: The story of the powerful wizard Gwydion and his nephew Lleu Llaw Gyffes.

Culhwch and Olwen: One of the oldest Arthurian tales featuring a massive hunt for the wild boar Twrch Trwyth.

The Welsh Triads: Short groups of three items used by bards to preserve tribal history, heroes, and ancient lore.

Tylwyth Teg: The fair folk or underground and underwater fairies known to swap human babes for changelings.

Afanc: A fierce lake monster resembling a giant beaver or crocodile.

Cŵn Annwn: Spectral hounds of the Otherworld whose booming barks signal upcoming death.

Ceffyl Dŵr: A shape-shifting, flying water-horse spirit.

Coblynau: Small, gnome-like miners who guide humans to rich ore veins or cause rockfalls.

Y Ddraig Goch: The red dragon of Wales said to reside at Dinas Emrys, battling the white dragon.

Cantre'r Gwaelod: The legendary sunken "Welsh Atlantis" lost to the sea due to drunken negligence.

Beddgelert: The touching and tragic grave site of a faithful hound belonging to Prince Llywelyn.
