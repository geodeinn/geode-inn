Yoruba Cosmology: Centers on Ile-Ife as the cradle of creation, featuring deities (Orishas) like Obatala (shaper of humanity), Shango (the god of thunder), and Oshun (goddess of river and fertility).

Igbo Mythology: Focuses on Chukwu (the supreme creator) and Ala (the powerful Earth goddess of morality and fertility), often utilizing rich proverb cycles and folk wisdom.

Hausa Epics: Comprises northern legends and historical myths, such as the heroic foundation story of the Queen of Daura.
