Kelpie: A shape-shifting water horse in lochs and rivers that lures riders onto its back before dragging them down to drown and eat them.

Selkie: Seals from the Orkney and Shetland Islands that can shed their skin to become human on land, often tied to tragic tales of captured human spouses.

Blue Men of the Minch: Malevolent storm-raising spirits inhabiting the water strait between the Outer Hebrides and the mainland.

Loch Ness Monster (Nessie): The world-famous aquatic beast said to inhabit the deep freshwater loch in the Scottish Highlands.

Brownie: A helpful household goblin or spirit that performs farm and house chores at night in exchange for a simple bowl of cream.

Bean Nighe: The "washerwoman of fate," a spectral harbinger seen at riverbanks washing the bloody clothes of those about to die.

Cailleach: The divine ancient hag and winter goddess associated with weather, creation of mountains, and the sovereignty of the land.

Trow: Underground Norse-derived troll figures prominent in Shetland and Orkney folklore that emerge at night.
