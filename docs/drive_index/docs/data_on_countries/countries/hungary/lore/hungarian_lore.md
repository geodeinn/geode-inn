Central to ancient Hungarian belief is the Táltos, a person born with extra teeth or bones who possesses supernatural healing, the ability to read minds, and the power to project their soul into the spirit realm (révülés). Another central pillar is the Turul bird, a giant raptor that appeared in a dream to Emese, prophesying that her descendants would become kings. Other notable creatures include:

Garabonciás: A wandering wizard who commands storms and rides dragons (Sárkány).

Szépasszony: The "beautiful lady" who dances in storms and lures young men away.

Mumus: The Hungarian equivalent of the boogeyman, used to frighten misbehaving children.

Hétmérföldes csizma: Seven-league boots used to cross the flat plains (Alföld) in a single step.

Tűzmadár: The firebird, which brings light, magic, and healing feathers.

Bűvös malom: A magical mill that continuously grinds out food, wealth, or even salt.
