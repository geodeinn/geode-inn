ythical Creatures and Beings

Mouras Encantadas: Enchanted Moorish maidens left behind by Moorish lords with spells; they guard hidden treasures in wells, caves, and rivers while combing their golden hair.

Piras: Northern creature-myths of wolf-fairies or shapeshifting beings with deep ties to nature and olf packs.

João Pestana: A gentle dream-lord figure who sprinkles magic dust on children's eyes to help them sp.

Coca: A dragon or ghost figure used in regional folklore to frighten or quiet misbehaving children.

The Cockerel of Barcelos (Galo de Barcelos): A miraculous rooster that crowed to save a falsely accused pilgrim from hanging, now a national symbol of faith and luck.

Dom Sebastião: The young Portuguese king who vanished at the 1578 Battle of Alcácer Quibir,-beloved by myth to return in a fog to save the nation.

Viriathus: The legendary Lusitanian shepherd-turned-warrior who led successful tribal resistances against the Roman legions.
