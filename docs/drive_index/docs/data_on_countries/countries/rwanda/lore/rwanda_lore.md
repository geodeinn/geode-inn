Imana: The supreme, monotheistic creator god who shapes life and nature.

Abatangana: Primordial giants created by Imana to shape the rivers, lakes, and hills of the landscape.

Inzoka: Malevolent snake-like water spirits believed to cause droughts or floods.

Ryangombe and the Imandwa: A pantheon of around 40 revered ancestral and heroic spirits worshipped and consulted through the traditional Kubandwa mystic cult Kubandwa.

Gihanga: The legendary first king and founder of Rwanda credited with bringing fire, metalworking, and cattle Gihanga.

Nyirarucyaba: Gihanga's daughter, central to the origin myth of discovering and bringing milk cows to the kingdom.

Ruganzu II Ndoli: A historical 17th-century king whose real military triumphs grew into legendary tales of superhuman feats, such as leaving footprints in solid rock.

Oral Literature, Songs, and Taboos

Ibitekerezo: Epic royal poems detailing historical dynasties and heroic acts.
