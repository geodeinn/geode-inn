Strigoi and Moroi: Troubled undead spirits or living vampires that feed on energy or blood.

Iele and Sânziene: Capricious or benevolent fairy spirits associated with summer solstices and wildflowers.

Zmeu and Muma Pădurii: Shape-shifting dragon-villains and the fierce guardian witch of the deep woods (Wood Mother).

Mărțișor: A spring-welcoming tradition on March 1st where men gift women a red-and-white talisman for luck.

Călușari: An acrobatic, ritual healing men's dance performed around Pentecost to ward off fairy sickness.

Hora: A vibrant, unifying circle dance danced communally at weddings and village holidays.
