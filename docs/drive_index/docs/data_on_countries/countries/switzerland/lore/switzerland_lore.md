William Tell: A marksman forced to shoot an apple off his son's head, sparking a revolt for Swiss freedom.

Teufelsbrücke (Devil's Bridge): A legend where the devil built a bridge across the Schöllenen Gorge in exchange for the first soul to cross it.

Mount Pilatus Dragons: Stories of healing dragons and stone-turned rulers residing high above Lucerne.

Barbegazi: Benevolent white dwarf-like creatures with giant feet that surf avalanches and help lost travelers.

Sennentuntschi: A straw doll brought to life by lonely alpine herdsmen in dark mountain folklore.

Kobolds & Dwarfs: Earth-men who taught farmers herbal medicine, cheese-making, and rifle-making secrets.

Tschäggättä: Wild carnival figures from the Lötschental region wearing wooden masks and animal pelts.

Schmutzli: St. Nicholas’s dark companion in Swiss tradition who admonishes misbehaving children.

Vogel Gryff: A Basel mid-winter ritual featuring a wild man on a raft crossing the Rhine.
