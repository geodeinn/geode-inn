Sundiata Keita: Celebrated as the "Lion King" and founder of the 13th-century Mali Empire.

Sogolon: Known in myth as the "buffalo woman" and Sundiata's resilient mother.

The Griots (Jelis): Oral historians and musicians who preserve and chant this national history across generations.

The Nommo: Amphibious, fish-like ancestral spirits who descended from the sky in a vessel of fire and thunder to shape human life.

Sirius Connection: Traditional cosmology includes complex celestial narratives regarding the star Sirius.

The Antelope (Chi Wara): Bambara legends credit a mythical antelope with teaching humans the secrets of agriculture.

Trickster Tales: Stories featuring clever animals like hares and monkeys outwitting powerful adversaries to teach moral lessons.
