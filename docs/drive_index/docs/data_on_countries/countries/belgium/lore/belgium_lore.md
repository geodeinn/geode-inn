Druon Antigoon & Brabo: An Antwerp giant who cut off the hands of sailors who did not pay tolls; slain by Silvius Brabo, who threw the giant's hand into the river (supposedly naming the city Hand-werpen or Antwerp).

Lange Wapper: A mischievous tall folkloric trickster from Antwerp who could stretch his body to peer into high windows.

Kabouter & Nutons: Traditional Flemish gnomes and underground earth spirits known for smithing, building, and occasional secret help or greed.

Kludde & Osschaert: Menacing shapeshifting water or night demons in Flemish and Walloon tradition that took the form of dark, terrifying horses or black dogs.

Carnival of Binche: Celebrated near Shrove Tuesday featuring masked Gilles wearing red, black, and yellow and throwing oranges to crowds.

Ducasse of Ath: A historic medieval summer celebration featuring large processional giants like Goliath (Gouyasse) fighting a mock battle with a shepherd David.

Ommegang of Brussels: A grand historical pageant in July commemorating the 1549 joyful entry of Holy Roman Emperor Charles V.
