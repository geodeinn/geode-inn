Fatma the Beautiful and the Ogre: A classic story of a brave girl using her wits to escape a dangerous ghoul.

Makkar the Fox: A playful animal fable about a sly fox sneaking into a farmer's watermelon patch.

Tajouj and Mahalliq: A legendary tragic romance exploring loyalty and societal rules.
