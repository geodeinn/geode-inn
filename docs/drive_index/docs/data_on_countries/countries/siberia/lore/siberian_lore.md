Ulgen: The supreme creator god and bringer of light and thunder in many Turkic-Siberian traditions.

Erlik: The lord of the underworld and main adversary or brother to Ulgen, ruling over darkness and disease.

Kutkh: A giant raven featured widely as a prominent trickster and culture hero who shaped the world in far-eastern Siberian lore.

The Abbasi: Terrific, monstrous underworld bullies in Yakut tradition that plague the living with sickness.

Shamans (Kamnans): Acted as spiritual mediators, healers, and psychopomps capable of traveling between upper, middle, and lower cosmic realms.

Animal Ancestry: Animals such as bears, elk, and wolves are frequently viewed as sacred ancestors, spouses, or shapeshifting guides to humans.
