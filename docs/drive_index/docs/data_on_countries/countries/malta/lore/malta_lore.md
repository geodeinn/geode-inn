The Ggantija Temples: Megalithic structures on Gozo are said to have been built by a race of giants. Legend specifically names a giantess called Sansuna, who allegedly carried massive boulders on her shoulders while living on broad beans and honey. [1, 2]

Il-Maqluba Sinkhole: This massive 300-meter perimeter sinkhole in Qrendi is said to have formed in a single night when God punished a sinful village by opening the earth and swallowing it whole, sparing only one pious woman.

The Headless Bride of Mdina: The ghost of Katerina is said to roam the quiet night streets of Mdina, reminding people of a tragic historical execution.

The Blue Lady of Verdala Palace: Known as Is-Sinjura tal-Verdala, this spirit allegedly haunts the palace after falling to her death from a high window while trying to escape imprisonment.

Il-Belliegha: A terrifying well-monster described as an eel-like creature with a fish face that snatched children who stood too close to water well ledges.

The Luzzu and the Eye of Horus: Traditional brightly painted fishing boats feature the protective eye of Osiris or Horus on their prows to ward off evil spirits, a direct legacy of ancient Phoenician maritime culture.

Il-Mara tal-Għar: A mythical cave-dwelling guardian woman of the sea who rewards humble fishermen and tests those out in coastal storms.
