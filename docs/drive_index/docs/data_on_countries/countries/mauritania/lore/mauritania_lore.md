The Ighyuwn: Traditional entertainer caste functioning as singers, poets, clowns, and social historians.

Occult Reputation: Historically feared and respected as keepers of secret knowledge and hidden powers.

Fables and Proverbs: Short moral fables passed down through generations in the Hassaniya dialect.

Whispering Dunes: Desert legends warn travelers of phantom voices and shifting sands that lead the proud or impatient astray.

The Jinn: Invisible desert spirits believed to play tricks on lone voyagers crossing the vast Sahara.

Azawan: A unique traditional musical system said to draw aesthetic inspiration from the calls of desert birds.

Folk Instruments: Performance centered around the tidinit (a traditional Moorish guitar) and the ardin (a harp-like instrument).

The Tea Ritual: The symbolic three-pour tea ceremony (atay) embodying hospitality, patience, and community connection.
