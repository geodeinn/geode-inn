Vodník: A malevolent water goblin dwelling in deep rivers and lakes who drowns unsuspecting swimmers and traps their souls in small jars.

Grgolica: A colossal, wild-haired forest giantess from regional lore said to ambush travelers who mimic her eerie woodland whistles.

Juraj Jánošík: The historical 17th-century highwayman turned legendary folk hero, celebrated as the Slovak equivalent of Robin Hood for robbing the rich to give to the poor.

Fujara and Instruments: A massive overblown overtone flute originating from Detva, recognized as a UNESCO Masterpiece of the Oral and Intangible Heritage of Humanity.

Kroj: Intricately detailed, brightly colored traditional folk garments and heavily embroidered lace unique to individual villages and valleys.

Architecture: Preserved living museum villages like Vlkolínec showcase traditional log-based folk architecture.

Východná Festival: The oldest and largest nationwide annual folklore celebration

Easter Dowsing: Ancient springtime fertility rituals involving young men splashing women with cold water and symbolic whipping for vitality.
