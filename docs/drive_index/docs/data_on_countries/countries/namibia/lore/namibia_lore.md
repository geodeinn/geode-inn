Omumborombonga Tree: The Herero people believe the first humans and cattle climbed out of the roots of this sacred leadwood tree, viewed as the "father of life". [1]

San Creation: San Bushmen folklore says God first created animals, then fashioned the first soft-skinned human from a springbuck.

Sun and Stars: San legends state the sun was once a man whose armpits shone light; children threw him into the sky, and his old blanket of darkness lets light peek through as stars.

Fairy Circles

Empty circles in the desert grass are attributed by Himba and local legends to the footprints of God, dancing fairies, or a sleeping underground dragon breathing fire.

Skeleton Coast

Dubbed "the land God made in anger," its stark shores inspire centuries-old stories of shipwrecks, ghost lights, and survival.
