Land of Eternity: Sumerian mythology revered Dilmun (modern-day Bahrain) as a pure, sacred paradise of the gods and the home of wisdom and water god Enki.

The Epic Connection: The hero Gilgamesh reportedly journeyed toward Dilmun seeking the secret to eternal life.

Twin Waters: The name Bahrain ("two seas") reflects the mingling of subterranean sweet water springs with salty gulf waters, mirroring creation myths.

Bu Draya: A fearsome deep-sea king or spirit of the waters who mimics drowning cries to lure unsuspecting sailors or causes disorientation and bad luck.

Dugong Origins: Local sea-cow legends inspired tales of mystic sea nymphs or maidens.

Fidjeri Music: An all-male choral genre created by pearl divers, utilizing rhythmic chanting and clapping to accompany dangerous tasks at sea.

Switchen: A mischievous or frightening jinn figure from local folklore said to hide behind palm tree trunks to scare misbehaving children.

The Tree of Life (Shajarat-al-Hayat): A 400-year-old tree standing alone in a barren desert without surface water, often surrounded by whispered local legends of resilient spirits.

Traditional Arts: Men’s sword dances like the Ardha accompanied by traditional drummers and regional poetry.
