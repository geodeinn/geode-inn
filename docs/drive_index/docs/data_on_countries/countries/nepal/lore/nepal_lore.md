Yeti: A giant, ape-like creature said to live in the snowy peaks of the Himalayas.

Ban Jhakri: A hairy forest spirit that traditional belief says abducts young children to teach them shamanism.

Kichkandi: The ghost of a woman who died an unfair death, appearing at night with long dark hair.

Lakhey: A fierce demon from Newar tradition who later reformed and is celebrated in masked street dances.
