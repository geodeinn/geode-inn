Zana e Malit: Mountain fairies known for their fierce beauty and aid to brave warriors.

Ora: Guardian spirits assigned to individuals, families, or entire tribes to dictate fate.

Kulshedra & Bolla: A serpentine demon that starts as a blind snake (bolla) and grows into a multi-headed storm-bringing dragon.

Drangue: Divine, winged human heroes born with celestial weapons who battle the Kulshedra to protect the earth.

Shtriga: A vampiric witch who takes the shape of a flying insect to steal life force from children at night.

Muji and Halili: Legendary brothers of the Kreshnikët warrior cycle who possess superhuman strength in northern Albanian epic songs.

Gjergj Elez Alia: A paralyzed knight who rises from his bed of nine years to defeat a monstrous invader (Katallan) and save his sister.

The Legend of Rozafa: A tragic walled-in sacrifice of a loyal mother built into the foundation of the Shkodra castle to keep the stone from falling.

Constantine and Doruntine: A chilling ballad where a dead brother rises from the grave to fulfill a solemn besa promise and bring his sister home.
