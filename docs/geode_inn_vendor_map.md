## The Silk Road Routes

The trade routes between platforms follow the mycorrhizal network — the forest's root connections. The routes are the city's streets. The Cartographer maps them. The Song and the Silk Road are the same network.

## Currency

- **Stones** for goods (the earth traded for the world — rarer stone = higher value)
- **Stories** for secrets (the Silk Road's deepest commerce — knowledge for knowledge)
