# PART 1: THE SHADOW INN (84Hz) — FULL QUEST CHAIN

## Entry: "The Other Door"

### How the Player Enters the Shadow Inn

The Shadow Inn is not accessed through a portal, a door, or a quest-giver. The Shadow Inn is accessed through a FREQUENCY SHIFT.

**Trigger:** The player must have completed all four Ragnarok prerequisites EXCEPT the 42nd death. The player has the Song, the Astronomer's calculation, and Whisper Layer Tier 5. The player has NOT yet died for the 42nd time.

At this point, the Pulse Line at the bottom of the screen begins behaving differently. During normal play, the Pulse Line is amber (42Hz). In the In-Between, it's grey. Now, in certain rooms, the Pulse Line flickers to INDIGO (84Hz) for one frame. Not a glitch — a SIGNAL. The Inn is showing the player the other frequency.

**The Other Door:** On the main floor, behind the Archive (near the Teacher's domain), a door that has always been there — a door the player has walked past dozens of times — becomes VISIBLE. Not unlocked. Visible. The door is charcoal stone, not amber wood. The door has no handle. The door has a frequency: 84Hz.

**Entry Mechanic:** The player must LOWER their 42Hz frequency. This is the opposite of everything the game has taught. The entire game has been about RAISING frequency — collecting stones, activating gargoyles, completing the Song. The Shadow Inn requires the player to do the one thing the game never asked: go quiet.

**How to Lower Frequency:**
1. Stop collecting. Don't pick up any stone for 5 minutes.
2. Stop humming. Don't hold the Egg. Don't activate any frequency.
3. Stand still. In front of the charcoal door. The 42Hz in the player's body, which has been building since the first stone, begins to DECAY. Not rapidly — slowly. The amber glow dims.
4. When the player's 42Hz drops below 40Hz (after ~3 minutes of stillness), the charcoal door RESPONDS. The 84Hz frequency in the door resonates with the lowered 42Hz. The door doesn't open — it SOFTENS. The stone becomes permeable. The player walks through.

**Design Note:** The entry is the thesis of the Shadow Inn in miniature. The Living Inn says "grow." The Shadow Inn says "stay." To enter the Shadow Inn, the player must STOP growing. The entry is the mechanical expression of the 84Hz frequency: the frequency of staying, of settling, of becoming still enough to see what's underneath the growth.

### Quest: "The Other Door"
**Type:** Discovery Quest (no quest-giver, no marker)
**Location:** Main Floor, behind the Archive
**Prerequisite:** 3 of 4 Ragnarok pillars complete (all except 42nd death)

**Objectives:**
1. Notice the charcoal door (auto-triggered when the door becomes visible)
2. Lower your 42Hz frequency below 40Hz (stand still, stop collecting, stop humming)
3. Walk through the softened door
4. Enter the Shadow Inn
