SHOP CONNECTION — STONE TO ZONE TO CREATURE MAPPING


Desert Bone → carnelian, turquoise, lapis lazuli, jasper (Sphinx's riddle stones)
Norse Zone → amber, runestone fragments, mead-hall quartz
Mediterranean → olive stones, labyrinth marble, Medusa's frozen fragments
Water Zone → sea glass, pearl, coral, the Pirate's salvaged stones
Fae Kingdom → labradorite, fluorite, rainbow moonstone, preseli blue stone (rare Fae stones)
National Parks → obsidian, ammolite, smoky quartz, peridot


Each real Soulstice Jewelry piece maps to a zone, a creature, and a character. The bestiary is the bridge: customer buys a labradorite pendant → discovers it's a Seelie Court stone → meets the creatures that guard it → enters the Inn.
