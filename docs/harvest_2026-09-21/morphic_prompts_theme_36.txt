### Prompt 1: Rome — Gangs of the Tiber


```
Slow tracking shot through an ancient Roman streetscape at golden hour. Not the marble ruins of modern Rome — the living city, ~300 BC. Tight brick-and-stone tenements lean over narrow cobstone alleyways. Laundry lines stretch between buildings. A street market buzzes with vendors selling amphorae, olive oil, and bronze tools. The Tiber glints in the distance. The aesthetic blends Dickens-on-the-Strand grit with Carnival Row fantasy — a multicultural Roman port city where humans and mythological beings share the street. A satyr pushes a cart. A lar (household spirit) flickers above a doorway. Warm amber and terracotta palette, dust motes in shafts of light, 35mm film aesthetic, slow forward dolly.
```

```

A lanista (trainer) watches from a shaded bench. Morning light catches the sand particles in the air. In the background, a donkey pulls a cart loaded with armor. Warm amber and sandy ochre palette, gentle handheld movement, 35mm film aesthetic.

### Prompt 3: Steampunk Tesla — The Workshop


```
Slow orbit around a steampunk workshop bathed in amber lamplight. Brass gears and copper coils cover every surface.
