# Strip #029 — "The Bones Are Honest"
**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Zone:** The Main Floor (near the funerary shelf)
**Characters:** The Skeleton Duck, The Hooded Death-Mask Duck, A Traveler
**Art Style:** Hobbit-hole warm palette (amber, gold, cream) — Gazette house style
**Player Adventures:** None — NPC feature (The Skeleton Duck; the Munsters-type cute ruling, the honest-bones law)

---

## Panel 1 — The Setup

A skeleton duck waddles across the warm Main Floor of the Geode Inn. Amber firelight, cozy hobbit-hole palette. The duck is fully skeletal — visible ribcage, skull with an empty beak, thin bone legs — but it moves like any duck, waddling with casual purpose. A new traveler stands frozen mid-step, drink halfway to mouth, watching the duck pass. The skeleton duck doesn't glance at the traveler.

**Caption:** "The duck had been at the Inn longer than the traveler. The Inn hadn't mentioned this. Neither had the duck."

## Panel 2 — The Moment

The skeleton duck reaches the funerary shelf on the far wall. The hooded death-mask duck stands guard there, tall, still, robed. The skeleton duck settles beside the hooded duck, tucking bone wings, and the two sit together like old companions — one hooded and silent, one skeletal and content. The traveler watches from across the warm room.

**Caption:** "The shelf had a keeper and a resident. One wore a hood. The other wore nothing but the truth. Neither felt the need to explain."

## Panel 3 — The Twist

The skeleton duck quacks. The sound is a perfectly ordinary, unmistakable duck quack — the most normal sound in the room. It echoes through the warm common room. The traveler, mid-sip, startles so badly the drink sloshes. The hooded death-mask duck turns its hooded head toward the traveler with a look that says everything without saying anything.

**Caption:** "The quack was the most normal sound in the room. The traveler was the anomaly. The hooded duck agreed."

## Panel 4 — The Button

Evening. The traveler has settled at the bar, drink replaced, composure mostly recovered. The skeleton duck waddles under the traveler's stool, pecking at crumbs. The hooded duck watches the shelf. The fire is warm. The room is full. Nobody is hiding anything.

**Caption:** "The Inn's trick was never hiding the dark. It was giving the dark a quack and a shelf and calling it staff. The house was monstrous, and the house was home."

---

## Notes

First strip featuring The Skeleton Duck and The Hooded Death-Mask Duck. Canonizes the Munsters-type cute ruling (Oct 9, 2026): "the monsters are the normal ones." The Inn's register is wholesome gothic — the family is monstrous and the house is home. The skeleton duck is honest about its insides; the hooded duck guards the funerary shelf; both are staff, not threats.

Voice check: Rothfuss precision (Panel 1 — "The duck had been at the Inn longer than the traveler"), Gaiman mythic-made-personal (Panel 2 — "One wore a hood. The other wore nothing but the truth"), Pratchett warmth (Panel 3 — the quack as the most normal sound, the traveler as the anomaly).

Played straight — the Child-Actor Law's spirit applies. No winking at the audience. The ducks are characters, not jokes.
