# Strip #025 — "The Form That Stays"

**Date:** 2026-10-05  
**Zone:** The Campgrounds → Main Floor  
**Characters:** Charles (The Chameleon Druid), A Campgrounds Sprite, The Postmaster  
**Art Style:** Alan Lee watercolor (golden-green) → hobbit-hole warm (amber/gold/cream)  
**Player Adventures:** None — NPC feature (Charles, the Chameleon Druid)

---

## Panel 1 — The Setup

**Art:** Alan Lee watercolor, golden-green morning light. The Campgrounds at dawn. Tents among trees, soft mist. Charles wakes inside his tent, caught mid-shapeshift — one fox's ear, a hawk's primary feather in his hair, a line of iridescent scales along one forearm. His expression: mildly inconvenienced. Not alarmed. Like someone who forgot to set an alarm.

**Caption:** "The Chameleon Druid woke up as three things at once. This was not unusual. This was Tuesday."

---

## Panel 2 — The Moment

**Art:** Alan Lee watercolor, golden-green. Charles walks through the Campgrounds toward the Inn, settling into himself — mostly. A fox's tail he can't shed. A line of scales along one forearm that won't fade. A Campgrounds sprite watches from a tent flap, curious.

**Caption:** "The parts that wouldn't leave were the parts that had been real. The fox had been cold and needed the warm ground. The hawk had needed to see. The man needed both. The tail stayed."

---

## Panel 3 — The Twist

**Art:** Alan Lee watercolor, golden-green. Close on the conversation — Charles and the sprite by the tent. The sprite has a cheeky, knowing grin. Charles looks resigned but content, the expression of someone who has had this conversation before and will have it again.

**Sprite:** "You've still got the tail."  
**Charles:** "I know. It's not done with me."  
**Sprite:** "They never are."  
**Charles, quieter:** "No. That's rather the point."

---

## Panel 4 — The Button

**Art:** Hobbit-hole warm, amber/gold/cream. The Main Floor. Charles enters, feather and tail and scales and all. The Inn doesn't blink. The Postmaster hands him a letter addressed to "The One Who Is Currently Charles." Nobody looks twice.

**Caption:** "The Inn never asked him to settle. The Inn asked him to stay. He did. As all of himself. The feather stayed too. It had earned the keeping."

---

## Strip Notes

**Voice register:** Pratchett warmth in the dry Tuesday framing. Rothfuss precision in the taxonomy of what stays and why. Gaiman mythic-made-personal in the final line — the shapeshifter who stays is the most radical act of shapeshifting, and the Inn's keeping theology extended to the one who changes.

**Canon:** First strip featuring Charles — The Chameleon Druid. Establishes the residue mechanic: shapeshifting in the Inn is not clean. Each form leaves a trace — feather, scale, tail — and the traces that stay are the ones that meant something. The Inn doesn't require him to choose one form. The Inn holds all of them. Connects to the keeping theology (strips #023, #024): what the Inn keeps, it keeps because it earned the keeping.

**Played straight:** The Child-Actor Law's spirit applies. Charles's condition is woven into the character, never announced. The residue IS the lived experience, rendered as fantasy. No diagnosis scene. No explanation. The tail stays because the tail stays.
