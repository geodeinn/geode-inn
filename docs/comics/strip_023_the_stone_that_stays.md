# Strip #023 — "The Stone That Stays"

**Date:** 2026-10-03  
**Zone:** The Artisan's Bench (Main Floor)  
**Art Style:** Hobbit-hole warm palette (amber, gold, cream), Klimt-influenced  
**Characters:** Vaulknut (the Selkie / Artisan Thief)  
**Player Adventures:** None — NPC feature (Vaulknut), Commission Ledger adjacent (the maker's mineral law, not a customer commission)

---

## Panel 1 — The Setup

**CAPTION:** Every mineral stays stable in the selkie's hands. Every one. The heavy silver stone doesn't.

**ART:** Vaulknut at her workbench in the warm lamplight, several dark hematite pieces laid out before her. She studies them the way a jeweler studies a stone she already knows is going to fight. Klimt gold-leaf warmth in the background. Hands poised, not yet touching.

## Panel 2 — The Moment

**CAPTION:** Hematite splits along its seams — clean, almost apologetic. The one mineral that argues with the maker's hands. Not malice. Just seams with opinions.

**ART:** She holds a piece of hematite that has just cracked in two. Clean break. She looks at the halves with bemused resignation — a slight wry smile, not anger. The split is gentle, almost respectful.

## Panel 3 — The Twist

**CAPTION:** She picks up the bubbly one. Botryoidal. No straight lines, no crystal planes, no argument in it. It sits in her palm, whole.

**ART:** Vaulknut holds the botryoidal hematite (kidney ore) — smooth, rounded, organic bubble shapes, no sharp edges. It sits perfectly whole and stable. Her expression is quiet surprise. The stone that doesn't fight is the stone that stays.

## Panel 4 — The Button

**CAPTION:** The workarounds became the design signature. Pyrite catches the lamp. Tiger iron shows its mercury-silver bands. And the bubbly paperweight stays where she put it — loyal, for no reason the mineralogy can explain, to the hands that hold it.

**ART:** Vaulknut has set the bubbly hematite aside and works with pyrite (gold cubic crystals) and tiger iron (banded red/gold/silver). Both catch the lamplight. In the background, the bubbly hematite sits undisturbed on the bench, whole and quiet. The selkie's hands are busy and content.

## Voice Notes

- **Rothfuss precision:** "Botryoidal. No straight lines, no crystal planes" — the mineralogy is exact.
- **Pratchett warmth:** "Seams with opinions" — the humor of a stone that won't cooperate, without malice.
- **Gaiman mythic-made-personal:** "Loyal, for no reason the mineralogy can explain, to the hands that hold it" — the deeper truth about what stays.

## Canon Source

Krista's hematite working law (2026-10-02): her energy cracks hematite specifically, the one mineral that won't stay stable in her hands. The bubbly botryoidal hematite paperweight is the sole exception — no seams to crack along. Pyrite and tiger iron serve as bench workarounds. The rainbow garnet discovery (iridescent andradite with hematite platelets) is adjacent canon but not featured in this strip.
