# The Oldest Cold Case

Strip #2 — 2026-09-12

Characters: The Flute-Keeper, The Hyena, Judge Tootenkowl, The Bone Crew
Zone: The Bone Room
Art style: Gazette house style — warm pen-and-ink with watercolor wash, Klimt-influenced, amber bone-cream tones

Player adventure: None — NPC feature (The Flute-Keeper & The Hyena)

1. The Bone Room's newest exhibit arrived with fifty thousand years of unresolved paperwork — a cave-bear femur with spaced holes, labeled "THE WORLD'S OLDEST SONG (RESULTS PENDING)."
2. The Flute-Keeper asked the Hyena: "Did you make the holes?" The Hyena: "I was hungry. The holes were a bonus."
3. Before Judge Tootenkowl and the politely rattling Bone Crew, the Hyena declared: "Prove I didn't make music." The court considered the evidence. The evidence grinned.
4. By the dockside fire, the Flute-Keeper played the bone softly. The Hyena listened. The bone was found with holes. The wind was here first. Verdict: kept open.

Lore notes: the strip canonizes THE FLUTE-KEEPER (the Neanderthal keeper of the Divje Babe bone — the question kept, not settled; the Qin's-mound law family) and THE HYENA (the unrepentant defendant of the oldest cold case; "I was hungry. The holes were a bonus." is the hyena's standing defense). Ties to the Instrument-Maker's Quest Line: the bone pipe is the quest line's first gate, and this strip is its origin story.
