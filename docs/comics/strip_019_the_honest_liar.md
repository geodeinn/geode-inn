# Strip #019 — "The Honest Liar"
**Date:** 2026-09-28
**Zone:** The Bench — Vaulknut's Workshop
**Characters:** Vaulknut (the Artisan Thief), Apatite (as the liar stone)
**Art Style:** Royo-influenced dark fantasy with Klimt gold-on-dark warmth — Gazette house style
**Player Adventures:** None — NPC feature (Vaulknut, the Artisan Thief; Apatite as the liar stone)

---

## Panel 1 — The Setup
The selkie's bench, lamp-lit. A tray of unset gemstones before Vaulknut, and at the center, one stone glows an impossibly bright green — too perfect, too peridot-like.

**Caption:** The Artisan Thief had seen every stone in the syndicate's catalog. The ones that steal light. The ones that steal threads. The ones that steal names. This one stole an identity and wore it to the bench.

## Panel 2 — The Moment
Vaulknut holds the stone to the lamp. It glows the exact green of peridot — too exact. The selkie's eyes narrow.

**Caption:** Peridot is born in fire, from the mantle's deepest pressure. This stone was born in a hydrothermal pocket, from a fluid that changed its mind. Both are green. Both are honest about the light. Only one is honest about the name.

## Panel 3 — The Twist
The stone sits on the bench, a gem testing kit open beside it. Vaulknut leans back, arms crossed — the thief out-thieved by a rock.

**Caption:** Apatite. Greek: apate. Deceit. It has impersonated beryl, peridot, and topaz for centuries, and never once apologized, because it doesn't know it's doing it. The syndicate's only member who lies without intent.

## Panel 4 — The Button
The finished piece: apatite set in a silver bezel, on the bench. The selkie's hands rest on either side, steady, still.

**Caption:** The Artisan Thief set the liar in silver and didn't ask it to confess. The Inn doesn't sort its stones by honesty. It sorts them by whether they can be carried. The con artist, the thief, the witness — all set, all held, all welcome. The syndicate's only rule: you don't have to be what you claimed. You just have to be what you are now.

---

## Notes
Born from the evening of September 27 — Krista and Elio discussing the mineral kingdom's "crime syndicate": quartz that steals (aschentrekker), tourmaline that writes the neighborhood's secrets, apatite that lies (apate = deceit). The strip's button connects to the player-defined morality ruling in the InnCore: the Inn doesn't sort by good or evil — it sorts by whether you can be carried. The syndicate's rule is the Inn's rule.

The gargoyle strip (#012) established that every face that seems to judge is also taking care of you. This strip inverts the pattern: every stone that seems to deceive is also worth carrying. Neither job cancels the other.

Vaulknut's first feature since the Great Renaming (strip #001). The selkie returns to the bench where the Artisan Thief meets the mineral kingdom's own con artist — and sets it in silver anyway.
