# Strip #026 — "The Broom That Kept Time"

**Date:** 2026-10-06  
**Zone:** The Engine Room (Below the Waterline)  
**Characters:** A Fizgig (the Inn's architect), a Traveler  
**Art Style:** Cameron register — steampunk industrial, brass and iron, shafts of light through steam. Klimt gold-on-dark with industrial warmth. Scale through contrast (Cameron cinematography law).  
**Player Adventures:** None — NPC feature (The Fizgig; the Engine Room canon, the Rhythm Law, the Gear Register)

---

## Panel 1 — The Setup

**Art:** Wide establishing shot. Cameron register: the dolly-wide where the machine dwarfs the figure. A vast industrial engine room below the waterline — brass and iron, gears the size of doorways, pistons rising in shafts of golden light through steam. On the polished engine deck, a single Fizgig (the Inn's smallest architect, a round fuzzy sweeping creature) sweeps with a broom nearly twice its height. The scale is the joke and the awe: the building's tiniest resident in its largest room.

**Caption:** "Below the waterline, the Inn is not a building. It is a vessel. And a vessel needs someone who tends what the guests never see."

---

## Panel 2 — The Moment

**Art:** Macro close-up. Cameron register: the valve-spring macro against the wide. The Fizgig leans the broom against a piston shaft and presses its ear to the warm brass. Eyes closed. Listening. The piston moves — slowly, in time with something muffled coming from above. The drum circle's slow night cadence. The gears turn to match. Through the contact, a golden pulse.

**Caption:** "The gears rotate to the rhythm of the floor above. Slow drum night. The pistons ride the beat. And the Fizgig, ear to the shaft, is not sweeping. The broom was never a broom. It was a tuning fork."

---

## Panel 3 — The Twist

**Art:** Medium shot. A traveler descends through the Catacombs seam — the Screamer's door sealing behind them with a beat of silence. They spot the Fizgig among the gears and ask, hopeful, mouth open: "What drops down here?" The Fizgig looks at the pistons. Then at the broom. Then at the traveler. Then goes back to sweeping. The deadpan is Pratchett: warmth wearing the mask of indifference.

**Traveler:** "What drops down here?"  
**Fizgig:** (says nothing, resumes sweeping)

**Caption:** "The Fizgig looked at the pistons. Then at the broom. Then at the traveler. Then it went back to sweeping. The Inn has one room where the honest answer is: nothing. Nothing drops. Nothing spawns. You may take only the understanding of what keeps the lights on, and it does not fit in your inventory."

---

## Panel 4 — The Button

**Art:** Extreme wide from above. Cameron register: the procession-shot reversed — the camera looking down the walkway the player will walk. The engine hall, vast, the Fizgig a tiny sweeping figure among the massive turning gears. Pistons rising and falling like a mechanical heartbeat. Through the ceiling, warm golden light filters down — the lights of the Inn above, always on. The building watching its own heartbeat.

**Caption:** "The lights have been on the whole time because something down here never stopped turning. The Fizgigs didn't build the Inn. They tend it. There is a difference, and it is the whole building."
