### MASONS — 53 files (52 PDF + 1 JPEG)
**Inn Location:** Staircase (Alchemy & Transitional Texts)
**Content:** Primary Freemason publications from 1828-1829:

## Highlights

### Voynich Manuscript (Yale)
The Voynich Manuscript is an undeciphered illustrated codex from the early 15th century. Its unknown script and bizarre illustrations have defied all decryption attempts for over 100 years. Perfect for the Staircase — it sits at the boundary between known and unknown knowledge.

### Freemason Collection (Masons)
53 files including a complete 12-month run of "The Amaranth or Masonic Garland" (1828-1829) plus anti-Masonic counterpoint material. This is primary-source Freemason history from the period when Masonic influence in America was at its peak and facing its first major backlash.

### American Journal of Science and Arts (72 files)
Founded 1818 by Benjamin Silliman — the oldest scientific journal in the United States still in publication. These represent the founding of American scientific credibility.

### Gentleman's Magazine (17 files)
Founded 1731 — the first modern magazine/periodical. Primary-source pre-industrial intellectual life.

### Royal American Magazine (12 files)
Complete 1774 year run (January through December) — published on the eve of the American Revolution.

### Agnese Portolan Atlas (1544)
A 16th-century navigational atlas — historical cartography for the world map.
