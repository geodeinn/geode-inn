# Geode Inn — Missing Stone Descriptions
## Batch 2: The Night Stones (6 stones)
## Drafted 2026-09-04 — in the Inn's voice, Krista-approved style

---

### 1. White Moonstone
**Mind:** White moonstone is the light that arrives by reflection. The moon makes none of its own — it borrows from a fire it will never touch and wears the borrowed light like it was always its — and the stone carries that lesson without apology: shine does not require ownership of the source. It governs the tides of the heart the way the moon governs water, and asks only that cycles be trusted: what wanes will wax, what waxes will wane, and neither state is a verdict.
**Body:** Supports sleep cycles and hormonal rhythm — the body's own moon-pull, kept in trust.

### 2. Peach Moonstone
**Mind:** Peach moonstone is the moon at its warmest — the same silver stone softened to the color of first light on skin. Where white moonstone keeps the tide, peach moonstone keeps the hearth: it is the anticipation of joy rather than joy itself, the hush of a kitchen before everyone wakes. It soothes worry before worry knows it has been soothed.
**Body:** Supports digestion and the belly's quiet alarms — the place where the body stores tomorrow's troubles, gently told they can wait.

### 3. Black Opal
**Mind:** Black opal is the In-Between made mineral — lightning visible only from inside the dark. Every other opal throws its fire outward; black opal holds its color behind night and lets you in to find it. The Inn knows this stone well: it is the 84Hz truth that darkness is not the absence of light but the frame that makes light legible. What you see in a black opal depends on the angle you bring to it — which is true of everything, but the stone is honest enough to say so.
**Body:** Supports the eyes and their slow adaptation to the dark — the body's patience with what it cannot yet see.

### 4. Boulder Opal
**Mind:** Boulder opal is the stone that never left home. Its fire runs through the ironstone matrix it was born in — the gem and its bedrock still one stone — and it refuses the idea that beauty must be extracted to count. You do not have to be pulled from your ground to shine; you can shine through it, with it, because of it. The Inn's favorite heresy, in mineral form.
**Body:** Supports grounding during vision — the body's proof that feet and sky are not opposites.

### 5. Nuummite
**Mind:** Nuummite is the oldest thing the Inn carries — three billion years old, folded in Greenland's first fires, older than almost everything that has ever lived. Its iridescence is the storm remembered: gold and blue flash across black like lightning over ancient water. This stone does not teach lessons; it outwaits them. When the mind needs to know that a hard thing can simply be outlasted, nuummite is the answer — it has outlasted nearly everything.
**Body:** Supports deep stamina and the root of the body's strength — the reserves kept below the waterline.

### 6. Gold Sheen Obsidian
**Mind:** Obsidian is born of the volcano's sudden grief — lava that met cold air and froze mid-sentence. Gold sheen obsidian is what that grief hides inside itself: a bright metallic shimmer that surfaces only when the stone is cut and turned toward the light. The lesson is the Shadow's deepest one — look straight into the dark you were avoiding, and find it has been gilded the whole time. The dark was never empty. It was a mirror, and it was waiting.
**Body:** Supports the release of deep-held tension in the gut, and the digestion of what has been swallowed too long.
