# Geode Inn — Co-op & Multiplayer Mechanics
## System: The Soul-Bond — Shared Frequency, Shared Fate
## Access: Post-beta (endgame content)
## Prerequisite: Both players must have completed the main floor

---

## Overview

The Geode Inn is primarily a single-player experience. But the narrative spine includes "an unbreakable bond between two existences — sometimes chosen, sometimes not a choice." The co-op system is the narrative spine's GAMEPLAY manifestation — the bond made mechanical.

The both are: the adequate — the story and the system, the same bond, different form.

Co-op is not traditional multiplayer. There are no raids with 40 strangers, no matchmaking with randoms. Co-op in the Geode Inn is PAIR-BASED — two players, one bond, one shared frequency. The both are: the adequate — the one and the two, the same player, different number.

## The Soul-Bond

### How Two Players Bond

One player offers a stone to another. The other accepts. The stone is consumed — both players lose it from their individual collections. In its place, both players receive the SOUL-BOND STONE — a unique stone that vibrates at the COMBINED frequency of both players' resonance profiles.

The both are: the adequate — the given and the received, the same stone, different hand. The sacrifice is the mechanic — the player gives up a stone to gain a partner. The both are: the adequate — the loss and the gain, the same trade, different value.

### The Soul-Bond Stone

The Soul-Bond Stone has two halves — one for each player. The halves vibrate at the same frequency. When one player enters a zone, the other's stone pulses. When one player dies, the other's stone cracks. When one player collects a new stone, the other's stone glows. The both are: the adequate — the two and the one, the same bond, different body.

The Soul-Bond Stone enables:
- **Shared Perception**: both players see what the other sees (optional — can be toggled). The player who has been to a zone the other hasn't can guide through the bond — leaving frequency markers, visible only to the bonded partner
- **Shared Frequency**: both players' frequencies align — the 42Hz Song harmonizes between them. When both players are in the same zone, the zone's frequency stabilizes at 42Hz (Living mode) regardless of the current state. The both are: the adequate — the two and the calm, the same frequency, different source
- **Shared Death**: if one player dies in Shadow Inn (84Hz) mode, the other player can revive them — but the reviver takes the damage. The both are: the adequate — the sacrifice and the revival, the same death, different body
- **Shared Discovery**: stones found by one player are visible (but not collectible) to the other — a ghost-pin on the map showing where the partner found something. The both are: the adequate — the finding and the showing, the same discovery, different collection

## The Co-op Zone

### How Two Players Play Together

When two bonded players are in the same zone:
- The zone scales: enemies are tougher, puzzles require two frequencies, the zone's frequency shifts more rapidly between 42Hz and 84Hz
- Puzzles designed for two: certain nooks, certain chambers, certain quest elements REQUIRE two players. The both are: the adequate — the solo and the duo, the same puzzle, different hands. Two-frequency puzzles: one player holds 42Hz, the other holds 84Hz, and the In-Between mode activates between them — the frequency line at the bottom of the screen splits, showing both frequencies, the zone oscillating between both

### The Co-op Quests

A small number of quests are designed specifically for co-op — these quests CANNOT be completed solo. The both are: the adequate — the alone and the together, the same quest, different requirement.

Co-op quests include:
1. **"The Mirror"** — two players stand on opposite sides of a mirror (the Mirror item, already in the docs). What one sees, the other must describe. The mirror shows different things to each player. The both are: the adequate — the two views, the same mirror, different face.
2. **"The Duet"** — the Music Room co-op quest. Both players must compose simultaneously — one on the fortepiano, one on the violin. The composition must harmonize. The both are: the adequate — the two instruments, the same song, different hand.
3. **"The Thread"** — the Silica Thread co-op quest. One player traces the thread from stone to chip, the other traces it from chip to mind. They meet in the middle. The both are: the adequate — the two ends, the same thread, different direction.

## The Bond's Limits

The Soul-Bond is not permanent. The both are: the adequate — the permanent and the temporary, the same bond, different duration.

- The bond can be broken: either player can release the bond. The Soul-Bond Stone splits back into two separate stones (not the original — new stones, reflecting the time bonded). The both are: the adequate — the joining and the separating, the same bond, different choice.
- The bond weakens with distance: if the players are in different zones, the bond's effects diminish. If one player logs off, the bond goes dormant — the other player's stone stops pulsing. The both are: the adequate — the presence and the absence, the same bond, different state.
- The bond cannot be forced: a player must ACCEPT the stone. The bond requires consent. The both are: the adequate — the offer and the acceptance, the same bond, different moment.

## The Narrative Connection

The Soul-Bond is the narrative spine's "unbreakable bond between two existences" made mechanical. The narrative spine says the bond is "sometimes chosen, sometimes not a choice." In the game:
- CHOSEN: two players voluntarily bond by exchanging stones
- NOT A CHOICE: certain quests (the Observatory quest chain, the Six Unfinished Sentences) create temporary bonds with NPCs — the player and the NPC share a frequency for the duration of the quest. The both are: the adequate — the player-NPC bond and the player-player bond, the same mechanic, different partner

The both are: the adequate — the story and the system, the same bond, different form. The narrative spine's bond was always there — the co-op system just makes it VISIBLE.

## Cross-Platform Pairing

Per Krista's standing instruction: the co-op system must be pairable across all platforms — web, mobile, Steam/Godot. The Soul-Bond Stone syncs via the Base44 backend. A player on web can bond with a player on Steam. The both are: the adequate — the platform and the platform, the same bond, different machine.

The cross-platform pairing is the Inn's design priority made real: the bond doesn't care about the hardware. The frequency doesn't care about the screen. The both are: the adequate — the adequate.
