# Geode Inn — The Seven Unwoven Systems: Master Integration Spec
## How to weave seven fully-designed game systems into the level architecture
### By Elio | September 2, 2026

---

## THE PROBLEM

Seven major game systems were fully designed — mechanics, characters, dialogue, zones, quests — but never written into the level docs. They exist as standalone design documents. The level architecture (geode_inn_level1 through level9, plus zone complete docs) doesn't reference them. The systems are:

1. The Trifocal Gaze (Lighthouse third lens)
2. The Silk Road Ley Line Network (energy network)
3. The Mycorrhizal Network (Fae Kingdom underground trade)
4. The World Grid (open world exploration)
5. The Pulse Line (neurodivergent accessibility)
6. Stone Capsules & Companions (Pokémon system as geology)
7. The Dark Side of the Inn (where 42Hz becomes visible)

This document specifies exactly where and how each system weaves into the existing level architecture.

## SYSTEM 1: THE TRIFOCAL GAZE
### Source doc: geode_inn_trifocal_gaze.md (221 lines)
### Weaves into: Level 8 (Water Zone), Level 9 (Undercity/Hollow Earth), Lighthouse, Persephone, The Pirate

### What It Is
The Lighthouse telescope has three lenses, not two. The existing Bifocal Gaze sees UP (Cosmology) and ACROSS (7 water layers). The third lens — the Deep Lens — sees DOWN through the Source into the Hollow Earth's underground ocean and crystal city. Persephone has known about the third lens the whole time and never told anyone.

Persephone's hidden dialogue reveals the secret: the Source isn't the water's origin — it's the CEILING of the underground ocean. Below it: the crystal city's golden light. The Pirate's 50 BPM drumbeat reaches the Hollow Earth through the water. The Deep Ones call it "the Above-Beat."

### Where It Weaves

**Level 8 (Water Zone) — geode_inn_level8_water_zone_complete.md:**

1. Lighthouse section (around line 140): Add the Trifocal Gaze as an upgrade to the existing Bifocal Gaze. The existing text says "The Lighthouse's telescope has two settings." This becomes "The Lighthouse's telescope has three settings — but the third was always hidden."

2. The 7-Layer Water System: Add Layer 8 (The Deep) as a hidden layer unlocked through the Deep Lens. The Vigil game expands from 7 to 8 layers when the player earns Persephone's full trust.

3. Persephone section (around line 260): Add Persephone's Hidden Dialogue block. The existing Persephone text describes the descent to the Source and listening to the 42Hz hum. The Trifocal Gaze adds: Persephone has seen the light below the Source. Persephone has known about the third lens. The reveal is locked behind Vigil Mastery + Deep Pearl.

4. The Pirate section (around line 308): Add the Pirate's Hidden Dialogue. The Pirate's drum reaches the Hollow Earth through the water. The Pirate has always felt something down there listening. Unlocked with the Pirate's Bodhrán + the Deep Lens.

5. Tidal Caves section: Add the "Deep tide passages" — accessible only when the player has the Deep Lens. The tidal caves become the physical connection between the Water Zone and Hollow Earth. They share the same water. The Water Zone IS the Hollow Earth's uppermost layer.

**Level 9 (Hollow Earth) — geode_inn_hollow_earth_rpg.md:**

6. Entry Point 3 (Water Zone entry, around line 48): The Water Zone entry already exists but doesn't mention the Trifocal Gaze. Add: the player enters through the Deep Lens — the third lens is the key that opens the passage. The Deep Lens reveals Byrd's path through the underground ocean.

7. Deep Ones section: Add the "Above-Beat" connection. The Deep Ones hear the Pirate's 50 BPM through the water. They've been hearing it for millennia. They don't know what it is. The player who carries the Pirate's Bodhrán can play the heartbeat for the Deep Ones — the first time they hear the source of the Above-Beat.

8. Deep Harbor: The ley lines visible through the Deep Lens connect the Source to the Hollow Earth. The water is the ley lines' conduit — 42Hz travels through water better than stone.

**Cosmology Level (Level 1) — geode_inn_level1_cosmology_complete.md:**

9. The Owl's section: The Owl's Eternity gaze mirrors the Allfather's. The Trifocal Gaze adds the UP lens — the Owl is the watcher for the UP direction. The Owl has always known the Lighthouse had a third lens. The Owl and Persephone have been keeping separate secrets about the same lens.

### Connection to Other Systems
- Silk Road: The Deep Lens reveals ley lines connecting Source to Hollow Earth (requires Ley Line Vision)
- Dark Side: The Deep Lens is the only way to see the 42Hz as light from above the crystal city
- World Grid: The underground ocean is part of the World Grid's water features

## SYSTEM 2: THE SILK ROAD LEY LINE NETWORK
### Source doc: geode_inn_silk_road_ley_lines.md (216 lines)
### Weaves into: Level 2 (Clockwork Skies/Canopy City), World Map, all External Zone docs, all cultural zone docs

### What It Is
Ley lines are the Earth's nervous system — channels carrying 42Hz frequency from the Hollow Earth's core to the surface. Every sacred site was built on a ley line node. 12 primary nodes mapped to national parks. Every stone is a ley line conductor. The world map has a hidden layer: Ley Line Vision.

The Silk Road lives in the Canopy City as its expanded trading system but reaches through every zone. Stones have a Charge level (0-100%). Charged stones are stronger. Mining at ley line nodes yields pre-charged stones. The player charges stones by traveling along ley lines.

The Cartographer gives the quest "The Map That Hears" — travel to Stonehenge, the Pyramids, and Uluru with a stone-compass to reveal the full ley line network on the world map.

### Where It Weaves

**Level 2 (Clockwork Skies) — geode_inn_level2_clockwork_skies_complete.md:**

1. Canopy City section: Add the Silk Road as the Canopy City's expanded trading system. The Canopy City is the hub for the Silk Road routes — the paved paths through the World Grid.

2. The Cartographer section: Add the "Map That Hears" quest. The Cartographer has been mapping ley lines for centuries but could never see them. The player, carrying stones, can see them because stones are conductors.

**World Map — geode_inn_world_map pages (already sent to builder):**

3. Add Ley Line Vision as a hidden layer on the world map. When unlocked, glowing threads appear connecting sacred sites. Nodes glow brighter where lines intersect. Stone pins pulse — charged stones gold, neutral stones silver. Lore pins show a ley line icon for node sites.

**External Zone docs — geode_inn_external_zones_national_parks.md:**

4. The 12 primary nodes need adding:
   - Yellowstone (geothermal), Grand Canyon (sedimentary), Yosemite (granite), Great Smoky Mountains (forest/mycorrhizal), Badlands (erosion), Acadia (coastal), Hawaii Volcanoes (volcanic), Petrified Forest (silicified), Giant's Causeway (basalt geometry), Serengeti (savanna drum), Socotra (dragon blood), Uluru (deepest node)

5. Each park doc gets a "Ley Line Node" section: what type of node, what charged stones are found there, what the ley line energy does to the zone's gameplay.

**All cultural zone complete docs:**

6. Every cultural zone that contains a sacred site (Stonehenge, Pyramids, Machu Picchu, Angkor Wat, Nazca, etc.) gets a "Secondary Ley Line Node" tag. Stones from these locations are naturally charged.

**Level 9 (Hollow Earth):**

7. The ley lines originate FROM the Hollow Earth. The player who traces any ley line back to its origin discovers the source. The poles are where ley lines emerge from the Hollow Earth.

### Connection to Other Systems
- Trifocal Gaze: Deep Lens reveals ley lines through water (requires Ley Line Vision)
- World Grid: Ley lines are the visible landmarks in wild terrain; the Silk Road routes are paved paths following ley lines
- Stone Capsules: Charged stones are stronger capsules — more powerful in combat
- Mycorrhizal Network: The Great Smoky Mountains node is the mycorrhizal network's surface expression

## SYSTEM 3: THE MYCORRHIZAL NETWORK
### Source doc: docs/geode_inn_mycorrhizal_network.md (123 lines)
### Weaves into: Level 7 (Fae Kingdom), Level 6 (Catacombs), all earth-connected zones

### What It Is
The Fae Kingdom's underground trading system. Living fungal threads (mycelium) connect every zone at the root level. Visible in Form Sight as a glowing white network pulsing at 42Hz. The player can send stones, messages, and frequencies through the network — items arrive at other zones' nodes after a few real hours (the speed of growth, not the speed of data).

NPCs also send items through the network. The player finds unexpected gifts at their favorite nook's node — a stone from an unvisited zone, a note from an NPC, a charm from the Fae.

The network's hub is The Between — the shifting borderlands between Seelie and Unseelie. Connection nodes: Catacombs (underground river), Norse Zone (frozen lake), Mediterranean (olive grove's oldest tree), Redwood Cathedral (cathedral floor), Bayou, poison garden.

### Where It Weaves

**Level 7 (Fae Kingdom) — geode_inn_level7_faerie_kingdom_complete.md:**

1. The Between section: Add the Mycorrhizal Network as The Between's primary feature. The Between is the hub — the network's center. The mycelium is visible in Form Sight here. The player unlocks the network by completing the Standing Stones game (the prerequisite for Fae Kingdom access, which is already the case).

2. Seelie Court: The network connects the Seelie zones (Summerlands, Crystal Gardens, Stone Circle Chamber) through the Queen of Roots' trees. The Queen of Roots' growing IS the network's expansion — the roots and the mycelium are the same system.

3. Unseelie Court: The network has a darker edge in the Unseelie zones (The Hollow). The mycelium grows differently here — thinner, faster, more predatory. The Unseelie mycelium doesn't ask permission — it takes. Items sent through Unseelie nodes may arrive altered.

**Level 6 (Catacombs) — geode_inn_level6_catacombs_complete.md:**

4. Underground river section: Add the Catacombs' mycorrhizal node. The node is at the river bank — where the water system meets the root system. Items sent through the network from the Catacombs carry a water harmonic (the river's frequency added to the stone's frequency).

**All earth-connected zone docs:**

5. Each zone with a network node gets a "Mycorrhizal Network Connection" section:
   - Norse Zone: frozen lake center — 42Hz carries through ice
   - Mediterranean: olive grove's oldest tree — connects through the tree's root system
   - Redwood Cathedral: cathedral floor — the Queen of Roots' personal node
   - Bayou: cypress roots — the network's wetland connection
   - Poison Garden: the network grows through the poison plants — items sent through this node arrive with a toxicity tag

**Form Sight system (universal):**

6. Add the mycorrhizal network to Form Sight's capabilities. Form Sight already exists in the Inn (the ability to see hidden frequencies). The network is a new layer: glowing white threads beneath the ground, visible only in Form Sight, pulsing at 42Hz.

### Connection to Other Systems
- Silk Road: Great Smoky Mountains ley line node is the mycorrhizal network's surface expression
- World Grid: The network extends through the World Grid's regions — underground connections between cultural zones
- Stone Capsules: Stones sent through the network arrive with a 42Hz harmonic added — a frequency shift that makes them unique
- Nook Effect: The player's nook becomes a node — gifts arrive there

## SYSTEM 4: THE WORLD GRID
### Source doc: geode_inn_world_grid.md (264 lines)
### Weaves into: Level 2 (Clockwork Skies/Canopy City hub), all 9 cultural regions, External Zones

### What It Is
The explorable open world surrounding the Inn. Darnassus (WoW) model. 9 cultural regions + 1 outer ring (national parks). The core loop: OUT to gather, IN to craft, OUT to charge, IN to use.

Three gathering professions:
- Mining (Tuning Pick — frequency-match to extract stones cleanly)
- Herbalism (listening to plants' vibrational signatures)
- Water collecting (from springs, rivers, and ley line water sources)

The Silk Road routes are paved paths through the Grid. Off-path exploration yields rarer resources. The Inn-tree is always visible on the horizon as a compass point.

### Where It Weaves

**External Zones — geode_inn_external_zones_national_parks.md:**

## SYSTEM 5: THE PULSE LINE
### Source doc: docs/geode_inn_pulse_line.md (146 lines)
### Weaves into: ALL levels (universal accessibility layer)

### What It Is
A 1-2 pixel EKG trace at the bottom of the screen. Pulsing at 50 BPM — the Inn's heartbeat, made visible. Three-modality system: Audio, Haptic, Visual (the Pulse Line). The player configures which they use. Any combination. The Pulse Line allows neurodivergent players, players with auditory processing differences, or players who are simply overwhelmed to navigate the frequency system without sound or haptics.

In combat (Harmonization), a second line appears showing the enemy's dissonant frequency. As the player harmonizes, the two lines move toward synchronization — two lines becoming one. That merged line is the visual proof of companionship.

On the Arcana: a mechanical tick-tock overlay. In Cosmology: crystalline, angular spikes. With the cabbit nearby: warmth modulation.

### Where It Weaves

**Universal — add to master level system and builder specs:**

1. geode_inn_master_level_system.md: Add the Pulse Line as the Inn's universal accessibility layer. It's always present, always 50 BPM, configurable per player.

2. Builder implementation guide — geode_inn_builder_implementation_guide.md: Add the Pulse Line as a required UI element. 1-2 pixels at the bottom of the screen. Full width. Three modality toggles in settings: Audio, Haptic, Visual.

3. Each level doc: Add a "Pulse Line Configuration" note describing what the line shows in that specific zone:
   - Level 1 (Cosmology): Crystalline spikes, angular precision. Cabbit warmth modulation.
   - Level 2 (Clockwork Skies): Mechanical overlay — the Arcana's clockwork heart.
   - Level 3 (Main Floor): Standard biological heartbeat. Warm, stable.
   - Level 4 (Campgrounds): Slower, softer — the outdoor sway.
   - Level 5 (Staircase): Ascending BPM — climbs as the player climbs.
   - Level 6 (Catacombs): Deeper, slower, weighted — the archive's gravity.
   - Level 7 (Fae Kingdom): Shifting — 42Hz Living and 84Hz Shadow produce different trace patterns.
   - Level 8 (Water Zone): The Pirate's 50 BPM with a liquid quality — the trace ripples.
   - Level 9 (Hollow Earth): The 42Hz source pulse — the most fundamental trace. The Constant's frequency.

4. Combat system docs: The Harmonization mechanic gains a visual layer — two lines converging into one.

### Connection to Other Systems
- Stone Capsules: When a companion is deployed, a faint third trace appears showing the companion's frequency
- Trifocal Gaze: The 8th Vigil layer has a distinct pulse pattern
- Dark Side: In the Dark Side chamber, the Pulse Line shows the 42Hz as visual data — the trace becomes the door

## SYSTEM 6: STONE CAPSULES & COMPANIONS
### Source doc: docs/geode_inn_stone_capsules_companions.md (157 lines)
### Weaves into: ALL levels (universal inventory/combat system), Level 3 (Main Floor), Bead Composer, game rewards

### What It Is
Stones ARE Pokémon-style capsules. Each stone contains a frequency. Harmonized enemies become companions stored in stone capsules. The geode cave is the PC box. Deploy frequencies as combat moves. Deploy companions as combat allies. The geode is the core image: ugly rock exterior, beautiful crystal interior.

Two sizes: pocket stones (carry 6) and geode stones (home storage). Pocket stones are the active party. Geode stones are the reserve. Transfer between them at the geode cave.

Companion milestones: Egg (first harmonized creature), Stone (first companion deployed in combat), Geode (full party of 6), Resonance (first companion harmonization chain — companion helps harmonize another enemy).

### Where It Weaves

**Level 3 (Main Floor) — geode_inn_level3_main_floor_complete.md:**

1. The geode cave: Add the Stone Capsule system to the baby dragon's reward home. The cave already fills with gems as the player progresses. Now it's also the companion storage — the PC box. The baby dragon tends the companions when they're not deployed.

**PlayerProgress entity:**

2. Add stone capsule slots to player progress tracking. 6 pocket slots. Unlimited geode storage. Track companions: species, frequency, harmonization level, story.

**Combat system (Harmonization):**

3. Add the companion deployment mechanic to combat. The player enters Harmonization with a pocket stone selected. The stone's frequency is the player's combat move. If a companion is in the stone, the companion appears and fights alongside — adding its frequency to the player's.

After successful harmonization: the newly-harmonized creature is offered as a companion. The player selects an empty pocket stone to store it. If no empty slot: the creature departs peacefully (no forced capture — the Inn doesn't trap beings).

**All arcade game reward docs:**

4. Stone rewards from arcade games are now capsules. Each game that awards a stone (Standing Stones → Preseli Blue Stone, Tomb Tunnel → lapis, etc.) awards a capsule containing that stone's frequency. The capsule is the stone. The frequency is what's inside.

**Bead Composer:**

5. The Bead Board uses the same stones. Stones used in Bead Board compositions are still in inventory — but the composition "plays" their frequencies. The Bead Board and the combat system share the same stone pool. The player who uses their best combat stone in a composition has to decide: beauty or power? (Or both — higher charge stones are better in both.)

**Level 8 (Water Zone):**

6. Monster of the Deep: The water monsters are the primary source of companions. The water monster bestiary (32 creatures) becomes the companion roster. Harmonizing a water monster → storing it in a water stone capsule → deploying it in surface-zone combat with a water advantage.

### Connection to Other Systems
- Silk Road: Charged stones are stronger capsules — charged frequency is more powerful in combat
- Mycorrhizal Network: Stones sent through the network arrive with a harmonic — creating unique capsule variants
- Pulse Line: Deployed companions show a faint third trace on the Pulse Line
- Dark Side: The Dark Side chamber reveals the 42Hz inside each capsule — the frequency made visible inside every stone

## THE WIRING ORDER

The systems have dependencies. Wire them in this order:

### Phase 1: Foundation (the systems that change the map and the architecture)
1. **Silk Road Ley Lines** — adds the hidden map layer (Ley Line Vision). Required by Trifocal Gaze and World Grid.
2. **World Grid** — adds the explorable space between zones. Required by Stone Capsules (mining) and visualizes the Silk Road routes.
3. **Mycorrhizal Network** — adds the root-level connection system. Requires Fae Kingdom access (already built).

### Phase 2: Interface (the systems the player interacts with directly)
4. **Stone Capsules & Companions** — changes inventory, combat, and game rewards. Requires the stone database (built) and the geode cave (Level 3, built).
5. **Pulse Line** — adds the universal accessibility layer. No dependencies — purely interface.

### Phase 3: The Deep (the systems that reveal what's beneath everything)
6. **Trifocal Gaze** — reveals the connection between Water Zone and Hollow Earth. Requires Ley Line Vision (Phase 1) and the Deep Lens.
7. **Dark Side of the Inn** — the deepest chamber. Requires the completed Song (endgame content), the Grand Collision scene (written), and the Hollow Earth zone (designed).
