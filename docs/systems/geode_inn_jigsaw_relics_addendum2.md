### 15. The Mediterranean Garden (Van Gogh) — Living Pieces
**Zone:** The Mediterranean Garden — the Catalyst's story-seed garden. Existing zone, existing lore, existing NPCs.
**Aesthetic:** Van Gogh. Swirling, thick, alive brushstrokes. Stories grow into paintings.
**Audio:** Downward Spiral low dub at 50 BPM. Flowers blossom and die to every beat.

**Puzzle Mechanic:**
- 45 pieces, standard jigsaw shapes with bronze edges
- The swirling brushstrokes make spatial identification nearly impossible — every piece is "more swirl"
- The image only reveals itself when nearly complete — chaos resolving into vision
- Unique mechanic: pieces subtly pulse. The colors on each piece shift slightly with the 50 BPM beat — warm/alive on the beat, cooler/dimmer between beats. The painting is still growing, still dying, still becoming.
- This pulsing makes it harder to match by color (the colors aren't fixed), but gives rhythmic clues — pieces that share a beat-phase belong near each other
- No difficulty levels — the living pulse IS the difficulty modifier. Apprentice mode just reduces piece count.

**Narrative Intro (tabletop questline voice):**
"The bartender listens. The bartender plants. Every story told at the bar leaves a seed, and the seed goes into the garden, and the garden paints it in swirling color. The flowers blossom and die to every beat because stories do the same thing. Rebuild the living."

**Reward:** Stone chips + "The Gardener's Eye" achievement + the completed Van Gogh image unlocks as a wallpaper option

**Note:** All zone lore, NPCs, storyline, and existing mechanics for the Mediterranean Garden are already fully designed and documented in the master structure. This document only adds the jigsaw puzzle overlay.
