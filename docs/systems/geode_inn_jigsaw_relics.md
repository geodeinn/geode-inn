# Geode Inn — Jigsaw Relics
## Zone Image Drag-and-Drop Puzzle System

---

## CONCEPT

Every zone concept image in the Geode Inn becomes a jigsaw puzzle. Players drag and drop pieces to reconstruct the zone's artwork. It's the Inn's most contemplative game — no timer, no pressure, just the quiet satisfaction of assembling something beautiful piece by piece. The act of solving the puzzle IS the act of building the Inn.

## MECHANICS

### Basic Flow
- Player selects a zone puzzle from the puzzle gallery (framed with the universal Celtic knotwork border)
- Puzzle pieces appear scattered around the frame's edges on a dark stone table surface
- The completed image area shows a faint ghost outline (optional toggle — on by default for easy, off for hard)
- Player drags pieces into position
- Pieces snap to a grid when close to correct position (with a satisfying click + gold flash)
- Incorrect placements don't snap — piece stays where dropped
- Progress bar fills with Klimt gold as pieces are placed

### Piece Shapes
- Classic jigsaw piece shapes (tabs and blanks) with slightly irregular edges
- Each piece has a thin bronze edge tint matching the Antikythera aesthetic
- Pieces carry their portion of the zone's concept art
- No two pieces alike — unique tab/blank combinations

### Difficulty Levels (per puzzle)
- **Apprentice:** Ghost outline visible, pieces snap from larger distance, tray sorting enabled — 50% fewer pieces than standard
- **Journeyman:** Ghost outline toggle (off by default), standard piece count, standard snap distance
- **Master:** No ghost outline, full piece count, tighter snap distance, pieces can rotate (double-tap to rotate 90°)
- **Archive Keeper:** Master rules + pieces start face-down, must be flipped before placement

### Drag-and-Drop Details
- Long-press to pick up a piece (piece lifts slightly, casts a shadow)
- Drag to position — piece follows finger/cursor with slight weight/inertia
- Release near correct spot: snaps in, gold flash, soft chime (pitch varies by zone)
- Release elsewhere: piece stays where dropped (player can organize pieces on the table)
- Multi-touch: can hold one piece while dragging another (tablet support)
- Pieces can be dragged to a "tray" area along the bottom edge for sorting

### Visual Polish
- **Table surface:** Dark stone with faint Celtic knotwork pattern (same gold inlay as the frame)
- **Frame:** Universal Celtic knotwork gold border — the puzzle area IS inside the frame
- **Completed sections:** As pieces connect, the joined area subtly glows (like the zone is "waking up")
- **Full completion:** The entire image pulses with Klimt gold, the zone's ambient sound plays (each zone has its own audio profile — Turner zone = ethereal chimes, Klimt Undercity = deep resonant tones, etc.)
- **Piece flip (Archive Keeper mode):** 3D flip animation matching the card flip from the card games

## REWARDS

### Per Puzzle Completion
- Stone chips (currency, scaled by difficulty)
- The completed zone image unlocks as a wallpaper/background option for the player's Inn profile
- First completion on Journeyman+: unlocks a piece of zone lore text (a passage from the Inn's narrative)

### Achievement System
- **First Light:** Complete your first puzzle (any zone, any difficulty)
- **The Builder:** Complete all 9 Core Inn puzzles (any difficulty)
- **The Architect:** Complete all 13 puzzles (any difficulty)
- **The Cartographer:** Complete all 13 puzzles on Journeyman
- **The Restorer:** Complete all 13 puzzles on Master
- **The Archive Keeper:** Complete all 13 puzzles on Archive Keeper
- **The Source:** Complete "The Source" puzzle on Archive Keeper — unlocks the Source Stone (166th stone, pure 42Hz)

### The Source Stone Reward
Completing all 13 puzzles on Archive Keeper difficulty awards the Source Stone — the 166th stone. Pure 42Hz, no mineral identity. The ultimate Bead Board upgrade (amplifies all stones to 100% charged). This connects jigsaw completion to the Bead Board progression system.

## AUDIO

- **Ambient:** Each puzzle plays the corresponding zone's ambient audio profile
- **Piece pickup:** soft stone-on-stone lift sound
- **Piece snap (correct):** satisfying click + gold chime (pitch varies by zone — Cosmology = high ethereal, Undercity = deep resonant)
- **Piece drop (incorrect):** soft stone placement sound (no penalty sound)
- **Section completion:** a warm harmonic builds (more sections = richer harmonic)
- **Full completion:** the zone's full ambient sound swells and resolves, like the room is finally "built" and breathing
- **The Source completion:** the full 42Hz chord plays — the deepest satisfaction sound in the game

## INTEGRATION

- Jigsaw Relics live in the Arcade as a fifth card alongside the arcade games and card games
- Puzzle gallery displays all 13 zones as framed thumbnails (universal Celtic knotwork border, naturally)
- Locked puzzles show a dark silhouette with a question mark — unlocked by progressing through the Inn's zone exploration
- The first 3 puzzles (Cosmology, Clockwork Skies, Main Floor) are available from the start
- Others unlock as the player explores corresponding zones in the Inn
- The Source is locked until all other 12 are completed at least once

## TECHNICAL NOTES FOR BUILDER

- Each puzzle needs the zone concept image sliced into a grid of jigsaw-shaped pieces
- Piece count per puzzle is configurable (scaled by difficulty)
- The ghost outline is a low-opacity version of the full image
- Snap tolerance: ~20px easy, ~10px standard, ~5px master/archive
- Rotation mode (Master+): pieces have a rotation property, must match correct rotation to snap
- Face-down mode (Archive Keeper): pieces start with back art (Royo gargoyle card back), flip on tap
- All zone images are already uploaded to app storage
- Frame: universal Celtic knotwork gold border template (already sent to builder)
