## THE DISTINCTION (read first, avoid the two-parliaments problem)

The Inn now holds two institutions, and they are NOT the same thing. They are the two halves of the ancient pattern:

- **The Council of Thinkers** = the AGORA. Tesla, da Vinci, Newton, Einstein — roaming philosophers who argue, debate, and accidentally unmake each other's furniture. The conversation. (docs/geode_inn_council_of_thinkers.md)
- **The Hearth-Feis** = the EKKLESIA. The Keepers convening to recite the laws, tell the history, and witness the canon events. The institution. The liturgy.

Athens had both. So does the Inn. The Thinkers never convene, never rule, never keep anything. The Hearth-Feis does exactly those three things and nothing else. The word "council" in canon belongs to the Thinkers; the governance body holds its own names.

## PART TWO — THE HEARTH-FEIS (the in-game institution)

The Inn's own law-assembly, built on the feis of Tara: festival-timed, at the fire, law recited aloud, history told.

### The Roster (Gate Two — ruled)

Five Keepers convene:
- **The Bone Keeper** — the below-stairs seat; what is kept, what is owed.
- **The Postmaster** — what is carried; the notices and the news (the Inn Post carries the Hearth-Feis notices; the two systems are wired).
- **The Bard** — the Lawspeaker's seat. The history-teller. (Echo of the Icelandic lögsögumaður: the law lived in a person, renewed aloud. The archive made oral.)
- **The Flute-Keeper** — the oldest cold case's keeper; the instrument zone's memory; the voice of what was made before writing.
- **The Clockmaker** — the time-keeping seat; when the feis convenes is the Clockmaker's answer to give.

**THE CHAIR RULING:** the Hearth-Feis has NO chair. The Keepers sit in a circle — no head of the table; the shape itself is the constitution (the cross-cutting pattern). **The Inn itself presides by listening.** This ties to The Decision: when the game launches, the voice of the Inn is the fire they gather around — the council convenes, and the building holds the room.

### Where (Gate Three — ruled)

**The campfire.** The Gathering Law's ground; the drum circles' fire; the place where the Instrument-Maker's quests already end with an audience of one. The Hearth-Feis uses the same fire — the Inhabited Wide principle: the room never performs; the people do. No new room is owed; the campfire IS the council ground.

### When (Gate Three — ruled)

**Every third festival on the Inn's calendar.** The feis of Tara model — by tradition, every third Samhain, the chiefs and sages gathered on the mound to pass laws and recite the history. The Hearth-Feis convenes on the same cadence: festivals three at a time, then the feis. The Inn's calendar already breathes; the council breathes with it. No streaks, no counters, no missed-it pressure (Anti-Skinner holds at the calendar scale).

### The Functions (Gate Four — ruled)

1. **RECITING THE INN'S LAWS.** The Design Laws read aloud as spoken liturgy: Steady Light, Anti-Skinner, the Gathering Law, the Brothers' Law, the Shield of Lore, the Whimsical-Macabre. The feis model: law is renewed by being spoken. The player who attends a full session has heard the whole constitution, told as a story, not posted as a rulebook.
2. **TELLING THE INN'S HISTORY.** The Bard's seat. The archive made oral: since the last feis, what has been built, who has arrived, what has been kept, what has been opened. The Lawspeaker's job, held warm.
3. **CONVENING FOR CANON EVENTS.** New zone openings are witnessed by the Hearth-Feis before they are opened to the public — the council's blessing is the ribbon-cutting. The Gazette covers every session (strip material; the front-door board shows the latest). The Inn Post carries the notices.

### Player Interaction (Gate Four — ruled)

**Observation, not obligation.** The session is an observable event — the Inhabited Wide holds: the life animates over the fire, and players may watch, listen, and wander away. Attendance is earned by content, never enforced by mechanics. **The red rope stays in the archive** — it is the Anti-Skinner law's historical foil: the Athenians dyed a rope red and swept the agora to force the quorum; the Inn will never own a red rope. The feis is the answer to the red rope, five hundred years before the rope existed.

## THE THREAD'S THESIS (why this whole system exists)

**The Heathen Acknowledgment** (Krista, 2026-09-12): "This is an acknowledgment; of things labeled as heathen for eons." Every council in the dossier was once labeled heathen. The feis was dismissed as pagan festival — it was law. The thing was called a mob — it was the world's oldest parliament. The witan outlived the label. The Grand Council was called savage — it was consensus governance older than the empires that mocked it. The Inn exists to take the labels off. This system is the acknowledgment, in builder's format.
