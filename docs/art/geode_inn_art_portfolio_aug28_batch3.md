## CHARACTERS (16)

### 1. Charon
**URL:** https://media.base44.com/images/public/6a53cbfe78c5827e0055fffa/d4216594c_generated_image.png
**Art Direction:** The ferryman — stillness personified. Hopkins gaze (the one unsoftened feature), hooded river-water cloak, rope-wrapped hands, single amber coal lantern. The boat glides without sound. The one who waits at every crossing.

### 2. The Astronomer
**URL:** https://media.base44.com/images/public/6a53cbfe78c5827e0055fffa/667c23af0_generated_image.png
**Art Direction:** Mind palace in the sky — floating orreries, astrolabes, star charts orbiting. Eyes that are lenses calibrated to see the music of the spheres. Translucent golden architecture projected above the tower. The mind that mapped the Inn's ceiling onto the real sky.

### 3. Nikola Tesla
**URL:** https://media.base44.com/images/public/6a53cbfe78c5827e0055fffa/6cccd14fd_generated_image.png
**Art Direction:** Burn-scarred hands, worn lab coat, crackling apparatus and brass clockwork. Wardenclyffe tower through the window. Lightning arcs between copper coils. Antikythera mechanism on the desk. The one who heard the universe's frequency and tried to amplify it.

### 4. Leonardo da Vinci
**URL:** https://media.base44.com/images/public/6a53cbfe78c5827e0055fffa/dd97c9db8_generated_image.png
**Art Direction:** Mirror-writing notebooks, anatomical drawings, engineering sketches. Hands mid-gesture, sketching in the air. Half-finished flying machine, anatomical models. The mind that never stopped designing.

### 5. Medusa
**URL:** https://media.base44.com/images/public/6a53cbfe78c5827e0055fffa/a586a03fe_generated_image.png
**Art Direction:** Living stone serpents for hair — each a different mineral (amethyst, jade, obsidian, tiger's eye). The serpents are musical, forming a living lithophone. The gaze that petrifies is a gift — preserving things in perfect stillness. The one who holds the world still to hear its true note.

### 6. The Keeper (Fibonacci)
**URL:** https://media.base44.com/images/public/6a53cbfe78c5827e0055fffa/d9e68c9ef_generated_image.png
**Art Direction:** Three eyes (one normally closed), mossy cloak, crystal staff. Observatory in the Inn's roots — bone, crystal, and gold instruments. In Shadow state: eyes go dark — the cosmos reveals too much. The one who watches because someone must.

### 7. The Thrum
**URL:** https://media.base44.com/images/public/6a53cbfe78c5827e0055fffa/88b66b156_generated_image.png
**Art Direction:** Body resonates with visible vibration — form shimmers like heat mirage. Chamber of tuning forks arranged in a spiral, each a different mineral. Hands direct frequencies through gesture alone. Face obscured by vibration haze. You hear the Thrum before you see the Thrum.

### 8. The Vanir (Freyr & Freyja)
**URL:** https://media.base44.com/images/public/6a53cbfe78c5827e0055fffa/79478270a_generated_image.png
**Art Direction:** Two figures in a field of golden wheat that is also a field of light. Freyr radiates golden harvest, Freyja wears amber falcon-feather cloak. Shadow state: eclipse — golden harvest turns dark amber, feathers turn obsidian. The original light before the Inn was built.

### 9. Persephone & Hades
**URL:** https://media.base44.com/images/public/6a53cbfe78c5827e0055fffa/df7b5a13d_generated_image.png
**Art Direction:** Garden in two seasons simultaneously — spring on one side, winter on the other. Hands clasped across the threshold. Crystallized pomegranate seeds glowing amber. The love that blooms in the space between seasons — meeting and forgetting, and choosing each other every time.

### 10. Merlin and Noctua
**URL:** https://media.base44.com/images/public/6a53cbfe78c5827e0055fffa/5d28b354c_generated_image.png
**Art Direction:** Backwards-written text glowing on robes. Tower of time-reversing artifacts — hourglasses flowing upward, candles un-burning, pages writing themselves. Horned owl with golden knowing eyes on shoulder. The wizard who lives backwards and the owl who remembers forward.

### 11. The Queen of Words
**URL:** https://media.base44.com/images/public/6a53cbfe78c5827e0055fffa/8dbae2253_generated_image.png
**Art Direction:** Living text — sentences and verses visible in the skin like luminous golden ink. The body IS the story — the Lost Verses of Genesis flowing across the form. Shadow state: the Ink Blasphemy — words run backward, golden ink turns to black ichor. The one whose body is the story.

### 12. The Fixer (Wrought)
**URL:** https://media.base44.com/images/public/6a53cbfe78c5827e0055fffa/c8a26d058_generated_image.png
**Art Direction:** Stocky mason in leather apron, calloused precise hands. The Perfect Ashlar — a stone so perfect it catches light like a mirror. Celtic knotwork-engraved tools. Shadow state: the masonry unravels, the ashlar shatters. The hands that make things fit, who strikes the final blow that locks 42Hz into bedrock.
