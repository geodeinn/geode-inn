## Cultural Regions & Game Mechanics

### Rome (~300 BC)
- **Style:** Don't Starve-style craft games + tower defense
- **Theme:** Gangs of New York / Dickens on the Strand / Carnival Row aesthetic

### Steampunk (Victorian)
- **Style:** Physics games
- **NPC:** Steampunk Tesla with a monocle
- **Note:** Tesla appears here AND in the Modern Day Legends nook

### Greece (~800 BC)
- **Characters:** Plato, Socrates, Aristotle, the Owl
- **Mechanic:** Game synchronization with Sparta

### Camelot / Arthurian
- **Characters:** Arthur, Merlin
- **Connected to:** Avalon

### Avalon
- **Mechanic:** Alchemy-based writing game
- **Connected to:** Herbalism

### Egypt (~1100 BC)
- **Style:** (open — connects to existing Egyptian Vault environment)

### Atlantis
- **Style:** TBD ("idk")

### Fae Kingdom
- **Style:** World of Warcraft-style MMO exploration

### Vikings (~900 AD)
- **Mechanic:** Don't Starve-style building game
- **Details:** Boats, thatch construction, folklore
- **Levels:** Yggdrasil levels where you hang out in Valhalla
- **NPC:** Charles (in faire garb, pestering Valkyries)

### Silk Road (700-1100 AD)
- **Mechanic:** Oregon Trail meets Monty Python
- **Theme:** Explore the thriving nomadically-based civilization

### Sumeria
- **Mechanic:** Little Alchemy-style game

### Anunnaki
- **Mechanic:** Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy style
- **Note:** Connects to the Owl's "42" easter egg

### The Rom (Romani)
- **Theme:** Ancient magic, symbols, and lore
- **Mechanic:** Magical music that enchants
- **Note:** This is Krista's deeply researched region — will be more detailed than others

### Ancient Trees
- **Mechanic:** Wood rings Simon Says (memory/pattern game)

### Catacombs
- **Mechanic:** Link to the Past dungeon-style
- **Portal system:** Portals in all areas with known catacombs
- **Dig mechanic:** Terraria-style — dig beneath our world
- **Dangers:** Big lizards, buried kingdoms, underground cities, entire civilizations
- **Temple Run:** Once you dig into a location, temple run mechanic activates
- **Enemies:** Mini MIB (Men in Black) chasing you
- **Caught mechanic:** When caught, you don't die — they make you forget everything
