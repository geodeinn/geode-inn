# Geode Inn — Morphic Video Generation Prompts
# Main Floor Zone (Artisan Market, Modern Legends Nook, Modern Religions Library)

## Prompt 1: Main Floor — The Great Hall Establishing Shot
```
Slow wide establishing shot of the interior of a massive living tree structure used as a grand hall. The space is warm and inviting — think hobbit-hole meets ancient library. Heavy wooden beams formed by living tree branches arch overhead, with amber lanterns hanging from curved iron hooks. The floor is smooth, worn stone with woven rugs in deep burgundy and forest green. Stalls and alcoves line the walls, displaying handcrafted jewelry — silver wirework, polished gemstones catching lantern light. A massive stone fireplace crackles at one end. Warm amber and gold color palette, soft volumetric light through high windows, 35mm film aesthetic, gentle camera movement.
```

## Prompt 2: Main Floor — The Artisan Market Stalls
```
Slow tracking shot moving along a row of artisan market stalls inside a living tree hall. Each stall is carved into the tree's inner wall, displaying different crafts — one holds polished gemstones arranged on velvet, another shows intricate Celtic silver knotwork jewelry on wooden busts, another has hand-bound leather journals with stone closures. Amber light from hanging lanterns warm each display. A glass case shows geode slices lit from below, their crystals sparkling purple and blue. The atmosphere is a bustling but peaceful market — warm, crafted, lived-in. No people visible. Cinematic, slow tracking, warm amber palette, shallow depth of field, 35mm.
```

## Prompt 3: Main Floor — The Jewelry Display (Soulstice Booth)
```
Slow push-in on a jewelry display case inside a warm wooden stall. Silver Celtic knotwork pendants rest on dark velvet, each holding a different stone — labradorite flashing blue-green, amethyst glowing purple, moonstone with soft blue sheen. The wirework is intricate, flowing, organic — each piece unique. Warm amber spotlights make the stones come alive. Behind the case, tools of the trade are visible on a workbench — round-nose pliers, coils of silver wire, polishing cloths. The space feels personal, artisanal, and lived-in. Cinematic, shallow depth of field, warm amber and jewel-tone palette, 35mm film aesthetic.
```

## Prompt 4: Main Floor — The Modern Legends Nook
```
Slow pan across a cozy reading nook carved into the living tree wall. A stone bench is covered with deep burgundy cushions and woven throws. A small fireplace glows in an iron grate. Bookshelves line the walls, holding leather-bound volumes with titles too small to read. A single oil lamp provides warm amber light. Above the mantel, a carved stone gargoyle sits — The Sage — its stone face serene and watchful. The nook feels like a hidden corner where stories are told. Dust motes drift through the lamplight. Cinematic, slow pan, warm amber with deep burgundy accents, 35mm.
```

## Prompt 5: Main Floor — The Modern Religions Library
```
Slow tracking shot through a circular library room within the living tree. Tall wooden bookcases curve along the walls, filled with volumes on world religions — spines in leather of every color, gold-leaf lettering. A round reading table sits at the center with an open atlas, weighted with a geode bookend. Stained glass windows filter colored light — deep blues, warm golds, soft greens — across the stone floor. The space is contemplative, scholarly, and warm. Amber oil lamps supplement the stained glass light. No people. Cinematic, slow tracking, warm multicolored light through stained glass, 35mm.
```

## Prompt 6: Main Floor — The Central Hearth
```
Slow crane shot rising from the great stone fireplace at the heart of the main floor. The fire crackles with warm amber and orange flames. Above the hearth, the living tree's trunk rises, its bark warm and textured, with carved spiral patterns. Lanterns hang in a spiral ascending the trunk, growing smaller with height. The camera rises slowly, revealing the full scope of the main floor — market stalls, reading nooks, the library entrance — all arranged around this central hearth. The atmosphere is the warm heart of a home. Cinematic, slow ascending crane, warm amber and gold palette, 35mm.
```

# Upstairs Zone (Cosmology & Sky Deities)

## Prompt 7: Upstairs — The Ascension to the Canopy
```
Slow upward tracking shot climbing a spiral staircase built into the living tree's inner trunk. As the camera rises, the walls transition from stone and bark to open lattice-work branches. Stars become visible through gaps in the tree. Amber lanterns give way to soft bioluminescent light — blue-white, like starlight. The air seems thinner, cooler, more ethereal. Carved constellations are inlaid into the wooden railing with faintly glowing metal. The transition from warm hearth to celestial observation deck. Cinematic, smooth upward tracking, transition from amber to cool blue-white starlight, 35mm.
```

## Prompt 8: Upstairs — The Celestial Observatory
```
Slow 360-degree pan from the center of an open-air canopy platform at the top of the living tree. The tree's branches spread outward like a natural observatory frame. The night sky is vast — deep indigo with scattered stars, a faint nebula glowing in purple and teal. Astronomical instruments of brass and crystal are arranged on stone pedestals around the platform's edge — an astrolabe, a celestial globe, a device of unknown purpose with moving crystal lenses. The floor is smooth living wood, warm beneath the feet. The atmosphere is contemplative, cosmic, humbling. Cinematic, slow rotation, deep indigo and starlight palette with warm wood tones, 35mm.
```

## Prompt 9: Upstairs — The Cosmology Ceiling Mural
```
Slow tilt-up shot revealing a massive painted dome ceiling above the cosmology level. The mural depicts ancient cosmologies — celestial spheres, orbital paths, and mythological sky beings painted in deep blues, purples, and gold leaf. The style is reminiscent of medieval astronomical charts and Renaissance dome frescoes. Soft uplighting makes the gold leaf shimmer. Painted stars and planets seem to glow faintly. At the dome's apex, a painted eye surrounded by radiating lines — the Watcher motif. The camera tilts slowly upward to take in the full scope. Cinematic, slow tilt-up, deep blue and gold palette, 35mm film aesthetic.
```

## Prompt 10: Upstairs — The Allfather's Throne
```
Slow dolly shot approaching an ancient stone throne at the far end of the cosmology platform. The throne is carved from a single piece of dark stone, with raven figures perched on its armrests — carved, not alive. Behind the throne, the night sky is visible through the tree's branches. Two ravens sit motionless in the branches above — real, watching, silent. A faint aurora shimmers in the distance — green and violet. A single amber lamp burns beside the throne. The atmosphere is solemn, ancient, and powerful — the seat of a god who wanders but always returns. Cinematic, slow dolly push, deep indigo with amber accent and aurora green-violet, 35mm.
```

## Prompt 11: Upstairs — The Planet Chamber
```
Slow orbiting shot around a mechanical orrery — a brass and crystal model of the solar system — at the center of a small domed room. The orrery's planets rotate slowly, each represented by a different gemstone — Earth as a blue-green tourmaline, Mars as red jasper, Jupiter as banded agate, Saturn with a ring of pale quartz. The mechanism ticks softly. Crystal lenses in the ceiling project faint moving light patterns representing planetary positions. The room is small, intimate, scholarly. Warm brass tones and cool starlight from above. Cinematic, slow orbit, brass-gold and cool blue palette, 35mm.
```

# Staircase Zone (Alchemical Texts — The Bridge)

## Prompt 12: Staircase — The Alchemical Landing
```
Slow push-in on a small landing midway up the grand staircase where the air shifts — cooler from below, warmer from above. The walls are lined with glass-fronted cabinets containing alchemical apparatus — glass retorts, copper distillation coils, ceramic crucibles, and ancient texts bound in cracked leather. Amber crystals embedded in the wall glow brighter at this midpoint. A stone gargoyle — The Watcher — sits in an alcove, its stone eyes seeming to follow the camera. Vials of liquid in amber, green, and deep blue sit on a shelf, catching the light. The atmosphere is transitional — between states of matter, between worlds. Cinematic, slow push, amber and emerald palette, 35mm.
```

## Prompt 13: Staircase — The Transformation Wall
```
Slow tracking shot along the staircase wall, which is carved with a continuous alchemical mural. The carving begins at the base with crude matter — stone, earth, roots — and ascends through stages of transformation. Midway, the carvings show distillation, sublimation, and crystallization in symbolic form. Higher still, the carvings become more abstract — light, stars, and geometric patterns suggesting spiritual transmutation. The carving style evolves from rough and earthy to refined and precise as we ascend. Faint amber light traces the path upward. The wall tells a story of transformation. Cinematic, slow upward tracking, transition from earthy browns to luminous gold, 35mm.
```

## Prompt 14: Staircase — The Alembic Window
```
Slow tilt-down from a tall narrow window in the staircase wall. Through the window, we see a copper alembic — a traditional alchemical distillation apparatus — with copper coils and glass chambers. Faint vapor rises through the glass tubing, catching amber light. The liquid inside shifts color — from murky brown at the bottom to clear gold at the top, then to a luminous pale blue in the highest chamber. The window frames it like a museum display. Behind the apparatus, ancient alchemical texts are visible, their pages showing diagrams of transformation processes. Cinematic, slow tilt-down, copper, amber, and pale blue palette, 35mm.
```

## Prompt 21: World Map — The Parchment World (Establishing)
```
Slow aerial descent toward an enormous aged parchment map laid across a landscape. As the camera descends, the parchment transforms — the flat map becomes the real terrain. Ink lines become rivers, drawn mountains rise into actual peaks, and small illustrated markers become standing stones, ruins, and ancient structures. The parchment edges burn away into living landscape. The transition is seamless — the world literally drawing itself into existence. Below, we see diverse biomes — desert, forest, tundra, jungle, mountains — each region distinct. Atmosphere is awe-inspiring, like watching creation unfold. Cinematic, slow descent, parchment sepia transitioning to full natural color, 35mm.
```

# Generation Order Suggestion
1. Start with the Catacomb prompts (already saved separately) — darkest, most atmospheric
2. Then the World Map surface prompts — establishes the open world
3. Then the Main Floor — the warm heart of the Inn
4. Then the Staircase — the transitional bridge
5. Then the Upstairs/Cosmology — the celestial high point
6. Then the Fae Kingdom — the magical deep zone

This order gives you a complete visual tour from the deepest roots to the highest canopy, with the surface world wrapping around it all.
