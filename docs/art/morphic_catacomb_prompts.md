# Geode Inn — Morphic Video Generation Prompts
# Catacomb Zone (Downstairs Level)

## How to Use
1. Go to morphic.com → Copilot or Canvas mode → Text to Video
2. Copy a prompt below and paste it into the prompt field
3. Hit generate
4. Each prompt is designed as a ~30-second clip (Seedance 2.5 limit)

---

## Prompt 1: Catacomb Entrance — Descent
```
Slow dolly camera movement descending ancient stone steps into a dimly lit catacomb corridor. Rough-hewn limestone walls glisten with moisture. Warm amber torchlight flickers from iron wall sconces, casting dancing shadows. Bioluminescent fungi in cool blue-green tones cling to cracks in the stone walls, providing ethereal counter-light. The floor is uneven flagstone with shallow puddles reflecting torchlight. Atmosphere is ancient, scholarly, mysterious, and warm — not horror. Think hobbit-hole warmth meets archaeological dig site. Cinematic, moody lighting, shallow depth of field, 35mm film aesthetic, amber and teal color grade.
```

## Prompt 2: Catacomb Corridor — The Long Hall
```
Steady tracking shot moving forward through a long, curving catacomb corridor. Walls are lined with ancient carved stone niches containing weathered artifacts — clay vessels, bronze tools, and fossilized specimens. Amber torchlight creates pools of warmth separated by shadow. Dust motes drift through the light beams. The corridor narrows slightly then opens into a wider passage. Faint blue-green bioluminescent glow emanates from moss growing between stones. Floor is ancient flagstone, slightly uneven, with thin streams of water trickling along the edges. Warm, scholarly, mysterious atmosphere. Cinematic, 35mm, amber and teal color grade.
```

## Prompt 3: Catacomb Chamber — The Map Room
```
Slow pan across a circular catacomb chamber with a massive stone table at its center. The table is carved with an intricate relief map of the ancient world — rivers, mountains, and cities etched in fine detail. Crystal formations grow from the walls, casting prismatic light across the chamber. Amber wall sconces provide warm base lighting. The ceiling is domed, with a small oculus admitting a single shaft of daylight. Ancient scrolls and stone tablets line the walls in carved niches. Atmosphere is a warm, hidden study — a place of deep knowledge. Cinematic, slow camera movement, amber and teal color grade, 35mm.
```

## Prompt 4: Catacomb — The Alchemist's Staircase
```
Slow upward crane shot rising along a spiraling stone staircase connecting the catacombs to the main floor. The walls transition from cold damp limestone to warmer, drier stone as the camera ascends. Alchemical symbols are carved into the stair risers — symbols for mercury, sulfur, salt, and the classical elements. Glowing amber crystals are embedded in the walls at intervals, increasing in brightness as we climb. The air seems to shift from heavy and ancient to lighter and warmer. Dust motes catch the light. Cinematic, smooth crane movement, warm amber palette with hints of gold, 35mm film aesthetic.
```

## Prompt 5: Catacomb — The Geode Cave
```
Slow push-in shot toward a massive geode formation in an underground cavern beneath the catacombs. The geode is cracked open, revealing sparkling purple, blue, and clear crystals catching torchlight. The cavern walls are rough natural stone, with mineral veins of gold and silver catching light. A small pool of perfectly still water reflects the geode like a mirror. The floor is scattered with loose crystals and smooth river stones. Warm amber light from a single torch, with the geode providing cool purple-blue counter-light. Quiet, sacred, wonder-filled atmosphere. Cinematic, shallow depth of field, amber and violet color grade, 35mm.
```

## Prompt 6: Catacomb — Water Monster Passage
```
Slow tracking shot through a flooded catacomb passage where the floor disappears into dark, still water. Ancient stone columns rise from the water, carved with serpentine and aquatic creature motifs — coiled sea serpents, kraken tentacles, and fish deities. Bioluminescent organisms in the water create an ethereal blue-green glow beneath the surface. Faint ripples suggest something large moving just below. Amber torchlight reflects off the water's surface, creating wavering light patterns on the vaulted ceiling. The passage curves into darkness. Atmosphere is mysterious and awe-inspiring, not frightening. Cinematic, slow tracking, teal and amber color grade, 35mm.
```

## Prompt 7: Catacomb — Ancient Civilization Archive
```
Slow panning shot across a vast catacomb chamber filled with stone shelves holding ancient clay tablets, cylinder seals, and carved bone artifacts. Egyptian hieroglyphics, Sumerian cuneiform, and Indus Valley script are carved into different wall sections. Amber oil lamps sit on stone pedestals, each flame steady and warm. Dust-covered pottery and ceremonial masks are arranged with scholarly care. The ceiling is low, supported by carved stone pillars. A single shaft of daylight enters through a small opening far above. Atmosphere is a sacred archive — quiet, ancient, full of stored knowledge. Cinematic, slow pan, warm amber palette, 35mm.
```

## Prompt 8: Catacomb — The Fae Portal Threshold
```
Slow dolly shot approaching a stone archway at the deepest point of the catacombs. The archway is carved with intricate Celtic knotwork and spiral patterns. Faint, otherworldly light — shifting between green, gold, and violet — emanates from beyond the archway. Vines and glowing mushrooms grow around the portal's edges. The stone floor has a faint circle of mushrooms growing in a ring before the archway — a fairy ring. The air shimmers slightly near the threshold. Amber torchlight behind the camera, with the portal's shifting colors ahead. Atmosphere is liminal, magical, inviting but mysterious. Cinematic, slow dolly push, amber to shifting green/gold transition, 35mm.
```

## Notes
- All prompts are written for Seedance 2.5 (30-second clips)
- Color grade consistently amber and teal (Geode Inn palette)
- Atmosphere is always warm, scholarly, and mysterious — never horror
- Camera movements are slow and contemplative (gentle sensory design)
- Each prompt avoids flashing/strobe effects (epilepsy-safe)
- The prompts progress narratively: entrance → corridors → chambers → staircase → geode cave → water passage → archive → Fae portal
