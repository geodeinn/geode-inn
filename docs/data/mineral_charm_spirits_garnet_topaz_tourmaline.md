## OVERVIEW & CLASS SPECIFICATIONS

All 15 spirits in this catalog belong to **Class 2: THE DENSE (Dynamic Driver — DD)**.

- **Class Sensation:** "A warm tone you both hear and feel. It resonates in the throat and sternum, the way a low bell carries through a stone wall."
- **Class Visual Style:** Heavier, more textured linework. Mineral-like bodies. More creature than person. These spirits have MASS—compact, stone-bodied forms that look like they weigh something.
- **Linework & Texture:** Heavy, weighty strokes with textured surfaces highlighting physical mineral properties (faceted faces, striations, drusy coatings, cleavage planes).
- **Frequency Visualization:** Pulses rather than ripples. The spirit's body visibly pulses and throbs with its stone frequency (Hz). Lower frequencies produce deeper, slower pulses; higher frequencies produce tight, intense rhythmic shockwaves.
- **Posture/Expression:** In the DD class, facial expressions are secondary to physical posture, weight distribution, and stance.

## FAMILY ARCHETYPES

### 1. GARNET FAMILY (5 Spirits — The Faceted Guardian)
- **Archetype:** Angular, dodecahedral spirits. Their bodies are visibly faceted with 12 geometric faces (rhombic dodecahedrons). Deep red is the historic dominant hue, but species vary in color (green, cinnamon-orange, mandarin, emerald druse).
- **Movement / Stance:** They **STAND**—solid, unmoving, heavy stone-bodied sentinels that anchor themselves to the earth like statues or castle guards.

### 2. TOPAZ FAMILY (4 Spirits — The Prismatic Sentinel)
- **Archetype:** Elongated, orthorhombic spirits. **TALL** for the DD class (6–8 inches). Prismatic crystal columns with prominent horizontal cleavage planes defining their posture and joints.
- **Movement / Stance:** They stand **RIGID & UPRIGHT**—imposing crystal monoliths emanating cold, piercing clarity and unbending structural integrity.

### 3. TOURMALINE FAMILY (6 Spirits — The Column Dancer)
- **Archetype:** Elongated, trigonal/triangular prismatic spirits with strong vertical striations and distinct color zoning (bicolor top/bottom gradients, tricolor stacks, or concentric watermelon core/rind).
- **Movement / Stance:** They **ROTATE**—their dense column bodies spin slowly on their central axis like floating mineral spindles, displaying their color zones as they throb with frequency.
