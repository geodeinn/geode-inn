### FAMILY OVERVIEW: THE BANDED DANCER
**Class:** The Present (Balanced Armature — BA)  
**Inn Layer:** Characters — the presences you meet  
**Sensation:** "A clear, precise tone. You hear this stone the way you hear a voice in a quiet room — present, distinct, intimate."  

**Family Archetype:** The Banded Dancer is a small, flowing spirit with visible bands and layers running through their body. Their form is rounded, smooth, and organic — never angular like quartz. The bands are their defining feature: concentric rings, undulating ribbons, or intricate dendritic branchings of color that shift and rotate as they move. Each stone variety possesses a unique band pattern (moss agate has leafy vine inclusions, fire agate has iridescent rainbow bubbles, crazy lace has wild zigzag lace, onyx has sharp parallel ribbons). They **DANCE** — their movement is fluid, circular, and rhythmic, like the mineral layers themselves in perpetual motion.
