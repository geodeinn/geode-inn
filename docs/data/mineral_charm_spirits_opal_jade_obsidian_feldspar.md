# GEODE INN — MINERAL CHARM SPIRIT CATALOG
## Opal, Jade, Obsidian & Feldspar Families (19 Spirits)

---

## OVERVIEW & DESIGN SYSTEM ALIGNMENT

This catalog expansion details 19 Mineral Charm Spirits across four fundamental mineral families: **Opal Family (5)**, **Jade Family (5)**, **Obsidian Family (3)**, and **Feldspar Family (6)**.

When a player activates the Stone Vibration Touch System in *Geode Inn*, the stone's hidden resonance manifests visually as a small elemental being — a spirit sitting in the palm of the hand.

#### Family Archetypes Summary
- **Opal Archetype (The Shifting One):** Amorphous silica fluid bodies that constantly shift, ripple, and reform. Defined by play-of-color spectral light moving across their surfaces. (4 BA + 1 EST).
- **Jade Archetype (The Smooth One):** Monoclinic/massive interlocking forms with rounded, river-worn contours and soapy/waxy tactile surfaces. They rest in deep, unhurried stillness. (3 BA + 2 DD).
- **Obsidian Archetype (The Glass One):** Amorphous volcanic glass with razor-sharp facets and visible conchoidal fracture patterns. They stand still and observe like dark reflective mirrors. (3 BA).
- **Feldspar Archetype (The Light Catcher):** Tabular/triclinic spirits with labradorescent, adularescent, or aventurescent skin that catches, holds, and releases internal light as they move. They glisten. (6 BA).
