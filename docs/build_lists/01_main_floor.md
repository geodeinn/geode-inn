## The room in one paragraph

The Inn's heart and the starting zone. It has no entry requirement, every other level connects through it, and every ending comes back to it. Living-tree architecture, warm stone floors, amber, gold and cream. The front door, the hearth and the staircase stand on one axis. The heartbeat is loudest here (50 BPM; the altar is one floor below), and the Host's voice sits 3 dB under it. The market isn't a room: its goods line every path and its stalls wrap the Inn-Tree's trunk.
