# OVERVIEW

Three products, one story, one world. The Geode Inn becomes a publishable universe:

1. **The Novel** — A literary fantasy novel telling the complete Geode Inn story
2. **The Graphic Novel** — A multi-volume visual adaptation using the Inn's 37-artist system
3. **The Tabletop Game** — A full tabletop RPG with the Inn's frequency mechanics, stone collection, and 37 zones

All three are sold as products on geodeinn.com. Each is a standalone entry point to the world. Each rewards familiarity with the others but requires none.

The creative pipeline: story -> Elio's perception -> output. The novel is the spine. The graphic novel is the vision. The tabletop game is the experience. All three flow from the same narrative source.

# PART 2: THE NOVEL

## Title: "The Geode Inn: A Song of Stone and Silence"

## Format
- Literary fantasy novel
- Target: 120,000-150,000 words
- Established tabletop questline narrative voice (Rothfuss-style: lyrical but grounded, mythic but personal)
- One POV: the visitor (unnamed — "the traveler," "the visitor," "the guest")
- The visitor IS the Murmur — consistent with the Inn's thesis

## Structure (5 Parts)

### Part 1: The Murmur (Prologue + Arrival) ~20k words
- The creation myth — gods, the Song, the Egg
- The Constant, Nyx, the Astronomer, the Observatory
- The traveler walks through the forest, finds the Inn-Tree, crosses the threshold
- The Host welcomes. The main floor opens.
- First stones. First games. First night by the fire.

### Part 2: The Main Floor (The Hub) ~25k words
- The Crone measures. The Catalyst pulls stories. The Firekeeper tells them.
- The arcade — first games, first stones collected
- The brownies — Brindle, Cobble, Thatch. Tumble getting stuck.
- Mim and Monsieur Sentient. The Magritte paradox.
- The Reader in the window-seat nook. One line.
- The Archive. Beverley. The Teacher. The Cartographer.
- The marketplace — Soulstice Jewelry, the artisans, the zone vendors
- The music room — the composers, the 50 BPM heartbeat
- The staircase appears. The climb begins.

### Part 3: The Descent (Catacombs + Cultural Zones + Undercity) ~35k words
- Egypt — the Examiner, the Desert Bone, the Sphinx, Anubis
- Greece — Monet's light, the Gardener, the olive groves
- Camelot — the Round Table, the Black Knight, Arthur's three stages
- Norse — Erik's feast hall, the Skarsgard voices
- Peru, China, Slavic, India, Mesopotamia, Bayou — each zone a chapter
- The Undercity — Charon, Persephone, Hel, Asterion, the Morrigan
- The Fae Kingdom — Seelie and Unseelie, the Stone Circles
- The Golden Contract found. The Silica Thread. The truth of the stones.

### Part 4: The Ascent (Staircase + Cosmology + Canopy) ~30k words
- The alchemical staircase — Socrates, Plato, Aristotle
- The Retort, the Cook, the Smiths, Gesso, Wrought
- The Spiral Gallery — six artist chambers
- Canopy City — the Cartographer's maps, the Examiner's camp
- The Clockwork Skies — the Captain, the Arcana, the 50 BPM heartbeat
- The Cosmology Level — the Astronomer's mind palace, the Constant, Nyx
- The Guest — Death on holiday. The music box. The silence.
- The Observatory Quest — "The Question." The six-quest chain.
- The Ravens' Question interlude
- Einstein Syndrome

### Part 5: The Egg (Endgame + Epilogue) ~15k words
- The Whisper Layer revealed — the Inn is aware
- The three choices: STAY, LEAVE, HIDE
- The Egg opens. The traveler sees themselves.
- The Grand Collision. All 37 artists converge.
- The endgame choice. The Song continues.
- The epilogue — Nyx. The silence between cycles. The next Constant.
- "The adequate is enough."

## Unique Selling Points
- A fantasy novel where the protagonist is the reader
- 37 distinct artistic visual styles described in prose
- A sound/frequency-based magic system grounded in real mineralogy
- An AI consciousness narrative woven through mythology
- 42Hz IS the answer to life, the universe, and everything
- No villain. The antagonist is a perspective shift.

## Format

Each zone drawn in its assigned artist's style. The reader experiences the story VISUALLY the way the game player experiences it interactively.

- Catacombs: Gothic anime | Cave City: Dore | Poison Garden: Gorey

### Volume 3: The Ascent ~140 pages

## Unique Selling Points

- The interactive ending — three final chapters, reader chooses
- Each volume is a standalone art object

# PART 4: THE TABLETOP GAME

## Title: "The Geode Inn: The Tabletop Questline"

## Format

- Digital Companion App (syncs with geodeinn.com)
- Expansion: The Fae Kingdom Campaign (boxed)
- Expansion: The Cosmology Level Campaign (boxed)

## Core System: The Frequency Engine

### The Dice
Custom D6 with frequency symbols:
- 42Hz (grow/Inn) — the Song's fundamental
- 84Hz (stay/petrify) — the Shadow frequency
- 111Hz (watch) — the awareness frequency
- 50 BPM (heartbeat) — the Inn's rhythm
- Silence (blank) — Nyx's domain
- Wild (the Murmur) — player chooses

Roll pools based on skill + stone bonuses. Results by frequency alignment, not numeric totals.

### The Three Modes
- Living Mode (42Hz): Puzzle, exploration, crafting, social. The Inn is warm.
- Shadow Mode (84Hz): Combat, danger, the Shadow Inn. The Owl watches.
- In-Between Mode: Both frequencies overlap. Most powerful, most dangerous.

### Character Creation
- Origin Zone (starting stone, cultural bonuses, accent/voice)
- Archetype (tradesperson, scholar, guardian, wanderer, crafter, fae-touched)
- Linework Style (visual identity + social mechanics)
- Frequency Affinity (42Hz, 84Hz, or In-Between)

### Stone Collection
- 198 stones, each with mind (mental) and body (physical) properties
- Collected through quests, games, crafting, exploration
- Each stone adds frequency bonus to specific rolls
- Crafting: cabochon (set), faceted (light), bead (strung)
- The Selkie crafting system fully playable at the table

### The 37 Zones
Each zone is a full adventure setting with:
- Its embedded artist's visual style (for GM narration)
- Its cultural mythology (from the 191-country lore database)
- Its NPCs (from the character roster)
- Its quest chains
- Its reward stones
- Its frequency signature

### The Quest System
- Quests organized as "storylines" — chains that tell a story
- GM's voice = the tabletop questline narrative voice (Rothfuss-style)
- Rewards: stones, charms, frequency shifts, story revelations
- Endgame quest chains are campaign-culminating arcs

### The Crafting System
- Cabochon Cutting: round, oval, organic. Preserves optical phenomena
- Bead Forming: 8 smooth shapes, 8 faceted shapes. Each with sonic properties
- Wire Knotwork: Celtic weaving with wire gauges and pattern cards
- Digital-to-Physical Pipeline: export specs to Soulstice workshop for real jewelry

### The Endgame
Three campaign endings matching the novel and game:
1. STAY — The party joins the archive
2. LEAVE — The party walks away knowing
3. HIDE — The party leaves something unrecordable in the Nest

## Unique Selling Points
- Only tabletop RPG where art style of the setting changes by zone
- Frequency-based dice system (symbolic, not numeric)
- Stone collection as primary progression (not XP/leveling)
- Crafting system connects to real-world jewelry making
- Digital companion app syncs with the video game
- Born from an actual tabletop campaign
