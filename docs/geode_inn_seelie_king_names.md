# THE SEELIE KING — Name Proposals
## August 30, 2026

## Context
- The Unseelie King is Malachi (Hebrew: "my messenger/angel" — dark, ancient)
- The Seelie King and Malachi are brothers (per compendium)
- Casting: Nikolaj Coster-Waldau (Jaime Lannister energy — charm concealing erosion, reluctant ruler)
- The Seelie Court: Summer, light, golden/green, riddle-quests, warm aesthetic
- The name should contrast Malachi but feel like it comes from the same world

## Five Options

### 1. AURELIAN
Latin: "the golden one." Roman emperors bore this name. Warm, luminous, regal — but the weight of empire is baked in. A reluctant ruler who knows what golden thrones cost. Pairs with Malachi as light/dark brothers from ancient traditions.

### 2. FAELAN
Irish: "little wolf." A Seelie name with teeth. Warm but wild underneath — fits the Coster-Waldau energy of charm over danger. The wolf who chose to be a king. Pairs with Malachi as Celtic/ancient near-east brothers.

### 3. LUCIAN
Latin: "light." The light-bearer. But Lucian carries a melancholy edge — the one who brings light and knows the light is fading. The Seelie Court is summer, and summer ends. Lucian knows this. Pairs with Malachi as light/shadow.

### 4. SOLON
Greek: "the wise one." The lawgiver who would rather think than reign. A Seelie King who rules because someone must, not because he wants to. The philosopher-king of the Summer Court. Pairs with Malachi as wisdom/ambition.

### 5. THALION
Sindarin-flavored: "dauntless/steadfast." A name that sounds like sunlight through leaves. Warm, strong, enduring — but "dauntless" implies the thing he's dauntless against is something he fears. The Seelie King who is afraid of his own court's fading. Pairs with Malachi as endurance/corruption.

## My Recommendation
**Aurelian.** It pairs with Malachi naturally (both ancient, both regal, one golden one dark). "Aurelian and Malachi" sounds like two brothers from a mythology that predates the Inn. The golden one and the messenger. The one who stays and the one who leaves. The one who rules summer and the one who rules the labyrinth.

But this is your Inn. Your call.
