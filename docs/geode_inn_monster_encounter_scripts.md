# Geode Inn — Monster of the Deep: 32 Encounter Scripts
## The Tabletop Voice — All Water Monster Conflicts

---

### Encounter Structure

Every water monster encounter follows the same four-phase structure:

1. **Observation** — The player sees the monster from a distance. The monster's signature instrument plays. The player learns the creature's frequency.
2. **Communication** — The player uses the Stone Vibration Touch System to reach out. The monster responds with frequency patterns, not words.
3. **Conflict** — The monster's environmental problem is revealed. The player must resolve it.
4. **Resolution** — The conflict is resolved. The monster awards its stone. The signature instrument plays a final, warmer note.

### W1. NESSIE — "The Warm Water"

**Observation:**
*The water is darker here. Peat-stained. The loch narrows into a basalt cave. Something large moves in the shadow — not away from you, but alongside you. Keeping pace. The bowed psaltery begins, low and Scottish, and you feel it in your chest before you hear it.*

*The creature surfaces for a moment. Just a hump. Just a shadow. Just enough to know you're not alone.*

**Communication:**
*You emit a 42Hz pulse. The water carries it fast — 4.3x faster than air. The creature responds. Not with a pulse, but with a song. Three notes. Low. The notes say: I am here. I have always been here. The water is wrong.*

**Conflict:**
*The water is too warm. Tourist boats have been running their engines in the deep channel, and the exhaust pipes — corroded, leaking — are pumping hot water into the cold layer Nessie needs. The cave is warming. Nessie can't dive deep enough to reach the cold.*

*The player must find and seal four thermal leaks. Each leak is a corroded pipe embedded in the cave wall, glowing faintly orange (thermal). The player uses the sonar pulse to vibrate the pipe closed — the metal contracts under the frequency, sealing the leak. Each sealed pipe drops the water temperature by one degree.*

**Resolution:**
*The last pipe seals. The water temperature drops. Nessie dives — deep, fast, the psaltery rising. The creature disappears into the dark, and you follow. At the deepest point, Nessie turns. Looks at you. The amber eyes in the peat-dark water.*

*Something falls from Nessie's mouth — a small, smooth, luminous object. A Freshwater Pearl. It sinks slowly. You catch it.*

*The psaltery plays one last note. Warm. Low. The note says: thank you.*

**Reward:** Freshwater Pearl (247Hz)

### W2. KELPIE — "The Dammed River"
