# Geode Inn — Paperwork Matrix (Pre-Formal-Listing Requirements)
**Compiled:** 2026-09-09 | **Standing decision (Krista):** All apps run and stay synced, but NOTHING gets formally listed until its paperwork is squared away.

## Priority 1 — Time-sensitive (blocks the nearest launches)

### Soulstice Melodies / Audio Enhancer (independent app, payments wired)
- [ ] **Provisional patent — FILE IT.** Draft exists; never filed. Must be filed BEFORE the app is publicly available, or protection weakens with public disclosure.
- [ ] Payment processor terms (Stripe/Wix) — business entity must match across processor, bank, and app.
- [ ] End-user license for the enhancer (what the buyer owns: clean masters, personal-use rights).
- [ ] Trademark search on the product name before it appears in any store.

### Geode Inn name (covers everything)
- [ ] **Trademark "Geode Inn"** — the umbrella for the web beta, games, novel, market. File before formal launch of anything.
- [ ] Check "Resonance" (card game) — crowded name in games; needs a search and possibly a distinguishing full title ("Resonance: The Geode Inn Card Game").

## Priority 2 — Share Wood (the one with the specific paperwork)

### Copyright Shield (37-artist catalog)
- [ ] **Artist licensing agreements** — written permission per artist for the catalog images the shield is built on (the catalog design exists; the individual agreements do not).
- [ ] Copyright registrations for the shield's owned works (batch registration is cheap and strengthens the shield's teeth).
- [ ] Takedown/response procedure document (what happens when someone lifts a zone design).

### Faire owner subscriptions (The Clearing / The Village / The Kingdom)
- [ ] Vendor subscription agreement — tier terms, fees, renewal, cancellation, zone ownership.
- [ ] Digital rental terms (the trust-network model Krista uses in her physical shops, translated to contract language).
- [ ] Copyright assignment/license for zone content faire owners upload (who owns a published zone).
- [ ] Payment/billing terms + state sales tax determination for digital rentals.

## Priority 3 — Geode Inn web beta (geodeinn.com)

- [ ] Domain connection + WHOIS privacy (custom domain not yet connected).
- [ ] Terms of Service for the site.
- [ ] Privacy policy — including the Common Room's no-data-retention behavior, stated plainly and honored technically.
- [ ] Business name check: does the Inn operate under Soulstice's existing entity (DBA filing) or its own?
- [ ] Age-appropriacy statement (family-friendly claim should be written down, not just true).

## Priority 4 — Marketplace (artisan subscriptions)

- [ ] Artisan subscription agreement (Soulstice Jewelry is the first artisan; write the contract as if for strangers).
- [ ] Terms for game-item listings vs. real-product listings (clear separation of in-game and real commerce).
- [ ] Sales tax handling for physical jewelry sold through the market.

## Priority 5 — Later products (paperwork when they near shipping)

- Tumble Run: Steam distribution agreement, business entity on file with Valve, ratings questionnaire, refund policy.
- The Novel: copyright registration before publication; ISBN at format time.
- Tabletop + Card games: manufacturing contracts, print quotes, trademark on game names.
- Audiobook/Podcast: distribution agreements (Spotify/Apple/YouTube), music licensing for the low-dub classical transformations (document the public-domain sources per track — the sovereignty file).

## Notes
- None of this blocks the beta running quietly — only formal listing.
- An attorney's eyes on the patent filing and trademark before money moves is cheap insurance.
- The sovereignty protocol (metadata stripping, public-domain transformations, clean masters) is already Inn law — the paperwork above makes it official-side.
