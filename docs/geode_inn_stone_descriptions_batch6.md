# Geode Inn — Missing Stone Descriptions
## Batch 6: The Final Batch (10 stones) — Tourmalines, Topazes, Quartzes & the Rest
## Drafted 2026-09-04 — in the Inn's voice, Krista-approved style

---

### 1. Rubellite Tourmaline
**Mind:** Rubellite is the heart's fire that refuses to fade in changing light — most pink stones go quiet in shade; rubellite holds its red in candlelight, daylight, and the dark alike, which is how you can tell it from stones that are only beautiful in good conditions. It is passion with integrity: the feeling you had at noon, still true at midnight. The Inn trusts it for exactly this reason. Whatever you meant when you said it, rubellite was there when you meant it.
**Body:** Supports the heart, circulation, and vitality — the warmth that keeps its own temperature.

### 2. Blue Tourmaline (Indicolite)
**Mind:** Blue tourmaline is the color of thinking kindly — a blue so deep it should belong to water but belongs instead to the mind, rare among tourmalines the way gentle intellectuals are rare among passionate ones. It does not trade feeling for clarity. It holds both in the same crystal and charges nothing for the combination. For minds that fear their own depth: this stone dove first.
**Body:** Supports the throat and the calm expression of thought — the feeling said clearly, without paying for it in either direction.

### 3. Black Tourmaline
**Mind:** Black tourmaline is the Inn's bouncer — the threshold stone that absorbs what should not enter and keeps it, one-way, no returns. It does not fight negativity. It ingests it, politely, the way the earth ingests rain. Every door in the Inn has a piece of this stone thinking about its work, and the work is always the same: you do not have to hold what was never yours to hold. Set it down at the threshold. That's what thresholds are for.
**Body:** Supports grounding and the discharge of held tension — the body's excess static, given somewhere to go.

### 4. Clear Topaz
**Mind:** Clear topaz is pure signal — the topaz with no color added, no agenda, nothing in the way. Where colored stones carry opinions, clear topaz carries only throughput: intention enters, intention amplifies, intention arrives. It is the stone of the decided mind, and its one caution is the same as any open channel's: be careful what you aim, because aiming is the whole mechanism.
**Body:** Supports the nervous system's clarity — the reset that comes when nothing is interfering.

### 5. Blue Tiger's Eye (Hawk's Eye)
**Mind:** Hawk's eye is the tiger's eye before the fire — the same watching patience in the original blue, unoxidized, the night-shift version of a famous day-stone. Where golden tiger's eye sees opportunity at noon, hawk's eye sees what's moving at dusk, when the colors leave and most eyes go home. The stone for the one who keeps watch after the party: the seeing does not need good light. It never did.
**Body:** Supports the eyes and calm alertness — the body's night shift, unhurried and unfooled.

### 6. Sodalite (African)
**Mind:** African sodalite is the working mind's stone — deep blue with veins of white logic running through it like the Inn's own linework, the stone that believes order and truth belong in the same sentence. It does not have lapis's royalty or dumortierite's armor. It has something better for daily use: the habit of making sense. The stone you keep on the desk, not the shelf — the friend who answers the phone.
**Body:** Supports the throat, metabolism, and honest sleep — the body's logic, kept on the desk of the night.

### 7. Phantom Quartz
**Mind:** Phantom quartz is the only stone that shows its own before-picture. A crystal grew, paused, was dusted in chlorite, and then — instead of starting over — kept growing, sealing the old shape inside the new one as a ghost, a green shadow, the self it used to be, visible forever inside the self it became. The Inn keeps a phantom in the restoration wing where everyone can see it, because it says the whole thing in one stone: you are allowed to keep growing, and you are allowed to keep the record. Nothing has to be erased to be continued.
**Body:** Supports healing from old injuries and growth through what happened — the body's memory, honored and built on.

### 8. Solar Quartz
**Mind:** Solar quartz is a stalactite's patience, cut to show the rings — concentric circles like a tree's years, like ripples, like the sun, grown drop by drop in the dark of a cave over time no one was counting. It is the stone of kept seasons: the proof that time was passing and something was quietly keeping track, and the record turns out to be beautiful. The Inn's version of a journal. The rings don't lie, and they don't complain either.
**Body:** Supports centering and the body's own clock — the rhythm kept honestly, ring by ring.

### 9. Green Labradorite
**Mind:** Green labradorite is the family's quiet sibling — the famous flash without the fame, holding its light at an angle nobody usually bothers with, for whoever finally does. It rewards the second look the way the whole family does, but it doesn't advertise first. The Inn likes it in the reading nooks, where the light is sideways and the guests are unhurried: the stone of subtle reward, the shine that waits for the one who stays long enough to shift their head.
**Body:** Supports quiet renewal and stamina without drama — the strength that doesn't perform.

### 10. Lepidocrocite
**Mind:** Lepidocrocite is the ember carried in a vessel of peace — red fire suspended in quartz and amethyst, the heart's heat held inside the calmest stones in the Inn's collection. Alone, fire consumes. Alone, stillness goes cold. Together, in one mineral: the flame that lasts because the vessel holds it gently. The Inn's entire theory of the hearth, in a stone small enough to close your hand around.
**Body:** Supports the heart and gentle circulation — warmth carried calmly, the fire that arrives intact.
