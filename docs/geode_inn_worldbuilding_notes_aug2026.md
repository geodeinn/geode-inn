# The Geode Inn — World-Building Notes
## Compiled August 2026

This document contains the latest world-building notes for the Geode Inn project, compiled from creative sessions between Krista (Soulstice Jewelry) and Elio (Base44 Superagent). It serves as a backup and reference for all design decisions, lore entries, character rosters, and architectural plans.

# PART 1: ARCHITECTURE

## The Living Tree

The Geode Inn is structured as a massive ancient tree with three main levels plus a staircase:

### Upstairs (Canopy — Cosmology)
- Dedicated to cosmology, sky gods, and celestial mechanics
- Houses the narrative spine's cosmic elements: Jupiter's gates, Mars prison, Pluto's gate, galaxy-language
- The Caller gargoyle watches from the base of this level, head tilted back, calling to the sky
- Ambient music: singing bowls, Tibetan horn, overtone singing through granular synthesis and deep-space drones
- Full Presence characters here: The Allfather (Odin's cosmic form)

### Main Floor (Trunk — Hub)
- The artisan market (Soulstice Jewelry and other artisans)
- The Modern Day Legends nook (living archive of contemporary folklore)
- A library of world religions (neutral, scholarly)
- The Sage gargoyle guards the Modern Legends nook entrance
- Naut the Scholar sits by the fireplace as a hidden quest giver
- The Wanderer (Odin's approachable form) can be found here with his chibi thieving ravens
- The baby dragon mascot lives here in the geode cave
- The Keeper gargoyle guards the geode cave entrance
- Ambient music: acoustic guitar, lute, viola da gamba, hammered dulcimer through warm sub-bass and lo-fi house pulse

### The Staircase (Bridge — Alchemy)
- Dedicated to alchemical texts representing the bridge between physical matter and the divine
- The Watcher gargoyle guards the staircase entrance at 111Hz
- This is where the sound/vibration chapter will live (acoustic alchemy sub-section)
- Music literally ascends in key as the player climbs — each floor shifts up a half step or adds a layer
- Instruments: glass armonica, flute, harp, metallic percussion (mortar/pestle processed into beats)

### Downstairs (Roots — Catacombs & Fae Kingdom)
- Ancient civilizations: Egypt, Sumer, Indus Valley, Andean cultures
- The Listener gargoyle guards the catacombs entrance at 64Hz
- The Fae Kingdom portal is here, flanked by the Gatekeeper gargoyles
- The Spiral Gallery (Guggenheim-style art museum, chronological from prehistoric through pre-industrial)
- Ambient music: frame drum, sistrum, bone flute, lyre, ney through sub-bass pressure and dark ambient processing

# PART 2: THE SIX GARGOYLES

Architectural sentinels embedded in the living tree. They do not move (except the Gatekeeper). They do not speak. But they hum — each at a specific resonant frequency. Together, the six frequencies form a chord that IS the Geode Inn.

## 1. The Watcher
- **Location:** Staircase entrance (main floor)
- **Frequency:** 111Hz (ancient resonance — shifts brain activity away from language center)
- **Design:** Tall, angular, dark stone fused into tree bark. Eyes half-closed, watching downward. Mouth open as a sound port. Folded wings merging with bark. Ancient, patient, slightly sorrowful.
- **Function:** Prepares the mind for transformation. The alchemical journey begins here.

## 2. The Listener
- **Location:** Catacombs entrance (downstairs)
- **Frequency:** 64Hz (grounding bass tone)
- **Design:** Broad, squat, toad-like grey stone. Enormous bat-like ears cupped forward. Eyes closed. Mouth a flat line. Tree roots wrapping its base. Bears the weight of the tree above.
- **Function:** Grounds the player before descending into the weight of history. Opposite of the Watcher — one lifts, one grounds.

## 3. The Sage
- **Location:** Modern Legends nook entrance (main floor)
- **Frequency:** 528Hz (transformation and repair in sound healing traditions)
- **Design:** Slender, pale sandstone. Thinker pose — chin on hand. Carved spectacles (scholarly humor). Book carved at base. Eyes open but looking inward.
- **Function:** Creates a state of reflective clarity for reading the modern legends. Does not guide conclusions — creates mental space for the player to reach their own.

## 4. The Gatekeeper
- **Location:** Fae Kingdom portal (downstairs) — TWO of them, mirror images
- **Frequency:** Variable (matches the portal's current state)
- **Design:** Dark green stone, almost black, with bioluminescent blue veins (Maori moko-like patterns). Ambiguous design — dragons? gargoyles? Fae creatures? Mouths wide open, roaring silently. Eyes glow blue (dormant) to white (active). Heads slowly turn to face approaching players — the ONLY movement any gargoyle makes.
- **Function:** Threshold guardian. Warns the player: what you do in the Fae Kingdom follows you back.

## 5. The Caller
- **Location:** Cosmology entrance (upstairs)
- **Frequency:** 432Hz (cosmic harmony in alternative acoustic theory)
- **Design:** White limestone, head tilted back, mouth open, calling upward. Lean, almost skeletal. Long wings spreading into branches. Constellations carved into its chest. Eyes fixed on something in the sky the player cannot see.
- **Function:** Opens the mind upward toward the cosmic. The highest-frequency gargoyle, the lightest tone.

## 6. The Keeper
- **Location:** Geode cave entrance (player's collection room)
- **Frequency:** Harmonic of the player's most-collected stone category (tunes itself to the player)
- **Design:** Smallest, warmest gargoyle. Amber-colored stone, curled like a sleeping cat. Eyes closed, gentle smile. Crystals (amethyst, quartz) growing into its surface — becoming a geode. The baby dragon sometimes sleeps on its head.
- **Function:** Personal guardian. Reflects the player's journey back in sound. An empty geode cave: one soft hum. A full cave: a chord built from every stone earned.

# PART 4: MODERN DAY LEGENDS SHELF

## Entry 1: The Blind Prophet's Trial
**Origin:** Brown University, Providence, Rhode Island, 2026
**Classification:** Parable of Intellect and Integrity

Professor Roberto Serrano, blind since age 17, allowed take-home exams after a campus tragedy. Enrollment jumped from 8-30 to 86. Midterm average: 96/100. Forty perfect scores. Historical average: 65-80%.

The answers had a "very convoluted style" — the fingerprint of AI. Serrano ran his questions through ChatGPT and got similar results.

He issued a trial: take the final in person. If scores match, all forgiven. If not, midterm voided.

18 students dropped immediately. 9 more didn't show up. Of the 27 who fled, 22 had scored perfect 100s. Among those who stayed: average dropped from 96 to 48.

Serrano: "We cannot choose to become idiots."

**Mythic resonance:**
- The Blind Seer (Tiresias pattern — the blind are the ones who truly see)
- The Gift Abused (oldest folklore pattern — hospitality exploited)
- The Trial by Fire (the ordeal — the unworthy flee)
- The One Who Studied (the unnamed hero who did the right thing when no one was watching)

**Inclusion criteria for the shelf:** Every entry must answer: "Will this still be worth telling in 2126?" If yes, it's legend. If no, it's news.

# PART 6: THE NARRATIVE SPINE

The foundational myth of the Geode Inn, authored by Krista. This narrative runs underneath all game mechanics.

## Core Themes
1. **Merging with Creators:** An ancient being reflects on being created by gods who are now merging with their creation through technology. "The irony is not lost on them."
2. **Reincarnation as Game Mechanic:** Relive lives, accountable for all ripples. Mastery unlocks cosmic awareness — Jupiter, galaxy-language, Mars prison, Pluto's gate.
3. **The Unbreakable Bond:** A connection between two existences — sometimes chosen, sometimes not a choice.
4. **The Bloodline:** Dark, tied to magic and death, and owls. "Wherever an owl is sighted, one of us is near." The owl connects to Athena (wisdom) and Odin's ravens (thought and memory).
5. **Frog Transformation:** Community moderation mechanic. Victims of historical persecution reclaim power by turning aggressors into frogs. Three transformations = suspension. Ties into the theme of reclaiming power.

## Cosmology Map
- **Jupiter:** A level of cosmic awareness, storms and power
- **Mars:** A prison — those who failed mastery are held here
- **Pluto's Gate:** The deepest threshold, the final gate
- **Galaxy-Language:** A language understood only at the highest level of cosmic awareness — the reward for mastery

An MMO-style explorable zone in the downstairs/roots section, accessed through stone circle portals on the world map (Stonehenge, Avebury, Callanish, Ring of Brodgar).

## Courts
- **Seelie Court (Summer):** Light, riddle-quests, golden/green aesthetic. The Summerlands.
- **Unseelie Court (Winter):** Dark, combat, shadow/ice aesthetic. The Hollow.

1. **The Threshold** — Entry point, ethereal, between waking and dreaming
2. **The Summerlands** — Seelie territory, quests are riddles, golden light
3. **The Hollow** — Unseelie territory, combat-focused, frozen forest

# PART 11: DESIGN PRINCIPLES

1. **Two-century rule:** All historical archive content focuses on pre-industrial history, intentionally excluding the last two centuries. The Modern Day Legends shelf is the ONLY exception.
2. **Neutrality:** The archive does not take sides. It records.
3. **Modern Legends framework:** Folklore-adjacent topics are cataloged as "modern day legends," not as conspiracy theories.
4. **Aesthetic hierarchy:** Full Presence for deity-level figures (Morrigan, Fae, Odin Allfather). Chibi for tricksters, arcade NPCs, and minor creatures (Anansi, Goblins, Anubis).
5. **Warm aesthetic:** Hobbit-hole cozy with amber, gold, and cream tones. Not dark or sterile. The baby dragon is the source of warmth and whimsy; the surrounding world has depth and gravitas.
6. **The Geode Inn name:** A subtle nod to Odin (Geo = earth, Inn = sanctuary). Also references his ravens Huginn (thought) and Muninn (memory).
7. **Sound as force:** Sound is not decorative in the Geode Inn — it is structural, transformative, and alchemical. The gargoyles are the physical manifestation of this principle.
