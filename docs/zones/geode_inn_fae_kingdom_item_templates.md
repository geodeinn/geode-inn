# Geode Inn — Interactive Item Templates: Fae Kingdom Zone
## Based on The Threshold + Six Sub-Zones (Architectural Template)

### Design Philosophy
The Fae Kingdom is the only zone that splits into two complete opposites within one system. The Seelie Court (Summer/Light) and Unseelie Court (Winter/Dark) are not good and evil — they are two honest answers to the same question: what does power look like when it grows wild? The Seelie answer: abundant, beautiful, seductive, rule-bound. The Unseelie answer: sharp, direct, predatory, free. The Threshold teaches players that the Fae Kingdom is a choice, not a destination. You don't explore one zone — you navigate the tension between two.

## Furniture Templates (Fae Kingdom)

### Seelie Furniture (Summerlands)

#### 1. Living Throne
**Description:** A throne grown from living vines and golden wood. Still growing. The Seelie ruler's seat.
**Materials:** Golden wood, living vine, sunpetal cushion
**Variants:** Seelie Throne (the template), Sunpetal Seat (smaller), Vine Chair (basic)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Still grows after placement (must be pruned to maintain shape)
- Sunpetal cushion self-repairs (damage heals over time)
- Sitting here grants Seelie reputation bonus while in the Summerlands
- The throne responds to the court's favor — if Seelie reputation drops, the throne withers

#### 2. Honey Glass Table
**Description:** A table made from crystallized Fae honey. Warm, amber, translucent.
**Materials:** Honey glass, golden wood legs
**Variants:** Round (riddle-table), Long (feast), Small (personal)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Items placed on it are visible through the amber (Fae love seeing what you bring)
- Warm to touch — the table retains the heat of the honey it was made from
- Riddle-table variant: the surface changes color when a riddle is being told (amber→bright gold when the answer is close)
- Cracks slowly heal (the honey is alive — it re-crystallizes)

#### 3. Fae Garden Bed
**Description:** A sleeping hollow in living moss and sunpetals. The bed grows around you.
**Materials:** Living moss, sunpetal, golden wood frame
**Variants:** Nest (single), Glade (shared, open), Bower (enclosed in growing vines)
**Interactive Properties:**
- The bed grows to fit the sleeper (moss expands, petals multiply)
- Bower variant: vines grow into a private enclosure (the Seelie version of privacy — living, not built)
- Resting here restores health faster than other zones (the Summerlands are generous)
- The bed emits a faint golden glow while occupied (visible to other players — Fae beds don't hide you)

### Unseelie Furniture (The Hollow)

#### 4. Black Iron Throne
**Description:** A throne carved from dead black iron wood. Cold. Sharp. Does not grow. Does not change.
**Materials:** Black iron wood, shadow crystal inlay, frost silk
**Variants:** Unseelie Throne (the template), Shadow Seat (smaller), Iron Chair (basic)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Permanent — does not grow, does not heal, does not change (the Unseelie way: honest permanence)
- Shadow crystal inlay absorbs light (the throne darkens the space around it — intentional)
- Sitting here grants Unseelie reputation bonus while in The Hollow
- The throne responds to the court's favor — if Unseelie reputation drops, the throne freezes over (frost crystals form)

#### 5. Frost Silk Hammock
**Description:** A hammock spun from ice spider silk. Cold. Translucent. Suspended between frozen points.
**Materials:** Frost silk, black iron wood anchors
**Variants:** Single (the standard), Double (rare — frost spider silk is scarce)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Cold to touch (the Unseelie don't value comfort — they value truth)
- Resting here is slower than Seelie rest (the Hollow is not generous) but restores focus/precision (different stat)
- The silk is nearly invisible (other players may not see the hammock — the Unseelie value stealth)
- Does not melt, does not warm, does not change — it is what it is

#### 6. Shadow Display Pedestal
**Description:** A pedestal of black iron wood with shadow crystal inlay. Items placed on it are visible but darkened — the Unseelie way of showing what you have without flaunting it.
**Materials:** Black iron wood, shadow crystal
**Variants:** Single (one item), Triple (three items — the Unseelie number), Shadow Case (enclosed — rare items)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Items placed on it are visible but darkened (honest display, not炫耀)
- Shadow Crystal inlay reveals the true nature of placed items (illusions don't work on this pedestal — it shows what something actually is)
- Triple variant: the three slots represent past, present, and future (the Unseelie see time as a frozen river, not a flowing one)

### Neutral Furniture (Crystal Gardens / Threshold)

#### 7. Garden Crystal Altar
**Description:** An altar made of pure garden crystal. Neither warm nor cold. The neutral ground's centerpiece.
**Materials:** Garden crystal, griffin feather inlay (optional)
**Variants:** Small (personal), Medium (group), Great (the Crystal Gardens template)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Neutral — works for both Seelie and Unseelie reputation
- Items placed here are judged on their true value, not their court alignment
- Griffin feather inlay: grants the "Griffin's Gaze" — the ability to see through both Seelie illusions and Unseelie shadows (quest reward)
- The altar is the only place where Seelie and Unseelie items can be combined without conflict

#### 8. Preseli Archway
**Description:** A small standing stone arch made of Preseli Blue Stone. A miniature portal. The deepest Fae Kingdom construction.
**Materials:** Preseli Blue Stone, ancient crystal
**Variants:** Small (decoration — hums with 42Hz), Medium (resonance chamber — amplifies the Song), Great (functional — mini-portal to another player's Fae Kingdom space)
**Interactive Properties:**
- All variants resonate with the 42Hz Song (the stone circle connection — the Fae Kingdom is accessed through stone circles, and Preseli is the original stone)
- Medium variant: amplifies the Song (the only place in the Fae Kingdom where 42Hz is louder than in the Cosmology zone — deeper, but louder)
- Great variant: connects two players' spaces (the Fae Kingdom is social — the deepest construction is a bridge between players)
- Preseli is the rarest material in the Kingdom — earned through quests, never found

## Build Rules (Fae Kingdom)

### Universal Rules
1. **Choose a court — or don't.** The Fae Kingdom allows Seelie building, Unseelie building, or neutral building. Each path has different materials, different rules, and different rewards. Neutrality is the hardest path but the most flexible.
2. **Reputation gates construction.** Seelie structures require Seelie reputation. Unseelie structures require Unseelie reputation. Neutral structures require both — or neither (the hardest to earn).
3. **The 42Hz Song persists.** Even here, deep in the roots, the Song hums through the standing stones. The Fae Kingdom is the deepest point where the Song reaches — it comes up through the roots of the Inn's tree.
4. **Everything is chibi-scale.** The Fae Kingdom is the MMO zone. NPCs, creatures, and most furniture are smaller than in other zones. The Fae are small. Build to their scale, not yours.
5. **The Portal is the gate.** You access the Fae Kingdom through stone circle portals in the Catacombs. The Standing Stones game (Tetris) activates the first portal. You cannot build in the Fae Kingdom until you've entered through a portal.

### Seelie Rules (Summerlands)
6. **Everything grows.** Seelie structures are alive. They grow, they heal, they change. Maintenance is pruning, not repair. Build too much and the Summerlands will overgrow your space.
7. **Light is abundant.** The Summerlands are never dark. The Seelie don't ration light — they drown in it. The challenge is not finding light — it's finding shadow.
8. **Generosity has a cost.** Seelie rest is fast, Seelie resources are plentiful, but Seelie reputation drops faster. The Seelie expect you to earn their favor continuously. Stop questing for them and the vines wither.

### Unseelie Rules (The Hollow)
9. **Everything is permanent.** Unseelie structures don't grow or change. What you build stays exactly as you built it. There is no maintenance — but there is also no healing. Damage is permanent.
10. **Light is scarce.** The Hollow is dark. The Unseelie don't create light — they steal it. Shadow crystal absorbs nearby light. The challenge is not finding dark — it's finding light.
11. **Honesty has a cost.** Unseelie rest is slow, Unseelie resources are scarce, but Unseelie reputation is more stable. The Unseelie respect directness. Once you've earned their trust, they don't withdraw it — unlike the Seelie.

### Neutral Rules (Crystal Gardens)
12. **Nothing grows and nothing freezes.** The Crystal Gardens are perfectly still. Crystal doesn't change. The only neutral zone where both courts' items work without conflict.
13. **The Griffin is the guardian.** The Crystal Gardens are guarded by the Griffin boss. Access to the deepest neutral materials requires defeating (or befriending) the Griffin.
14. **Neutrality is the deepest path.** The Stone Circle Chamber is beneath the Crystal Gardens. It contains the rarest materials (Preseli Blue Stone, Ancient Crystal, Root Stone). Only neutral-aligned players can access it.

## Faction System

### Seelie Court (Summer)
**Aesthetic:** Golden-green, warm, abundant, living
**Linework:** Soft flowing (lore/interaction)
**Build Method:** Grow (living materials that expand)
**Currency:** Sunstone (warm amber, found in deep Summerlands)
**Reputation:** Earned through riddle-quests, knowledge tests, and gift-giving. Drops if neglected.
**Rewards:** Rare stones — Labradorite, Fluorite, Rainbow Moonstone
**Faction NPCs:** Seelie Fae (chibi, golden, mischievous but kind), Summer Ents (including the Laughing One)
**Quests:** Riddle-quests, knowledge challenges, garden-tending, diplomatic missions to The Between

### Unseelie Court (Winter)
**Aesthetic:** Blue-violet, cold, sharp, permanent
**Linework:** Sharp angular (guardian/combat)
**Build Method:** Carve (dead materials that don't change)
**Currency:** Bloodstone (deep red, found in deep Hollow)
**Reputation:** Earned through combat, honesty tests, and proving yourself. Stable once earned.
**Rewards:** Rare stones — Shadow Crystal, Bloodstone, Preseli Blue Stone (shared)
**Faction NPCs:** Unseelie Fae (chibi, dark, sharp, honest), Goblins, Ice Trolls
**Quests:** Combat challenges, truth-tests, territorial disputes in The Between, hunts

### Neutral (Crystal Gardens)
**Aesthetic:** Silver-gold, crystal, starlight
**Linework:** No-outline (cosmological/reverence)
**Build Method:** Place (crystal doesn't grow or carve — it simply exists)
**Currency:** Garden Crystal (neutral, found in the gardens)
**Reputation:** Earned through fairness, balance, and Griffin trials. The hardest to earn.
**Rewards:** Preseli Blue Stone (the rarest), Ancient Crystal, access to the Stone Circle Chamber
**Faction NPCs:** The Griffin (boss/guardian), neutral Fae (rare, seen only in the gardens)
**Quests:** Griffin trials, balance quests (helping both courts equally), deep exploration

## Player Discovery Flow (Fae Kingdom)
1. Player wins the Standing Stones game (Tetris) in the Catacombs
2. The stone circle portal activates — the first portal hums with 42Hz
3. Player steps through — arrives at The Threshold
4. Sees both courts simultaneously — summer left, winter right, the split is immediate
5. The Gatekeeper (chibi, neutral) offers no advice — just watches you choose
6. Player chooses Seelie or Unseelie first (or tries to go straight — the Between is too unstable without reputation in at least one court)
7. Explores the chosen court — discovers the build method (grow for Seelie, carve for Unseelie)
8. Completes first quests — earns reputation, access to court-specific materials
9. Discovers the limitation — Seelie reputation drops if neglected, Unseelie reputation is stable but resources are scarce
10. Discovers The Between — the shifting borderlands where both courts' materials exist but are unstable
11. Finds the Crystal Gardens — the neutral zone — and encounters the Griffin
12. Realizes the deepest materials (Preseli, Ancient Crystal) require neutrality
13. Begins the longest path: balancing both courts' reputation to access neutral ground
14. Reaches the Stone Circle Chamber — the deepest point — where the 42Hz Song is louder than anywhere except the Cosmology zone
15. Builds a Preseli Archway — connecting their Fae Kingdom space to another player's
16. The Fae Kingdom becomes social — the deepest construction is a bridge between players

The Fae Kingdom teaches by duality. The player builds by choosing, then balancing.
