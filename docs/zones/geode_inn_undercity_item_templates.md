# Geode Inn — Interactive Item Templates: Undercity Zone
## Based on Scarlett's Room (Architectural Template)

### Design Philosophy
Every interactive item in the Undercity inherits its visual language, material set, and behavior from Scarlett's room. When players build structures in the Undercity, they select from these templates. The items teach the zone's aesthetic through use.

---

## Material Palette (Undercity)
| Material | Source | Properties |
|----------|--------|------------|
| Dark Stone | Scarlett's walls | Base building material. Cold to touch. Crystal veins running through. |
| Klimt Gold Leaf | Scarlett's arches/doorways | Surface treatment. Applied to stone. Glows faintly in low light. |
| Crystal Veins | Scarlett's wall crystals | Light source. Color depends on crystal type (amber, blue-white, deep red). |
| Dark Fabric | Scarlett's bed draping | Soft material. Crimson, black, deep purple. Used for privacy and warmth. |
| Carved Wood | Scarlett's desk | Heavy, dark, ancient. Iron fittings. |
| Amber Glass | Scarlett's fairy lights | Light source. Warm red-amber glow. Crafted, not found. |
| Iron | Desk fittings, hardware | Dark metal. Used for hinges, brackets, frames. |

---

## Lighting System (Undercity)
The Undercity has no sunlight. All light is generated.

| Light Type | Source | Color | Range | Behavior |
|------------|--------|-------|-------|----------|
| Crystal Glow | Wall crystal veins | Blue-white to amber | Short (2-3 blocks) | Constant. Natural. Flickers when stone is struck. |
| Fairy Lights | Amber glass bulbs on string | Warm red-amber | Medium (5-6 blocks) | Constant. Crafted item. Can be strung in patterns. |
| Gold Reflector | Klimt gold-leaf surfaces | Gold (reflected) | Short (1-2 blocks) | Passive. Reflects and amplifies nearby light sources. |
| Oil Lamp | Iron and glass | Warm yellow | Medium (4-5 blocks) | Flickering. Requires fuel. Can be extinguished. |
| Stone Lantern | Carved stone with crystal core | Depends on crystal | Short (2-3 blocks) | Constant. Placeable on walls and floors. |

### Light Interaction Rule
Gold-leaf surfaces reflect and warm any light source placed near them. A blue-white crystal next to Klimt gold produces a warm gold-amber glow — the gold TRANSFORMS the light. This is the Undercity's signature visual: cold light made warm by gold.

## Furniture Templates (Undercity)

### 1. Stone Alcove Bed
**Based on:** Scarlett's bed
**Description:** A sleeping alcove carved directly into the stone wall, draped with dark fabrics.
**Materials:** Stone (structure), dark fabric (draping), iron (brackets)
**Variants:**
- Narrow (1-person) — Scarlett's style. A nest.
- Wide (2-person) — family size
- Deep (recessed, with shelf at foot) — the scholar's alcove
**Interactive Properties:**
- Player can sleep/rest here
- Fabric color is customizable (crimson, black, purple, deep blue)
- Can be draped for privacy (closes off the alcove)
- Books/items can be placed on the stone shelf at the foot

### 2. Heavy Carved Desk
**Based on:** Scarlett's desk
**Description:** A massive dark wood desk with iron fittings. Faces the wall by default (introvert positioning).
**Materials:** Carved wood, iron
**Variants:**
- Writing desk (flat surface, inkwell groove)
- Reading desk (angled top, book rest)
- Combined (both surfaces, larger footprint)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Player can write/read here
- Items can be placed on the surface
- Desk can be rotated (wall-facing = +focus, room-facing = +social)
- Drawers are lockable (iron key)

### 3. Stone Shelf
**Based on:** Scarlett's manga stacks
**Description:** Crude stone shelves carved into the wall, or freestanding stone slab shelves.
**Materials:** Stone
**Variants:**
- Wall-carved (built into the stone, permanent)
- Freestanding (movable, stackable)
- Corner (L-shaped, fits alcoves)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Books, manga, stones, items can be placed on shelves
- Freestanding shelves can be stacked (risk of collapse if too high without support)
- Weight limit per shelf (heavy items on bottom, light on top)

### 4. Klimt-Gold Mirror
**Based on:** Scarlett's full-length mirror
**Description:** A mirror with a gold-leaf frame in Klimt organic patterns.
**Materials:** Glass, gold leaf, iron (backing)
**Variants:**
- Full-length (standing)
- Wall-mounted (smaller)
- Hand (portable, for close work)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Player can see their character reflection
- Gold frame glows faintly in low light
- Can be angled (affects what is visible in reflection)
- Some mirrors in the Undercity show... other things (rare/quest-linked)

### 5. Fairy Light String
**Based on:** Scarlett's amber-red fairy lights
**Description:** Strung glass bulbs on thin iron wire, each containing a small glowing crystal fragment.
**Materials:** Amber glass, iron wire, crystal fragments
**Variants:**
- Amber (standard, warm red-amber glow)
- Blue-white (cold crystal, for contrast)
- Mixed (alternating warm and cool)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Can be strung along walls, ceilings, around doorways
- Pattern is player-defined (zigzag, straight, draped)
- Provides consistent light (no fuel needed)
- Crystal fragments can be replaced (changes color)

### 6. Stone Window Alcove
**Based on:** Scarlett's window seat
**Description:** A narrow window carved into the stone wall, looking out into the Undercity's luminous gloom.
**Materials:** Stone (frame), crystal (glow source from outside)
**Variants:**
- Narrow slit (archer's window, minimal view)
- Wide (seating ledge, like Scarlett's)
- Arched (Klimt-gold trim, decorative)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Provides ambient crystal-glow light from outside
- Wide variant can be sat in (reading nook)
- View depends on location (other structures, crystal caverns, darkness)
- Can be draped with fabric for privacy

### 7. Crystal Sill Collection
**Based on:** Scarlett's windowsill stones
**Description:** A small personal collection of stones and crystals arranged on a windowsill or shelf.
**Materials:** Various stones and crystals
**Variants:**
- Mineral collection (stones from games/quests)
- Crystal garden (raw crystal clusters)
- Mixed (stones + crystals + found objects)
**Interactive Properties:**
- Each stone can be examined (displays mineral data)
- Arrangement is player-controlled
- Stones with sonic signatures can be played (tap to hear frequency)
- Collection grows as player progresses

### 8. Boot Rack / Entry Station
**Based on:** Scarlett's chunky boots by the door
**Description:** A simple iron and stone rack for footwear and outerwear near a room's entrance.
**Materials:** Iron, stone
**Interactive Properties:**
- Player can store/swap footwear
- Outerwear can be hung
- Entry mat (stone or fabric) catches Undercity dust

## Build Rules (Undercity)

1. **No sunlight.** All light must be crafted or crystal-sourced.
2. **Gold transforms light.** Blue-white crystal near gold becomes warm amber. This is the zone's visual identity.
3. **Stone is the foundation.** Everything is carved from or built upon stone. Wood exists but is rare and imported.
4. **Privacy is sacred.** Fabric curtains, alcove beds, wall-facing desks — the Undercity is where introverts live. Build for privacy.
5. **Depth = status.** The deeper you build, the more established you are. Surface-level Undercity rooms are temporary. Deep rooms are homes.
6. **Crystal is currency.** Crystals provide light, sound, and beauty. The more crystal veins in your walls, the richer your space.
7. **Klimt gold is earned.** Gold-leaf trim is not a default — it's applied by players who have reached a certain level of crafting. Bare stone is the starting point.

## Player Discovery Flow
1. Player enters the Undercity for the first time
2. Discovers Scarlett's room (NPC home — the template example)
3. Observes the materials, lighting, furniture, and layout
4. Receives basic build tools: bare stone walls, stone shelves, oil lamp
5. Builds a basic room — bare, cold, dark
6. Discovers crystal veins while expanding — first light source
7. Learns to craft fairy lights (amber glass + crystal fragments)
8. Discovers Klimt gold leaf recipe (gold + adhesive + pattern) — applies gold to arches
9. Room transforms from bare stone to Scarlett-level warmth
10. Other players visit and learn the same vocabulary

The homes teach by example. The player builds by imitation, then innovation.
