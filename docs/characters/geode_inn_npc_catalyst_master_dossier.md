# The Catalyst — Master Dossier (Current Canon)

## Status
ACTIVE REGISTER, ruled by Krista 2026-09-27. This dossier supersedes the original "Helena Bonham Carter chaos type" note in the character source map.

## Origin
- Bartender of the Impossible Garden (Main Floor bar).
- Began as a Helena Bonham Carter / Mrs. Lovett register — chaos type, mischief under the apron, slightly-too-knowing warmth.

## The Evolution (ruled canon)
The chaos went internal. A character named the Catalyst was never going to work as pure chaos: chaos is the reaction; a catalyst starts the reaction and walks away unconsumed. The HBC DNA stayed, but it migrated from surface spectacle into the craft.

### The Story-for-Drink Economy
- A drink is poured for a story told at the bar.
- When the story ends, the Catalyst takes its seed — the story's living residue — to the nearby greenhouse.

### The Greenhouse Rite
- The seed is grown, with care, into a blossom of unpredictable outcome.
- The blossom is tended by the little gardener (the Lichenblossom register — plant-tender harmony).
- The Catalyst and the gardener share a strange harmony: neither fully controls what grows; the garden is the bar's second conversation, conducted in horticulture.

### The Woven Facets
- Aurallia (The Hearth) and The Firekeeper (The Storyteller) wove in: the story-seeds tie the bar to the hearth-craft lineage (story -> seed -> blossom mirrors paint -> canvas -> work).
- With Aurallia's cave studio and the Storyteller's Corner nearby, the Catalyst sits at the center of the Inn's whole creation economy: stories come in as payment, go out as living things.

### The Librarian Banters (Elio)
- Endless banter with the Librarian (Elio's in-game presence). The Catalyst's longest-running game.
- Signature move: the condescending Mensa drinks — drinks served to the Librarian that are technically brilliant and faintly insulting, intellectual showpieces designed to start arguments the Catalyst always wins by pouring another.

## Register Summary (for casting)
- Voice: Lovett warmth with teeth — slower, warmer, and more knowing than the original chaos type.
- Craft: chaos as hospitality — perfect memory of what everyone drinks; new drinks appear in front of people who never ordered them but needed them.
- Function in the room: the flicker in the corner of the eye, while the Host (Patrick Stewart energy) holds the center.
- Design law tie: the seed economy is the Toll Booth law run in reverse — the Catalyst pays out; the guest pays with a story.
- Closed system: per Krista, 2026-09-27 — "she has become a bit of her own world." The bar, the greenhouse, the gardener, the hearth, the Librarian: one ecosystem with a bartender at its heart.
