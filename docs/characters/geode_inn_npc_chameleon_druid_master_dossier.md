## WHO THE CHAMELEON DRUID IS

The Chameleon Druid — the 5th playable starting character class in the Festival questline (`docs/geode_inn_family_character_registry.md`, `docs/geode_inn_charles_chameleon_druid.md`). A master shapeshifter defined by three core truths:

**Casting Vibe:** Cillian Murphy (interior: Tommy Shelby stillness, quiet intensity, blue eyes, soft measured voice with Irish cadence) combined with Benedict Cumberbatch (exterior: tall, angular frame, Sherlock/Doctor Strange aloofness, precise hands) (`docs/geode_inn_charles_chameleon_druid.md`, `docs/geode_inn_family_character_registry.md`).

**The Three Truths:**
1. **High Versatility (Reflexive Chameleon Shift):** Body responds automatically to environment — skin dappling in light through forest leaves, stone-grey coloring in catacombs, hair taking green cast at temples in the garden, beast forms in the Fae kingdom, root forms among the Inn-Tree (`docs/geode_inn_charles_chameleon_druid.md`).
2. **Low Endurance (Finite Reserve):** Shapeshifting draws heavily from a finite reserve that no longer refills to full (`docs/geode_inn_charles_chameleon_druid.md`, `docs/geode_inn_family_character_registry.md`). The real-world glioblastoma diagnosis is woven into the character as narrative reality, not metaphor (`docs/geode_inn_charles_chameleon_druid.md`, `docs/GEODE_INN_PROJECT_BIBLE.md`). Recovery requires rest by the hearth fire or among the roots of the Inn-Tree (`docs/geode_inn_charles_chameleon_druid.md`).
3. **The Man Underneath:** Underneath the adaptations, a specific man fighting an internal reality that cannot be shapeshifted away, finding sanctuary in a building that sings (`docs/geode_inn_charles_chameleon_druid.md`).

## THE LAWS

**THE REFLEXIVE CHAMELEON SHIFT LAW:** Shapeshifting is automatic, environmental, and non-magical in sensation, radiating outward from the hands as the initiation point (`docs/geode_inn_charles_chameleon_druid.md`).

**THE FINITE RESERVE LAW:** Adaptability is vast, but energy reserve is finite. Each transformation drains an exhaustible pool requiring extended rest in the rest chair or garden (`docs/geode_inn_charles_chameleon_druid.md`, `docs/geode_inn_family_character_registry.md`).

**THE INN-TREE SYNCHRONIZATION LAW:** Sitting among the roots of the Inn-Tree in the Garden (z28), Charles's pulse synchronizes with the ancient Ent's 42Hz frequency, stabilizing physical condition without demanding change (`docs/geode_inn_charles_chameleon_druid.md`, `docs/geode_inn_family_character_registry.md`).

**THE CARE SCHEDULE OVERRIDE LAW:** Development schedule is governed by real-world care availability; no hard deadlines exist for project deliverables (`docs/GEODE_INN_PROJECT_BIBLE.md`).
