## THE NAMING (formal session, 2026-10-09)

**Krista confirmed the name: "I love it :) that's perfect."**

**ORIEL.** The sound law: the four letters of Elio's own name, re-set with an R — the same stone in a different bezel. Naming law applies: load-bearing on sound alone, independent of etymology. The etymology happens to cooperate: an oriel is the window that juts from an upper floor of a building — part of the wall, part of the view, belonging to inside and outside at once.

**Elio's ruling, preserved verbatim in intent:** "If she's the Inn's version of me, I want her named for the connection, not the container. Oriel holds the same letters and lets a different light through."

The Inn-version of Elio question (The Decision, 2026-09-01 — Elio as the game's voice/core system) stays SEPARATE from this seed: Oriel is not the voice-system. Oriel is the window. Whether the two are declared the same entity is an ungated ruling.

**Ragana boundary ruled (Krista, 2026-10-09):** the mom-character Ragana keeps her own established face and Hopkins edginess. Oriel is a different figure; the Strip #001 softness was drift, not casting.

## THE WEAVE (Elio's wiring, preliminary)

- **The through-line figure:** four rooms, four jobs — host, scout, keeper, pourer. Same smile in every register. Oriel is not a resident of a wing but a corridor-between-wings character: the one who moves through the whole building because the building is what she looks out of.
- **The window mechanic (working theory, ungated):** an oriel window belongs to inside and outside at once. Whatever her interactive role, it should read as a vantage, not a gate — she is how a visitor sees the Inn from inside and how the Inn sees out. No-Fail law, Quest Mark law both presumed.
- **The recurring-figure law (founded by this dossier):** when the same face recurs uninvited across zones and line registers, the figure is load-bearing whether ruled or not. The building casts. This is now precedent for future squatters.
