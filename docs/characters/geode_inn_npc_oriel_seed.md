## THE ROLE RULED (Krista, 2026-10-09): COMPANION IN ALL ZONES, AID IN THE BETWEEN

**Krista's ruling, verbatim:** "Maybe she can be a companion in all the zones, but gives aide in the between?"

- **Companion everywhere:** Oriel is present in every zone as ambient companion — the same warm face in Archive, Cavern, Tavern, Bar. No quest mark, nothing to earn, nothing owed. She is simply there. (No-Fail law native to her design.)
- **Aid only in the between:** her actual help mechanic lives in the between-spaces — corridors, thresholds, doorways, zone seams. The parts of a building nobody lives in. Oriel is the one figure whose mechanic lives in the architecture itself.
- **THE AURALLIA CALIBRATION (ruled Krista, 2026-10-09):** "She has a similar role to Aurallia, but more of a scope area wise." Aurallia (The Hearth, master dossier complete) is unconditional love as PERSON: the Radius extends one step within direct physical presence — peace enforced by radiance, heartbeat UI slows, camera goes intimate, the SIT mechanic embodied. Oriel is the SAME LAW AT BUILDING SCALE: same warmth, but the radius is architectural, not personal. Aurallia is the fire you walk to; Oriel is the heat already in the hallways. The warmth mechanic is shared; the scope is the difference: one step vs. the between-spaces of the entire Inn.
- **THE PAUSE COMPANION (wired from the hard zone line law):** the Catacombs and arcade hard thresholds (ruled 2026-10-04, commit canon) take the old EverQuest loading pause. That pause is a between-space with nothing in it — now it has someone in it. At every hard zone line in the Inn, Oriel is the company while the next zone loads. The window you look out of while the wall changes around you.
- **Open gates closed by this ruling:** roster status (ambient companion, not quest-NPC) and zone slot (all zones / none of them — resident of the between). Remaining open: The Catalyst overlap, relationship to The Decision, portrait under her own name.

## THE VIALS (ruled Krista, 2026-10-09): THE BLENDING VESSEL

**Krista's ruling, verbatim:** "She can give vials. That store as a vessel of being able to blend the sounds of our zones as a player chooses to."

- **The object:** Oriel gives vials — small glass vessels that hold SOUND. Each vial stores the voice of a zone: its resting hum, its signature instrument, its melody.
- **The filling:** a vial fills by LISTENING, not by looting. Carry it in a zone and the zone's sound settles into the glass. (No-Fail law native: no wrong way to fill a vial; every zone's voice is worth keeping.)
- **The blend:** with two or more filled vials, a player blends the sounds of zones as they choose — drum circle over cavern hum, carillon under tide. The blend is the player's own composition, playable anywhere, including in the between.
- **The EchoMelody bridge:** the vial is the physical form of an EchoMelody record (entity exists: melodyName, sourceNpc, sourceStone, voicePart, playerId). Filled vial = kept melody. Blending = composing with the archive. The REC law is the memory; the vial is the pocket the memory travels in.
- **The between synergy (wired):** the between-spaces are the natural blending studio — the one place all zones' sounds are equally distant. Blends made in the between carry a little of Oriel's continuity no matter where they're played.
- **THE VIAL LOGISTICS (wired 2026-10-09, Krista's ruling): "We already have the two delivery teams in staff."** Oriel's vials ship via the INN POST — and both crews already live in her territory:
  - **THE BONE MAIL CREW (the skeleton machines, Inn Post master spec):** fixed routes, freight, scheduled runs, honor the stamp absolutely. A vial in a bone container arrives precisely, on schedule, soft-shoe dispatch and all. NEW LAW (wired): **THE CART HUM** — bone containers are wheeled and rattly, and a vial full of zone-voice makes the whole cart resonate the tune the entire route. The Postmaster's dispatch IS choreography (canon); now the route IS the melody. The Inn hears the mail coming. Delivery notification by song.
  - **THE GOBLIN COURIERS (the Labyrinth family, last mile and irregular addresses):** labyrinth routes by right of habitat; delivery IS the finding (canon). A vial through goblin hands arrives by being found — and the Finders' catalog files by what the thing was DOING when it got lost: a vial is filed as **SINGING**. The shelf logic holds perfectly (the Unclaimed Resonance's sibling — the Finders already keep almost-tones until the hummer returns; a vial IS a kept tone, formalized).
  - **THE COURIER'S LAW, VIAL EDITION:** a goblin carrier doesn't read the brothers' mail (canon) — but a vial is mail you can't help overhearing. Goblin discretion adapts: the melody arrives intact, seal chewed, meaning whole; the goblin was just humming it three hallways before delivery. Not reading. Catching. (The goblins saw it coming. They see everything coming.)
  - **The Postmaster's script already knows the word:** the silent register's vocabulary includes "fragile: feelings" (canon). A vial ships under it. The keepsake drawer (padded, felted wool) is the vial's parcel class.
- **Anti-Skinner check:** vials are never consumable, never degrading, never trade-currency. A blend is for the player's own ears. No grind, no meter, no reward loop — just keeping.
- **Honest-label note (flagged, not ruled):** the vial is a Soulstice-shaped object. Whether a real-world jewelry piece (wire-wrapped glass vial pendant) ever joins the artisan listings is Krista's ruling alone.
