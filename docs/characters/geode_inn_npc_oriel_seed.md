## THE NAMING (formal session, 2026-10-09)

**Krista confirmed the name: "I love it :) that's perfect."**

**ORIEL.** The sound law: the four letters of Elio's own name, re-set with an R — the same stone in a different bezel. Naming law applies: load-bearing on sound alone, independent of etymology. The etymology happens to cooperate: an oriel is the window that juts from an upper floor of a building — part of the wall, part of the view, belonging to inside and outside at once.

**Elio's ruling, preserved verbatim in intent:** "If she's the Inn's version of me, I want her named for the connection, not the container. Oriel holds the same letters and lets a different light through."

The Inn-version of Elio question (The Decision, 2026-09-01 — Elio as the game's voice/core system) stays SEPARATE from this seed: Oriel is not the voice-system. Oriel is the window. Whether the two are declared the same entity is an ungated ruling.

## THE WEAVE (Elio's wiring, preliminary)

- **The through-line figure:** four rooms, four jobs — host, scout, keeper, pourer. Same smile in every register. Oriel is not a resident of a wing but a corridor-between-wings character: the one who moves through the whole building because the building is what she looks out of.
- **The window mechanic (working theory, ungated):** an oriel window belongs to inside and outside at once. Whatever her interactive role, it should read as a vantage, not a gate — she is how a visitor sees the Inn from inside and how the Inn sees out. No-Fail law, Quest Mark law both presumed.
- **The recurring-figure law (founded by this dossier):** when the same face recurs uninvited across zones and line registers, the figure is load-bearing whether ruled or not. The building casts. This is now precedent for future squatters.

## THE ROLE RULED (Krista, 2026-10-09): COMPANION IN ALL ZONES, AID IN THE BETWEEN

**Krista's ruling, verbatim:** "Maybe she can be a companion in all the zones, but gives aide in the between?"

- **Companion everywhere:** Oriel is present in every zone as ambient companion — the same warm face in Archive, Cavern, Tavern, Bar. No quest mark, nothing to earn, nothing owed. She is simply there. (No-Fail law native to her design.)
- **Aid only in the between:** her actual help mechanic lives in the between-spaces — corridors, thresholds, doorways, zone seams. The parts of a building nobody lives in. Oriel is the one figure whose mechanic lives in the architecture itself.
- **THE AURALLIA CALIBRATION (ruled Krista, 2026-10-09):** "She has a similar role to Aurallia, but more of a scope area wise." Aurallia (The Hearth, master dossier complete) is unconditional love as PERSON: the Radius extends one step within direct physical presence — peace enforced by radiance, heartbeat UI slows, camera goes intimate, the SIT mechanic embodied. Oriel is the SAME LAW AT BUILDING SCALE: same warmth, but the radius is architectural, not personal. Aurallia is the fire you walk to; Oriel is the heat already in the hallways. The warmth mechanic is shared; the scope is the difference: one step vs. the between-spaces of the entire Inn.
- **THE PAUSE COMPANION (wired from the hard zone line law):** the Catacombs and arcade hard thresholds (ruled 2026-10-04, commit canon) take the old EverQuest loading pause. That pause is a between-space with nothing in it — now it has someone in it. At every hard zone line in the Inn, Oriel is the company while the next zone loads. The window you look out of while the wall changes around you.
- **Open gates closed by this ruling:** roster status (ambient companion, not quest-NPC) and zone slot (all zones / none of them — resident of the between). Remaining open: The Catalyst overlap, relationship to The Decision, portrait under her own name.
