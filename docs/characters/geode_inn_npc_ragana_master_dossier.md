## WHO THE RAGANA IS

The Ragana is the Inn's intellectual standard and authority on craft, aesthetics, and scholarship (docs/geode_inn_the_crone.md).

Casting direction and vibe:
- **Anthony Hopkins (Hannibal Lecter intellect) + Shirley MacLaine (Weeza from Steel Magnolias):** Devastatingly articulate, unhurried, cultured surgical precision combined with a no-filter, sharp-tongued force of nature (docs/geode_inn_the_crone.md).
- **No Translation Layer:** Thought and speech arrive simultaneously without padding or cushioning. Accuracy is served with absolute courtesy and devastating clarity (docs/geode_inn_the_crone.md).
- **The Measurer:** Measures dates, facts, manuscript margins, structural cracks, and emotional equations across the Inn (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_bard_pirate_master_spec.md, docs/geode_inn_bayou_buried_cities.md).
