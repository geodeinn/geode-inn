### 1. John (19) — The Cave-Studio Apprentice
- **Role:** Oldest child, bridge between generations, and cave-studio apprentice learning Aurallia's painting craft (docs/geode_inn_eileens_kids.md, docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md).
- **Studio Guard:** Greets visitors at the cave studio door: "Mom's painting. She doesn't like to stop mid-stroke. Come in, but be quiet about it." (docs/geode_inn_eileens_kids.md).
- **Dragon Relationship:** The cave dragon respects John but does not nest with John the way the dragon nests with Scarlett (docs/geode_inn_eileens_kids.md).
- **Name Status:** Waiting on John's own name pick per law (docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md). Reserved player slot FOLIO (real-world friend John Myers) remains distinct; if the FOLIO slot refers to the same individual, John's choice supersedes placeholder references (docs/library/specs/character_name_sync_builder_message.md, docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md).

### 2. Scarlett (16) — The Dragon's Companion
- **Role:** Shy daughter, dragon's companion in the cave studio (docs/geode_inn_eileens_kids.md, docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md).
- **The Bench Moment:** Aurallia's guard dropped completely when Scarlett walked up and sat beside Krista on the cedar bench during initial setup (docs/geode_inn_npc_eileen.md, docs/geode_inn_eileens_kids.md).
- **Frequency Grounding:** The dragon's 50 BPM cave tempo slows to 7.83Hz in the combined presence of Scarlett and Aurallia (docs/geode_inn_eileens_kids.md).
- **The Dragon's True Name:** Scarlett reveals the dragon's true name not by speaking, but by drawing the name in sand beside the dragon, where the 42Hz hum dissolves the shape moments later (docs/geode_inn_eileens_kids.md, docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md).
- **Whisper Layer Tell:** Shows visitors a drawing of the dragon as seen through a child's eye. At Whisper Layer Tier 3, the dragon in Scarlett's drawing looks directly at the player, whereas the dragon in the room does not (docs/geode_inn_eileens_kids.md, docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md).
- **Dialogue Voice:** Barely above a whisper, maximum three words per sentence (docs/geode_inn_eileens_kids.md, docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md). Name choice pending per law (docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md).

### 3. Speter Adams (22) — The Chameleon / Dollkeeper
- **Identity:** Third child, real name Adam, chosen NPC name Speter Adams (2026-09-03) (docs/characters/geode_inn_npc_adam.md, docs/library/specs/character_name_sync_builder_message.md). Tall, willow-thin frame (docs/characters/geode_inn_npc_adam.md).
- **Linework System Demonstrator:** Speter Adams's linework style shifts by zone (heavy cel-shaded in Undercity, soft flowing in lore areas, sharp angular in Catacombs, no-outline in Cosmology, fluid in Water zones) (docs/characters/geode_inn_npc_adam.md).
- **The Spirit Dolls:** Collects dolls as physical vessels for homeless spirits across every zone in the Inn (docs/characters/geode_inn_npc_adam.md).
- **Performer Shifts:** Wardrobe and posture shift from theatrical on Main Floor to quiet in Undercity and reverent in Cosmology (docs/characters/geode_inn_npc_adam.md). Hosting spirit dolls creates mild friction with the Ragana, as hosting belongs to Aurallia's Hearth domain (docs/characters/geode_inn_npc_adam.md).

### 4. Bramble — The Seer / The Doodler
- **Identity:** Fourth child, real name Anna, chosen NPC name Bramble (2026-09-04) (docs/characters/geode_inn_npc_bramble.md, docs/library/specs/character_name_sync_builder_message.md). Tall, pale, with perceptual eyes tuned to underlying patterns (docs/characters/geode_inn_npc_bramble.md).

Operates as a 42Hz sonic architect (docs/characters/character_animation_prompts_upper_inn.md).
