## THE NAME (ruled 2026-09-11, named by Krista 2026-09-03)

**VAULKNUT**  the echo of the VALKNUT, Odin's knot. The Inn's name nods to Odin's ravens; the selkie's name nods to Odin's knot: the keeper of the doors between, wearing the knot's name. The one who moves between worlds as naturally as breathing, named for the sign that hangs between.

## WHO SHE IS

The Artisan Thief  one of the five starting classes, and the only one that is a real person's own character (the Lichenblossom precedent). Originally defined as: nimble fingers, low charisma  the jeweler who makes beautiful things and can't sell them. Deepened into: a selkie who CHOSE the land. Not trapped  not the selkie whose skin was stolen, not the selkie who weeps on the shore. She came ashore, shed her skin, picked up tools, and made something. And the making was so good she stayed.

**"Theft" is redistribution**  the selkie's wild sense of justice from the sea, where hoarding sinks you. (The presence panel, canon 2026-09-11: the raven-masked figure slipping a carved gemstone animal into a child's satchel.)

**Human form:** resembles a feminine depiction of the Sandman (Netflix series). THE DOCTOR-MASK REGISTER (ruled 2026-09-11): the plague-doctor-raven mask blend is canon for all future Vaulknut art, held as painted in the presence panel (acc95d68f). ## THE SKIN

The selkie's skin is her JEWELER'S APRON. Nobody touches the apron. The Ragana once reached for it  motherly instinct  and the selkie's eyes went black and cold and ancient for one second, and the Ragana, who is afraid of nothing, took a step back. Neither ever mentioned it again. The Ragana's glasses are her skin. The apron is the selkie's. Some things are not shared.

When she ties the apron, the seal slides off and the maker slides on. The clasp sounds like bone on leather.

## THE DUAL WORLD  AND THE SISTER

The land (the market, the bench, the tools, the making) and the sea (the water zones, weightless and home). The sea never stops calling: the workbench sounds are waves on a rocky shore; the deep hum of the Song of Making is the ocean heard from very far away; the selkie's sea voice is the 40Hz ocean memory  not spoken, felt.

**SISTERS WITH THE SIREN** (ruled 2026-09-11): Vaulknut and KATYA THE SIREN  the Pirate's wife, the water guardian of the 7-Layer Deep, the magnetic one who "hears everything. It's the magnetism. I can't turn it off." The two sisters ARE the Dual World made family:

When Vaulknut hears the sea in the clink of her tools, it is not the ocean calling her home  it is her sister's house. Two sisters, one water system: the siren guards the deep crossing ("Depth."  the one-word warning that is also a navigation instruction); the selkie crosses it.

**THE SPELLING LAW:** Katya has two names and the whole Inn knows exactly one of them is hers. When Vaulknut  and only Vaulknut  addresses her directly, she is **KATIJA**: the way she actually wants it, indulged in quiet defiance of their mother's wrath. The name exists only when Vaulknut says it; Katya never speaks it, only receives it. The name carries grandmother Velta  fluent in Russian and Latvian by age two, rumored Russian royal lineage.

## THE WORKSHOP (the Tidal Caves, passed canon 2026-09-10)

The master crafter works alone in the caves, because the caves hold the only lathe the craft requires: THE TIDE ITSELF. The Vorticist geometry in motion  the water's push and pull is the power source, the rhythm is the tool. The visiting hours are the drain; the craft is the flood.

Her law, overheard, never lectured: **work WITH the breath, never against it.** The artisan never asks the player for anything. The scene's only exchange is the craft itself, seen. (Written as witnessed-only, the Hel precedent  the solitude respected, flagged for Krista's personal review, approved as written.)

**Reward:** the witnessing  the artisan's rhythm internalized: anything the player makes in a water zone comes out truer, worked twice, by the maker and the breath.

**Hook:** "Make it true."  master crafter, Tidal Caves, the three-act crafting quest line.

## THE VOICE

The Bog Fairy Golem's register  the maker's avatar: **the "what if you..." voice.** Asks in curves, lands in exact; generous pauses that are thinking, not hesitation; the question that arrives one word before the answer. The third rung is THE ROOM  Vaulknut stops offering options and simply makes one true. The maker's rung 3 is always a thing that exists now. Cadence: Gaiman mythic-personal + Bradbury fire. Voice profile: warm, precise, generative.
