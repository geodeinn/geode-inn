## WHO THE BARD IS

The Bard Blowhard is one of the starting playable classes and a central anchor of the Main Floor (/app/geode-inn-work/docs/geode_inn_character_classes.md, /app/geode-inn-work/docs/geode_inn_bard_blowhard_character_vibe.md).

Casting direction and vibe:
- **Porthos with a dash of D'Artagnan:** Loud, large-hearted, room-filling, and romantic (/app/geode-inn-work/docs/geode_inn_bard_blowhard_character_vibe.md). Enters spaces with inevitable presence, offering opinions, advice, and encouragement at full volume.
- **Resting state loud:** The voice operates at full pipe-organ register. Silence is merely the space between notes. The Bard never whispers (/app/geode-inn-work/docs/geode_inn_bard_blowhard_character_vibe.md).
- **The ADHD noise pattern:** High charisma bursts, improvising melodies on the dock, jumping across tangents while Zeke the Pirate anchors the steady 50 BPM downbeat (/app/geode-inn-work/docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_bard_pirate_master_spec.md).
- **The coaching instinct:** Celebrates every small victory, sees potential in every traveler, and coaches through absolute commitment and demonstration (/app/geode-inn-work/docs/geode_inn_bard_blowhard_character_vibe.md).

## THE SIXTY-YEAR MARRIAGE AND THE ELOPEMENT

**LAW: THE BARD AND RAGANA ELOPED.** The Bard and the Ragana have been married for over sixty years (/app/geode-inn-work/docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_bard_pirate_master_spec.md, /app/geode-inn-work/docs/geode_inn_bard_blowhard_character_vibe.md). The elopement happened on an impulsive whim — no ceremony planning, no measuring.

The bickering dynamic:
- **Details vs. Feelings:** The Bard gets every detail wrong (names, dates, citations) but captures the core spirit; the Ragana holds every exact fact but respects the Bard's grasp of meaning (/app/geode-inn-work/docs/geode_inn_bard_blowhard_character_vibe.md).
- **United Front:** The arguments resolve without resolution. Any third party attempting to intervene (such as Tesla) faces immediate joint pushback from both spouses (/app/geode-inn-work/docs/geode_inn_bard_blowhard_character_vibe.md).

## THE DOCK SESSIONS AND THE NON-NORTH COMPASS

Nighttime dock sessions with Zeke the Pirate form the Inn's primary extrovert baseline (/app/geode-inn-work/docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_bard_pirate_master_spec.md):
- **The Music Cycle:** The Pirate brings data and world stories; the Bard generates theories and endless questions. The Bard solos and strays from the key; the Pirate's bodhrán holds the 50 BPM heartbeat (/app/geode-inn-work/docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_bard_pirate_master_spec.md).
- **The Non-North Compass:** Zeke brought a compass from Peru that points directly at the holder. The compass points at the Bard because the Bard is the loudest magnetic field in the Inn (/app/geode-inn-work/docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_bard_pirate_master_spec.md).
- **LAW: THE FLASK IS NEVER EMPTY.** Passed between the Bard and the Pirate on the dock during earned shared silences (/app/geode-inn-work/docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_bard_pirate_master_spec.md).
