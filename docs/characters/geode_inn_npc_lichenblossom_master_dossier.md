## WHO LICHENBLOSSOM IS

The Plant Necromancer — the walking Van Gogh garden, the source NPC for the Catalyst, and the master of reanimation (docs/geode_inn_npc_katy.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_walkthrough_dissection_13_main_floor.md). Lichenblossom possesses the power to reanimate and control dead plant life — turning death magic into life magic (docs/geode_inn_npc_katy.md).

**The Reanimation Doctrine (Reanimate, Not Resurrect):**
- **New Forms:** Lichenblossom does not resurrect dead plants into previous shapes; dead wood reanimates into entirely new botanical forms (docs/geode_inn_npc_katy.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_walkthrough_dissection_13_main_floor.md).
- **Control Through Relationship:** Plants reach toward Lichenblossom willingly; control is maintained through relationship rather than domination (docs/geode_inn_npc_katy.md).
- **The Dreads as Mycorrhizal Antenna:** Long, deep henna-red dreadlocks function as the physical antenna connecting Lichenblossom to the mycorrhizal root network (docs/geode_inn_npc_katy.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_walkthrough_dissection_13_main_floor.md).

## THE BEAT-SYNCED VAN GOGH BLOOM-DIE EFFECT

Lichenblossom wanders the Main Floor garden paths (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_walkthrough_dissection_13_main_floor.md).

**Mechanic Details:**
- **Beat-Synced Pulse:** As Lichenblossom moves through the garden, chaos-Druid energy causes the surrounding flora to swirl and pulse (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_walkthrough_dissection_13_main_floor.md).
- **Rhythmic Cycle:** Plants bloom and die on each beat of the grinding music — turning sonic frequency into visible petal motion (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_walkthrough_dissection_13_main_floor.md).
- **The Three Garden Keepers:** The Catalyst grows what shouldn't grow (red / will); the Greenward keeps what grows held gently (green / patience); Lichenblossom makes the growth visible (the Van Gogh effect) (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_walkthrough_dissection_13_main_floor.md).
