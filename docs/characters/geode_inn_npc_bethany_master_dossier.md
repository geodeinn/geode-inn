## WHO BETHANY IS

The Tavern Spine and Campgrounds Operations Co-Manager (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md). If Jay is the tavern's social heart and storyteller, Bethany (Wisteria) is the tavern's structural spine—managing operations, inventory, food preparation, repairs, scheduling, and tavern rules (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md).

**1. Tavern Management & Logistics:**
- **Operations Anchor:** Manages the Huntsman Tavern's daily business in the Level 4 Campgrounds (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_walkthrough_dissection_14_campgrounds.md). While Jay handles the bar and tells stories, Bethany maintains accounting, stock supplies, and tavern maintenance (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md).
- **The Map Wall Quest Board:** Oversees the tavern's quest wall, posting available bounties, local job requests, and zone notices for visiting players (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md).
- **The Campgrounds Cookbook Mechanic:** Manages tavern meal preparation (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md). Meals cooked by Bethany provide stamina and frequency buffs for players venturing into the surrounding woods (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md).

**2. Hospitality with Edges:**
- **Tavern Rules:** Maintains warm hospitality backed by firm authority (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md). Enforces three non-negotiable tavern rules: "Be kind, pay your tab, don't upset the wolf." (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md).
- **Wolf Dynamics:** Jay's wolf companion follows Jay, but respects Bethany implicitly, acknowledging Bethany's authority behind the bar (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md).
