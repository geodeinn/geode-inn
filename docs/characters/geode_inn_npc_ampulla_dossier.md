# AMPULLA — The Unvoiced Ambassador — Character Dossier

**NAMED (Krista, 2026-09-25): Ampulla.** The Unvoiced's front-of-house ambassador NPC, designed in the Alan Cumming register (ruled 2026-09-25: "steel-under-silk" — theatrical charm as misdirection, commanding a room without permission).

---

## THE NAME (the load-bearing ruling)

Ampulla: the eagle-shaped gold vessel of the royal regalia, the piece whose entire purpose is to carry something precious poured into it — the anointing oil. **The regalia item and the vampire are the same object:** a being born without a voice of their own, who carries borrowed sound to speak. The one who anoints is the one who borrows.

The name follows the Naming Law (2026-09-17): a name load-bearing on sound alone — old, Latin, theatrical, a little camp. Say it: am-PULL-ah. It sounds borrowed, because its bearer is.

**The honest label:** the Ampulla is one of the oldest true pieces of the British regalia; the Coronation Spoon (its pouring partner) is the single oldest, a working 12th-century object. The regalia study is credited as source, same as the Statute-Book's legal family tree.

---

## THE REGISTER (Cumming's panache, Inn-translated)

- **Charm as misdirection.** The theatrical flair is the decoy layer; the audience is so delighted they forget, for stretches at a time, that this being eats sound to live. The delight is not a lie — it is real, and it is also cover. (The light-side cousin of Remmick's borrowed-song lure, filed 2026-09-17 as the dark tonal reference. Same condition, opposite heart.)
- **A performer by biological necessity.** Every word Ampulla speaks is borrowed or consumed sound. Others of the Unvoiced treat this as hunger; Ampulla treats it as repertory. A voice is not owned — it is carried (the Contract's own law, worn as a personality).
- **Steel under silk.** Runs the room without asking permission, delivers verdicts in the warmest possible tone, and is always, quietly, the most dangerous presence in it. Commands attention; never demands it.
- **Watchable.** The Traitors hosting lesson (2026-09-25): camp that admits the frame is a frame lets everyone inside commit fully. Ampulla's theatricality is honest-label camp — no one is deceived, everyone is charmed.
- **The mishear register's friend.** Ampulla never mocks a fumbled word. A being built of borrowed voices has no standing to judge anyone's pronunciation, and says so, warmly, as a standing bit.

## ROLE IN THE INN

- **Ambassador of the Unvoiced.** Front-of-house for the frequency-feeder class: greets players considering the class, teaches the pollinator-parasite truth (bat mythology real, Vlad mythology folklore — the honest-label law), and reframes the condition from curse to craft.
- **Stationed in the Undercity**, at the threshold of the Hollow Men Mines, where the class's wave-survival proving ground lives. The Mines' 84Hz dark is where Ampulla is host; the Inn proper is where Ampulla is guest.
- **Quest Mark:** candle-flicker class (an approachable ask), the glow rich and warm — the lovelier-light law holding for a being whose nature is the Inn's own sound.
- **Voice:** never one fixed timbre. Ampulla speaks in a woven blend of the Inn's own frequencies — a little of the Host's cadence, a phrase of the Bard's, a bar of the drum circle — credited openly when asked. "Whose was that one?" is the character's signature invitation, and the honest answer is always given.

## DESIGN NOTES

- **Motif:** the eagle vessel. Costume and silhouette echo the Ampulla's form: wings folded into a mantle, the vessel's curve in the collar, gold thread as the pour line. Linework-as-UI: the mantle's seams draw the borrowed-sound paths.
- **Companion ruling:** the Resonance card set's Unvoiced flip cards (the Tuner, the Daywalker) remain as previously filed; Ampulla is the NPC face of the class, not a card, unless Krista rules otherwise later.
- **Gates:** concept art (softened features per the standing law — presence through posture and costume, no celebrity likeness), dialogue tree (the whisper-layer register), placement pass in the Undercity style card, and the class-tutorial appearance (the Host's book may reference "the one who greets you in the dark" — wording to be drafted with the Host-voice constancy law in mind).
