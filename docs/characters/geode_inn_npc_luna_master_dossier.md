## WHO LUNA IS

The Zen Jeweler of the Campgrounds and master of the stone-setting craft dialect (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_walkthrough_dissection_14_campgrounds.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_craft_curriculum_master_spec.md). Raised by hippies, possessing wild brown hair and a deep jeweler's calm, Luna provides the peaceful counterweight to Jay's loud showmanship in the Campgrounds (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_walkthrough_dissection_14_campgrounds.md, docs/characters/geode_inn_npc_jay.md).

**1. The Studio and Environment:**
- **Luna's Tent-Studio:** Canvas tent with sides rolled up, positioned in the Level 4 Campgrounds Market Field, open to the weather and visitors (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_walkthrough_dissection_14_campgrounds.md).
- **The Bench:** The studio's central altar where stones are shaped, polished, and set (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_walkthrough_dissection_14_campgrounds.md). Watching Luna work functions as a meditative idle rest-state for players, providing the Campgrounds' counterpart to the Library Nook (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_walkthrough_dissection_14_campgrounds.md).
- **The Stone Library:** Mineralogy collection organized by Mohs hardness, serving as the Inn's interactive geology classroom (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_walkthrough_dissection_14_campgrounds.md).
- **Ambient Soundscape:** The rhythmic sound of stone-on-stone and the slow, steady rotation of the lapidary tumbler (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_walkthrough_dissection_14_campgrounds.md).

**2. The Craft Discipline & Bead Composer:**
- **The Wearable Songs:** Display board showcasing finished jewelry pieces known as "Wearable Songs"—bead compositions encoding musical melodies (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_walkthrough_dissection_14_campgrounds.md, docs/geode_inn_bead_composer.md).
- **Bead Composer Tutorial Anchor:** Luna's studio serves as the physical tutorial layer and inspiration gallery for the Bead Composer system (docs/geode_inn_bead_composer.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_bead_composer_master_spec.md).
- **Pillar One Mastery (The Stone):** Teaches the stone-setting dialect of the Craft Curriculum under the law that Pattern = Frequency = Function (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_craft_curriculum_master_spec.md). Under the anti-gatekeeping law, players who focus exclusively on Luna's bench achieve complete craft mastery without mandatory cross-discipline penalties (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_craft_curriculum_master_spec.md).

- **Lapidary & Gemology:** Master-level stone faceting, polishing, hardness classification, and structural setting (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_walkthrough_dissection_14_campgrounds.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_bead_composer_master_spec.md).
- **Sonic Bead Composition:** Expertise in translating musical frequencies and melodic scales into physical bead sequences (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_craft_curriculum_master_spec.md, docs/geode_inn_bead_composer.md).
- **Culinary Precision:** Prepares the Huntsman Tavern stew correctly, balancing Jay's experimental cooking with grounded quality (docs/characters/geode_inn_npc_jay.md).
