## WHO CHARON IS

Charon is the ferryman of the underground crossing, holding the water boundary between the catacombs and the deeper layers of the Inn (docs/geode_inn_charon.md). Charon is neither a skeleton nor a grim reaper, but an honest worker whose purpose is moving travelers from one shore to the other (docs/geode_inn_charon.md).

**1. The Ferryman of the 84Hz River:**
- **Subterranean Waterway:** In the deepest strata of the catacombs—below ancient burial sites and stone ruins—runs an underground 84Hz river that flows beneath the Undercity streets (docs/geode_inn_charon.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_catacombs_weave_spec.md). Charon poles an ancient boat across this dark water, providing the only passage between levels (docs/geode_inn_charon.md).
- **Purpose Over Fear:** The Inn strips frightening mythological figures to honest core functions: the Morrigan washes, Anubis weighs, and Charon moves (docs/geode_inn_charon.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_anubis_master_spec.md). Charon does not judge, preach, or welcome; Charon carries (docs/geode_inn_charon.md).

**2. The Obol Mechanic (Stone Payment):**
- **The Mineral Toll:** To cross the subterranean river, Charon holds out a weathered hand (docs/geode_inn_charon.md). The traveler must yield one stone from the inventory (docs/geode_inn_charon.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_anubis_master_spec.md). Charon accepts the stone and drops the mineral into the dark river, returning the stone to the earth (docs/geode_inn_charon.md).
- **The Act of Letting Go:** The stone toll is not a penalty, but a ritual of release (docs/geode_inn_charon.md). Payment requires choosing which stone—and which memory—to relinquish in order to proceed deeper into the world (docs/geode_inn_charon.md). Charon waits patiently if a traveler hesitates, because Charon has all the time in the world (docs/geode_inn_charon.md).

**3. The LOZEN Reveal & Memory's Illusion:**
- **The River's True Name:** On initial world maps and early zone charts, the subterranean river is designated as "Memory's Illusion" (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_atlantis_quest_chain.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_tide_system_tie_in.md).
- **100% Water System Completion:** When a player completes 100% of all water-system quests across the Inn, Charon pauses the boat mid-crossing, looks directly at the traveler, and reveals the river's true name: LOZEN (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_atlantis_quest_chain.md). Upon this reveal, the world map permanently updates from MEMORY'S ILLUSION → LOZEN (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_atlantis_quest_chain.md).

**4. Persephone's Third Home & The Stygobite Below-Court:**
- **Seasonal Ferry Transit:** The 84Hz underground river serves as Persephone's third home and seasonal transit corridor between the surface caves and the winter station pit (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_tide_system_tie_in.md).
- **The Stygobite Escort:** As Persephone rides the underground tide, Charon's boat is flanked by the stygobite below-court—eyeless, transparent, slow-adapted creatures belonging to the anchialine dark water system (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_tide_system_tie_in.md). This subterranean layer keeps itself, undisturbed by surface politics (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_tide_system_tie_in.md).

## THE VOICE AND DUAL ACTOR LAW

- **Anthony Hopkins Dual Performance Model:** Shared vocal archetype with the Ragana (docs/geode_inn_charon.md). While the Ragana embodies Anthony Hopkins' razor-sharp intellect and verbal measurement, Charon embodies Anthony Hopkins' silence, weight, and stillness (docs/geode_inn_charon.md). Draws on Stevens in *The Remains of the Day* (devotion to duty), Lecter in *Silence of the Lambs* (piercing clarity), and Ford in *Westworld* (system understanding) (docs/geode_inn_charon.md).
- **Signature Dialogue Line:** Speaks only three words per crossing upon reaching the destination shore: "...There you are." (docs/geode_inn_charon.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_anubis_master_spec.md). Delivered with deep, clear, unhurried resonance (docs/geode_inn_charon.md).
- **Acoustic Frequency:** ~110Hz or deep silence (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_mesopotamia_master_spec.md, docs/geode_inn_charon.md).
- **Zone Domain:** Catacombs (Level 4) ferry landing and the 84Hz subterranean river (docs/geode_inn_charon.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_catacombs_weave_spec.md).

## EQUIPMENT & ARTIFACTS

- **The Boat:** An ancient, warped wooden vessel with water sitting in the bilges that floats on purpose rather than mere buoyancy (docs/geode_inn_charon.md). Holds exactly two: Charon in the stern with pole in hand, and the passenger in the bow (docs/geode_inn_charon.md).
- **The Pole:** A long wooden staff worn smooth at handgrips by millennia of poling (docs/geode_inn_charon.md). The wood exhibits two distinct shades divided by the waterline: dry above (the living land) and wet below (the deep water) (docs/geode_inn_charon.md).

## THE WEAVE AND CONNECTIONS

- **The Ragana:** Passage counterpart (docs/geode_inn_charon.md). The Ragana measures life and records annotations; Charon carries what the Ragana measures (docs/geode_inn_charon.md).
- **Anubis:** Parallel death guide in the Egyptian zone (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_anubis_master_spec.md). Anubis weighs the heart while Charon poles the boat (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_anubis_master_spec.md).
- **The Morrigan:** Decision guide vs. transport worker (docs/geode_inn_charon.md). The Morrigan determines readiness at the ford, while Charon provides passage at the river (docs/geode_inn_charon.md). The Morrigan calls Charon "reliable" (docs/geode_inn_charon.md).
- **Katya the Siren:** Katya guards the Water Door into the 7-Layer Deep under the single-word law "Depth." and greets Charon's ferry passengers upon transit (docs/characters/geode_inn_npc_katya_master_dossier.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_tide_system_tie_in.md).
- **The Host:** Main Floor counterpart (docs/geode_inn_charon.md). The Host once walked down through the catacombs to the river's edge to share a warm cup of tea with Charon in silent camaraderie (docs/geode_inn_charon.md).
