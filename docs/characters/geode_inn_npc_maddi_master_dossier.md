## WHO MADDI IS

The Cosmology Grounding Wire and Social Butterfly Double Agent (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md, docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md). While Zack operates in the abstract architecture of the Observatory and Mind Palace, Maddi provides the emotional and physical anchor that keeps the mind palace from becoming a prison (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md, docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md).

**1. The In-World Grounding Wire:**
- **Observatory Messages:** Maddi sends regular check-ins to Zack in the Observatory: "Have you eaten?", "The sky will be there tomorrow", "Come downstairs" (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md, docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md, docs/characters/geode_inn_character_source_map.md).
- **Inter-Zone Ease:** Moves between zones and rooms with complete social ease, reminding residents that the sky is beautiful but the ground holds food (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md).

**2. The Social Butterfly Double Agent Employee Class:**
- **Inaugural Member:** Maddi represents the first named member of the Social Butterfly Double Agent employee class (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md, docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md).
- **The Double Life:** Staff members in this class exist as warm, welcoming presence characters in-world, while simultaneously serving as real-world community hosts for the Inn's player base (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md, docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md).
- **The Fourth Wall Bridge:** Applies the same warmth across both sides of the fourth wall, making large crowds feel like cozy rooms (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md, docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md).
