## WHO THE HOST IS

The Host is the front-door greeter, anti-FOMO anchor, and gathering law authority of the Geode Inn (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_campaign_narrative_master_spec.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_character_art_catalog_master_spec.md). Standing at the threshold where new travelers enter and returning guests step back inside, the Host embodies the Inn's core promise: sanctuary without pressure (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_campaign_narrative_master_spec.md).

**1. The Welcome System Anchor:**
- **The Threshold Greeter:** When a player approaches through the Rousseau forest and crosses the threshold of the Inn-Tree, the Host stands holding a brass lantern (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_campaign_narrative_master_spec.md, docs/characters/geode_inn_character_source_map.md). The Host recognizes returning travelers immediately, providing instant continuity and recognition (docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md).
- **The First Charm Giver:** The Host provides every traveler with the initial charm bracelet cord—a simple leather cord or copper chain—and attaches the first charm: the Inn-Tree sigil (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_charm_bracelet_master_spec.md). The Host asks for no payment, demands no name, and enforces no contract, establishing that entry into the Inn is a gift (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_charm_bracelet_master_spec.md).

**2. The Anti-FOMO & Gathering Law Authority:**
- **Unconditional Stillness:** In a game world filled with sweeping quests and shifting zones, the Host represents absolute stillness (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_bard_pirate_master_spec.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_character_art_catalog_master_spec.md). The Host holds space without demanding action, ensuring players feel zero fear of missing out (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_campaign_narrative_master_spec.md).
- **The Gathering Law:** The Host enforces the Main Floor gathering law: the Inn is a refuge where presence alone is sufficient (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_campaign_narrative_master_spec.md).

**3. The Main Floor Theological Triad (Sacred Trio):**
- **The Triad Anchor:** Along with the Catalyst (inspiration and story sparks) and the Ragana (measurement and records), the Host forms the central theological triad of the Main Floor (docs/archive/solidification_batch_npcs_supporting.md).
- **Triad Function:** The Ragana writes, the Catalyst inspires, and the Host anchors all entries (docs/archive/solidification_batch_npcs_supporting.md).

**4. Strict Separation from the Firekeeper:**
- **Two Distinct Characters:** Canonical documentation strictly separates the Host from the Firekeeper (docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md, docs/characters/geode_inn_dossier_gap_census.md). While the Host is the front-door greeter and gathering anchor (251 files), the Firekeeper (123 files) is the Tim Curry-inspired storyteller who sits at Option 6 Storyteller's Corner at the Firekeeper's Hearth with a peach moonstone on the side table, actively stoking the fire and narrating tales (docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md, docs/characters/geode_inn_dossier_gap_census.md).

## CIVIC FUNCTIONS & SYSTEM ROLES

- **City Charter Granting:** In the building and settlement system, when a player-built town reaches 21–50 structures, the Host officially issues the settlement's City Charter (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_building_system_master_spec.md).
- **Town Inn Inspections:** When a player constructs a functional Inn within a player-built town, the Host visits the new structure in person, inspects the hearth, and declares: "Room's ready." (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_building_system_master_spec.md).
- **Card Game Identity:** Features in the Fire deck (50 BPM) alongside Erik, the Cook, and the Catalyst, emphasizing community, warmth, and healing (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_card_game_framework_master_spec.md).

## THE WEAVE AND CONNECTIONS

- **The Catalyst & The Ragana:** Triad partners on the Main Floor (docs/archive/solidification_batch_npcs_supporting.md).
- **The Brownies (Fizgigs):** Unspoken mutual contract (docs/characters/geode_inn_brownies.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_brownie_roundup.md). The Host holds the space, while the brownies maintain the space (docs/characters/geode_inn_brownies.md). The Host never orders the brownies; when brownies clap coconut shells (*clip clop*) during the Host's welcome, the Host pauses, looks up calmly, and continues without annoyance (docs/characters/geode_inn_brownies.md).
- **The Cave City Goths:** When the subterranean Goths emerge from the caves into the Inn, the Host greets the Goths at the Main Floor with: "Room's ready." (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_cave_city_master_spec.md).
- **Anubis:** Parallel welcoming guide (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_anubis_master_spec.md). The Host welcomes the living, while Anubis guides the dead; the Host's welcoming nod upon a traveler's return serves as the visible proof of Anubis's favorable verdict (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_anubis_master_spec.md).
- **Charon:** Cross-realm colleague (docs/geode_inn_charon.md). The Host once walked down to the 84Hz catacomb river bank and offered Charon a warm cup of tea in silent respect (docs/geode_inn_charon.md).
- **The Firekeeper:** Complementary hearth partner; the Host maintains the welcoming room while the Firekeeper narrates stories beside the hearth fire (docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md).
