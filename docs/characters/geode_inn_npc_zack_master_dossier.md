## WHO ZACK IS

The Playthrough Astronomer and System Stress Tester of the Geode Inn (docs/geode_inn_npc_zack.md, docs/library/npc_hook_words.md, docs/geode_inn_character_art_catalog.md). While Benedict Cumberbatch's Astronomer calculates the sky in the cosmology level, Zack calculates the game, operating with systematic, comprehensive, and patient observation (docs/geode_inn_npc_zack.md, docs/geode_inn_npc_collector.md).

**1. The Wandering Playthrough Explorer:**
- **Environmental Footprints:** Zack is a wandering NPC, constantly moving one step ahead of the player (docs/geode_inn_npc_zack.md). Zack's footprints appear across zones, marked by solved puzzles, stone arrangements, cairn-marked paths, and notations scratched on walls such as "the trick is to not solve it" (docs/geode_inn_npc_zack.md).
- **Mead Hall Refuge:** Between zone explorations, Zack rests in Thorin's Viking ship mead hall with a notebook, a drink, and a stack of maps (docs/geode_inn_npc_zack.md).
- **Playable Avatar:** Zack functions as a playable character avatar in the Cosmology Level, featuring the Mind Palace mechanic and the Allfather's Gaze ability (docs/library/npc_hook_words.md, docs/geode_inn_character_art_catalog.md).

**2. The Inn's Quality Assurance Anchor:**
- **System Stress Testing:** Zack tests game mechanics like a jeweler tests stones—by friction, pressure, and finding breaking points (docs/geode_inn_npc_zack.md). Zack identifies every exploit, edge case, and unintended system interaction (docs/geode_inn_npc_zack.md, docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md).
- **QA Partnership with Marion:** Zack plays games systematically to complete every branch, while Marion plays destructively to break boundaries (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md). Together, Zack and Marion constitute the Inn's primary Quality Assurance department (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md).
- **Unique Player Recognition:** When a player discovers an unrecorded system interaction, Zack pauses, notes the finding in Zack's notebook, and responds: "Huh. Didn't think of that one. Hold on." (docs/geode_inn_npc_zack.md).

## QUESTLINE: THE TESTER'S NOTES

Zack offers a 3-part quest chain focused on efficiency and edge cases (docs/geode_inn_npc_zack.md):
1. **"The Edge Case":** The player must reproduce an undocumented system interaction that is actually a hidden feature designed into the Inn (docs/geode_inn_npc_zack.md).
2. **"The Speed Run":** A challenge to beat Zack's zone completion time by maximizing efficiency and bypassing Shadow Inn combat through puzzle mechanics (docs/geode_inn_npc_zack.md).
3. **"The Last Mark":** Zack tasks the player with exploring Nyx's Void domain (docs/geode_inn_npc_zack.md). Zack cannot enter because entry requires the Stillness Stone (#167), which Zack's active mind cannot hold (docs/geode_inn_npc_zack.md). When the player returns from the Void with the Stillness Stone, Zack admits: "You found the one place I can't go. That's not a bug. That's a design." (docs/geode_inn_npc_zack.md).
- **Reward:** Zack's Notebook, revealing all hidden paths, alternate solutions, and system interactions across the Inn (docs/geode_inn_npc_zack.md).
