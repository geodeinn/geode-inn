## WHO THE TAVERN CHILDREN ARE

TJ and Gabby are presence characters in the Campgrounds — the tavern kids who represent the Inn's next generation growing up in the surrounding woods (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md).

### TJ (The Explorer)
- **Age:** 12 (filler name for now; an in-game name choice will occur later) (docs/characters/character_rename_ledger.md, docs/library/specs/character_name_sync_builder_message.md).
- **Personality:** Brave, curious, exceptionally intelligent while remaining quiet about the fact, "really cool kid" (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md, docs/characters/character_rename_ledger.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_voice_block_registry.md).
- **Role in the Inn:** Explores the woods surrounding the Huntsman Tavern. Jay's derpy wolf companion joins TJ on these expeditions (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md).
- **Linework Aesthetic:** Cel-shaded (tradesman style) (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md, docs/geode_inn_character_art_catalog.md).
- **Dialect:** Quiet-precise — thinks in full paragraphs, speaks in chosen sentences (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_voice_block_registry.md).
- **Hook Word:** "Can I touch it?" (docs/library/npc_hook_words.md, docs/characters/npc_hook_words.md).

### GABBY (The Confident Child)
- **Identity:** Younger kid, quiet, observant, bookish, confident (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_voice_block_registry.md).
- **Role in the Inn:** Scratches pictures in the dirt outside the tavern and draws on tavern napkins — creating images of events that have not occurred yet (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md, docs/geode_inn_character_art_catalog.md).
- **Linework Aesthetic:** Cel-shaded character rendering, with napkin drawings rendered in soft flowing lore style (docs/geode_inn_npc_new_roster.md, docs/geode_inn_character_art_catalog.md).
- **Dialect:** Bright-certain — reflecting the fluency of someone who has read everything the library holds (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_voice_block_registry.md).
- **Hook Word:** "I know this one." (docs/library/npc_hook_words.md, docs/characters/npc_hook_words.md).
