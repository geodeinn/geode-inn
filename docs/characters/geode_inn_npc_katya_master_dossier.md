## THE NAME AND THE SPELLING LAW

**KATYA**  water guardian of the 7-Layer Deep, command voice of the Water Zone (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_bard_pirate_master_spec.md).

In-world naming rules:
- Known universally across the Inn as Katya or the Siren (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_bard_pirate_master_spec.md).
- **THE SPELLING LAW:** Katya is spelled **Katya** in all public files, dialogue, and UI elements. Katya is spelled **Katija** ONLY when Vaulknut addresses Katya directly in dialogue, in quiet defiance of their mother's wrath (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_bard_pirate_master_spec.md, docs/characters/geode_inn_npc_vaulknut_master_dossier.md, docs/comics/strip_001_the_great_renaming.md).
- Ancestral lineage: Spelled to honor grandmother Velta, who was fluent in Russian and Latvian by age two, with rumored Russian royal lineage (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_bard_pirate_master_spec.md).

## WHO KATYA IS

Katya is the Siren, command voice and guardian doorkeeper of the Water Zone (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_bard_pirate_master_spec.md).

Vibe and role:
- Married to Zeke the Pirate; the dock serves as Katya's front porch (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_bard_pirate_master_spec.md).
- Guardian of the 7-Layer Deep; holds the authority and keys to deeper water layers (docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_bard_pirate_master_spec.md, docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_walkthrough_dissection_06_water_zone.md).
- Sister to Vaulknut: the selkie chose the land, the siren kept the sea (docs/characters/geode_inn_npc_vaulknut_master_dossier.md).
