## 1. Speter Adams — The Chameleon / The Dollkeeper
**Eileen's Third Child | Age: 22 | Zone: ALL ZONES (only NPC with a home on every level)**

### Core Mechanic
- Linework style CHANGES by zone — the only character who does this
- Dolls placed in every zone as vessels for spirits
- Can fill any missing NPC role — the Inn never feels empty
- Side quest: find every doll across all zones

### Homes (6)
1. Main Floor — The Stage (dressing room behind arcade)
2. Undercity — The Manga Nook (shared silence with Scarlett)
3. Staircase — The Changing Room (transformation alcove)
4. Catacombs — The Crone's Discomfort (appeared uninvited)
5. Cosmology — The Constellation (dolls as stars, reverent)
6. Water Zone — The Floating Stage (singing, most unguarded)

### The Crone Dynamic
The Crone measures everything. Speter Adams cannot be measured. Speter Adams's charisma makes the Crone hospitable against her nature. Slow-burn quest: the Crone eventually admits Speter Adams is "not unwelcome."

## 3. Jay — The Huntsman / The Tavern Keeper
**Zone: Campgrounds (outside the Inn) | Charles' best friend, Dani's friend**

### Core Identity
- 7+ feet tall. Wolf comes up to the knee (the scale reveal).
- Cherokee cheekbones, Voltaire-goth humor. Heavy cel-shaded linework.
- The Wolf: constant companion. DERPY. Lovable goober. Full-body-weight lean = the wolf's handshake.

### The Tavern
- Small, intentionally small. In the campgrounds outside the Inn.
- True Renaissance faire energy. The faire that never packed up.
- Trade hub, quest giver, information broker, stamina restoration.
- Campgrounds = the Inn's front porch, most accessible zone.

### No-BS Personality
Love is structural — a tavern with a chair that's always yours. Calls nonsense with a grin. Hides intelligence behind volume (same as Charles hides it behind druidic mystery).

## 4. The Clockmaker — Undercity NPC
**Zone: Undercity (deep chamber, past Blacksmith's forge)**

### Core Identity
- Tall, thin, impossibly precise fingers. Ageless. Pale grey eyes behind thick spectacles.
- Canvas coat covered in brass filings — accidental Klimt gold.
- Hands never stop moving — computational tapping even at rest.

### The Workshop
- Bare stone — NO Klimt gold (the only place in Undercity without it)
- The Master Clock: mechanical model of the Inn-Tree's root system. Decades in progress. 42 BPM. Shows tidal states, not hours.
- 11 candles in engineered positions for shadow-free illumination.

### Role
- Makes Element Charm mechanisms (frequency-tuned gear assemblies)
- Makes Quest Charm housings and Clockwork Skies components
- Supply chain with the Blacksmith: 4 conversations in 12 years, all about tolerances. The work IS the care.
- Studies Anna's doodles — they show what the gears actually do.

### Voice
"...Time is not a river. ...Time is a gear. ...It turns, and returns, and turns again."

## 5. The Blacksmith — Undercity NPC
**Zone: Undercity (side chamber, where roots thicken)**

### Core Identity
- Broad, thick, fire-and-metal build. Deep voice, warm, laconic.
- Hands like tools — scarred, calloused, precise differently from the Clockmaker.

### The Forge
- Geode-fire forge — burns with crystal fire. Hotter where the crystal grows.
- Forges items that carry the 42Hz Song in their structure.
- The flame tells the Blacksmith what it wants to become.

### Role
- Forges raw materials into gears for the Clockmaker
- Final charm assembly (combines mechanism + housing)
- Teaches the forging skill tree

### Voice
"The forge doesn't care about your name. The forge cares about your material."
