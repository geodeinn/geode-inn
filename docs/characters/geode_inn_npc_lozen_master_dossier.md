## WHO LOZEN IS

The Sentinel — the 6th playable starting character class in the Festival questline, and the hidden guardian of the Geode Inn (`docs/characters/geode_inn_character_class_sentinel.md`, `docs/geode_inn_family_character_registry.md`). Defined by a former Golden Gloves champion boxing background, an INFJ personality type, and a compact physical build with a dropped center of gravity (`docs/characters/geode_inn_character_class_sentinel.md`, `docs/characters/lozen_questline.md`).

**The Presence Aura:** Perception is deceptive. On first encounter, NPCs and players perceive Lozen as physically larger than the actual physical frame due to the felt weight of total, unwavering presence. On second encounter, the perception corrects, creating confusion that opens unique dialogue paths (`docs/geode_inn_family_character_registry.md`).

**Physical & Tactical Identity:** Built like a boxer — wide stance, dropped center, hands positioned with exact discipline (`docs/geode_inn_family_character_registry.md`). Lozen fills spaces with structural gravity rather than noise or flair, acting as the load-bearing wall of the Inn (`docs/geode_inn_family_character_registry.md`, `docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md`).

**Core Stats & Traits:**
- **Dexterity:** HIGH (boxing footwork and timing, not brute force) (`docs/characters/lozen_questline.md`).
- **Intelligence:** HIGH (reads people and situations instantly) (`docs/characters/lozen_questline.md`).
- **Perception:** HIGH (sees what others miss — first to notice threats, lies, or hidden doors) (`docs/characters/lozen_questline.md`).
- **Intimidation:** HIGH / passive (the stance is in the bones) (`docs/characters/lozen_questline.md`).
- **Approachability:** LOW (instinctive defense) (`docs/characters/lozen_questline.md`).
- **Defense:** HIGH / faceted (multiple layers that must be earned through separately) (`docs/characters/lozen_questline.md`).
- **Endurance:** MODERATE (can sustain, but cannot hold walls forever) (`docs/characters/lozen_questline.md`).
- **Charisma:** LOW (surface) / HIDDEN (core — the warmest presence in the room when reached) (`docs/characters/lozen_questline.md`).

## THE LAWS

**THE KATYA 'DEPTH.' GUARD-VOICE LAW FAMILY:** "The two guardians answer the same way because they hold the same kind of trust." Katya the Siren guards the Water Door ("Depth."); Lozen the Sentinel guards the Front Door ("Halt." / "Oi.") (`docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_voice_block_registry.md`). When Lozen stands in the path at Rung 3, the water behind the player goes still, uniting the Sentinel and the river in one response (`docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_voice_block_registry.md`).

**THE FRONT DOOR AND WATER DOOR LAW:** Lozen holds the surface threshold at the Front Door; Katya holds the bedrock threshold at the Water Door (`docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_voice_block_registry.md`, `docs/MASTER_ASSET_LEDGER.md`, `docs/MASTER_STRUCTURE_BLUEPRINT.md`).

**THE WATER ZONE NAMING LAW:** No underground feature receives a true name until earned through the full water-system quest chain. The underground river 'Memory's Illusion' reveals its true name as LOZEN (`docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_tide_system_tie_in.md`, `docs/MASTER_STRUCTURE_BLUEPRINT.md`).

**THE COSMIC RIDDLE LAW:** Core knowledge is *love, loyalty, and knowledge*. Lozen bypasses cosmic puzzles by lowering layers, dropping defense stats to zero during use (`docs/characters/lozen_questline.md`).

## THE QUESTLINE — SENTINEL'S VIGIL (10-Part Arc)

Sourced from `docs/characters/lozen_questline.md`:

### ACT I: THE LAYERS (Early Game)
- **Quest 1: "Stand" (`river_met`):** First encounter at the Common Room corridor. Unlocks **Quick Read** (passive NPC disposition indicator).
- **Quest 2: "The Gloves" (`river_round_1`):** Golden Gloves maintaining ritual by the fire. Unlocks **Iron Stance** (passive immunity to knockback/stagger).
- **Quest 3: "The Quick Read" (`river_weight`):** World Map analysis of gaps between zones. Unlocks **Sentinel's Sight** (active map thread revelation).

### ACT II: THE CRACKS (Mid Game)
- **Quest 4: "The Shift" (`river_whisper`):** Cinema sequence aftermath; reading the Inn's 42Hz frequency shift.
- **Quest 5: "The Anchor" (`river_dark`):** Escorting the player to the Catacombs entrance. Lozen acts as the anchor for the Chameleon Druid (Charles).
- **Quest 6: "The Circuit" (`river_circuit`):** Shadow Inn discovery; watching the brownies (Brindle, Cobble, Thatch) and the Owl's network.
- **Quest 7: "The Cosmic Riddle" (`river_frequency`):** Staircase wavering at 42Hz/84Hz transition. Reveals core truth (*love, loyalty, knowledge*). Unlocks **The Cosmic Riddle** active ability.

### ACT III: THE LOWERING (Endgame)
- **Quest 8: "The Unwrapping" (`river_unwrap`):** Unwrapping the Golden Gloves in the Common Room. Grants **Golden Gloves** equipment (**Frequency Hold** ability).
- **Quest 9: "The Last Vigil" (`river_final_vigil`):** Standing guard at the door behind the Inn leading to the Final Room.
- **Quest 10: "After" (`river_after`):** Resolution following endgame choice (STAY / LEAVE / HIDE).

## THE VOICE

- **Dialect:** Plainspoken, warm under the armor; boxer cadence — flat, complete, whole warning in one syllable (`docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_voice_block_registry.md`).
- **Delivery:** Short sentences, questions upward, orders flat. Low, even, never raised (`docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_voice_block_registry.md`).
- **Hook Word:** "Stand." (`docs/characters/lozen_questline.md`) / "Halt." (`docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_voice_block_registry.md`).
- **Key Tell:** "Halt." (first fire) → "Oi." (tell fire) (`docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_voice_block_registry.md`).
- **Repeat Ladder:**
  1. Full warning.
  2. "Oi." — thrown like a jab.
  3. THE PATH — steps into the path, idle-stop hold; water behind goes still (`docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_voice_block_registry.md`).
- **Epitaph / Canon Line:** "The river kept the water. I kept the watch." (`docs/zone_specs/geode_inn_voice_block_registry.md`).
