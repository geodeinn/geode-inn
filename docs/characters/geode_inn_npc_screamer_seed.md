## THE HONEST LABEL (source canon, dated)

- **Dungeon Master**, FTL Games, 1987 (SNES port 1992). The game where doors seal behind you and drop a floating, tentacled critter into the room with you.
- The Screamer is a real monster in the source game: the floating thing that reads like a jellyfish that spent a wild night with an alien.
- **Personal canon (Krista, load-bearing):** Dungeon Master is one of the only games Krista has never beaten. The Screamer-trap doors are the wall the run always ended against. The cartridge holds a permanent place on the SNES shelf — **a permanent nemesis.** The honest label is not just the game; it is the never-finished argument with it.

## THE CHARACTER AS RULED (the nomination, now canon)

- **A Catacombs resident. The threshold critter of the hard zone seam.**
- Its whole job: **making sure you mean it before the level takes you.**
- The mechanic: the door seals behind you at the hard seam, and the Screamer is what's inside with you. It never warns. It closes the door and makes you deal with what's in the room. That is its love language.

---

## THE WEAVE (Elio's wiring of the ruling)

- **The hard seam's doorman:** the Catacombs hard-threshold ruling (ruled 2026-10-04, honor of the EverQuest zone-line) needs a resident moment of trepidation at the seam. The Screamer IS that moment. Where Lozen's wall keeps the receipts (Gazette #024, "The Wall That Keeps Receipts"), the Screamer keeps the door.
- **Trepidation, never a gate (No-Fail law holds):** the scream and the sealing are ceremony, not punishment. Failure is information; the Screamer is intensity. The door reopens. The scream is the price of admission, not a wall against it.
- **Toll Booth law, light register:** crossing the Catacombs seam costs something — the way back closes until the threshold is done. The Screamer is the toll taken in gooseflesh instead of coin.
- **Anti-Skinner law:** the Screamer never spawns to farm. It appears at the seam because the seam is where it lives. Trepidation as atmosphere, not as a loop to be optimized.
- **80s-kid register (approved by name):** the design brief is nostalgia-fear, the specific cold-water jolt of a sealed door and a floating silhouette. Soften the geometry, never the jolt.
