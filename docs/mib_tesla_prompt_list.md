# Geode Inn — MIB & Tesla Morphic Prompt Collection

A clean, curated list of all Morphic video prompts relating to:
1. **Men in Black / Mini MIB** (catacomb pursuits, memory erasure, Area 51, subterranean bureaucracy)
2. **Nikola Tesla / Steampunk Tesla** (workshop, resonance chamber, clockwork pigeon, white pigeon, modern legends nook, acoustic alchemy, Council of Thinkers)

Includes existing prompts from `morphic_all_prompts_clean.md` as well as **[NEW]** prompts synthesized from the `council_of_thinkers_design_spec.md` to cover all visual content and encounter gaps.

## Existing Prompts (Extracted from Master Collection)

### 1. Steampunk Tesla — The Workshop

```
Slow orbit around a steampunk workshop bathed in amber lamplight. Brass gears and copper coils cover every surface. In the center, a figure — Tesla, but steampunk — wearing a monocle with brass framing, a waistcoat with copper buttons, and leather gloves stained with machine oil. He adjusts a device that crackles with faint blue-white electrical arcs between two brass spheres. Papers covered in equations flutter on a drafting table. A small clockwork pigeon pecks at scattered gears on the floor. The air shimmers with ozone. Warm brass and copper palette with electric blue accents, slow circular camera, 35mm film aesthetic, shallow depth of field.
```

### 2. Steampunk Tesla — Resonance Chamber

```
Slow tilt-up shot inside a copper-lined resonance chamber. Tesla stands at the base, adjusting dials on a console of brass and glass. The chamber hums — the air itself vibrates visibly, making dust motes dance in geometric patterns. Copper coils ascend the walls in a spiral. At the top of the chamber, a crystal suspended in a brass gimbal begins to glow faintly amber, then blue. Tesla's monocle catches the light. The walls of the chamber are etched with frequency diagrams. The atmosphere is one of discovery — a man on the edge of understanding something the world isn't ready for. Warm copper and amber shifting to cool electric blue, slow tilt-up, 35mm film aesthetic.
```

### 3. Main Floor — Tesla in the Modern Legends Nook

```
Slow pan across the modern legends nook. The Sage gargoyle watches from its alcove. Bookshelves line the walls. A fire crackles in the iron grate. And there, in the corner, a figure — Tesla. Not the historical Tesla, but the steampunk version — brass monocle, copper-buttoned waistcoat, adjusting something on a workbench covered in brass apparatus, scattered papers, and a small clockwork pigeon. He mutters about resonance frequencies. The nook's warm amber light catches the copper. The atmosphere is cozy genius at work. Warm amber and copper palette, slow pan, 35mm film aesthetic, shallow depth of field, firelight flicker.
```

### 4. Clockwork Pigeon — Tesla's Companion

```
Slow push-in on a small clockwork pigeon sitting on a brass workbench. It is beautifully engineered — brass feathers, tiny gear-driven wings, ruby eyes that catch the lamplight. It tilts its head with a soft click of internal mechanisms. It pecks at a scattered gear, picks it up, and re-deposits it in a small tray. The pigeon is clearly Tesla's companion — patient, precise, and slightly absurd. The atmosphere is Victorian steampunk charm. Warm brass and copper palette with ruby accents, slow push, 35mm film aesthetic, shallow depth of field.
```

### 5. Catacombs — Mini MIB Chase

```
Fast tracking shot through narrow catacomb corridors. The camera moves as if fleeing — looking back over a shoulder. Behind, three figures in dark suits — the Mini MIB — pursue with eerie, synchronized movements. They don't run — they glide, as if the floor doesn't affect them. Their faces are blank, smooth, no expression. Ahead, the corridor splits — torchlight marks the left path. The walls are ancient stone with faint carved symbols that pulse amber as the chase passes. The atmosphere is tense, dreamlike, and slightly absurd — these aren't monsters, they're bureaucrats of forgetting. Dark stone with amber torch-glow and cold gray MIB accents, fast tracking, 35mm film aesthetic, motion blur.
```

### 6. Catacombs — The Forgetting

```
Slow push-in on a figure slumped against a catacomb wall. The Mini MIB have caught up. One of them extends a hand — not to strike, but to touch the figure's forehead with a single finger. A ripple of pale blue light passes from the finger into the figure's head. The figure's expression goes from panic to confusion to blank calm. The memory of what they found — the buried city, the ancient structures — fades like mist. The MIB figure straightens its tie, turns, and glides away. The figure sits up, wondering how they got here. The atmosphere is eerie, melancholic, and thought-provoking — the real danger isn't death, it's erasure. Dark stone with pale blue memory-light and amber accents, slow push-in, 35mm film aesthetic, shallow depth of field.
```

### 7. Area 51 — Mini MIB Pursuit

```
Fast tracking shot through the Nevada desert at night. A figure runs across cracked desert floor, sagebrush whipping past. Behind, black SUV headlights appear — then the vehicles seem to shimmer and become three figures in dark suits, gliding across the desert floor without disturbing the sand. The sky above pulses with faint light — something is out there. A chain-link fence with a warning sign appears ahead. The atmosphere is the iconic alien-chase energy, but with the Inn's twist — the MIB don't want to hurt you, they want to make you forget. Cool dark blue desert with amber distant glow and cold gray MIB, fast tracking, 35mm film aesthetic, heat shimmer.
```

### 8. [NEW] Staircase — Tesla and Newton's Acoustic Alchemy Apparatus

```
Slow ascending tracking shot along a swirling stone staircase lined with carved gargoyle sconces and etched alchemical symbols. Steampunk Tesla, in his brass monocle and copper-buttoned waistcoat, stands at a landing adjusting a copper coil and brass tuning fork apparatus linked to a gargoyle's mouth. Isaac Newton stands beside him, holding an alchemical ledger, adjusting his wig in quiet fascination. As Tesla strikes the tuning fork, a 111Hz standing wave of electric blue light resonates through the stone gargoyle, causing its eyes to glow soft gold and dust motes to shimmer in geometric frequency nodes. Warm torchlight blending with cool electric blue, slow ascending camera, 35mm film aesthetic, shallow depth of field, atmospheric dust.
```
