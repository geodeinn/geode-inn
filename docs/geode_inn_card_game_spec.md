# OVERVIEW

A collectible card game set in the Geode Inn universe. Playable online (ranked, casual, multiplayer) and offline (vs AI, solo campaigns, practice). Every NPC, creature, stone, and charm in the Inn is a card. Every zone is a board. The 50 BPM heartbeat is the turn timer. The frequency system is the core mechanic.

This is the sixth product in the Geode Inn line. It is also the connective tissue — the card game is the arcade the traveler plays in the novel, the visual spectacle in the graphic novel, the physical deck in the tabletop game, the sound design in the audiobook, and the daily topic in the podcast.

# THE CARD TYPES

## 1. Stone Cards (198) — The Resource System

Every stone in the Geode Inn database is a card. Stones are the universal resource — every hero can use them. Each stone has:

- **Cost:** Based on Mohs hardness (1-10 → 1-10 Resonance cost)
- **Mind effect:** Mental/spell-like ability (buffs, draws, reveals)
- **Body effect:** Physical/board effect (damage, healing, summoning)
- **Rarity:** Based on category (Precious = Legendary, Semi-Precious = Epic, Chalcedony = Rare, etc.)
- **Art:** The stone's actual appearance, rendered in the hero's zone artist style

When played, the stone sounds its unique tone (Mohs-to-frequency mapping). The tone is the card's audio signature — players learn to recognize stones by sound.

Example cards:
- **Diamond** (Mohs 10) — Cost 10. Mind: Reveal opponent's hand. Body: Destroy all enemy minions with cost <= 5. Legendary.
- **Quartz** (Mohs 7) — Cost 7. Mind: Draw 2 cards. Body: Give all friendly minions +1/+1. Common.
- **Obsidian** (Mohs 5) — Cost 5. Mind: Silence all minions. Body: Deal 3 damage to a minion with Taunt. Rare.
- **Labradorite** (Mohs 6) — Cost 6. Mind: Transform a minion into a random minion of the same cost (adularescence — the shifting flash). Body: Gain 3 Resonance this turn only. Epic.
- **Fire Opal** (Mohs 6) — Cost 6. Mind: Deal 5 damage to the enemy hero. Body: Summon three 1/1 Fire elementals. Epic. (The only faceted opal — the fire exception.)

## 3. Spell Cards — The Frequency System

Spells are organized by frequency. Each frequency has a distinct playstyle:

### 42Hz Cards (Growth/Inn)
- **Song of Making** (Cost 5) — Give all friendly minions +1/+1. If you have 7+ friendly minions, summon the Geode (10/10 with Taunt).
- **The Golden Contract** (Cost 7) — Both players draw 3 cards. Both players gain 3 Resonance. "The Song shall not be silenced."
- **Gargoyle Watcher** (Cost 3) — Set a minion's attack to 1 for 2 turns. (111Hz petrification, softened.)
- **Cabochon Cut** (Cost 2) — Give a minion Divine Shield. If it has an optical phenomenon, also give it Stealth.

### 84Hz Cards (Stay/Petrify)
- **Shadow Inn** (Cost 6) — Destroy all minions. Both players discard their hands. Draw 3 cards.
- **Gorgon's Gaze** (Cost 4) — Destroy a minion. If it was attacking, destroy the attacker too.
- **The Owl Watches** (Cost 3) — Force a minion to attack a minion of your choice. If it survives, give it -2/-2.
- **Petrify** (Cost 2) — Destroy a minion with Taunt. Summon a 0/2 Stone Statue in its place.

### 111Hz Cards (Watch/Awareness)
- **Ceiling Gnome** (Cost 2) — Reveal the opponent's hand. Place a tracking mark on one card (you see when they play it).
- **Whisper Layer** (Cost 5) — For the rest of the game, whenever the opponent draws a card, you see what type it is.
- **The Pulse Line** (Cost 1) — Look at the top card of both decks. Keep one, put the other back.

### 432Hz Cards (Resonance)
- **Caller's Note** (Cost 4) — Resonate: copy the last spell played (by either player).
- **Keeper's Shield** (Cost 3) — Give a minion +0/+5 and "Cannot be targeted by spells."
- **Sage's Wisdom** (Cost 2) — Draw a card. If it's a Stone card, draw another card.

### The In-Between Cards (Hybrid/Shift)
- **Frequency Shift** (Cost 3) — All 42Hz effects on the board become 84Hz effects and vice versa. Minions with Divine Shield lose it and gain +2/+2 instead. Minions with +2/+2 lose it and gain Divine Shield.
- **Einstein's Burrito** (Cost 1) — Do nothing this turn. Gain 3 extra Resonance next turn. "The mundane IS the cosmic."
- **The Murmur** (Cost 10) — Reset the board to its state at the start of the game. Both players shuffle all minions back into their decks. "Before the Song, there was the Murmur."

## 4. Charm Cards — Equipment/Aura

The 118 element charms + quest charms become equipment cards:

- **Iron Charm** (Cost 1) — Give a minion +1/+1. (Element: Iron, atomic number 26)
- **Gold Charm** (Cost 3) — Give a minion +2/+2 and Divine Shield. (Element: Gold, atomic number 79)
- **Quicksilver Charm** (Cost 2) — Give a minion +3 Attack and -1 Health. (Element: Mercury, atomic number 80)
- **Stillness Stone Charm** (Cost 4) — Silence all other minions. The equipped minion gains Taunt. (#167)
- **Octave Stone Charm** (Cost 5) — Double a minion's current Attack and Health. (#168)

## 5. Gargoyle Cards — Persistent Board Effects

Gargoyles are played onto the board and persist until destroyed:

- **The Watcher (111Hz)** (Cost 4, 0/8) — At the start of each turn, look at the opponent's top card.
- **The Listener (64Hz)** (Cost 3, 0/6) — At the end of each turn, heal all friendly minions for 1.
- **The Sage (528Hz)** (Cost 5, 0/10) — All Stone cards cost 1 less.
- **The Gatekeeper** (Cost 4, 2/8) — Enemy minions cannot attack the hero while this is on the board.
- **The Caller (432Hz)** (Cost 3, 0/6) — At the start of each turn, copy the last spell played into your hand.
- **The Keeper** (Cost 5, 0/12) — Your hero cannot take more than 2 damage per turn.

## 6. Companion/Pet Cards

Pets are special minions that sit beside the hero (not on the board) and provide passive effects:

- **Tumble** — Once per game, when a minion would be destroyed, Tumble gets stuck in the doorway and blocks it. The minion survives. Cost: 0. (The Inn's most innocent mechanic.)
- **The Duck** — Once per game, steal a Stone card from the opponent's deck. Cost: 0. (Electroreception.)
- **The Cabbit** — Your hero power costs 1 less. If your hero power is used 5 times, transform the Cabbit into a 5/5 with Charge. Cost: 0. (50 BPM purr.)
- **Bloop** — At the start of your turn, 50% chance to draw a card. (Tesla's cat. Unpredictable.)
- **Huginn** — At the end of your turn, if you have more cards in hand than the opponent, draw a card.
- **Muninn** — At the end of your turn, if the opponent has more cards in hand than you, the opponent discards a random card.

# THE BOARD

## Zone-Based Boards (37 boards)

Each match takes place in an Inn room. The board determines:
- Visual style (the zone's assigned artist)
- Ambient audio (the zone's soundtrack at 50 BPM)
- Board-specific mechanics (some boards have unique interactive elements)

### Board Mechanics Examples
- **The Archive:** Books on shelves can be clicked — each click reveals a random card from either deck.
- **The Main Floor Hearth:** The fire crackles. Clicking it warms a random minion (+1 Health).
- **The Catacombs:** Stone walls. Clicking a wall reveals a Bosch shadow (cosmetic — frequency-dependent silhouette).
- **The Tidal Caves:** The tide rises and falls every 3 turns. At high tide, water minions gain +1/+1. At low tide, non-water minions gain +1/+1.
- **The Cosmology Level:** Dali clocks melt. Time is flexible — the first turn of each player's round takes 60 seconds instead of 90.
- **The Canopy City:** The board sways. Every 5 turns, all minions shift one position left.
- **The Staircase:** The board ascends. Each turn, the active player's minions gain +1/+1 (the ascending semitone).
- **The Shadow Inn (84Hz):** The board is dark. Minions with Divine Shield lose it. The 84Hz hum plays instead of 42Hz.

## Board Skins

The BoardSkin entity already exists in the database. Each skin changes the board's appearance:
- Material type (wood, stone, brass, lapis lazuli, glass)
- Base color, highlight color, shadow color
- Grain direction and texture style
- Rarity and unlock condition
- Zone of origin

Players unlock board skins by completing zone quests. Some skins are zone-specific; others are universal.

# DECK BUILDING

## Rules
- 30-card decks (standard CCG format)
- Maximum 2 copies of each Common/Rare card
- Maximum 1 copy of each Epic/Legendary card
- Must choose a Hero (determines hero power)
- Must choose a Zone (determines available zone-specific cards and board)
- Stone cards are universal (any deck can include any stone)
- Pet cards: maximum 1 pet per deck

## Deck Archetypes (emerging from the design)

### Frequency Decks
- **42Hz Growth:** Stone-heavy, buff-focused, overwhelm with numbers. The Song of Making deck.
- **84Hz Control:** Removal-heavy, petrify-focused, grind the opponent down. The Shadow Inn deck.
- **111Hz Scrying:** Information-focused, track the opponent's hand and deck, win by knowing everything. The Whisper Layer deck.
- **In-Between Shift:** Frequency Shift as centerpiece — flip the board state. The Einstein deck.

### Zone Decks
- **Fae Kingdom:** Chibi Fae minions, Seelie/Unseelie synergy, charm equipment.
- **Water Zone:** Water monster synergy, tide mechanics, the Siren's lure.
- **Undercity:** Death mechanics, the Morrigan's harvest, Charon's crossing.
- **Cosmology:** Time manipulation, the Astronomer's deduction, Nyx's silence.
- **Camelot:** Knight synergy, Arthur's three stages, the Round Table buff.

### Hero-Specific Decks
- **The Crafter (Selkie):** Crafting-focused. Stone cards have enhanced effects. Cabochon Cut as signature spell.
- **The Storyteller (Firekeeper):** Burn damage. The fire crackles. Tale cards deal damage to all enemies.
- **The Carrier (Constant):** Ramp deck. Crystal tokens reduce costs. Play big stones early.
- **The Shapeshifter (Brim):** Transformation deck. Monsieur Sentient as the signature weapon. Vessel swaps minions.

# GAME MODES

## Online Modes

### Ranked Play
- Uses the Leaderboard entity (already designed)
- Weekly seasons (weekNumber + weekYear fields)
- Pair scoring, combo multipliers, faeReputation
- Rewards: stone cards, board skins, charm packs
- Ranked modes: Standard (base set + current expansion), Wild (all cards)

### Casual Play
- Unranked. Any deck. Any zone.
- Friendly matches (challenge specific players)

### The Arena (Draft)
- Draft a deck from random cards
- Play until 12 wins or 3 losses
- Rewards scale with wins
- The Inn's "arcade" — quick, high-stakes, fun

### The Festival (Special Event)
- Based on the "Festival in the Middle of a Raid" questline
- 6 portal encounters as special rulesets:
  - Orc Village: Both players have 10 extra health
  - Murloc Hut: Only Murloc-type minions can be played
  - Elf Hot Tub: Minions gain +1/+1 each turn they survive
  - Dwarf Basketball: Minions can "pass" (swap positions for free)
  - LARP Field: All minions are "in character" (gain random class abilities)
  - Harpy Daycare: Minions that die return to hand with -1/-1

## Offline Modes

### vs AI
- Multiple difficulty levels (Brownie, Guardian, Astronomer, Constant)
- AI uses established NPC decks (play against Beverley's Archivist deck, the Firekeeper's Storyteller deck, etc.)
- AI characters speak in their established voices when making plays
- Unlocks: defeat each AI hero to unlock that hero for play

### Solo Campaigns
- Story-based campaigns following the novel's plot
- Each campaign is 10-15 matches with narrative scenes between them
- Campaign 1: The Arrival (Main Floor — learn the basics)
- Campaign 2: The Descent (Catacombs + Cultural Zones — advanced mechanics)
- Campaign 3: The Ascent (Staircase + Cosmology — high-level play)
- Campaign 4: The Endgame (The Three Choices — final boss matches)
- Completing campaigns unlocks heroes, cards, and board skins

### Practice Mode
- Test decks against AI with no rewards
- Sandbox mode: both players have unlimited Resonance (test combos)

## Monthly: The Egg

- Each month, a new Egg-hatched character becomes a card
- The card is free for all players during its active month
- At month's end, the card "retires" — enters the Echo pool
- Echo cards are available in Wild mode and the Arena
- Community vote determines which retired card returns permanently
- The daily comic strip + podcast cover the Egg card's "story" during its active month

# AUDIO DESIGN

## The 50 BPM Heartbeat
Every match runs at 50 BPM. The heartbeat is the turn timer — each turn is one heartbeat cycle (1.2 seconds per beat, 60 seconds per turn). The heartbeat:
- Plays underneath the entire match
- Speeds up slightly when the opponent is running out of time
- Stops during Nyx's Silence effects
- Returns when silence ends

## Stone Tones
Each stone card sounds its unique tone when played. The Mohs-to-frequency mapping means:
- Hard stones (diamond, corundum) have high, clear tones
- Soft stones (talc, gypsum) have low, soft tones
- Players learn to recognize cards by sound — the audio is a gameplay element, not decoration

## Zone Ambient Audio
The board's zone determines the ambient soundtrack. This is the SAME audio as the podcast and the audiobook — the rooms overlap. A player who has listened to the podcast's Archive episode recognizes the Archive board's ambient audio immediately.

## Voice Lines
Each hero has voice lines for:
- Greeting (match start)
- Playing a stone card
- Playing a minion
- Using hero power
- Taking damage
- Winning
- Losing (conceding)
- The Egg hatching (monthly event)

Voice lines use the established accents and cadences. Beverley's "Dismiss" is clipped and precise. The Teacher's lines shift between warm and cold. The Firekeeper is theatrical. The Reader says one line per match.

# CROSS-PRODUCT INTEGRATION

## The Card Game IS the Arcade
In the novel, the traveler plays these card games in the Inn's arcade. The card game is the in-fiction game the novel describes. The novel's card battle scenes are the card game's solo campaign cutscenes.

## Graphic Novel Integration
Card battle panels in the graphic novel are drawn in the zone's artist style. The cards' art IS the graphic novel's art. The visual language is shared.

## Tabletop Integration
The tabletop game's stone cards and charm cards are physical versions of the digital cards. The digital card game and the tabletop game share the same card database. A player who owns the tabletop cards can scan them to unlock digital equivalents.

## Audiobook Integration
The audiobook's stone tones are the same tones as the card game's stone cards. The audiobook describes card battles using the same audio signatures. The rooms overlap.

## Podcast Integration
The daily podcast discusses card strategies, new card reveals, and the Egg card's monthly story. The podcast is the card game's editorial coverage — Krista's journalism training applied to a game about her game.

## Comic Strip Integration
The daily comic strip shows card battle moments. When a character plays a dramatic card, the strip shows the moment. The card art appears in the strip. The strip IS the card game's highlight reel.
