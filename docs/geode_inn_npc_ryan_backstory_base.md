# Ryan — Base Backstory Structure
## The Greek Assassin Who Chose the Campfire

*This is a starting structure. Evolve it. Change anything. Add anything. The Inn adapts to you.*

---

### Origin
- Greek. Aegean island. A family with a name and a trade — merchants, sailors, fishermen, something with salt in it.
- The family was respected. The island was small enough that everyone knew everyone. Large enough that secrets had room to hide.

### The Creed
- A young person from a respected family discovers there is a hidden war underneath the visible world. The both are: the adequate — the surface life and the secret life, the both are: the same person, different hour.
- The creed: a brotherhood of those who know. Not the only secret society in the Inn — the bloodline, the owl, the narrative spine all run underneath the visible world too. Ryan's creed connects to that undercurrent.
- The training: rooftops, hidden blades, the long patience of the hunt, the ability to become invisible in a crowd. The both are: the adequate — the assassin's skill and the bartender's skill, the both are: the same observation, different purpose.

### The Loss
- Something was taken. Someone was lost. The moment everything changed. Every assassin's story has this moment — Ezio's father and brothers, the betrayal that turns a life upside down.
- This is yours to define. What did you lose? Who betrayed you? What was the thing that made the blade feel necessary?

### The Long Path
- Years of the work. The both are: the adequate — the blade and the creed, the both are: the same purpose, different target.
- You got very good at it. The coat still has the weather of those years. The hat still hints at the hunt.
- But something started to shift. The blade solved things but didn't fix things. The hunt never ended because the hunt WAS the problem.

### The Campfire
- The choice. The moment you put the blade down and picked up the cup. The both are: the adequate — the blade and the bar, the both are: the same hands, different tool.
- Why a bar? Because a bar is the opposite of the blade. The blade takes people out of the world. The bar brings them in. Xenia — Greek sacred hospitality. The duty to welcome any stranger who comes to your door.
- The campfire IS Ithaca. The Odyssey ends when the wanderer stops wandering and chooses home. The pirate who chose to host instead of raid. The assassin who chose the hearth.

### The Three Daughters
- The family you built after the fight. The both are: the adequate — the family you lost and the family you chose, the both are: the same love, different generation.
- Their curiosity is the antidote to the assassin's silence — the kids who ask the questions you learned never to ask.
- Their roles in the Inn are still being designed. They are yours to shape.

### The Inn
- The campfire found the Inn, or the Inn found the campfire. The both are: the adequate.
- A Viking longship bar in a tree. The Greek who runs a Norse hall. The both are: the adequate — the Greek and the Norse, the both are: the same sea, different coast.
- The bartender who hears every story. The pirate who knows the after. The assassin who chose to pour instead of cut.

## Inn Connections (Already in the World)

- **Xenia (Greek sacred hospitality):** Ryan IS xenia. The bartender who welcomes everyone.
- **The Odyssey:** Ryan's backstory is Odysseus-shaped — the wanderer who comes home.
- **The Symposium:** The Greek tradition of drinking, talking, and philosophy. The bartender IS the symposium host.
- **The Bloodline/Owl (narrative spine):** Ryan's creed connects to the Inn's hidden war — the bloodline, the owl, the deeper currents running underneath the visible world.
- **Persephone (Tide Queen):** Greek. Already redesigned as regal anchor for the water system.
- **Medusa:** Greek. Lives at the olive grove border.
- **Nyx:** Greek primordial. The silence between notes.
- **The Pirate (Mandy Patinkin):** The man who lives in the AFTER — after the quest, after the revenge, after the thing that defined you is done. Ryan and the Pirate share the same frequency. The both are: the adequate — the pirate and the bartender, the both are: the same after, different bar.
- **The Host (Patrick Stewart):** The Inn's Fezzik. The both are: the adequate — the Innkeeper and the bartender, the both are: the same hospitality, different floor.
