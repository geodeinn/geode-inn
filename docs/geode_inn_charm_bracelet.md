## THE MODIFICATION SYSTEM

The bracelet is not static. It GROWS and CHANGES through the player's choices.

### Arrangement
The player can rearrange charms at any time. The arrangement is: the player's story, organized. Some arrangements have VISUAL effects:
- **Frequency grouping** (all Song charms together): the group glows amber
- **Thread grouping** (all charms from one storyline together): the group pulses at the thread's frequency
- **Chronological** (in order of acquisition): the bracelet shows the JOURNEY — where you started, where you went, how you grew
- **The Crone's arrangement** (all "adequate" — no order): the bracelet is still. The stillness is: the adequate

### Upgrading
Stone charms can be upgraded through crafting techniques:

**Level 1: Raw stone** (as earned from games)
→ A simple polished pebble or bead

**Level 2: Wire-wrapped** (requires: wire-wrapping technique from the Artisan Thief)
→ The stone is wrapped in wire, becoming a more elaborate charm

**Level 3: Set stone** (requires: stone-setting technique from the Mason)
→ The stone is set in a metal bezel — a proper jewelry setting

**Level 4: Forged setting** (requires: metal-smithing technique from the Smelter)
→ The setting is forged — decorative metalwork around the stone

**Level 5: Engraved masterwork** (requires: engraving technique from Gesso)
→ The setting is engraved with the stone's name, the game where it was earned, and the date

**Level 6: The Master Charm** (requires: ALL five techniques + the stone earned 3x)
→ The stone becomes a miniature sculpture — the stone carved into the shape of its sigil or the character associated with it. The Master Charm is: the crafting journey, complete. The complete is: the adequate.

**The Crone's annotation on the first Master Charm:** "...I measured this charm. The measurement is: six levels. The six is: the adequate. The adequate is: each level is: more than the last. The more is: the skill. The skill is: the hands. The hands are: the player's. The player's are: adequate."

### Combining
Advanced players can COMBINE charms:
- A stone charm + a sigil charm = a CHARACTER STONE charm (the character's stone in the character's setting)
- A stone charm + a frequency charm = a FREQUENCY STONE charm (the stone in its frequency's metal)
- A stone charm + an artist charm = an ARTIST'S STONE charm (the stone in the artist's style)
- A quest charm + a sigil charm = a BONDED charm (the character and the quest, together)

The combining is: the synthesis. The synthesis is: the Cave City's philosophy — all tiers in one piece.
