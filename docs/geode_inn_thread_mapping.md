## THE STRUCTURAL ARGUMENT

The novel has a gap in Part 3 (The Descent). Here's why:

**What the novel says happens in Part 3:**
1. Catacombs (Ch. 17) — dark, patient
2. Egypt, Greece, Camelot, Norse (Ch. 18-21) — cultural zones
3. Other Zones (Ch. 22) — summary tour
4. Undercity (Ch. 23) — Charon, Persephone, three gifts
5. Fae Kingdom (Ch. 24) — Seelie, Unseelie, Golden Contract

**What SHOULD happen in Part 3 but doesn't:**
1. The Shadow frequency (84Hz) introduced — the inverse of 42Hz
2. The Shadow Inn encountered — the dark mirror below the Undercity
3. The Hollow Men — T.S. Eliot's poem made physical
4. The Poison Garden — beauty that kills, restraint as gameplay
5. The Desert Bone zone — the Examiner's territory
6. The Keeper — guardian of the Song, crystal eye bequest
7. The Veil Map — discovered as Archive lore

**Part 4 (The Ascent) also needs:**
1. Council of Thinkers — Tesla, Da Vinci, Newton (only Einstein appears)
2. The Wanderer — main-floor Odin (only the Allfather is mentioned)
3. Schumann 7.83Hz — earth's calibration frequency

The missing threads aren't optional. The Shadow/84Hz is the binary counterpart to the 42Hz Song. Without it, the Egg's "Grand Collision" (Ch. 35) has no weight — the collision is between 42Hz and 84Hz, and if 84Hz is never introduced, the collision reads as abstract. The Hollow Men are the physical manifestation of the Whisper Layer's thesis. Without them, the Whisper Layer chapter (Ch. 32) lacks its deepest foundation.
