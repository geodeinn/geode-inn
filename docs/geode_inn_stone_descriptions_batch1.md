# Geode Inn — Missing Stone Descriptions
## Batch 1: The Agates & Jaspers (10 stones)
## Drafted 2026-09-04 — in the Inn's voice, pending Krista's review

Style: Mind carries the stone's essence (one mythic line, one precise effect). Body carries the practical. Each entry passes the six-writer gate: precision, warmth, the mythic made personal.

---

### 1. Plumed Agate
**Mind:** Plumed agate is the proof that beauty can grow in the dark without anyone watching. Its plumes — crimson, gold, violet — bloom inside solid stone over millennia, the way a private thought becomes a whole inner world. It helps the mind trust what it creates before anyone else sees it.
**Body:** Supports circulation and the lymphatic system — the body's own quiet transport, moving what needs to move without announcement.

### 2. Flower Agate
**Mind:** Flower agate carries the bloom before the bloom — the pattern of petals suspended in chalcedony, waiting. It is the stone of potential that has not yet announced itself, the way a seed holds the entire tree in miniature. For those standing at the edge of something new, it offers patience: the flower is already in the stone.
**Body:** Supports the reproductive system and hormonal balance — the body's cycles, honored rather than fought.

### 3. Dendritic Agate
**Mind:** Dendritic agate holds the branching pattern — manganese trees frozen mid-growth, the same shape as lightning, river deltas, lung, and nerve. The Inn's oldest lesson in mineral form: the universe reuses its best idea at every scale. It brings abundance the way a forest is abundant — not by hoarding, by branching.
**Body:** Supports the nervous system and healthy circulation — the body's own dendrites, reaching.

### 4. Fossil Agate (Turritella)
**Mind:** Turritella agate is a council of small spiral towers, each one a life that stacked itself upward one chamber at a time and then became stone. It connects the mind to deep ancestry — not the family you can name, the family that goes all the way down. Every spiral in it is a survivor.
**Body:** Supports stamina, nutrient absorption, and recovery from fatigue — the patience of small things that kept going.

### 5. Montana Moss Agate
**Mind:** Montana moss agate carries the quiet of wide country — river-tumbled clear chalcedony holding landscapes the size of a thumbnail. It is the stone of seeing far in a small space, of finding a whole prairie inside a pocket. It clears the static so the distance becomes visible again.
**Body:** Supports healthy circulation and eyesight — the body's long view.

### 6. Bumblebee Jasper
**Mind:** Bumblebee jasper wears its warning colors like a banner: sulfur-yellow, orange, and black, beautiful and bold enough to make you look twice before touching. The stone's first lesson is respect — some of the most vivid things in the world ask to be admired with a little distance. Its second lesson is the one the bee knows: you can be small, striped, and entirely unbothered, and still own the whole garden.
**Body:** Supports energy and digestive fire. (A note the Inn keeps on file: this stone contains sulfur — admired freely, handled with the respect owed to anything bright and wild.)

### 7. Desert Jasper
**Mind:** Desert jasper holds the palette of dry country — terracotta, sand, the red of rock at noon. It teaches the desert's particular wisdom: that scarcity is a rumor spread by the thirsty. Somewhere in every barren place is water that knows how to hide. This stone helps the mind find it.
**Body:** Supports hydration, temperature regulation, and the kidneys — the body's desert-craft, keeping its own oasis.

### 8. Ocean Jasper
**Mind:** Ocean jasper is the tide made mineral — orbicular patterns like drops on the surface of water, found in only one stretch of Madagascar shoreline, at the waterline, reachable only at low tide. The mine is gone now; the stones that exist are the stones that exist. It carries the rhythm of release: what the wave brings, the wave takes, and the ocean is not poorer for it. For grief that comes in waves, this stone keeps the tide's promise — it always goes out, and it always comes back.
**Body:** Supports restful sleep, lymphatic flow, and the release of deep-held tension — the body's low tide.

### 9. Picasso Jasper
**Mind:** Picasso jasper is the abstract composition the earth painted without a signature — bold strokes and shadows that resolve into meaning only when you stop insisting on one. It gives the artist's core power: to look at randomness and see arrangement, to reframe the accident as the intention. Every slab is a painting the stone insists it did not mean. Nobody believes it.
**Body:** Supports eyesight and releases the physical tension of creative block — the neck and shoulders where unfinished work goes to wait.

### 10. Sunset Jasper
**Mind:** Sunset jasper holds the whole sky's farewell — amber, rose, the ember-red of a day ending well. It is the stone of good endings: closure without grief, the day reviewed with love instead of judgment. It asks the question the sunset always asks — what did today give you? — and waits for the honest answer.
**Body:** Supports the body's evening wind-down — easing restlessness, deepening the descent into sleep.
