# Geode Inn — The Sonic Mineral Codex
## System: The Frequency Encyclopedia
## Access: All zones — the Codex is a portable interface (no physical location)
## Prerequisite: First stone collected (automatic unlock)

### Overview

The Sonic Mineral Codex is the Inn's frequency encyclopedia — the portable reference system that catalogs every stone's sonic signature, geological data, and cultural history. The Codex is the player's primary tool for understanding WHAT they're collecting and WHY it matters.

The Codex is not a menu — it's a PERCEPTION. When the player activates the Codex (via a hotkey or the Stone Vibration Touch System), the current zone's stone surfaces become annotated: each stone-type visible in the environment displays its frequency, hardness, geological origin, and cultural significance as floating text. The Codex is the Inn's X-RAY — the ability to see the mineral world's hidden data layer.

The Codex serves three functions:
1. **Identification** — identify unknown stones by their sonic signature
2. **Education** — learn the geological and cultural history of each mineral
3. **Composition** — understand which frequencies harmonize (for the Bead Composer)

## The Codex Interface

### Identification Mode
When the player touches a stone surface with the Stone Vibration Touch System, the Codex identifies the mineral:
1. **Frequency detection** — the stone's vibration is analyzed and matched to the frequency table
2. **Mineral identification** — the frequency maps to a Mohs hardness, which maps to a mineral family
3. **Visual scan** — the stone's color, texture, and crystal structure are compared to the Codex's database
4. **Result** — the stone is identified with a confidence percentage (100% for well-known stones, lower for unusual specimens)

### Education Mode
Each identified stone displays:
- **Mineral name** (common and scientific)
- **Chemical formula** (real-world data)
- **Crystal system** (real geological data)
- **Mohs hardness** (1-10)
- **Frequency** (from the Codex mapping)
- **Geological origin** (how the mineral forms in nature)
- **Cultural history** (how the mineral has been used by humans — amulets, jewelry, tools, pigments)
- **Inn usage** (how the mineral appears in the Geode Inn — which zones, which quests, which NPCs)
- **Harmonic partners** (which other stones' frequencies harmonize with this one — for the Bead Composer)

### Composition Mode
The Codex's harmonic analysis tool — for use with the Bead Composer:
- **Consonant pairs** — stone pairs whose frequencies produce pleasing harmonics (e.g., quartz 528Hz + apatite 396Hz = perfect fifth)
- **Dissonant pairs** — stone pairs whose frequencies clash (useful for Shadow Inn combat — dissonant frequencies disrupt enemies)
- **Chords** — groups of 3+ stones that produce a recognizable harmonic (e.g., the Solfeggio chord: 396 + 417 + 528 + 639 + 741 + 852 = the full Solfeggio scale)
- **Zone harmonics** — each zone has a "base frequency" that resonates with specific stones (Desert Bone 64Hz pairs with Bastet's sistrum stones; Yellowstone 111Hz pairs with the geothermal stones)

The deeper layer transforms the Codex from a geological reference into a NARRATIVE archive. Each stone becomes a character — with a history, a meaning, and a story. The Codex is the Inn's quietest achievement: a system that makes a rock collection into a library.

## The Stone Vibration Touch System

The Stone Vibration Touch System is the Codex's INPUT method — the physical interaction that activates the Codex's identification mode. The system works as follows:

### How It Works
1. The player approaches a stone surface (a boulder, a crystal, a cliff face, a gemstone in a display)
2. The player activates the Stone Vibration Touch (a key press or button on touch devices)
3. The player's hand (in-game) presses against the stone surface
4. The stone's frequency is transmitted through the player's hand — felt as a controller rumble (on Steam Deck/console) or as an audio pulse (on web/mobile)
5. The Codex identifies the stone and displays its data

### Advanced Applications
- **Shadow detection:** touching a stone in Shadow Inn (84Hz mode) reveals the stone's "shadow frequency" — the 84Hz version of its normal frequency. Shadow frequencies are always one octave higher (double the Hz).
- **Whisper Layer detection:** touching a stone at the Whisper crack frequency (41.7Hz) reveals whether the stone has been "watched" — whether the Inn has been cataloguing the player's interaction with this stone.
- **Form Sight integration:** touching a stone in Form Sight reveals the stone's "bone" — its essential form, stripped of detail.
- **Gorgon's Gaze integration:** touching a stone with the Gorgon's Gaze active reveals the stone's "peak" — its most beautiful state.
- **Ley Line detection:** touching a stone that sits on a ley line reveals the ley line's frequency and direction.
