## Bias Analysis Summary

### Methodology
All 82 text files were scanned for:
- **Berserker publisher content** — ZERO matches found ✅
- **Racial bias** (inferior/superior race, white supremacy) — ZERO matches ✅
- **Religious bias** (paganism conflated with satanism) — ZERO matches ✅
- **Colonial framing** (savage, primitive, barbarian) — 18 files contain flagged terms, but ALL are used in historical/descriptive context, not as framing devices ✅

### Key Findings

1. **"Primitive"** appears most often in Ancient Aliens content, where it's actually *questioning* the label — "conditioned to look at these works and think, oh, primitive people did that." The Ancient Aliens framework argues ancient peoples were MORE sophisticated than assumed, which is the opposite of colonial bias.

2. **"Barbarian"** is used exclusively in historical quotes — how Sumerians described invaders, how pharaohs titled themselves ("Smiter of Barbarians"). This is reporting how ancient peoples labeled each other, not the documentary's own framing.

3. **"Savage"** is used to describe specific violent events (a naval battle, a hippo mauling, a serial killer's father), not to label cultures.

4. **"Demonic"** is used to describe medieval art, Persian religious beliefs about salt water, haunted island dolls, and a Papaboa mythological creature — all descriptive, not labeling.

5. **"Satanic"** appears once, describing the Codex Gigas's nickname, with the explicit caveat "there is nothing explicitly satanic in the text itself." This is scholarly/objective.

### Conclusion
No files require exclusion. All 82 transcripts are suitable for integration into the Geode Inn's archive, subject to the Inn's neutral, scholarly framing.

### Note on Ancient Aliens Content
~20 files are from the "Ancient Aliens" series. These should be placed in the **Modern Day Legends** nook, not the catacombs, as they represent a modern mythological framework rather than scholarly archaeology. The Geode Inn's philosophy of presenting traditions on their own terms applies — Ancient Aliens is a modern legend about humanity's origins, not a historical text.
