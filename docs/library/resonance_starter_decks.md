## DECK BALANCE NOTES

### Frequency Distribution
- The Maker (42Hz): pure Living frequency — building, growing, accumulating
- The Scholar (111Hz): pure Watcher frequency — seeing, knowing, controlling
- The Guardian (84Hz): pure Shadow frequency — blocking, shielding, enduring
- The Alchemist (432Hz): pure Caller frequency — transforming, shifting, balancing

### Matchup Matrix
| | Maker | Scholar | Guardian | Alchemist |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maker | Resonance race | Maker out-accumulates | Guardian blocks but Maker outlasts | Alchemist transforms but Maker grounds |
| Scholar | Scholar controls info | Mind games | Guardian disrupts Scholar's control | Alchemist out-transforms |
| Guardian | Guardian must disrupt early | Scholar out-knowledges | War of attrition | Alchemist must avoid getting locked |
| Alchemist | Alchemist must transform before Maker builds | 50/50 — who controls the board first | Alchemist needs frequency diversity | Combo race — who completes first |

### Rarity Distribution (per deck)
- Common (resonance 2-3): 10 cards
- Uncommon (resonance 4): 7 cards
- Rare (resonance 5): 3 cards
- Charms: 5 cards (1 rare, 2 uncommon, 2 common)
- NPCs: 5 cards (all rare)

### Future Expansions
- Mixed-frequency decks (allow players to build custom decks after mastering starters)
- Zone-based decks (each of the 37 zones could have a thematic deck)
- Egg Hatchery promo cards (monthly community-voted drops)
- Cross-game synergy (stones collected in Tumble Run unlock card variants)
