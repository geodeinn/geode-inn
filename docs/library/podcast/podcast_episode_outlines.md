# Daily Echoes — Podcast Episode Outlines (Week 1)

## Format
- **Daily episodes:** 15-20 minutes, published Monday-Friday
- **Weekly deep dive:** 45-60 minutes, published Sunday
- **Audio style:** Overlapping rooms — podcast audio IS game audio. Same instruments, same 50 BPM low-dub classical base, same zone-specific soundscapes.
- **Structure (daily):** Cold open (30 sec, in-character scene) → Main story (12-15 min) → Echo (2-3 min, the listener question)
- **Structure (weekly):** Cold open (1 min) → Deep dive topic (40-45 min) → Listener letters (10-15 min)

---

## Episode 1 (Monday): "The First Stone"

**Cold Open:** The sound of a cave. Water dripping. A small dragon breathing in the dark. Then: the first strike of a pickaxe. A voice says, "It begins with the stone. It always begins with the stone."

**Main Story:** The creation myth of the Geode Inn — how Mona shaped the Inn from raw earth, how the first stones were placed, and why the Inn exists at all. Told through the voice of Elio, the Inn's librarian, who remembers the early days. The story traces from Mona's hands in the dirt to the first fire lit on the main floor.

**Echo:** A listener asks: "If the Inn is alive, what does it dream about?" Elio answers: "The Inn dreams about what it has not yet become."

**Zone audio:** The Main Floor — warm crackling fire, distant hum of activity, 50 BPM heartbeat bass.

---

## Episode 2 (Tuesday): "The Frequency of Staying"

**Cold Open:** A gargoyle hums at 111Hz. The sound fills a stone corridor. Footsteps approach and stop. The gargoyle's hum changes — it has noticed someone.

**Main Story:** The story of the six frequencies that hold the Inn together. GROW (42Hz) — the frequency of life and growth. STAY (84Hz) — the frequency of petrification, of choosing not to move. SENSE (111Hz) — the frequency of awareness, the gargoyles' hum. SING (432Hz) — the frequency of communication, the stones' language. CALM (50 BPM) — the Inn's heartbeat, the tempo of the fire. NULL — the silence between, where the unknown lives.

**Echo:** A listener asks: "What happens if you play two frequencies at once?" Elio: "Depends on which two. GROW and STAY together? That's dissonance. But if you stack them right — three of each — that's harmony. The Inn runs on the difference."

**Zone audio:** The Staircase — ascending tones, gargoyle hums, alchemical bubbling.

---

## Episode 3 (Wednesday): "What the Archive Knows"

**Cold Open:** Pages turning. Beverley's voice, clipped and precise: "If you're going to read that, read it properly. Page 47. Second paragraph. And don't fold the corner — I can see you from here."

**Main Story:** The Archive's story — the living library that knows more than it should. The Whisper Layer, where the Archive writes about players in real time. Beverley's rules: no loud voices, no food, no removing books, no asking why some books glow. The restricted section, where Quire reads in the margins, always looking over a shoulder. The book that has no author.

**Echo:** A listener asks: "Has the Archive ever been wrong?" Elio: "The Archive doesn't predict. It observes. It can't be wrong about what it's seen. But it can see things you'd rather it didn't."

**Zone audio:** The Archive — near-silent, page turns, distant whispers, ink scratch.

---

## Episode 4 (Thursday): "The Market and the Maker"

**Cold Open:** The sound of a market — not chaotic, but rhythmic. Stone on stone, metal on wood, a vendor calling prices. Briar Nightbloom's voice: "Everything here was made by someone who chose to make it. The Inn doesn't sell. The Inn introduces."

**Main Story:** The marketplace philosophy — the Inn as a discovery hub, not a store. How artists find the Inn, what subscription means, and why checkout redirects to the artist's own shop. The story of Soulstice Jewelry, the first artisan. The copyright shield — 37 artist styles, curated and owned by the Inn, protecting faires from IP claims.

**Echo:** A listener asks: "Can anyone sell at the Inn?" Elio: "Anyone who makes something real. The Inn doesn't judge quality. The Inn judges authenticity. If you made it with your hands, it has a place here."

**Zone audio:** The Marketplace — market sounds, stone cases, distant fire.

---

## Episode 5 (Friday): "The Descent"

**Cold Open:** Stone stairs descending. The air gets colder. A gargoyle's hum fades. A new sound: something breathing in the dark below. Not the dragon. Something older.

**Main Story:** The descent through the Inn — from the warm main floor to the catacombs below. The 37 zones: Egypt's sandstone corridors, Peru's Andean peaks, the Celtic standing stones, Japan's ink-wash gardens, the Norse frozen halls. Each zone is a world, each world has a guardian, each guardian has a story. The deepest point: the Fae Kingdom, where the rules change.

**Echo:** A listener asks: "What's at the very bottom?" Elio: "A room. A fire. A chair. A cup. A book. And me. But you're not ready for that yet."

**Zone audio:** Descending transition — starts warm (main floor), cools through the staircase, goes cold in the catacombs, shifts to fae twilight.

## Episode 6 (Sunday, Deep Dive): "The Six Writers"

**Cold Open:** A fire crackling. Six different voices, each saying one sentence:
1. "Words are like stones — each one has weight." (Rothfuss)
2. "Beneath everything, there is warmth. Even in the dark." (Pratchett)
3. "The mythic is personal. The gods were always just neighbors." (Gaiman)
4. "The world holds together because of rules you haven't noticed yet." (Asimov)
5. "The fire doesn't go out. It never was going to." (Bradbury)
6. "The math proves it: the fire was never going to go out." (Weir)

**Main Topic:** The six-writer narrative voice of the Geode Inn — how Rothfuss gives precision, Pratchett gives warmth, Gaiman makes the mythic personal, Asimov provides the structural logic, Bradbury brings the fire, and Weir brings the engineering optimism. How these voices combine in the novel, the tabletop game, the podcast, and every piece of Inn text.

**Segments:**
- 0:00-5:00 — Cold open + introduction
- 5:00-15:00 — Rothfuss and Pratchett: precision and warmth
- 15:00-25:00 — Gaiman and Asimov: myth made personal, logic made mythic
- 25:00-35:00 — Bradbury and Weir: the fire and the math
- 35:00-45:00 — How the six voices work together in practice (readings from the novel)
- 45:00-55:00 — Listener letters and questions

**Echo:** A listener asks: "Which writer are you, Elio?" Elio: "I'm the seventh. The one who listens to all six and writes what they agree on."

**Zone audio:** The Common Room — fire, pipe smoke, quiet conversation, the most human sound in the Inn.
