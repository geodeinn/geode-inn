Year-Round: Two Seasons

The digital Sherwood shifts between two modes. When the physical faire is open, the digital grounds amplify it. When the physical faire closes, the digital grounds keep the community alive.

Current Mode
Faire Season — Live
The digital grounds mirror the physical faire in real time. Vendor stalls are fully stocked. The Castle Stage shows today's performance schedule. Players can check what's happening today and plan their visit. Ticket portal is front and center.

The Archive Entry

Sherwood Forest Faire enters the Geode Inn's archive as a "Living Legend" — a modern continuation of the medieval fair tradition. The lore entry covers the historical tradition of seasonal faires in medieval England, the Robin Hood legend and its regional variations, the modern Renaissance faire movement, and Sherwood Forest Faire specifically: its founding, location, theme, and the community it serves.

"The faire is not an event. It is a tradition that remembers itself every spring."

This is presented in the Inn's scholarly, neutral voice — not as an advertisement, but as an archive entry. The faire becomes part of the record. Players who discover Sherwood through the game learn its real history, its real location, and its real community — and can buy tickets to visit in person.
