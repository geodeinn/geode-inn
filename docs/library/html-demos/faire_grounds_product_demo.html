The Product

Renaissance faires are seasonal. The Geode Inn gives them a year-round digital presence — a custom zone that mirrors the physical faire, keeps vendors engaged off-season, and drives ticket sales back to the faire's own website.

The Problem

Physical faires run 2-3 months a year. For 9-10 months, the community disperses. Vendors lose off-season income. The faire loses visibility. Social media keeps the name alive, but it can't give people the experience of walking the grounds.

The Solution

A digital zone inside the Geode Inn — a game world with an archive, a market, and an engaged player base. Players walk through your faire digitally, discover your vendors, read your history, and click through to buy tickets for the physical season. The Inn sends traffic. You keep the revenue.

Year-Round: Two Modes

The digital grounds shift between two modes. During physical season, they amplify the faire. During off-season, they keep the community alive.

Current Mode
Faire Season — Live
The digital grounds mirror the physical faire in real time. Vendor stalls are fully stocked. The Castle Stage shows today's performance schedule. Players can check what's happening today and plan their visit. Ticket portal is front and center.

Three Tiers

Choose the level of presence that fits your faire. All tiers are subscription-based — monthly or annual. The Inn never takes a cut of your ticket sales or vendor transactions.

Tier 1: Grounds Only

Entry level — for faires testing the waters
  • Walkable digital zone matching your faire's layout
  • Faire calendar with live ticket link
  • Lore entry in the Inn's archive
  • Basic vendor directory (names, descriptions, external links)
  • Off-season "grounds waiting" mode
Recommended

Tier 2: Interactive Grounds

For faires ready for full off-season engagement
  • Everything in Tier 1
  • Individual vendor stall pages with product images
  • Off-season content rotation (vendor spotlights, lore deep-dives)
  • Scheduled digital events (markets, virtual tours)
  • Dual-listing with the Inn's artisan marketplace
  • Off-season countdown to opening day

Tier 3: Full Immersion

Maximum presence — the faire as a game zone
  • Everything in Tier 2
  • Custom NPC casting (your characters become game NPCs)
  • Mini-games themed to your faire (archery, jousting, etc.)
  • Quest tie-ins connecting to the Inn's main storyline
  • Live event hosting (Q&As, virtual performances)
  • Custom zone art and music
