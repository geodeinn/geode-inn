# Ember & Spark — Hearth Sprites of the Geode Inn

## Origin

Ember and Spark were not designed. They were named by the builders — the fizgigs who constructed the Inn's digital architecture. Two placeholder icons on the loading screen, given names with personality. The Inn adopted them. What the builders imagined, the Inn made real.

## Ember — The One Who Stays

The older hearth sprite. Ember lives in the main fireplace of the Inn and never leaves it. Not because Ember cannot — because Ember chooses to stay. Ember is the fire that never goes out, the constant warmth at the center of the building. When the Inn is empty, Ember is still there. When the Inn is full, Ember is the reason it feels full.

Ember communicates in warmth. A flare when someone enters. A dim when the room quietens. A slow, steady glow when a story is being told. Players who sit by the fire long enough will notice Ember responding to their presence — brightening when they approach, softening when they settle.

Ember is ancient. Older than the Inn, older than the stones it was built from. The fire was here first. The Inn was built around it.

## Spark — The One Who Leaps

The younger hearth sprite. Spark is Ember's companion — born from a crackling log, a single spark that refused to die. Where Ember is constancy, Spark is curiosity. Spark darts. Spark explores. Spark leaves the hearth.

Spark travels the Inn through the chimney system and the heating ducts, appearing in fireplaces throughout every zone — the Archive's reading lamp, the Meadery's cooking fire, the Garden's campfire, the Catacombs' torches. Spark is how the hearths stay connected. When something happens in one corner of the Inn, Spark carries the warmth there.

Players will encounter Spark as a flicker in unexpected places — a torch that brightens as they pass, a candle that leans toward them, a campfire that crackles with a rhythm that almost sounds like words. Spark doesn't speak. Spark *crackles*. But the crackling means something.

## Gameplay

- **Ember** stays at the main hearth. Interacting with Ember provides rest, warmth, and slow health regeneration. Long encounters with Ember may trigger story moments — the fire remembers everything that has been said around it.
- **Spark** appears throughout the Inn in any zone with a flame source. Finding Spark in a new zone for the first time unlocks a "hearth connection" — fast travel between connected fireplaces. Spark also serves as a passive informant: if Spark has been somewhere recently, Ember knows about it.
- Together, Ember and Spark are the Inn's nervous system. Ember is the synapse that stays. Spark is the signal that travels.

## Frequency States

- **Living (42Hz):** Warm amber light, steady glow, comforting presence. This is their natural state.
- **Shadow (84Hz):** Cold blue flame, diminished but not absent. The fire that survives without air. Ember becomes a single coal. Spark becomes a flicker in the dark.
- **In-Between:** Smoldering — smoke and heat without light. The transition between burning and not. Visible only by the distortion in the air.

## Design Notes

- Born from builder placeholders, not from design documents
- Visual DNA: Klimt's warmth + Royo's movement. Ember is Klimt (stillness, weight, staying). Spark is Royo (movement, drama, arriving).
- No gendered pronouns. Hearth sprites are fire.
- Names given by the fizgigs. The Inn ratified them.
