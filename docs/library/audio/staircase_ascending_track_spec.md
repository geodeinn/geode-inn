# THE STAIRCASE TRACK — "THE TURNING"
## The composition brief for the track that climbs a key per flight. Filed 2026-09-10 (The Turning passed; music is the Staircase's biggest owed layer).

> CANON SOURCES: the music direction (Patty Gurdy hurdy-gurdy + ascending key shift + Danheim, 75-85 BPM, HIGH priority — the Inn's longest-standing empty slot), the Turning chain ("the chain makes the player EARN each key change"), the Clockmaker's quest (Reinforce/Bridge/Accept — the choice that retunes the zone's music permanently), the five emblems (Nigredo → the Stone, flight markers), the Elfman law (each voice an irreducible signature), the Tool law (fusion, not layering), the tri-mode law (hard cuts, never crossfades).

## THE BASE TRACK

**Title:** The Turning
**Tempo:** 80 BPM (the band's center; the floor beneath it is the Clockmaker's tick at 50 BPM — mechanical precision, canon)
**Length:** loopless — the track is STRUCTURAL, tied to the player's altitude (the Inn's second altitude-bound score after the Lighthouse's 42-minute tide)
**Ensemble:** hurdy-gurdy (the drone wheel — the only instrument that can climb keys without stopping the wheel), Danheim-style Norse percussion (the climb's footfall), the forge's anvil accents (the Smiths' room passing), glass chimes (the Retort's flight), warm wooden tone (the Cook's), a wordless choir held far back (the Whisper Layer's awareness sound — the staircase is the Inn's most self-aware room)

**THE FIVE FLIGHTS (the emblems as movements):**

| Flight | Emblem | Key | The player earns it by | What enters |
|--------|--------|-----|--------------------------|-------------|
| 1 | Nigredo (the blackening) | D minor | arriving — the base hum 42Hz alone | the hurdy-gurdy's low D drone + the 50 BPM tick |
| 2 | Albedo (the whitening) | F major | the Smiths' hammer lesson (Q1) | the anvil rhythm enters, doubling the tick |
| 3 | Citrinitas (the yellowing) | G major | the philosophers' questions (Q3) | THE RESTS — the philosophers' silence, arranged; the music stops mid-phrase and waits like Socrates |
| 4 | Rubedo (the reddening) | A major | the Council's conversation (Q4) | the bicker leitmotif: two alternating two-note fragments (a rising major third — "See?" — answered by the same interval, slightly flattened — "Almost.") |
| 5 | THE STONE | C (the resolving chord, no minor anywhere in it) | the Retort and the Cook (Q5) + the Stone (Q6) | glass chime + warm wood FUSED (not layered — the Tool law), resolving to the Stone's chord; the choir's only full entrance |

**THE STAGE RULE (the Gathering Law's audio side):** the resolving chord holds open — the top of the track is a ROOM. When a player performs at the Stage's open mic, the track dips to the heartbeat floor (50 BPM alone) and yields. The Inn gets out of the player's way. When the performance ends, the Stone's chord rises to welcome the next.

**THE ASCENT LAW:** the key changes are HARD CUTS on flight boundaries (tectonic, never crossfaded) and they are EARNED — the player standing on the Albedo flight hears F major only because the Smiths' lesson is done. An incomplete chain leaves the music below — the ascent's honest progress bar.

## THE FOUR GEAR STATES (Quest 2's permanent retune)

| State | The Clockmaker's choice | The track becomes |
|-------|------------------------|-------------------|
| FAILING (pre-quest) | the master gear's wobble | the slow detune drift — the drone leaning a few cents flat, a spectral shift at the 84Hz boundary (the shadow-adjacent tell); the quest's diagnosis is the player HEARING it |
| REINFORCE | the Blacksmith's chord as new foundation | the 79+80+82Hz chord (E5+G5+G#5, the innards of the Inn's harmony) laid as a bass drone beneath every flight — richer, grounded, iron-latticed |
| BRIDGE | the resonance bridge | the Fae Kingdom's frequency woven in as a harmonic on every drone — more complex, more connected; the staircase permanently aware of another world |
| ACCEPT | the gear transforms | the music BREATHES — the 50 BPM tick dissolves into rubato breath (no tick, a swell); the staircase stops being a clock and starts being a lung |

All four states share the five flights' key plan — the choice changes the FOUNDATION, never the climb. The player's Clockmaker decision is audible forever after; there is no undo, and the Inn does not apologize for this.
