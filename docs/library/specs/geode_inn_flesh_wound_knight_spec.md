# THE FLESH WOUND KNIGHT  Master Character Specification
## The Whole Game's Small Hopping Notification (ruled by Krista, 2026-09-11)

---

## WHAT HE IS

The Black Knight's comedy sibling  the second reading of the same armor. Camelot's gate guardian (the empty armor, played straight, the question-asker) has a second face in the Inn: THE FLESH WOUND KNIGHT, the knight who cannot lose because he refuses to acknowledge losing. Every limb gone, still standing, still declaring victory.

**THE OFFICE:** he is the game's LOW-STATE NOTIFICATION  the small hopping sprite who crosses the screen whenever a meter runs low (energy, life, lantern fuel, whatever the local game tracks). The comedy IS the warning: the player's state is reported by the one character who would never report it honestly about himself.

**THE LAW OF HIS DENIAL:** he never says "you are low." He says he is FINE. The player reads the mirror: the lower the state, the less of the knight remains  and the more certain he is of victory.

## THE HOP (the animation)

- **STATE 1 (low):** the full small knight hops across the screen, armor jingling, speech bubble: "A scratch."
- **STATE 2 (lower):** one arm gone  he hops and waves with the remaining one. Bubble: "Flesh wound. FLESH wound."
- **STATE 3 (critical):** he is just a helmet. The helmet hops. The hop is somehow jaunty. Bubble: "Still winning."
- **THE NEVER-DOWN LAW:** no state ever shows him stopping. He has no "defeated" frame. The hop continues at every severity  that is the whole character.

## THE SOUND

The hops land ON the heartbeat  50 BPM, each landing a soft metallic clank (the Empire Ears law: the notification is rhythmic, never alarming). The Inn never panics at the player; even its warnings arrive on the pulse. In a crisis state the clank doubles  the heartbeat's own hurry, rendered in metal.

## THE TIES

- **CAMELOT (origin):** the gate's straight guardian and the hopping denial are the same armor read twice  gravity and comedy, the Inn's both-at-once law. His origin post: a corner of the Camelot chamber, where the armor stands. The little version hops off the pedestal when a meter somewhere runs low. No explanation (the Adequacy Law holds).
- **THE WHOLE GAME (jurisdiction):** any zone, any game, any screen with a meter. In the meterless zones (the no-meter laws: water, flight, the Inn proper) he has nothing to report  and does not appear. He is the notification; no meter, no office.
- **UI CANON:** speech bubbles per the trigger-effect law (old-school comic bubbles, the Lozen "Oi." template's cousin).
- **LINEWORK:** chibi, heavy cel-shaded (the tradesman's line  he is working staff, not legend, despite everything he believes).
- **FLIP-BOOK PIPELINE:** 64px sprite sheet, 2-3 frame hop cycle per state  the proven Sept 9-10 technique.
