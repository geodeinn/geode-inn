# Geode Inn Tabletop — Zone Map Set Specification

## Overview

A set of 37 illustrated maps for the Tabletop Questline, one for each zone of the Geode Inn. These maps serve as the game board for exploration encounters, zone quests, and combat. Each map reflects the zone's assigned artist style, frequency, and atmospheric tone.

## Map Format

- **Size:** A3 (297mm × 420mm) — large enough for miniatures and tokens
- **Material:** 250gsm matte coated card stock (foldable for packaging)
- **Finish:** Soft-touch matte with spot UV on key features (rivers, stone circles, fire)
- **Folding:** Tri-fold (fits in the deluxe box when folded)
- **Scale:** Abstract — not grid-based. Distance is narrative, measured in "rooms" (each room = 1 zone traversal action)

## The 37 Maps

### Upstairs (3 maps)

**1. Cosmology** — Dalí style
- Star map with constellations that form the Inn's ceiling
- The cosmic crew's observatory platform at center
- Star-glass bridges between floating platforms
- NULL zones where stars are missing (hidden areas)
- Color: Deep blue-black with gold and silver star points

**2. Clockwork Skies** — Da Vinci mechanical style
- Gear-driven sky with visible mechanism
- Weather control levers at cardinal points
- The Clockwork Finch nest at the highest gear
- Steam Pixie vents releasing colored vapor
- Color: Brass, copper, steam-white on dark

**3. The Observatory** — Escher perspective style
- Staircases that go in impossible directions
- The telescope at the center (points both up and down simultaneously)
- The Allfather's throne at the apex
- Huginn and Muninn perches flanking the throne
- Color: Grayscale with gold accents

- The central fire at the heart
- The Common Room (campfire seating circle)
- Entrance to the Archive (guarded by Beverley's desk)
- Market spiral wrapping outward

- The entrance door (where players arrive)
- Color: Warm ambers, golds, oranges — the warmest map in the set

**5. The Archive** — Gorey/Dore style
- Endless bookshelves stretching into darkness
- Beverley's desk at the entrance
- The Restricted Section (behind a locked door)
- Reading nooks with firefly lamps

**6. The Marketplace** — Canaletto style

- Easels with dragon kitten paintings

**10. The Campgrounds** — Bierstadt/Carr style

**13. The Middle Staircase** — Escher style

## Map Features System

Each map includes standardized features:

**Navigation Points:** Marked with a compass rose icon. 3-5 per map.
**Encounter Markers:** Marked with a frequency symbol. The symbol indicates what type of encounter is likely there.
**Stone Sources:** Marked with a small gem icon. Indicates where stones can be found.
**Zone Transitions:** Marked with a doorway icon. Shows where players can travel to adjacent zones.
**Healing Points:** Marked with a CALM symbol. Where players can reduce Dissonance.
**Hidden Areas:** Marked with a NULL symbol (face down — revealed when discovered).

- **Format:** A3 tri-fold, 250gsm matte coated

- Full-resolution maps displayed on screen (tablet/phone)

- Fog of war option (GM reveals areas as players explore)
