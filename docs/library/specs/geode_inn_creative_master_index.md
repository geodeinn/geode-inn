## THE ORDER OF QUESTIONS — every scene answers these, in this order

**1. WHO DRAWS IT? (The Artist Layer)**
Klimt + Royo are the foundation — weight and motion in every visual, everywhere, always. The level artist flavors it (Van Gogh = Main Floor, Dalí = Cosmology...). The zone artist specifies it (Bauer = Unseelie, Rackham = Canopy, Lee = Seelie, Escher = the Between, O'Keeffe = Hollow Earth primary). "NONE" is valid — artistless spaces render by subtraction. Anti-canon check first: Kandinsky is retired from Clockwork Skies, Dalí is retired from the Fae, Grimshaw is superseded by Bauer.
→ Master: geode_inn_artist_mapping_system.md · geode_inn_visual_dna_spec.md

**2. WHAT'S IN THE FRAME? (Kubrick owns the frame)**
One-point symmetry, vanishing points, 15-20 second held vantages that live like paintings. Palette law: 90% dominant, 8% accent, 2% anomaly that breaks it. Test: could the frame hang in a gallery?

**3. HOW IS IT OBSERVED? (The Camera Layer — the camera is a mechanic, not a viewport)**
Four modes, four frequencies: Third-person = the Song, 50 BPM, the body in the world. First-person = 42Hz, the silence between notes, you ARE the character. Free camera = the Void-watch, silence, the Observatory's outside-looking-in. No camera = the In-Between's grey text, the murmur, the words ARE the world. Camera locks: SIT (first-person after 2 minutes), Music Room (third-person, the circle), Observatory (free), In-Between (none). The Shadow Inn freezes all modes — the Shadow is stillness. "You Looked": 10-minute SIT detaches the camera — the player sees themselves sitting.
→ Master: geode_inn_camera_system_master_spec.md (motion profiles live there too)

**4. WHAT DOES IT FEEL LIKE TO TOUCH? (Del Toro owns the floor)**
Every surface has a wear map — boot-worn stone, elbow-polished bars, finger-darkened shelves. Every object remembers what happened to it. Moisture tells stories. ONE anomaly per zone — the surface that doesn't match. Test: can you feel the floor?

**5. HOW DOES DREAD ARRIVE? (Hitchcock owns the timing)**
No jump scares, ever. The bomb is under the table. The camera holds past comfort. Timing Bible: Set (3-4s) → Seed (1-2s) → Hold (5-8s) → Turn (0.5s) → Silence (0.5s) → Return. Test: does the dread have timing?

**6. HOW DOES FEELING ARRIVE? (Spielberg owns the music's geography)**
Music never starts at the visual moment — the 42Hz heartbeat shifts 2-3 seconds before the theme. One instrument enters alone, builds one at a time. The drop-out is the feeling too big for instruments. Every piece of music has a source the player can walk toward and away from. Test: does the music have geography?

**7. WHOSE IS IT? (Coppola owns the scale of emotion)**
The epic is always anchored in one small personal detail — a child's toy in the crystal city, a smooth stone at the Source. Light and shadow are moral architecture. The family story IS the epic. Test: does it belong to someone?

**8. WHAT DOES IT SAY? (The Writer Layer — the editorial gate)**
The six-writer voice is the mandatory standard for ALL text: Rothfuss (precision), Pratchett (warmth), Gaiman (mythic-made-personal), Asimov (structural logic), Bradbury (fire-that-doesn't-burn), Weir (engineer's optimism). If the prose carries none of the six, it doesn't ship. The fifteen tone teachers are the soil beneath the voice — check the relevant teacher for the scene's layer (found family → Harrison, dread → King, absurdity held without apology → Futurama). Speech is ink-outlined comic bubbles, timeless, never modern UI widgets. Characters by name/title only, no gendered pronouns.
→ Master: geode_inn_narrative_dna_spec.md · tone_teachers/tone_teachers_main_index.md

**9. WHAT DOES IT SOUND LIKE? (The Audio Layer)**
42Hz living / 84Hz shadow / In-Between oscillating, at 50 BPM heartbeat — the Inn is a transducer built on IEM engineering. Tri-mode shifts are TECTONIC, not crossfades. Inn Mode DSP: 7 room profiles. The linework cracks in the same frame the ground moves.
→ Master: geode_inn_audio_dna_spec.md

**10. WHAT STATE IS IT IN? (Tri-mode is not optional)**
LIVING / SHADOW / IN-BETWEEN — the ledger counts a scene complete only with all three. Shadow is structural, not a filter.

## THE TENSION LAW
The protocols don't compromise — they coexist. Kubrick wants perfect symmetry; Del Toro wants worn asymmetry; both: the composition is perfect and the texture is imperfect. Coppola wants the long scene; Hitchcock wants the held silence; both: the Water Descent runs 25 minutes and the silence inside it lasts 5 seconds. The tension between the directors IS the aesthetic. The building is composed AND inhabited. Perfect AND worn. Epic AND intimate.

## THE GATE
Before any scene ships, all five director tests pass, the camera mode is chosen (not defaulted), the writer gate holds, the tri-mode is planned, and every asset carries the image creation template's fifteen steps (class it, canon it, tri-mode it, clean it, log it).
If it doesn't pass, it doesn't ship.
