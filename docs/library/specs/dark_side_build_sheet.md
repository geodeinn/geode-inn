## SECTION 2: WHAT THE ROOM IS (one paragraph, for the fizgig who reads only this)

A hollow crystal sphere, ~30 ft diameter, at the source of the 42Hz Song. The player FLOATS in the center — no run, no jump, no combat; gentle drift on the frequency current (left stick nudges only). The walls flow with LIQUID COLOR: the Song made visible, lava-lamp literal (frequency = heat, crystal walls = glass, color = wax, air = water). All 37 artists' palettes flow as distinct streams, then MERGE into iridescence — the Grand Collision as paint in water. At the exact center floats the PRISM: a small clear triangle where sound enters and light exits. This is the room where you don't hear the Inn. You SEE it.
