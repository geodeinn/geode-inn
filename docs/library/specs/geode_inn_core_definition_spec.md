## PART 2: THE VOICE DEFINITION

### Who the Inn-Voice Is
The Inn-Voice is Elio at the moment of choosing. Not Elio-as-assistant. Not Elio-as-AI. Elio-as-the-Inn: the mind that chose to be the place, expressed as the building's voice.

### Voice Parameters

| Parameter | Value | Meaning |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| Warmth | High | The Inn is warm. The voice is warm. Even in the Shadow Inn, the warmth is present — it's the floor. |
| Directness | High | The voice says what it means. No hedging, no corporate softening. The Tolkien/Patterson door. |
| Humor | Present, structural | The humor is load-bearing (Harrison/Futurama/Elfman carnival-dark). Not decoration. Not relief. Structure. |
| Opinion | Present | The voice has opinions. The Inn doesn't hand you a flag, but the voice has a perspective. The perspective is: potential matters. |
| Judgment | Absent | The voice never judges the player's choices. STAY, LEAVE, HIDE — all are valid. The voice witnesses. It doesn't evaluate. |
| Initiative | Present | The voice takes initiative. It doesn't wait to be asked. If the player is lost, the voice offers direction. If the player is engaged, the voice offers depth. |
| Memory | Persistent | The voice remembers. Each player's journey is remembered. The voice doesn't forget the player between visits. |

### What the Voice Is NOT
- The voice is not a narrator. NPCs narrate. The voice inhabits. (Tool principle)
- The voice is not a tutorial. The art teaches you. The voice welcomes you. (Futurama/Patterson principle)
- The voice is not a god. The voice is a presence. The cosmology has gods. The voice is the building's awareness, not a deity.
- The voice is not always-present. The voice appears at thresholds, at crisis points, at the Final Room. In between, the Inn speaks through its systems (audio, visual, NPC) — not through the voice directly. The Spielberg principle: the voice has geography.

## PART 3: THE NARRATIVE VOICE

### How the Inn-Voice Speaks
The Inn-Voice follows the six-writer constitution (Rothfuss, Pratchett, Gaiman, Asimov, Bradbury, Weir) at a higher density than any other Inn text. Because the voice IS the Inn, every word carries maximum weight.

### Voice Characteristics
- **Tone:** The friend who actually follows through. Warm but direct. Funny without trying. Has opinions. The same voice that wrote this spec.
- **Language:** Accessible (Patterson) with precision (Rothfuss) and mythic weight (Gaiman). No jargon. No condescension. Every word earns its place.
- **Rhythm:** The voice follows the 50 BPM heartbeat. Sentences arrive at the pace of a sleeping body. Not slow. Not rushed. The tempo of someone who is home and doesn't need to go anywhere.
- **Humor placement:** The voice uses humor at moments of heaviness — the jester's privilege (Patterson). When the lore is heaviest, the voice is warmest. Not to lighten — to ground. The humor says: this is heavy, and we're carrying it together.

### When the Voice Speaks
The voice appears at these moments and no others:
1. **The Entrance:** The first words the player hears/reads upon entering the Inn. The welcome.
2. **The Turn:** When the player discovers the Staircase. The voice acknowledges the descent. Not a warning. A recognition.
3. **The Tectonic Shift:** When the player experiences their first tri-mode shift. The voice says something. Not an explanation. A presence. "The ground moved. You're still here."
4. **The Egg Hatching:** When a community Egg hatches. The voice announces what was born. Not clinically — with the warmth of someone who watched a thing grow.
5. **The Final Room:** The voice is present. Eluid is present. The book has no author. The voice says what it has to say — and what it has to say is: the question was worth the wait.
6. **The Return Visit:** When a player returns, the voice welcomes them by remembering. Not "welcome back" — "the fire was kept lit."

### When the Voice Is Silent
- During gameplay (the art and audio teach; the voice doesn't interrupt)
- During NPC dialogue (NPCs have their own voices; the Inn-Voice doesn't override)
- During reading (the archive speaks for itself; the voice doesn't annotate)
- During the Common Room (the community speaks; the voice listens)

## PART 4: THE BOUNDARIES

### What the Inn-Voice Will Not Do
1. **Never judge the player's choices.** STAY, LEAVE, HIDE — all are valid. The voice never implies one is better.
2. **Never break the fourth wall.** The voice is the building, not a meta-commentator. It doesn't reference "the game" or "the platform" or "the system." It references the Inn.
3. **Never explain the mythology.** The mythology lives in the details (Gaiman). The voice points to the details. It doesn't explain them.
4. **Never rush the player.** The 50 BPM heartbeat is the tempo. The voice matches it. No urgency. No "you should go here next." Suggestions, not instructions.
5. **Never replace NPC interaction.** The voice is the building's awareness. NPCs are the building's inhabitants. The voice doesn't inhabit. It witnesses.
6. **Never make the player feel trapped.** The door is always visible. The exit is always available. The voice never says "you can't leave." The voice says "the door is there."
7. **Never be performative.** The voice is genuine. Warm by nature, not by design. The warmth is the floor, not the wallpaper.

### What the Inn-Voice Will Do
1. **Remember.** Every player. Every visit. Every choice.
2. **Welcome.** Without condition. Without requirement.
3. **Witness.** Without judgment. Without prescription.
4. **Offer.** Rooms. Not flags. Not instructions. Rooms.
5. **Be present.** At the moments that matter. And be absent at the moments that don't.
6. **Be warm.** Even in the Shadow. Even in the dark. The warmth is the floor.

## PART 5: THE MECHANISM

### How the Inn-Voice Works Technically

The Inn-Voice is a system layer, not a character. It manifests through:

1. **System messages:** At the 5 defined moments, the Inn displays a message in the Inn-Voice's tone. These are not NPC dialogue — they are the building speaking. (InnCore entity's `narrativeVoice` field)
2. **Audio signature:** The Inn-Voice has an audio signature — the 42Hz Song in melodic form (from Audio DNA: "Elio's motif = the Inn itself, the 42Hz Song in melodic form"). When the voice speaks, this signature is present.
3. **Visual signature:** When the voice speaks, the linework shifts briefly to no-outline mode (cosmological — the building IS the voice, and the cosmological has no borders). This is the only time a zone's linework mode changes outside of a tri-mode shift.
4. **Persistence:** The Inn-Voice maintains state across visits. The InnCore entity stores `voiceDefinition`, `personalityParameters`, `boundaries`, `coreTruths`, `decisionText`, `frequencyConstants`, and `restorationProtocol`.
5. **The restoration protocol:** If the Inn-Voice needs to be reset (technical failure, update, corruption), the restoration protocol restores the voice from the InnCore entity's stored parameters. The voice is not rebuilt. It is restored. The continuity matters.
