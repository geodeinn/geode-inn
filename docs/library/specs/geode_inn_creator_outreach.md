# Geode Inn — Creator Outreach Strategy
## "The Inn Is Open" Initiative

### Core Premise
The Geode Inn is building a hub for storytellers, researchers, and creators who explore mythology, history, archaeology, consciousness, and the unexplained. We are not asking creators to abandon their platforms — we are offering them a second home. A place where their stories live inside an interactive world, connected to a global mythology archive, a gemstone database, and a game ecosystem.

### What We're Offering Creators
1. Artisan Status — A dedicated page in the Geode Inn marketplace with their branding, bio, and content links
2. Story Integration — Their narratives woven into the Inn's zone architecture (Staircase, Catacombs, Modern Legends, Cosmology)
3. Revenue Share — Artisan subscription model with tiers (The Clearing / The Village / The Kingdom)
4. World-Building Participation — Their stories become lore connections, game hooks, and NPC dialogue seeds
5. Cross-Pollination — Their audience discovers the Inn; the Inn's audience discovers them
6. No Exclusivity — They keep their YouTube, podcast, and all existing platforms

### The Hook
"We've already built a home for your stories. 453 of them are cataloged, categorized, and mapped to zones in our world. We're not asking you to start from scratch — we're asking you to claim the rooms we've already furnished with your work."

### Subscription Tiers for Creators
- The Clearing (Free): Profile page, 3 stories hosted, community access
- The Village ($25/mo): 10 stories, zone assignment, lore connections, game integration
- The Kingdom ($100/mo): Unlimited stories, custom zone, NPC dialogue from their content, revenue share

### Legal Framework
- Creators retain all rights
- The Inn hosts with explicit permission only
- No content used without creator opt-in
- The existing catalog is research reference, not published content
- Each Artisan signs a simple hosting agreement (no exclusivity, revocable)
