## THE FIVE JUICIEST GAPS

1. **The Void** — a game played at 0Hz, in silence, with Death's silver harmonica as the only object. What does a silence game look like? The Inn has never answered. It's the top of the tree and it's empty on purpose — the question is whether "on purpose" means "not yet."
2. **Brim's Nook** — Brim's own game. The mimic game: a Magritte world of shapes that are not what they seem, transformation tokens, Monsieur Sentient. The In-Between just showed what Brim carries. A nook game would show what Brim plays at.
3. **Stone Circle Chamber** — the deepest tone the Inn ever produces, and there's no game at the bottom of the world. The final room wants to be earned. This is the natural capstone game.
4. **The Gargoyles** — six frequencies, acoustic alchemy, the staircase's architecture. A frequency-matching game is practically pre-designed by the canon.
5. **Underworld Queens** — three queens of death/rebirth and a pomegranate bridge. The Catacombs' emotional core with no playable layer.

## NOTE

The pattern in the gaps: they're almost all INTERIOR, QUIET, EMOTIONAL spaces — the Void, the nooks, the workshop rooms, the deepest chamber. The loud zones got their games first. What's left is the Inn's inner life. The In-Between was the proof: the quietest gap held the best game in the house.
