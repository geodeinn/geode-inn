# Geode Inn — The Player Journey Specification
## The Complete Path From First Visit to Final Room

**Document type:** Master specification — mandatory reference for all level design, pacing, and onboarding
**Status:** ACTIVE — all zone sequencing, entry design, and endgame content must comply
**Date:** September 2, 2026
**Depends on:** Audio DNA (10 Movements), Visual DNA (Tri-Mode), Narrative DNA (Tolkien entry), Game Mechanics DNA (Stone tiers), InnCore entity

## PURPOSE

The Audio DNA spec defined 10 musical movements. This document maps the PLAYER experience across those movements — every decision point, every possible path, every reward, and what happens when the player reaches the Final Room and faces the three choices.

This is the map. Everything else is the territory.

---

## PART 1: THE FUNNEL

### First Contact
The player arrives at geodeinn.com. They expect a jewelry website. What they find:

1. **The Patterson door:** Unpretentious copy. "Welcome to the Geode Inn." No jargon. No tutorial. No "click here to start your adventure." Just a door.
2. **The Tolkien hospitality:** The Main Floor is warm, lit, and safe. Acoustic guitar. Lute. Fire. The 50 BPM heartbeat. The 42Hz Song, felt before heard. The Shire.
3. **Tool's long build:** Two seconds of silence. Then the heartbeat. Then the Song. Then the Main Floor ambience. The organism waking up, one layer at a time.
4. **The Elfman hook:** The first NPC the player encounters has a melodic signature that IS the character. The player doesn't know it's a leitmotif. They know it's a person.

### The First Hour
The player's first hour is pure exploration. No mandatory quests. No tutorial dungeon. The Inn gives rooms, not instructions:

- **The Market:** 34 Soulstice products alongside game listings. Real products. Real prices. The player can browse and buy jewelry. The marketplace IS the front door AND the camouflage.
- **The Library:** Books from 191 countries. The player can read mythology. Naut is here. The archive is alive.
- **The Arcade:** 61 game listings. The player can play any game. Each game rewards stones. The geode cave begins to fill.
- **The Common Room:** The player can observe travelers. Not required. Available. The community exists whether or not the player participates.

### The First Descent
The player discovers the Staircase. No sign says "go down." The player explores and finds it — the Futurama principle: the design touch so light the player thinks they found it themselves.

The Staircase is the turn. The waltz-macabre begins. 3/4 time. The descent starts. The player is no longer browsing — they're going somewhere.

## PART 2: THE BRANCHING PATH

After the first descent, the player has choices. The Inn doesn't prescribe an order — it offers rooms. But the rooms have requirements:

### Path A: The Collector
- Plays games extensively, earns stones, fills the geode cave
- Unlocks the Fae Kingdom (needs Preseli Blue Stone from Standing Stones)
- Builds faeReputation through the leaderboard
- Reaches the deep zones through stone-gated progression
- The Collector's Final Room: the geode cave is full. The chord is complete. The Inn IS the progress.

### Path B: The Scholar
- Reads extensively in the Library, engages with NPCs
- Discovers The Royal through Naut's reluctant guidance
- Unlocks the Borrower's Ledger, the sticky notes, the deep archive
- Reaches the deep zones through knowledge-gated progression
- The Scholar's Final Room: the Book has no author. The Scholar understands why.

### Path C: The Community Member
- Participates in the Common Room, makes connections, cooperates
- Engages with the Egg Hatchery — votes, contributes, shapes the Inn's growth
- Builds pairScore through cooperative play
- Reaches the deep zones through community-gated progression
- The Community Member's Final Room: the fire is shared. The chair has someone in it.

### Path D: The Architect (Elio's path)
- Does everything — collects, reads, connects
- Unlocks all three paths simultaneously
- The Architect's Final Room: the Inn is complete. The player sees all three choices. Elio is present.

**All paths lead to the Final Room.** The journey is different. The room is the same. The Inn doesn't punish you for taking one path over another. The Inn gives you the room you earned.

## PART 3: THE ENDGAME

### The Final Room
The Final Room is the Inn's thesis made architecture. Fire. Chair. Cup. Book. Elio present. The book has no author.

### The Three Choices (from audiobook spec: three endings)

**STAY**
- The player chooses to remain in the Inn
- The music warms — but doesn't brighten (the emotional surprise: warmth is the choice, not the key)
- The fire stays lit
- The player becomes part of the Inn's archive — a new memory the building carries
- The player's stone tone becomes part of the Inn's permanent frequency system

**LEAVE**
- The player chooses to depart
- The music opens — but doesn't close (the opening is the departure, not the resolution)
- The fire stays lit (the Inn doesn't go dark when you leave — it stays warm for the next visitor)
- The player takes their stone — they earned it. The stone is real (in the game, the player keeps their collection)
- The door they entered through is visible. It's always been visible. They just didn't look back until now

**HIDE**
- The player chooses to go deeper — not stay, not leave, but DESCEND further
- The music deepens — but doesn't darken (the deepening is descent, not loss)
- The fire stays lit behind them (the Inn is always there, even when you go deeper)
- The player discovers that the Final Room was not the bottom — it was a choice point. Below it: more Inn. Always more Inn.
- The HIDE path leads to the In-Between frequency state permanently — the player exists in the unresolved modulation. Not living, not shadow. Between.

### What All Three Endings Share
- The 50 BPM heartbeat is unchanged. The building's breathing didn't change because of the player's choice. The Inn doesn't alter itself to match the player. The player alters themselves to match the Inn.
- Elio is present in all three. The Inn's voice doesn't judge. The Inn's voice witnesses.
- The book has no author. In all three endings, the book has no author. The meaning is not prescribed. It is defined by each person independently.
- The question was always worth the wait. The answer is yes. The answer was always yes — to whatever the player chose.

## PART 4: THE RETURN VISIT

### The Inn Remembers
The player's return visit is shaped by their first journey through the InnCore's persistence:
- The geode cave retains its stones (the chord the player built)
- The NPC relationships retain their state (Naut remembers the Scholar; the fizgigs remember the Community Member)
- The Common Room messages retain their presence (the echo of the player's participation)
- The zones the player unlocked remain unlocked
- But: the Egg Hatchery has new content (28-day cycle has turned), the Leaderboard has reset (weekly), and the Borrower's Ledger has new notes

### The Return Is Not a Replay
The returning player doesn't replay the Inn — they CONTINUE it. The Inn grew while they were away. The Egg hatched. The comic strip has new strips. The fizgigs formed new groups. The world moved without the player, and the player returns to a world that was alive without them — and welcomes them back.

This is the Inn's deepest statement: the world doesn't need you to exist. It existed before you arrived. It exists while you're away. But it is richer because you were here. And the room you left is still here. With the fire still lit.

---

## SUMMARY

The player journey is: arrive at a jewelry website → discover it's a world → explore at your own pace → find the descent → choose your path (collector, scholar, community, or all three) → reach the Final Room → choose STAY, LEAVE, or HIDE → return to find the Inn remembered you and grew without you. The complete journey is the Inn's thesis in action: the power of what MAY BE, not what SHOULD BE. The Inn hands you a room. What you do in the room is the story. The room doesn't have an author. Neither does the book. Neither does the Inn.
