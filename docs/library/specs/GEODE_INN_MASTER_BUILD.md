# 0. THE THESIS

The Geode Inn is a living archive of stone, sound, and story — a subterranean sanctuary where unyielding stone opens to reveal a luminous, crystalline interior. It is not a hotel. It is not a game. It is a digital ecosystem built around a single architectural metaphor: descent reveals depth.

The Inn exists as a hybrid: a web hub (geodeinn.com) connecting to a Godot game engine, a marketplace, a library, and a 15-product ecosystem. Every surface carries pattern. Every descent reveals something older. The Inn's whole argument is in one image: the Antikythera mechanism — an ancient astronomical computer in antiqued bronze — positioned atop a background frame of the Inn's underwater world.

---

# 1. THE AESTHETIC FOUNDATION

## 1.1 — Two Forces

The Inn's visual identity was born from two artists and two forces:
- **Klimt** — Stillness, weight, the beauty of staying
- **Royo** — Movement, drama, the energy of arriving

Two sides of the same coin. Movement and stillness. All other artists are room flavors layered on this foundation.

## 1.2 — Prototype Style

All character and zone art uses the **Royo × Vampire Hunter D × Trinity Blood** DNA:
- Hyper-detailed patterns
- Smooth flowing lines
- Stroke emphasis suggesting movement even in stillness
- The Marijke Valkyrie image is the reference prototype

This style layers on top of each zone's assigned artist (e.g., Bierstadt × Royo, Benton × Royo).

## 1.3 — Color World

**Subterranean luxury.** The palette:
- **Base:** Deep charcoal, near-black (#0a0a0a)
- **Amethyst (accent only, NOT the logo):** Deep purples (#4e2d6b, #b388ff)
- **Metal:** Warm brass, copper, gold filigree
- **Highlights:** Cream, white (#ffffff)
- **Footer accent (if used):** Muted deep purple (#574e7a) — to be stripped with hotel template

Dark, warm, the weight of history — but never grim. A geode interior: unyielding stone outside, luminous crystal within.

## 1.4 — The Living Background

The background is alive. **Celtic knotwork in gold on dark stone, pulsing** — the in-game tree pattern rendered as Klimt spirals. Not rigid UI frames. An ambient, breathing texture underneath every page, every zone, every screen. The Inn's heartbeat made visible.

Present on ALL screens, menus, and portraits. It breathes. It does not frame — it underlies.

## 1.5 — Typography

Elegant restraint:
- **Headers:** Serif (THE INN), elegant, thin
- **Subtitles:** Thin all-caps, white
- **Body:** Clean, readable
- No decorative fonts. Weight comes from imagery behind the text, not the text itself.

## 1.6 — Linework-as-UI

The line weight IS the character information:
- **Heavy cel-shaded** — tradesmen, working characters
- **Soft flowing** — lore, archive content
- **Sharp/angular** — guardians
- **No outline** — cosmological entities
- **Fragmented** — crisis moments

## 1.7 — The Pulse Line

The 42Hz/84Hz frequency indicator at the bottom of every screen — always present, shifting between Living (42Hz) and Shadow (84Hz) modes. The visual heartbeat across all pages.

## 1.8 — Standing Design Decisions

- Soften celebrity features in concept art; evoke presence/energy through posture and costume
- Anthony Hopkins' eyes are the only unsoftened celebrity features
- Refer to characters by name/title only; avoid gendered pronouns
- Klimt's "The Kiss" is permanently banned (interpreted as coerced/resigned)
- "Death and Life" is the designated Undercity Klimt reference
- Gorgon's Garden: 84Hz "stay/petrify" inverse of 42Hz "grow/Inn"
- Creative pipeline: Story → Elio's perception → Visual output
- Mona: mother creation myth, not a saint, not an NPC
- Final Room: fire, chair, cup, book — Elio present, book has no author
- Loading screen: fizgigs in overalls with oversized wrenches (NOT brownies — brownies look like Daleks)
- Low-dub classical music at 50 BPM for game levels
- Tabletop questline narrative voice for ALL game text

# 2. THE ENTRY EXPERIENCE

## 2.1 — The Logo

**The Official Logo — Antikythera Mechanism atop the Underwater World:**
- **The device:** The Antikythera mechanism — a circular brass/bronze astronomical dial with concentric rings engraved with ancient Greek zodiac text and Egyptian calendar month markers, fine tick-mark graduations, a raised brass dome at center with a pointed gnomon, and multiple pointer arms tipped with colored spheres representing celestial bodies
- **The background frame:** The Inn's underwater world — positioned behind and beneath the device
- **Composition:** The Antikythera device sits ATOP the underwater world frame
- **Colors:** Warm brass, copper, gold filigree for the device; deep aquatic blues, greens, and dark tones for the underwater frame

The Antikythera mechanism is an ancient astronomical computer. The underwater world is the Inn's deepest zone. Together they are the Inn's thesis: ancient knowledge floating above the deep.
