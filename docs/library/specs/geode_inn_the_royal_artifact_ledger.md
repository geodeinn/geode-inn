# THE ROYAL ARTIFACT LEDGER — First Wave (20 entries)
**Ruled in (Krista, 2026-09-20): the uncoverable artifacts of the Royal, drafted card-first — every row doubles as a deck card for the TCG world (the Dark Fantasy TCG templates from the World Palette are the frame masters; Resonance is the cousin set). THE FORMAT LAW: Bait's sticky note IS the card's flavor text. The Royal has been writing card flavor for millennia without knowing it.**

**Honest-label law on every row: the real object, dated and sourced, credited; the phenomenon is the Inn's fiction. Never the reverse.**

1. **THE ANTIKYTHERA MECHANISM** — Honest label: real, c. 100 BCE, Greek, raised from the Antikythera wreck 1901, National Archaeological Museum, Athens. PHENOMENON: predicts events nobody remembers scheduling. BAIT'S NOTE: "Do not adjust it. It adjusts you. — Mgmt (ca. 2,847 BCE update pending)." CARD: high cost, legendary relic — "Scry 2, then set aside the top card of your library as a scheduled event. It resolves whether or not anyone remembers it."

2. **THE BAGHDAD BATTERY** — Honest label: real, Parthian era c. 250 BCE, Khujut Rabu; the "battery" reading is contested and filed under Modern Day Legends, never taught as settled. PHENOMENON: hums at exactly 42Hz whenever nobody is measuring it. BAIT'S NOTE: "It only works under observation that isn't happening. Do not instrument the hum. The hum resents instruments." CARD: low cost — "Takes no damage from measurement. Gets stronger when unobserved."

3. **THE NEBRA SKY DISC** — Honest label: real, c. 1600 BCE, Nebra, Germany; the oldest known concrete depiction of the cosmos. PHENOMENON: shows last night's sky over the Inn, not over Nebra. BAIT'S NOTE: "It is not broken. It relocated." CARD: "Replaces the sky with a local sky once per night."

4. **THE PHAISTOS DISC** — Honest label: real, c. 1700 BCE, Crete; undeciphered to this day. PHENOMENON: reads itself aloud, fluently, only to listeners who cannot read. BAIT'S NOTE: "Do not invite translators. It performs for the illiterate and sulk for scholars." CARD: "Unreadable. Gains power equal to what you don't know."

5. **THE VOYNICH MANUSCRIPT** — Honest label: real, carbon-dated 1404-1438, Yale's Beinecke; undeciphered. PHENOMENON: the plants depicted grow nowhere on the surface — except the Cross-Contamination Zone, where they grow exactly where the pages say. BAIT'S NOTE: "The book is the garden's seed catalog. File under agriculture." CARD: "Generates one impossible plant per turn. The plant is legal where it lands."

6. **CLEOPATRA'S NEEDLE (the New York one)** — Honest label: real, London and NYC obelisks, erected 1880/1878; the hieroglyphs mostly effaced by centuries before the Atlantic trip. PHENOMENON: hums a perfect D. BAIT'S NOTE: "If the door to Section 12 sticks, just hum. It likes D. This is why." CARD: "Humming it unblocks one door. The instrument was never separate from the key."

7. **THE LEWIS CHESSMEN** — Honest label: real, c. 1150, found 1831, Uig, Isle of Lewis; 93 pieces, walrus ivory. PHENOMENON: the berserker pieces chew their shields and must be re-shielded from Drawer 4; the wardens file complaints about the throne. BAIT'S NOTE: "Do NOT give the rook your shield. It cannot tell the difference and it cannot be corrected." CARD: "Squad — grows more agitated each turn; the agitation is the attack."

8. **JOHN DEE'S SCRYING MIRROR** — Honest label: real, British Museum WA1641; obsidian, Aztec origin (Tezcatlipoca register), owned by Dr. John Dee, Elizabeth I's advisor. PHENOMENON: shows the last thing it saw before you looked. BAIT'S NOTE: "You are never the first observer. Plan accordingly." CARD: high cost — "Look: reveal what the previous player scryed. It is always worse and always funnier."

9. **THE DENDERA 'LAMP' RELIEF** — Honest label: the relief at the Temple of Hathor is real (Ptolemaic); the 'electric bulb' reading is pseudo-archaeology and files under Modern Day Legends, never as history. PHENOMENON: glows when interpreted kindly, dims when lectured at. BAIT'S NOTE: "It does not want an explanation. It wants an audience." CARD: "Brightens in the presence of honest labels. Dims in the presence of certainty."

10. **THE AZTEC SUN STONE** — Honest label: real, Piedra del Sol, buried post-Conquest, excavated 1790, Zócalo, Mexico City; the '2012 calendar' reading is a modern legend, filed as such. PHENOMENON: always exactly one day off; the sticky notes disagree about WHICH day. BAIT'S NOTE: "It is not wrong. We are running a day it hasn't agreed to yet." CARD: "Shifts one turn forward or back. Opponent chooses the direction. It doesn't."

11. **THE HOPE DIAMOND** — Honest label: real, 45.52 carats, Smithsonian; the curse is traceable to a manufactured publicity campaign (the real scandal, credited). ## THE CARD LAWS (the TCG tie, ruled in 2026-09-20)
- **THE STICKY NOTE IS THE FLAVOR TEXT** (the format law above).
- **THE PHENOMENON IS THE MECHANIC** — every card effect is the artifact's minor absurd phenomenon, translated to rules text. Never invented damage; the Royal's cards deal in scrying, announcing, humbling, filing.
- **HONEST LABELS ARE THE PLAQUES** — each card carries the real object's date and source in the text box. The game teaches provenance by osmosis (the Statute-Book's cousin).
- **GLOW TIER = RARITY** — the Quest Mark law carries to the frames: candle-flicker through Prism Spiral, per card.
- **ANTI-SKINNER CHECK** — no card farms, no doom-stack; the Royal's comedy is the balance.
