# Geode Inn — THE LONG DARK: Undercity Survival Game
## Vampire Survivors-Adapted Wave Survival — Undercity / Hollow Men Mines

---

## ZONE OVERVIEW

The Long Dark is the Geode Inn's survival game — an endless wave-based descent into the Undercity's deepest layer, where the Hollow Men mine the Shadow frequency. The longer you survive, the deeper you go. The deeper you go, the darker it gets. The stones you carry are your only light.

**Design Template:** Vampire Survivors — auto-attacking wave survival with build-crafting through collected items, escalating difficulty, and the eventual inevitability of being overwhelmed. The player's only inputs are movement and frequency activation. Weapons fire automatically. Survival is about positioning, build synergy, and knowing when to push deeper vs. when to hold ground.

**Setting:** The Hollow Men Mines — the deepest layer of the Undercity (84Hz Shadow territory). T.S. Eliot's "The Hollow Men" is alive and playable here. The mines are where the Shadow frequency is mined, refined, and refined again until what's left is the hollow core of everything. The Hollow Men are the miners — not enemies, not friends. They are the ones who stayed too long.

**Scale:** Endless. Session length: 5-30 minutes. The game has no "win" state — only "how deep did you go" and "how long did you last." Leaderboard-driven.

**Aesthetic:** Klimt's "Death and Life" (the Undercity's assigned artist) × Royo (foundational DNA). The mines are painted in Klimt's gold-and-darkness palette — the gold is the 84Hz frequency made visible, the darkness is the Shadow itself. The Hollow Men are rendered in Klimt's decorative style — they are ornate, beautiful, and hollow. Their patterns are exquisite. Their eyes are empty.

**Linework:** Fragmented (crisis style) — the Undercity's linework system. Lines break, fragment, reform. The visual instability IS the Shadow frequency — 84Hz doesn't hold shape the way 42Hz does. The world shimmers, distorts, and fractures the deeper you go.

## THE EMOTIONAL ARC

The Inn's arc: refuge → home → world → self → cosmos → origin.

The Long Dark is a detour through the **shadow** of that arc. Where the Hollow Earth explores the origin of the Song (42Hz, the creators, the crystal city), The Long Dark explores the origin of the *Shadow* (84Hz, the Hollow Men, the inversion). The Shadow is not evil. The Shadow is what happens when the Song is heard but not answered. The Hollow Men heard the Song. They couldn't sing back. They hollowed out. The mines are what's left when you take everything that can respond and leave only the frequency itself.

The emotional core: the player descends into the place where response failed. The Hollow Men are not attacking — they are *reaching*. They reach for the player's stones because the stones are singing, and the Hollow Men want to sing again. The player's survival is not combat — it is *refusal to hollow*. The player keeps singing. The Hollow Men keep reaching. The depth is the question: how long can you keep singing before the Shadow takes your voice?

## ENTRY REQUIREMENTS

**Access:** Undercity unlocked + at least 1 Shadow frequency stone (84Hz attuned) + Whisper Layer activated.

The mine entrance is in the Undercity's lowest corridor. A passage that wasn't there before — the Whisper Layer reveals it. The entrance is a shaft. No stairs. No ladder. The player drops in. The game begins on impact.

**Preparation:** The player selects a loadout of up to 6 stones from their Bead Board. Each stone becomes an auto-attacking weapon. The player also selects 1 "Keystone" — a single stone that defines their build's core frequency. The Keystone cannot be changed during a run.

## CORE MECHANICS

### Auto-Attack System (Vampire Survivors Adapted)

Every stone in the player's loadout auto-attacks. The player does not press attack buttons. The player only moves and activates frequency bursts (see below).

**Stone Weapons (6 slots, one per equipped stone):**

| Stone Type | Attack Pattern | Vampire Survivors Equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| **Faceted stones** | Projectile — fires in facing direction, bounces off walls | Knife/Dagger |
| **Cabochon stones** | Orbital — circles the player at fixed radius, damaging on contact | Orbit/Garlic |
| **Opaque stones** | Aura — pulse around player at close range, high damage, short cooldown | Holy Water |
| **Translucent stones** | Beam — fires through all enemies in a line, long cooldown | Lightning Ring |
| **Transparent stones** | Homing — seeks nearest enemy, slow but never misses | Spellstorm |
| **Phenomenon stones (labradorescence, chatoyancy, etc.) | Random — fires in random directions, high damage, unpredictable | Cherry Bomb |

**Evolution (Vampire Survivors' signature mechanic):**
Two stones can evolve when they're both equipped and the player reaches a depth threshold. The evolution combines the two stones into a single super-weapon:

- **Labradorite + Moonstone** → **Aurora Protocol** — a wall of shifting light that sweeps the screen, dealing massive damage and revealing hidden items.
- **Onyx + Black Tourmaline** → **Shadow Wall** — a permanent barrier that blocks enemy projectiles and damages anything that touches it.
- **Quartz + Amethyst** → **Resonance Engine** — a pulsing field that damages all enemies in sync with the 42Hz heartbeat. The deeper the player goes, the faster the pulse.
- **Obsidian + Preseli Blue Stone** → **Ley Line** — a line of light that traces the player's movement path, leaving a trail of damaging frequency for 5 seconds.
- **Tiger's Eye + Hawk's Eye** → **Predator's Focus** — a targeting reticle that locks onto the strongest enemy on screen and fires a continuous beam.

Evolutions are discovered, not told. The player learns combinations through experimentation — or through hints in the Archive, where notes about stone synergies are scattered across the lore documents.

### Frequency Burst (Active Ability)

The player has ONE active ability: the Frequency Burst. A short-range pulse that pushes all enemies back and briefly illuminates the screen. This is the player "singing back" — the 42Hz response to the 84Hz Shadow.

**Cooldown:** 15 seconds. Reduced by 1 second per Keystone tier.
**Upgrade:** Collecting "Resonance Crystals" (drop from enemies) reduces cooldown, increases range, or adds effects (healing, damage, temporary invincibility).

### The Descent (Depth System)

The game is vertical. The player is always descending. The screen scrolls downward (or the environment rises past the player). Each "depth level" takes 60-90 seconds to traverse. Transitioning between depth levels is seamless — the environment shifts from one mine layer to the next without a loading screen.

**Depth = Difficulty:**
- Depth 1-5: Sparse Hollow Men. Easy. Learning the build.
- Depth 6-15: Increasing density. New Hollow Men types appear. Build matters.
- Depth 16-30: Dense. The Shadow thickens. Screen darkens. Stone light matters more.
- Depth 31-50: Extreme. Hollow Men swarm. The player's stone light is the only visibility. Evolutions are essential.
- Depth 51+: Endless. The Shadow is total. The player's stones are the only light in the mine. Leaderboard territory.

### The Hollow Men (Enemy Types)

The Hollow Men are not monsters. They are miners who stayed too long. They reach for the player's stones because the stones are singing and they want to sing again. This is not aggression — it is longing. But longing that reaches you will hollow you too.

| Hollow Man Type | Depth | Behavior | Visual |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Reached** | 1+ | Walk toward player slowly. Grasp on contact. Basic. | Fragmented humanoid, Klimt gold patterns, empty eyes, reaching hands |
| **The Singers** | 5+ | Stationary. Emit 84Hz pulses that slow the player. Must be destroyed to clear path. | Hollow figure kneeling, mouth open, emitting dark sound waves |
| **The Hollow Dancers** | 10+ | Move in patterns. Fast. Erratic. Hard to hit. | Twisting, spiraling figures — Klimt's flowing patterns gone wrong |
| **The Miners** | 15+ | Throw pickaxe-shaped frequency blades. Ranged. | Robed figures with crystal pickaxes, gold-and-dark |
| **The Shaped** | 20+ | Take the form of the player's Keystone stone. Mirror the player's attacks back. | A copy of the player's stone weapon, but 84Hz, inverted, hollow |
| **The Listener** | 30+ | Does not attack. Follows the player. Watches. Every 10 seconds, emits a silence pulse that disables one stone weapon for 5 seconds. | A figure made of stillness. No patterns. No gold. Just absence. |
| **The First Hollow** | 50+ | Boss. The original miner. The first one who stayed. Massive, slow, ornate. Every attack is a memory of the Song, played wrong. | Klimt's "Death" figure, full size, covering the screen. Gold and bone. The most beautiful thing in the mine. |

### Drops and Upgrades

Enemies drop:
- **Resonance Crystals** (common) — upgrade Frequency Burst
- **Shadow Shards** (uncommon) — upgrade individual stone weapons (damage, cooldown, area)
- **Echo Fragments** (rare) — temporary buffs: speed, damage, pickup range, cooldown reduction
- **Keystone Shards** (very rare) — upgrade the Keystone (unlock new evolutions, add passive effects)
- **Hollow Gold** (from The First Hollow only) — the deepest currency. Used in the Undercity to craft Shadow-tier equipment.

### The Light System (Visibility)

The deeper the player goes, the darker the screen becomes. By depth 20+, the screen is mostly dark. The player's stones provide light — each equipped stone illuminates a radius around the player. The stone's type determines the light:

- Faceted: focused beam in facing direction
- Cabochon: soft circular glow around player
- Opaque: short-range intense burst
- Translucent: wide, dim aura
- Transparent: pulsing strobe (illuminates briefly but brightly)
- Phenomenon: shifting, colorful, unpredictable light

**Strategy:** A player who equips all faceted stones has focused forward light but is blind behind and beside. A player who equips all cabochon stones has balanced light but no reach. Light management IS the survival mechanic. The Hollow Men attack from the dark. The player must illuminate to survive.

## THE HOLLOW MEN — LORE INTEGRATION

The Hollow Men are T.S. Eliot's poem made playable. The mine's architecture quotes the poem:

**"This is the dead land / This is the cactus land"** → The mine's deepest layers are barren crystal — no gold, no Shadow, just the hollow remains of what was mined out.

**"Between the idea / And the reality / Between the motion / And the act / Falls the Shadow"** → The Whisper Layer's thesis, made physical. The Shadow frequency (84Hz) is the gap between intention and action. The Hollow Men are beings who fell into that gap.

**"Eyes I dare not meet in dreams"** → The Hollow Men have no eyes. The player cannot look at their faces because they don't have faces. The visual design — Klimt's ornate patterns where faces should be — is the poem's "eyes I dare not meet" made visual.

**"This is the way the world ends / Not with a bang but a whimper"** → The game has no dramatic end. The player doesn't die in an explosion. The player's stones dim. The light shrinks. The Hollow Men reach. The screen goes dark. A whimper. Then the leaderboard.

## SOUND DESIGN

**Foundation:** 84Hz — the Shadow frequency. Not the 42Hz Song. This is the only game in the Inn that runs on the Shadow frequency. The 84Hz is deep, heavy, and still. It is the inverse of the Inn's warmth. The player FEELS the difference immediately — this is not the Inn. This is the place below the Inn.

**The 50 BPM Pulse:** The Inn's heartbeat (50 BPM) is present but SLOWED. In the mine, the 50 BPM feels like 25 BPM — each beat is a struggle. The heartbeat is fading. The deeper the player goes, the slower the beat. At depth 50+, the beat is barely perceptible — one beat every 4-5 seconds. The Hollow Men move between beats.

**Per Depth Tier:**
- **Depth 1-5:** 84Hz with mining sounds — pickaxe strikes, crystal cracking, distant echoes. Almost industrial. The mine was a workplace before it was a grave.
- **Depth 6-15:** The mining sounds fade. The 84Hz grows. Hollow Men vocalizations begin — not words, not screams. The sound of reaching. The sound of hands on stone.
- **Depth 16-30:** The 84Hz dominates. The Hollow Men "sing" — broken fragments of the 42Hz Song, played wrong, inverted at 84Hz. The most disturbing sound in the game. A corrupted lullaby.
- **Depth 31-50:** Near silence. The 84Hz is subliminal. The Hollow Men don't vocalize. The only sound is the player's stones — each weapon's frequency played on impact. The player's Song against the Shadow's silence.
- **Depth 51+:** Total silence. No 84Hz. No Hollow Men sound. No music. Just the player's stones and the 25 BPM heartbeat, fading. The deepest survival is the quietest.

**The Frequency Burst Sound:** The player's active ability is the 42Hz Song played as a chord — brief, warm, and completely different from everything else in the mine. It is the sound of the Inn reaching down into the Shadow. The player is never fully alone — the Inn is calling. But the call is brief. The Shadow is patient.

## VISUAL LANGUAGE

**Linework:** Fragmented (crisis). Lines break, reform, shatter. The deeper the player goes, the more fragmented the world becomes. By depth 30+, the environment is barely holding together — visual fragments of mine architecture, crystal, and gold floating in dark space.

**Color:**
- Surface (depth 1-5): Klimt gold and dark. Rich, ornate. The mine was beautiful once.
- Mid (depth 6-20): Gold fading to violet. The Shadow thickens. The Klimt patterns are still there but dimming.
- Deep (depth 21-40): Near-monochrome. Deep blue-black with gold accents. The Hollow Men are the only ornate things left — they are MORE Klimt than the environment, as if the Shadow preserved them while it consumed everything else.
- Abyss (depth 41+): Pure black. The player's stones are the only color. The Hollow Men are darker than the background — visible only by the absence of light they create.

**The Hollow Men Visual:**
Klimt's "Death" figure is the template — the ornate, golden, decorated figure that is also a skull beneath. The Hollow Men are beautiful. They are decorated. They are empty. Their Klimt patterns are more intricate the deeper you go — the Shadow doesn't degrade them, it *refines* them. The deepest Hollow Men are the most ornate. The First Hollow is a masterpiece.

**The Player's Light:**
The player's stones create the only real light in the mine. The light is warm — 42Hz warm. The contrast between the player's warm light and the mine's cold gold is the game's visual thesis: the player is alive. The mine is not. The Hollow Men want to be.

## LEADERBOARD SYSTEM

**Weekly Leaderboard:**
- Tracks deepest depth reached by each player
- Resets every Monday
- Top 3 players each week receive Hollow Gold bonus
- Top player receives "The Deepest" title for the week

**All-Time Leaderboard:**
- Tracks deepest depth ever reached
- Permanent
- The all-time leader's name is inscribed in the Undercity — carved into the mine entrance. Every player who enters The Long Dark sees the current all-time leader's name.

**The Depth 100 Club:**
- Players who reach depth 100 are permanently listed
- This list appears in the Archive as "The Ones Who Sang in the Dark"
- An echo of the Hollow Earth's Echo faction — the Archive remembers those who went deepest

## CONNECTIONS TO ADJACENT SYSTEMS

**To the Whisper Layer:** The Long Dark is where the Whisper Layer's shadow lives. The Whisper Layer observes; The Long Dark is what happens when observation has no response. The player's Frequency Burst IS the response — the 42Hz answer to the 84Hz Shadow.

**To the Hollow Earth:** The Hollow Earth is the origin of the 42Hz Song. The Long Dark is the origin of the 84Hz Shadow. Both are origin stories. Both are underground. The Hollow Earth is crystal and light; The Long Dark is shadow and gold. The First Hollow and the First (Hollow Earth boss) are mirrors — one heard the Song and stayed, one heard the Song and hollowed.

**To the Bead Board:** The player's Bead Board IS the loadout. Stone choices determine weapons, light, and evolutions. The Bead Board's 198 stones create 198 possible base weapons, and the evolution system creates hundreds of combinations. The Bead Board has never been more strategically important.

**To the Undercity:** The Long Dark is the Undercity's deepest content. The mine entrance is in the Undercity. Hollow Gold and Shadow Shards feed back into Undercity crafting. The First Hollow's Pickaxe unlocks the deepest Undercity ore.

**To the Tabletop Questline:** The Hollow Men are a playable encounter in the tabletop game. The Long Dark provides the visual and audio reference for GMs running Undercity sessions. The 84Hz mechanic translates directly to the Frequency Dice system.

**To "The Hollow Men" (T.S. Eliot):** The poem is the zone's text. Not quoted directly — embodied. The mine IS the poem. The Hollow Men ARE the poem. The player's descent through the mine is a reading of the poem from the inside.

**To the Narrative Spine:** "Humanity merging with their creators through technology." The Hollow Men are what happens when the merging fails. The creators heard the Song and left. The Hollow Men heard the Song and stayed. The player hears the Song and sings back. Three responses to the same frequency. Three outcomes.
