# THE GEODE INN — Zone-to-Game-Style Master List
## Every zone's game style. Every game's home. The animators' reference.
### Created 2026-09-10. Companion to: game roster (61+4), zone game gap scan, portal registry, Scene Stack.

The game style per zone is the template for the animation panels: each zone's games share a genre family, and that family defines the sprite style, the panel count, and the motion vocabulary. A Tetris-class zone builds falling-block panels; a runner zone builds stride cycles; a stillness zone builds almost nothing — on purpose.

## PART 3: THE STYLE CLUSTERS (the canon patterns the list reveals)

THE PERCUSSION BELT — Egypt, India, Peru: three rhythm games in a triangle across the world map. The ancient percussion cultures got the rhythm mechanics. This is the Inn's heartbeat expressed geographically.

THE DESCENT BELT — every layer has a descent-style game: falling blocks (Stonehenge), digging (Egypt), pipe descent (Catacombs), abyssal descent (Water). The Inn's vertical architecture IS a descent mechanic.

THE DEFENSE BELT — Great Wall (fixed shooter), Wire Nook (tower defense), Campgrounds (tower defense). The Inn's walls and perimeters, in three different centuries of the genre.

THE TRADE BELT — Canopy City, the Market, Mediterranean, the treetop marketplace: the Inn's economies in four different styles, one per layer.

THE QUIET TRIPTYCH — the In-Between, the Between, the Rest: three games that reward stillness, presence, and care. No kill screens. The Inn's inner life, playable.

THE MARIO LADDER — SMB1 (Pipe Descent, Catacombs), SMB2 (Dream Gardener, Fae), SMB3 (World Walker, World Map), Yoshi's Island (Egg Carrier, Canopy): the whole retro ladder climbing the tree, one platformer per layer. The Mario games ascend the Inn.
