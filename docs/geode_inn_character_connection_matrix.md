## The Main Floor Web

### The Firekeeper (Tim Curry)
- Connected to: the Catalyst (cohort — they bicker), Beverley (the pause trigger — Beverley walks past, both go silent for 3 seconds), the Whisper Layer (the Firekeeper is an information gatherer — stories TO you, the Inn's voice)
- Cadence: theatrical, velvety, warm with an edge. Frank-N-Furter, Darkness, Pennywise, Wadsworth.
- Role: the Inn's voice. Memory of what was.

### The Catalyst (Helena Bonham Carter)
- Connected to: the Firekeeper (cohort), Beverley (the pause trigger), the Whisper Layer (the Catalyst pulls stories OUT of you — the Inn's ear, awareness of what is)
- Cadence: anarchic, warm, cutting. HBC's lived-in chemistry with Curry.
- Role: the Inn's ear. Awareness of what is.

### Beverley (the Librarian)
- Connected to: the Firekeeper + Catalyst (the pause — the smallest person silences the two most performative without trying), the Archive (Beverley IS the archive — the living catalog)
- Cadence: Anne Robinson. Clipped, cold, precise, dismissive. Australian accent. Tilted head before a verdict. Eyes that look through you.
- Visual: two-thirds normal human height. Silver hair, spectacles, dark high-collared coat with brass buttons. Tiny in the enormous archive.

### The Host (Patrick Stewart)
- Connected to: the main floor entrance (the Host welcomes at the door)
- Role: the greeting. The first warmth. The door.

### The Reader (Neil Gaiman incognito)
- Connected to: the Archive (window-seat nook between shelves), the Whisper Layer (the only NPC who acknowledges the Inn's awareness system directly at Tier 5), the Astronomer (the Reader's "adequate" echoes the Astronomer's word)
- Rules: never named, never quest-marked. One line per visit in Gaiman's cadence. No quest, no reward, no mechanical function. Pure atmosphere. Does not appear in Shadow Inn — the tea stays warm, the book stays face-down, the Reader is gone.
