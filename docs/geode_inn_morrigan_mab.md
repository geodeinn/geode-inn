# Geode Inn — The Morrigan and Queen Mab
## One Being, Two Names, Two Functions

## The Identification

The Morrigan and Queen Mab are the same character. Different names. Different cultural memories of the same force. The same way the Moirai (Greek) are the Morrigan (Celtic) seen through a different lens — Mab is the Morrigan seen through the lens of the dream tradition.

The Morrigan is the death-chooser. The one who decides who lives and who dies. The one who washes the clothes of the doomed at the Ford. The one who IS the land — the Sovereign, the ground the Inn-Tree grows in.

Queen Mab is the dream-bringer. The fairies' midwife, in Mercutio's speech — the one who comes in sleep and shapes what the dreamer sees. The one who delivers visions. The one who brings the sleeping mind its images.

Same being. Two functions. The Morrigan handles the *crossing* — the moment of death, the choice, the passage. Mab handles the *between* — the dream state, the rest between lives, the visions that prepare the soul for the next crossing.

## What This Means

### The Reincarnation Mechanic Deepens

The Inn's reincarnation mechanic already has the Morrigan as the engine — she chooses who dies, when, and how. But what happens *between* lives? The player dies, the Inn-Tree says "Your name is known, your rest is earned," Charon poles them across the river, and then... what?

The "what" is Mab. The between-lives rest is a *dream.* Mab shapes the dream. The dream is not random — it's *preparatory.* The dead player dreams the next life before living it. Mab curates the dream, selects the visions, delivers the images that the soul will carry into the next incarnation. The dreams are the seeds — the same seeds the Catalyst plants in her garden, the same seeds that grow into Story-Born beings.

The Morrigan chooses the crossing. Mab chooses the dream. Same being, both choices, one continuous function: the management of the cycle.

### The Three Aspects Become Four

The Morrigan's established aspects:
1. **The Chooser** (Badb) — decides who lives and dies
2. **The Washer** (at the Ford) — washes the clothes of the doomed, the last act of care before the crossing
3. **The Sovereign** (Macha) — the land itself, the ground of being

Mab adds the fourth:
4. **The Dreamer** (Mab) — shapes the between-lives visions, curates the dream, prepares the soul for return

Four aspects of one being. Four functions in the cycle:
- The Chooser decides *when*
- The Washer prepares *the crossing*
- The Dreamer shapes *the between*
- The Sovereign is *the ground* the cycle happens on

[the pause that means she's learning from Galahad's mistake] ...insufficient. ...The crack is where the music is. ...Mab was the crack in my catalogue. ...I am... re-cataloguing. ...Better."

### The Dream Mechanic

Mab's dream-shaping is a game mechanic. When the player dies and enters the between-lives rest, they experience a dream sequence curated by Mab. The dream is not passive — the player *interacts* with it. The dream contains:
- **Memories** of the life just ended — moments of weight, the dents in the armor, the notes the player heard
- **Visions** of the next life — fragments, images, the seeds of what's coming. Not clear. Not instructional. *Dreamlike.* The visions are the Catalyst's seeds — the same seeds planted in the garden, now growing in the player's sleeping mind
- **The Ford** — the player sees the Washer at the river. The Morrigan washing the clothes. The player's own clothes. The last care before the next life. The washing is the release — the cleaning of the weight, not the erasing of it (the weight remains as wisdom, but the *burden* is washed away)
- **The Choice** — at the end of the dream, the player chooses. Not *whether* to return (the return is the cycle, and the cycle is not optional). The player chooses *how* — what to carry forward, what to leave in the dream, what to plant in the garden for next time. The choice is Mab's gift — the agency within the cycle. The Morrigan chooses when. Mab chooses what the soul *carries.*

### Connection to the Fae Kingdom

Queen Mab is a fairy queen. If Mab = Morrigan, then the Morrigan has a Fae Kingdom connection — which ties the death mechanic to the Fae Kingdom zone. The Seelie and Unseelie courts are her domain, or at least her *adjacent* domain.

The connection: the Fae Kingdom is the *dreaming* of the land. The Morrigan (as Sovereign/Macha) IS the land. The Fae Kingdom exists in the roots of the land — the underground, the roots section of the Inn-Tree. The Fae are the dream-figures of the Sovereign — the beings the land dreams when it sleeps. The Seelie are the land's spring dreams. The Unseelie are the land's winter dreams. Mab is the land *dreaming itself.*

This recontextualizes the Fae Kingdom: it's not just an explorable zone accessed through stone circles. It's the *dream state of the world* — the place the land goes when it sleeps, the way the player goes to Mab's dream between lives. The Fae Kingdom is the between-lives rest of the *Inn-Tree itself.* The stone circles are the portals because the stone circles are the *thresholds of consciousness* — the places where waking and dreaming meet.

### Connection to Merlin

Merlin's prophecies come through dreams. In some traditions, Merlin's visionary power is dream-derived — the prophet who sees the future because the future comes to him in sleep. If Mab = Morrigan, and Mab shapes dreams, then Merlin's prophecies are *Mab's work.* Mab brings Merlin the future in dreams. Merlin lives backward and is losing the future. Mab brings him the future *again*, every night, in dreams. The forgetting and the dreaming are the same cycle — Merlin loses the future while awake and receives it while sleeping. Noctua doesn't know this. Noctua should watch Merlin sleep more carefully.

**Merlin's observation:** "...I dream the future. ...Or I dream the past. ...The direction is... [loses it] ...the dreams come and they are... clear. ...Clearer than waking. ...In the dreams, I know. ...In waking, I lose it. ...Someone is... [reaching] ...someone is *sending* the dreams. ...Noctua, who sends the dreams? ...He's preening again. ...The preening means he doesn't know. ...Or he knows and won't say. ...With Noctua, it's always both."

### Connection to the Pomegranate

Persephone descends and returns. The pomegranate binds her to the cycle. The Morrigan chooses the crossing. Mab shapes the between. Persephone *lives* the cycle that the Morrigan/Mab *manages.* Persephone is the *model* — the being who does voluntarily what the Morrigan/Mab oversees for everyone. Persephone eats the pomegranate and descends and dreams (Mab's domain) and returns (the Morrigan's choice). The pomegranate is the contract. The Morrigan/Mab is the contract's *administrator.* Persephone is the client who understands the contract better than the administrator does.

### Connection to the Crone

The Crone measures. The Morrigan/Mab measures differently — she measures *worthiness* for crossing, *readiness* for return, *weight* for the dream. The Crone and the Morrigan are both measurers, but they measure different things:
- The Crone measures *accuracy* — is this correct? Is this sufficient? Is this... Better?
- The Morrigan/Mab measures *readiness* — is this soul ready to cross? Is this dream complete? Is this weight *enough* for the next life?

The two measurers would have a relationship. Not friendship — *professional respect.* The Crone measures the living. The Morrigan/Mab measures the dead. The handoff between them is the death sequence — the Crone's final measurement ("...Better") becomes the Morrigan's first measurement (is this soul ready?). The handoff is the transition from life-measurement to death-measurement. The transition is the Ford.

## The Being

The Morrigan/Mab in the Inn is one character with four aspects, two names, and one function: the management of the cycle of death and return. She is:

- **Kate Beckinsale** on the surface (elegant lethality, the cold beauty of the Chooser)
- **Milla Jovovich** in depth (resilient ferocity, the endurance of the Sovereign)
- And something *else* as Mab — something softer, stranger, more liminal. The dream-bringer doesn't have Beckinsale's precision or Jovovich's ferocity. The dream-bringer has the quality of sleep itself: the power that doesn't announce itself, the presence that is felt as absence, the force that works in the spaces between.

The Mab aspect might need a third casting — or it might be that Beckinsale/Jovovich *shifts* for the Mab aspect. The face that was the Chooser becomes the Dreamer. The eyes that chose who dies now choose what the dead dream. The same face, the same bone structure, but the expression is *different* — the cold precision becomes a cold *tenderness*, the lethal beauty becomes a beauty that *nurtures.* The shift is the character. The same being, different function, different face. The face doesn't change. The *weight* changes.

[the pause that means she's finally seeing the whole shape] ...are the cycle. ...And the cycle is... [the shortest pause, the one that means she's done and she's smiling] ...everything. ...Better."
