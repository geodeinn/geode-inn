### Overview

The Planet Chamber is the Inn's solar system — a small, domed room at the center of which stands a mechanical orrery: a brass and crystal model of the solar system, each planet represented by a different gemstone. The orrery is the Inn's most concentrated piece of clockwork — a miniature cosmos, ticking softly, the planets rotating in their orbits at scaled speeds.

The chamber is the Inn's answer to the question the Astronomer won't complete. The Planet Chamber doesn't calculate the Song's completion — the Planet Chamber IS the completion, in miniature. The orrery is the Song made mechanical: each planet's orbit corresponds to a frequency, and the frequencies together form a chord. The chord is the Brísingamen chord — the same four tones Freyja taught. The planets ARE the chord. The solar system IS the Song.

The chamber is also the Inn's Hitchhiker's Guide moment. The theme brainstorm note says: "Planet Chamber → Anunnaki (Hitchhiker's Guide style)." The 42 easter egg lives here. 42Hz is the Song. 42 is the answer. The joke — the Inn's deepest joke, the one that runs beneath the entire game — is that Douglas Adams was right.
