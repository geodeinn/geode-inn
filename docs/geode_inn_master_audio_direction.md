# Geode Inn — Master Audio Direction
## The Inn IS a Musical Instrument

### Origin
Krista's sonic instinct was formed by peripheral listening — Depeche Mode through a sister's bedroom door, tempo as architecture, texture as language. The Inn's audio system is that instinct made into a building. Every frequency, every zone, every silence is placed the way a producer places a mix. The Inn is not a game with a soundtrack. The Inn IS the soundtrack.

---

## I. THE IEM-BASED ACOUSTIC ARCHITECTURE

The entire Inn functions as a transducer — an in-ear monitor system scaled to building size. Based on Empire Ears IEM engineering:

| Driver Type | Inn Equivalent | Frequency Range | What You Feel |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dynamic Driver | Stones / Matter | Sub-bass — the 42Hz Song | Frequencies you FEEL |
| Balanced Armature | Characters / Voices | Mid-range — dialogue, vocals | Frequencies you HEAR |
| Electrostatic | Cosmology / Sky Gods | High-shimmer — stars, glass, crystals | Frequencies you SENSE |
| Bone Conduction | Catacombs / 42Hz Song | Deep resonance — the ground state | Frequencies you KNOW |

### System Constants
- **42Hz** — The Song of Making (Living frequency — growth, warmth, the Inn)
- **84Hz** — Shadow Inn inverse (Gorgon's Garden — stay/petrify, the 84Hz "stay" is the inverse of 42Hz "grow")
- **111Hz** — Gargoyle Watchers (resonant sentinels, ancient frequency)
- **50 BPM** — Canopy Heartbeat (the Inn's resting pulse — the sway of the tree, the dragon's sleeping breath)
- **7.83Hz** — Schumann Resonance (Earth's electromagnetic baseline — calibration tone)

## II. STYLE TARGETS — THE SONIC PALETTE

These are TEXTURE and COMPOSITIONAL STYLE targets for AI-generated original compositions. We are stealing the METHOD, not the MELODY. Every track is an original Inn-proprietary composition that RESEMBLES the listed artist's texture, instrumentation, tempo, and emotional quality.

**Emotional Quality:** Ancient, ceremonial, grounding — the sound of humans reaching back to something older than language

## III. CINEMATIC SCORE STYLE TARGETS

### Epic Dark Fantasy
**Reference:** Game of Thrones (Ramin Djawadi)
**Compositional Techniques:** Heavy cello ostinatos, dark strings, modal minor keys, sparse piano motifs over drone, thunderous low brass, theme recurrence through variation
**Tempo Range:** 60-120 BPM (varied — tension to battle)
**Emotional Quality:** Political weight, moral complexity, the cost of power
**Zones:** Catacombs, Shadow Inn, Undercity, Hollow Earth, any zone involving the Hollow Men or deep conflict

### Mythic Orchestral
**Reference:** Lord of the Rings (Howard Shore)
**Compositional Techniques:** Full orchestral with folk elements, solo vocal themes (wordless), pennywhistle for innocence, hardanger fiddle for northern cultures, choral passages in constructed languages, leitmotif architecture binding zones together
**Tempo Range:** 50-140 BPM (full range — from Shire gentleness to war charge)
**Emotional Quality:** The weight of a world — ancient, vast, beautiful, worth fighting for
**Zones:** Fae Kingdom (Seelie), Cosmology, World Map exploration, epic quest moments, any zone where the Inn reveals its full scale

### Dark Age Battle / Period Grit
**Reference:** The Last Kingdom
**Compositional Techniques:** Dark age instrumentation (frame drums, bone flutes, chant), period-accurate instruments, tension-building drones, battle rhythm, raw vocal textures, minimal orchestration — power through sparseness
**Tempo Range:** 70-130 BPM (tension to combat)
**Emotional Quality:** Mud, blood, shield walls — history as it felt to the people living it
**Zones:** Staircase (alchemy/combat transition), Hollow Men mines, Shadow Inn combat zones, any zone involving ancient warfare or the 84hz "stay" mechanic

### Baroque Modern / Period Tension
**Reference:** Harlots (composition style)
**Compositional Techniques:** Harpsichord, baroque strings (viola da gamba, theorbo), period instrumentation produced with modern atmosphere, refined elegance with underlying tension, the contrast of civility and danger, stately rhythms that feel slightly too controlled
**Tempo Range:** 70-110 BPM (measured, stately, controlled)
**Emotional Quality:** A beautiful room where something is wrong — refinement as armor, politeness as weapon
**Zones:** Main Floor (evening mode), Artisan Market (high-end), any zone involving social hierarchy, negotiation, or the Inn's more civilized dangers

## IV. COMPOSITIONAL TECHNIQUE TARGETS

### The Devil's Interval (Tritone)
**Reference:** Sweeney Todd (Stephen Sondheim)
**Technique:** The tritone (augmented fourth / "diabolus in musica") as structural foundation — not as a moment of dissonance, but as the INTERVAL the entire zone is built around. Sondheim built Sweeney Todd's score on the tritone so that the wrongness is constant, ambient, architectural. The audience feels unease before they know why.
**Application:** Undercity, Hollow Men mines, Erasure sections of the In-Between, Gorgon's Garden (84Hz petrification zones). The tritone should be the ZONE's DNA, not a jump scare. Beautiful on the surface, menace underneath — the barber chair feeling.
**Implementation:** Compose zone ambient tracks using tritone-based harmonies as the drone foundation. Layer folk/period instruments on top so the dissonance is felt, not heard consciously. The player should feel that something is wrong in these zones without being able to identify the musical source.

### Overlapping Counterpoint
**Reference:** Les Miserables (Claude-Michel Schonberg)
**Technique:** Multiple independent melodies sung simultaneously, each one a complete character perspective, converging into a unified harmonic structure. "One Day More" — every character sings their own truth at the same time, and the truths don't cancel each other out — they CREATE each other. The counterpoint IS the point: multiple valid perspectives existing at once.
**Application:** Main Floor (bustling mode — multiple NPC voices layering), Fae Kingdom court scenes (Seelie and Unseelie themes overlapping), campfire singalongs (multiple NPCs joining), any moment where the Inn feels alive with overlapping voices. Also the architectural metaphor for the Inn itself — every zone has its own melody, and the Inn is all of them playing at once.
**Implementation:** Compose tracks with 3-5 independent melodic lines that work alone but create unexpected harmony when layered. Each line represents a character or zone perspective. When the player enters a space where multiple NPCs are active, their theme lines layer into the counterpoint.

## V. THE LOW-DUB PIPELINE

All public domain classical recordings are transformed into original Inn-proprietary ambient tracks through the low-dub process:

### Process
1. **Source:** Public domain recording (Bach, Beethoven, Vivaldi, Satie, etc. from Wikimedia/Musopen)
2. **Tempo shift:** Slowed to 50 BPM (the Canopy Heartbeat — the Inn's resting pulse)
3. **Low-pass filter:** Remove high frequencies, retain sub-bass and fundamental tones
4. **Sub-bass boost:** Enhance frequencies below 80Hz for chest resonance
5. **Reverb:** Cavernous, long-tail reverb to create spatial depth
6. **Result:** Original ambient composition that RESEMBLES the source but is legally a derivative transformation

- **Backing track:** Low-dub version of each folk song (50 BPM, sub-bass enhanced, reverb-drenched)

## VIII. COMPOSITIONAL PRINCIPLES

### 1. The Inn Is a Transducer, Not a Speaker
Music in the Inn is not played AT the player. It is FELT through the player. The IEM architecture ensures that every frequency has a physical correspondence — sub-bass is felt in the chest (stones), mid-range is heard in the ears (voices), high shimmer is sensed in the skull (cosmology), and deep resonance is known in the bones (catacombs). Compose for the BODY, not just the ears.

### 2. Steal the Method, Not the Melody
Every style target is a TEXTURE reference, not a song to copy. We study how Danheim builds tribal tension through layered chanting, then build ORIGINAL chanting in the Inn's own linguistic tradition. We study how Sondheim uses the tritone, then build ORIGINAL tritone-based compositions. The Inn's music is proprietary because the METHOD is public domain and the MELODY is ours.

### 3. 50 BPM Is the Heartbeat
The Inn's resting pulse is 50 BPM — the sleeping dragon's breath, the canopy sway, the tempo of a body at rest. Even when zone tracks run faster, the 50 BPM sub-pulse should be felt underneath, like a heartbeat under conversation. This is the unifying rhythm that makes the Inn feel like ONE place, not 37 different soundtracks.

### 4. Frequency as Architecture
42Hz and 84Hz are not just sound design — they are the Inn's structural duality. Living zones hum at 42Hz. Shadow zones press at 84Hz. The In-Between exists at the threshold. Every composition should DECLARE its frequency identity through its bass content. The player should feel the frequency change in their body when they cross a zone boundary.

### 5. Silence Is Music
The most important sound in the Inn is the absence of sound. The In-Between proves this — near-silence as a complete sonic environment. The Modern Legends Nook proves this — one piano note with infinite reverb. The Music Room's Tchaikovsky moment proves this — the silence after the last note is the most emotional sound in the Inn. Compose with silence as a primary instrument, not as the absence of music.

### 6. The Les Mis Principle
When multiple voices are present, they should not take turns. They should overlap. Each voice is a complete melody. Together they create harmony that no single voice could predict. This is the Inn's social philosophy in sound: every perspective is valid, every voice is complete, and the community is not a chorus singing one melody — it is a counterpoint of independent truths that create something bigger than any one of them.

### 7. The Sondheim Principle
When something is wrong, the music should know before the player does. The tritone, the slight detuning, the rhythm that's almost regular but not quite — these are the Whisper Layer's musical expression. The player should feel unease in the Undercity before they understand what the Undercity is. The music does the philosophizing before the brain gets a vote.
