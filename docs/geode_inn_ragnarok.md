## 1. Overview & Philosophical Foundation

Ragnarok in the Geode Inn is not an event of destruction, annihilation, or failure. Ragnarok is a **COMPLETION EVENT**. It is the majestic, inevitable moment when a universal cycle completes its designated harmonic pattern, closes its ledger, and opens the door for the next cycle to begin. 

The cosmos of the Geode Inn moves in an eternal sequence of Songs. Each universe is defined by its fundamental resonant frequency:
* **The Previous Universe:** Sang at **41.7Hz** (The Ancient Tone — deep, resonant, heavy with memory).
* **The Current Universe:** Sings at **42Hz** (The Inn's Song — balanced, vibrant, holding the World Tree in bloom).
* **The Interstitial Hush:** Pulses at **0.3Hz** (The Void Heartbeat — the fetal pulse in Nyx's womb between cycles).
* **The Next Universe:** Will sing at an **Unknown Frequency** (The Unwritten Tone — waiting to be struck).

When the 42nd death of a cycle aligns precisely with the 42Hz Song, the protective shell of the Cosmic Egg cracks. The cycle reaches its mathematical and musical climax. Ragnarok is the graduation ceremony of the cosmos. The Traveler who triggers and completes Ragnarok has not lost the game; the Traveler has finished the Inn's story, fulfilled the Golden Contract, and earned passage into the permanent archive or the void beyond.

Throughout the Ragnarok event, the fundamental truth of the Geode Inn is spoken in the Refrains of the Adequate:

> *The both are: the adequate — the death and the birth, the same moment, different name.*
> *The both are: the adequate — the ending and the completing, the same event, different meaning.*
