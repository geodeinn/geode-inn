# Geode Inn — The Crystal Canticle
## Quest Chain: Crystal-Energy Devices, Ancient to Modern

> STATUS: SPEC (host RULED: Leonardo, confirmed by the Witan 2026-09-26; name pending)
> Foundation layer: Mohs-frequency vibration base (all games) + the Singing Stones facet (2026-09-26)

### Overview

A 7-gate construction quest chain in which the player builds, in historical order, every device humanity has ever made that uses a crystal as its working heart — from the first ground lens to the oscillator ticking in the player's own pocket. The chain is the honest-label biography of crystal energy: how the world went from *believing* stones held power to *engineering* it, and discovered the engineering was the better magic.

This is the Singing Stones facet made playable. The base law it stands on: hardness is a vibrational property (soft stones absorb, hard stones ring), and the same quartz family that fills the jeweler's trays keeps time in every satellite, phone, and watch on Earth. The player learns the whole arc by hand.

**Design laws in force:** Anti-Skinner (no compulsion loops; construction happens in Build mode at the 50 BPM heartbeat), Toll Booth (each device extracts a real cost — see Gate 6), Honest Label (every gate carries a dated real-source label; folklore is taught warm, never mocked, physics is taught proud), Quest Mark law (approach marks glow per difficulty tier), No-Fail (a mis-tuned device doesn't break — it hums wrong and teaches).

**Mechanical base:** Each construction uses the Mohs-frequency register. The player must tune the device to the correct vibrational register of the stone they've chosen — a soft stone in a hard-stone slot produces a dull, audible failure that is information, not punishment. Stone choice is the puzzle; the fixture is the lesson.

## Gate 1: "The Seer's Stone"
**Device: the crystal ball (rock crystal quartz).**
**Zone: z07 The Archive → z02 Clockwork Skies**
**Honest label:** Scrying spheres were real objects of polished rock crystal; the oldest worked quartz spheres date to antiquity, and lapidaries ground them by hand for months. The seeing was folklore — the *grinding* was engineering, and it was magnificent. Label taught warm: people weren't foolish to look into a perfect sphere and feel something; they were early.

The player receives a raw quartz point and learns to grind, sand, and polish it into a sphere at Calder's bench (multi-session craft, Build-mode rhythm). The finished sphere sits on a stand and *works as a lens*: light through it projects the Archive's candle flame onto the wall. The first device converts light into image.

**Mechanic:** Lapidary patience-craft. Toll: months of in-game sessions, the player's own hands.
**Quest Mark tier:** candle flicker (approachable to all).

---

## Gate 2: "The First Spark"
**Device: the piezoelectric striker.**
**Zone: z02 Clockwork Skies workshop.**
**Honest label:** 1880, Pierre and Jacques Curie: squeeze a quartz crystal and it produces a spark of electricity. Piezoelectricity — pressure-electricity — named the year it was found. Every gas lighter clicked since is a Curie demonstration.

The player assembles a striker: a cut quartz blank, a spring hammer, a strike plate. Click: a visible spark. The chain's thesis lands in one sound — *the stone speaks when struck, and its speech is lightning.*

**Mechanic:** First tuning puzzle (Mohs register: only the hard-stone blank sparks; a soft-stone blank gives a dull click — the audible difference is the lesson).
**Quest Mark tier:** candle flicker.

---

## Gate 3: "The Cat's Whisker"
**Device: the crystal radio.**
**Zone: z02 workshop → the Campgrounds.**
**Honest label:** Early 1900s: a galena crystal touched by a fine wire ("cat's whisker") pulls voices and music out of the open air with NO battery, NO power of its own. Crystal sets were the first electronics many families ever owned. The stone was the radio.

The player winds the coil, mounts the galena, hunts for the sweet spot on the crystal face with the whisker wire — and the Inn's own campfire broadcast (the drum-circle feed) emerges from nothing. First contact with the frequency world.

**Mechanic:** Dexterity + patience: the sweet-spot hunt. The whisker's found point is where the Mohs register of galena meets the wire's pressure. No power source anywhere in the build — the air provides.
**Quest Mark tier:** lantern glow.
**Toll:** the player must donate their Gate 1 sphere to the Archive's display case to fund the wire. (The first device becomes an exhibit; the craft always feeds the building.)

## Gate 4: "The Singing Sliver"
**Device: the quartz oscillator.**
**Zone: z02 Clockwork Skies — Leonardo's own bench.**
**Honest label:** 1917–1920s: quartz crystals first hold a fixed frequency for sonar and radio; by WWII quartz was a strategic war mineral, the Allies nearly ran out, and the race to grow it in laboratories began. A singing stone became national infrastructure.

The player cuts, plates, and mounts a quartz blank into an oscillator circuit. When it ticks, the room's other machines visibly settle — the Zone's ambient mechanisms synchronize to it for one visible minute. Leonardo's bench clock drifts no more.

**Mechanic:** Precision craft; the tuning fork of the chain. The blank must be cut to thickness for the target register; the player hears wrong-cut as detune and right-cut as ring.
**Quest Mark tier:** hearth light.

---

## Gate 5: "The Wrist Heart"
**Device: the quartz watch.**
**Zone: z02 Clockwork Skies → the Spiral Gallery (timekeeping hall).**
**Honest label:** December 25, 1969: Seiko Astron, the first quartz wristwatch. Inside, a crystal beating 32,768 times per second — 2 to the 15th — divided down to exactly one pulse per second. Every sleeping phone and watch on Earth carries a stone counting powers of two. The heartbeat of the modern world is arithmetic in a crystal.

The player miniaturizes: oscillator, divider circuit, display. The finished watch is wearable (equippable, cosmetic + one mechanical function: it displays true in-game time, visible to the player at any moment, in any zone).

**Mechanic:** Assembly puzzle; Build-mode entrainment is highest here — the player's hammering syncs to the 50 BPM heartbeat as the calibre comes together.
**Quest Mark tier:** hearth light.

---

## Gate 6: "The Grown Garden"
**Device: the grown crystal itself.**
**Zone: The Greenhouse (z28 Garden wing) → z02.**
**Honest label:** Natural quartz is rarely pure enough, so industry grows its own: hydrothermal autoclaves feed seed crystals mineral-rich water and stones accrete by the ton; the Czochralski process pulls pure silicon from a melt as a single enormous crystal, sliced like a geode into every chip on Earth. Nobody mines the heartbeat. They farm it. Every silicon wafer is one grown crystal, and every crystal a sliced geode — the Inn's name written in the supply chain.

The player tends an autoclave: feeds the seed, waits at biological-time tempo (real in-game days — gentle, no Skinner loop), harvests a grown crystal, and slices it into blanks. Enough blanks for one final device — and one gift.

**Mechanic:** Stewardship-craft, the EverQuest register: keeping a light on in a pressure vessel. Toll Booth (heaviest toll of the chain): the seed crystal for the autoclave must be a stone FROM THE PLAYER'S OWN COLLECTION — purchased stones don't take. The garden only grows from what you were keeping anyway.
**Quest Mark tier:** Prism Spiral (the chain's hardest ask — loveliest light).

---

## Gate 7: "The Player's Pulse"
**Device: your own oscillator, tuned to you.**
**Zone: The Campfire.**
**Honest label:** No date. This one is yours.

The player builds a final oscillator from a grown-Garden blank, tuned not to any standard register but to the player's own signature frequency (derived from their play history: their stones, their zones, their hours — the game computes a personal note). The finished Heartbeat Stone is placed on the Pulse Line, where it ticks at the murmur's edge of hearing from then on, in every zone the player visits. Their room audibly keeps them.

**Witness (Historical Instrument precedent):** on completion, a Historical Master appears at the campfire to witness the device — Leonardo, who watched the whole chain build, hearing a sliver of stone keep time more perfectly than any pendulum he ever drew. He says one line and lets it be.

**Reward:** the Heartbeat Stone (permanent, personal, zone-portable), title "Timekeeper", QuestCharm entry below.
