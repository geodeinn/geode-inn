**Source:** Walkthrough Dissection 13, Option 1 (the Great Hall) + the Level 3 Main Floor doc (8 spaces). The spec is COMPLETE. This parcel changes nothing; it stages what already exists.

The Inn's spine: the front door, the hearth's glow ahead, the staircase rising behind. Kubrick's single framed axis — front door, hearth, staircase — held for the first full minute of the arrival shot, then free camera.

- **The Host is the room's greeting.** "Come in, you're safe here" on the first visit; warmer, never longer, every visit after. The lamps were lit before you arrived.
- **The Pulse Line is native here.** Warm amber-gold, 50 BPM, the Inn's vital sign — and the accessibility tutorial happens by firelight, not by menu: the Host's three gifts (audio, haptic, visual ways to feel 50 BPM), one per early visit.

- **Aesthetic:** Klimt's amber and weight under Matisse's light, hobbit-cozy meets ancient library, Del Toro's worn asymmetry (every chair sat in, every table scratched with use).
- **Audio:** the heartbeat at its loudest anywhere in the Inn — the altar is one level below, and the Main Floor is the drum's own skin. The Host's voice sits 3dB under the pulse. No one talks over the heartbeat.
