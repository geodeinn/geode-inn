## WHAT YOU'RE LOOKING AT (THE JEWELRY TRANSLATION)

You are holding a finished armature with settings in every stage of the craft:

- **The central cylinder** — the strand. The core wire the whole piece is strung on.
- **The rectangular spokes** — the prong-work. Wire arms reaching out from the strand.
- **The rooms at the spoke ends** — the settings. Each bezel holds one room of the Inn.
- **The West Staircase helix** — the chain. Four turns of wrapped wire connecting every layer, the way a chain carries the whole piece.
- **The Prism at the bottom point** — the pendant. The pulsing stone the whole piece hangs from (50 BPM, steady light, always).

### The three stone states (the legend)

| Status | Color | Jewelry meaning |
|--------|-------|-----------------|
| **LIVE** | ember glow | Set and polished — the stone is in the bezel and you can visit it in the web beta today |
| **SPEC** | gold | The setting is fully built on paper — the stone is cut, the mount is drawn, the setter is waiting |
| **DRAFT** | dim | Unset — the concept exists, the cabochon is still rough in the tray |

Current honest count: **19 rooms — 1 live · 11 spec · 7 draft.** The dim settings are not failures. They are the work tray. Every ember in the building was dim once.

## WALK THE INN (TRYING THE PIECE ON)

- **Tap any room** (in orbit view) and you are standing at its door — from inspecting the piece to wearing it, at whichever setting you choose.
- **WALK THE INN** button drops you from jewelers' loupe view to eye height on the Main Floor step — the default front door.
- **W / arrow up** — step forward. **S / arrow down** — step back.
- **A / D or left/right arrows** — sidestep.
- **Q / E** — move between levels (downstairs, upstairs).
- **Drag** — look around.
- **Tap WALK again** — stop walking, float back to workshop view.
- On a phone: drag to walk/look, tap the buttons for levels.

The scaffolding walk is exactly what it sounds like — walking through the armature before the stones are set. The true player experience lives in the web beta; this is the workbench view.

## THE FINDINGS LAYER (YOUR INSIGHT, CANONIZED)

In jewelry, findings are the quiet hardware — clasps, jump rings, crimps — that hold the showpiece together. Nobody admires them; nothing holds without them.

The Inn has a findings layer too: the **West Staircase** (the clasp that makes one piece of many layers), the **stone circle portals** (jump rings — pass through and you're on another strand entirely), the **thresholds and doorways** (crimps — small, load-bearing, everywhere), and the **quest links** (the solder joints — where two rooms become one story).

**The room-by-room walk with the printed portfolio in hand is the findings-and-links session:** each bezel gets its stone confirmed, and every finding that connects it gets named. Nothing in this piece is strung alone.
