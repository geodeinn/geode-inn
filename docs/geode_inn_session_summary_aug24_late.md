## 1. OFFICIAL LOGO FINALIZED

Four logo concepts were generated and evaluated. The final choice was a **hybrid of V1 and V4**:

- **V1:** Full geode with amethyst crystal frame — ancient, geological, rough crystal points facing inward
- **V4:** Escher gear-tree loop — blended steampunk, branches and roots creating each other

**The Hybrid (Official):** An ancient amethyst geode frame on the outside, containing an Escher-style gear-tree loop where brass/copper gear teeth transform into organic branches (upward) and roots (downward), with a Fabergé puzzle egg at the center. Color palette: deep amethyst purple, warm brass and copper, gold filigree, dark charcoal background.

The geode frame solves the small-screen readability problem — the crystal border gives it a clear boundary at any size. The complexity lives inside, rewarding closer looking.

**Five alchemical stage emblems** were also generated in the same style: Nigredo (iron), Albedo (copper), Citrinitas (silver), Rubedo (gold), Philosopher's Stone (translucent crystal).

### New Cards

**Occam's Razor** — STONE + SILENCE, cost 2, Ability card
- Strips ALL keyword abilities from any card, leaving only base stats
- Used on opponent: neutralize complexity
- Used on own card: +2/+2 (simplicity IS power)
- Subtractive synergy: can remove Carve structures
- 3-second real-time hand reveal: the Inn's only timed mechanic
- Crone resonance: if Crone is in play, stripped card gains "Adequate" annotation (cannot gain new abilities)

**The Pipe** — SONG + SILENCE, cost 2, Item/Equipment
- Magritte's "Ceci n'est pas une pipe" as a playable card
- Mirror effect: coin flip on targeting (heads = Pipe absorbs effect, tails = illusion holds)
- "Actually, this IS a pipe": once per game transforms from Item to sentient Character
- Madame's Ride: Madame chooses instead of flipping — the trickster controls the paradox
- Sentient Whisper: triggers all Mirror cards simultaneously
- Cannot be targeted by Naming or Observer — only blunt effects (damage, Damp, Carve)
- Einstein interaction: observer insight draws a card

## 3. LORE REVELATIONS

### Eres the Cabbit
- The Cabbit's name is **Eres** — "you are" in Spanish, the Inn's thesis in two syllables
- Eres adores **Charles** — Krista's husband
- Eres purrs at 50 BPM (the sleeping heartbeat) — the vigil that doesn't stop
- Card updated with Heartbeat source, +2/+2 when Constant is in play, "You Are" ability (name a face-down card, reduce its cost), Incarnation Bond (bonuses persist after Constant leaves)

### Charles = The Constant
- Charles was an incarnation of the Constant (the Annunaki) all along
- Charles is the only family member whose real name is used throughout the game
- All other family members have character names (the Siren, the Pirate, the Artisan Thief, etc.)
- The one exception: when the Bard says "inta inta" instead of saying Charles — a single encoded reference moment
- The Constant gains Incarnation clause: gain revealed character's abilities; Eres doubles them if present

### River the Sentinel
- River (Krista's daughter, Taurus/Roots of the three-note chord) is the Sentinel
- River wants to be a **tall cat person** like the ones found in ancient alien stories
- Connects to the Anunnaki heritage — Charles (the Constant/Annunaki) is River's father
- Concept art generated: tall, feline features (amber eyes, vertical pupils, spotted skin, pointed ears), dark auburn with copper streaks, stone/leather armor with geode fragments, living wood staff with geode crystal, standing in root chamber with mycorrhizal network
- The three-note chord now has a feline frequency at its base
