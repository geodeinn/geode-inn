# Geode Inn — Phase 1 Concept Art Prompts

## Shared Aesthetic Direction
All art should follow this tone: scholarly, atmospheric, mythological. Dark, warm, weight-of-history. Think "A Discovery of Witches" meets an ancient library. The baby dragon mascot is cute, but the surrounding world has depth and gravitas. NOT a cartoon/kids' game aesthetic. Colors: deep earth tones, crystal accents, warm amber lighting against dark stone. Think illuminated manuscripts, candlelit stone, gemstone refraction in darkness.

---

## The Baby Dragon Mascot (used across all games)

### Prompt: Baby Dragon — Full Character Sheet
A small, chubby baby dragon with iridescent crystalline scales that subtly shift color like the inside of a geode. Big expressive amber eyes. Tiny wings too small to fly yet — they flutter when excited. The dragon's belly is translucent, and you can see tiny crystals forming inside (the geode is growing within). Curious, intelligent expression — not babyish or dumb, but wide-eyed with ancient knowing. Warm cream and amber tones for the body, with veins of crystal color (blue, purple, green) running through the scales. The tail ends in a small crystal formation. Style: semi-realistic fantasy illustration, painterly, NOT cartoonish. Think Studio Ghibli meets illuminated manuscript. Three poses: standing, sitting on a crystal, and curled up sleeping inside a geode. Size reference: fits in a teacup.

### Prompt: Baby Dragon — Riding on Nessie's Back
The baby dragon mascot perched on the head of the Loch Ness Monster as it surfaces from dark water. The dragon looks delighted, tiny wings spread. Nessie is a massive serpentine creature with dark green-grey scales, ancient and weathered. The dragon is tiny in comparison — about the size of Nessie's eye. Moody Scottish loch lighting, mist, dark water. Painterly semi-realistic style.

## Game 1: Standing Stones (Stonehenge — Tetris)

### Prompt: Game Background — Stonehenge at Twilight
Stonehenge at twilight, viewed from inside the stone circle. The massive trilithon arches frame a deep amber and purple sky. The stones are weathered, covered in ancient lichen and faint glowing runes that pulse with soft golden light. The ground is grassy with small crystals embedded in the earth between the stones. In the distance, the Salisbury Plain stretches out under a sky full of stars just appearing. The atmosphere is reverent, ancient, weight-of-history. Warm candlelight glow emanating from between the stones. Dark, moody, painterly semi-realistic style. NOT cartoonish.

### Prompt: Falling Block — Trilithon Arch Piece
A Tetris-style game piece shaped like a Stonehenge trilithon — two vertical standing stones with a horizontal lintel stone on top. The piece glows with faint amber runes along its edges. Weathered grey stone texture with lichen. The piece has weight and mass — it looks like real stone, not a cartoon block. Dark background with crystalline particles floating. Semi-realistic, painterly.

### Prompt: Falling Block — Single Standing Stone
A single standing stone Tetris piece — a weathered monolith with ancient carvings running vertically. Glowing faintly with blue-white crystal energy at its base. Mossy, ancient, heavy. Dark background. Semi-realistic.

### Prompt: Enemy — Chibi Druid
A small but menacing chibi druid emerging from between standing stones. Wearing a dark hooded robe with Celtic knotwork embroidery in gold thread. Holding a twisted wooden staff topped with a glowing amber crystal. Face is shadowed under the hood, but eyes glow faint green. NOT cute — unsettling and ancient. Small stature but powerful presence. The druid's robe has faint Ogham script along the hem. Painterly semi-realistic chibi style — small proportions but detailed and atmospheric.

### Prompt: Reward Stone — Preseli Blue Stone
A raw Preseli Blue Stone (dolerite) crystal floating in a shaft of amber light. The stone is blue-grey with white speckles (the actual Preseli bluestone from Wales, used at Stonehenge). It pulses with inner light — blue and white energy. The stone sits on a dark crystal formation. Dramatic lighting against a dark background. Semi-realistic, detailed mineral illustration.

## Game 2: Tomb Tunnel (Egypt — Dig Dug)

### Prompt: Game Background — Egyptian Underground
Cross-section view of an Egyptian tomb underground, Dig Dug style. Layers of sand, limestone, and darker bedrock visible as horizontal strata. The player tunnels through these layers, leaving carved paths. Embedded in the rock: golden scarabs, canopic jars with jackal heads, fragments of hieroglyphics, and small lapis lazuli crystals. Deeper layers are darker and older — the color shifts from warm sand-yellow at the top to deep stone-grey at the bottom. Faint torchlight glow from the tunnel paths. Atmospheric, dark, weighted with ancient secrets. Semi-realistic painterly style.

### Prompt: Enemy — Ammit
A chibi version of Ammit, the Egyptian devourer of the dead. Small but intimidating. Body combines lion, hippo, and crocodile features — lion's mane and front legs, hippo's round body, crocodile head with rows of tiny teeth. Golden-bronze skin with scales. Wearing a small Egyptian collar with lapis inlay. Eyes glow amber. The creature is chunky and heavy-looking but moves with predatory intent. Chibi proportions (big head, small body) but NOT cute — menacing and ancient. Painterly semi-realistic style with Egyptian art motifs.

### Prompt: Guide — Chibi Anubis
A small chibi Anubis — jackal-headed guide. Slender, elegant even in chibi form. Black-furred jackal head with golden eyes. Wearing a simple white Egyptian kilt and a narrow gold collar. Holding a small ankh that glows with warm light. The character points downward, guiding the player deeper underground. Regal, mysterious, gentle but powerful. Painterly semi-realistic style with ancient Egyptian aesthetic.

### Prompt: Enemy — El Naddaha
A chibi siren of the Nile — a beautiful but eerie female figure visible in the water that seeps through the underground tunnels. Long dark hair, pale skin, ancient Egyptian jewelry. Her lower body trails off into water and shadow. She sings — visualized as faint golden sound waves emanating from her. Her eyes are hypnotic, glowing blue. Small chibi proportions but hauntingly beautiful and dangerous. Painterly, atmospheric.

### Prompt: Reward Stone — Lapis Lazuli
A raw lapis lazuli crystal formation deep inside the Egyptian tomb. Deep blue with gold flecks (pyrite) that look like stars in a night sky. The stone sits on a small golden scarab base. Golden torchlight reflects off the gold flecks. Dramatic, jewel-like, luminous against dark stone. Semi-realistic detailed mineral illustration.

## Game 3: Andean Run (Peru — Temple Run)

### Prompt: Game Background — Machu Picchu Terrace Run
A view along the stone terraces of Machu Picchu, Temple Run perspective — third-person, looking forward along the path. The terraces drop away to the left into cloud-filled valleys. To the right, ancient Incan stone walls rise up. The path winds through grassy terraces, stone archways, and cliffside steps. Mountains and clouds fill the background — the Andes in golden hour light. Small crystals and gold artifacts are scattered along the path as collectibles. Inti (sun god) symbols are carved into the stone walls, glowing with warm golden light. The atmosphere is breathtaking, vertiginous, ancient. Semi-realistic painterly style, rich colors.

### Prompt: Enemy — El Muki
A chibi El Muki — the Peruvian cave goblin that chases the player through the mines. Small, stocky, muscular. Dark reddish-brown skin, glowing red eyes, wild black hair. Wearing tattered miner's clothes and carrying a rusty pickaxe. His feet are backwards (a detail from the actual folklore — his footprints lead the wrong way). He charges forward aggressively, pickaxe raised. Menacing but small. Chibi proportions with detailed, weathered texture. Painterly semi-realistic style with Andean setting.

### Prompt: Enemy — Chullachaqui
A chibi Chullachaqui — the Amazonian jungle dwarf who mimics loved ones to lead you astray. Small, hunched, with one foot that is a twisted root or deer hoof (from the folklore). His face can shift — one moment it looks like a friendly face, the next it reveals a wild, feral creature underneath. He hides behind jungle foliage on the edge of the path, half-visible, with an unsettling knowing smile. Green and brown earthy tones with subtle supernatural blue glow. Chibi proportions but uncanny and eerie. Painterly semi-realistic.

### Prompt: Collectible — Inti Blessing
A golden sun disc symbol of Inti (the Incan sun god), floating along the runner's path. Radiating warm golden light rays. Intricate Andean geometric patterns carved into its surface. Small, collectible-sized, glowing against the darker path. Semi-realistic, luminous.

### Prompt: Reward Stone — Blue Opal
A raw Peruvian blue opal, translucent blue-green with flashes of pink and green fire inside. Sitting on a carved Incan stone pedestal. The opal glows from within with shifting colors — ocean blue, sky blue, flashes of pink. The stone pedestal has Andean step patterns carved into it. Dramatic lighting, jewel-like. Semi-realistic detailed mineral illustration.

## Game 4: Monster of the Deep (Loch Ness — Water Monster Game)

### Prompt: Game Background — Loch Ness
A side-view cross-section of Loch Ness — the dark, deep Scottish lake. Above the water: moody Scottish Highlands, misty mountains, grey sky, a few tiny tourists on the shore with cameras. Below the water: deep, dark, layered blues getting darker as you descend. Ancient stone formations and kelp forests at the bottom. The water has particles, bubbles, and the occasional fish swimming by. A few small wooden boats and kayaks float on the surface with tiny people in them. The atmosphere is mysterious, cold, ancient. The depth is intimidating. Painterly semi-realistic, atmospheric.

### Prompt: Player Character — Nessie
The Loch Ness Monster as a game character, side-view, swimming through the water. A massive serpentine creature — long neck, dark green-grey scales with moss and age, small head with expressive amber eyes. The body extends behind with a long tail that creates a wake. The body is textured with age — barnacles, ancient scars, faded bioluminescent patterns that pulse faintly along her flanks. She looks ancient, powerful, and slightly amused. The baby dragon rides on her head. NOT a cartoon — realistic, painterly, mythic creature. Side-scrolling game character perspective.

### Prompt: Enemy — Tourist in Canoe
A tiny tourist in a red kayak, viewed from the side (the player/monster's perspective). The tourist has a camera and is excitedly trying to photograph the water. They're oblivious to the massive creature approaching from below. The kayak is bright and small against the dark water. Comedic but atmospheric. Semi-realistic, detailed small character.

### Prompt: Special Attack — Tail Splash
A visual effect of Nessie's tail slapping the water surface, creating a massive wave that knocks boats over. The water crashes outward in a dramatic splash, boats tumbling, tourists flying off with surprised expressions. The splash catches amber light from the sky above. Dynamic, energetic, painterly. Semi-realistic.

### Prompt: Reward Stone — Freshwater Pearl
A luminous freshwater pearl sitting on a dark river stone at the bottom of the loch. The pearl is creamy white with pink and green overtones, perfectly round, glowing softly in the dark water. Small ripples of light emanate from it. Ethereal, precious, quiet. Semi-realistic detailed illustration.

## UI Elements (Shared Across Games)

### Prompt: Game Hub — The Geode Cave
The interior of a geode — the baby dragon's home and reward room. The camera looks out from inside a massive hollow geode. The walls are lined with crystals of every color — amethyst purple, quartz clear, citrine amber, tourmaline green, sapphire blue. Some crystal slots are filled (glowing with earned stones), most are empty (dark, waiting). The baby dragon sits in the center on a smooth stone floor, looking up at the crystal walls. Warm light emanates from the filled crystals. The empty slots are dark and shadowy. The geode is enormous — cathedral-like interior space. Painterly, semi-realistic, breathtaking.

### Prompt: World Map — Pin Styles
Four types of map pins for the world map, shown as a set:
1. **Stone Pin**: A small crystal/gemstone icon, glowing in the stone's color
2. **Lore Pin**: An ancient scroll/quill icon, warm amber glow
3. **Ancient Site Pin**: A golden temple/pyramid icon, animated pulse
4. **Water Body Pin**: A teal ripple/wave icon with a subtle monster silhouette below

All pins should be small, elegant, and readable at a glance. Semi-realistic, not cartoonish. Set against a dark parchment-style world map background.

### Prompt: The Inn — Main Hub Background
The main hall of the Geode Inn — a massive underground chamber that is literally the inside of a geode. The walls curve inward and are covered in crystal formations of every color. Warm amber lanterns hang from crystal formations. A large stone archway leads deeper into the inn (the tree structure). To one side, a wooden market stall with jewelry displayed on crystal shelves. To another, a spiral staircase carved from stone leading both up (to cosmology) and down (to catacombs). The ceiling opens to show stars and cosmic formations (upstairs hint). The floor is polished stone with natural geode formations embedded. Cozy, warm, ancient, wonder-inducing. NOT a cartoon inn — a geological cathedral. Painterly, semi-realistic, breathtaking scale.
