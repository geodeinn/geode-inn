# Geode Inn — Missing Stone Descriptions
## Batch 4: The Jades & Garnets (6 stones)
## Drafted 2026-09-04 — in the Inn's voice, Krista-approved style

---

### 1. Guatemalan Jade
**Mind:** The Maya had gold. They used it to decorate. For treasure, they chose jade — the color of living water and young corn, the stone that took longer to carve than a life sometimes lasted, and was worth it. Guatemalan jade is that judgment call, still standing: a whole civilization looked at two shiny things and correctly identified which one mattered. It carries the courage of good values — the strength to treasure the durable thing in a room full of the loud one.
**Body:** Supports the kidneys and the body's filtration — the slow, essential work that no one applauds and everything depends on.

### 2. White Jade
**Mind:** White jade is decision, distilled. Where other jades carry color, white jade carries quiet — the nephrite essence with nothing added, the stone equivalent of a cleared desk. It does not tell you what to choose. It removes enough noise that choosing becomes obvious, which the Inn notes is the same service, performed honestly. The mind that holds white jade stops arguing with itself long enough to notice it already knew.
**Body:** Supports the immune system and overall calm — the body's white room, where healing happens fastest.

### 3. Jadeite
**Mind:** Jadeite is jade at its most essential — the rarer, harder, more transparent of the two stones the world calls jade, the one that holds the title Imperial. It forms under pressures that would ruin lesser minerals and comes out not hardened but refined: what remains when everything nonessential has been squeezed out of the material. It is the stone of the self after the fire — not what survived, but what was worth surviving as. The Inn respects few stones more.
**Body:** Supports the heart and the body's deep detoxification — the slow refinement that happens under pressure.

### 4. Grossular Garnet
**Mind:** Grossular garnet is the garnet family's gardener — named for the gooseberry, and faithful to it: green like new leaves, honey-gold like the afternoon they ripen in, cinnamon like the season after. While its cousins carry fire and passion, grossular keeps the small sweetnesses: the taste of a good ordinary year. It teaches abundance at the right scale — not a fortune, a harvest.
**Body:** Supports digestion and the body's quiet absorption — the work of taking sweetness in and making it last.

### 5. Ruby with Kyanite
**Mind:** Ruby with kyanite is two stones sharing one pendant: the fire and the rudder. Ruby burns — passion, courage, the engine at red heat. Kyanite aligns — the blade that never needs cleansing, true at any frequency. Alone, each is half a method: fire without direction exhausts itself, and direction without fire never leaves the harbor. Together they are the Inn's favorite kind of marriage, in mineral form: the flame that stays on course because something steady is holding the compass.
**Body:** Supports the blood and circulation — vitality that moves with purpose instead of scattering.

### 6. Sapphire (Tundra)
**Mind:** Tundra sapphire is the sapphire that came out of the ground honest — earth-toned, silk-threaded, bearing its inclusions the way the far north bears its weather, openly. Where a flawless sapphire is impressive the way a vacant sky is impressive, a tundra sapphire has a history you can see. Its wisdom is the kind that arrives scratched: the knowledge that did not come free, and does not pretend otherwise.
**Body:** Supports the eyes and long endurance — the body that has learned to see clearly through imperfect conditions.
