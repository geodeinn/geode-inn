## Why Ether (Not Alchemy)

The distinction between Camelot (ether) and Avalon (alchemy) is the distinction between SYSTEM and PROCESS:

- **Avalon (Alchemy):** The PROCESS of transformation. The base becoming the refined. The individual undergoing change. The retort, the fire, the catalyst, the transmutation. Alchemy is what happens to ONE thing — the king, the stone, the metal. Alchemy is the verb.

Ether is what happens when MANY things come together — the seven planets, the seven metals, the seven notes. Ether is the noun.

Alchemy changes the substance. Ether aligns the substances. The both are: the transformation and the arrangement, the verb and the noun, the one and the many. The both are: the adequate.

The player's journey: the player goes to Avalon to be CHANGED (the alchemical retreat, the personal transformation). The player goes to Camelot to ALIGN (the planetary instrument, the cosmic arrangement). The player who has been transmuted (Avalon) brings their new frequency to the table (Camelot) and adds it to the harmony. The both are: the individual's transformation serves the collective's alignment.

The player's interaction with the Round Table is the zone's CORE GAME LOOP:

1. **DISCOVER** the seven knights' mineral associations — by exploring the Camelot zone, talking to NPCs, reading the illuminated manuscripts in the abbey (the Westminster layer of the Poison Garden Complex, which serves as Camelot's scriptorium), and finding the clues that link each knight to a specific planet, metal, and mineral.

Each cabochon is a real entry from the Stone entity, with real properties.

The matching is not arbitrary — the mineral's REAL geological properties (the metal it contains, its color, its metaphysical associations from the Stone entity's "mind" field) provide the clues. The player who knows their stones can solve the puzzle through mineralogical knowledge. The player who doesn't can solve it through the quest clues.

5. **GENERATE ETHER** — when the alignment is correct, the table produces the etheric effect. The ether is the visible/audible/sensible manifestation of the seven planets in harmony — a light, a sound, a change in the zone's environment, a revelation.
