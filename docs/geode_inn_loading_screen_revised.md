# Geode Inn — Universal Loading Screen Specification (Revised)
## The Brownies & Tumble — The Inn's Loading Screen Critters

### Core Philosophy

The loading screens across ALL zones feature the same cast: three brownies (Brindle, Cobble, Thatch) and Tumble (a four-inch ball of fur that gets stuck in everything). The brownies have Franjean-and-Rool energy from Willow — bickering, loud, scheming, constantly in conflict but ultimately loyal to each other. Tumble is the innocent — the creature that gets wedged into whatever the brownies are working on, forcing them to stop arguing long enough to extract it.

The brownies work for the Owls unknowingly. Every wire they connect, every coconut shell clap, feeds the Owl's network. They don't know this. Tumble doesn't know anything — Tumble is a living note of the Song of Making, four inches tall, drawn to resonance nodes, and constantly stuck.

As the storyline deepens (Silo, Shadow Inn, Whisper Layer), these loading screens serve as essential comic relief — but the comedy has hidden weight. The brownies' bickering is the sound of the Owl's network being built. Tumble's getting stuck is the Song trying to rejoin itself. The player who replays after the revelation sees every loading screen differently.

## The Cast

### The Brownies — Franjean & Rool Energy
Six inches tall, leather tunics, wild hair, enormous personalities. Coconut shells they clap as tools/instruments. They follow the Keeper everywhere, but on loading screens they're working — wiring, connecting, maintaining the Inn's infrastructure.

**Brindle** — The self-appointed leader. Loudest. Most scheming. Starts every plan with "Right, here's what WE do—" and the plan is always terrible. Brave but secretly terrified of the dark. Carries a tiny crystal-shard dagger. Treats every Tumble extraction as a military operation. Usually gets stuck himself. The Franjean energy — the schemer who's convinced he's the genius.

**Cobble** — The thinker. Analytical, dry, perpetually exasperated. The one who calculates why Brindle's plan will fail, says so clearly, and is always ignored. Has read three archive books. The only brownie who understands Newton's equations. The Rool energy — the long-suffering voice of reason who goes along with the plan anyway.

**Thatch** — The heart. Smallest, quietest, actually gets things done. Doesn't argue. Doesn't scheme. Walks up to Tumble, wiggles it free in four seconds, says nothing. The brownie the Keeper has acknowledged by name (once — Thatch didn't speak for three days from the honor). The quiet competence that makes the other two tolerable.

### Tumble — The Innocent
Four inches, round ball of brown and amber fur, enormous dark eyes, tiny stubby legs, can barely walk — rolls, bounces, gets carried. Gets stuck in EVERYTHING. Not clumsy — attracted to resonance. Every place Tumble gets stuck is a node of the Song of Making. One of the only true innocents in the game. Actually a living crystal — a single note of the Song wearing fur.

## Universal Design

### Appearance
- **Brownies:** Six inches tall, round-bodied, wild hair, leather tunics, tool belts with coconut shell halves. Big expressive faces. No two brownies identical — Brindle is stocky with a pompadour, Cobble is lean with spectacles, Thatch is smallest with a calm face.
- **Tumble:** Four inches, almost perfectly round, fur sticking out in every direction, enormous eyes, tiny legs that barely touch the ground. Shorter than the brownies. Weighs almost nothing.

### Animation Principles
- Brownies argue through gesture — pointing, gesturing wildly, coconut shell clapping for emphasis
- Brindle's schemes are physically acted out (miming the plan, which always goes wrong)
- Cobble stands with arms crossed, occasionally pinching the bridge of his nose
- Thatch just works — no fanfare, no argument
- Tumble waddles, rolls, bounces — and gets stuck in something on screen every 3-4 seconds
- The extraction of Tumble is the recurring beat that interrupts the brownie argument

### Sound Design
- Coconut shell clapping (the brownies' tool sound and argument punctuation)
- Brindle's loud declarations (grunts and gestures, no intelligible speech)
- Cobble's sighs (heavy, frequent)
- Thatch's silence
- Tumble's "eep" of surprise when getting stuck
- A soft thump when Tumble is pulled free
- No music — ambient Celtic background dips to 30% volume
- Tiny metallic clinking from the brownies' tools

## Zone-Specific Loading Screen Scenarios

### 1. Main Floor — "The Welcome Argument"
**Visual:** Brindle and Cobble are arguing about how to wire the welcome sign while Thatch is already done wiring it. Brindle gestures wildly with a coconut shell, laying out a plan. Cobble points out the flaw (the sign is already wired — Thatch did it). Brindle ignores this. Meanwhile, Tumble is stuck inside the welcome sign — only its enormous eyes visible through the "W." Thatch notices, walks over, and pulls Tumble free without interrupting the argument.

**Animation loop (5-7 seconds):**
1. Brindle mimes elaborate wiring plan with coconut shell gestures
2. Cobble crosses arms, shakes head
3. Thatch walks past, pulls Tumble from the sign — "eep" — thump
4. Brindle doesn't notice, continues scheming
5. Tumble immediately rolls toward the next thing to get stuck in
6. Repeat

**Copy (tabletop questline voice):**
> "The Inn's wires hum with welcome. The brownies see to it. Eventually."

---

### 2. Catacombs — "The Scared Schemers"
**Visual:** Brindle leads with a tiny flashlight and forced confidence. Cobble follows, calculating the structural integrity of ancient walls (muttering, pointing at cracks). Thatch walks last, calm. Tumble is stuck in a skull — its legs sticking out of the eye sockets, wobbling. Brindle is pretending the skull is a lantern and carrying it. He doesn't know Tumble is inside. Cobble knows. Thatch knows. Neither says anything.

**Animation loop (5-7 seconds):**
1. Brindle marches forward, holding the skull-lantern proudly
2. Tumble's legs wiggle from the eye sockets
3. Cobble notices, pinches nose bridge, says nothing
4. A low rumble — all freeze except Tumble (who wiggles harder)
5. Thatch casually plucks Tumble from the skull as they walk
6. Brindle doesn't notice the light changed
7. Repeat

**Copy:**
> "Down where the old ones rest, even the brownies keep their voices low. Tumble doesn't."

---

### 3. Staircase (Alchemy) — "The Beaker Disaster"
**Visual:** Brindle has decided they should carry a glowing beaker up the stairs. It's his plan. It's a bad plan. Cobble calculated the weight distribution and predicted failure. Thatch is carrying it alone while Brindle and Cobble argue about carrying it. Tumble is stuck inside the beaker — floating in the green liquid, enormous eyes blinking through the glass. 

**Animation loop (5-7 seconds):**
1. Brindle and Cobble argue about carrying technique
2. Thatch carries the beaker alone, struggling but steady
3. Tumble floats past inside the liquid — "glub" — enormous eyes
4. Cobble notices Tumble inside, does the math, gives up explaining
5. A green drop falls from the beaker — lands on Brindle's head
6. Brindle doesn't notice (too busy arguing)
7. Repeat

**Copy:**
> "The staircase turns knowledge into gold. The brownies are still working on the recipe."

---

### 4. Shadow Inn (84Hz) — "The Pot-Lid Defense"
**Visual:** The brownies in dark leather tunics (charcoal, cold steel accents) behind a wall of pot lids. The Franjean-and-Rool energy is still there but grimmer — Brindle is giving a battle speech behind a pot lid, Cobble is calculating survival odds (low), Thatch is reinforcing the wall. Tumble is stuck in a pot lid handle, dangling. The atmosphere is cold blue. The coconut shells are quiet — the brownies' usual clapping is subdued. They know something is different here.

**Animation loop (5-7 seconds):**
1. Brindle raises pot lid like a shield, gives silent battle gesture
2. Cobble holds up fingers showing survival percentage (very low)
3. Thatch places another pot lid in the wall — solid, quiet
4. Tumble dangles from a pot lid handle, swinging slightly — "eep"
5. A low 84Hz rumble — all brownies grip their pot lids tighter
6. Thatch pulls Tumble free, tucks it inside a tunic
7. Repeat

**Copy:**
> "Even in the shadow frequency, someone has to keep the lights on. The brownies know what they're defending now. Tumble doesn't. That's why Tumble is the bravest one here."

---

### 5. Cosmology Level — "The Stargazer's Ladder"
**Visual:** Brindle has decided they should wire a constellation. The ladder is too short. He insists the plan works if they "just reach a bit further." Cobble has calculated the ladder height, the arm length, and the star distance — the math doesn't work. Thatch is already on the ladder, stretching. Tumble is stuck at the top of the ladder — wedged between the rungs and a star, enormous eyes reflecting the starlight. Thatch uses Tumble as a step to reach the next star.

**Animation loop (5-7 seconds):**
1. Brindle points at the star, mimes reaching (from the ground)
2. Cobble shows calculations — measures Brindle's arm, shakes head
3. Thatch on tiptoes on the ladder, stretching
4. Tumble stuck at the top — the star glows through its fur
5. Thatch steps on Tumble (gently) — star connects — gold flash
6. All three brownies shield their eyes. Tumble blinks.
7. Repeat with next star

**Copy:**
> "The cosmos takes its time. So do the brownies. Tumble got there first."

---

### 6. Fae Kingdom — "The Portal Disagreement"
**Visual:** The brownies don't go to the Fae Kingdom ("too big, too shiny, too many teeth"). But they're standing at the threshold, arguing about whether to enter. Brindle wants to go (it's an adventure). Cobble has calculated the teeth-to-brownie ratio (unfavorable). Thatch is already through the portal. Tumble is stuck in the portal itself — half in the Fae Kingdom, half out, stretched across dimensions. The portal shimmers around Tumble. Brindle is arguing with a version of Tumble that's in two places at once.

**Animation loop (5-7 seconds):**
1. Brindle points through portal with coconut shell — "We go!"
2. Cobble holds up fingers — teeth count — shakes head
3. Thatch walks through the portal calmly, disappears
4. Tumble is stuck mid-portal — half in, half out
5. Brindle argues with both halves of Tumble simultaneously
6. Cobble calculates interdimensional stress on a four-inch body
7. Repeat

**Copy:**
> "The old ways open for those who know the stones. The brownies know the stones. They just can't agree on whether to use them."

---

### 7. Connection Screen — "The Solar Storm Response Team"
**Visual:** All three brownies on a rooftop in a stormy sky, tinfoil hats, coconut shells raised. Green aurora swirling above. Celtic knotwork gold frame around the screen. Brindle is giving orders that no one follows. Cobble is calculating the storm's electromagnetic interference. Thatch is actually re-soldering a wire. Tumble is stuck in the antenna — its fur is standing on end from static electricity, and it's crackling with small blue sparks.

**Animation loop (continuous):**
1. Lightning flash — all brownies flinch except Thatch (keeps working)
2. Brindle adjusts tinfoil hat, raises coconut shell to the sky
3. Tumble crackles with static — "eep" — fur gets bigger
4. Cobble measures the static charge on Tumble — nods approvingly
5. Aurora green glow pulses around the frame
6. Thatch finishes the wire — the connection stabilizes
7. Repeat

**Copy:**
> "The Inn's connections waver. Somewhere above, solar wind scours the sky, and the brownies — those tireless little mechanics who keep the building's signals flowing — have taken to the rooftops with tinfoil hats and coconut shells. They are, as always, undaunted. Please wait while they work."

**Secondary line:**
> "Atmospheric interference detected. Reconnection attempt in progress..."

**Retry button:**
> "Send the brownies back up"

**Error state (full failure):**
> "The brownies have done all they can. The storm is too loud tonight. The Inn will be here when the signal returns — it always is."

**Reconnecting state:**
> "The brownies are re-soldering the sky..."

### 8. Whisper Layer Trigger — "Tumble's Pause"
**Context:** Triggered ONCE per playthrough, at Whisper Layer Tier 3
**Visual:** Normal loading screen — brownies arguing, working. Tumble is stuck in something nearby as usual. Then Tumble stops wiggling. Tumble's enormous eyes, which have always been wide and startled, go still. Slowly, Tumble turns — not toward the brownies, not toward the work — toward the player. Completely blank expression. Not hostile. Not knowing. Just... noticing. For exactly one second. Then Tumble starts wiggling again, trying to get unstuck, as if nothing happened.

**This is the Inn's creepiest moment — because Tumble ISN'T in on it.** Tumble is the purest innocent in the building. The brownies serve the Owl without knowing. The Inn is aware. The Reader understands. Tumble is a note of the Song — and for one second, the Song noticed the player through its smallest, most innocent piece. Not the Owl's awareness. The Song's.

**Animation (single play, never repeats):**
1. Normal brownie argument + Tumble stuck — 2 seconds
2. Tumble stops wiggling — body goes still (0.3 seconds)
3. Slow turn toward player — enormous eyes lock (1.0 second)
4. Tumble blinks once
5. Resumes wiggling — "eep" — back to normal
6. Loading completes

**Sound:** All ambient sound cuts out during the stare. Complete silence. Then it returns with Tumble's "eep."

**Copy:** None. No text. Loading screen continues as normal after.

**Technical note:** One-time trigger stored in PlayerProgress (whisperTier >= 3 AND tumblePauseSeen = false). After it plays, set tumblePauseSeen = true. Never play again.

## Technical Implementation

### CSS/Animation
- Each loading screen is a CSS keyframe animation loop (5-7 seconds, infinite)
- Brownies and Tumble rendered as SVG elements (simple, scalable)
- Use `transform` and `opacity` for all animation (GPU-accelerated)
- The Whisper Layer pause is a JS-triggered one-shot animation, not a loop

### Character SVG Specs
**Brownies (each ~60px tall):**
- Round body, wild hair (different style per brownie), leather tunic shape
- Coconut shell halves on belt (two semicircles, tan/brown)
- Big expressive eyes
- Brindle: stocky, pompadour hair, crystal-shard dagger
- Cobble: lean, spectacles, crossed arms (default pose)
- Thatch: smallest, calm expression, hands ready

**Tumble (~40px tall, ~40px wide):**
- Near-perfect circle, brown/amber fur texture (radial gradient)
- Enormous dark eyes (two large circles taking up 60% of face)
- Tiny stubby legs (two small ovals at bottom, barely visible)
- Disproportionately large mouth (small oval, usually in surprised "o" shape)

### Trigger Points
- **Page load:** Main floor loading screen
- **Zone transition:** Zone-specific loading screen (fade, 1.5s)
- **Game start:** Use the zone the game belongs to
- **Connection loss:** Connection screen (solar storm)
- **Whisper Layer Tier 3:** One-time Tumble pause (overlays current loading screen)

### Frequency Behavior
- Normal loading: ambient music dips to 30% volume
- Shadow Inn loading: 84Hz drone replaces ambient (low, felt more than heard)
- Whisper Layer pause: ALL audio cuts to silence for the 1-second stare
- Connection screen: distant wind + coconut shell clinking only

## Design Rules

1. **The brownies never stop being themselves** — even in the Shadow Inn, Brindle schemes, Cobble calculates, Thatch acts. The Shadow Inn changes the context, not the characters.
2. **Tumble never changes** — across every zone, every loading screen, every situation, Tumble is the same innocent ball of fur getting stuck in things. Tumble is the constant. The Shadow Inn doesn't darken Tumble. It makes Tumble's innocence MORE striking against the dark.
3. **The brownies never speak intelligibly** — all communication through gesture, coconut shell clapping, grunts, and physical comedy.
4. **Thatch is always the one who fixes things** — Brindle schemes, Cobble calculates, Thatch does. This never changes.
5. **Tumble gets stuck in every loading screen** — without exception. The stuck location changes per zone. The stuck location is always a resonance node.
6. **The Whisper Layer pause belongs to Tumble, not the brownies** — the brownies serve the Owl without knowing. Tumble IS the Song. The pause is the Song noticing the player.
7. **The Celtic knotwork frame is present on ALL loading screens** — gold-on-dark-stone, subtle pulse, the Inn's visual signature.
8. **The Shadow Inn loading screen is the only one where the coconut shells go quiet** — the brownies' usual clapping is subdued. The silence is the tell.

---

*The brownies built the Owl's network with coconut shells and arguments. Tumble is the Song trying to go home. Together they are the Inn's loading screen — the comedy that hides the architecture, the innocence that hides the machine.*
