## The Character

### What She Is

Nyx is the night. Not the night sky (that's the Astronomer's domain — the stars, the visible, the things you can see). The night ITSELF — the dark that the stars exist inside. The substance of darkness. The medium that makes light visible.

In Greek mythology, Nyx emerged from Chaos at the beginning of everything. She is one of the first beings — older than the Olympians, older than the Titans, older than the earth. She is the mother of Death (Thanatos), Sleep (Hypnos), Dreams (Oneiroi), the Fates (Moirai), Doom (Moros), Retribution (Nemesis), and many others. She is the dark from which the terrifying and the beautiful both come.

In the Geode Inn, Nyx is the third resident of the cosmology level — the level's medium. The Astronomer studies the sky (the content). The Constant carries through the void (the space). Nyx IS the darkness (the substance). Three residents, three relationships with the unknowable: the one who calculates it (Astronomer), the one who trusts it (Constant), the one who IS it (Nyx).

### The Cloak of Universes

Nyx's cloak is the most ancient garment in existence. It is woven from the starlight crystals of every cycle that has ever been — each crystal a universe's compressed Song, threaded into the fabric of the night. The Constant carries one crystal at a time through the void. Nyx wears ALL of them. The Constant's sequence — the ordered cycles, the path through the void — is woven into Nyx's cloak. The Constant put the cycles in order; Nyx wears the order. The cloak is the history of everything, worn as clothing.

The cloak looks like the night sky — but deeper. When the player looks at the cloak, they see not stars but *universes.* Each point of light on the cloak's surface is a starlight crystal — a dead universe's final Song, compressed into mineral form, glowing faintly with the frequency of a cosmos that no longer exists. The cloak contains billions of these points. The cloak is the largest thing in the Inn, worn as clothing by the smallest-feeling person in the cosmology level.

The cloak moves like a night sky moving — slow, vast, the fabric rippling like aurora but deeper. When the cloak moves, the crystals shift, and the shift creates a sound — the overlapping of billions of Songs from billions of cycles, each one slightly different, each one the frequency of a universe that was. The sound is the closest thing to the "sound of everything" that the Inn can produce. It's not the 42Hz Song (that's this cycle's Song). It's the sound of ALL the Songs — the cosmic accumulation, the music of every cycle layered on every other cycle. The sound is overwhelming if you hear it clearly. Nyx wears it so you don't have to. The cloak muffles the sound. The cloak IS the muffling. The darkness dampens the noise of everything that was, so that this cycle's Song can be heard.

The Alanis "Thank U" energy: Nyx is grateful for every dead universe in her cloak. "Thank you, 41.7. Thank you, 41.3. Thank you, 40.8. Thank you, every Song that was loud enough to become a crystal and quiet enough to become a thread." The gratitude is not for the good things — it's for ALL the things. The dark and the light. The dead universes and the living one. The Alanis gratitude that includes the terrible: "Thank you, terror. Thank you, consequence. Thank you, nothing."

### The Children

Nyx is the mother of the dark things. In the Inn, her children are not literal offspring but *thematic ancestors* — the Inn's dark mechanics all trace back to her:

- **Death (Thanatos)** → the Morrigan (the Chooser), Charon (the Ferryman), the reincarnation mechanic. The Morrigan chooses WHO dies. Charon carries the dead. Nyx birthed the concept of dying itself. The mechanism traces to her.
- **Sleep (Hypnos)** → the sleeping giants, the One Who Is Awake (who stays awake while others sleep). Sleep is Nyx's gift — the kindness of unconsciousness, the rest from the cycle. The giants sleep because Nyx sent Sleep to them.
- **Dreams (Oneiroi)** → the Peruvian guide (who tends the giant's dreams), the dream-weather, the Inca terraces as dream-language. Dreams are Nyx's children. The giant's dreams — the weather, the terraces, the 42Hz Song dreaming itself — are Nyx's grandchildren.
- **The Fates (Moirai)** → the Queen of Webs (who weaves fate), the Morrigan (who chooses fate). The Fates are Nyx's children. The Queen of Webs' thread is a fate-thread. The Morrigan's choosing is a fate-act. Both derive from Nyx's primordial mothering of the concept of destiny.
- **Doom (Moros)** → the Constant's knowledge that each cycle ends. Doom is not punishment — it's *inevitability.* The Constant carries the crystal knowing the cycle will end. That knowing is Nyx's child — the awareness of finitude.
- **Retribution (Nemesis)** → the frog transformation mechanic (community moderation, reclaiming power). Nemesis is balance — the force that restores equilibrium. The frog transformation is the Inn's Nemesis — the mechanism that lets victims reclaim power. Nemesis is Nyx's child.

Nyx doesn't control her children. She birthed them. She let them go. The Alanis "Head Over Feet" energy: the mother who loves her children and lets them be what they are, even the scary ones. "I birthed Death and I birthed Dreams and they're both mine and they're both necessary and I don't apologize for either."
