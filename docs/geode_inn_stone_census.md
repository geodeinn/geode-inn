# The Stone Census — All 200 Stones Mapped to the World
## The Storyline Tie, Phase 1: Placement
## Drafted 2026-09-04 — for Krista's review before full storyline entries are drafted

Every stone in the Inn's cases, placed where it lives in the world. One line each: the zone, the hook, the beginning of the story. Phase 2 will expand these into full entries (zone presence, quest ties, spirit connections, Song role).

---

## COSMOLOGY (upstairs — the stars, the source, the Allfather)

- **Moonstone (Celestial)** — the Allfather's own stone; the light that governs tides from a room with no water.
- **White Moonstone** — the moon's borrowed light, kept at the Observatory rail for guests who arrive at night.
- **Peach Moonstone** — first light on skin; the dawn watch's stone, kept where the upstairs meets the day.
- **Rainbow Moonstone** — the Fae moonstone, but the original lives in the Observatory — the Fae borrowed it, and both parties remember.
- **Diamond** — pure carbon, the star that finished; the Allfather's floor has one set into it, unretrievable on purpose.
- **Herkimer Diamond** — the sky's double-terminated clarity; the only quartz allowed upstairs without an escort.
- **Clear Quartz** — the universal amplifier; the Observatory's tuning fork, the stone the Song is calibrated against.
- **Aqua Aura** — quartz with gold fused to it by human fire; the upstairs forgives the intrusion, because the result is sky-colored.
- **Angelite** — the stone with "angel" in its name and no interest in denying it; the softest presence in the highest room.
- **Tanzanite** — the deepest blue-violet the eye can hold; found in one place on earth, near a mountain that is climbing; the cosmology's own color.
- **Octave Stone** — the Inn's own; the eighth note, the one that completes the scale and starts it over; the Song's stone, kept at the 42Hz source.
- **Stillness Stone** — the Inn's own; the stone that does not resonate; the cosmology keeps one so guests can hear what silence actually is.
- **Kunzite** — the heart's high-frequency stone; pale pink that deepens in daylight — the upstairs teaches it patience.
- **Morganite** — the beryl of gentle hearts; kept near the Observatory rail where the engaged couples stand.

## CLOCKWORK SKIES (the cosmic crew, time, the machinery above)

- **Sunstone** — the day's own stone; the Clockmaker keeps one on the workbench as the standard all clocks are set against.
- **Alexandrite** — the boundary keeper; green in daylight, red by lamplight; the only stone the Examiner trusts to know what time it actually is.
- **Zultanite** — the color that changes with every light source; the Cartographer's reference stone — location is relative, and this stone agrees.
- **Andalusite** — the stone that shows different patterns from every angle; the crew's reminder that the same sky looks different from every ship.
- **Iolite** — the Viking compass; the stone that finds the sun through fog; every sky-ship navigator carries one, and the Cartographer signs them out like keys.
- **Sphene/Titanite** — (if added later) the fire-crew's prism.

## MAIN FLOOR (the commons, the hearth, the market, the welcome)

- **Rose Quartz** — the hearth's stone; the first case every guest passes; the Inn's welcome, in mineral form.
- **Carnelian** — the Firekeeper's stone; the ember color; courage that starts small and holds.
- **Citrine** — the merchant's stone; the Market's good-faith gold; never needs cleansing, which the artisans appreciate.
- **Amber** — the hearth fire's memory; sunlight that got stuck; the Common Room's warmest case.
- **Smoky Quartz** — the hearth at night; the stone that stayed by the fire until it went the color of the room.
- **Lepidocrocite** — the Inn's theory of the hearth in one stone; ember in a vessel of peace; the hearthstone itself is backed with it.
- **Rutilated Quartz** — the message caught mid-delivery; gold threads in clear stone; the Common Room's gossip, fossilized.
- **Tourmalinated Quartz** — the boundary drawn inside the stone; the bouncer's off-duty cousin; the Common Room keeps both stones side by side, and regulars know why.
- **Sard & Sardonyx** — the carved stones of old soldiers; the Main Floor's memorial shelf; courage in bands.
- **Bloodstone** — the Mender's stone; the root of first aid; kept behind the bar with the bandages.
- **Pounamu** — the stone that is given, never bought; the guest book is set on a slab of it; the Maori law holds upstairs too: it finds its person.
- **Unakite** — the stone of balanced partnerships; green and pink, the two forces agreeing; the Inn's own family stone, quietly.
- **Gold** — the forge's masterpiece; the Market prices things in it; the Main Floor keeps one raw nugget to remind everyone what money looked like before it was money.

## STAIRCASE (the alchemy — the bridge between matter and the divine)

- **Moldavite** — the staircase's own stone; born of a meteorite strike, matter transformed by fire from heaven; the only stone in the Inn that arrived from off-world, and it knows it.
- **Tektite** — Moldavite's plainer sibling; the same event, less famous, more available; the alchemists' teaching stone about not needing to be the exception to be part of the miracle.
- **Fire Opal** — the flame that stayed; the alchemist's first successful experiment, frozen at the moment of success.
- **Obsidian (Gold Sheen, Rainbow, Snowflake)** — the volcano's sudden grief, three ways; the staircase's descent cases; the snowflake one teaches the alchemists' favorite lesson: even frozen mid-sentence, the pattern continues.
- **Charoite** — the transformation stone; the swirl that looks like it is still happening; the staircase's spiral, in mineral form.
- **Nuummite** — the oldest fire; three billion years; the staircase's foundation stone, literally — two of them hold up the lowest landing.
- **Pyrite** — "fool's gold"; the alchemists' caution case; the most instructive stone in the collection because it is beautiful AND wrong.
- **Amethyst** — the transmutation purple; the color of the change itself; the staircase's most stolen and most returned stone.
- **Shungite** — carbon, almost pure; the water-cleaning stone; the staircase's practical alchemy — transformation you can drink.
- **Copper** — the first metal befriended; the conductor; every alchemical instrument in the Inn is wired with it.
- **Covellite** — copper mid-becoming; the alchemists' progress bar.
- **Sugilite** — the violet shield; the healer's alchemy; the staircase's protective case, kept near the descent.
