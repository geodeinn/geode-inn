## INTRODUCTION & DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

The Unique Stones are the catalog's loners, mystics, and singular anomalies — stones that do not belong to the major mineral families (such as Quartz, Agate, Jasper, Beryl, or Garnet). They have no family archetype to fall back on; each spirit possesses a entirely bespoke body geometry, movement style, and vibrational personality.

Whether born from ancient metamorphic pressures, cosmic meteor impacts, rare copper infiltrations, or complex organic-mineral concretions, these spirits represent the most individual presences in the Geode Inn.
