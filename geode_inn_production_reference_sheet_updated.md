### Structural Staple Rules
- Any new art for a structural staple zone MUST reference the staple image's palette, texture, lighting, and architectural language
- Player-buildable structures in these zones are constrained to the staple's visual vocabulary
- The staple image takes precedence over any conflicting concept art generated later
- New zones that achieve structural staple status should be added to this table
