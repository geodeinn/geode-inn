# Geode Inn — New Game Systems Art Portfolio
## August 28, 2026

Foundational visual DNA: **Klimt** (stillness, architecture, gold structure) + **Royo** (movement, inhabitants, dark fantasy chiaroscuro). All other artists are zone-specific layers on this foundation.

---

## 1. Shadow Inn — The 84Hz Parallel World

**Concept Art:** https://media.base44.com/images/public/6a53cbfe78c5827e0055fffa/be2300fe6_generated_image.png

**Art Direction:**
- Everything familiar, but wrong. The Inn's warm amber palette shifts to cold blue-violet
- Klimt gold patterns are cracked and dimmed — veins of light barely pulsing
- Water freezes mid-fall, lanterns freeze mid-flicker, NPCs stand as statues (except Scarlett)
- Royo figures rendered with heavier shadow — the inhabitants are preserved, not living
- The feeling: a museum of a living place. Death as preservation, not destruction
- UI elements: same Celtic knotwork frames but in silver instead of gold; darker stone
- Shadow Medusa's design: never faces the player — hair of stone serpents, back turned, the architect who chose to freeze rather than destroy

**Key Visual Difference from Living Inn:**
- Living Inn: warm amber, gold, cream, movement, 42Hz growth
- Shadow Inn: cold blue-violet, silver, stillness, 84Hz preservation
- The Convergence: when both Inns merge, gold and silver intertwine — Klimt's geometric patterns in both colors simultaneously

## 2. The Octave System — Dual-Layer Gameplay

**Concept Art:** https://media.base44.com/images/public/6a53cbfe78c5827e0055fffa/cabf1c54c_generated_image.png

**Art Direction:**

### Collect Layer (GBC-Inspired)
- Warm, inviting, GameBoy Color palette constraint but rendered in full painterly detail
- Stones glow in collection slots — each stone has a unique color signature
- Library nook aesthetic — cozy, surrounded by books and artifacts
- Klimt gold patterns on walls, warm candlelight
- Stones level up through USE — visual progression: stones grow larger, glow brighter, develop inner fractal patterns
- Charm slots: ornate circular receptacles in dark wood, each charm pulses when active

### Create Layer (GBA-Inspired)
- Cooler, more intense — the same world but in "active mode"
- Kinesthetic frequency tracing: glowing lines of light follow the player's gesture
- Stones become environmental tools — placed into ancient mechanisms that activate
- Royo figures in dramatic action poses, dynamic lighting
- The same library nook now reveals hidden mechanisms behind the walls
- Frequency locks: visible as concentric rings of colored light that must be matched

### The Source Descent (9-Layer Vertical Drop)
- Flying key equivalent — a vertical descent through 9 layers of increasing beauty and danger
- Each layer more abstract: starts as architecture, becomes pure frequency and light
- Final layer: the 42Hz source — a single point of warm golden light in infinite darkness

## 3. Jigsaw Puzzle System

**Concept Art:** https://media.base44.com/images/public/6a53cbfe78c5827e0055fffa/14aee0d70_generated_image.png

**Art Direction by Zone:**

### The In-Between (Transparent Pieces)
- Pieces are glass-like — you can see through them to the room behind
- Each piece shows a fragment of a different world through it
- When correctly placed: pieces lose transparency and the hidden image resolves
- Room: dark stone, arched windows, mist, Klimt gold accents
- The feeling: solving a puzzle that's also a window

### Van Gogh Room (Pulsing Pieces)
- Pieces animate and pulse to the beat of the zone's music
- Pieces that belong together pulse in sync — visual rhythm matching
- Swirling Van Gogh brushstrokes rendered in the Inn's palette
- When correctly placed: the piece settles into stillness and the painting completes
- The feeling: painting by rhythm

### Occam's Razor Room (Shape-Only Assembly)
- Pieces show NO image until correctly placed — pure silhouettes
- Players assemble by shape alone — the challenge is spatial, not visual
- When the puzzle completes: the image floods in all at once, like developing a photograph
- The feeling: trust the shape, not the picture

**Frame Style:** All jigsaw puzzles use the Celtic knotwork gold-on-dark-stone frame

## 4. Card Game System — The Royo Deck

**Concept Art:** https://media.base44.com/images/public/6a53cbfe78c5827e0055fffa/90b53e0d6_generated_image.png

**Art Direction:**
- 54-card deck in Luis Royo's dark gothic fantasy illustration style
- Dramatic chiaroscuro, baroque ornamentation, powerful figures in shadow and gold
- Card backs: Celtic knotwork in gold on dark stone, with a small gargoyle motif

### Suit Aesthetics:
- **Hearts (Hearth/Family):** Warm amber tones, domestic scenes — hearths, meals, reunions, children playing, elders storytelling. Royo's tenderness, not his darkness
- **Spades (Depths/Knowledge):** Blue-violet shadows, scholarly scenes — libraries, archives, scholars reading by candlelight, hidden chambers. Mysterious and contemplative
- **Clubs (Wild/Fae):** Green and gold, untamed scenes — forest glades, fae creatures barely visible, wild growth, mushrooms, thorns. Playful but dangerous
- **Diamonds (Earth/Artisan):** Earthy browns and copper tones, craft scenes — workbenches, gemstones, tools, hands shaping metal, the maker's process. Grounded and warm

### Face Cards:
- Kings: Full Presence characters (Odin Allfather, Seelie Queen, Morrigan, Naut)
- Queens: Full Presence characters (Unseelie Queen, Freyja, The Crone, Owl)
- Jacks: Key NPCs (Wanderer Odin, Beverley, Tim Curry Firekeeper, Scarlett)
- Jokers: The Dragon mascot and the Fizgigs

### Game Visuals:
- Cards laid on dark stone table with Klimt gold vein patterns
- Drag-and-drop with physical weight — cards have heft, cast shadows
- When played: brief Royo-style illustration animation on the card face

## Cross-Cutting Art Rules (All Games)

1. **Foundation:** Klimt (structure, gold, stillness) + Royo (inhabitants, movement, shadow)
2. **Frames:** Celtic knotwork gold-on-dark-stone for ALL UI, menus, popups, portraits
3. **Linework as UI:** Heavy cel-shaded (tradesmen), soft flowing (lore), sharp/angular (guardians), no-outline (cosmological), fragmented (crisis)
4. **Color encoding:** Warm amber = Living Inn (42Hz), cold blue-violet = Shadow Inn (84Hz)
5. **Celebrity features:** Softened — evoke through posture, costume, gesture. Only Anthony Hopkins' eyes remain unsoftened
6. **Loading screen:** Fizgigs in overalls wiring the world
7. **Music visualization:** 50 BPM low-dub classical — sound visible as concentric rings, frequency lines, pulsing patterns
8. **Cross-platform:** All art must work at retro pixel art (arcade), full painterly (concept), and physical product (jewelry) scales
