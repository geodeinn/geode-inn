# The Geode Inn — Playable Class Ability Sets

> *"The Inn doesn't give you a sword and tell you to save the world. It gives you a seat by the hearth, a stone that hums at the frequency of your own pulse, and asks if you brought any wood for the fire."*

---

## Overview & System Integration

In the **Geode Inn**, character identity is grounded in **vibration and frequency rather than static gender or traditional class archetypes**. The 5 starting playable classes represent family members whose personal resonances shape how they perceive, build, and interact with the living world inside the Inn-Tree and beyond.

### System Mechanics Reference

*   **Primary Stat & IEM Driver System:** Each class channels kinetic, acoustic, and perceptual input through a dedicated IEM driver architecture:
    *   **Bone Conduction:** Deep, low-frequency kinetic feedback felt through bones, stance, and load-bearing weight before it is heard.
    *   **Dynamic Driver:** Visceral, moving-air kinetic impact and acoustic punch; translating rhythm, percussion, and tidal pressure.
    *   **Balanced Armature:** High-precision clinical acoustic clarity; detecting micro-fractures in stone, lapidary resonance, and locks.
    *   **Electrostatic:** Ultra-high frequency ethereal sensing; tracking vocal harmonics, cosmological shifts, and ghost frequencies.
*   **Linework-as-UI System:** Visual feedback dynamically shifts based on class identity and state:
    *   **Heavy Cel-Shaded:** Applied to tradesmen, crafting, and heavy material work.
    *   **Soft Flowing:** Applied to lore, vocal resonance, and water alignment.
    *   **Sharp / Angular:** Applied to guardians, load-bearing stances, and defensive vectors.
    *   **No-Outline:** Applied to cosmological phasing, Anunnaki shifts, and celestial crystal states.
    *   **Fragmented:** Applied to crisis moments, autoimmune dissonance, and acoustic disruptions.
*   **Camera & Observation Mechanics:**
    *   **Third-Person:** Default exploration and combat camera.
    *   **First-Person:** Activated during intimate craft, micro-frequency listening, and precision targeting.
    *   **SIT Mechanic:** Interacting with benches, roots, or hearths shifts the camera into observation mode, synchronizing player frequency with the Inn's 42Hz drone to reveal hidden lore and recover reserves.
*   **Persephone's 7-Layer Water System:**
    1. *Surface Docks* (Harbor, coast, entry point)
    2. *Shallow Water* (Wading depth, tidal pools, selkie entry point)
    3. *Tidal Caves* (Sea-level gating, pirate's domain, rhythmic timing)
    4. *Deep Water* (Autoimmune labyrinth, siren's domain)
    5. *Underwater Cities* (Submerged ruins, Atlantis connection)
    6. *Crystal Core* (Water meets crystal, 42Hz core)
    7. *The Abyss* (Void below water, Nyx's domain)
*   **Building System (7 Base Materials):** Wood, Stone, Clay, Sand, Ore, Hemp, Thatching.
*   **Acoustic Stone Frequency:** Every stone operates on its native Mohs hardness scale coupled with the Inn-Tree's foundational **42Hz carrier drone**.

## 1. The Siren (Andra)

### 1. Class Name and Identity
*   **Class Name:** The Siren (Andra)
*   **Identity:** *The Acoustic Sovereign / The Dual Cadence*. Auburn redhead with raw, smoky Amy Winehouse magnetism, effortless vocal authority, and deep emotional resonance. Vibration is her identity: an oscillating dual cadence featuring an outer cello-cadence (fast, sweeping, public performance) and an inner piano-cadence (deliberate, deep, hidden self-recognition).
*   **Linework UI:** Soft Flowing (Lore), shifting to Fragmented (Crisis) when autoimmune dissonance surfaces.

### 2. Primary Stat
*   **Electrostatic Driver (Ethereal Sensing):** Captures ultra-high vocal harmonics, subtle acoustic room reflections, and liquid stone resonance. Allows Andra to perceive hidden soundscapes and water-depth frequencies that bypass ordinary ears.

### 3. Core Ability Set

#### 1. Smoky Cadence (Outer Voice)
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"She doesn't sing to be heard; she sings because the air feels empty without her. The room doesn't have a choice—it bends toward the cello in her throat."*
*   **Mechanic:** Projects an acoustic cone (15m, 60° arc). Hostiles caught within are *Charmed* and *Silenced* for 4 seconds as their attack cadence harmonizes with her song. Allies inside the cone gain +20% movement speed and continuous HP regeneration.
*   **Cooldown:** 14 seconds.

#### 2. Autoimmune Labyrinth (Inner Voice)
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"The hardest fight isn't against the beast at the gate. It's when the body turns inside out and decides its own heart is an intruder. She knows that fight intimately."*
*   **Mechanic:** Enters First-Person view, releasing a deep 32Hz piano pulse. Redirects 40% of all incoming party damage into a localized acoustic vortex for 6 seconds. When the vortex detonates, it heals allies equal to 50% of redirected damage and inflicts *Disruption* (Fragmented UI linework) on enemies, reducing their armor by 30%.
*   **Cooldown:** 22 seconds.

#### 3. The Mirror and the Shore
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"You can sing to ten thousand strangers, but until you hear your own voice echoed back from the dark, you're just making noise."*
*   **Mechanic:** Leverages Persephone's 4th water layer (Deep Water) to project an acoustic mirror. Instantly cleanses all status ailments on self and allies while reflecting enemy debuffs back onto attackers. Triggers the SIT mechanic prompt to reveal hidden submerged lore.
*   **Cooldown:** 30 seconds.

#### 4. Drown the Dissonance
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"Some sounds shouldn't exist. When she drops her voice an octave lower than the sea floor, those sounds simply cease to be."*
*   **Mechanic:** Anchors to the floor and emits a 21Hz electrostatic sub-bass shockwave (20m radius). Enemies suffer *Acoustic Stun* for 3 seconds. Instantly shatters low-tier structures built from Sand or Clay.
*   **Cooldown:** 25 seconds.

#### 5. Sovereign's Resplendence (Passive / Active)
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"A queen doesn't ask for room. She takes up space by breathing."*
*   **Mechanic:** **Passive:** Emits an aura healing party members for +2% HP/s, but cannot heal herself through this aura (her perfection is her wall). **Active:** Consumes 10% of current HP to grant all allies a shimmering liquid barrier absorbing 300% of the sacrificed HP in damage.
*   **Cooldown:** 30 seconds.

### 4. Signature Stone
*   **Aqua Aura Quartz** (Mohs Hardness: 7.0 / Resonates at 528Hz + 42Hz carrier drone). Gold-fused quartz glowing with iridescent peacock teal, amplifying vocal harmonics and deep-water acoustic projection.

### 5. Zone Affinity
*   **Deep Water Layer** (Persephone's 4th Water Layer) & **The Dock / Harbor Coast**. Thrives where open salt water meets living tree bark.

### 6. Unique Interaction with the Building System
*   **Acoustic Thatching & Wood Weaving:** When building with **Thatching** and **Wood**, Andra weaves acoustic channels into ceilings and soundboards, converting structures into living resonators (Tier 3 Sacred Buildings). Structures built by Andra amplify party health regeneration by +15% and hum with a 42Hz drone during rainfall.

### 7. Unique Interaction with the Fae Kingdom
*   **Faction Leaning:** Leans toward the **Unseelie Court** (Winter Court / Deep Shadow / The Queen of Webs).
*   **Unique Mechanic — Silk Thread Harmonics:** Can attach the Queen of Webs' vibrating acoustic threads to Unseelie structures, bypassing Winter Court riddle locks and turning dark fae hostility into harmonious musical power points.

### 8. Level Progression Path
*   **Tier 1 (Novice) — Busker of the Docks:** Unlocks *Smoky Cadence* and basic water-wading movement bonuses.
*   **Tier 2 (Adept) — Chanteuse of the Deep:** Unlocks *Autoimmune Labyrinth* and the *Acoustic Thatching* building trait.
*   **Tier 3 (Master) — Acoustic Sovereign:** Unlocks *The Mirror and the Shore* and full aquatic voice-casting across all Persephone water layers without breath loss.

## 2. The Pirate

### 1. Class Name and Identity
*   **Class Name:** The Pirate
*   **Identity:** *The Percussive Harbor Master / The Rhythm Keeper*. Drummer from Mexico with dual land/sea heritage, embodying the Inigo Montoya ethos after the blade is set down. Rich tenor-to-bass voice and a 50 BPM bodhrán beat. Vibration is his identity: a syncopated, driving physical rhythm that acts as the Inn's maritime heartbeat.
*   **Linework UI:** Heavy Cel-Shaded (Tradesmen / Maritime Craftsman) with dynamic pulse-line indicators.

### 2. Primary Stat
*   **Dynamic Driver (Heard + Felt):** Delivers moving-air kinetic impact and low-end acoustic punch. Translates drumbeats, sea surges, and heavy timber movement into physical momentum.

### 3. Core Ability Set

#### 1. Bodhrán Heartbeat (50 BPM)
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"A sword cuts one man at a time. A drum beat tells twenty men when to strike and forty when to hold."*
*   **Mechanic:** Strikes his bodhrán, establishing a rhythmic 50 BPM acoustic field (12m radius). Party members gain +25% attack speed and +15% stamina recovery when striking on the beat (guided by the visual pulse-line).
*   **Cooldown:** 12 seconds.

#### 2. Tidal Gating
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"The sea doesn't ask permission to flood the cellar. You either know when the gate opens, or you drown with the wine."*
*   **Mechanic:** Manipulates Persephone's 3rd water layer (Tidal Caves). Temporarily lowers sea levels by 1 tier for 10 seconds to clear flooded corridors, or raises water levels to slow enemies by 40% and sweep them into environmental traps.
*   **Cooldown:** 20 seconds.

#### 3. Forward-Pointing Compass
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"North is just where the needle settled because it got tired. This compass points to what comes after."*
*   **Mechanic:** Activates the compass artifact, revealing hidden passages, secret building compartments, and unmapped quest markers within 30m. Boosts party travel speed by +30% when moving toward uncharted territory.
*   **Cooldown:** 25 seconds.

#### 4. Dread Roberts' Reckoning
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"The title is passed from hand to hand. The weight stays the same, but good hands can hold anything."*
*   **Mechanic:** A heavy melee strike backed by dynamic driver kinetic force. Deals heavy physical damage (scaling with **Ore** in inventory) and knocks down hostiles in a 6m cone. Stuns targets for 3.5 seconds if executed on ship decks or docks.
*   **Cooldown:** 16 seconds.

#### 5. Harbor Master's Anchor (Passive)
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"You can't capsize a vessel that knows where its keel is."*
*   **Mechanic:** **Passive:** The Pirate and nearby allies gain +30% knockback resistance and total immunity to environmental movement penalties (mud, sea currents, loose sand).

### 4. Signature Stone
*   **Larimar** (Mohs Hardness: 5.0 / Resonates at 432Hz + 42Hz carrier drone). Rare volcanic blue pectolite born of island sea energy, carrying wave-cycle harmonics and maritime stability.

### 5. Zone Affinity
*   **Tidal Caves** (Persephone's 3rd Water Layer) & **The Harbor Master's Vessel / Docklands**.

### 6. Unique Interaction with the Building System
*   **Piling & Ore Reinforcement:** Master of **Ore** and **Wood** (maritime timber). When placing stilt foundations, docks, or harbor walls, he reinforces joints with heavy metal bands. His structures gain +50% durability against tidal erosion and add 2 additional storage slots per cargo chest built inside.

### 7. Unique Interaction with the Fae Kingdom
*   **Faction Leaning:** Leans toward the **Seelie Court** (Summer Court / Open Trade / Golden Light).
*   **Unique Mechanic — Fair-Trade Smuggling:** Establishes contraband trading routes between human settlements and the Seelie Court, exchanging human crafts for rare fae minerals (such as Preseli Blue Stone) without alignment penalties.

### 8. Level Progression Path
*   **Tier 1 (Novice) — Deckhand Drummer:** Unlocks *Bodhrán Heartbeat* and shallow-water navigation bonuses.
*   **Tier 2 (Adept) — Harbor Master:** Unlocks *Tidal Gating* and the *Piling & Ore Reinforcement* building trait.
*   **Tier 3 (Master) — Dread Pirate Roberts:** Unlocks *Forward-Pointing Compass* mastery and airship/flagship navigation across the world map.

## 3. The Sentinel (River)

### 1. Class Name and Identity
*   **Class Name:** The Sentinel (River)
*   **Identity:** *The Grounded Guardian / The Load-Bearing Wall*. Tall cat person of Anunnaki feline lineage, possessing a compact, wide boxing stance and immense spatial presence. Vibration is her identity: a deep, immutable infrasonic hum (64Hz) that anchors space and commands respect without loudness.
*   **Linework UI:** Sharp / Angular (Guardians), with wide, grounded baseline framing.

### 2. Primary Stat
*   **Bone Conduction (Deep, Felt):** Transmits low-frequency kinetic energy and physical weight through bone structure, stance, and earth contact, allowing her to absorb and deflect force before it registers audibly.

### 3. Core Ability Set

#### 1. Iron Stance (Boxing Grounding)
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"You can hit her with a sledgehammer if you've got the time. Just don't expect her to move."*
*   **Mechanic:** Roots River to the ground for up to 6 seconds. She becomes completely immune to knockbacks, pulls, and displacement effects. Reduces incoming damage by 50% and stores 30% of absorbed damage to empower her next counter-strike.
*   **Cooldown:** 12 seconds.

#### 2. Presence Aura (Gravitational Shift)
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"She doesn't raise her voice. She just walks into the room and the room remembers where its floor is."*
*   **Mechanic:** Emits a bone-conduction aura (10m radius). On first encounter, hostiles perceive her as 3x larger, drawing 100% aggro and reducing enemy attack accuracy by 25%. On second encounter, the perception corrects, inflicting *Exposed* (+30% party damage taken) on confused foes.
*   **Cooldown:** 18 seconds.

#### 3. Counter-Strike Precision
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"She doesn't swing first. She lets you make your mistake, and then she corrects it."*
*   **Mechanic:** A surgical boxing counter-strike playable within 1.5 seconds of parrying or taking damage. Deals heavy physical damage scaling with time held in *Iron Stance* (up to +300% bonus damage) and interrupts spellcasting.
*   **Cooldown:** 8 seconds.

#### 4. Faceted Shield (Anunnaki Aegis)
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"Three things last when the roof falls in: love, loyalty, and knowing where to stand."*
*   **Mechanic:** Slams her fists into the earth, raising a protective wall of crystalline stone absorbing up to 500 damage. Allies standing within 3m gain the *Load-Bearing* buff (+20% physical defense).
*   **Cooldown:** 22 seconds.

#### 5. Load-Bearing Wall (Passive)
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"If she drops, the room drops with her. So she doesn't drop."*
*   **Mechanic:** **Passive:** When an ally within 8m takes lethal damage, 50% of that damage is redirected to River's shield/HP pool instead, preventing immediate death.

### 4. Signature Stone
*   **Black Tourmaline** (Mohs Hardness: 7.5 / Resonates at 64Hz + 42Hz carrier drone). Dense grounding mineral that absorbs negative frequencies and converts kinetic pressure into piezoelectric stability.

### 5. Zone Affinity
*   **Deep Roots Zone** (Inn-Tree Catacombs Entrance) & **Earth Gate**.

### 6. Unique Interaction with the Building System
*   **Stone & Clay Masonry (Load-Bearing Integrity):** Master of **Stone** and **Clay**. Applying Anunnaki geometry, River's walls, pillars, and foundations feature double structural durability, zero stress fatigue, and can support Tier 4 Mausoleums or heavy vaults without collapsing.

### 7. Unique Interaction with the Fae Kingdom
*   **Faction Leaning:** Strict Neutrality / Guardian of the Threshold (Bridges Seelie and Unseelie Courts without taking sides).
*   **Unique Mechanic — Threshold Wall:** Builds stone and root barriers at Fae kingdom transit points, stopping cross-faction raids and creating permanent safe resting sanctuaries in root zones.

### 8. Level Progression Path
*   **Tier 1 (Novice) — Boxing Sentinel:** Unlocks *Iron Stance* and basic aggro drawing mechanics.
*   **Tier 2 (Adept) — Anunnaki Pillar:** Unlocks *Presence Aura* and the *Stone & Clay Masonry* building trait.
*   **Tier 3 (Master) — The Load-Bearing Wall:** Unlocks *Counter-Strike Precision* mastery and complete sanctuary construction in root zones.

## 4. The Artisan Thief (Selkie)

### 1. Class Name and Identity
*   **Class Name:** The Artisan Thief (Selkie)
*   **Identity:** *The Lapidary Redistribution Specialist / The Feminine Sandman*. Gothic elegance, pale and dark-haired, wearing a heavy leather jeweler's apron that IS her selkie skin. Vibration is her identity: micro-crystalline resonance (432Hz)—precise, literal, and caring through craft rather than social performance.
*   **Linework UI:** Heavy Cel-Shaded (Tradesmen), blending into Soft Flowing (Lore) at water boundaries.

### 2. Primary Stat
*   **Balanced Armature (Precise Hearing):** Delivers hyper-detailed clinical acoustic clarity. Detects micro-cracks in gemstones, tumbler frequencies in locks, hidden trap triggers, and subtle structural stresses.

### 3. Core Ability Set

#### 1. Lapidary Redistribution (Steal & Craft)
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"Hoarding sinks a ship and spoils a room. She doesn't steal to keep; she steals to carve something better out of what you were wasting."*
*   **Mechanic:** Siphons raw building materials (**Ore**, **Stone**, **Sand**) directly from hostiles or containers. Reduces enemy armor by 25% while instantly crafting temporary throwing blades or mineral-dust consumables for the party.
*   **Cooldown:** 10 seconds.

#### 2. Selkie Skin Dive
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"Land is fine for stones, but when the room gets too loud, she slips back into the water like a secret slipping back into the dark."*
*   **Mechanic:** Submerges into Persephone's 2nd water layer (Shallow/Tidal Water) or liquid floor surfaces. Grants complete invisibility and +40% movement speed for 8 seconds, enabling passage through flooded tunnels and narrow underwater grates.
*   **Cooldown:** 15 seconds.

#### 3. Malachite Toxicity (Gemstone Weaponization)
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"Every stone has a story. Copper carbonate's story is that if you grind it fine enough, even a king stops breathing."*
*   **Mechanic:** Crushes copper mineral dust (**Malachite / Ore**) to coat weapons or throw in an envelope. Enemies in a 6m radius take heavy Nature/Poison damage over 12 seconds and suffer *Acoustic Blindness* (UI linework becomes fragmented).
*   **Cooldown:** 18 seconds.

#### 4. Jeweler's Eye (Micro-Precision)
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"Where you see a solid wall, she sees seven structural flaws and a hairline fracture waiting for a needle."*
*   **Mechanic:** Toggles First-Person precision mode. Highlights enemy critical weak points, lock pins, hidden traps, and mineral veins through walls. Attacks landed during *Jeweler's Eye* deal guaranteed 100% Critical Strikes.
*   **Cooldown:** 20 seconds.

#### 5. Silversmith's Grace (Passive)
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"Her hands move like she's setting diamonds in a ring—quiet, exact, and never twice."*
*   **Mechanic:** **Passive:** Crafting speed increased by +100%, material costs reduced by 25% across all recipes, and movement produces zero footsteps noise.

### 4. Signature Stone
*   **Labradorite** (Mohs Hardness: 6.5 / Resonates at 432Hz + 42Hz carrier drone). The stone that drew her ashore; flashes with schillerizing blue-green light, revealing hidden optical and acoustic spectrums.

### 5. Zone Affinity
*   **Artisan Marketplace** & **Shallow Water Layer** (Persephone's 2nd Water Layer).

### 6. Unique Interaction with the Building System
*   **Sand & Hemp Precision Crafting:** Master of **Sand** (glassworking) and **Hemp** (weaving/ropework). Crafts precision stained glass windows (Tier 3 acoustic tuning), mechanical trap triggers, and filigree reinforcements. Her structures double as artisan trade shops with bonus item yields.

### 7. Unique Interaction with the Fae Kingdom
*   **Faction Leaning:** Slight leaning toward the **Seelie Court** (Craft & Exchange), operating as an independent contractor.
*   **Unique Mechanic — Gemstone Barter & Relic Duplication:** Replicates rare Fae artifacts using Labradorite and Sand, allowing players to fulfill Fae quest requirements without sacrificing original rare items.

### 8. Level Progression Path
*   **Tier 1 (Novice) — Apprentice Lapidary:** Unlocks *Lapidary Redistribution* and basic stealth mechanics.
*   **Tier 2 (Adept) — Selkie Carver:** Unlocks *Selkie Skin Dive* and *Sand & Hemp Precision Crafting*.
*   **Tier 3 (Master) — Master Artisan Thief:** Unlocks *Jeweler's Eye* and underwater traversal across all Persephone water layers.

## 5. The Chameleon Druid (Charles / The Constant)

### 1. Class Name and Identity
*   **Class Name:** The Chameleon Druid (Charles / The Constant)
*   **Identity:** *The Constant / Anunnaki Shapeshifter*. The oldest being in the Inn, carrying a pale blue-white starlight crystal from the preceding universe (which sang at 41.7Hz). Cillian Murphy quiet intensity mixed with Benedict Cumberbatch aloof intelligence. Vibration is his identity: a primordial cosmic shift matching the background frequency of whatever environment he inhabits.
*   **Linework UI:** No-Outline (Cosmological) when phasing/shifting, transitioning to Soft Flowing (Lore) in rest state.

### 2. Primary Stat
*   **Electrostatic Driver (Void Harmonic / Ethereal Sensing):** Tuned to celestial frequency shifts, cosmic skin-metamorphosis, and the ghost frequency (41.7Hz) of past universes.

### 3. Core Ability Set

#### 1. Reflexive Metamorphosis (Chameleon Shift)
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"He doesn't decide to change. The stone says 'grey' and his skin listens; the root says 'green' and his hair obeys."*
*   **Mechanic:** Passively shifts form to match terrain (Forest = Root-Beast, Catacombs = Stone-Phaser, Water = Aquan). **Active:** Phases through natural terrain (**Stone**, **Wood**, **Clay**) for 5 seconds, ignoring damage and collision.
*   **Cooldown:** 14 seconds.

#### 2. Starlight Seed (41.7Hz Ghost Pulse)
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"In his palm is a piece of a sky that died before this sun was warm. When he opens his hand, the room remembers what came before."*
*   **Mechanic:** Unveils his starlight crystal, pulsing at 41.7Hz (18m radius). Freezes enemy cooldown timers for 4 seconds, restores 25% resource energy to allies, and shifts UI linework to *No-Outline (Cosmological)*.
*   **Cooldown:** 25 seconds.

#### 3. Inn-Tree Synchronization (Rest Chair)
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"The reserve isn't what it used to be. But the tree doesn't ask how long you've got. The tree just hums."*
*   **Mechanic:** Uses the SIT mechanic by the Inn-Tree or garden hearth. Enters immobility, synchronizing his pulse with the Inn-Tree's 42Hz drone. Fully restores party health, cleanses debuffs, and recharges his finite shifting reserves over 6 seconds.
*   **Cooldown:** 40 seconds.

#### 4. Anunnaki Order (Starlight Tether)
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"He was putting things in order when the mountains were soft as clay. He can put this room in order too."*
*   **Mechanic:** Projects a starlight grid over a 12m area. Tethered enemies replicate 40% of damage taken across all tethered targets. Restructures damaged building blocks within the grid back to full durability.
*   **Cooldown:** 22 seconds.

#### 5. Finite Endurance (Passive)
*   **Tabletop Voice:** *"He has watched universes burn out. He knows energy isn't infinite—so every shift is a promise he keeps."*
*   **Mechanic:** **Passive:** Shifting forms grants high stat boosts (+40% Damage/Defense depending on form), but each shift consumes 20% of his *Endurance Bar*. When depleted, he must use *Inn-Tree Synchronization* or SIT at a hearth to replenish reserves.

### 4. Signature Stone
*   **Moldavite / Starlight Trovant** (Mohs Hardness: 5.5 / Resonates at 41.7Hz + 42Hz carrier drone). Tektite born of cosmic impact, carrying void glass resonance and the pulse of the previous universe.

### 5. Zone Affinity
*   **Sacred Grove / Canopy of Sála** & **The Hearthwood Hollow**.

### 6. Unique Interaction with the Building System
*   **Living Wood & Thatching Synthesis:** Master of **Wood**, **Clay**, and **Thatching**. Shapes living trees and roots directly into structural elements without felling trees (Tier 1 Shelters & Tier 5 Crimean Cave City structures). His buildings self-heal over time (+5% durability/min) and harmonize with the 42Hz drone.

### 7. Unique Interaction with the Fae Kingdom
*   **Faction Leaning:** Absolute Faction Neutrality / Elder Reverence (Respected by both Seelie and Unseelie Courts as an ancient primordial entity).
*   **Unique Mechanic — Cycle Unification:** Mediates between Seelie and Unseelie rulers, unlocking cross-court quests and opening locked Stone Circle Portals without needing to complete arcade games.

### 8. Level Progression Path
*   **Tier 1 (Novice) — Root Walker:** Unlocks *Reflexive Metamorphosis* and basic environmental blending.
*   **Tier 2 (Adept) — Chameleon Druid:** Unlocks *Starlight Seed* and *Living Wood & Thatching Synthesis*.
*   **Tier 3 (Master) — The Constant (Anunnaki Master):** Unlocks *Anunnaki Order* and continuous starlight phasing when synchronized with the 42Hz hearth.
